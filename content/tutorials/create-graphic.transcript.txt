Create a Graphic
Turn a saved build or a team into a shareable image, with Guide Examples clearly labelled.

[0:00] Welcome
Want to show off a build? Create a Graphic turns your roster into a shareable image in seconds.

[0:08] Build cards
Pick Build card and a character. My Build uses exactly what you saved in My Roster, so there's nothing to type. Switch to Guide Example for PawPixl's example build. It's labelled on the card, so nobody mistakes it for your own.

[0:25] Templates and sizes
Then Choose a template... Showcase is made for sharing, and Detailed build shows every piece and stat. Anything you haven't saved shows as missing, never invented. Pick a size, vertical, square or landscape, and choose what to show, like constellation, level or stats.

[0:43] Team cards
Team cards work the same way. Any numbers on a team card are modelled estimates, not team DPS.

[0:51] Sharing
Then download the PNG, or copy the image. Cards are made in your browser, and only show build details and pawpixl.com, never your UID or email.

[1:02] All set
now go show off that build Traveler! See you around!

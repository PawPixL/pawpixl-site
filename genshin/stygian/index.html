Stygian Onslaught 7.2 Guide: Bosses and Teams (Nov 11 – Dec 15)

Stygian Onslaught guide for Version 7.2, Nov 11 – Dec 15, 2026: boss breakdowns and team picks. Featuring Mitya, Yae Miko, Odette, Valeriy, Sucrose, Cyno.

Last updated: November 11, 2026 · Game version 7.2 · Nov 11 – Dec 15, 2026

Bosses

Wavebinder: Stormfang (Boss 1, Hydro)

What it does:

  • Drinking builds Courage; Electro DMG drains it.
  • Stellar Glimmer Electro reactions drain more Courage.
  • Apply Radiance: Stellar Glimmer while it drinks to slow Courage buildup.
  • Full Courage grants a shield and increased RES.
  • Depleting Courage removes the shield and deals massive backlash DMG.

How to beat it:

  • Prioritize Electro DMG to drain Courage.
  • Trigger Stellar Glimmer reactions for faster Courage reduction.
  • Apply Radiance: Stellar Glimmer while it drinks to slow Courage buildup.
  • Avoid wasting your strongest attacks against its shield.
  • Once Valiant Assault ends, unleash your strongest attacks during its backlash window.

Emperor of Fire and Iron (Boss 2, Pyro)

What it does:

  • Pyro horns grant the boss massive RES to all elements.
  • Horns have greatly increased durability in Stygian Onslaught.
  • Sternshield Crabs can appear during the fight.
  • Watch for Radiant Coronation, a powerful AoE attack.

How to beat it:

  • Bring a strong Hydro applicator to break the Pyro horns.
  • Focus on the horns before attacking the boss.
  • Once broken, the boss is stunned and loses its RES boost.
  • Use the stun window to unleash your strongest attacks.
  • Watch your positioning to avoid AoE attacks and crab pressure.

Radiant Moongecko (Boss 3, Geo)

What it does:

  • Geo shield has 18 layers on Dire; Geo and blunt attacks break it.
  • Shield break stuns the boss and lowers Geo RES from 70% to 10%.
  • Radiant Cocoon drains HP and reduces Crit Rate by 100%.
  • Healing everyone to 100% removes the debuff and grants 15% Crit Rate and 50% DMG.
  • Moonstone Anchor wards must be destroyed within 20 seconds or the boss gains 30% ATK.

How to beat it:

  • Bring Geo or blunt attacks to break the shield quickly.
  • Use a strong team-wide healer to restore everyone to 100% HP.
  • Heal through Radiant Cocoon to remove the Crit Rate debuff and gain its damage bonus.
  • Destroy all Moonstone Anchor wards within 20 seconds.
  • Save your strongest attacks for the shield-break stun window.

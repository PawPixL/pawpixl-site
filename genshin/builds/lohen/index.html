Lohen Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

Lohen build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Disaster and Remorse, Staff of Homa), artifacts (A Day Carved From Rising Winds), main stats.

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Role: Main DPS

Lohen is an on-field Cryo DPS who builds resources to unleash powerful Skill attacks. He rewards strong rotations and active off-field teammates.

Best weapons for Lohen

  1. Disaster and Remorse: Signature CRIT Rate polearm with broad damage buffs; stronger after the Hexerei: Secret Rite condition.
  2. Staff of Homa: Provides 66.2% CRIT DMG, 20% HP, and an ATK bonus based on Max HP, making it a strong 5-star alternative for Lohen.
  3. Fractured Halo: CRIT DMG and ATK after Skill or Burst. The Lunar-Charged team bonus needs the wielder to create a shield. Shield-creation passive unavailable.
  4. Primordial Jade Winged-Spear: CRIT Rate and stacking ATK with hits; needs field time to reach full stacks.
  5. Sacrificer's Staff: CRIT Rate polearm that stacks ATK and ER when Skill hits; a practical lower-rarity option.
  6. Deathmatch: Provides 36.8% CRIT Rate and up to 24% ATK at R1 against fewer than two enemies, making it a strong 4-star alternative for Lohen.
  7. Missive Windspear: Provides 41.3% ATK and, at R5, grants 24% ATK and 96 EM after triggering an Elemental Reaction, making it a solid F2P option for Lohen.
  8. Tamayuratei no Ohanashi: stacks granted on Skill use — the combo already casts Skill once every cycle.

Best artifacts for Lohen

  • A Day Carved From Rising Winds (4 pcs) · Main DPS: Provides 18% ATK and up to 25% additional ATK plus 20% CRIT Rate after Witch’s Homework, making it Lohen’s best-in-slot artifact set. Main stats: ATK% · Cryo DMG Bonus/ATK% · CR/CD.
  • Blizzard Strayer (4 pcs) · Main DPS: Provides 15% Cryo DMG and up to 40% CRIT Rate against Cryo-affected or Frozen enemies, making it a situational alternative for Lohen in Freeze teams.
  • Marechaussee Hunter (4 pcs) · Main DPS: Provides 15% Normal and Charged Attack DMG and up to 36% CRIT Rate when HP changes, making it a situational option for Lohen if his team provides consistent HP fluctuations.

Main stats

  • Sands: ATK%
  • Goblet: Cryo DMG Bonus / ATK%
  • Circlet: Crit Rate / Crit DMG

Stat targets

  • ATK: 2,000+
  • Crit Rate: 60% - 70%
  • Crit DMG: 180% - 200%+
  • Elemental Mastery: 0-200 (Melt)

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 9+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 9+

Best teams for Lohen

  • Witch Please: Lohen (Main DPS), Nicole (Support), Citlali (Res Shred), Durin (Sub DPS). Lohen deals Cryo damage and can trigger Reverse Melt using Durin’s Pyro application. Nicole buffs the team, while Citlali shields and supports reactions.
  • Blizzard Queen: Lohen (Main DPS), Furina (Buffs), Citlali (Support), Escoffier (Support). Lohen drives on-field Cryo damage, Furina provides Hydro application and DMG buffs, Citlali adds shielding and Cryo application, and Escoffier provides healing, Cryo RES shred, and off-field damage.
  • Amber Alerted Us: Lohen (Main DPS), Vodyanitsa (Buffs), Mona (Hex), Escoffier (Support). Lohen drives on-field Cryo damage, Vodyanitsa provides Hydro application, healing, and RES shred, Mona boosts damage with Omen, and Escoffier adds Cryo/Hydro RES shred and healing.
  • Lohen at Home: Lohen (Main DPS), Sucrose (EM Build), Bennett (Support), Xiangling (Sub DPS). Xiangling maintains Pyro for Lohen to trigger Reverse Melt. Bennett heals and buffs ATK, while Sucrose shares EM and lowers Cryo resistance when she swirls Cryo.

Pros

  • High Cryo damage
  • Strong Skill damage
  • Fast rotations
  • Strong team synergy

Cons

  • Setup dependent
  • Rotation sensitive
  • Wants off-field DPS
  • Constellation scaling

Build variations

Cryo Skill Carry (On-field DPS)

Use Lohen’s resource-driven Skill attacks for personal Cryo damage.

  • Weapons: Disaster and Remorse, Staff of Homa, Deathmatch
  • Artifacts: A Day Carved From Rising Winds (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ATK/CRIT focus; account for set and weapon bonuses.
  • Teams: Cryo buffs and compatible off-field reactions.
  • When it applies: Rotation and resource timing matter.

Furina CRIT Alternative (On-field DPS)

An alternative to the primary set in HP-fluctuation teams.

  • Weapons: Disaster and Remorse, Staff of Homa
  • Artifacts: Marechaussee Hunter (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Adjust CRIT Rate around Marechaussee stacks.
  • Teams: Furina plus reliable HP changes and healing.
  • When it applies: Only if HP changes keep the artifact buff active.

Lohen talents

Normal Attack: Spear of Favonius — Broken Oath

Normal Attack
Performs up to 5 consecutive strikes.

Charged Attack
Consumes a certain amount of Stamina to perform a forward thrust.

Plunging Attack
Plunges from mid-air to strike the ground below, damaging opponents along the path and dealing AoE DMG upon impact.

Elemental Skill: Unforeseen Strike

Cooldown: 18s

As he searches for weaknesses, Lohen switches to Masterstroke mode. While in this state:
· Lohen can actively accumulate Joy, using it to unleash the special Elemental Skill Etched Into Bone and Soul.
· Nearby party members can accumulate Will to Win for Lohen, increasing the DMG dealt by Etched Into Bone and Soul.
Lohen can unleash Etched Into Bone and Soul up to 3 times while in Masterstroke mode, after which, he will no longer be able to accumulate Joy.

Elemental Burst: Manifest Judgment

Cooldown: 15s · Energy cost: 60

Performs a swift series of forward strikes, consuming all Will to Win and dealing Cryo DMG. This DMG will be increased based on the amount of Will to Win consumed. If Lohen is in Masterstroke mode when this is performed, the duration of Masterstroke will be extended by 1.65s.

Passive talent: Moratorium on Questioning

When nearby party members accumulate Will to Win for Lohen by dealing DMG while he is in Masterstroke mode, if that DMG is not less than 3,000% of Lohen's Base ATK, it will accumulate an extra 60 Will to Win.

Passive talent: Flippant Masterpiece

While in Masterstroke mode, for 8s after another nearby party member triggers a Cryo Reaction, that character's ATK is increased by 15%, while Lohen's ATK is increased by 15%.

Passive talent: When the Mood Strikes

Lohen gains "High Spirits" for 9s after he unleashes the Elemental Skill Unforeseen Strike: Increases the Skill Level of Unforeseen Strike by 1.
When unleashing an Elemental Skill, if the Level of any other party member's Normal Attack, Elemental Skill, or Elemental Burst is not lower than Lohen's Elemental Skill Level, the duration of this instance of High Spirits will be increased by an additional 6s.
High Spirits can be gained once every 18s.

Lohen constellations

C1: O Breezes, That So Oft Bear Sorrowful Lament

Lohen's maximum Will to Win limit is increased to 300% of its original value.
Additionally, when other nearby party members deal DMG while Lohen is in Masterstroke mode, they accumulate 500% as much Will to Win for him as they originally would have.

C2: In Flight, I Strike Whatever Flies

When in Masterstroke mode, after the special Elemental Skill Etched Into Bone and Soul hits an opponent or the Elemental Burst Manifest Judgment is unleashed, Lohen gains "Evilsbane Blade" for 4s: The next time Lohen hits an opponent with a Normal or Charged Attack while in Masterstroke mode, he will follow up with an additional strike that deals AoE Cryo DMG equal to 500% of his ATK, and increase the Elemental Mastery of other nearby party members by 200 for 8s. Evilsbane Blade can be triggered once every 4s.

C3: Only the Spear That Wounds Can Heal

Increases the Level of Unforeseen Strike by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C4: Radiant Love, Laughing Death

When Lohen unleashes the Elemental Burst Manifest Judgment while in Masterstroke mode, his Will to Win will be maxed out, causing this instance of his Elemental Burst to gain the maximum possible DMG increase.

Additionally, when Lohen switches to Masterstroke mode, the following will occur depending on how much Elemental Energy he has:
· When Elemental Energy is not full: Lohen recovers 15 Elemental Energy.
· When Elemental Energy is full: If, during the next 15s, Lohen unleashes the Elemental Burst Manifest Judgment, he will recover 15 Elemental Energy.

C5: Never Ask, Nor Trouble You to Know

Increases the Level of Manifest Judgment by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C6: To Drown, to Sink, Unconscious — Supreme Joy

Using the special Elemental Skill Etched Into Bone and Soul and hitting an opponent with it or, while Masterstroke mode is active, unleashing the Elemental Burst Manifest Judgment, will not consume any Will to Win. Instead, Lohen's Joy will be maxed out, and if he hits an opponent the next time he uses the special Elemental Skill Etched Into Bone and Soul, the duration of this instance of Masterstroke will be extended by 1.25s. This effect can be triggered once every 7s.
Additionally, the CRIT DMG of all the DMG described above is increased by 175%, and the number of times Lohen can unleash Etched Into Bone and Soul during this instance of Masterstroke is increased by 2.

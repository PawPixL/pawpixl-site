Lohen Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams (7.2)

Lohen build guide for Genshin Impact 7.2: best weapons (Disaster and Remorse, Staff of Homa), artifacts (A Day Carved From Rising Winds), main stats.

Last updated: November 12, 2026 · Game version 7.2

Role: Main DPS

Lohen is an on-field Cryo DPS who builds resources to unleash powerful Skill attacks. He rewards strong rotations and active off-field teammates.

Best weapons for Lohen

  1. Disaster and Remorse: Signature CRIT Rate polearm with broad damage buffs; stronger after the Hexerei: Secret Rite condition.
  2. Staff of Homa: Provides 66.2% CRIT DMG, 20% HP, and an ATK bonus based on Max HP, making it a strong 5-star alternative for Lohen.
  3. Fractured Halo: CRIT DMG and ATK after Skill or Burst. The Lunar-Charged team bonus needs the wielder to create a shield. Shield-creation passive unavailable.
  4. Primordial Jade Winged-Spear: CRIT Rate and stacking ATK with hits; needs field time to reach full stacks.
  5. Sacrificer's Staff: CRIT Rate polearm that stacks ATK and ER when Skill hits; a practical lower-rarity option.
  6. Deathmatch: Provides 36.8% CRIT Rate and up to 24% ATK at R1 against fewer than two enemies, making it a strong 4-star alternative for Lohen.
  7. Missive Windspear: Provides 41.3% ATK and, at R5, grants 24% ATK and 96 EM after triggering an Elemental Reaction, making it a solid F2P option for Lohen.
  8. Tamayuratei no Ohanashi: stacks granted on Skill use — the combo already casts Skill once every cycle.

Best artifacts for Lohen

  • A Day Carved From Rising Winds (4 pcs) · Main DPS: Provides 18% ATK and up to 25% additional ATK plus 20% CRIT Rate after Witch’s Homework, making it Lohen’s best-in-slot artifact set. Main stats: ATK% · Cryo DMG Bonus/ATK% · CR/CD.
  • Blizzard Strayer (4 pcs) · Main DPS: Provides 15% Cryo DMG and up to 40% CRIT Rate against Cryo-affected or Frozen enemies, making it a situational alternative for Lohen in Freeze teams.
  • Marechaussee Hunter (4 pcs) · Main DPS: Provides 15% Normal and Charged Attack DMG and up to 36% CRIT Rate when HP changes, making it a situational option for Lohen if his team provides consistent HP fluctuations.

Main stats

  • Sands: ATK%
  • Goblet: Cryo DMG Bonus / ATK%
  • Circlet: Crit Rate / Crit DMG

Stat targets

  • ATK: 2,000+
  • Crit Rate: 60% - 70%
  • Crit DMG: 180% - 200%+
  • Elemental Mastery: 0-200 (Melt)

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 9+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 9+

Best teams for Lohen

  • Witch Please: Lohen (Main DPS), Nicole (Support), Citlali (Res Shred), Durin (Sub DPS). Lohen deals Cryo damage and can trigger Reverse Melt using Durin’s Pyro application. Nicole buffs the team, while Citlali shields and supports reactions.
  • Blizzard Queen: Lohen (Main DPS), Furina (Buffs), Citlali (Support), Escoffier (Support). Lohen drives on-field Cryo damage, Furina provides Hydro application and DMG buffs, Citlali adds shielding and Cryo application, and Escoffier provides healing, Cryo RES shred, and off-field damage.
  • Amber Alerted Us: Lohen (Main DPS), Vodyanitsa (Buffs), Mona (Hex), Escoffier (Support). Lohen drives on-field Cryo damage, Vodyanitsa provides Hydro application, healing, and RES shred, Mona boosts damage with Omen, and Escoffier adds Cryo/Hydro RES shred and healing.
  • Lohen at Home: Lohen (Main DPS), Sucrose (EM Build), Bennett (Support), Xiangling (Sub DPS). Xiangling maintains Pyro for Lohen to trigger Reverse Melt. Bennett heals and buffs ATK, while Sucrose shares EM and lowers Cryo resistance when she swirls Cryo.

Pros

  • High Cryo damage
  • Strong Skill damage
  • Fast rotations
  • Strong team synergy

Cons

  • Setup dependent
  • Rotation sensitive
  • Wants off-field DPS
  • Constellation scaling

Build variations

Cryo Skill Carry (On-field DPS)

Use Lohen’s resource-driven Skill attacks for personal Cryo damage.

  • Weapons: Disaster and Remorse, Staff of Homa, Deathmatch
  • Artifacts: A Day Carved From Rising Winds (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ATK/CRIT focus; account for set and weapon bonuses.
  • Teams: Cryo buffs and compatible off-field reactions.
  • When it applies: Rotation and resource timing matter.

Furina CRIT Alternative (On-field DPS)

An alternative to the primary set in HP-fluctuation teams.

  • Weapons: Disaster and Remorse, Staff of Homa
  • Artifacts: Marechaussee Hunter (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Adjust CRIT Rate around Marechaussee stacks.
  • Teams: Furina plus reliable HP changes and healing.
  • When it applies: Only if HP changes keep the artifact buff active.

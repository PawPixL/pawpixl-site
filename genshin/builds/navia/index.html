Navia Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

Navia build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Verdict, A Thousand Blazing Suns), artifacts (Nighttime Whispers in the Echoing Woods), main stats.

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Role: Main DPS

Navia is an ATK-scaling Geo DPS who delivers massive frontloaded damage through her Elemental Skill. She generates Crystal Shrapnel to empower devastating shotgun blasts.

Best weapons for Navia

  1. Verdict: Provides CRIT Rate, ATK, and increased Elemental Skill damage.
  2. A Thousand Blazing Suns: Provides CRIT Rate, ATK, and powerful damage-boosting buffs.
  3. Serpent Spine: Provides CRIT Rate and a stacking damage bonus for consistent output.
  4. Beacon of the Reed Sea: CRIT Rate and ATK boosts from Skill hits or taking damage; HP bonus when unshielded.
  5. Skyward Pride: Energy Recharge and a universal damage bonus; its vacuum-blade passive needs on-field attacks.
  6. Tidal Shadow: Provides ATK% and increases ATK after receiving healing.
  7. "Ultimate Overlord's Mega Magic Sword": Offers Energy Recharge and an ATK bonus that improves with completed Melusine quests. An accessible event weapon for Navia.
  8. Prototype Archaic: Craftable ATK claymore with an occasional on-field hit. Budget fallback; better options exist. Only if stronger weapons unavailable.

Best artifacts for Navia

  • Nighttime Whispers in the Echoing Woods (4 pcs) · Best Overall: Boosts ATK and Geo damage, especially with a Crystallize shield active. Main stats: ATK% · Geo DMG Bonus · CR/CD.
  • Marechaussee Hunter (4 pcs) · If paired with Furina: Furina enables consistent HP fluctuations, maximizing CRIT Rate and Skill damage. Main stats: ATK% · Geo DMG Bonus · CR/CD.
  • Archaic Petra + Gladiator's Finale (2 + 2 pcs) · Alt DPS: Combines Geo DMG Bonus and ATK%, strengthening her overall damage. Main stats: ATK% · Geo DMG Bonus · CR/CD.

Main stats

  • Sands: ATK%
  • Goblet: Geo DMG Bonus
  • Circlet: CRIT Rate / CRIT DMG

Stat targets

  • ATK: 2,000+
  • Crit Rate: 70% - 80%
  • Crit DMG: 160%+
  • Energy Recharge: 120% - 140%

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 9+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 9+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 9+

Best teams for Navia

  • Natlan Hypercarry: Navia, Mavuika, Xilonen, Bennett. Navia deals massive damage while Mavuika, Xilonen, and Bennett provide buffs.
  • Circle Impact: Navia, Zhongli, Furina, Bennett. Navia deals massive damage while Furina buffs, Zhongli shreds, Bennett boosts ATK.
  • Lunar Rocks: Navia, Columbina, Illuga, Linnea. Navia leads Geo damage while Columbina, Illuga, and Linnea amplify Lunar-Crystallize reactions.
  • Noelles the main now. Its hopeless: Navia, Noelle, Yelan, Furina. Noelle drives Geo attacks with Furina and Yelan providing Hydro damage and buffs. Navia contributes quickswap Skill damage when Crystallize shards are available.

Pros

  • Massive Damage
  • Flexible Teams
  • Strong Burst
  • Easy Gameplay

Cons

  • Shrapnel Management
  • Crit Investment
  • Frontloaded Damage
  • Energy Requirements

Build variations

Standard Geo DPS (On-field DPS)

Maximize front-loaded Skill damage.

  • Weapons: Verdict, Beacon of the Reed Sea
  • Artifacts: Nighttime Whispers in the Echoing Woods (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ATK% / Geo / CRIT.
  • Teams: Reliable Crystallize generation and Geo support.

Furina CRIT (On-field DPS)

Use when HP fluctuations consistently maintain Marechaussee stacks.

  • Weapons: Verdict, Serpent Spine
  • Artifacts: Marechaussee Hunter (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Account for 36% artifact CRIT Rate.
  • Teams: Furina plus reliable healing.

Role: Support / Sub DPS

Aino is an off-field Hydro support whose Burst enables reactions while her Moonsign passive raises the team's Moonsign level. Pair her with another Nod-Krai character for Ascendant Gleam, improving her Hydro application and Silken Moon buffs. Prioritize Burst uptime.

  2. Makhaira Aquamarine

Pros

  • Off-field Hydro application
  • Boosts Moonsign level
  • Strong Lunar team support
  • Free story character

Cons

  • Modest personal damage
  • Burst reliant
  • Limited Hydro application
  • Wants Nod-Krai teammate

Build variations

  • Lunar-Charged EM Support (Off-field Support): Best for Moonsign and Lunar-Charged teams needing Hydro, EM buffs, and a short team rotation.
  • Overvape Support (Off-field Support): Provides Hydro, Lunar-Charged and EM buffs for mixed Vape/Overloaded teams.
  • Hyperbloom Secondary Hydro (Reaction Support): Adds some Hydro and buffs while teammates generate and trigger most Dendro Cores.
  • Noblesse ATK Buffer (Support): Use when ATK buffs offer more value than Lunar buffs or another teammate already holds Silken Moon.

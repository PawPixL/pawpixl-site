Aino Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

Aino build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Favonius Greatsword, Makhaira Aquamarine), artifacts (Silken Moon's Serenade), main stats.

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Role: Support / Sub DPS

Aino is an off-field Hydro support whose Burst enables reactions while her Moonsign passive raises the team's Moonsign level. Pair her with another Nod-Krai character for Ascendant Gleam, improving her Hydro application and Silken Moon buffs. Prioritize Burst uptime.

Best weapons for Aino

  1. Favonius Greatsword: ER and neutral particles help Aino and the team Burst reliably.
  2. Makhaira Aquamarine: EM secondary stat and an EM-based party ATK buff support reaction teams that already have enough Energy. For ATK-scaling teams with sufficient ER.
  3. Flame-Forged Insight: Event weapon gives substantial EM and Energy support.
  4. Master Key: Craftable EM and Energy option for players without the event weapon.
  5. Forest Regalia: ER secondary stat and a collectible EM-granting Leaf after Bloom; pickup can be inconvenient. Bloom teams; teammate must collect Leaf. R5 improves EM buff.
  6. Sacrificial Greatsword: A second Skill can generate more Hydro particles and help battery an ally, but is less flexible than Favonius. Works best with high refinement. High refinement preferred.
  7. Katsuragikiri Nagamasa: Craftable ER weapon with a flat Energy refund that helps stabilize Burst uptime. Useful when already crafted/refined.

Best artifacts for Aino

  • Silken Moon's Serenade (4 pcs) · Best: Best Lunar support set: grants ER, team EM and Lunar Reaction DMG bonuses. Main stats: ER/EM · EM · EM/CR.
  • Instructor (4 pcs): Aino triggers an on-field reaction to grant teamwide EM. Main stats: ER/EM · EM · EM/CR.
  • Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs): Burst grants an ATK buff to the whole team; best when teammates value ATK more than EM. Main stats: ER · EM/Hydro · CR/EM.

Main stats

  • Sands: Energy Recharge / Elemental Mastery
  • Goblet: Elemental Mastery / Hydro DMG
  • Circlet: CRIT Rate / Elemental Mastery

Stat targets

  • ER: 155% - 205% (C0 Favonius)
  • ER: 190% - 250%+ (C0 no Fav)
  • ER: 125% - 165% (C4 Favonius)
  • ER: 100% - 140% (Flame-Forged)
  • CRIT Rate: 40% - 60% (Fav)

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 6+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 6-8

Best teams for Aino

  • Moonlit Circuit: Aino (Off-field DPS / Enabler), Flins (On-field DPS), Kuki Shinobu (Healer / Reaction Trigger), Sucrose (EM Buffer / Debuffer). Flins drives Lunar-Charged; Aino enables Ascendant Gleam and Hydro, Shinobu supplies Electro/healing, and Sucrose shreds RES and shares EM.
  • Ducky Power Grid: Aino (Off-field DPS / Enabler), Ineffa (Off-field DPS / Shielder), Ororon (Off-field DPS / Battery), Ifa (On-field Driver). Aino and Ineffa enable Ascendant Gleam and Lunar-Charged. Ororon adds Electro damage, while Ifa drives reactions and boosts them.
  • Overvape Fireworks: Aino (Off-field DPS / Enabler), Yoimiya (On-field DPS), Ineffa (Off-field DPS / Shielder), Bennett (Healer / Buffer). Aino and Ineffa enable Ascendant Gleam, Hydro and Lunar-Charged; Bennett buffs Yoimiya. Vaporize consistency remains matchup-dependent.
  • Ducky Hyperbloom: Aino (Off-field DPS / Enabler), Baizhu (Healer / Shielder), Raiden Shogun (Reaction Trigger), Xingqiu (Off-field DPS / Hydro Enabler). Xingqiu supplies reliable Hydro, Baizhu applies Dendro and heals, Raiden triggers Hyperbloom; Aino adds off-field Hydro and team buffs.

Build variations

Lunar-Charged EM Support (Off-field Support)

Best for Moonsign and Lunar-Charged teams needing Hydro, EM buffs, and a short team rotation.

  • Weapons: Makhaira Aquamarine, Favonius Greatsword, Flame-Forged Insight, Master Key
  • Artifacts: Silken Moon's Serenade (4 pcs); Instructor (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Reach ER first, then emphasize EM; add CRIT Rate with Favonius.
  • Teams: Flins or Ineffa; second Nod-Krai character for Ascendant Gleam.
  • When it applies: Aino plus another Nod-Krai unit enables Ascendant Gleam.

Overvape Support (Off-field Support)

Provides Hydro, Lunar-Charged and EM buffs for mixed Vape/Overloaded teams.

  • Weapons: Favonius Greatsword, Makhaira Aquamarine, Master Key
  • Artifacts: Silken Moon's Serenade (4 pcs); Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Focus on ER and team buffs; personal damage is secondary.
  • Teams: Ineffa plus a slower-applying Pyro damage dealer such as Yoimiya.
  • When it applies: Vaporize timing can be inconsistent; do not rely on Aino as solo Hydro for faster Pyro application.

Hyperbloom Secondary Hydro (Reaction Support)

Adds some Hydro and buffs while teammates generate and trigger most Dendro Cores.

  • Weapons: Favonius Greatsword, Forest Regalia, Master Key
  • Artifacts: Silken Moon's Serenade (4 pcs); Instructor (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ER until Burst uptime is secure; EM is secondary if Aino rarely owns Bloom reactions.
  • Teams: Dendro + EM-built Electro trigger + a primary Hydro applicator.
  • When it applies: Aino alone does not provide enough Hydro for consistent core production.

Noblesse ATK Buffer (Support)

Use when ATK buffs offer more value than Lunar buffs or another teammate already holds Silken Moon.

  • Weapons: Favonius Greatsword, Makhaira Aquamarine, Sacrificial Greatsword
  • Artifacts: Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs); Instructor (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ER/EM Sands, CRIT Rate if Favonius; skip personal-damage targets.
  • Teams: ATK-scaling carry; no better Noblesse wearer in the team.
  • When it applies: Noblesse 4pc buff does not stack with another Noblesse user.

Aino talents

Normal Attack: Bish-Bash-Bosh Repair

Normal Attack
Performs up to 3 consecutive strikes.

Charged Attack
Drains Stamina over time to perform continuous spinning attacks against all nearby opponents.
At the end of the sequence, performs a more powerful slash.

Plunging Attack
Plunges from mid-air to strike the ground below, damaging opponents along the path and dealing AoE DMG upon impact.

Elemental Skill: Musecatcher

Cooldown: 10s

Aino throws a Musecatcher forward, dealing Hydro DMG and dragging her along behind it. When the dragging stops, Aino deals AoE Hydro DMG to nearby opponents.
Holding the Skill will cause it to behave differently.

Hold
Enter Aiming Mode to adjust the Musecatcher's throwing direction.

Elemental Burst: Precision Hydronic Cooler

Cooldown: 13.5s · Energy cost: 50

Aino deploys a custom water-spraying device, the Cool Your Jets Ducky to establish a Focused Hydronic Cooling Zone.
While active, the Cool Your Jets Ducky will periodically fire water balls at nearby opponents, dealing Hydro DMG.

Passive talent: Modular Efficiency Protocol

Aino will be granted the corresponding buff effects based on the party's Moonsign.

Moonsign: Ascendant Gleam: Her Elemental Burst Precision Hydronic Cooler is enhanced: The Cool Your Jets Ducky will fire water balls more frequently, and the water balls will deal AoE Hydro DMG over a larger area of effect.

Passive talent: Structured Power Booster

Aino's Elemental Burst DMG is increased by 50% of her Elemental Mastery.

Passive talent: Moonsign Benediction: Force Limit Analysis

When Aino is in the party, the party's Moonsign will increase by 1 level.

Passive talent: Miniaturized Detection Sensor

Displays the location of nearby resources unique to Nod-Krai on the mini-map.
Additionally, Aino appears to be able to alter the Cool Your Jets Ducky's appearance in some way...

Aino constellations

C1: The Theory of Ash—Field Equilibrium

After Aino uses her Elemental Skill Musecatcher or her Elemental Burst Precision Hydronic Cooler, her Elemental Mastery will be increased by 80. The Elemental Mastery of other nearby active party members will be increased by 80 for 15s.

The Elemental Mastery-increasing effects of this Constellation do not stack.

C2: The Principle of Transference in Gear Differentials

If Aino is off-field while the Focused Hydronic Cooling Zone of her Elemental Burst Precision Hydronic Cooler is active, when your active party member hits a nearby opponent with an attack, the Cool Your Jets Ducky will fire an additional water ball at that opponent, dealing AoE Hydro DMG equal to 25% of Aino's ATK and 100% of her Elemental Mastery. This DMG is considered Elemental Burst DMG. This effect can be triggered once every 5s.

C3: Cake and the Art of Mechanism Repair

Increases the Level of Precision Hydronic Cooler by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C4: Butter and Cats and the Law of Energy Supply

When the Elemental Skill Musecatcher hits an opponent, it will restore 10 Elemental Energy for Aino. Energy can be restored to her in this manner once every 10s.

C5: Perpetual Turbine of Metal and Light

Increases the Level of Musecatcher by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C6: The Burden of Creative Genius

For the next 15s after using the Elemental Burst Precision Hydronic Cooler, DMG from nearby active characters' Electro-Charged, Bloom, Lunar-Charged, Lunar-Bloom, and Lunar-Crystallize reactions is increased by 15%.
Moonsign: Ascendant Gleam: DMG from the aforementioned reactions will be further increased by 20%.

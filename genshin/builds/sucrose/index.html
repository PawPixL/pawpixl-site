Sucrose Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

Sucrose build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (A Thousand Floating Dreams, Sunny Morning Sleep-In), artifacts (Viridescent Venerer), main stats.

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Role: EM Build

Anemo support who groups enemies, shares EM, and boosts reaction damage.

Best weapons for Sucrose

  1. A Thousand Floating Dreams: Provides EM to boost Sucrose's Swirl DMG and reaction support, while its passive grants additional EM or Elemental DMG bonuses based on teammates' elements.
  2. Sunny Morning Sleep-In: Provides Elemental Mastery and boosts Swirl damage, making it an excellent weapon for maximizing Sucrose's reaction damage and team support.
  3. Sacrificial Fragments: Provides Elemental Mastery and a chance to reset her Skill cooldown when it damages enemies, enabling extra Swirls.
  4. Thrilling Tales of Dragon Slayers: Provides a powerful 48% ATK boost to the next character switched in, making Sucrose an excellent F2P buffer for ATK-scaling DPS teams.
  5. Hakushin Ring: Provides Energy Recharge and grants an Elemental DMG Bonus to elements involved when Sucrose triggers an Electro reaction.
  6. Magic Guide: Provides Elemental Mastery and increases damage against enemies affected by Hydro or Electro. A budget Swirl option.
  7. Favonius Codex: Energy Recharge and team particles on CRIT; build enough CRIT Rate for reliable triggers.

Best artifacts for Sucrose

  • Viridescent Venerer (4 pcs) · Best Support: Sucrose's best artifact set, increasing Swirl damage by 60% and reducing enemies' RES to the element Swirled by 40%. Essential for reaction-focused teams. Main stats: EM · EM · EM.
  • Instructor (4 pcs) · Support: Provides Elemental Mastery and boosts the entire team's EM after triggering an elemental reaction. A solid F2P option for reaction-focused teams. Main stats: EM · EM · EM.
  • Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs) · Support: Boosts the team's ATK by 20% after Sucrose uses her Elemental Burst. A situational support option for ATK-scaling DPS teams. Main stats: EM · EM · EM.

Main stats

  • Goblet: Elemental Mastery
  • Circlet: Elemental Mastery

Stat targets

  • Elemental Mastery: 800+
  • Energy Recharge: 130% ~ 160%

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 9+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 9+

Best teams for Sucrose

  • Lunar-Charged Team #1: Sucrose, Xingqiu, Fischl, Ineffa. Sucrose drives Swirls while Xingqiu applies Hydro and Fischl supplies Electro. Ineffa adds Electro support and helps enable Lunar-Charged damage.
  • Vaporization Team: Sucrose, Klee, Furina, Bennett. Sucrose groups enemies and shreds Pyro RES while Klee delivers explosive damage. Furina provides Hydro application and a powerful damage buff, while Bennett boosts ATK and provides healing.
  • Pyro Case Files: Sucrose, Arlecchino, Nicole, Mona. Sucrose provides grouping, EM sharing, and RES Shred while Nicole buffs the team and Mona boosts burst damage. Arlecchino leads with powerful Pyro damage, with Mona adding Hydro application for Vaporize opportunities.
  • Aggravate Academy: Sucrose, Keqing, Nahida, Fischl. Sucrose provides Electro RES Shred and EM sharing while Keqing and Fischl trigger powerful Aggravate damage. Nahida supplies consistent Dendro application to keep reactions flowing.

Pros

  • Excellent EM sharing.
  • Strong enemy grouping.
  • F2P-friendly support.

Cons

  • Limited against heavy enemies.
  • Energy-hungry Burst.
  • Low personal damage.

Build variations

Reaction EM Support (Support / Driver)

Share EM and reduce enemy resistance with Viridescent Venerer.

  • Weapons: Sacrificial Fragments, Thrilling Tales of Dragon Slayers, Magic Guide
  • Artifacts: Viridescent Venerer (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Stack EM; add ER as needed.
  • Teams: Swirlable elements and strong off-field damage.
  • When it applies: Swirl does not CRIT in its usual form.

Budget ATK Buffer (Support)

Use a three-star weapon and Instructor for accessible buffs.

  • Weapons: Thrilling Tales of Dragon Slayers, Magic Guide
  • Artifacts: Instructor (4 pcs); Viridescent Venerer (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ER and EM as appropriate.
  • Teams: ATK-scaling carry; swap directly after TTDS.
  • When it applies: TTDS swap order is essential.

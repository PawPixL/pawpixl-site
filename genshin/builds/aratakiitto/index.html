Arataki Itto Build Guide: Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

Arataki Itto build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Redhorn Stonethresher, Serpent Spine), artifacts (Husk of Opulent Dreams), main stats.

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Role: Main DPS

Arataki Itto is an on-field Geo DPS who converts DEF into ATK during his Burst. Build Superlative Superstrength with Normal Attacks and Ushi, then spend stacks on powerful Arataki Kesagiri Charged Attacks. Keep his Burst ready, and finish setting up teammates before he takes the field.

Best weapons for Arataki Itto

  1. Redhorn Stonethresher: Best in slot: huge CD, extra DEF.
  2. Serpent Spine: CRIT Rate and stacking DMG Bonus make this an exceptional alternative; at R5 it can approach R1 Redhorn. Protect stacks with a shield. Keep passive stacks active. R5 is especially strong.
  3. Skyward Pride: ER helps maintain Burst uptime.
  4. Whiteblind: Best craftable choice: DEF% and stacking DEF/ATK increase his Burst damage. Build Normal Attack stacks before Bursting when practical. Accessible alternative. R5 recommended.
  5. Blackcliff Slasher: CRIT DMG is useful, but its ATK bonus requires defeating enemies and does little in boss fights. Best against multiple waves.
  6. "Ultimate Overlord's Mega Magic Sword": Energy Recharge plus ATK buffs make a comfortable event option; ATK is less valuable than DEF or CRIT for Itto. If already obtained.
  7. Tidal Shadow: Craftable ATK% claymore whose ATK buff activates after healing. A fallback if Whiteblind is unavailable. Requires healing.

Best artifacts for Arataki Itto

  • Husk of Opulent Dreams (4 pcs) · Best: His strongest general set: stacks increase DEF and Geo DMG while he deals Geo damage or waits off-field. Main stats: DEF% · Geo DMG · CRIT.
  • Retracing Bolide (4 pcs) · Shielded Option: A viable alternative with 40% Normal and Charged Attack DMG while protected by a shield. Main stats: DEF% · Geo DMG · CRIT.
  • Husk of Opulent Dreams + Archaic Petra (2 + 2 pcs) · Temporary Mix: Combine DEF% from Husk with Geo DMG from Archaic Petra while farming a complete Husk set. Main stats: DEF% · Geo DMG · CRIT.

Main stats

  • Sands: DEF%
  • Goblet: Geo DMG Bonus
  • Circlet: CRIT Rate / CRIT DMG

Stat targets

  • CRIT Rate: 65% - 80%
  • CRIT DMG: 140% - 200%+
  • DEF: 1,800 - 2,400+
  • ER: 115% - 140% (Geo teams)
  • ER: Up to 150%+ (Less battery)

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 8-9+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 8
  • Elemental Burst: Level 8-9+

Best teams for Arataki Itto

  • Oni Royalty: Arataki Itto (Main DPS), Gorou (DEF / Geo Buff), Chiori (Off-field Geo), Zhongli (Shield / RES Shred). Gorou supplies DEF and Geo buffs, Chiori deals off-field damage and makes particles, and Zhongli shields and shreds RES for safe Kesagiri combos.
  • Oni Goes to Fontaine: Arataki Itto (Main DPS), Xilonen (Geo Shred / Heal), Furina (DMG Buff / Hydro), Bennett (ATK Buff / Heal). Xilonen heals and shreds Geo RES in this double-Geo setup. Furina boosts team damage, while Bennett adds healing and an ATK buff.
  • Full House Geo: Arataki Itto (Main DPS), Gorou (DEF / Geo Buff), Albedo (Off-field Geo), Zhongli (Shield / RES Shred). A classic four-Geo shell: Gorou buffs Itto, Albedo adds off-field damage and Geo particles, and Zhongli enables safe, uninterrupted slashes.
  • OniFans: Arataki Itto (Main DPS), Gorou (DEF / Geo Buff), Kachina (Geo Support), Diona (Shield / Heal). Gorou and Kachina give Itto a three-Geo core and more particles. Diona shields and heals, letting him finish his Burst combos safely.

Pros

  • Stamina-free slashes
  • Great with Gorou

Cons

Build variations

Double Geo with Xilonen (On-field Geo DPS)

Use to access Furina’s team damage bonus and Xilonen’s healing and Geo RES shred while keeping Itto as your main carry.

  • Weapons: Redhorn Stonethresher, Whiteblind, Serpent Spine
  • Artifacts: Husk of Opulent Dreams (4 pcs)
  • Stats: DEF% remains standard; a DEF% Goblet can compete only with unusually high external DMG buffs. Check ER for the rotation.
  • Teams: Xilonen plus two Pyro/Hydro/Electro/Cryo teammates, e.g. Furina and Bennett.
  • When it applies: Xilonen requires two eligible non-Geo elements for her standard support mode.
  • Notes: Set up healing and buffs before Itto’s Burst.

F2P / Craftable (On-field Geo DPS)

A practical low-spend setup that gets strong value from craftable Whiteblind and existing Geo supports.

  • Weapons: Whiteblind, Tidal Shadow
  • Artifacts: Husk of Opulent Dreams (4 pcs); Husk of Opulent Dreams + Archaic Petra (2 + 2 pcs)
  • Stats: Prioritize CRIT and ER on artifacts to compensate for Whiteblind’s DEF% stat.
  • Teams: Gorou and another Geo teammate help charge Burst; bring shields or healing.
  • When it applies: Whiteblind stacks its passive through Normal/Charged Attack hits.
  • Notes: R5 Whiteblind is a strong investment if you already own billets.

Shielded Kesagiri (On-field Geo DPS)

Useful when shield comfort matters or you already own strong Retracing Bolide pieces. Husk is preferred for most builds.

  • Weapons: Serpent Spine, Redhorn Stonethresher, Whiteblind
  • Artifacts: Retracing Bolide (4 pcs); Husk of Opulent Dreams (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Maintain normal DEF / Geo DMG / CRIT main stats; sustain enough ER for each rotation.
  • Teams: A reliable shielder such as Zhongli or Diona.
  • When it applies: Bolide’s damage bonus only works while shielded. Serpent Spine wants uninterrupted stacks.
  • Notes: Shield before Itto enters his Burst window.

Arataki Itto talents

Normal Attack: Fight Club Legend

Normal Attack
Performs up to 4 consecutive strikes.
When the 2nd and 4th strikes hit opponents, Itto will gain 1 and 2 stacks of Superlative Superstrength, respectively.
Max 5 stacks. Triggering this effect will refresh the current duration of any existing stacks.

Additionally, Itto's Normal Attack combo does not immediately reset after sprinting or using his Elemental Skill, "Masatsu Zetsugi: Akaushi Burst!"

Charged Attack
When holding to perform a Charged Attack, Itto unleashes a series of Arataki Kesagiri slashes without consuming Stamina. Instead, each Arataki Kesagiri slash consumes 1 stack of Superlative Superstrength. When the final stack is consumed, Itto delivers a powerful final slash.
If no stacks of Superlative Superstrength are available, Itto will perform a single Saichimonji Slash.

Plunging Attack
Plunges from mid-air to strike the ground, damaging opponents along the path and dealing AoE DMG upon impact.

Elemental Skill: Masatsu Zetsugi: Akaushi Burst!

Cooldown: 10s

Hurls Ushi, the young akaushi bull and auxiliary member of the Arataki Gang, dealing Geo DMG to opponents on hit. When Ushi hits opponents, Arataki Itto gains 1 stack of Superlative Superstrength.

Ushi will remain on the field and provide support in the following ways:
· Taunts surrounding opponents and draws their attacks.
· Inherits HP based on a percentage of Arataki Itto's Max HP.
· When Ushi takes DMG, Arataki Itto gains 1 stack of Superlative Superstrength. Only 1 stack can be gained in this way every 2s.
· Ushi will flee when its HP reaches 0 or its duration ends. It will grant Arataki Itto 1 stack of Superlative Superstrength when it leaves.

Hold
Adjust throwing angle.

Ushi is considered a Geo construct. Arataki Itto can only deploy 1 Ushi on the field at any one time.

Elemental Burst: Royal Descent: Behold, Itto the Evil!

Cooldown: 18s · Energy cost: 70

Time to show 'em the might of the Arataki Gang! For a time, Itto lets out his inner Raging Oni King, wielding his Oni King's Kanabou in battle.
This state has the following special properties:
· Converts Itto's Normal, Charged, and Plunging Attacks to Geo DMG. This cannot be overridden.
· Increases Itto's Normal Attack SPD. Also increases his ATK based on his DEF.
· On hit, the 1st and 3rd strikes of his attack combo will each grant Arataki Itto 1 stack of Superlative Superstrength.
· Decreases Itto's Elemental and Physical RES by 20%.

The Raging Oni King state will be cleared when Itto leaves the field.

Passive talent: Arataki Ichiban

When Arataki Itto uses consecutive Arataki Kesagiri, he obtains the following effects:
· Each slash increases the ATK SPD of the next slash by 10%. Max ATK SPD increase is 30%.
· Increases his resistance to interruption.

These effects will be cleared once he stops performing consecutive slashes.

Passive talent: Bloodline of the Crimson Oni

Arataki Kesagiri DMG is increased by 35% of Arataki Itto's DEF.

Passive talent: Woodchuck Chucked

When a party member uses attacks to obtain wood from a tree, they have a 25% chance to get an additional log of wood.

Arataki Itto constellations

C1: Stay a While and Listen Up

After using Royal Descent: Behold, Itto the Evil!, Arataki Itto gains 2 stacks of Superlative Superstrength. After 1s, Itto will gain 1 stack of Superlative Superstrength every 0.5s for 1.5s.

C2: Gather 'Round, It's a Brawl!

After using Royal Descent: Behold, Itto the Evil!, each party member whose Element is Geo will decrease that skill's CD by 1.5s and restore 6 Energy to Arataki Itto.
CD can be decreased by up to 4.5s in this manner. Max 18 Energy can be restored in this manner.

C3: Horns Lowered, Coming Through

Increases the Level of Masatsu Zetsugi: Akaushi Burst! by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C4: Jailhouse Bread and Butter

When the Raging Oni King state caused by Royal Descent: Behold, Itto the Evil! ends, all nearby party members gain 20% DEF and 20% ATK for 10s.

C5: 10 Years of Hanamizaka Fame

Increases the Level of Royal Descent: Behold, Itto the Evil! by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C6: Arataki Itto, Present!

Arataki Itto's Charged Attacks deal +70% CRIT DMG. Additionally, when he uses Arataki Kesagiri, he has a 50% chance to not consume stacks of Superlative Superstrength.

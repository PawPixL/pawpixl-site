Role: Main DPS

Arataki Itto is an on-field Geo DPS who converts DEF into ATK during his Burst. Build Superlative Superstrength with Normal Attacks and Ushi, then spend stacks on powerful Arataki Kesagiri Charged Attacks. Keep his Burst ready, and finish setting up teammates before he takes the field.

Best weapons for Arataki Itto

  1. Redhorn Stonethresher
  2. Serpent Spine
  3. Skyward Pride
  4. Whiteblind
  5. Blackcliff Slasher
  6. "Ultimate Overlord's Mega Magic Sword"
  7. Tidal Shadow

Best artifacts

  • Husk of Opulent Dreams (4 pcs) · Best
  • Retracing Bolide (4 pcs) · Shielded Option
  • Husk of Opulent Dreams + Archaic Petra (2 + 2 pcs) · Temporary Mix

Main stats

  • Sands: DEF%
  • Goblet: Geo DMG Bonus
  • Circlet: CRIT Rate / CRIT DMG

Stat targets

  • CRIT Rate: 65% - 80%
  • CRIT DMG: 140% - 200%+
  • DEF: 1,800 - 2,400+
  • ER: 115% - 140% (Geo teams)
  • ER: Up to 150%+ (Less battery)

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 8-9+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 8
  • Elemental Burst: Level 8-9+

Best teams

  • Oni Royalty: Arataki Itto, Gorou, Chiori, Zhongli
  • Oni Goes to Fontaine: Arataki Itto, Xilonen, Furina, Bennett
  • Full House Geo: Arataki Itto, Gorou, Albedo, Zhongli
  • Budget Bonk Brigade: Arataki Itto, Gorou, Kachina, Diona

Pros

  • Strong Geo AoE
  • DEF scaling damage
  • Stamina-free slashes
  • Great with Gorou

Cons

  • Burst dependent
  • Needs field time
  • Energy hungry
  • Geo-focused teams

Build variations

  • Double Geo with Xilonen (On-field Geo DPS): Use to access Furina’s team damage bonus and Xilonen’s healing and Geo RES shred while keeping Itto as your main carry.
  • F2P / Craftable (On-field Geo DPS): A practical low-spend setup that gets strong value from craftable Whiteblind and existing Geo supports.
  • Shielded Kesagiri (On-field Geo DPS): Useful when shield comfort matters or you already own strong Retracing Bolide pieces. Husk is preferred for most builds.

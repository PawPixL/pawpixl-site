Kaedehara Kazuha Build Guide: Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

Kaedehara Kazuha build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Freedom-Sworn, Xiphos' Moonlight), artifacts (Viridescent Venerer), main stats.

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Role: Support / Buffer

Kazuha groups enemies, amplifies Pyro, Hydro, Electro, or Cryo DMG through his EM-scaling passive, and shreds their RES with Viridescent Venerer. Prioritize enough ER for teams needing his Burst, then stack EM. He cannot directly buff Dendro or Geo DMG with his passive.

Best weapons for Kaedehara Kazuha

  1. Freedom-Sworn: EM boosts Swirl and Kazuha’s Elemental DMG buff.
  2. Xiphos' Moonlight: EM converts to ER for Kazuha and shares some with allies.
  3. Favonius Sword: Generates neutral particles on CRIT hits.
  4. Sacrificial Sword: ER substat and Skill reset can provide extra grouping and Anemo particles.
  5. Skyward Blade: High base ATK and ER help Burst uptime, though its offensive passive contributes little to an EM support build. Use when lacking better EM or team-battery options.
  6. Iron Sting: Craftable EM stat boosts Swirl damage and Kazuha’s elemental buff; easy to use without wishing. May require an ER Sands if Burst is essential. R1 is sufficient for support.
  7. Toukabou Shigure: Provides substantial EM for Swirl and elemental buffs; its marked-target passive mostly benefits personal damage. Event-limited; manage ER through artifacts.
  8. Fleuve Cendre Ferryman: Free fishing sword supplies ER; its Skill CRIT bonus is not a substitute for EM or Favonius team particles. Use when ER is the priority and other swords are unavailable.

Best teams for Kaedehara Kazuha

  • Hydro Hurricane: Kaedehara Kazuha (Anemo Buff), Neuvillette (Main DPS), Furina (Hydro Buff), Xilonen (Healer / Shred). Kazuha groups foes and buffs Hydro with Swirl/VV; Furina boosts damage and Xilonen adds healing, buffs, and RES Shred.
  • Emberleaf Waltz: Kaedehara Kazuha (Buffer / Debuffer), Arlecchino (On-field DPS), Xingqiu (Off-field DPS / Hydro Enabler), Bennett (Healer / Buffer). Kazuha shreds and buffs Pyro for Arlecchino; Xingqiu enables Vaporize and Bennett boosts ATK. Avoid unwanted Hydro absorption.
  • Thunder in the Leaves: Kaedehara Kazuha (Buffer / Debuffer), Keqing (On-field DPS), Fischl (Off-field DPS / Battery), Yaoyao (Healer / Dendro Enabler). Yaoyao sustains Quicken; Kazuha buffs and shreds Electro, while Keqing and Fischl trigger Aggravate.
  • Budget Leaf Blower: Kaedehara Kazuha (Buffer / Debuffer), Xiangling (Off-field DPS / Pyro Enabler), Xingqiu (Off-field DPS / Hydro Enabler), Bennett (Healer / Buffer). Accessible National core: Xingqiu enables Vaporize, Bennett powers Xiangling, and Kazuha groups and shreds Pyro/Hydro RES.

Build variations

Favonius Team Battery (Support)

Choose this over pure EM when smoother team rotations are worth more than a slightly larger elemental buff.

  • Weapons: Favonius Sword, Xiphos' Moonlight
  • Artifacts: Viridescent Venerer (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Get enough ER to Burst as required; add ~20%+ CRIT Rate to trigger Favonius reliably.
  • Teams: Energy-hungry allies; a target for Kazuha to hit while on-field.
  • When it applies: Favonius must CRIT while Kazuha is on-field.
  • Notes: Exact ER depends on rotation, skills, particles and team batteries.

Burst-Optional Quick Swap (Elemental Buffer)

Use shorter Skill-only rotations where Burst would lengthen setup or disrupt Melt/Vaporize.

  • Weapons: Freedom-Sworn, Iron Sting, Toukabou Shigure
  • Artifacts: Viridescent Venerer (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Favor EM; reduce ER if Burst is intentionally skipped.
  • Teams: An element Kazuha can Swirl; team does not rely on Burst application.
  • When it applies: Only when the team does not require his Burst.
  • Notes: The VV shred requires Kazuha to trigger a Swirl on-field.

F2P Elemental Support (Support)

An accessible setup without premium swords; 4pc VV remains the long-term priority.

  • Weapons: Iron Sting, Fleuve Cendre Ferryman, Toukabou Shigure
  • Artifacts: Viridescent Venerer (4 pcs); Instructor (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Aim for EM/EM/EM; switch to ER Sands when Burst uptime cannot be met.
  • Teams: Pyro/Hydro/Electro/Cryo allies who appreciate VV shred.
  • When it applies: Toukabou Shigure was event-limited.
  • Notes: Instructor is a temporary or niche 4-star alternative, not an equal substitute for VV.

Electro Reaction Driver (On-field Reaction Driver)

Use a specialized on-field cooldown-reduction playstyle for fun or niche multi-target teams.

  • Weapons: Xiphos' Moonlight, Iron Sting, Favonius Sword
  • Artifacts: Thundering Fury (4 pcs); Viridescent Venerer (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Build EM and sufficient ER; prioritize reactions over personal CRIT.
  • Teams: Frequent Electro-related reactions with an Electro aura and off-field applicators.
  • When it applies: Thundering Fury requires Kazuha to trigger its qualifying reactions.
  • Notes: More field time than normal; usually less universally useful than 4pc VV support.

C6 Anemo Carry (On-field DPS)

Optional investment build that exploits C6 Anemo infusion and EM-based attack bonuses.

  • Weapons: Freedom-Sworn, Primordial Jade Cutter, Mistsplitter Reforged
  • Artifacts: Viridescent Venerer (4 pcs); Thundering Fury (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Hybrid EM/ATK/Anemo/CRIT is team-dependent; compare to EM-centric normal builds.
  • Teams: C6 Kazuha, strong personal-damage buffs and planned on-field time.
  • When it applies: Requires C6; not a general recommendation.
  • Notes: Weapon and artifact optimization differs substantially from EM-support Kazuha.

Kaedehara Kazuha talents

Normal Attack: Garyuu Bladework

Normal Attack
Performs up to 5 rapid strikes.

Charged Attack
Consumes a certain amount of Stamina to unleash 2 rapid sword strikes.

Plunging Attack
Plunges from mid-air to strike the ground below, damaging opponents along the path and dealing AoE DMG upon impact. If this Plunging Attack is triggered by Chihayaburu, it will be converted to Plunging Attack: Midare Ranzan.

Plunging Attack: Midare Ranzan
When a Plunging Attack is performed using the effects of the Elemental Skill Chihayaburu, Plunging Attack DMG is converted to Anemo DMG and will create a small wind tunnel via a secret blade technique that pulls in nearby objects and opponents.

Elemental Skill: Chihayaburu

Cooldown: Press: 6s / Hold: 9s

Unleashes a secret technique as fierce as the rushing wind that pulls objects and opponents towards Kazuha's current position before launching opponents within the AoE, dealing Anemo DMG and lifting Kazuha into the air on a rushing gust of wind.
Within 10s of remaining airborne after casting Chihayaburu, Kazuha can unleash a powerful Plunging Attack known as Midare Ranzan.

Press
Can be used in mid-air.

Hold
Charges up before unleashing greater Anemo DMG over a larger AoE than Press Mode.

Plunging Attack: Midare Ranzan
When a Plunging Attack is performed using the effects of the Elemental Skill Chihayaburu, Plunging Attack DMG is converted to Anemo DMG. On landing, Kazuha creates a small wind tunnel via a secret blade technique that pulls in nearby objects and opponents.
Midare Ranzan's DMG is considered Plunging Attack DMG.

Elemental Burst: Kazuha Slash

Cooldown: 15s · Energy cost: 60

The signature technique of Kazuha's self-styled bladework — a single slash that strikes with the force of the first winds of autumn, dealing AoE Anemo DMG.
The blade's passage will leave behind a field named "Autumn Whirlwind" that periodically deals AoE Anemo DMG to opponents within its range.

Elemental Absorption
If Autumn Whirlwind comes into contact with Hydro/Pyro/Cryo/Electro, it will deal additional elemental DMG of that type.
Elemental Absorption may only occur once per use.

Passive talent: Soumon Swordsmanship

If Chihayaburu comes into contact with Hydro/Pyro/Cryo/Electro when cast, this Chihayaburu will absorb that element and if Plunging Attack: Midare Ranzan is used before the effect expires, it will deal an additional 200% ATK of the absorbed elemental type as DMG. This will be considered Plunging Attack DMG.
Elemental Absorption may only occur once per use of Chihayaburu.

Passive talent: Poetics of Fuubutsu

Upon triggering a Swirl or Stellar Swirl reaction, Kaedehara Kazuha will grant all party members a 0.04% Elemental DMG Bonus to the element absorbed by Swirl for every point of Elemental Mastery he has for 8s. Bonuses for different elements obtained through this method can co-exist.

Passive talent: Cloud Strider

Decreases sprinting Stamina consumption for your own party members by 20%.
Not stackable with Passive Talents that provide the exact same effects.

Kaedehara Kazuha constellations

C1: Scarlet Hills

Decreases Chihayaburu's CD by 10%.
Using Kazuha Slash resets the CD of Chihayaburu.

C2: Yamaarashi Tailwind

The Autumn Whirlwind field created by Kazuha Slash has the following effects:
· Increases Kaedehara Kazuha's own Elemental Mastery by 200 for its duration.
· Increases the Elemental Mastery of characters within the field by 200.

The Elemental Mastery-increasing effects of this Constellation do not stack.

C3: Maple Monogatari

Increases the Level of Chihayaburu by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C4: Oozora Genpou

When Kaedehara Kazuha's Energy is lower than 45, he obtains the following effects:
·Pressing or Holding Chihayaburu regenerates 3 or 4 Energy for Kaedehara Kazuha, respectively.
·When gliding, Kaedehara Kazuha regenerates 2 Energy per second.

C5: Wisdom of Bansei

Increases the Level of Kazuha Slash by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C6: Crimson Momiji

After using Chihayaburu or Kazuha Slash, Kaedehara Kazuha gains an Anemo Infusion for 5s. Additionally, each point of Elemental Mastery will increase the DMG dealt by Kaedehara Kazuha's Normal, Charged, and Plunging Attacks by 0.2%.

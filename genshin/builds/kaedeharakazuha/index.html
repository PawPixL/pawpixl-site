Role: Support / Buffer

Kazuha groups enemies, amplifies Pyro, Hydro, Electro, or Cryo DMG through his EM-scaling passive, and shreds their RES with Viridescent Venerer. Prioritize enough ER for teams needing his Burst, then stack EM. He cannot directly buff Dendro or Geo DMG with his passive.

Pros

Cons

  • Energy needs vary
  • Limited Dendro/Geo buffing
  • Grouping fails on bosses

Build variations

  • Favonius Team Battery (Support): Choose this over pure EM when smoother team rotations are worth more than a slightly larger elemental buff.
  • Burst-Optional Quick Swap (Elemental Buffer): Use shorter Skill-only rotations where Burst would lengthen setup or disrupt Melt/Vaporize.
  • F2P Elemental Support (Support): An accessible setup without premium swords; 4pc VV remains the long-term priority.
  • Electro Reaction Driver (On-field Reaction Driver): Use a specialized on-field cooldown-reduction playstyle for fun or niche multi-target teams.
  • C6 Anemo Carry (On-field DPS): Optional investment build that exploits C6 Anemo infusion and EM-based attack bonuses.

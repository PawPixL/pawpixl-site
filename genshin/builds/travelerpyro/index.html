Traveler (Pyro) Build Guide: Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

Traveler (Pyro) build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Exaiphanes Blade, Freedom-Sworn), artifacts (Scroll of the Hero of Cinder City), main stats.

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Role: Support / Sub DPS

Pyro Traveler supplies off-field Pyro through their Nightsoul Skill and boosts teammates with C1 and Scroll of the Hero of Cinder City. Tap Skill follows melee allies; Hold Skill targets enemies for ranged teams. Their Pyro application is slow.

Best weapons for Traveler (Pyro)

  1. Exaiphanes Blade: Best all-round option: strong ER and team-neutral particles when Pyro Traveler crits on-field.
  2. Freedom-Sworn: Premium EM sword with an additional teamwide attack buff after triggering reactions.
  3. Favonius Sword: EM adds reaction DMG while the passive gives Pyro Traveler and allies ER.
  4. Xiphos' Moonlight: At R5, a Skill reset can support shorter rotations; otherwise less consistent than Favonius. Primarily for ~16-second rotations. R5 strongly preferred.
  5. Sacrificial Sword: Provides ER and improves Skill damage. A good legacy option when Favonius particles are unnecessary. Past limited event; unavailable to new players.
  6. Festering Desire: Fishing sword grants ER and temporary post-Skill ER, helping a budget support Burst consistently.
  7. Fleuve Cendre Ferryman: Craftable EM sword for Burgeon or Overloaded triggering after meeting Burst energy needs. Reaction-trigger setup; little help with ER.
  8. Iron Sting: Craftable ER option; its EM leaf buff is conditional on triggering Dendro-related reactions. Best with Burning or Burgeon; ally must collect the leaf.

Best artifacts for Traveler (Pyro)

  • Scroll of the Hero of Cinder City (4 pcs): Best general support set: Nightsoul-aligned Pyro reactions grant large Elemental DMG buffs to relevant team elements. Main stats: ER · Pyro DMG/ATK% · CRIT Rate (Fav).
  • Tenacity of the Millelith (4 pcs): Skill hits maintain the team ATK buff when Scroll is already assigned to another support. Main stats: ER · HP%/Pyro DMG · CRIT Rate (Fav).
  • Deepwood Memories (4 pcs): For Kinich/Emilie Burning teams when no teammate holds Deepwood; reduces enemy Dendro RES. Main stats: ER · Pyro DMG/ATK% · CRIT Rate (Fav).

Main stats

  • Sands: Energy Recharge
  • Goblet: Pyro DMG / ATK%
  • Circlet: CRIT Rate (Favonius) / CRIT DMG

Stat targets

  • Energy Recharge: 210% - 235% (Fav, typical support)
  • ER: 160% - 220% (energy-rich teams)
  • ER: 255% - 300% (low-energy, non-Fav)
  • CRIT Rate: 40% - 60% (Favonius only)
  • Elemental Mastery: 700+ (Burgeon alternative)

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: 8+
  • Elemental Burst: 8+

Best teams for Traveler (Pyro)

  • Wildfire Vanguard: Traveler (Pyro) (Pyro Enabler / Support), Kinich (On-field DPS), Emilie (Off-field DPS), Bennett (Healer / Buffer). Maintains Burning for Kinich and Emilie. Equip Traveler with Deepwood if nobody else uses it; Bennett buffs Kinich.
  • Overload Patrol: Traveler (Pyro) (Pyro Enabler / Support), Raiden Shogun (On-field DPS / Battery), Kujou Sara (ATK Buffer), Chevreuse (Healer / Buffer). Off-field Pyro sustains Overloaded for Chevreuse; Sara boosts Raiden. Scroll on Traveler provides team buffs.
  • Frostfire Relay: Traveler (Pyro) (Pyro Enabler / Support), Wriothesley (On-field DPS), Nahida (Dendro Enabler / Buffer), Bennett (Healer / Buffer). Nahida helps sustain Burning so Wriothesley can Melt; Traveler adds Pyro and Scroll buffs. Pyro alone is not enough.
  • Shark-Fin Spark: Traveler (Pyro) (Pyro Enabler / Support), Mualani (On-field DPS), Xilonen (Healer / Debuffer), Sucrose (EM Buffer / Debuffer). A precise single-target Vape setup for Mualani; Traveler’s slow Pyro can struggle in multi-target encounters. Scroll may be redundant.

Pros

  • Free off-field Pyro
  • Excellent Scroll user
  • C1 buffs active allies
  • Flexible tap/hold Skill

Cons

  • Slow Pyro application
  • Limited multi-target coverage
  • High Burst ER needs
  • Low personal damage

Build variations

Kinich Burning Support (Off-field Support)

Use to maintain Burning and shred Dendro RES while Kinich and Emilie deal damage.

  • Weapons: Favonius Sword, Fleuve Cendre Ferryman
  • Artifacts: Deepwood Memories (4 pcs); Scroll of the Hero of Cinder City (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Prioritize ER to Burst consistently; CRIT Rate if using Favonius.
  • Teams: Kinich plus Dendro/Burning; put Deepwood on Traveler only if nobody else carries it.
  • When it applies: Deepwood effect must be maintained on targets.

Burgeon Trigger (Reaction DPS)

Tap Skill can trigger Burgeon on nearby Dendro Cores. Use when Traveler owns the reaction damage.

  • Weapons: Xiphos' Moonlight, Iron Sting, Favonius Sword
  • Artifacts: Flower of Paradise Lost (4 pcs); Gilded Dreams (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Level 90; ER until reliable Burst, then EM/EM/EM when feasible.
  • Teams: Consistent Hydro and Dendro application, healing/sustain, and Bloom Core creation.
  • When it applies: Keep Skill in range of Cores; manage Burning interference.

Overloaded Reaction Support (Off-field Support)

Supplies off-field Pyro and optional Scroll buff to Pyro/Electro damage dealers.

  • Weapons: Favonius Sword, Xiphos' Moonlight
  • Artifacts: Scroll of the Hero of Cinder City (4 pcs); Gilded Dreams (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ER first; EM matters only when Traveler frequently owns Overloaded reactions.
  • Teams: Chevreuse teams restricted to Pyro/Electro; enough Electro application.
  • When it applies: Reaction ownership varies; do not assume all Overloads belong to Traveler.

C6 On-Field Pyro (On-field DPS)

C6 enables Pyro-converted Normal/Charged attacks during Nightsoul Blessing for an invested alternative build.

  • Weapons: Mistsplitter Reforged, Primordial Jade Cutter, Wolf-Fang
  • Artifacts: Obsidian Codex (4 pcs); Marechaussee Hunter (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ATK%/Pyro DMG/CRIT; balance CRIT around Obsidian Codex and Energy needs.
  • Teams: C6 Traveler; strong external buffs, with Furina specifically for Marechaussee.
  • When it applies: Requires C6; niche compared with dedicated on-field Pyro DPS.

Vaporize / Melt Burst (Burst DPS)

Use an invested Burst for a single amplified Pyro hit while retaining some off-field utility.

  • Weapons: Mistsplitter Reforged, Favonius Sword
  • Artifacts: Emblem of Severed Fate (4 pcs); Scroll of the Hero of Cinder City (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ER for each Burst; then CRIT, ATK and EM on reaction-enabled hits.
  • Teams: Reliable Hydro or Cryo aura before Traveler's initial Burst strike.
  • When it applies: Reaction aura and Burst availability required.

Alternative ATK Buffer (Off-field Support)

Tenacity gives consistent ATK buff through Skill ticks; Noblesse is an option for Burst-dependent setups.

  • Weapons: Favonius Sword, Fleuve Cendre Ferryman
  • Artifacts: Tenacity of the Millelith (4 pcs); Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ER first; Favonius CRIT Rate if equipped.
  • Teams: Another teammate already uses Scroll; main carry values ATK buffs.
  • When it applies: Do not stack redundant artifact buffs.

Traveler (Pyro) talents

Normal Attack: Foreign Blaze

Normal Attack
Performs up to 5 rapid strikes.

Charged Attack
Consumes a certain amount of Stamina to unleash 2 rapid sword strikes.

Plunging Attack
Plunges from mid-air to strike the ground below, damaging opponents along the path and dealing AoE DMG upon impact.

Elemental Skill: Flowfire Blade

Cooldown: 18s

Wields a searing flame that can incinerate the very earth. Has different effects when Tapped or Held.

Tap
Call forth flames in a raging blaze. When used, the Traveler gains 42 Nightsoul points and enters the Nightsoul's Blessing state, summoning a Blazing Threshold that follows the current active character.
When the Blazing Threshold gets close to opponents, it will deal Nightsoul-aligned Pyro DMG to nearby opponents at intervals.

Hold
Unleash fire in a composed manner, dealing Nightsoul-aligned AoE Pyro DMG to nearby opponents. When used, the Traveler gains 42 Nightsoul points and enters the Nightsoul's Blessing state, summoning a Scorching Threshold that follows the current active character.
When the current active character within a Scorching Threshold deals DMG to opponents, the Threshold will launch a Coordinated Attack that deals Nightsoul-aligned Pyro DMG based on the Traveler's ATK. This effect can occur once every 3s.

Nightsoul's Blessing: Traveler
Continuously consumes Nightsoul points. The Traveler's Nightsoul's Blessing state lasts 12s at maximum, and when their Nightsoul points are exhausted, this state will end. If this Elemental Skill is used again while active, the Traveler's Nightsoul points will be reset to 42 points.
Blazing and Scorching Thresholds will disappear once the Traveler's Nightsoul's Blessing state ends, and only 1 Blazing or Scorching Threshold summoned by the Traveler themselves can exist at any one time.

Elemental Burst: Plains Scorcher

Cooldown: 18s · Energy cost: 70

Condense the flames into a mark, dealing Nightsoul-aligned AoE Pyro DMG to opponents up ahead. The Traveler restores 7 Nightsoul points per second for the next 4s.

Passive talent: True Flame of Incineration

When the Traveler has over 20 Nightsoul points, the attack AoE of the Blazing and Scorching Thresholds from the Elemental Skill Flowfire Blade after they hit opponents is increased.

Passive talent: Embers Unspent

After the current active character inside a Blazing Threshold or Scorching Threshold triggers a Pyro-related reaction, the Traveler will regain 5 Energy. This can occur once every 12s. Additionally, when a nearby party member triggers a Nightsoul Burst, the Traveler will regain 4 Energy.

Traveler (Pyro) constellations

C1: Starfire's Flowing Light

While Blazing Threshold or Scorching Threshold are active, the current active character deals 6% increased DMG. If said character is in the Nightsoul's Blessing state, they will deal an additional 9% DMG.

C2: Ever-Lit Candle

Within 12s of using the Elemental Skill Flowfire Blade, after nearby party members trigger a Pyro-related reaction on an opponent, the Traveler will regain 14 Nightsoul points. Each use of Flowfire Blade will restore at most 28 Nightsoul points in this way.

C3: Relayed Beacon

Increases the Level of Flowfire Blade by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C4: Ravaging Flame

After using the Elemental Burst Plains Scorcher, the Traveler gains 20% Pyro DMG Bonus for 9s.

C5: The Fire Inextinguishable

Increases the Level of Plains Scorcher by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C6: The Sacred Flame Imperishable

While in the Nightsoul's Blessing state, the Traveler's Normal, Charged, and Plunging Attacks will be converted to Nightsoul-aligned Pyro DMG that cannot be overridden, and the CRIT DMG of these attacks is increased by 40%.

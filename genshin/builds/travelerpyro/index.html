Traveler (Pyro) Build Guide | Weapons, Artifacts & Teams

Traveler (Pyro) build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Exaiphanes Blade, Freedom-Sworn), artifacts (Scroll of the Hero of Cinder City), main stats…

Role: Support / Sub DPS

Pyro Traveler supplies off-field Pyro through their Nightsoul Skill and boosts teammates with C1 and Scroll of the Hero of Cinder City. Tap Skill follows melee allies; Hold Skill targets enemies for ranged teams. Their Pyro application is slow.

Best weapons for Traveler (Pyro)

  1. Exaiphanes Blade
  2. Freedom-Sworn
  3. Favonius Sword
  4. Xiphos' Moonlight
  5. Sacrificial Sword
  6. Festering Desire
  7. Fleuve Cendre Ferryman
  8. Iron Sting

Best artifacts

  • Scroll of the Hero of Cinder City (4 pcs)
  • Tenacity of the Millelith (4 pcs)
  • Deepwood Memories (4 pcs)

Main stats

  • Sands: Energy Recharge
  • Goblet: Pyro DMG / ATK%
  • Circlet: CRIT Rate (Favonius) / CRIT DMG

Stat targets

  • Energy Recharge: 210% - 235% (Fav, typical support)
  • ER: 160% - 220% (energy-rich teams)
  • ER: 255% - 300% (low-energy, non-Fav)
  • CRIT Rate: 40% - 60% (Favonius only)
  • Elemental Mastery: 700+ (Burgeon alternative)

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: 8+
  • Elemental Burst: 8+

Best teams

  • Wildfire Vanguard: Traveler (Pyro), Kinich, Emilie, Bennett
  • Overload Patrol: Traveler (Pyro), Raiden Shogun, Kujou Sara, Chevreuse
  • Frostfire Relay: Traveler (Pyro), Wriothesley, Nahida, Bennett
  • Shark-Fin Spark: Traveler (Pyro), Mualani, Xilonen, Sucrose

Pros

  • Free off-field Pyro
  • Excellent Scroll user
  • C1 buffs active allies
  • Flexible tap/hold Skill

Cons

  • Slow Pyro application
  • Limited multi-target coverage
  • High Burst ER needs
  • Low personal damage

Build variations

  • Kinich Burning Support (Off-field Support): Use to maintain Burning and shred Dendro RES while Kinich and Emilie deal damage.
  • Burgeon Trigger (Reaction DPS): Tap Skill can trigger Burgeon on nearby Dendro Cores. Use when Traveler owns the reaction damage.
  • Overloaded Reaction Support (Off-field Support): Supplies off-field Pyro and optional Scroll buff to Pyro/Electro damage dealers.
  • C6 On-Field Pyro (On-field DPS): C6 enables Pyro-converted Normal/Charged attacks during Nightsoul Blessing for an invested alternative build.
  • Vaporize / Melt Burst (Burst DPS): Use an invested Burst for a single amplified Pyro hit while retaining some off-field utility.
  • Alternative ATK Buffer (Off-field Support): Tenacity gives consistent ATK buff through Skill ticks; Noblesse is an option for Burst-dependent setups.

Diona Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts & Teams

Diona build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Favonius Warbow, Sacrificial Bow), artifacts (Noblesse Oblige), main stats (HP% / Energy Recharge /…

Role: Shield, Healer

Diona is a Cryo support who shields with her Hold Skill, heals with her Burst, and generates Cryo particles. At C6 she grants EM to healthier allies; with Witch's Revelation unlocked, she also buffs Stellar-Conduct and Stellar Swirl teams.

Best weapons for Diona

  1. Favonius Warbow
  2. Sacrificial Bow
  3. Elegy for the End
  4. Breezeborne Refrain
  5. Recurve Bow

Best artifacts

  • Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs) · General Support
  • Tenacity of the Millelith (4 pcs) · Stellar Support
  • Song of Days Past (4 pcs) · Healing Alternative

Main stats

  • Sands: HP% / Energy Recharge
  • Goblet: HP%
  • Circlet: HP% / CRIT Rate (Fav.) / Healing Bonus

Stat targets

  • ER: 130–150% (C1+, double Cryo/Fav.)
  • ER: 160–180% (C0, double Cryo/Fav.)
  • ER: Higher without Cryo battery
  • HP: 25,000–35,000+
  • CRIT Rate: Enough for Favonius

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1
  • Elemental Skill: Level 8–9+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 8–9+

Best teams

  • The Purrfect Storm: Diona, Sandrone, Yae Miko, Odette
  • Cat-5 Snowstorm: Diona, Vesna, Odette, Faruzan
  • Freeze Frame: Diona, Kamisato Ayaka, Mona, Kaedehara Kazuha
  • Melted Ice Cream: Diona, Ganyu, Bennett, Xiangling

Pros

  • Reliable Shields
  • Burst Healing
  • Cryo Battery
  • C6 Reaction Buffs

Cons

  • Low Personal DMG
  • Burst Field Limited
  • ER Needs Vary
  • C6 Buff Conditions

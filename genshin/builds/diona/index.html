Diona Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

Diona build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Favonius Warbow, Sacrificial Bow), artifacts (Noblesse Oblige), main stats, talent priority, teams.

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Role: Shield, Healer

Diona is a Cryo support who shields with her Hold Skill, heals with her Burst, and generates Cryo particles. At C6 she grants EM to healthier allies; with Witch's Revelation unlocked, she also buffs Stellar-Conduct and Stellar Swirl teams.

Best weapons for Diona

  1. Favonius Warbow: Generates neutral particles for the whole team. Build CR to trigger its passive on-field.
  2. Sacrificial Bow: Can reset her multi-hit Hold Skill for extra Cryo particles and improved shield coverage.
  3. Elegy for the End: Provides ER and team ATK/EM buffs when its sigils activate; buff timing can be inconsistent.
  4. Breezeborne Refrain: Provides ER and a team Stellar Glimmer DMG buff after using Skill.
  5. Recurve Bow: HP% improves her shield and healing, but provides no Energy support for her Burst. Need more ER from artifacts.

Best artifacts for Diona

  • Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs) · General Support: Buffs party ATK after Burst. Best for ATK-scaling carries when nobody else uses Noblesse. Main stats: HP%/ER · HP% · HP%/CR.
  • Tenacity of the Millelith (4 pcs) · Stellar Support: Witch's Revelation follow-up paws can maintain ATK buffs during Cryo Swirl or Superconduct/Stellar reactions. Main stats: HP%/ER · HP% · HP%/CR.
  • Song of Days Past (4 pcs) · Healing Alternative: Converts Burst healing into extra damage for the active character when Noblesse is already covered. Main stats: HP%/ER · HP% · HP%/Healing.

Main stats

  • Sands: HP% / Energy Recharge
  • Goblet: HP%
  • Circlet: HP% / CRIT Rate (Fav.) / Healing Bonus

Stat targets

  • ER: 130–150% (C1+, double Cryo/Fav.)
  • ER: 160–180% (C0, double Cryo/Fav.)
  • ER: Higher without Cryo battery
  • HP: 25,000–35,000+
  • CRIT Rate: Enough for Favonius

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1
  • Elemental Skill: Level 8–9+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 8–9+

Best teams for Diona

  • The Purrfect Storm: Diona (Shield / Buffer), Sandrone (Main DPS), Yae Miko (Electro DPS), Odette (Cryo Support). Sandrone drives Stellar-Conduct; Yae supplies Electro and Odette buffs the reaction. C6 Diona shields and buffs from her Burst field.
  • Cat-5 Snowstorm: Diona (Shield / Buffer), Vesna (Main DPS), Odette (Cryo Support), Faruzan (Anemo Support). Vesna drives Stellar Swirl, Odette enables and buffs it, and Faruzan boosts Anemo damage. C6 Diona supplies shields and reaction buffs.
  • Freeze Frame: Diona (Shield / Healer), Kamisato Ayaka (Main DPS), Mona (Hydro Support), Kaedehara Kazuha (Anemo Support). Ayaka deals Cryo damage, Mona applies Hydro and boosts Burst damage, and Kazuha groups and shreds Cryo RES. Diona batteries Ayaka.
  • Melted Ice Cream: Diona (Shield), Ganyu (Main DPS), Bennett (Healer / Buffer), Xiangling (Pyro DPS). Diona shields Ganyu's Charged Shots, Bennett buffs ATK, and Xiangling applies Pyro for Reverse Melt. Avoid Diona Burst if it steals Melts.

Build variations

Stellar-Conduct C6 (Shield / Reaction Buffer)

Witch’s Revelation follow-up Icy Paws help sustain Tenacity while her C6 Burst area boosts reaction damage.

  • Weapons: Breezeborne Refrain, Favonius Warbow, Elegy for the End
  • Artifacts: Tenacity of the Millelith (4 pcs); Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ER to Burst reliably, then HP% and Favonius CRIT.
  • Teams: Stellar-Conduct enabler (Sandrone, Odette or Cryo Traveler), Electro unit and C6 Diona.
  • When it applies: C6 + Witch’s Revelation unlocked; active ally in Burst field
  • Notes: Tenacity uptime has brief gaps; avoid duplicate Noblesse.

Stellar Swirl C6 (Shield / Reaction Buffer)

Protect the Stellar Swirl driver and apply Diona’s C6 reaction bonus while the driver remains inside her Burst.

  • Weapons: Breezeborne Refrain, Favonius Warbow
  • Artifacts: Tenacity of the Millelith (4 pcs); Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ER as needed, then HP%; CRIT Rate if Favonius.
  • Teams: Vesna or Mizuki with Odette/Cryo Traveler; ideally another Anemo buffer.
  • When it applies: C6 + Witch’s Revelation unlocked
  • Notes: For Mizuki, ensure a separate Stellar Swirl enabler.

Cryo Battery / Freeze (Shield / Battery)

Sacrificial Bow can provide a second Hold Skill and additional Cryo energy for Burst-hungry Cryo damage dealers.

  • Weapons: Sacrificial Bow, Favonius Warbow
  • Artifacts: Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ER until Burst is consistent, then HP% for shields.
  • Teams: Cryo carry such as Ayaka or Ganyu; Hydro for Freeze teams.
  • When it applies: Sacrificial reset depends on cooldown and rotation
  • Notes: Diona works at C0; C1 lowers ER demand.

Healing Focus (Healer / Shield)

Trade some shield strength for stronger Burst healing; Song of Days Past adds damage to the active ally.

  • Weapons: Favonius Warbow, Sacrificial Bow, Recurve Bow
  • Artifacts: Song of Days Past (4 pcs)
  • Stats: HP% / HP% / Healing Bonus; get enough ER for Burst.
  • Teams: A team with another Noblesse holder and a carry who can benefit from healing-based damage.
  • When it applies: Song value depends on healing and rotation
  • Notes: Healing Bonus improves healing, not the shield.

Diona talents

Normal Attack: Kätzlein Style

Normal Attack
Performs up to 5 consecutive shots with a bow.

Charged Attack
Performs a more precise Aimed Shot with increased DMG.
While aiming, biting frost will accumulate on the arrowhead. A fully charged frost arrow will deal Cryo DMG.

Plunging Attack
Fires off a shower of arrows in mid-air before falling and striking the ground, dealing AoE DMG upon impact.

Elemental Skill: Icy Paws

Cooldown: Press: 6s / Hold: 15s

Fires an Icy Paw that deals Cryo DMG to opponents and forms a shield on hit.
The shield's DMG Absorption scales based on Diona's Max HP, and its duration scales off the number of Icy Paws that hit their target.

Press
Rapidly fires off 2 Icy Paws.

Hold
Dashes back quickly before firing 5 Icy Paws.
The shield created by a Hold attack will gain a 75% DMG Absorption Bonus.

The shield has a 250% Cryo DMG Absorption Bonus, and will cause your active character to become affected by Cryo at the point of formation for a short duration.

Elemental Burst: Signature Mix

Cooldown: 20s · Energy cost: 80

Tosses out a special cold brew that deals AoE Cryo DMG and creates a Drunken Mist in an AoE.

Drunken Mist
·Deals continuous Cryo DMG to opponents within the AoE.
·Continuously regenerates the HP of characters within the AoE.

Passive talent: Cat's Tail Secret Menu

Characters shielded by Icy Paws have their Movement SPD increased by 10% and their Stamina Consumption decreased by 10%.

Passive talent: Drunkards' Farce

Opponents who enter the AoE of Signature Mix have 10% decreased ATK for 15s.

Passive talent: Complimentary Bar Food

When a Perfect Cooking is achieved on a dish with restorative effects, there is a 12% chance to obtain double the product.

C2: Shaken, Not Purred

Additionally, when paws hit their targets, creates a shield for other nearby characters on the field with 50% of the Icy Paws shield's DMG Absorption for 5s.

C5: Double Shot, on the Rocks

Lisa Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

Lisa build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Kagura's Verity, Lost Prayer to the Sacred Winds), artifacts (Thundering Fury), main stats.

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Role: Main DPS / Sub DPS

Lisa is an Electro DPS who excels in on-field Aggravate teams. Her attacks and Burst apply Electro often, while her Burst lowers enemy DEF by 15%. Build CRIT, ATK and EM, maintain Quicken, and use her charged Skill to deal damage and generate Energy.

Best weapons for Lisa

  1. Kagura's Verity: Skill stacks boost Electro damage. Best with repeated Skill casts.
  2. Lost Prayer to the Sacred Winds: CRIT Rate and on-field damage bonus. Needs sustained field time.
  3. Skyward Atlas: High ATK boosts direct Electro damage.
  4. A Thousand Floating Dreams: Large EM bonus benefits Aggravate. Lower direct damage in some builds.
  5. The Widsith: Strong but random temporary buffs. Time the proc for Lisa’s damage window.
  6. Solar Pearl: CRIT Rate; boosts Skill and Burst. Use Normal Attacks to activate passive.
  7. Mappa Mare: Craftable EM and reaction damage. Trigger reactions to gain stacks.
  8. Favonius Codex: ER and team particles ease Burst use. Requires CRIT Rate to trigger.

Best artifacts for Lisa

  • Thundering Fury (4 pcs) · Best: Aggravate boost; cuts Skill CD. Main stats: ATK%/EM · Electro · CRIT.
  • Gilded Dreams (4 pcs): EM and ATK for Aggravate. Main stats: ATK%/EM · Electro · CRIT.
  • Emblem of Severed Fate (4 pcs): Boosts off-field Burst damage. Main stats: ER/ATK% · Electro · CRIT.

Main stats

  • Sands: ATK% / Elemental Mastery
  • Goblet: Electro DMG Bonus
  • Circlet: CRIT Rate / CRIT DMG

Stat targets

  • CR: 60% - 75%
  • CD: 120% - 180%
  • ER: 120% - 160%
  • EM: 100 - 300

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 6-8+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 8-9+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 8-9+

Best teams for Lisa

  • Library of Thunder: Lisa (Electro DPS / Debuffer), Fischl (Off-field DPS / Battery), Nahida (Dendro Enabler / Buffer), Xilonen (Healer / Debuffer). Nahida maintains Quicken, Fischl batteries Lisa and triggers Aggravate, while Xilonen supplies resistance shred and healing.
  • Storm Study Group: Lisa (Electro Driver / Debuffer), Fischl (Off-field DPS / Battery), Yaoyao (Healer / Dendro Enabler), Sucrose (EM Buffer / Debuffer). A practical Aggravate team: Yaoyao sustains Quicken and heals; Fischl batteries Lisa while Sucrose shares EM and shreds Electro RES.
  • Budget Lightning: Lisa (Electro Driver / Debuffer), Fischl (Off-field DPS / Battery), Traveler (Dendro) (Dendro Enabler / Battery), Sayu (Healer / Debuffer). Dendro Traveler enables Quicken; Fischl provides Electro damage and particles, and Sayu heals and applies VV Electro shred.
  • Rainy Research: Lisa (Electro Driver / Debuffer), Fischl (Off-field DPS / Battery), Nahida (Dendro Enabler / Buffer), Xingqiu (Off-field DPS / Hydro Enabler). Quickbloom mixes Aggravate with Hyperbloom. Lisa must Normal Attack to trigger Xingqiu; heavy Hydro may reduce Quicken uptime.

Build variations

Off-Field Burst DPS (Off-field DPS)

Use Lisa for her Burst’s repeated Electro hits and 15% enemy DEF reduction while another unit plays on-field.

  • Weapons: Favonius Codex, Skyward Atlas, The Widsith
  • Artifacts: Emblem of Severed Fate (4 pcs); Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Build enough ER to Burst each rotation; use Electro goblet and CRIT circlet.
  • Teams: A team that funnels Electro particles to Lisa.
  • When it applies: Burst uptime varies with team Energy.
  • Notes: Hold Skill supplies particles; tap Skill supplies none.

Noblesse DEF-Shred Support (Support)

Choose when Lisa’s DEF shred and a teamwide ATK buff matter more than her individual damage.

  • Weapons: Favonius Codex, The Widsith
  • Artifacts: Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ER first, then CRIT Rate for Favonius; personal damage is secondary.
  • Teams: An ATK-scaling team without another Noblesse user.
  • When it applies: Requires reliable Burst uptime.

Accessible Aggravate DPS (On-field DPS)

Use a craftable or affordable catalyst while learning Lisa’s on-field Aggravate combos.

  • Weapons: Mappa Mare, The Widsith, Solar Pearl
  • Artifacts: Thundering Fury (4 pcs); Gilded Dreams (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Balance EM or ATK sands with Electro goblet and CRIT circlet.
  • Teams: Dendro for Quicken, preferably another Electro for Energy.
  • When it applies: Keep Quicken active.
  • Notes: At level 90, Aggravate damage improves.

Quickbloom Driver (On-field DPS)

Use for mixed Aggravate and some Hyperbloom damage, with reaction ownership depending on the exact team.

  • Weapons: A Thousand Floating Dreams, Mappa Mare, The Widsith
  • Artifacts: Thundering Fury (4 pcs); Gilded Dreams (4 pcs)
  • Stats: EM becomes more valuable; still build CRIT and enough ER for Burst.
  • Teams: Reliable Dendro, Electro battery and Hydro application.
  • When it applies: Hydro application may reduce Quicken uptime.
  • Notes: Not the same as a guaranteed full-EM Hyperbloom trigger.

Lisa talents

Normal Attack: Lightning Touch

Normal Attack
Performs up to 4 lightning attacks that deal Electro DMG.

Charged Attack
Consumes a certain amount of Stamina to deal AoE Electro DMG after a short casting time.

Plunging Attack
Gathering the might of Electro, Lisa plunges towards the ground from mid-air, damaging all opponents in her path. Deals AoE Electro DMG upon impact with the ground.

Elemental Skill: Violet Arc

Cooldown: Press: 1s / Holding: 16s

Channels the power of lightning to sweep bothersome matters away.

Press
Releases a homing Lightning Orb.
On hit, it deals Electro DMG and applies a stack of the Conductive status (max 3 stacks) to opponents in a small AoE.

Hold
After an extended casting time, calls down lightning from the heavens, dealing massive Electro DMG to all nearby opponents.
Deals great amounts of extra damage to opponents based on the number of Conductive stacks applied to them, and clears their Conductive status.

Elemental Burst: Lightning Rose

Cooldown: 20s · Energy cost: 80

Summons a Lightning Rose that unleashes powerful lightning bolts, launching surrounding opponents and dealing Electro DMG.
The Lightning Rose will continuously emit lightning to knock back opponents and deal Electro DMG throughout the ability's duration.

Passive talent: Induced Aftershock

Hits by Charged Attacks apply Violet Arc's Conductive status to opponents.

Passive talent: Static Electricity Field

Opponents hit by Lightning Rose have their DEF decreased by 15% for 10s.

Passive talent: General Pharmaceutics

When Lisa crafts a potion, she has a 20% chance to refund a portion of the crafting materials used.

Lisa constellations

C1: Infinite Circuit

Lisa regenerates 2 Energy for every opponent hit while holding Violet Arc.
A maximum of 10 Energy can be regenerated in this manner at any one time.

C2: Electromagnetic Field

Holding Violet Arc has the following effects:
·Increases DEF by 25%.
·Increases Lisa's resistance to interruption.

C3: Resonant Thunder

Increases the Level of Lightning Rose by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C4: Plasma Eruption

Lightning Rose now unleashes 1-3 lightning bolts when it attacks.

C5: Electrocute

Increases the Level of Violet Arc by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C6: Pulsating Witch

Role: Main DPS / Sub DPS

Lisa is an Electro DPS who excels in on-field Aggravate teams. Her attacks and Burst apply Electro often, while her Burst lowers enemy DEF by 15%. Build CRIT, ATK and EM, maintain Quicken, and use her charged Skill to deal damage and generate Energy.

Pros

Cons

  • Longer field-time rotations

Build variations

  • Off-Field Burst DPS (Off-field DPS): Use Lisa for her Burst’s repeated Electro hits and 15% enemy DEF reduction while another unit plays on-field.
  • Noblesse DEF-Shred Support (Support): Choose when Lisa’s DEF shred and a teamwide ATK buff matter more than her individual damage.
  • Accessible Aggravate DPS (On-field DPS): Use a craftable or affordable catalyst while learning Lisa’s on-field Aggravate combos.
  • Quickbloom Driver (On-field DPS): Use for mixed Aggravate and some Hyperbloom damage, with reaction ownership depending on the exact team.

Role: Support / Shielder

Lan Yan is an ATK-scaling Anemo shielder who protects the on-field carry while reducing Pyro, Hydro, Electro or Cryo resistance with Viridescent Venerer. Her Skill can absorb one of those elements for stronger matching-element protection. NA also swirls.

Best weapons for Lan Yan

  1. Starcaller's Watch
  2. Crane's Echoing Call
  3. Thrilling Tales of Dragon Slayers
  4. Hakushin Ring
  5. Sacrificial Fragments
  6. Favonius Codex
  7. Prototype Amber
  8. Wandering Evenstar

Best artifacts

  • Viridescent Venerer (4 pcs) · Best
  • Scroll of the Hero of Cinder City (4 pcs) · Anemo Carry
  • Golden Troupe (4 pcs) · Skill DPS

Main stats

  • Sands: ATK% / Energy Recharge
  • Goblet: ATK%
  • Circlet: ATK% / CRIT Rate

Stat targets

  • ATK: 2,000 - 2,500+ (shield)
  • ER: 100% - 140% (Burst optional)
  • ER: 180% - 220% (solo Anemo, frequent Burst)
  • CRIT Rate: 40% - 60% (Favonius)

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1
  • Elemental Skill: Level 8-9
  • Elemental Burst: Level 8-9

Best teams

  • Crimson Featherguard: Lan Yan, Arlecchino, Yelan, Bennett
  • Electro Featherdance: Lan Yan, Clorinde, Fischl, Nahida
  • Frozen Flight: Lan Yan, Kamisato Ayaka, Xingqiu, Rosaria
  • Wanderer Windguard: Lan Yan, Wanderer, Faruzan, Bennett

Pros

  • Strong ATK-scaling shield
  • 40% RES shred with VV
  • Flexible support weapons
  • C2 restores shield HP

Cons

  • Short shield duration
  • 16s Skill cooldown
  • Burst can be ER-hungry
  • Needs setup for key Swirls

Build variations

  • Aggravate Shield Buffer (Support): Use in Clorinde, Keqing or other Aggravate teams for shields, Electro RES shred and conditional Hakushin buffs.
  • Anemo Carry Shield (Support): Use Scroll to grant a modest Anemo DMG buff while shielding your Anemo carry; VV cannot shred Anemo RES.
  • On-Field Swirl Driver (On-field Driver): Drive reactions with Anemo Normal Attacks and Swirls while benefiting from her own shield.
  • Quickswap Skill DPS (Quickswap DPS): Use repeated Skill casts for personal damage rather than maximizing the shield or team buffs.
  • F2P ATK Shield (Support): A low-investment defensive option: shield the carry, trigger Swirl for VV shred and pass the TTDS buff.
  • Aggravate Shield Buffer (Support): Use in Clorinde, Keqing or other Aggravate teams for shields, Electro RES shred and conditional Hakushin buffs.

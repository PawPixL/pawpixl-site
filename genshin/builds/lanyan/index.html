Lan Yan Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

Lan Yan build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Starcaller's Watch, Crane's Echoing Call), artifacts (Viridescent Venerer), main stats.

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Role: Support / Shielder

Lan Yan is an ATK-scaling Anemo shielder who protects the on-field carry while reducing Pyro, Hydro, Electro or Cryo resistance with Viridescent Venerer. Her Skill can absorb one of those elements for stronger matching-element protection. NA also swirls.

Best weapons for Lan Yan

  1. Starcaller's Watch: Elemental Mastery and a strong team damage buff when she shields an ally; excellent premium support choice. Shielding support; ATK shield can be weaker than with high-ATK weapons.
  2. Crane's Echoing Call: High base ATK supports stronger shields, with a plunge buff for teams using a plunging carry. Plunge teams with Xianyun or Xiao; passive requires plunge hits.
  3. Thrilling Tales of Dragon Slayers: At R5, gives the next character 48% ATK for 10s, making it an outstanding accessible carry buff. ATK-scaling on-field carry; low weapon base ATK lowers shield strength. R5 strongly recommended.
  4. Hakushin Ring: Energy Recharge plus elemental damage buffs after Electro-related reactions; especially useful in Aggravate teams. Electro team and a relevant reaction triggered by Lan Yan. Higher refinements improve the buff.
  5. Sacrificial Fragments: Provides Elemental Mastery and can reset Skill cooldown for extra shields, Swirls and flexible rotations. On-field driver or long rotations; reset depends on refinement. R3+ preferred for consistency.
  6. Favonius Codex: Energy Recharge and neutral particles reduce the team's Energy needs when she lands a CRIT hit. Build enough CRIT Rate to trigger the passive reliably. Higher refinements improve consistency.
  7. Prototype Amber: Restores Energy and heals the team after Burst; useful for extra comfort when Burst is available. Only valuable when Bursting regularly; HP% does not strengthen her ATK shield. Higher refinements improve healing and Energy.
  8. Wandering Evenstar: Elemental Mastery converts partly into flat ATK for Lan Yan and nearby allies, even while off-field. EM-focused driver or hybrid build. Higher refinements improve ATK sharing.

Best artifacts for Lan Yan

  • Viridescent Venerer (4 pcs) · Best: Swirl cuts the affected Pyro, Hydro, Electro or Cryo RES by 40% for 10s; ideal for most elemental carries. Main stats: ATK% / ATK% / ATK%; ER or CRIT Rate as needed.
  • Scroll of the Hero of Cinder City (4 pcs) · Anemo Carry: Grants 12% Anemo DMG Bonus through Swirl, useful for Wanderer or Xiao when VV does not buff the main damage type. Main stats: ATK% / ATK% / ATK%; optional ER.
  • Golden Troupe (4 pcs) · Skill DPS: Boosts her Skill damage in a quickswap build; sacrifices the valuable team buffs provided by VV or Scroll. Main stats: ATK% or EM / Anemo DMG / CRIT.

Main stats

  • Sands: ATK% / Energy Recharge
  • Goblet: ATK%
  • Circlet: ATK% / CRIT Rate

Stat targets

  • ATK: 2,000 - 2,500+ (shield)
  • ER: 100% - 140% (Burst optional)
  • ER: 180% - 220% (solo Anemo, frequent Burst)
  • CRIT Rate: 40% - 60% (Favonius)

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1
  • Elemental Skill: Level 8-9
  • Elemental Burst: Level 8-9

Best teams for Lan Yan

  • Crimson Featherguard: Lan Yan (Shielder / Debuffer), Arlecchino (On-field DPS), Yelan (Off-field DPS / Buffer), Bennett (Healer / Buffer). Lan Yan shields Arlecchino, shreds Pyro RES with VV and can pass a TTDS ATK buff. Bennett boosts ATK; Yelan enables Vaporize.
  • Electro Featherdance: Lan Yan (Shielder / Debuffer), Clorinde (On-field DPS), Fischl (Off-field DPS / Battery), Nahida (Dendro Enabler / Buffer). Lan Yan protects Clorinde and shreds Electro RES. Fischl and Nahida maintain Aggravate; Hakushin Ring is valuable here.
  • Frozen Flight: Lan Yan (Shielder / Debuffer), Kamisato Ayaka (On-field DPS), Xingqiu (Off-field DPS / Hydro Enabler), Rosaria (Off-field DPS / CRIT Buffer). Lan Yan groups enemies, shields Ayaka and shreds Cryo RES. Xingqiu supplies Hydro while Rosaria adds Cryo damage.
  • Wanderer Windguard: Lan Yan (Shielder / Debuffer), Wanderer (On-field DPS), Faruzan (Anemo Buffer / Debuffer), Bennett (Healer / Buffer). Lan Yan protects Wanderer from interruption and can use Scroll to grant Anemo DMG. Faruzan and Bennett amplify his damage.

Pros

  • Strong ATK-scaling shield
  • 40% RES shred with VV
  • Flexible support weapons
  • C2 restores shield HP

Cons

  • Short shield duration
  • 16s Skill cooldown
  • Burst can be ER-hungry
  • Needs setup for key Swirls

Build variations

Aggravate Shield Buffer (Support)

Use in Clorinde, Keqing or other Aggravate teams for shields, Electro RES shred and conditional Hakushin buffs.

  • Weapons: Hakushin Ring, Favonius Codex, Thrilling Tales of Dragon Slayers
  • Artifacts: Viridescent Venerer (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ATK% main stats for shield; add ER for C4 Burst buff and CRIT Rate for Favonius.
  • Teams: Electro carry plus Dendro support; Lan Yan must Swirl Electro.
  • When it applies: Hakushin requires an Electro-related reaction by Lan Yan.

Anemo Carry Shield (Support)

Use Scroll to grant a modest Anemo DMG buff while shielding your Anemo carry; VV cannot shred Anemo RES.

  • Weapons: Favonius Codex, Thrilling Tales of Dragon Slayers, Crane's Echoing Call
  • Artifacts: Scroll of the Hero of Cinder City (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ATK% for stronger shields; ER only if Bursting every rotation.
  • Teams: Wanderer or Xiao as the main damage dealer; other teammates handle Anemo buffs.
  • When it applies: Shield lasts 12.5s and may not cover an entire extended rotation.

On-Field Swirl Driver (On-field Driver)

Drive reactions with Anemo Normal Attacks and Swirls while benefiting from her own shield.

  • Weapons: Sacrificial Fragments, Starcaller's Watch, Wandering Evenstar
  • Artifacts: Viridescent Venerer (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Elemental Mastery / EM / EM; level to 90 for stronger Swirl damage.
  • Teams: Off-field Hydro and Electro or Electro and Dendro partners for repeated reactions.
  • When it applies: EM reduces shield strength compared with ATK%; C2 helps shield sustain.

Quickswap Skill DPS (Quickswap DPS)

Use repeated Skill casts for personal damage rather than maximizing the shield or team buffs.

  • Weapons: Sacrificial Fragments, Starcaller's Watch, Favonius Codex
  • Artifacts: Golden Troupe (4 pcs); Viridescent Venerer (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ATK% or EM Sands, Anemo DMG Goblet, CRIT Circlet; sufficient ER if Bursting.
  • Teams: Teammates provide major buffs or another Anemo handles VV shred.
  • When it applies: Sacrificial reset or C6 charge enables extra Skills; Golden Troupe lowers team utility.

F2P ATK Shield (Support)

A low-investment defensive option: shield the carry, trigger Swirl for VV shred and pass the TTDS buff.

  • Weapons: Thrilling Tales of Dragon Slayers, Hakushin Ring, Prototype Amber
  • Artifacts: Viridescent Venerer (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ATK% / ATK% / ATK%; shield strength comes from ATK rather than HP.
  • Teams: Best with an ATK-scaling Pyro, Hydro, Electro or Cryo carry.
  • When it applies: Use R5 TTDS where possible; its low base ATK weakens the shield.

Lan Yan talents

Normal Attack: Black Pheasant Strides on Water

Normal Attack
Wields her Feathermoon Rings to perform up to 4 attacks, dealing Anemo DMG.

Charged Attack
Consumes a certain amount of Stamina to throw out a Feathermoon Ring, dealing Anemo DMG.

Plunging Attack
Gathers the power of Anemo and plunges towards the ground from mid-air, damaging all opponents in her path. Deals AoE Anemo DMG upon impact with the ground.

Elemental Skill: Swallow-Wisp Pinion Dance

Cooldown: 16s

Summoning forth the Swallow-Wisp Shield, a secret art passed down across generations, Lan Yan dashes forward and, upon making contact with her target, will leap back into the air.
The Swallow-Wisp Shield's DMG absorption scales based on her ATK, and has a 250% absorption efficiency against Anemo DMG.
While in mid-air due to the effects of her Elemental Skill: Swallow-Wisp Pinion Dance, pressing her Elemental Skill again or pressing Normal Attack will cause Lan Yan to hurl her Feathermoon Rings at an opponent, and they will spin between nearby opponents twice upon hit, dealing Anemo DMG.
Holding the Skill will cause it to behave differently.

Hold
Enter Aiming Mode to adjust the dash direction.

Elemental Burst: Lustrous Moonrise

Cooldown: 15s · Energy cost: 60

Lan Yan creates a Feathermoon Swallow Array, pulling in nearby opponents and objects and dealing multiple instances of AoE Anemo DMG.

Passive talent: Four Sealing Divination Charms

When the dash from the Elemental Skill Swallow-Wisp Pinion Dance hits a target, if it interacts with Hydro/Pyro/Cryo/Electro, the Swallow-Wisp Shield will undergo Elemental Absorption, with the converted shield absorbing DMG from the corresponding element with 250% efficiency.
Additionally, if this use of Swallow-Wisp Pinion Dance causes the Swallow-Wisp Shield to undergo Elemental Absorption, the Feathermoon Rings that Lan Yan throws at her foes additionally deal Elemental DMG equal to 50% of the original in the corresponding Elemental Type. This DMG is considered Elemental Skill DMG.

Passive talent: Skyfeather Evil-Subduing Charm

The Elemental Skill Swallow-Wisp Pinion Dance and the Elemental Burst Lustrous Moonrise deal increased DMG equal to 309% and 774% of Lan Yan's Elemental Mastery respectively.

Passive talent: Thought and Intent, Like Silken Scent

When Lan Yan is in the party, your characters will not startle Crystalflies and certain other animals when getting near them.
Check the "Other" sub-category of the "Living Beings / Wildlife" section in the Archive for creatures this skill works on.

Lan Yan constellations

C1: "As One Might Stride Betwixt the Clouds"

After triggering the Elemental Absorption from the Ascension Talent "Four Sealing Divination Charms," this instance of Lan Yan's Elemental Skill Swallow-Wisp Pinion Dance will produce another Feathermoon Ring when they are thrown at opponents.
You must first unlock the Ascension Talent "Four Sealing Divination Charms."

C2: "Dance Vestments Billow Like Rainbow Jade"

While the Swallow-Wisp Shield is active, when your active party member's Normal Attacks deal DMG, 40% of the Shield's DMG absorption will be restored, up to its original maximum absorption. This effect can occur once every 2s.

C3: "On White Wings Pierce Through Cloud and Fog"

Increases the Level of Swallow-Wisp Pinion Dance by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C4: "With Drakefalcon's Blood-Pearls Adorned"

After Lan Yan uses her Elemental Burst Lustrous Moonrise, the Elemental Mastery of all nearby party members increases by 60 for 12s.

C5: "Having Met You, My Heart is Gladdened"

Increases the Level of Lustrous Moonrise by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C6: "Let Us Away on Sylphic Wing, the Silvered Ornaments to Ring"

Swallow-Wisp Pinion Dance gains 1 additional charge.

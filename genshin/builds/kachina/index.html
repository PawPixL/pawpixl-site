Kachina Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

Kachina build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Mountain-Bracing Bolt, Favonius Lance), artifacts (Scroll of the Hero of Cinder City), main stats.

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Role: Support, Sub DPS

Kachina is a DEF-scaling Geo support whose Turbo Twirly deals off-field damage. She excels at triggering Crystallize to activate Scroll of the Hero of Cinder City, boosting Geo and the reacted element. Favonius Lance supports team Energy.

Best weapons for Kachina

  1. Mountain-Bracing Bolt: Boosts Skill damage and DEF-based hits. For personal damage.
  2. Favonius Lance: Generates particles to support the team. Needs CRIT hits.
  3. Skyward Spine: Energy Recharge helps Burst uptime.
  4. Deathmatch: CRIT Rate and DEF improve damage. Best versus multiple foes.
  5. Footprint of the Rainbow: Large DEF boost after using her Skill.
  6. Prototype Starglitter: Craftable Energy Recharge stat stick.
  7. "The Catch": Helps Burst damage, not her Skill hits.

Best artifacts for Kachina

  • Scroll of the Hero of Cinder City (4 pcs): Crystallize buffs team damage. Main stats: ER/DEF% · Geo/DEF% · CR/DEF%.
  • Tenacity of the Millelith (4 pcs): Skill hits boost team ATK. Main stats: ER/DEF% · Geo · CR.
  • Obsidian Codex (4 pcs): For on-field Turbo Twirly damage. Main stats: DEF% · Geo DMG · CRIT.

Main stats

  • Sands: Energy Recharge / DEF%
  • Goblet: Geo DMG / DEF%
  • Circlet: Crit Rate / DEF%

Stat targets

  • ER: 150% - 180%
  • CR (Fav): 40%+
  • DEF: 1,600+

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 9+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 6-8

Best teams for Kachina

  • Crystal Cannon: Kachina (Support), Navia (Main DPS), Bennett (Buffer), Xiangling (Sub DPS). Kachina fuels Navia with Crystallize shards and Scroll buffs; Bennett and Xiangling provide Pyro support.
  • Rock Solid: Kachina, Noelle, Gorou, Furina. Kachina buffs Geo damage, Gorou boosts DEF, and Noelle heals the team to support Furina’s damage buff.
  • Surf and Stone: Kachina, Mualani, Xiangling, Candace. Kachina can buff Hydro with Scroll; Xiangling supplies Pyro for Mualani’s Vaporizes. Manage aura timing.
  • Crystal Budget: Kachina, Ningguang, Bennett, Fischl. Fischl enables Electro Crystallize for Scroll buffs; Ningguang supplies Geo damage and Bennett boosts ATK.

Pros

  • Strong Scroll buffs
  • Off-field Geo hits
  • F2P-friendly gear
  • Useful Geo battery

Cons

  • Low personal damage
  • Small Skill area
  • Needs Crystallize
  • Buff requires setup

Build variations

On-field Turbo Twirly (On-field DPS)

For players who enjoy riding Turbo Twirly on-field. More personal damage, but generally less team value than Scroll support.

  • Weapons: Footprint of the Rainbow, Mountain-Bracing Bolt, Deathmatch
  • Artifacts: Obsidian Codex (4 pcs)
  • Stats: DEF% sands, Geo DMG goblet, CRIT circlet; prioritize DEF and CRIT after needed ER.
  • Teams: Appreciates Geo battery and DEF buffs such as Gorou.
  • When it applies: Needs on-field Nightsoul uptime.
  • Notes: A fun alternative rather than her strongest overall team contribution.

Favonius Team Battery (Support)

Use if the team struggles to recharge. Kachina still provides Scroll buffs after triggering Crystallize.

  • Weapons: Favonius Lance, Prototype Starglitter
  • Artifacts: Scroll of the Hero of Cinder City (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Build enough CRIT Rate to reliably trigger Favonius on-field; prioritize ER to meet Burst needs.
  • Teams: Teams where allied Bursts need extra particles.
  • When it applies: Favonius passive needs an on-field CRIT hit.
  • Notes: ER needs vary by team and whether Burst is used every rotation.

Kachina talents

Normal Attack: Cragbiter

Normal Attack
Performs up to 4 consecutive spear strikes.

Charged Attack
Consumes a certain amount of Stamina to lunge forward, dealing damage to opponents along the way.

Plunging Attack
Plunges from mid-air to strike the ground below, damaging opponents along the path and dealing AoE DMG upon impact.

Elemental Skill: Go, Go Turbo Twirly!

Cooldown: 20s

Fight alongside the turbo trusty Turbo Twirly!
Summons Turbo Twirly. When the skill is Held and released, Kachina will summon and ride Turbo Twirly.
After the Skill is used, Kachina will gain 60 Nightsoul points and enter the Nightsoul's Blessing state.

Nightsoul's Blessing: Kachina
Turbo Twirly's various actions consume Kachina's Nightsoul points.
The Nightsoul's Blessing state will end when these points have been depleted.

Turbo Twirly
Can exist independently or be ridden and operated by Kachina. When Turbo Twirly is present, Kachina's Elemental Skill, "Go, Go Turbo Twirly!", will change to an option to Mount/Dismount Turbo Twirly.
·When present independent of Kachina, it will slam the ground at intervals, dealing Nightsoul-aligned AoE Geo DMG based on Kachina's DEF.
·When Kachina is riding Turbo Twirly, she can control it to move and climb swiftly, and use Normal Attacks to slam the ground, dealing Nightsoul-aligned AoE Geo DMG based on Kachina's DEF.

Turbo Twirly will disappear once Kachina's Nightsoul's Blessing state ends, and it is considered a Geo Construct. Only 1 Turbo Twirly created by Kachina herself can exist on the field at any one time.

Elemental Burst: Time to Get Serious!

Cooldown: 18s · Energy cost: 70

Striking the ground, Kachina deals AoE Geo DMG based on her DEF and creates a Turbo Drill Field.
The Field increases Turbo Twirly's attack AoE and its Movement SPD when Kachina is riding it.

If Kachina's own Turbo Twirly is present when this is used, the Turbo Twirly will be summoned to her side.

Passive talent: Mountain Echoes

After nearby party members trigger a Nightsoul Burst, Kachina's Geo DMG Bonus increases by 20% for 12s.

Passive talent: The Weight of Stone

Turbo Twirly's DMG is increased by 20% of Kachina's DEF.

Passive talent: Night Realm's Gift: Heart of Unity

After her Nightsoul points are depleted, Kachina will switch to consuming Phlogiston to maintain her Nightsoul's Blessing.
While in an area with Phlogiston Mechanics within Natlan, she can use Nightsoul Transmission: Kachina. When the active character is currently sprinting, climbing, in a movement mode caused by certain Talents, or at a certain height in the air, the following will trigger when switching to Kachina: Kachina will enter the Nightsoul's Blessing state and ride Turbo Twirly when taking the field while gaining 30 Nightsoul points. Nightsoul Transmission can be triggered once every 10s by your own team.

Additionally, while in Natlan, Kachina will consume 90% less Stamina when climbing while riding Turbo Twirly.

Passive talent: Boon of Crystal Flame

While in an area with Phlogiston Mechanics within Natlan, 20 Stamina will be restored when interacting with some harvestable items. Additionally, the location of nearby resources unique to Natlan will appear on your mini-map.

Kachina constellations

C1: Shards Are Gems Too

When Kachina mounts or dismounts Turbo Twirly, she will absorb nearby Elemental Shards created by Crystallize.
Additionally, when a party member picks up such a Shard, or triggers Lunar-Crystallize reactions, 3 Energy will be restored to Kachina. Energy can be restored this way once every 5s.

C2: Never Leave Home Without... Turbo Twirly

When Kachina uses "Time to Get Serious!", she will regain 20 Nightsoul points. If there is no Turbo Twirly belonging to her on the field, she will enter Nightsoul's Blessing and summon one.

C3: Improved Stabilizer

Increases the Level of Go, Go Turbo Twirly! by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C4: More Foes, More Caution

When there are 1/2/3/4 (or more) opponents within the Turbo Drill Field created by "Time to Get Serious!", the active character within the field gains 8%/12%/16%/20% increased DEF.

C5: All I've Collected Till Now

Increases the Level of Time to Get Serious! by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C6: This Time, I've Gotta Win

When your active character's Shield is replaced or destroyed for any reason, Kachina will deal 200% of her DEF as AoE Geo DMG. This effect can be triggered once every 5s.

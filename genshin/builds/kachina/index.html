Kachina Build Guide | Best Weapons, Artifacts & Teams

Kachina build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Mountain-Bracing Bolt, Favonius Lance), artifacts (Scroll of the Hero of Cinder City), main stats…

Role: Support, Sub DPS

Kachina is a DEF-scaling Geo support whose Turbo Twirly deals off-field damage. She excels at triggering Crystallize to activate Scroll of the Hero of Cinder City, boosting Geo and the reacted element. Favonius Lance supports team Energy.

Best weapons for Kachina

  1. Mountain-Bracing Bolt
  2. Favonius Lance
  3. Skyward Spine
  4. Deathmatch
  5. Footprint of the Rainbow
  6. Prototype Starglitter
  7. "The Catch"

Best artifacts

  • Scroll of the Hero of Cinder City (4 pcs)
  • Tenacity of the Millelith (4 pcs)
  • Obsidian Codex (4 pcs)

Main stats

  • Sands: Energy Recharge / DEF%
  • Goblet: Geo DMG / DEF%
  • Circlet: Crit Rate / DEF%

Stat targets

  • ER: 150% - 180%
  • CR (Fav): 40%+
  • DEF: 1,600+

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 9+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 6-8

Best teams

  • Crystal Cannon: Kachina, Navia, Bennett, Xiangling
  • Rock Solid: Kachina, Noelle, Gorou, Furina
  • Surf and Stone: Kachina, Mualani, Xiangling, Candace
  • Crystal Budget: Kachina, Ningguang, Bennett, Fischl

Pros

  • Strong Scroll buffs
  • Off-field Geo hits
  • F2P-friendly gear
  • Useful Geo battery

Cons

  • Low personal damage
  • Small Skill area
  • Needs Crystallize
  • Buff requires setup

Build variations

  • On-field Turbo Twirly (On-field DPS): For players who enjoy riding Turbo Twirly on-field. More personal damage, but generally less team value than Scroll support.
  • Favonius Team Battery (Support): Use if the team struggles to recharge. Kachina still provides Scroll buffs after triggering Crystallize.

Role: Off-Field DPS

Yae Miko delivers consistent off-field Electro damage with strong reaction synergy. Her versatility makes her a great fit for Stellar-Conduct and Aggravate teams.

  2. Reliquary of Truth
  3. Skyward Atlas
  4. A Thousand Floating Dreams
  5. The Widsith
  6. Echoes of the Heart
  7. Flowing Purity
  8. Oathsworn Eye

  • Gilded Dreams + Emblem of Severed Fate (4 pcs) · Alt Stellar Sub DPS
  • Golden Troupe (4 pcs) · Electro Sub DPS

Best teams

Yae Miko Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

Yae Miko build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Kagura's Verity, Reliquary of Truth), artifacts (Disenchantment in Deep Shadow), main stats.

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Role: Off-Field DPS

Yae Miko delivers consistent off-field Electro damage with strong reaction synergy. Her versatility makes her a great fit for Stellar-Conduct and Aggravate teams.

Best weapons for Yae Miko

  1. Kagura's Verity: Yae's signature weapon, providing massive CRIT DMG and stacking Elemental Skill DMG bonuses that perfectly complement her Sesshou Sakura.
  2. Reliquary of Truth: Provides substantial CRIT DMG, additional CRIT Rate, and Elemental Mastery, making it a powerful alternative across multiple builds.
  3. Skyward Atlas: High Base ATK and ATK% with an Elemental DMG bonus; strong unconditional offensive stats.
  4. A Thousand Floating Dreams: Large Elemental Mastery stat and modest party EM utility; strong for reaction-focused teams.
  5. The Widsith: Offers valuable CRIT DMG and powerful random buffs, making it one of Yae's strongest 4-star damage options.
  6. Echoes of the Heart: A strong craftable option providing ATK, Elemental Mastery, and Stellar-Conduct DMG bonuses. Particularly effective in teams with reliable reaction uptime.
  7. Flowing Purity: Craftable ATK% catalyst that can grant Elemental DMG after using a Skill, provided its Bond of Life is cleared with healing.
  8. Oathsworn Eye: Event catalyst with ATK% and extra Energy Recharge after using her Skill. Helpful for Burst uptime.

Best artifacts for Yae Miko

  • Disenchantment in Deep Shadow (4 pcs) · Stellar Sub DPS: Best personal Stellar-Conduct set; boosts reaction damage and CRIT against affected enemies. Avoid Electro DMG Goblet. Main stats: CR / CD > ATK% > EM > ER.
  • Gilded Dreams (4 pcs) · Alt Stellar Sub DPS: Gives EM and ATK after reactions. Useful for Aggravate or reaction-focused setups. Main stats: ER > CR / CD > ATK% > EM.
  • Golden Troupe (4 pcs) · Electro Sub DPS: Strong traditional off-field Skill set. Its DMG bonus does not improve Stellar-Conduct reaction damage. Main stats: CRIT Rate / CRIT DMG > EM > ATK% > ER (as needed).

Main stats

  • Sands: ATK% / EM
  • Goblet: ATK% / EM (Stellar); Electro DMG (Aggravate)
  • Circlet: Crit Rate / Crit DMG

Stat targets

  • CR: Balance after set buffs
  • CD: Aim for a 1:2 CRIT ratio
  • EM: ~100–300 (Stellar)
  • ATK: After CRIT and EM
  • ER: Not a Stellar priority

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 9+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 9+

Best teams for Yae Miko

  • Three Geniuses & Qiqi: Yae Miko (Off-field DPS), Sandrone (On-field DPS), Nicole (Shielder / Buffer), qiqi (Healer). Yae and Sandrone provide off-field Electro damage and coordinated attacks, while Nicole buffs the team and Qiqi keeps everyone alive. A flexible, sustained-damage team with plenty of off-field pressure.
  • Sparkle Prince: Yae Miko (Off-field DPS), Mitya (Off-field DPS / Enabler), qiqi (Healer), Odette (Cryo Enabler / Buffer). Yae provides off-field Electro damage while Mitya and Odette drive the team’s Lunar reactions. Qiqi adds healing and sustain, keeping the team comfortable through extended fights.
  • I’m Aggravated: Yae Miko (Off-field DPS), Alhaitham (On-field DPS), Nahida (Dendro Enabler / Buffer), Baizhu (Healer / Shielder). Alhaitham drives on-field Dendro damage while Yae triggers Aggravate with consistent Electro application. Nahida boosts Dendro damage and EM, while Baizhu provides healing and additional reaction support.
  • Resting Ei Face: Yae Miko (Off-field DPS), Raiden Shogun (On-field DPS), Nicole (Shielder / Buffer), Chevreuse (Healer / Buffer). Raiden handles on-field damage while Yae adds off-field Electro. Nicole shields and buffs ATK, and Chevreuse strengthens Pyro and Electro damage through Overloaded.

Pros

  • Excellent off-field damage
  • Stellar-Conduct synergy
  • Flexible teams
  • Long-lasting turret uptime

Cons

  • Burst requires field time.
  • Energy-hungry outside Stellar-Conduct
  • Reaction-dependent damage ceiling

Build variations

Aggravate Turrets (Off-field DPS)

Sustain turret damage and frequent Aggravates.

  • Weapons: Kagura's Verity, A Thousand Floating Dreams
  • Artifacts: Golden Troupe (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ATK% or EM / Electro / CRIT.
  • Teams: Dendro partner to maintain Quicken.

Stellar-Conduct (Off-field DPS)

Use reaction-specific bonuses only where their conditions trigger.

  • Weapons: Kagura's Verity, Skyward Atlas
  • Artifacts: Disenchantment in Deep Shadow (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ATK% or EM Sands; ATK%/EM Goblet; CRIT Circlet. Target ~100–300 EM; Burst is optional.
  • Teams: A qualifying Stellar-Conduct reaction team.
  • When it applies: Stellar-Conduct only; Electro DMG bonuses and Golden Troupe do not buff reaction damage.

Yae Miko talents

Normal Attack: Spiritfox Sin-Eater

Normal Attack
Summons forth kitsune spirits, initiating a maximum of 3 attacks that deal Electro DMG.

Charged Attack
Consumes a certain amount of Stamina to deal AoE Electro DMG after a short casting time.

Plunging Attack
Plunges towards the ground from mid-air, damaging all opponents in her path with thunderous might. Deals AoE Electro DMG upon impact with the ground.

Elemental Skill: Yakan Evocation: Sesshou Sakura

Cooldown: 4s

To Yae, such dull tasks as can be accomplished by driving spirits out need not be done personally.
Moves swiftly, leaving a Sesshou Sakura behind.

Sesshou Sakura
Has the following properties:
· Periodically strikes one nearby opponent with lightning, dealing Electro DMG
· When there are other Sesshou Sakura nearby, their level will increase, boosting the DMG dealt by these lightning strikes.

This skill has three charges.
A maximum of 3 Sesshou Sakura can exist simultaneously. The initial level of each Sesshou Sakura is 1, and the initial highest level each sakura can reach is 3. If a new Sesshou Sakura is created too close to an existing one, the existing one will be destroyed.

Elemental Burst: Great Secret Art: Tenko Kenshin

Cooldown: 22s · Energy cost: 90

Legends of "kitsunetsuki," or the manifestation of a kitsune's might, are common in Inazuma's folktales. One that particularly captures the imagination is that of the Sky Kitsune, said to cause lightning to fall down upon the foes of the Grand Narukami Shrine. Summons a lightning strike, dealing AoE Electro DMG.
When she uses this skill, Yae Miko will unseal nearby Sesshou Sakura, destroying their outer forms and transforming them into Tenko Thunderbolts that descend from the skies, dealing AoE Electro DMG. Each Sesshou Sakura destroyed in this way will create one Tenko Thunderbolt.

Passive talent: The Shrine's Sacred Shade

When casting Great Secret Art: Tenko Kenshin, each Sesshou Sakura destroyed resets the cooldown for 1 charge of Yakan Evocation: Sesshou Sakura.

Passive talent: Enlightened Blessing

Every point of Elemental Mastery Yae Miko possesses will increase Sesshou Sakura DMG by 0.15%.

Passive talent: Meditations of a Yako

Has a 25% chance to get 1 regional Character Talent Material (base material excluded) when crafting. The rarity is that of the base material.

Yae Miko constellations

C1: Yakan Offering

Each time Great Secret Art: Tenko Kenshin activates a Tenko Thunderbolt, Yae Miko will restore 8 Elemental Energy for herself.

C2: Fox's Mooncall

Sesshou Sakura start at Level 2 when created, their max level is increased to 4, and their attack range is increased by 60%.

C3: The Seven Glamours

Increases the Level of Yakan Evocation: Sesshou Sakura by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C4: Sakura Channeling

When Sesshou Sakura lightning hits opponents, the Electro DMG Bonus of all nearby party members is increased by 20% for 5s.

C5: Mischievous Teasing

Increases the Level of Great Secret Art: Tenko Kenshin by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C6: Forbidden Art: Daisesshou

The Sesshou Sakura's attacks will ignore 60% of the opponents' DEF.

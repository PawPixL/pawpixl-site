Role: Off-Field DPS

Yae Miko delivers consistent off-field Electro damage with strong reaction synergy. Her versatility makes her a great fit for Stellar-Conduct and Aggravate teams.

Best weapons for Yae Miko

  1. Kagura's Verity
  2. Reliquary of Truth
  3. Skyward Atlas
  4. A Thousand Floating Dreams
  5. The Widsith
  6. Echoes of the Heart
  7. Flowing Purity
  8. Oathsworn Eye

Best artifacts

  • Disenchantment in Deep Shadow (4 pcs) · Stellar Sub DPS
  • Gilded Dreams (4 pcs) · Alt Stellar Sub DPS
  • Golden Troupe (4 pcs) · Electro Sub DPS

Main stats

  • Sands: ATK% / EM
  • Goblet: ATK% / EM (Stellar); Electro DMG (Aggravate)
  • Circlet: Crit Rate / Crit DMG

Stat targets

  • CR: Balance after set buffs
  • CD: Aim for a 1:2 CRIT ratio
  • EM: ~100–300 (Stellar)
  • ATK: After CRIT and EM
  • ER: Not a Stellar priority

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 9+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 9+

Best teams

  • Three Geniuses & Qiqi: Yae Miko, Sandrone, Nicole, qiqi
  • Sparkle Prince: Yae Miko, Mitya, qiqi, Odette
  • I’m Aggravated: Yae Miko, Alhaitham, Nahida, Baizhu
  • Resting Ei Face: Yae Miko, Raiden Shogun, Nicole, Chevreuse

Pros

  • Excellent off-field damage
  • Stellar-Conduct synergy
  • Flexible teams
  • Long-lasting turret uptime

Cons

  • Burst requires field time.
  • Energy-hungry outside Stellar-Conduct
  • Reaction-dependent damage ceiling

Build variations

  • Aggravate Turrets (Off-field DPS): Sustain turret damage and frequent Aggravates.
  • Stellar-Conduct (Off-field DPS): Use reaction-specific bonuses only where their conditions trigger.

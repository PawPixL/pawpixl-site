Role: Support / Buffer

Yun Jin is a DEF-scaling Geo support whose Burst adds damage to teammates' Normal Attack hits. She excels with fast Normal Attack carries, but needs enough Energy Recharge to Burst every rotation. It does not buff Charged Attacks.

Best weapons for Yun Jin

  1. Engulfing Lightning
  2. Favonius Lance
  3. Footprint of the Rainbow
  4. Rightful Reward
  5. Prototype Starglitter
  6. "The Catch"
  7. Kitain Cross Spear

Best artifacts

  • Husk of Opulent Dreams (4 pcs) · Best
  • Husk of Opulent Dreams + Emblem of Severed Fate (2 + 2 pcs) · ER option
  • Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs) · Team ATK

Main stats

  • Sands: DEF% / Energy Recharge
  • Goblet: DEF%
  • Circlet: DEF% / Crit Rate

Stat targets

  • ER: 160% - 220%
  • DEF: 2000 - 2600
  • CR: 40% - 55%

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 6+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 9+

Best teams

  • Firework Finale: Yun Jin, Yoimiya, Yelan, Zhongli
  • Wanderer Spotlight: Yun Jin, Wanderer, Faruzan, Bennett
  • Ayato Encore: Yun Jin, Kamisato Ayato, Furina, Jean
  • Geo Opera: Yun Jin, Noelle, Gorou, Furina

Pros

  • Strong Normal Attack buffs
  • Excellent DEF scaling
  • Useful at C0
  • Easy artifact priorities

Cons

  • High Energy requirements
  • Normal Attack teams only
  • 30-hit Burst buff limit
  • Little personal damage

Build variations

  • Solo Geo / High Energy Needs (Normal Attack Buffer): When Yun Jin is the only Geo character and must reliably Burst every rotation. ER takes priority over maximum DEF until the Burst is consistent.
  • DEF-Focused Normal Attack Buff (Normal Attack Buffer): When the team already supplies ample Energy. This maximizes the additional Normal Attack damage from Yun Jin’s DEF-scaling Burst buff.
  • Noblesse Team Buff (Support / Team Buffer): When the team benefits substantially from an extra ATK buff and Noblesse is otherwise unused.

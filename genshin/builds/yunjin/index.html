Yun Jin Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams (7.2)

Yun Jin build guide for Genshin Impact 7.2: best weapons (Engulfing Lightning, Favonius Lance), artifacts (Husk of Opulent Dreams), main stats.

Last updated: November 12, 2026 · Game version 7.2

Role: Support / Buffer

Yun Jin is a DEF-scaling Geo support whose Burst adds damage to teammates' Normal Attack hits. She excels with fast Normal Attack carries, but needs enough Energy Recharge to Burst every rotation. It does not buff Charged Attacks.

Best weapons for Yun Jin

  1. Engulfing Lightning: High ER helps maintain Burst uptime.
  2. Favonius Lance: CRIT hits generate team Energy. Aim for ~45% CRIT Rate; hit on-field. R5 ideal.
  3. Footprint of the Rainbow: Huge DEF boost strengthens NA buffs. Use when ER needs are already met.
  4. Rightful Reward: Healing restores Energy to Yun Jin. Requires reliable healing on or off field.
  5. Prototype Starglitter: ER stat supports reliable Bursts.
  6. "The Catch": ER helps Burst; passive is minor.
  7. Kitain Cross Spear: Skill refunds Energy, with timing care. Use Skill before Burst; EM is wasted.

Best artifacts for Yun Jin

  • Husk of Opulent Dreams (4 pcs) · Best: Maximizes DEF-based Normal buffs. Main stats: ER/DEF% · DEF% · DEF%/CR.
  • Husk of Opulent Dreams + Emblem of Severed Fate (2 + 2 pcs) · ER option: Balances DEF with Burst uptime. Main stats: ER/DEF% · DEF% · DEF%/CR.
  • Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs) · Team ATK: Adds team ATK after Burst. Main stats: ER/DEF% · DEF% · DEF%/CR.

Main stats

  • Goblet: DEF%
  • Circlet: DEF% / Crit Rate

Stat targets

  • ER: 160% - 220%

  • CR: 40% - 55%

Talent priority

  • Elemental Burst: Level 9+

Best teams for Yun Jin

  • Firework Finale: Yun Jin (Normal Attack Buffer), Yoimiya (Main DPS), Yelan (Hydro Sub DPS), Zhongli (Shield / RES Shred). Yun Jin buffs each of Yoimiya’s fast Normal Attack hits. Yelan enables Vaporize; Zhongli provides safety and Geo Resonance.
  • Wanderer Spotlight: Yun Jin (Normal Attack Buffer), Wanderer (Main DPS), Faruzan (Anemo Buffer), Bennett (Healer / ATK Buffer). Yun Jin boosts Wanderer’s Normal Attack combos, Faruzan boosts Anemo damage, and Bennett heals and buffs ATK. Use a Normal Attack-focused rotation.
  • Ayato Encore: Yun Jin (Normal Attack Buffer), Kamisato Ayato (Main DPS), Furina (Hydro Sub DPS / Buff), Jean (Healer / VV). Ayato’s Skill attacks count as Normal Attack damage and gain Yun Jin’s buff. Furina adds damage and buffs; Jean heals and shreds Hydro resistance.
  • Geo Opera: Yun Jin (Normal Attack Buffer), Noelle (Main DPS / Healer), Gorou (DEF / Geo Buffer), Furina (Hydro Sub DPS / Buff). Noelle uses DEF scaling and Normal Attack strings to benefit from Yun Jin and Gorou. Her team healing builds Furina’s Fanfare.

Build variations

Solo Geo / High Energy Needs (Normal Attack Buffer)

When Yun Jin is the only Geo character and must reliably Burst every rotation. ER takes priority over maximum DEF until the Burst is consistent.

  • Weapons: Favonius Lance, Prototype Starglitter, Engulfing Lightning, "The Catch"
  • Artifacts: Husk of Opulent Dreams (4 pcs); Husk of Opulent Dreams + Emblem of Severed Fate (2 + 2 pcs)
  • Stats: Use ER Sands if needed; aim around 195–210% ER with Favonius, potentially 240%+ without it. Build enough CRIT Rate to proc Favonius.
  • Teams: Solo Geo team with an on-field Normal Attack DPS; more team Energy generation makes the build easier.
  • When it applies: Actual ER depends on teammate particles, Favonius procs and rotation length.
  • Notes: Favonius procs require Yun Jin to land a CRIT hit while on-field.

DEF-Focused Normal Attack Buff (Normal Attack Buffer)

When the team already supplies ample Energy. This maximizes the additional Normal Attack damage from Yun Jin’s DEF-scaling Burst buff.

  • Weapons: Footprint of the Rainbow, Favonius Lance, Prototype Starglitter
  • Artifacts: Husk of Opulent Dreams (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Use DEF% Sands, Goblet and Circlet after meeting Energy needs; favor DEF% substats.
  • Teams: Prefer a second Geo teammate and/or strong particle generation to lower her ER requirement.
  • When it applies: Do not sacrifice reliable Burst uptime to chase DEF.
  • Notes: Footprint is craftable; her Burst buff does not add damage to Charged Attacks.

Noblesse Team Buff (Support / Team Buffer)

When the team benefits substantially from an extra ATK buff and Noblesse is otherwise unused.

  • Weapons: Favonius Lance, Footprint of the Rainbow, Prototype Starglitter
  • Artifacts: Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Maintain sufficient ER first; then prioritize DEF%. CRIT Rate matters with Favonius.
  • Teams: ATK-scaling Normal Attack carry, with no other 4pc Noblesse Oblige holder.
  • When it applies: Noblesse 4pc team buffs do not stack with another Noblesse holder.
  • Notes: Usually a situational alternative to 4pc Husk rather than the default.

Yun Jin talents

Normal Attack: Cloud-Grazing Strike

Normal Attack
Performs up to 5 consecutive spear strikes.

Charged Attack
Consumes a certain amount of Stamina to lunge forward, dealing damage to opponents along the way.

Plunging Attack
Plunges from mid-air to strike the ground below, damaging opponents along the path and dealing AoE DMG upon impact.

Elemental Skill: Opening Flourish

Cooldown: 9s

Ms. Yun may just be acting out fights on stage, but her skills with the spear are real enough to defend against her foes.

Press
Flourishes her polearm in a cloud-grasping stance, dealing Geo DMG.

Hold
Takes up the Opening Flourish stance and charges up, forming a shield. DMG Absorption is based on Yun Jin's Max HP and has 150% effectiveness against all Elemental DMG and Physical DMG. The shield lasts until she finishes unleashing her Elemental Skill.
When the skill is released, when its duration ends, or when the shield breaks, Yun Jin will unleash the charged energy as an attack, dealing Geo DMG.
Based on the time spent charging, it will either unleash an attack at Charge Level 1 or Level 2.

Elemental Burst: Cliffbreaker's Banner

Cooldown: 15s · Energy cost: 60

Deals AoE Geo DMG and grants all nearby party members a Flying Cloud Flag Formation.

Flying Cloud Flag Formation
When Normal Attack DMG is dealt to opponents, Bonus DMG will be dealt based on Yun Jin's current DEF.

The effects of this skill will be cleared after a set duration or after being triggered a specific number of times.
When one Normal Attack hits multiple opponents, the effect is triggered multiple times according to the number of opponents hit. The number of times that the effect is triggered is counted independently for each member of the party with Flying Cloud Flag Formation.

Passive talent: True to Oneself

Using Opening Flourish at the precise moment when Yun Jin is attacked will unleash its Level 2 Charged (Hold) form.

Passive talent: Breaking Conventions

The Normal Attack DMG Bonus granted by Flying Cloud Flag Formation is further increased by 2.5%/5%/7.5%/11.5% of Yun Jin's DEF when the party contains characters of 1/2/3/4 Elemental Types, respectively.

Passive talent: Light Nourishment

When Perfect Cooking is achieved on Food with Adventure-related effects, there is a 12% chance to obtain double the product.

Ningguang Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

Ningguang build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Lost Prayer to the Sacred Winds, Kagura's Verity).

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Role: Main DPS

Ningguang is an ATK scaling Geo DPS who deals ranged single target damage through Charged Attacks, Jade Screen, and her Burst. She excels in flexible quickswap teams and benefits greatly from Geo Resonance and shielding.

Best weapons for Ningguang

  1. Lost Prayer to the Sacred Winds: Strong CRIT Rate and high Base ATK; gains more Elemental DMG during longer on-field windows. Passive is weaker in quickswap teams.
  2. Kagura's Verity: High CRIT DMG and useful Skill damage; stronger when multiple Jade Screen casts build passive stacks. More effective with C2 and longer rotations.
  3. Skyward Atlas: High Base ATK, ATK% and unconditional Elemental DMG make it a reliable choice. Especially useful without large external ATK buffs.
  4. Memory of Dust: Large ATK bonuses when shielded; pairs naturally with Zhongli or reliable Crystallize shields. Requires sustained shield uptime.
  5. Solar Pearl: CRIT Rate plus Skill/Burst bonuses after Normal Attacks. Does not buff Charged Attacks.
  6. The Widsith: Strong CRIT DMG and occasional burst windows, but the random EM buff provides little value. Short field time can favor its cooldown timing.
  7. Flowing Purity: Craftable ATK% catalyst with Elemental DMG bonuses after Skill use once its Bond of Life is cleared. Needs effective healing to clear Bond of Life.
  8. Dodoco Tales: Free event catalyst supporting Charged Attack damage after Normal Attacks. Only available to players who obtained the past event weapon.

Best artifacts for Ningguang

  • Nighttime Whispers in the Echoing Woods (4 pcs) · Best Overall: Best general DPS choice with shield uptime: Skill use and Crystallize shielding boost Geo DMG. Main stats: ATK% / Geo DMG / CRIT.
  • Marechaussee Hunter (4 pcs) · With Furina: Strong with Furina: HP changes grant up to 36% CRIT Rate; avoid overcapping CRIT. Main stats: ATK% / Geo DMG / CRIT DMG.
  • Archaic Petra + Gladiator's Finale (2 + 2 pcs) · Flexible Alt: Straightforward Geo DMG and ATK bonuses without shield or HP-change requirements. Main stats: ATK% / Geo DMG / CRIT.

Main stats

  • Sands: ATK%
  • Goblet: Geo DMG Bonus
  • Circlet: Crit Rate / Crit DMG

Stat targets

  • ATK: 1,800 - 2,200
  • Crit Rate: 65% - 75%
  • Crit DMG: 130% - 180%
  • Energy Recharge: 110% - 140%

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 9+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 8+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 9+

Best teams for Ningguang

  • Jade Chamber Elite: Ningguang (Main DPS), Chiori (Sub DPS), Nicole (Shield), Fischl (Sub DPS). Nicole shields and buffs ATK, Chiori adds off-field Geo damage, and Fischl supplies Electro damage for Crystallize and extra DPS.
  • Golden Fireworks: Ningguang (Main DPS), Zhongli (Shield), Bennett (Buffer), Xiangling (Sub DPS). Zhongli protects and shreds RES, Bennett boosts ATK, and Xiangling adds Pyro damage and Pyro Resonance.
  • Gemstone Tide: Ningguang (Main DPS), Furina (Sub DPS), Yelan (Sub DPS), Xilonen (Healer). Furina and Yelan add strong Hydro damage and buffs, while Xilonen heals and shreds RES. Ningguang drives the team.
  • Budget Brilliance: Ningguang (Main DPS), Kachina (Sub DPS), Bennett (Healer), Fischl (Sub DPS). Kachina enables Geo Resonance and Scroll buffs, Bennett heals and buffs ATK, and Fischl adds off-field damage.

Pros

  • Strong single-target damage
  • Flexible rotations
  • Low-cost Burst
  • Geo Resonance synergy

Cons

  • Limited AoE damage
  • Wants ATK buffs
  • Projectiles can miss
  • Shield-dependent sets

Build variations

On-field Geo Carry (On-field DPS)

Use regular Charged Attacks and Burst damage.

  • Weapons: Lost Prayer to the Sacred Winds, Memory of Dust, Solar Pearl
  • Artifacts: Nighttime Whispers in the Echoing Woods (4 pcs); Archaic Petra + Gladiator's Finale (2 + 2 pcs)
  • Stats: ATK% / Geo DMG / CRIT.
  • Teams: Geo resonance or strong ATK buffers.
  • When it applies: Shield or Crystallize access improves Nighttime Whispers.

Furina CRIT Setup (On-field DPS)

Marechaussee works as an HP-change alternative.

  • Weapons: Lost Prayer to the Sacred Winds, Solar Pearl
  • Artifacts: Marechaussee Hunter (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Reduce sheet CRIT Rate to account for stacks.
  • Teams: Furina and reliable team healing.
  • When it applies: Requires consistent HP fluctuation.

Ningguang talents

Normal Attack: Sparkling Scatter

Normal Attack
Shoots gems that deal Geo DMG.
Upon hit, this grants Ningguang 1 Star Jade.

Charged Attack
Consumes a certain amount of stamina to fire off a giant gem that deals Geo DMG.
If Ningguang has any Star Jades, unleashing a Charged Attack will cause the Star Jades to be fired at the enemy as well, dealing additional DMG.

Plunging Attack
Gathering the might of Geo, Ningguang plunges towards the ground from mid-air, damaging all opponents in her path. Deals AoE Geo DMG upon impact with the ground.

Elemental Skill: Jade Screen

Cooldown: 12s

Ningguang creates a Jade Screen out of gold, obsidian, and her great opulence, dealing AoE Geo DMG.

Jade Screen
·Blocks opponents' projectiles.
·Endurance scales based on Ningguang's Max HP.

Jade Screen is considered a Geo Construct and can be used to block certain attacks, but cannot be climbed. Only one Jade Screen may exist at any one time.

Elemental Burst: Starshatter

Cooldown: 12s · Energy cost: 40

Gathering a great number of gems, Ningguang scatters them all at once, sending homing projectiles at her opponents that deal massive Geo DMG.
If Starshatter is cast when a Jade Screen is nearby, the Jade Screen will fire additional gem projectiles at the same time.

Passive talent: Backup Plan

When Ningguang is in possession of Star Jades, her Charged Attack does not consume Stamina.

Passive talent: Strategic Reserve

A character that passes through the Jade Screen will gain a 12% Geo DMG Bonus for 10s.

Passive talent: Trove of Marvelous Treasures

Displays the location of nearby ore veins used in forging on the mini-map.

Ningguang constellations

C1: Piercing Fragments

When a Normal Attack hits, it deals AoE DMG.

C2: Shock Effect

When Jade Screen is shattered, its CD will reset.
Can occur once every 6s.

C3: Majesty Be the Array of Stars

Increases the Level of Starshatter by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C4: Exquisite be the Jade, Outshining All Beneath

Jade Screen increases nearby characters' Elemental RES by 10%.

C5: Invincible Be the Jade Screen

Increases the Level of Jade Screen by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C6: Grandeur Be the Seven Stars

When Starshatter is used, Ningguang gains 7 Star Jades.

Role: Main DPS

Ningguang is an ATK scaling Geo DPS who deals ranged single target damage through Charged Attacks, Jade Screen, and her Burst. She excels in flexible quickswap teams and benefits greatly from Geo Resonance and shielding.

Best weapons for Ningguang

  1. Lost Prayer to the Sacred Winds
  2. Kagura's Verity
  3. Skyward Atlas
  4. Memory of Dust
  5. Solar Pearl
  6. The Widsith
  7. Flowing Purity
  8. Dodoco Tales

Best artifacts

  • Nighttime Whispers in the Echoing Woods (4 pcs) · Best Overall
  • Marechaussee Hunter (4 pcs) · With Furina
  • Archaic Petra + Gladiator's Finale (2 + 2 pcs) · Flexible Alt

Main stats

  • Sands: ATK%
  • Goblet: Geo DMG Bonus
  • Circlet: Crit Rate / Crit DMG

Stat targets

  • ATK: 1,800 - 2,200
  • Crit Rate: 65% - 75%
  • Crit DMG: 130% - 180%
  • Energy Recharge: 110% - 140%

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 9+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 8+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 9+

Best teams

  • Jade Chamber Elite: Ningguang, Chiori, Nicole, Fischl
  • Golden Fireworks: Ningguang, Zhongli, Bennett, Xiangling
  • Gemstone Tide: Ningguang, Furina, Yelan, Xilonen
  • Budget Brilliance: Ningguang, Kachina, Bennett, Fischl

Pros

  • Strong single-target damage
  • Flexible rotations
  • Low-cost Burst
  • Geo Resonance synergy

Cons

  • Wants ATK buffs
  • Projectiles can miss
  • Shield-dependent sets

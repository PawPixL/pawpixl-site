Eula Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

Eula build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Song of Broken Pines, Beacon of the Reed Sea), artifacts (Pale Flame), main stats, talent priority.

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Role: Main DPS

Eula is an on-field Physical DPS who builds Lightfall Sword stacks before a massive Burst detonation. Electro teammates enable Superconduct to lower Physical RES, while Cryo batteries help fund her costly Burst. Keep her on-field through the explosion when using Mika’s buffs.

Best weapons for Eula

  1. Song of Broken Pines: High base ATK, Physical DMG and team ATK SPD synergize with her Normal Attacks and Lightfall stacking. Reliable all-round signature; speed changes combos.
  2. Beacon of the Reed Sea: CR and ATK buffs make this a top contender, sometimes stronger than her signature.
  3. Wolf's Gravestone: Huge ATK and an enemy-low-HP team ATK buff strengthen her Burst and Normal Attacks.
  4. The Unforged: ATK stat and shield-enhanced ATK stacks offer strong DMG when protected.
  5. Skyward Pride: Energy Recharge eases Burst uptime; its vacuum blades add Physical damage during normal attacks. Excellent if struggling to fund her Burst.
  6. Serpent Spine: CRIT Rate and stacked DMG bonus provide excellent sustained damage at higher refinements. A shielder helps preserve stacks. High refinement improves value.
  7. Akuoumaru: Raises Burst damage based on the party’s combined Energy costs, rewarding Burst-heavy teams. Use in high team-Burst-cost compositions. R5 particularly valuable.
  8. Tidal Shadow: Craftable ATK weapon whose passive grants more ATK after Eula receives healing. Requires reliable healing and passive uptime. Refinements improve ATK buff.

Best artifacts for Eula

  • Pale Flame (4 pcs): Best general Physical build: Skill hits stack ATK and double the 2-piece Physical DMG bonus. Main stats: ATK% · Physical DMG · CRIT.
  • Pale Flame + Bloodstained Chivalry (2 + 2 pcs): Unconditional 50% Physical DMG bonus, easier to use when 4-piece Pale Flame stacks are inconsistent. Main stats: ATK% · Physical DMG · CRIT.
  • Marechaussee Hunter (4 pcs): With Furina’s HP changes, gains up to 36% CRIT Rate, making damage stats easier to balance. Main stats: ATK% · Physical DMG · CRIT (Furina only).

Main stats

  • Sands: ATK% / Energy Recharge (if needed)
  • Goblet: Physical DMG
  • Circlet: CRIT Rate / CRIT DMG

Stat targets

  • CRIT Rate: 65% - 80% (Before buffs)
  • CRIT DMG: 140% - 200%+
  • Energy Recharge: 120% - 160%
  • ATK: 1,800 - 2,400 (Wep dependent)
  • Marechaussee: ★ 40% - 64%

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 8-9+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 6-8
  • Elemental Burst: Level 8-9+

Best teams for Eula

  • Keomi Fox Vengeance: Eula (On-field DPS), Raiden Shogun (On-field DPS / Battery), Furina (Off-field DPS / Buffer), Mika (Healer / Physical Buffer). Keomi Fox brings the vengeance! Raiden enables Superconduct and energy; Furina buffs damage; Mika heals and boosts Physical attacks.
  • Frostbound Retribution: Eula (On-field DPS), Raiden Shogun (On-field DPS / Battery), Rosaria (Off-field DPS / CRIT Buffer), Zhongli (Shielder / Debuffer). Raiden enables Superconduct, Rosaria supplies Cryo energy and CRIT support, and Zhongli shields Eula during Burst stacking.
  • Knights of High Heels and Mika: Eula (On-field DPS), Fischl (Off-field DPS / Battery), Rosaria (Off-field DPS / CRIT Buffer), Mika (Healer / Physical Buffer). Fischl maintains Superconduct, Rosaria helps battery Eula, and Mika provides healing and Physical buffs.
  • Budget Icebreaker: Eula (On-field DPS), Fischl (Off-field DPS / Battery), Diona (Shielder / Healer), Lisa (Electro DPS / Debuffer). Fischl enables Superconduct; Diona shields, heals, and batteries; Lisa offers DEF reduction with her Burst and Thrilling Tales support.

Pros

  • Strong Physical attacks

  • Powerful at C0

Cons

  • High energy demands
  • Burst can miss

Build variations

Furina Marechaussee (On-field Physical DPS)

When Furina is on the team and her HP fluctuations reliably maintain Marechaussee Hunter stacks.

  • Weapons: Song of Broken Pines, Beacon of the Reed Sea, Serpent Spine
  • Artifacts: Marechaussee Hunter (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Target 40–64% sheet CRIT Rate before the 36% set bonus; favor CRIT DMG after reaching effective CRIT cap.
  • Teams: Furina plus a reliable healer such as Mika; an Electro teammate for Superconduct.
  • When it applies: Requires consistent HP changes.
  • Notes: Mika’s buffs need Eula to stay on-field through the Burst detonation.

Shielded Physical Carry (On-field Physical DPS)

Best for comfortable Burst stacking and maintaining Serpent Spine stacks or The Unforged shield condition.

  • Weapons: The Unforged, Serpent Spine, Song of Broken Pines
  • Artifacts: Pale Flame (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Balance ATK and CRIT; ensure enough ER despite the lack of an ER weapon.
  • Teams: Bring Zhongli, Diona, or another reliable shielder plus an Electro Superconduct enabler.
  • When it applies: Shield uptime must cover Eula’s combo.
  • Notes: Shielding improves consistency but does not remove her energy requirements.

Energy-Friendly Burst (On-field Physical DPS)

When inconsistent Burst uptime is a bigger damage loss than dropping some offensive substats.

  • Weapons: Skyward Pride, Akuoumaru, Tidal Shadow
  • Artifacts: Pale Flame (4 pcs); Pale Flame + Bloodstained Chivalry (2 + 2 pcs)
  • Stats: Use ER Sands if necessary; often 140–160%+ ER on teams with weak Cryo battery support.
  • Teams: An Electro teammate and preferably a Cryo battery such as Rosaria or Diona.
  • When it applies: ER varies greatly with teammates and Favonius procs.
  • Notes: Prioritize getting Burst every rotation over chasing a fixed ATK target.

Craftable F2P (On-field Physical DPS)

For low-spender or F2P teams seeking a reliable craftable weapon and accessible teammates.

  • Weapons: Tidal Shadow
  • Artifacts: Pale Flame (4 pcs); Pale Flame + Bloodstained Chivalry (2 + 2 pcs)
  • Stats: ATK% / Physical DMG / CRIT; build ER as needed for consistent Burst uptime.
  • Teams: A healer to activate Tidal Shadow, Electro for Superconduct, and ideally a Cryo battery.
  • When it applies: Tidal Shadow passive requires receiving healing.
  • Notes: Diona can provide both healing and Cryo energy.

Eula talents

Normal Attack: Favonius Bladework - Edel

Normal Attack
Performs up to 5 consecutive strikes.

Charged Attack
Drains Stamina over time to perform continuous slashes.
At the end of the sequence, performs a more powerful slash.

Plunging Attack
Plunges from mid-air to strike the ground, damaging opponents along the path and dealing AoE DMG upon impact.

Elemental Skill: Icetide Vortex

Cooldown: Press: 4s / Hold: 10s

Sharp frost, swift blade.

Press
Slashes swiftly, dealing Cryo DMG.
When it hits an opponent, Eula gains a stack of Grimheart that stacks up to 2 times. These stacks can only be gained once every 0.3s.

Grimheart
Increases Eula's resistance to interruption and DEF.

Hold
Wielding her sword, Eula consumes all the stacks of Grimheart and lashes forward, dealing AoE Cryo DMG to opponents in front of her.
If Grimheart stacks are consumed, surrounding opponents will have their Physical RES and Cryo RES decreased.
Each consumed stack of Grimheart will be converted into an Icewhirl Brand that deals Cryo DMG to nearby opponents.

Elemental Burst: Glacial Illumination

Cooldown: 20s · Energy cost: 80

Brandishes her greatsword, dealing Cryo DMG to nearby opponents and creating a Lightfall Sword that follows her around for a duration of up to 7s.
While present, the Lightfall Sword increases Eula's resistance to interruption. When Eula's own Normal Attack, Elemental Skill, and Elemental Burst deal DMG to opponents, they will charge the Lightfall Sword, which can gain an energy stack once every 0.1s.
Once its duration ends, the Lightfall Sword will descend and explode violently, dealing Physical DMG to nearby opponents.
This DMG scales on the number of energy stacks the Lightfall Sword has accumulated.
If Eula leaves the field, the Lightfall Sword will explode immediately.

Passive talent: Roiling Rime

If 2 stacks of Grimheart are consumed upon unleashing the Holding Mode of Icetide Vortex, a Shattered Lightfall Sword will be created that will explode immediately, dealing 50% of the basic Physical DMG dealt by a Lightfall Sword created by Glacial Illumination.

Passive talent: Wellspring of War-Lust

When Glacial Illumination is cast, the CD of Icetide Vortex is reset and Eula gains 1 stack of Grimheart.

Passive talent: Aristocratic Introspection

When Eula crafts Character Talent Materials, she has a 10% chance to receive double the product.

Eula constellations

C1: Tidal Illusion

Every time Icetide Vortex's Grimheart stacks are consumed, Eula's Physical DMG is increased by 30% for 6s.
Each stack consumed will increase the duration of this effect by 6s up to a maximum of 18s.

C2: Lady of Seafoam

Decreases the CD of Icetide Vortex's Holding Mode, rendering it identical to Press CD.

C3: Lawrence Pedigree

Increases the Level of Glacial Illumination by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C4: The Obstinacy of One's Inferiors

Lightfall Swords deal 25% increased DMG against opponents with less than 50% HP.

C5: Chivalric Quality

Increases the Level of Icetide Vortex by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C6: Noble Obligation

Lightfall Swords created by Glacial Illumination start with 5 stacks of energy. Normal Attacks, Elemental Skills, and Elemental Bursts have a 50% chance to grant the Lightfall Sword an additional stack of energy.

Eula Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts & Teams

Eula build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Song of Broken Pines, Beacon of the Reed Sea), artifacts (Pale Flame), main stats (ATK% / Energy…

Role: Main DPS

Eula is an on-field Physical DPS who builds Lightfall Sword stacks before a massive Burst detonation. Electro teammates enable Superconduct to lower Physical RES, while Cryo batteries help fund her costly Burst. Keep her on-field through the explosion when using Mika’s buffs.

Best weapons for Eula

  1. Song of Broken Pines
  2. Beacon of the Reed Sea
  3. Wolf's Gravestone
  4. The Unforged
  5. Skyward Pride
  6. Serpent Spine
  7. Akuoumaru
  8. Tidal Shadow

Best artifacts

  • Pale Flame (4 pcs)
  • Pale Flame + Bloodstained Chivalry (2 + 2 pcs)
  • Marechaussee Hunter (4 pcs)

Main stats

  • Sands: ATK% / Energy Recharge (if needed)
  • Goblet: Physical DMG
  • Circlet: CRIT Rate / CRIT DMG

Stat targets

  • CRIT Rate: 65% - 80% (Before buffs)
  • CRIT DMG: 140% - 200%+
  • Energy Recharge: 120% - 160%
  • ATK: 1,800 - 2,400 (Wep dependent)
  • Marechaussee: ★ 40% - 64%

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 8-9+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 6-8
  • Elemental Burst: Level 8-9+

Best teams

  • Keomi Fox Vengeance: Eula, Raiden Shogun, Furina, Mika
  • Frostbound Retribution: Eula, Raiden Shogun, Rosaria, Zhongli
  • Knights of High Heels and Mika: Eula, Fischl, Rosaria, Mika
  • Budget Icebreaker: Eula, Fischl, Diona, Lisa

Pros

  • Huge Burst finishers
  • Strong Physical attacks
  • Superconduct synergy
  • Powerful at C0

Cons

  • Backloaded Burst damage
  • High energy demands
  • Burst can miss
  • Physical-resistant foes

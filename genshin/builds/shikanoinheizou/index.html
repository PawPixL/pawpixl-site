Shikanoin Heizou Build Guide: Weapons, Artifacts and Teams (7.2)

Shikanoin Heizou build guide for Genshin Impact 7.2: best weapons (Skyward Atlas, Kagura's Verity), artifacts (Viridescent Venerer), main stats.

Last updated: November 12, 2026 · Game version 7.2

Role: Anemo DPS

Heizou is an Anemo catalyst DPS who combines rapid attacks with devastating Elemental Skill punches. He excels as an on-field reaction driver or a Skill-focused damage dealer.

Best weapons for Shikanoin Heizou

  1. Skyward Atlas: High Base ATK and ATK% scaling make Skyward Atlas an excellent DPS weapon for Heizou.
  2. Kagura's Verity: High CRIT DMG and stacking Skill damage after using Skill; best with repeated Skill casts.
  3. A Thousand Floating Dreams: Provides massive EM and boosts reaction DMG.
  4. The Widsith: Provides substantial CD and powerful randomized buffs.
  5. Sacrificial Fragments: Elemental Mastery and a chance to reset Skill cooldown; extra casts depend on Skill hits.
  6. Solar Pearl: Provides valuable CR and boosts Normal Attack, Skill, and Burst DMG.
  7. Mappa Mare: Refines give Elemental Mastery and boost elemental DMG after triggering a reaction.
  8. Wandering Evenstar: Battle Pass option that boosts Skill, Burst, and Normal Attack DMG after hits.

Best artifacts for Shikanoin Heizou

  • Viridescent Venerer (4 pcs) · Anemo DPS: Boosts Swirl DMG and reduces enemies' resistance to the elements Swirled. Main stats: ATK% · Anemo DMG · CR/CD.
  • Golden Troupe (4 pcs) · Quickswap: Boosts Skill DMG significantly, making it a strong alternative for Heizou's Skill-focused DPS build.
  • Viridescent Venerer + Gladiator's Finale (2 + 2 pcs) · Anemo DPS: Anemo DMG plus ATK for personal damage. Temporary 2-piece mix; 4-piece VV gives better team utility.

Main stats

  • Sands: EM / ATK%
  • Goblet: EM / Anemo DMG
  • Circlet: EM / Crit Rate / Crit DMG

Stat targets

  • CRIT Rate: 65% - 75%
  • CRIT DMG: 130% - 160%
  • ATK: 1,800 - 2,200
  • EM: 100 - 250 (CRIT build)
  • EM: 700 - 900+ (Swirl build)

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 9+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 9+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 8+

Best teams for Shikanoin Heizou

  • The Final Suspect: Shikanoin Heizou (Main DPS), Faruzan (Support), Yelan (Sub DPS), Bennett (Support). Faruzan amplifies Heizou's Anemo damage while Yelan provides powerful off-field damage and Bennett boosts ATK and provides healing. A strong hypercarry team built around maximizing Heizou's powerful Skill hits.
  • Anemo Authority: Shikanoin Heizou (Main DPS), Xianyun (Support), Furina (Buffs), Bennett (Support). Xianyun enables powerful Plunging Attacks while boosting their damage, Furina provides off-field Hydro damage and a teamwide DMG bonus, and Bennett adds healing and ATK buffs. A strong plunge-focused Heizou DPS team.
  • Anemo-nomicon: Shikanoin Heizou (Main DPS), Fischl (Sub DPS), Xingqiu (Sub DPS), Ineffa (Support). Fischl and Ineffa provide consistent off-field Electro damage while Xingqiu adds Hydro application and defensive utility. Heizou drives powerful Electro-Charged reactions while keeping the team swirling.
  • CSI: Mondstadt: Shikanoin Heizou (Main DPS), Xingqiu (Sub DPS), Xiangling (Sub DPS), Bennett (Support). Xingqiu and Xiangling provide powerful off-field Hydro and Pyro damage while Bennett supplies healing and ATK buffs. Heizou drives constant reactions and Swirls, amplifying the team's damage with Viridescent Venerer.

Pros

  • Strong Skill nukes
  • Excellent reaction driver
  • Flexible Team Options
  • Fun playstyle

Cons

  • Requires Skill stacks
  • Limited crowd control
  • Limited personal damage ceiling

Build variations

Anemo Skill Nuke (On-field DPS)

Focus on his large Elemental Skill hit and personal Anemo damage.

  • Weapons: Skyward Atlas, Kagura's Verity, The Widsith
  • Artifacts: Golden Troupe (4 pcs); Viridescent Venerer + Gladiator's Finale (2 + 2 pcs)
  • Stats: ATK% / Anemo DMG / CRIT; balance ER.
  • Teams: Off-field elements to stack Declension.
  • When it applies: Golden Troupe benefits Skill hits, not his whole kit.

Swirl Driver (On-field Driver)

Use Viridescent Venerer to amplify teammates through Swirl.

  • Weapons: A Thousand Floating Dreams, Sacrificial Fragments
  • Artifacts: Viridescent Venerer (4 pcs)
  • Stats: EM main stats and sufficient ER; CRIT secondary.
  • Teams: Off-field damage dealers and Swirlable auras.
  • When it applies: Needs elements that can be Swirled.

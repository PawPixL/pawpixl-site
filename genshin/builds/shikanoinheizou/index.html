Shikanoin Heizou Build Guide: Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

Shikanoin Heizou build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Skyward Atlas, Kagura's Verity), artifacts (Viridescent Venerer), main stats.

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Role: Anemo DPS

Heizou is an Anemo catalyst DPS who combines rapid attacks with devastating Elemental Skill punches. He excels as an on-field reaction driver or a Skill-focused damage dealer.

Best weapons for Shikanoin Heizou

  1. Skyward Atlas: High Base ATK and ATK% scaling make Skyward Atlas an excellent DPS weapon for Heizou.
  2. Kagura's Verity: High CRIT DMG and stacking Skill damage after using Skill; best with repeated Skill casts.
  3. A Thousand Floating Dreams: Provides massive EM and boosts reaction DMG.
  4. The Widsith: Provides substantial CD and powerful randomized buffs.
  5. Sacrificial Fragments: Elemental Mastery and a chance to reset Skill cooldown; extra casts depend on Skill hits.
  6. Solar Pearl: Provides valuable CR and boosts Normal Attack, Skill, and Burst DMG.
  7. Mappa Mare: Refines give Elemental Mastery and boost elemental DMG after triggering a reaction.
  8. Wandering Evenstar: Battle Pass option that boosts Skill, Burst, and Normal Attack DMG after hits.

Best artifacts for Shikanoin Heizou

  • Viridescent Venerer (4 pcs) · Anemo DPS: Boosts Swirl DMG and reduces enemies' resistance to the elements Swirled. Main stats: ATK% · Anemo DMG · CR/CD.
  • Golden Troupe (4 pcs) · Quickswap: Boosts Skill DMG significantly, making it a strong alternative for Heizou's Skill-focused DPS build.
  • Viridescent Venerer + Gladiator's Finale (2 + 2 pcs) · Anemo DPS: Anemo DMG plus ATK for personal damage. Temporary 2-piece mix; 4-piece VV gives better team utility.

Main stats

Best teams for Shikanoin Heizou

  • The Final Suspect: Shikanoin Heizou (Main DPS), Faruzan (Support), Yelan (Sub DPS), Bennett (Support). Faruzan amplifies Heizou's Anemo damage while Yelan provides powerful off-field damage and Bennett boosts ATK and provides healing. A strong hypercarry team built around maximizing Heizou's powerful Skill hits.
  • Anemo Authority: Shikanoin Heizou (Main DPS), Xianyun (Support), Furina (Buffs), Bennett (Support). Xianyun enables powerful Plunging Attacks while boosting their damage, Furina provides off-field Hydro damage and a teamwide DMG bonus, and Bennett adds healing and ATK buffs. A strong plunge-focused Heizou DPS team.
  • Anemo-nomicon: Shikanoin Heizou (Main DPS), Fischl (Sub DPS), Xingqiu (Sub DPS), Ineffa (Support). Fischl and Ineffa provide consistent off-field Electro damage while Xingqiu adds Hydro application and defensive utility. Heizou drives powerful Electro-Charged reactions while keeping the team swirling.
  • CSI: Mondstadt: Shikanoin Heizou (Main DPS), Xingqiu (Sub DPS), Xiangling (Sub DPS), Bennett (Support). Xingqiu and Xiangling provide powerful off-field Hydro and Pyro damage while Bennett supplies healing and ATK buffs. Heizou drives constant reactions and Swirls, amplifying the team's damage with Viridescent Venerer.

Build variations

Anemo Skill Nuke (On-field DPS)

Focus on his large Elemental Skill hit and personal Anemo damage.

  • Weapons: Skyward Atlas, Kagura's Verity, The Widsith
  • Artifacts: Golden Troupe (4 pcs); Viridescent Venerer + Gladiator's Finale (2 + 2 pcs)
  • Stats: ATK% / Anemo DMG / CRIT; balance ER.
  • Teams: Off-field elements to stack Declension.
  • When it applies: Golden Troupe benefits Skill hits, not his whole kit.

Swirl Driver (On-field Driver)

Use Viridescent Venerer to amplify teammates through Swirl.

  • Weapons: A Thousand Floating Dreams, Sacrificial Fragments
  • Artifacts: Viridescent Venerer (4 pcs)
  • Stats: EM main stats and sufficient ER; CRIT secondary.
  • Teams: Off-field damage dealers and Swirlable auras.
  • When it applies: Needs elements that can be Swirled.

Shikanoin Heizou talents

Normal Attack: Fudou Style Martial Arts

Normal Attack
Performs up to 5 fisticuffs empowered by a mighty wind, dealing Anemo DMG.

Charged Attack
Consumes a certain amount of Stamina and performs a sweeping kick that deals Anemo DMG.

Plunging Attack
Calling upon the surging wind, Heizou plunges towards the ground from mid-air, damaging all opponents in his path. Deals AoE Anemo DMG upon impact with the ground.

Elemental Skill: Heartstopper Strike

Cooldown: 10s

Tap
Wields the swift winds to launch a Heartstopper Strike that deals Anemo DMG.

Hold
Charges energy to unleash an even stronger blow. He will obtain the Declension effect while charging, which will increase the power of the Heartstopper Strike. When the skill button is released or the skill finishes charging, he will strike forward, dealing Anemo DMG.

Declension
Increases the power of the next Heartstopper Strike. Max 4 stacks.
When you possess 4 Declension stacks, the Conviction effect will be produced, which will cause the next Heartstopper Strike to be even stronger and have a larger AoE.

Elemental Burst: Windmuster Kick

Cooldown: 12s · Energy cost: 40

Leaps into the air and uses the Fudou Style Vacuum Slugger and kicks his opponent. The Vacuum Slugger will explode upon hit and create an Arresting Windtunnel that pulls in nearby objects and opponents, dealing AoE Anemo DMG.
When Vacuum Slugger hits opponents affected by Hydro/Pyro/Cryo/Electro, these opponents will be afflicted with Windmuster Iris. This Windmuster Iris will explode after a moment and dissipate, dealing AoE DMG of the corresponding aforementioned elemental type.
Vacuum Slugger can afflict a maximum of 4 opponents with the Windmuster Iris. A single opponent cannot be under the effect of Windmuster Irises of different elements at the same time.

Passive talent: Paradoxical Practice

When Shikanoin Heizou activates a Swirl or Stellar Swirl reaction while on the field, he will gain 1 Declension stack for Heartstopper Strike. This effect can be triggered once every 0.1s.

Passive talent: Penetrative Reasoning

After Shikanoin Heizou's Heartstopper Strike hits an opponent, increases all party members' (excluding Shikanoin Heizou) Elemental Mastery by 80 for 10s.

Passive talent: Pre-Existing Guilt

Decreases sprinting Stamina consumption for your own party members by 20%.
Not stackable with Passive Talents that provide the exact same effects.

Shikanoin Heizou constellations

C1: Named Juvenile Casebook

For 5s after Shikanoin Heizou takes the field, his Normal Attack SPD is increased by 15%. He also gains 1 Declension stack for Heartstopper Strike. This effect can be triggered once every 10s.

C2: Investigative Collection

The pull effect of the Arresting Windtunnel created by Windmuster Kick is enhanced, and its duration is increased to 1s.

C3: Esoteric Puzzle Book

Increases the Level of Heartstopper Strike by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C4: Tome of Lies

The first Windmuster Iris explosion in each Windmuster Kick will regenerate 9 Elemental Energy for Shikanoin Heizou. Every subsequent explosion in that Windmuster Kick will each regenerate an additional 1.5 Energy for Heizou.
One Windmuster Kick can regenerate a total of 13.5 Energy for Heizou in this manner.

C5: Secret Archive

Increases the Level of Windmuster Kick by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C6: Curious Casefiles

Each Declension stack will increase the CRIT Rate of the Heartstopper Strike unleashed by 4%. When Heizou possesses Conviction, this Heartstopper Strike's CRIT DMG is increased by 32%.

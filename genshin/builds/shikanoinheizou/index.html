Role: Anemo DPS

Heizou is an Anemo catalyst DPS who combines rapid attacks with devastating Elemental Skill punches. He excels as an on-field reaction driver or a Skill-focused damage dealer.

Pros

  • Flexible Team Options
  • Fun playstyle

Cons

  • Limited crowd control
  • Limited personal damage ceiling

Build variations

  • Anemo Skill Nuke (On-field DPS): Focus on his large Elemental Skill hit and personal Anemo damage.
  • Swirl Driver (On-field Driver): Use Viridescent Venerer to amplify teammates through Swirl.

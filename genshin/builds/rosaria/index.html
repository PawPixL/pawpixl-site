Rosaria Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

Rosaria build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Staff of the Scarlet Sands, Staff of Homa), artifacts (Gilded Dreams), main stats, talent priority.

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Role: Support

Rosaria is a Cryo polearm Sub-DPS who provides consistent off-field Cryo damage, strong Burst support, and a valuable CRIT Rate buff for the team. She shares up to 15% CRIT Rate with her passive.

Best weapons for Rosaria

  1. Staff of the Scarlet Sands: Provides massive CRIT Rate and converts Elemental Mastery into ATK, making it an exceptional choice for Rosaria's Reverse Melt build.
  2. Staff of Homa: Provides substantial CRIT DMG and ATK, making it a powerful universal option for Rosaria's personal damage across multiple team types.
  3. Fractured Halo: CRIT DMG and ATK after Skill or Burst. Its Lunar-Charged party bonus requires the wielder to create a shield. Shield-creation passive unavailable.
  4. Calamity Queller: High Base ATK and Elemental DMG bonus with stacking ATK after Skill, including off-field.
  5. Wavebreaker's Fin: High Base ATK and Burst damage scaling with team Energy costs; better at high refinements.
  6. Dragon's Bane: Elemental Mastery and extra damage against Pyro/Hydro-affected enemies; situational.
  7. Missive Windspear: Event ATK polearm that raises ATK and EM after triggering reactions.
  8. "The Catch": Provides Energy Recharge, Burst DMG, and Burst CRIT Rate, making it a strong free option for Rosaria's Burst-focused damage and support builds.

Best artifacts for Rosaria

  • Gilded Dreams (4 pcs) · Reverse Melt: Strong for Reverse Melt at manageable ER needs; boosts EM and ATK after reactions. Not a universal best set. Main stats: CRIT Rate > CRIT DMG > EM > ER > ATK%.
  • Blizzard Strayer (4 pcs) · Freeze / Sub DPS: Best in Freeze teams. Grants substantial CRIT Rate against Cryo-affected or Frozen enemies; avoid CRIT overcap. Main stats: CRIT Rate > CRIT DMG > ATK% > ER.
  • Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs) · Support: Best general team-buff option for ATK-scaling allies when no teammate already uses it. Main stats: CRIT Rate > ER > CRIT DMG > ATK%.

Main stats

  • Sands: Elemental Mastery / ATK%
  • Goblet: Cryo DMG
  • Circlet: Crit Rate / Crit DMG

Stat targets

  • CR: Tune to team and set
  • CD: Balance after CR
  • ER: Depends on rotation
  • EM: For Reverse Melt

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 9+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 9+

Best teams for Rosaria

  • Melt & Mayhem: Rosaria (Off-field DPS / CRIT Buffer), Mavuika (On-field DPS), Citlali (Shielder / Buffer), Bennett (Healer / Buffer). Rosaria and Citlali provide Cryo application for Mavuika's Melt reactions. Bennett adds healing, ATK buffs, and Pyro Resonance.
  • Cryo Queens: Rosaria (Off-field DPS / CRIT Buffer), Skirk (On-field DPS), Furina (Off-field DPS / Buffer), Escoffier (Healer / Debuffer). Rosaria provides additional Cryo damage and CRIT Rate sharing while Furina supplies Hydro application and damage buffs. Escoffier provides healing and Cryo/Hydro RES reduction.
  • Ghosts & Vapors: Rosaria (Off-field DPS / CRIT Buffer), Hu Tao (On-field DPS), Xingqiu (Off-field DPS / Hydro Enabler), Zhongli (Shielder / Debuffer). Xingqiu enables Vaporize while Rosaria adds Cryo application for occasional Melt reactions. Zhongli provides shielding and resistance reduction.
  • Budget Blizzard: Rosaria (Cryo DPS / Battery), Kaeya (Off-field Cryo DPS), Xiangling (Pyro application), Bennett (ATK buffer / Healer). Rosaria and Kaeya provide consistent Cryo damage and application while Xiangling triggers Reverse Melt. Bennett supplies healing, ATK buffs, and energy support.

Build variations

Cryo Burst Support (Off-field DPS)

Noblesse supports team ATK while her Burst deals Cryo damage.

  • Weapons: Staff of Homa, Wavebreaker's Fin
  • Artifacts: Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs); Blizzard Strayer (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ER to Burst consistently; CRIT for personal hits and sharing.
  • Teams: Cryo damage or Physical teams using Cryo application.
  • When it applies: CRIT sharing does not apply to all snapshot cases.

Freeze Sub DPS (Off-field DPS)

Use Blizzard Strayer for Cryo-affected or Frozen targets.

  • Weapons: Staff of Homa, Wavebreaker's Fin
  • Artifacts: Blizzard Strayer (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Balance CRIT around Freeze bonuses and team buffs.
  • Teams: Hydro plus Cryo teams.
  • When it applies: Freeze is less reliable versus bosses.

Melt Quickswap (Off-field DPS)

Build for Rosaria’s personal reverse Melt damage.

  • Weapons: Staff of the Scarlet Sands, Dragon's Bane
  • Artifacts: Gilded Dreams (4 pcs)
  • Stats: EM helps only for Rosaria-triggered Melts; balance ER/CRIT.
  • Teams: Pyro aura and dependable Cryo Burst hits.
  • When it applies: Reaction ownership changes how much EM is useful.

Rosaria talents

Normal Attack: Spear of the Church

Normal Attack
Performs up to 5 consecutive spear strikes.

Charged Attack
Consumes a certain amount of Stamina to lunge forward, dealing damage to opponents along the way.

Plunging Attack
Plunges from mid-air to strike the ground below, damaging opponents along the path and dealing AoE DMG upon impact.

Elemental Skill: Ravaging Confession

Cooldown: 6s

Rosaria swiftly shifts her position to appear behind her opponent, then stabs and slashes them with her polearm, dealing Cryo DMG.
This ability cannot be used to travel behind opponents of a larger build.

Elemental Burst: Rites of Termination

Cooldown: 15s · Energy cost: 60

Rosaria's unique take on this prayer ritual: First, she swings her weapon to slash surrounding opponents; then, she summons a frigid Ice Lance that strikes the ground. Both actions deal Cryo DMG.
While active, the Ice Lance periodically releases a blast of cold air, dealing Cryo DMG to surrounding opponents.

Passive talent: Regina Probationum

When Rosaria strikes an opponent from behind using Ravaging Confession, Rosaria's CRIT Rate increases by 12% for 5s.

Passive talent: Shadow Samaritan

Casting Rites of Termination increases CRIT Rate of all nearby party members (except Rosaria herself) by 15% of Rosaria's CRIT Rate for 10s.
CRIT Rate Bonus gained this way cannot exceed 15%.

Passive talent: Night Walk

At night (18:00 – 06:00), your party members gain the Swift Stride effect: Movement SPD increased by 10%.
Does not take effect in Domains, Trounce Domains, or Spiral Abyss. Swift Stride does not stack.

Rosaria constellations

C1: Unholy Revelation

When Rosaria deals a CRIT Hit, her ATK SPD increases by 10% and her Normal Attack DMG increases by 10% for 4s.

C2: Land Without Promise

The duration of the Ice Lance created by Rites of Termination is increased by 4s.

C3: The Wages of Sin

Increases the Level of Ravaging Confession by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C4: Painful Grace

Ravaging Confession's CRIT Hits regenerate 5 Energy for Rosaria.
Can only be triggered once each time Ravaging Confession is cast.

C5: Last Rites

Increases the Level of Rites of Termination by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C6: Divine Retribution

Rites of Termination's attack decreases opponents' Physical RES by 20% for 10s.

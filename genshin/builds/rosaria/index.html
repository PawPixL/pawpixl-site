Rosaria Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts & Teams

Rosaria build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Staff of the Scarlet Sands, Staff of Homa), artifacts (Gilded Dreams), main stats (Elemental Mastery…

Role: Support

Rosaria is a Cryo polearm Sub-DPS who provides consistent off-field Cryo damage, strong Burst support, and a valuable CRIT Rate buff for the team. She shares up to 15% CRIT Rate with her passive.

Best weapons for Rosaria

  1. Staff of the Scarlet Sands
  2. Staff of Homa
  3. Fractured Halo
  4. Calamity Queller
  5. Wavebreaker's Fin
  6. Dragon's Bane
  7. Missive Windspear
  8. "The Catch"

Best artifacts

  • Gilded Dreams (4 pcs) · Reverse Melt
  • Blizzard Strayer (4 pcs) · Freeze / Sub DPS
  • Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs) · Support

Main stats

  • Sands: Elemental Mastery / ATK%
  • Goblet: Cryo DMG
  • Circlet: Crit Rate / Crit DMG

Stat targets

  • CR: Tune to team and set
  • CD: Balance after CR
  • ER: Depends on rotation
  • EM: For Reverse Melt

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 9+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 9+

Best teams

  • Melt & Mayhem: Rosaria, Mavuika, Citlali, Bennett
  • Cryo Queens: Rosaria, Skirk, Furina, Escoffier
  • Ghosts & Vapors: Rosaria, Hu Tao, Xingqiu, Zhongli
  • Budget Blizzard: Rosaria, Kaeya, Xiangling, Bennett

Pros

  • Off-field Cryo damage
  • CRIT Rate buff
  • Low field time
  • Flexible teams

Cons

  • Limited range
  • ER hungry
  • Limited application
  • Struggles vs. mobile enemies

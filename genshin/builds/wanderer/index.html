Wanderer Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts & Teams

Wanderer build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Tulaytullah's Remembrance, Cashflow Supervision), artifacts (Desert Pavilion Chronicle), main stats…

Role: Main DPS

Wanderer is a fast, mobile Anemo DPS who dominates from the air with rapid ranged attacks and flexible team options. As a totally unbiased Wanderer main, he’s best boy.

Best weapons for Wanderer

  1. Tulaytullah's Remembrance
  2. Cashflow Supervision
  3. Skyward Atlas
  4. Kagura's Verity
  5. Solar Pearl
  6. The Widsith
  7. Dodoco Tales
  8. Mappa Mare

Best artifacts

  • Desert Pavilion Chronicle (4 pcs)
  • Echoes of an Offering (4 pcs)
  • Marechaussee Hunter + Desert Pavilion Chronicle (4 pcs)

Main stats

  • Sands: ATK%
  • Goblet: Anemo DMG
  • Circlet: Crit Rate / Crit DMG

Stat targets

  • Crit Rate: 70% - 80%
  • Crit DMG: 160%+
  • ATK: 2,000+
  • Energy Recharge: 110% - 130%

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 10 👑
  • Elemental Skill: Level 9+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 9+

Best teams

  • PawPixL's Team: Wanderer, Nicole, Durin, Faruzan
  • Nora is Cool: Wanderer, Ineffa, Columbina, Faruzan
  • Mile High Club: Wanderer, Furina, Bennett, Faruzan
  • Spirit Airlines: Wanderer, Bennett, Xingqiu, Faruzan

Pros

  • Great mobility
  • Strong ranged DMG
  • Flexible teams
  • Amazing exploration

Cons

  • Easily interrupted
  • Wants Faruzan
  • Buff dependent
  • Limited AoE

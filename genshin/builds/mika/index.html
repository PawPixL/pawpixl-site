Mika Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams (7.2)

Mika build guide for Genshin Impact 7.2: best weapons (Symphonist of Scents, Favonius Lance), artifacts (Noblesse Oblige), main stats, talent priority.

Last updated: November 12, 2026 · Game version 7.2

Role: Support

Mika increases the active character's ATK SPD, boosts Physical DMG, and provides healing through his Elemental Burst. He's particularly valuable for Eula and other Physical DPS characters.

Best weapons for Mika

  1. Symphonist of Scents: Symphonist of Scents increases Mika's ATK and provides a teamwide ATK buff when he heals. It's a supportive option for ATK-scaling DPS teams.
  2. Favonius Lance: Provides Energy Recharge and generates extra particles for the team, helping Mika and his teammates maintain Burst uptime.
  3. Dialogues of the Desert Sages: Dialogues of the Desert Sages boosts Mika's HP and restores Energy when he heals, improving his healing while helping maintain Burst uptime.
  4. Engulfing Lightning: High Energy Recharge with ER-to-ATK conversion. Best when frequent Bursts matter.
  5. "The Catch": An Energy Recharge stat stick that helps Burst uptime. Its offensive passive adds little value to a healer and Physical support.
  6. Rightful Reward: Craftable HP% polearm that restores Energy after the wielder receives healing.
  7. Prototype Starglitter: Craftable Energy Recharge; the Normal and Charged Attack passive has limited support value.
  8. Black Tassel: HP% stat stick; easy option for HP-scaling support or healing.

Best artifacts for Mika

  • Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs) · Best Support: Noblesse boosts Mika's Burst damage and grants the entire team a 20% ATK buff after using his Burst, making it excellent for supporting ATK-scaling DPS. Main stats: ER/HP% · HP% · Healing Bonus/HP%.
  • Ocean-Hued Clam (4 pcs) · Support: Ocean-Hued Clam converts Mika's healing into additional damage through its bubble effect, adding some personal damage while maintaining his healer role. Main stats: ER/HP% · HP% · Healing Bonus/HP%.
  • Maiden Beloved (4 pcs) · Support: Maiden Beloved increases Mika's healing output, improving team survivability. However, it offers no team damage buffs, making Noblesse a stronger overall support option. Main stats: ER/HP% · HP% · Healing Bonus/HP%.

Best teams for Mika

  • Frozen Thunder: Mika (Healer / Physical Buffer), Eula (On-field DPS), Raiden Shogun (On-field DPS / Battery), Furina (Off-field DPS / Buffer). Mika boosts Eula's Physical DMG and ATK SPD while providing consistent healing to support Furina's Fanfare stacks. Raiden enables Superconduct, provides Energy, and helps the team maximize Eula's devastating Burst damage.
  • Glacial Execution: Mika (Healer / Physical Buffer), Eula (On-field DPS), Rosaria (Off-field DPS / CRIT Buffer), Fischl (Off-field DPS / Battery). Mika buffs Eula's Physical DMG and ATK SPD while healing the team. Fischl enables Superconduct, while Rosaria provides Cryo application and CRIT support.
  • Frozen Depths: Mika (Healer / Physical Buffer), Freminet (On-field DPS), Furina (Off-field DPS / Buffer), Fischl (Off-field DPS / Battery). Mika boosts Freminet's Physical DMG and ATK SPD while providing healing. Furina buffs team damage, while Fischl enables Superconduct and provides consistent off-field damage.
  • Mondstadt Wolfpack: Mika (Healer / Physical Buffer), Razor (On-field DPS), Rosaria (Off-field DPS / CRIT Buffer), Fischl (Off-field DPS / Battery). Mika boosts Razor's Physical DMG and ATK SPD while providing healing. Fischl enables Superconduct, while Rosaria adds Cryo application and CRIT Rate support.

Build variations

Physical Carry Support (Support)

Prioritize reliable Burst healing and Physical damage support.

  • Weapons: Favonius Lance, Dialogues of the Desert Sages
  • Artifacts: Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ER and HP% for healing; CR for Favonius.
  • Teams: Physical on-field carry that benefits from ATK Speed.
  • When it applies: Physical buff value depends on carry and enemy count.

General Healer (Healer)

Sacrifice some buffing utility for consistent healing and comfort.

  • Weapons: Favonius Lance, Dialogues of the Desert Sages, Rightful Reward
  • Artifacts: Ocean-Hued Clam (4 pcs); Maiden Beloved (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ER and HP%; Healing Bonus optional.
  • Teams: Teams needing healing rather than Physical damage buffs.
  • When it applies: His Physical buffs offer limited value outside Physical teams.

Mika increases the active character's ATK SPD, boosts Physical DMG, and provides healing through his Elemental Burst. He's particularly valuable for Eula and other Physical DPS characters.

Best teams

  • Frozen Thunder: Mika, Eula, Raiden Shogun, Furina
  • Glacial Execution: Mika, Eula, Rosaria, Fischl
  • Frozen Depths: Mika, Freminet, Furina, Fischl
  • Mondstadt Wolfpack: Mika, Razor, Rosaria, Fischl

Pros

  • Boosts Phy. DMG and ATK SPD.
  • Provides teamwide healing.
  • Excellent for Eula and Physical DPS.
  • Easy to build with low investment.
  • Strong Furina synergy.

Cons

  • Limited Cryo application.
  • High ER needs.
  • Best with his C6

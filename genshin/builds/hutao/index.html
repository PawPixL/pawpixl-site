Hu Tao Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams (7.2)

Hu Tao build guide for Genshin Impact 7.2: best weapons (Staff of Homa, Staff of the Scarlet Sands), artifacts (Crimson Witch of Flames), main stats.

Last updated: November 12, 2026 · Game version 7.2

Role: Main DPS

Hu Tao is an on-field Pyro DPS who uses her Skill to strengthen Charged Attacks and trigger powerful Vaporize reactions. She converts HP into damage and rewards careful stamina management. Double Hydro teams are especially comfortable, while Furina teams need reliable healing.

Best weapons for Hu Tao

  1. Staff of Homa: CD, HP, and an ATK bonus based on Max HP make this her strongest all-around weapon.
  2. Staff of the Scarlet Sands: Huge CR and ATK based on EM make it excellent for Vaporize.
  3. Primordial Jade Winged-Spear: CR and stacking ATK help her DMG.
  4. Lumidouce Elegy: CRIT Rate is useful, but the passive focuses on ATK and Burning damage. Treat it mainly as a premium stat option for Hu Tao. Passive has limited synergy.
  5. Ballad of the Fjords: CR and a large EM bonus work well for Vaporize when your team includes at least three different elements.
  6. Dragon's Bane: EM and extra damage against Pyro/Hydro-affected foes. Great with consistent Hydro; high refinements improve it.
  7. Deathmatch: High CRIT Rate makes balancing her stats easier. Its ATK bonus is helpful, though less valuable than HP and reaction bonuses. Reliable across team types.
  8. White Tassel: Free CRIT Rate and Normal Attack damage at R5. Use more Normal Attacks between Charged Attacks.

Best artifacts for Hu Tao

  • Crimson Witch of Flames (4 pcs) · Vaporize Standard: Boosts Pyro and Vaporize damage without demanding a special team setup. A dependable general build. Main stats: HP% or EM / Pyro DMG / CRIT.
  • Marechaussee Hunter (4 pcs) · With Furina: Grants up to 36% CRIT Rate when HP changes. Best with Furina and a healer who can refresh the stacks. Main stats: HP% or EM / Pyro DMG / CRIT.
  • Shimenawa's Reminiscence (4 pcs) · Charged Attack Focus: Boosts Normal and Charged Attacks after her Skill, but drains Energy and makes her Burst harder to use. Main stats: HP% or EM / Pyro DMG / CRIT.

Best teams for Hu Tao

  • Director of Disaster: Hu Tao (On-field DPS), Furina (Off-field DPS / Buffer), Yelan (Off-field DPS / Buffer), Xilonen (Healer / Debuffer). Furina and Yelan provide Hydro damage and powerful buffs. Xilonen heals and reduces resistance, letting Hu Tao Vaporize consistently.
  • Plunge into Trouble: Hu Tao (On-field DPS), Furina (Off-field DPS / Buffer), Yelan (Off-field DPS / Buffer), Xianyun (Healer / Plunge Buffer). Furina and Yelan apply Hydro and boost damage. Xianyun heals for Fanfare and enables Vaporized plunges between Hu Tao attacks.
  • Double Hydro Trouble: Hu Tao (Main DPS), Xingqiu (Sub DPS), Yelan (Sub DPS), Zhongli (Shield). Xingqiu and Yelan keep Hydro active for Vaporize. Zhongli protects Hu Tao and reduces enemy resistance without forcing heals.
  • Budget Afterlife: Hu Tao (On-field DPS), Xingqiu (Off-field DPS / Hydro Enabler), Thoma (Shielder / Pyro Enabler), Sucrose (On-field Driver / EM Buffer). Xingqiu enables Vaporize, Thoma shields, and Sucrose shares EM and shreds resistance. Set up Pyro Swirl carefully.

Pros

Cons

  • Stamina hungry at C0

Build variations

Classic Vaporize (On-field DPS)

Consistent Charged Attack Vaporize damage without relying on Furina.

  • Weapons: Staff of Homa, Dragon's Bane
  • Artifacts: Crimson Witch of Flames (4 pcs); Shimenawa's Reminiscence (4 pcs)
  • Stats: HP% or EM Sands; Pyro DMG Goblet and CRIT Circlet.
  • Teams: Reliable Hydro application, ideally Xingqiu or Yelan.
  • When it applies: Manage stamina at C0.

Furina HP-Shift (On-field DPS)

Use Furina’s damage buff and HP changes to enable Marechaussee.

  • Weapons: Staff of Homa, Staff of the Scarlet Sands
  • Artifacts: Marechaussee Hunter (4 pcs); Crimson Witch of Flames (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Account for Marechaussee CRIT Rate; build HP and EM as needed.
  • Teams: Furina plus a healer capable of sustaining Fanfare.
  • When it applies: Requires healing despite Hu Tao’s low-HP preferences.

Hu Tao talents

Normal Attack: Secret Spear of Wangsheng

Normal Attack
Performs up to 6 consecutive spear strikes.

Charged Attack
Consumes a certain amount of Stamina to lunge forward, dealing damage to opponents along the way.

Plunging Attack
Plunges from mid-air to strike the ground below, damaging opponents along the path and dealing AoE DMG upon impact.

Elemental Skill: Guide to Afterlife

Cooldown: 16s

Only an unwavering flame can cleanse the impurities of this world.
Hu Tao consumes a set portion of her HP to knock the surrounding enemies back and enter the Paramita Papilio state.

Paramita Papilio
·Increases Hu Tao's ATK based on her Max HP at the time of entering this state. ATK Bonus gained this way cannot exceed 400% of Hu Tao's Base ATK.
·Converts attack DMG to Pyro DMG, which cannot be overridden by any other elemental infusion.
·Charged Attacks apply the Blood Blossom effect to the enemies hit.
·Increases Hu Tao's resistance to interruption.

Blood Blossom
Enemies affected by Blood Blossom will take Pyro DMG every 4s. This DMG is considered Elemental Skill DMG.
Each enemy can be affected by only one Blood Blossom effect at a time, and its duration may only be refreshed by Hu Tao herself.

Paramita Papilio ends when its duration is over, or Hu Tao has left the battlefield or fallen.

Elemental Burst: Spirit Soother

Cooldown: 15s · Energy cost: 60

Commands a blazing spirit to attack, dealing Pyro DMG in a large AoE.
Upon striking the enemy, regenerates a percentage of Hu Tao's Max HP. This effect can be triggered up to 5 times, based on the number of enemies hit.
If Hu Tao's HP is below or equal to 50% when the enemy is hit, both the DMG and HP Regeneration are increased.

Passive talent: Flutter By

When a Paramita Papilio state activated by Guide to Afterlife ends, all allies in the party (excluding Hu Tao herself) will have their CRIT Rate increased by 12% for 8s.

Passive talent: Sanguine Rouge

When Hu Tao's HP is equal to or less than 50%, her Pyro DMG Bonus is increased by 33%.

Passive talent: The More the Merrier

When Hu Tao cooks a dish perfectly, she has an 18% chance to receive an additional "Suspicious" dish of the same type.

Hu Tao constellations

C1: Crimson Bouquet

While in a Paramita Papilio state activated by Guide to Afterlife, Hu Tao's Charged Attacks do not consume Stamina.

C2: Ominous Rainfall

Increases the Blood Blossom DMG by an amount equal to 10% of Hu Tao's Max HP at the time the effect is applied.
Additionally, Spirit Soother will also apply the Blood Blossom effect.

C3: Lingering Carmine

Increases the Level of Guide to Afterlife by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C4: Garden of Eternal Rest

Upon defeating an enemy affected by a Blood Blossom that Hu Tao applied herself, all nearby allies in the party (excluding Hu Tao herself) will have their CRIT Rate increased by 12% for 15s.

C5: Floral Incense

Increases the Level of Spirit Soother by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C6: Butterfly's Embrace

Triggers when Hu Tao's HP drops below 25%, or when she suffers a lethal strike:
Hu Tao will not fall as a result of the DMG sustained. Additionally, for the next 10s, her All Elemental and Physical RES is increased by 200%, her CRIT Rate is increased by 100%, and her resistance to interruption is greatly increased.
This effect triggers automatically when Hu Tao has 1 HP left.
Can only occur once every 60s.

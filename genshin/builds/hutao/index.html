Role: Main DPS

Hu Tao is an on-field Pyro DPS who uses her Skill to strengthen Charged Attacks and trigger powerful Vaporize reactions. She converts HP into damage and rewards careful stamina management. Double Hydro teams are especially comfortable, while Furina teams need reliable healing.

Best weapons for Hu Tao

  1. Staff of Homa
  2. Staff of the Scarlet Sands
  3. Primordial Jade Winged-Spear
  4. Lumidouce Elegy
  5. Ballad of the Fjords
  6. Dragon's Bane
  7. Deathmatch
  8. White Tassel

Best artifacts

  • Crimson Witch of Flames (4 pcs) · Vaporize Standard
  • Marechaussee Hunter (4 pcs) · With Furina
  • Shimenawa's Reminiscence (4 pcs) · Charged Attack Focus

Main stats

  • Sands: HP% / Elemental Mastery
  • Goblet: Pyro DMG Bonus
  • Circlet: CRIT Rate / CRIT DMG

Stat targets

  • HP: 27,000 - 35,000
  • Elemental Mastery: 100 - 250
  • CRIT Rate: 60% - 75%
  • CRIT DMG: 140% - 200%

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 9+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 9+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 6 - 8

Best teams

  • Director of Disaster: Hu Tao, Furina, Yelan, Xilonen
  • Plunge into Trouble: Hu Tao, Furina, Yelan, Xianyun
  • Double Hydro Trouble: Hu Tao, Xingqiu, Yelan, Zhongli
  • Budget Afterlife: Hu Tao, Xingqiu, Thoma, Sucrose

Pros

  • Excellent single-target damage
  • Strong Vaporize potential
  • Great at C0
  • Flexible Hydro partners

Cons

  • Charged Attack cancels matter
  • Stamina hungry at C0
  • Needs reliable Hydro
  • Limited area coverage

Build variations

  • Classic Vaporize (On-field DPS): Consistent Charged Attack Vaporize damage without relying on Furina.
  • Furina HP-Shift (On-field DPS): Use Furina’s damage buff and HP changes to enable Marechaussee.

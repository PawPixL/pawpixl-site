Nicole Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams (7.2)

Nicole build guide for Genshin Impact 7.2: best weapons (Angelos' Heptades, Starcaller's Watch), artifacts (Celestial Gift), main stats, talent priority.

Last updated: November 12, 2026 · Game version 7.2

Role: Support

Nicole is a versatile support who empowers Hexerei teams with strong buffs and utility while requiring very little field time. She also breathes new life into older ATK-scaling carries.

Best weapons for Nicole

  1. Angelos' Heptades: Angelos' Heptades is Nicole's BiS because its massive Base ATK and ATK% maximize her team buffs while providing additional DMG bonuses and Energy restoration.
  2. Starcaller's Watch: Shield-oriented passive suits her play pattern, but the substat is not ATK%.
  3. Skyward Atlas: Same high base ATK plus ATK% substat; its ATK-boosting proc adds a little more toward the 4000 threshold.
  4. Thrilling Tales of Dragon Slayers: Zero-investment pick: the swap proc hands the next character 48% ATK.
  5. Oathsworn Eye: Provides ATK as its secondary stat and boosts Energy Recharge after using her Skill. Useful when Burst uptime is difficult.
  6. Flowing Purity: A craftable ATK catalyst that gains Elemental DMG after Skill use. A healer helps clear its Bond of Life effect.
  7. Favonius Codex: Particles for the whole team lower everyone else's ER needs.
  8. Eye of Perception: ATK% catalyst whose bouncing Normal/Charged Attack passive offers limited support value.

Best artifacts for Nicole

  • Celestial Gift (4 pcs) · Support: Celestial Gift is Nicole's BiS because its 4-piece bonus provides a 20% Pyro DMG Bonus, upgraded to 40% Pyro and 40% active-element DMG Bonus with Hexerei: Secret Rite active, greatly amplifying team damage. Main stats: ATK%/ER · ATK% · ATK%.
  • Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs) · Support: Noblesse Oblige boosts Nicole's Burst damage and provides a 20% teamwide ATK buff, making it a strong alternative support set.
  • Gladiator's Finale + A Day Carved From Rising Winds (2 + 2 pcs) · Support: Two ATK 2-piece bonuses give 36% ATK. Budget placeholder until a stronger support set is available.

Main stats

  • Sands: ATK% / Energy Recharge
  • Goblet: ATK%
  • Circlet: ATK%

Stat targets

  • ATK: 3,000 - 4,000 (buff goal)
  • Energy Recharge: 160%+
  • CRIT Rate: 40% - 50% (Favonius)

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 9+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 9+

Best teams for Nicole

  • Hot & Bothered: Nicole (Support), Varka (Main DPS), Prune (Buffer), Durin (Sub DPS). Varka drives Anemo damage, Prune amplifies Anemo, Durin provides off-field Pyro damage, and Nicole boosts team damage through buffs and Hexerei synergy.
  • Melt Down: Nicole (Support), Lohen (Main DPS), Citlali (Res Shred), Durin (Sub DPS). Lohen drives Cryo damage and triggers Melt, Durin provides off-field Pyro damage, Citlali enables Melt and shreds Pyro RES, and Nicole buffs both damage dealers.
  • PawPixls main: Nicole (Shielder / Buffer), Wanderer (Main DPS), Faruzan (Support), Durin (Off-field DPS / Buffer). Wanderer drives Anemo damage, Faruzan amplifies Anemo, Durin provides off-field Pyro damage and RES shred, and Nicole boosts team damage through buffs and Hexerei synergy.
  • Budget Cuts: Nicole (Shielder / Buffer), Razor (On-field DPS), Fischl (Off-field DPS / Battery), Chevreuse (Healer / Buffer). Razor and Fischl provide Electro damage while Nicole supplies Pyro and buffs. Chevreuse supports Overloaded teams with healing and resistance reduction.

Build variations

Hexerei Team Buffer (Support)

Maximize teamwide bonuses with minimal field time.

  • Weapons: Angelos' Heptades, Thrilling Tales of Dragon Slayers
  • Artifacts: Celestial Gift (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ER as needed; prioritize support stats over personal damage.
  • Teams: Qualifying Hexerei allies and an ATK-scaling carry if using TTDS.
  • When it applies: TTDS requires deliberate swap order.

General ATK Support (Support)

Use Noblesse and accessible catalyst support options.

  • Weapons: Thrilling Tales of Dragon Slayers, Oathsworn Eye
  • Artifacts: Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ER to Burst as needed; prioritize utility.
  • Teams: A team benefiting from ATK buffs outside Hexerei.
  • When it applies: Avoid duplicate Noblesse buffs.

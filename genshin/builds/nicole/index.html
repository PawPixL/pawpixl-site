Role: Support

Nicole is a versatile support who empowers Hexerei teams with strong buffs and utility while requiring very little field time. She also breathes new life into older ATK-scaling carries.

Best weapons for Nicole

  1. Angelos' Heptades
  2. Starcaller's Watch
  3. Skyward Atlas
  4. Thrilling Tales of Dragon Slayers
  5. Oathsworn Eye
  6. Flowing Purity
  7. Favonius Codex
  8. Eye of Perception

Best artifacts

  • Celestial Gift (4 pcs) · Support
  • Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs) · Support
  • Gladiator's Finale + A Day Carved From Rising Winds (2 + 2 pcs) · Support

Main stats

  • Sands: ATK% / Energy Recharge
  • Goblet: ATK%
  • Circlet: ATK%

Stat targets

  • ATK: 3,000 - 4,000 (buff goal)
  • Energy Recharge: 160%+
  • CRIT Rate: 40% - 50% (Favonius)

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 9+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 9+

Best teams

  • Hot & Bothered: Nicole, Varka, Prune, Durin
  • Melt Down: Nicole, Lohen, Citlali, Durin
  • PawPixls main: Nicole, Wanderer, Faruzan, Durin
  • Budget Cuts: Nicole, Razor, Fischl, Chevreuse

Pros

  • Strong team buffs
  • Low field time
  • Great utility
  • Flexible support

Cons

  • Hexerei focused
  • Low personal DMG
  • Team dependent
  • Limited outside niche

Build variations

  • Hexerei Team Buffer (Support): Maximize teamwide bonuses with minimal field time.
  • General ATK Support (Support): Use Noblesse and accessible catalyst support options.

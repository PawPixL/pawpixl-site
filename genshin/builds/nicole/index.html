Nicole Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

Nicole build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Angelos' Heptades, Starcaller's Watch), artifacts (Celestial Gift), main stats, talent priority.

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Role: Support

Nicole is a versatile support who empowers Hexerei teams with strong buffs and utility while requiring very little field time. She also breathes new life into older ATK-scaling carries.

Best weapons for Nicole

  1. Angelos' Heptades: Angelos' Heptades is Nicole's BiS because its massive Base ATK and ATK% maximize her team buffs while providing additional DMG bonuses and Energy restoration.
  2. Starcaller's Watch: Shield-oriented passive suits her play pattern, but the substat is not ATK%.
  3. Skyward Atlas: Same high base ATK plus ATK% substat; its ATK-boosting proc adds a little more toward the 4000 threshold.
  4. Thrilling Tales of Dragon Slayers: Zero-investment pick: the swap proc hands the next character 48% ATK.
  5. Oathsworn Eye: Provides ATK as its secondary stat and boosts Energy Recharge after using her Skill. Useful when Burst uptime is difficult.
  6. Flowing Purity: A craftable ATK catalyst that gains Elemental DMG after Skill use. A healer helps clear its Bond of Life effect.
  7. Favonius Codex: Particles for the whole team lower everyone else's ER needs.
  8. Eye of Perception: ATK% catalyst whose bouncing Normal/Charged Attack passive offers limited support value.

Best artifacts for Nicole

  • Celestial Gift (4 pcs) · Support: Celestial Gift is Nicole's BiS because its 4-piece bonus provides a 20% Pyro DMG Bonus, upgraded to 40% Pyro and 40% active-element DMG Bonus with Hexerei: Secret Rite active, greatly amplifying team damage. Main stats: ATK%/ER · ATK% · ATK%.
  • Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs) · Support: Noblesse Oblige boosts Nicole's Burst damage and provides a 20% teamwide ATK buff, making it a strong alternative support set.
  • Gladiator's Finale + A Day Carved From Rising Winds (2 + 2 pcs) · Support: Two ATK 2-piece bonuses give 36% ATK. Budget placeholder until a stronger support set is available.

Talent priority

  • Elemental Skill: Level 9+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 9+

Best teams for Nicole

  • Hot & Bothered: Nicole (Support), Varka (Main DPS), Prune (Buffer), Durin (Sub DPS). Varka drives Anemo damage, Prune amplifies Anemo, Durin provides off-field Pyro damage, and Nicole boosts team damage through buffs and Hexerei synergy.
  • Melt Down: Nicole (Support), Lohen (Main DPS), Citlali (Res Shred), Durin (Sub DPS). Lohen drives Cryo damage and triggers Melt, Durin provides off-field Pyro damage, Citlali enables Melt and shreds Pyro RES, and Nicole buffs both damage dealers.
  • PawPixls main: Nicole (Shielder / Buffer), Wanderer (Main DPS), Faruzan (Support), Durin (Off-field DPS / Buffer). Wanderer drives Anemo damage, Faruzan amplifies Anemo, Durin provides off-field Pyro damage and RES shred, and Nicole boosts team damage through buffs and Hexerei synergy.
  • Budget Cuts: Nicole (Shielder / Buffer), Razor (On-field DPS), Fischl (Off-field DPS / Battery), Chevreuse (Healer / Buffer). Razor and Fischl provide Electro damage while Nicole supplies Pyro and buffs. Chevreuse supports Overloaded teams with healing and resistance reduction.

Build variations

Hexerei Team Buffer (Support)

Maximize teamwide bonuses with minimal field time.

  • Weapons: Angelos' Heptades, Thrilling Tales of Dragon Slayers
  • Artifacts: Celestial Gift (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ER as needed; prioritize support stats over personal damage.
  • Teams: Qualifying Hexerei allies and an ATK-scaling carry if using TTDS.
  • When it applies: TTDS requires deliberate swap order.

General ATK Support (Support)

Use Noblesse and accessible catalyst support options.

  • Weapons: Thrilling Tales of Dragon Slayers, Oathsworn Eye
  • Artifacts: Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ER to Burst as needed; prioritize utility.
  • Teams: A team benefiting from ATK buffs outside Hexerei.
  • When it applies: Avoid duplicate Noblesse buffs.

Nicole talents

Normal Attack: Allegoria

Normal Attack
Proclaims a decree in the primordial tongue and performs up to 3 attacks that deal Pyro DMG.

Charged Attack
Consumes a certain amount of Stamina to deal AoE Pyro DMG to opponents in front of her after a short casting time.

Plunging Attack
Nicole plunges towards the ground from mid-air, damaging all opponents in her path. Also deals AoE Pyro DMG upon impact with the ground.

Elemental Skill: Revelation: Uncreated Light

Cooldown: 16s

Recreates a fleeting glimpse of an age long past, when the angels acted on the authority of the heavens, bestowing the Grace of Kenosis effect on nearby party members. This increases their ATK and deals AoE Pyro DMG, as well as creates a Shield of Blazing Light.
The Shield of Blazing Light's DMG Absorption is based on Nicole's ATK, and it will absorb Pyro DMG at 250% effectiveness.

Elemental Burst: Revelation: Ladder of Divine Ascent

Cooldown: 15s · Energy cost: 60

As Nicole weaves the path of anchored fate with golden threads of light, she deals AoE Pyro DMG and switches to Silent Contemplation mode.
In this mode, when nearby active party members hit an opponent, an Arcane Projection is summoned to perform a Coordinated Attack against the opponent, dealing AoE Elemental DMG of the character's corresponding Elemental Type. This DMG scales based on the corresponding character's ATK and will be considered as having been dealt by them.
This effect can occur once every 3s, and can be triggered up to 4 times each time Nicole switches to Silent Contemplation mode.

It is worth noting that Hexerei characters' Arcane Projections appear to differ somehow in appearance from the others...

Passive talent: Methexis

For 20s after unleashing the Elemental Skill Revelation: Uncreated Light, Nicole will continuously "observe" the other current active party members. After the observed character has remained on the field for 3s, their Grace of Kenosis is upgraded to Guidance of Theosis, increasing their ATK by an additional 300. If the observed character is a Hexerei character, Grace of Kenosis is upgraded to Guidance of Theosis straight away.
When characters other than Nicole who have Guidance of Theosis leave the field, their effect reverts to Grace of Kenosis.

Passive talent: Philokalia

When Elemental DMG from nearby active party members hits an opponent, Nicole's own Grace of Kenosis effect is upgraded to Guidance of Theosis for 8s.

Passive talent: Nepsis

When you are in Teyvat and not in combat, Nicole's Charged Attack becomes "Emissarial Guidance" if you have already obtained the current region's Treasure Compass. Emissarial Guidance: Calls upon nearby Seelie to show the way, triggering an effect similar to the region's Treasure Compass.
This effect has a cooldown time of 5 seconds and does not work in Domains, Trounce Domains, and the Spiral Abyss.

Nicole constellations

C1: "Do Not Be Afraid, Child Who Is Loved"

When the current active character of your own in the party hits an opponent, an additional special "Arcane Projection: Unity" will be summoned to perform a Coordinated Attack against the opponent, dealing AoE Elemental DMG of the character's corresponding Elemental Type at 600% of their ATK. This DMG will be considered as having been dealt by the corresponding character.
This effect can trigger once every 6s.

C2: "I Will Guide You and Show You the Path You Should Tread"

Enhances the effects of the Elemental Skill Revelation: Uncreated Light: Grace of Kenosis increases ATK by an additional 300 (which does not count toward this effect's ATK Bonus limit), while Guidance of Theosis decreases nearby opponents' Elemental RES based on the corresponding character's Elemental Type by 25%. This effect does not stack for the same Element.
Additionally, when Nicole unleashes her Elemental Skill Revelation: Uncreated Light, nearby active party members will also gain a Shield of Blazing Light.

C3: "A Lamp by Your Side, A Light to Shine the Way"

Increases the Level of Revelation: Uncreated Light by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C4: "Whether Left or Right, No Matter Which Way You Turn"

When nearby party members have their Grace of Kenosis upgraded to Guidance of Theosis, Nicole also bestows upon them the Pathfinder's Blessing effect for 20s. Each character can gain this effect once every 16s.
The Normal Attack, Charged Attack, Plunging Attack, Elemental Skill, and Elemental Burst DMG of characters with Pathfinder's Blessing is increased by 70% of Nicole's ATK. This effect will be removed once it has taken effect 8 times or when its duration is over.
When multiple opponents are hit at once, instances of Pathfinder's Blessing are consumed equal to the number of opponents hit. Each character's Pathfinder's Blessing is counted independently.

C5: "You Will Hear My Voice Beside You"

Increases the Level of Revelation: Ladder of Divine Ascent by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C6: "This Is the Path, Walk It Without Delay"

When Nicole's Grace of Kenosis is upgraded to Guidance of Theosis, all nearby party members' Grace of Kenosis will also be upgraded to Guidance of Theosis. Once upgraded, Guidance of Theosis will no longer revert to Grace of Kenosis.
Additionally, DMG dealt by characters with Guidance of Theosis will ignore 40% of opponents' DEF.

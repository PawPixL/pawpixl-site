Role: Support / Sub DPS

Dendro Traveler supplies off-field Dendro through their Burst's Lea Lotus Lamp. Prioritize Energy Recharge to sustain it. Hydro expands the Lamp's range, Electro speeds its hits, and Pyro destroys it, so plan reaction order carefully.

Best weapons for Traveler (Dendro)

  1. Exaiphanes Blade
  2. Favonius Sword
  3. Sacrificial Sword
  4. Skyward Blade
  5. Freedom-Sworn
  6. Xiphos' Moonlight
  7. Sapwood Blade
  8. Fleuve Cendre Ferryman

Best artifacts

  • Deepwood Memories (4 pcs)
  • Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs)
  • Instructor (4 pcs)

Main stats

  • Sands: Energy Recharge
  • Goblet: Dendro DMG Bonus / EM
  • Circlet: CRIT Rate / CRIT DMG

Stat targets

  • Solo ER: 200-250%
  • 2 Dendro: 160-200%
  • Bloom ER: 200-250%
  • Fav CR: 40-60%

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Not Priority
  • Elemental Skill: Lvl 6 - 8
  • Elemental Burst: Lvl 8 - 9

Best teams

  • Seeds of Thunder: Traveler (Dendro), Kuki Shinobu, Xingqiu, Yaoyao
  • Quickened Circuit: Traveler (Dendro), Keqing, Fischl, Zhongli
  • Lotus Bloom: Traveler (Dendro), Nilou, Sangonomiya Kokomi, Collei
  • Controlled Burgeon: Traveler (Dendro), Thoma, Xingqiu, Sangonomiya Kokomi

Pros

  • Reliable off-field Dendro
  • Free constellations
  • Strong Deepwood carrier
  • Fits many reaction teams

Cons

  • High Energy requirements
  • Burst can explode to Pyro
  • Fixed Burst area
  • Limited personal damage

Build variations

  • Noblesse Team Buffer (Support): Use for Quicken or other teams where an existing Deepwood holder covers resistance reduction. Maintain Burst uptime for the party ATK buff.
  • Nilou Bountiful Bloom (Bloom Support): Use Traveler to sustain Dendro and expand the Lamp with Hydro for coverage. Bloom ownership is mixed; Traveler EM only affects cores they create.
  • Quicken Personal Damage (Off-field Sub DPS): With reliable Burst uptime, Traveler can add Spread damage alongside Dendro application. Electro transfiguration may help single-target hits.
  • Budget Dendro Support (Support): An accessible setup for early-to-midgame teams. Sapwood offers craftable ER and an EM leaf; Instructor is a low-rarity team buff alternative.
  • Burgeon: Hydro-First Lamp (Dendro Enabler): Use Traveler Burst, immediately contact the Lamp with Hydro, then activate off-field Pyro. A Pyro-transfigured Lamp explodes and stops applying Dendro.

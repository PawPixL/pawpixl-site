Traveler (Dendro) Build Guide: Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

Traveler (Dendro) build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Exaiphanes Blade, Favonius Sword), artifacts (Deepwood Memories), main stats.

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Role: Support / Sub DPS

Dendro Traveler supplies off-field Dendro through their Burst's Lea Lotus Lamp. Prioritize Energy Recharge to sustain it. Hydro expands the Lamp's range, Electro speeds its hits, and Pyro destroys it, so plan reaction order carefully.

Best weapons for Traveler (Dendro)

  1. Exaiphanes Blade: Restores 3 Energy every 5s and boosts CRIT Rate, helping Dendro Traveler maintain Burst uptime
  2. Favonius Sword: Generates white particles on crit, helping the whole team fund their Burst costs and improving…
  3. Sacrificial Sword: A chance to reset Skill cooldown allows more reaction triggers and faster energy recharge. Used by 8%…
  4. Skyward Blade
  5. Freedom-Sworn: Boosts all party members' ATK and normal/charged/plunging damage after triggering reactions.
  6. Xiphos' Moonlight
  7. Sapwood Blade: Craftable option that provides Energy Recharge and creates a Leaf of Consciousness.
  8. Fleuve Cendre Ferryman

Best artifacts for Traveler (Dendro)

  • Deepwood Memories (4 pcs): Shreds Dendro RES for team damage. Main stats: ER · Dendro/EM · CRIT.
  • Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs): Burst boosts party ATK by 20%. Main stats: ER · Dendro · CRIT.
  • Instructor (4 pcs): Cheap team EM buff after reactions. Main stats: ER · EM · CRIT Rate.

  • Circlet: CRIT Rate / CRIT DMG

Best teams for Traveler (Dendro)

  • Seeds of Thunder: Traveler (Dendro) (Support / Sub DPS), Kuki Shinobu (Hyperbloom), Xingqiu (Off-field), Yaoyao (Support). Traveler and Yaoyao maintain Dendro, Xingqiu creates cores, and full-EM Kuki triggers Hyperbloom. Let Hydro touch the Lamp before any Pyro.
  • Quickened Circuit: Traveler (Dendro), Keqing, Fischl, Zhongli. Traveler maintains Quicken for Keqing and Fischl's Aggravate hits; Zhongli protects and reduces enemy RES. Electro transfiguration is useful.
  • Lotus Bloom: Traveler (Dendro), Nilou, Sangonomiya Kokomi, Collei. Nilou converts Bloom cores into Bountiful Cores; Kokomi heals and applies Hydro while Traveler and Collei supply Dendro. Hydro-expand the Lamp.
  • Controlled Burgeon: Traveler (Dendro), Thoma, Xingqiu, Sangonomiya Kokomi. Apply Hydro to Traveler's Lamp before Thoma's Pyro; Xingqiu and Kokomi generate cores. Build Thoma with EM and enough ER.

Build variations

Noblesse Team Buffer (Support)

Use for Quicken or other teams where an existing Deepwood holder covers resistance reduction. Maintain Burst uptime for the party ATK buff.

  • Weapons: Favonius Sword, Sacrificial Sword, Sapwood Blade
  • Artifacts: Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs); Instructor (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Keep ER high; CRIT Rate helps Favonius. Personal damage is secondary.
  • Teams: Another ally must carry Deepwood for Dendro RES shred when needed.
  • When it applies: Do not duplicate Noblesse on another teammate.

Nilou Bountiful Bloom (Bloom Support)

Use Traveler to sustain Dendro and expand the Lamp with Hydro for coverage. Bloom ownership is mixed; Traveler EM only affects cores they create.

  • Weapons: Favonius Sword, Sacrificial Sword, Xiphos' Moonlight
  • Artifacts: Deepwood Memories (4 pcs); Instructor (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ER first, then EM; EM goblet/circlet are viable if Traveler owns some Blooms.
  • Teams: Nilou plus only Hydro/Dendro allies; include a strong healer and ideally a second Dendro.
  • When it applies: Nilou passive requires Hydro/Dendro-only party.

Quicken Personal Damage (Off-field Sub DPS)

With reliable Burst uptime, Traveler can add Spread damage alongside Dendro application. Electro transfiguration may help single-target hits.

  • Weapons: Freedom-Sworn, Favonius Sword, Skyward Blade
  • Artifacts: Deepwood Memories (4 pcs); Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs)
  • Stats: After ER requirements, use Dendro goblet and CRIT circlet; EM and ATK% are useful.
  • Teams: Needs sustained Electro for Quicken; have another Deepwood holder if using Noblesse.
  • When it applies: ER requirement takes precedence over damage stats.

Budget Dendro Support (Support)

An accessible setup for early-to-midgame teams. Sapwood offers craftable ER and an EM leaf; Instructor is a low-rarity team buff alternative.

  • Weapons: Sapwood Blade, Skyrider Sword, Fleuve Cendre Ferryman
  • Artifacts: Deepwood Memories (4 pcs); Instructor (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Stack ER first. CRIT Rate matters with Favonius if you obtain it later.
  • Teams: Any Dendro reaction team; another Dendro unit makes Energy easier.
  • When it applies: Instructor is a 4-star artifact set.

Burgeon: Hydro-First Lamp (Dendro Enabler)

Use Traveler Burst, immediately contact the Lamp with Hydro, then activate off-field Pyro. A Pyro-transfigured Lamp explodes and stops applying Dendro.

  • Weapons: Favonius Sword, Sacrificial Sword, Sapwood Blade
  • Artifacts: Deepwood Memories (4 pcs); Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs)
  • Stats: High ER for reliable Lamp uptime; personal EM is not Burgeon trigger EM.
  • Teams: Need consistent Hydro, a Pyro core trigger built for EM, and sustain.
  • When it applies: Requires safe rotation order. Avoid early Pyro near Lamp.

Traveler (Dendro) talents

Normal Attack: Foreign Fieldcleaver

Normal Attack
Performs up to 5 rapid strikes.

Charged Attack
Consumes a certain amount of Stamina to unleash 2 rapid sword strikes.

Plunging Attack
Plunges from mid-air to strike the ground below, damaging opponents along the path and dealing AoE DMG upon impact.

Elemental Skill: Razorgrass Blade

Cooldown: 8s

With a flourish of your blade, you unleash a spray of razor-sharp leaves that go before you and deal Dendro DMG.

Elemental Burst: Surgent Manifestation

Cooldown: 20s · Energy cost: 80

Calling upon the might of the flora all around you, you create a Lea Lotus Lamp.
This Lamp will deal continuous Dendro DMG to opponents within its AoE.

Lotuslight Transfiguration
The Lea Lotus Lamp will undergo the following changes after it comes into contact with Hydro/Electro/Pyro:
·Hydro: the Lamp's AoE and the AoE of its attacks are increased.
·Electro: the Lamp's ATK SPD is increased.
·Pyro: the Lamp will explode after a short delay and then disappear, dealing AoE Dendro DMG.
The Lea Lotus Lamp can only undergo one Lotuslight Transfiguration in its duration.

Only one Lamp created by the Traveler can exist at any one time.

Passive talent: Verdant Overgrowth

Lea Lotus Lamp will obtain one level of Overflowing Lotuslight every second it is on the field, increasing the Elemental Mastery of active character(s) within its AoE by 6. Overflowing Lotuslight has a maximum of 10 stacks.

Passive talent: Verdant Luxury

Every point of Elemental Mastery the Traveler possesses increases the DMG dealt by Razorgrass Blade by 0.15% and the DMG dealt by Surgent Manifestation by 0.1%.

Traveler (Dendro) constellations

C1: Symbiotic Creeper

After Razorgrass Blade hits an opponent, it will regenerate 3.5 Energy for the Traveler.

C2: Green Resilience

Lea Lotus Lamp's duration is increased by 3s.

C3: Whirling Weeds

Increases the Level of Razorgrass Blade by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C4: Treacle Grass

After the Lea Lotus Lamp triggers a Lotuslight Transfiguration, it will obtain 5 stacks of the Overflowing Lotuslight effect from the Ascension Talent "Verdant Overgrowth."
You must first unlock the Ascension Talent "Verdant Overgrowth."

C5: Viridian Transience

Increases the Level of Surgent Manifestation by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C6: Withering Aggregation

The Dendro DMG Bonus of the character under the effect of Overflowing Lotuslight as created by the Lea Lotus Lamp is increased by 12%. If the Lamp has experienced a Lotuslight Transfiguration previously, the character will also gain 12% DMG Bonus for the corresponding element.

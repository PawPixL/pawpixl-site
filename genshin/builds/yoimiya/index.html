Yoimiya Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

Yoimiya build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Thundering Pulse, Aqua Simulacra), artifacts (Shimenawa's Reminiscence), main stats, talent priority.

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Role: Main DPS

Yoimiya is an on-field Pyro DPS who rapidly fires infused Normal Attacks after using her Skill. Vaporize teams excel against single targets, especially with a shielder to protect her full attack string. Prioritize Normal Attack talent levels; her Burst is optional, particularly with Shimenawa.

Best weapons for Yoimiya

  1. Thundering Pulse: Signature CRIT DMG bow with ATK and stacking Normal Attack DMG. Shimenawa energy consumption can help maintain its third stack. Manage energy below 100% for all three stacks.
  2. Aqua Simulacra: Huge CD and unconditional HP.
  3. The First Great Magic: Provides CD and Charged Attack/ATK bonuses.
  4. Skyward Harp: Provides CRIT Rate, CRIT DMG, and high base ATK, making it an easy general-purpose option.
  5. Polar Star: High CRIT Rate and Skill/Burst DMG buffs; its ATK stacking is awkward for a Normal Attack-focused rotation. Not all passive stacks are convenient to maintain.
  6. Rust: One of her best bows at high refinements: directly boosts her Normal Attack damage, which supplies most of her output. Especially strong at R4–R5. R4–R5 recommended.
  7. Slingshot: Outstanding 3★ CRIT Rate option that buffs fast, close-range Normal Attack hits. Can rival premium bows with strong ATK buffs. Use Bennett and/or Yun Jin; stay close enough for passive. R5 recommended.
  8. Hamayumi: Accessible ATK bow that buffs Normal Attacks, but its full-energy passive conflicts with Shimenawa energy consumption. Better without 4pc Shimenawa; avoid spending energy for full passive. R5 improves bonus.

Best artifacts for Yoimiya

  • Shimenawa's Reminiscence (4 pcs) · Best Overall: A reliable 50% Normal Attack DMG boost after Skill use. Main stats: ATK% / EM · Pyro DMG · CRIT.
  • Echoes of an Offering (4 pcs) · Normal Attack Alternative: Can rival Shimenawa with frequent Normal Attack procs; performance varies with latency and reaction timing. Main stats: ATK% / EM · Pyro DMG · CRIT.
  • Marechaussee Hunter (4 pcs) · Furina Teams: With Furina-driven HP changes. Main stats: ATK% / EM · Pyro DMG · CRIT; lower sheet CRIT Rate.

Main stats

  • Sands: ATK% / Elemental Mastery (Vaporize)
  • Goblet: Pyro DMG Bonus
  • Circlet: CRIT Rate / CRIT DMG

Stat targets

  • CRIT Rate: 65% - 80%
  • CRIT DMG: 140% - 200%+
  • ATK: 1,700 - 2,300+
  • Elemental Mastery: 100 - 250 (Vape)

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 8-9
  • Elemental Skill: Level 8-9
  • Elemental Burst: Level 6 - 8 (optional)

Best teams for Yoimiya

  • Fireworks Finale: Yoimiya (Main DPS), Yelan (Hydro Sub DPS), Yun Jin (Normal Attack Buffer), Zhongli (Shield / RES Shred). Yelan enables Vaporize, Yun Jin strengthens rapid Normal Attacks, and Zhongli prevents interruptions while shredding RES.
  • Summer Night Encore: Yoimiya (On-field DPS), Yelan (Off-field DPS / Buffer), Furina (Off-field DPS / Buffer), Jean (Healer / Debuffer). Double Hydro supports Vaporize; Furina buffs damage and Jean heals and can shred Pyro RES when Pyro is Swirled.
  • Sparkler Overload: Yoimiya (On-field DPS), Fischl (Off-field DPS / Battery), Chevreuse (Healer / Buffer), Beidou (Off-field DPS). Pyro/Electro-only lineup unlocks Chevreuse buffs; Fischl and Beidou add damage and Beidou aids interruption resistance.
  • Discount Fireworks: Yoimiya (On-field DPS), Xingqiu (Off-field DPS / Hydro Enabler), Bennett (Healer / Buffer), Layla (Shielder / Cryo Enabler). Xingqiu enables Vaporize and adds resistance; Bennett supplies ATK and heals while Layla protects Normal Attack strings.

Pros

  • Strong single-target DMG
  • Easy ranged Normal Attacks
  • Excellent 4★ and 3★ bows
  • Flexible Pyro team options

Cons

  • Limited AoE damage
  • Needs full attack strings
  • Easily interrupted
  • Skill uptime limits damage

Build variations

Furina / Marechaussee (On-field Vaporize DPS)

Use when Furina is available and can maintain the HP fluctuations required for Marechaussee.

  • Weapons: Thundering Pulse, Aqua Simulacra, Rust, Slingshot
  • Artifacts: Marechaussee Hunter (4 pcs); Shimenawa's Reminiscence (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Use less sheet CRIT Rate to account for up to 36% from Marechaussee. ATK% or EM Sands based on buffs.
  • Teams: Furina plus sufficient party healing; add reliable Hydro application if needed.
  • When it applies: Requires consistent HP changes; healers matter.
  • Notes: Marechaussee is strong with Furina, not a generic set for standard Yoimiya teams.

High-Refinement Rust (On-field Normal Attack DPS)

Use when you have a high-refinement Rust but do not want to pull a signature 5★ bow.

  • Weapons: Rust, Thundering Pulse, Aqua Simulacra, Skyward Harp
  • Artifacts: Shimenawa's Reminiscence (4 pcs); Echoes of an Offering (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Balance CRIT stats from artifacts because Rust gives ATK% rather than CRIT.
  • Teams: Any Yoimiya team that preserves complete Normal Attack sequences.
  • When it applies: R4–R5 recommended for top performance.
  • Notes: Strong in Vaporize or raw Pyro builds; monitor Shimenawa Energy requirement.

F2P Slingshot Vape (On-field Vaporize DPS)

Low-spender setup using an accessible R5 three-star bow without giving up strong single-target damage.

  • Weapons: Slingshot, Hamayumi
  • Artifacts: Shimenawa's Reminiscence (4 pcs); Echoes of an Offering (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Prioritize ATK% buffs and CRIT DMG; Slingshot provides substantial CRIT Rate. Add EM for Vaporize.
  • Teams: Bennett or Yun Jin strongly recommended, plus Xingqiu or Yelan; shielding helps.
  • When it applies: R5 Slingshot; stay close enough for its hit-speed passive.
  • Notes: If using Hamayumi, prefer sets that do not drain Energy and preserve its full-Energy bonus.

Chevreuse Overloaded (On-field Pyro DPS)

Use when you prefer a reaction team that does not need Hydro or Vaporize setups.

  • Weapons: Thundering Pulse, Rust, Skyward Harp, Slingshot
  • Artifacts: Shimenawa's Reminiscence (4 pcs); Echoes of an Offering (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Favor ATK% and CRIT; EM is less important for a raw Pyro damage-focused Chevreuse setup.
  • Teams: Chevreuse with only Pyro and Electro teammates; sustain/interruption resistance recommended.
  • When it applies: Chevreuse passive team restriction must be met.
  • Notes: Fischl and Beidou are accessible Electro damage options; Beidou Burst favors multi-target fights.

Yoimiya talents

Normal Attack: Firework Flare-Up

Normal Attack
Performs up to 5 consecutive shots with a bow.

Charged Attack
Performs a more precise Aimed Shot with increased DMG.
While aiming, flames will accumulate on the arrowhead before being fired off as an attack. Has different effects based on how long the energy has been charged:
·Charge Level 1: Fires off a flaming arrow that deals Pyro DMG.
·Charge Level 2: Generates a maximum of 3 Kindling Arrows based on time spent charging, releasing them as part of this Aimed Shot. Kindling Arrows will home in on nearby opponents, dealing Pyro DMG on hit.

Plunging Attack
Fires off a shower of arrows in mid-air before falling and striking the ground, dealing AoE DMG upon impact.

Elemental Skill: Niwabi Fire-Dance

Cooldown: 18s

Yoimiya waves a sparkler and causes a ring of saltpeter to surround her.

Niwabi Enshou
During this time, arrows fired by Yoimiya's Normal Attack will be Blazing Arrows, and their DMG will be increased and converted to Pyro DMG. During this time, Normal Attack: Firework Flare-Up will not generate Kindling Arrows at Charge Level 2.

This effect will deactivate when Yoimiya leaves the field.

Elemental Burst: Ryuukin Saxifrage

Cooldown: 15s · Energy cost: 60

Yoimiya leaps into the air along with her original creation, the "Ryuukin Saxifrage," and fires forth blazing rockets bursting with surprises that deal AoE Pyro DMG and mark one of the hit opponents with Aurous Blaze.

Aurous Blaze
All Normal/Charged/Plunging Attacks, Elemental Skills, and Elemental Bursts by any party member other than Yoimiya that hit an opponent marked by Aurous Blaze will trigger an explosion, dealing AoE Pyro DMG.
When an opponent affected by Aurous Blaze is defeated before its duration expires, the effect will pass on to another nearby opponent, who will inherit the remaining duration.

One Aurous Blaze explosion can be triggered every 2s. When Yoimiya is down, Aurous Blaze effects created through her skills will be deactivated.

Passive talent: Tricks of the Trouble-Maker

During Niwabi Fire-Dance, shots from Yoimiya's Normal Attack will increase her Pyro DMG Bonus by 2% on hit. This effect lasts for 3s and can have a maximum of 10 stacks.

Passive talent: Summer Night's Dawn

Using Ryuukin Saxifrage causes nearby party members (not including Yoimiya) to gain a 10% ATK increase for 15s. Additionally, a further ATK Bonus will be added on based on the number of Ascension Talent "Tricks of the Trouble-Maker" stacks Yoimiya possesses when using Ryuukin Saxifrage. Each stack increases this ATK Bonus by 1%.

Passive talent: Blazing Match

When Yoimiya crafts Decoration, Ornament, and Landscape-type Furnishings, she has a 100% chance to refund a portion of the materials used.

Yoimiya constellations

C1: Agate Ryuukin

The Aurous Blaze created by Ryuukin Saxifrage lasts for an extra 4s.
Additionally, when an opponent affected by Aurous Blaze is defeated within its duration, Yoimiya's ATK is increased by 20% for 20s.

C2: A Procession of Bonfires

When Yoimiya's Pyro DMG scores a CRIT Hit, Yoimiya will gain a 25% Pyro DMG Bonus for 6s.
This effect can be triggered even when Yoimiya is not the active character.

C3: Trickster's Flare

Increases the Level of Niwabi Fire-Dance by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C4: Pyrotechnic Professional

When Yoimiya's own Aurous Blaze triggers an explosion, Niwabi Fire-Dance's CD is decreased by 1.2s.

C5: A Summer Festival's Eve

Increases the Level of Ryuukin Saxifrage by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C6: Naganohara Meteor Swarm

During Niwabi Fire-Dance, Yoimiya's Normal Attacks have a 50% chance of firing an extra Blazing Arrow that deals 60% of its original DMG. This DMG is considered Normal Attack DMG.

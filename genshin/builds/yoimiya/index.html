Role: Main DPS

Yoimiya is an on-field Pyro DPS who rapidly fires infused Normal Attacks after using her Skill. Vaporize teams excel against single targets, especially with a shielder to protect her full attack string. Prioritize Normal Attack talent levels; her Burst is optional, particularly with Shimenawa.

Best weapons for Yoimiya

  2. Aqua Simulacra
  3. The First Great Magic
  4. Skyward Harp
  5. Polar Star
  6. Rust
  7. Slingshot
  8. Hamayumi

  • Marechaussee Hunter (4 pcs) · Furina Teams

Main stats

  • Sands: ATK% / Elemental Mastery (Vaporize)
  • Goblet: Pyro DMG Bonus
  • Circlet: CRIT Rate / CRIT DMG

Stat targets

  • CRIT Rate: 65% - 80%
  • CRIT DMG: 140% - 200%+
  • ATK: 1,700 - 2,300+
  • Elemental Mastery: 100 - 250 (Vape)

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 8-9
  • Elemental Skill: Level 8-9
  • Elemental Burst: Level 6 - 8 (optional)

Best teams

  • Fireworks Finale: Yoimiya, Yelan, Yun Jin, Zhongli
  • Summer Night Encore: Yoimiya, Yelan, Furina, Jean
  • Sparkler Overload: Yoimiya, Fischl, Chevreuse, Beidou
  • Discount Fireworks: Yoimiya, Xingqiu, Bennett, Layla

Pros

  • Strong single-target DMG
  • Easy ranged Normal Attacks
  • Excellent 4★ and 3★ bows
  • Flexible Pyro team options

Cons

  • Limited AoE damage
  • Needs full attack strings
  • Easily interrupted
  • Skill uptime limits damage

Build variations

  • Furina / Marechaussee (On-field Vaporize DPS): Use when Furina is available and can maintain the HP fluctuations required for Marechaussee.
  • High-Refinement Rust (On-field Normal Attack DPS): Use when you have a high-refinement Rust but do not want to pull a signature 5★ bow.
  • F2P Slingshot Vape (On-field Vaporize DPS): Low-spender setup using an accessible R5 three-star bow without giving up strong single-target damage.
  • Chevreuse Overloaded (On-field Pyro DPS): Use when you prefer a reaction team that does not need Hydro or Vaporize setups.

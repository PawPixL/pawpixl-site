Albedo Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

Albedo build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Peak Patrol Song, Uraku Misugiri), artifacts (Husk of Opulent Dreams), main stats, talent priority.

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Role: Off-Field DPS

Albedo deals consistent off-field Geo damage through his Skill. Hexerei upgrades add valuable team buffs, especially alongside other Hexerei characters.

Best weapons for Albedo

  1. Peak Patrol Song: High DEF and a teamwide Elemental DMG buff after two Normal Attack hits. Best when prioritizing team damage.
  2. Uraku Misugiri: CRIT DMG, DEF and Skill bonuses make it excellent for Albedo’s personal damage.
  3. Cinnabar Spindle: Powerful DEF-based Skill damage at R5. This weapon was exclusive to a past event. R5 preferred.
  4. Harbinger of Dawn: Strong CRIT option if Albedo stays above 90% HP. Stay above 90% HP.
  5. Primordial Jade Cutter: CRIT Rate substat with HP-scaled ATK.
  6. Flute of Ezpitzal: Craftable DEF sword that supports Skill damage and Hexerei DEF needs.
  7. Wolf-Fang: CRIT Rate and Skill damage make it a usable Battle Pass alternative. Passive stack uptime varies.

Best artifacts for Albedo

  • Husk of Opulent Dreams (4 pcs) · Sub DPS: Boosts DEF and Geo DMG, strengthening Transient Blossoms. Excellent for Albedo’s personal off-field damage. Main stats: DEF% · DEF%/Geo DMG · DEF%/CR/CD.
  • Golden Troupe (4 pcs) · Sub DPS: Boosts off-field Elemental Skill damage. Strong alternative to Husk, depending on weapon and team buffs.
  • Celestial Gift (4 pcs) · Hexerei Team Support: Team-buff option: grants Elemental DMG Bonus after Skill, with stronger benefits in qualifying Hexerei teams.

Main stats

  • Sands: DEF%
  • Goblet: DEF% / Geo DMG
  • Circlet: CR / CD / DEF%

Stat targets

  • DEF: Aim near 3,000
  • CR: 60–70%
  • CD: 140%+

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 9+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 6-8

Best teams for Albedo

  • Rock & Reload: Albedo, Navia, Durin, Nicole. Navia drives Geo damage; Albedo adds off-field hits and Crystallize. Durin supplies Pyro and buffs; Nicole shields and buffs.
  • Chemical Romance: Albedo, Lohen, Durin, Nicole. Lohen leads Cryo damage; Durin enables Melt. Albedo contributes Geo and Hexerei buffs while Nicole protects the team.
  • Fullmetal Father: Albedo, Arlecchino, Xilonen, Nicole. Arlecchino drives Pyro damage. Albedo adds Geo and Crystallize; Xilonen shreds RES while Nicole supports and shields.
  • Hopeless Impact: Albedo, Noelle, Durin, Gorou. Noelle drives Geo and heals; Albedo adds off-field hits. Gorou buffs DEF, while Durin provides Pyro and team utility.

Pros

  • Excellent off-field DMG
  • Very low field time
  • Flexible team slot
  • Strong Hexerei synergy

Cons

  • Breakable Geo construct
  • Hexerei team preference
  • Limited reaction utility

Build variations

Hexerei Team Buffer (Off-field Support)

Choose when Albedo’s teamwide damage buffs are more valuable than maximizing his personal Skill hits.

  • Weapons: Peak Patrol Song, Flute of Ezpitzal
  • Artifacts: Celestial Gift (4 pcs)
  • Stats: DEF% Sands; DEF% or Geo DMG Goblet; CRIT or DEF% Circlet. Meet ER only if using Burst.
  • Teams: A qualifying Hexerei teammate boosts the set’s team-buff value.
  • When it applies: Do not duplicate Celestial Gift on another support.

Personal Skill Damage (Off-field DPS)

Choose when you want Albedo’s off-field Skill to contribute the most personal damage.

  • Weapons: Uraku Misugiri, Cinnabar Spindle, Harbinger of Dawn, Flute of Ezpitzal
  • Artifacts: Husk of Opulent Dreams (4 pcs); Golden Troupe (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Prioritize DEF% and CRIT. Geo DMG Goblet can compete with DEF% depending on buffs.
  • Teams: Flexible Geo teams; no special reaction requirement.
  • When it applies: Harbinger of Dawn needs over 90% HP; Cinnabar Spindle is past-event only.

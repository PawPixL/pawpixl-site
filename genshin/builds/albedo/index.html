Albedo Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts & Teams

Albedo build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Peak Patrol Song, Uraku Misugiri), artifacts (Husk of Opulent Dreams), main stats (DEF% / DEF% / Geo…

Role: Off-Field DPS

Albedo is a low-field-time Geo off-field DPS who deals in consistent DEF scaling damage. His Hexerei upgrades boost both his damage and team utility.

Best weapons for Albedo

  1. Peak Patrol Song
  2. Uraku Misugiri
  3. Cinnabar Spindle
  4. Lightbearing Moonshard
  5. Harbinger of Dawn
  6. Primordial Jade Cutter
  7. Flute of Ezpitzal
  8. Wolf-Fang

Best artifacts

  • Husk of Opulent Dreams (4 pcs) · Sub DPS
  • Golden Troupe + Archaic Petra (4 pcs) · Sub DPS
  • Celestial Gift + Archaic Petra (4 pcs) · Sub DPS

Main stats

  • Sands: DEF%
  • Goblet: DEF% / Geo DMG
  • Circlet: DEF% / Crit Rate / Crit DMG

Stat targets

  • DEF%: 2,000-3,000
  • Crit Rate: 60-70
  • Crit DMG: 140%+

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 9+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 9+

Best teams

  • Rock & Reload: Albedo, Navia, Durin, Nicole
  • Chemical Romance: Albedo, Lohen, Durin, Nicole
  • Fullmetal Father: Albedo, Arlecchino, Xilonen, Nicole
  • Hopeless Impact: Albedo, Noelle, Durin, Gorou

Pros

  • Excellent off-field DMG
  • Very low field time
  • Flexible team slot
  • Strong Hexerei synergy

Cons

  • Constructs can break if not Hex
  • Wants Hexerei teammates
  • Limited reaction utility.

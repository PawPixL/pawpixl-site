Role: Main DPS / Support

Dehya is a flexible Pyro bruiser who unleashes Burst punches or protects allies with DMG mitigation and interruption resistance. Her on-field DMG needs substantial ER and investment; off-field support and Burgeon instead focus on her Skill. Her Burst punches do not trigger Normal Attack-linked effects.

Pros

Cons

Build variations

  • Furina Vaporize Burst (On-field Burst DPS): Choose this for an invested on-field Dehya with reliable HP fluctuations and Furina buffs.
  • High-Constellation Burst (On-field Burst DPS): For players with C4–C6 who can maintain damage-taken stacks and Burst uptime.
  • Off-Field Defensive Support (Off-field Support): Pick this for Skill-based protection and Tenacity ATK buffs instead of Dehya’s personal damage.
  • Burgeon Skill Trigger (Off-field Reaction DPS): Use Dehya’s Skill to trigger occasional Burgeons without using Burst.
  • F2P-Friendly Burst (On-field Burst DPS): For accessible weapon options while retaining Dehya’s on-field Burst playstyle.

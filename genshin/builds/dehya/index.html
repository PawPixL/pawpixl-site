Dehya Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams (7.2)

Dehya build guide for Genshin Impact 7.2: best weapons (Beacon of the Reed Sea, Wolf's Gravestone), artifacts (Emblem of Severed Fate), main stats.

Last updated: November 12, 2026 · Game version 7.2

Role: Main DPS / Support

Dehya is a flexible Pyro bruiser who unleashes Burst punches or protects allies with DMG mitigation and interruption resistance. Her on-field DMG needs substantial ER and investment; off-field support and Burgeon instead focus on her Skill. Her Burst punches do not trigger Normal Attack-linked effects.

Best weapons for Dehya

  1. Beacon of the Reed Sea: High CRIT Rate and ATK buffs when Skill hits or Dehya takes damage; boosts HP when unshielded. Best for on-field damage; avoid shields for the HP passive.
  2. Wolf's Gravestone: Large ATK bonus and a teamwide ATK buff against low-HP enemies; straightforward Burst damage. Team ATK buff activates below 30% enemy HP.
  3. Redhorn Stonethresher: Very high CRIT DMG helps Burst builds, but its DEF-based Normal and Charged Attack passive does not buff Burst punches. Primarily a CRIT stat stick.
  4. Skyward Pride: Energy Recharge helps costly Burst rotations; high base ATK supports her punches. Vacuum blades require Normal or Charged Attacks and do not trigger from Burst punches.
  5. Akuoumaru: Boosts Burst damage based on the total Energy costs of the team, suiting high-cost Burst lineups. Best with high combined team Burst costs. High refinements improve value.
  6. Serpent Spine: CRIT Rate and stacking damage bonus can strengthen Burst DPS; stacks are harder to maintain when taking hits. A shielder helps retain stacks, but blocks some Beacon/Vourukasha synergies. Higher refinements increase damage.
  7. Favonius Greatsword: Generates neutral particles with on-field CRIT hits, helping teammates and offering ER for Dehya. Support utility; build enough CRIT Rate to trigger. Refinements help proc reliability.
  8. Tidal Shadow: Craftable ATK claymore; its ATK buff activates after Dehya receives healing, making it useful with a healer. Requires healing to activate the passive.

Best artifacts for Dehya

  • Emblem of Severed Fate (4 pcs) · Burst DPS: A consistent Burst-focused set: Energy Recharge and Burst DMG fit her high-cost on-field rotation. Main stats: ER% / ATK% · Pyro DMG · CRIT.
  • Marechaussee Hunter (4 pcs) · With Furina: HP changes grant CRIT Rate during on-field play; strong with Furina or reliable self-damage and healing. Main stats: ER% / ATK% · Pyro DMG · CRIT DMG.
  • Vourukasha's Glow (4 pcs) · Invested DPS: Boosts Skill and Burst damage after Dehya takes damage; stronger with reliable stacks and lower ER needs. Main stats: ATK% / ER% · Pyro DMG · CRIT.

Main stats

  • Sands: Energy Recharge / ATK% (HP% at C1)
  • Goblet: Pyro DMG Bonus
  • Circlet: CRIT Rate / CRIT DMG

Stat targets

  • CRIT Rate: 60% - 75%
  • CRIT DMG: 120% - 180%+
  • Energy Recharge: 150% - 225%
  • ATK: 1,600 - 2,100+
  • HP: 25,000 - 35,000+ (C1/support)

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 8-9
  • Elemental Burst: Level 8-9

Best teams for Dehya

  • Flame-Mane Encore: Dehya (Burst DPS), Furina (Hydro Sub DPS / Buffer), Kaedehara Kazuha (Anemo Buffer), Bennett (Healer / ATK Buffer). Furina enables Vaporize and damage buffs, Kazuha shreds Pyro RES, and Bennett buffs and heals Dehya. Manage Furina team HP.
  • Burn Notice: Dehya (Burst DPS), Emilie (Burning Sub DPS), Bennett (Healer / ATK Buffer), Kaedehara Kazuha (Anemo Buffer). Burning activates Emilie while Dehya drives Pyro damage. Bennett powers Burst; Kazuha groups and shreds Pyro RES.
  • Overload and Out: Dehya (Burst DPS), Raiden Shogun (Electro DPS / Battery), Chevreuse (Overload Buffer / Healer), Fischl (Electro Sub DPS). Chevreuse amplifies Pyro/Electro teams, Fischl supplies off-field Electro, and Raiden helps recharge Dehya’s costly Burst.
  • Budget Fire Brigade: Dehya (Burst DPS), Xiangling (Pyro Sub DPS), Bennett (Healer / Battery), Sucrose (Anemo Driver / VV). An accessible Mono Pyro core: Bennett batteries and buffs, Xiangling adds damage, and Sucrose groups and shreds Pyro RES.

Pros

Build variations

Furina Vaporize Burst (On-field Burst DPS)

Choose this for an invested on-field Dehya with reliable HP fluctuations and Furina buffs.

  • Weapons: Beacon of the Reed Sea, Wolf's Gravestone, Tidal Shadow
  • Artifacts: Marechaussee Hunter (4 pcs); Emblem of Severed Fate (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Build enough ER to Burst every rotation, then CRIT and ATK. Marechaussee adds up to 36% CRIT Rate; avoid overcapping.
  • Teams: Furina with dependable healing, plus Pyro energy support; Kazuha or another RES shredder helps.
  • When it applies: Furina + consistent HP changes; Burst punches cannot trigger Xingqiu/Yelan Burst attacks.
  • Notes: Furina application, reaction frequency and ER needs vary by rotation; test your exact team.

High-Constellation Burst (On-field Burst DPS)

For players with C4–C6 who can maintain damage-taken stacks and Burst uptime.

  • Weapons: Beacon of the Reed Sea, Wolf's Gravestone, Akuoumaru
  • Artifacts: Vourukasha's Glow (4 pcs); Emblem of Severed Fate (4 pcs)
  • Stats: C1 makes HP% more valuable; C4 reduces ER burden, permitting more CRIT and offensive stats.
  • Teams: Pyro battery and healing; Dehya needs to take damage for Vourukasha stacks.
  • When it applies: Damage must reach Dehya; shield-heavy teams can reduce set uptime.
  • Notes: Emblem is still a dependable alternative, especially when ER demands stay high.

Off-Field Defensive Support (Off-field Support)

Pick this for Skill-based protection and Tenacity ATK buffs instead of Dehya’s personal damage.

  • Weapons: Favonius Greatsword, Sacrificial Greatsword, Wolf's Gravestone
  • Artifacts: Tenacity of the Millelith (4 pcs); Instructor (4 pcs)
  • Stats: HP% / HP% / HP% for mitigation; use CRIT Rate circlet with Favonius. Little or no Burst ER required.
  • Teams: An on-field ally who values interruption resistance and Pyro application. Team may still need healing.
  • When it applies: Her Skill has downtime; defensive mitigation is not a full substitute for healing.
  • Notes: Skill coordinated hits maintain Tenacity during field uptime. Instructor is a lower-rarity reaction alternative.

Burgeon Skill Trigger (Off-field Reaction DPS)

Use Dehya’s Skill to trigger occasional Burgeons without using Burst.

  • Weapons: Mailed Flower, Makhaira Aquamarine, Favonius Greatsword
  • Artifacts: Flower of Paradise Lost (4 pcs); Gilded Dreams (4 pcs)
  • Stats: EM / EM / EM; prioritize character level 90 and EM, with HP% if more protection is needed. No Burst ER required.
  • Teams: Hydro and Dendro sources to produce Bloom cores plus a healer or additional sustain.
  • When it applies: Coordinated Skill hits are slow and may miss cores; Burgeon performance is limited.
  • Notes: Do not recommend her as an especially consistent sole Burgeon trigger.

F2P-Friendly Burst (On-field Burst DPS)

For accessible weapon options while retaining Dehya’s on-field Burst playstyle.

  • Weapons: Tidal Shadow, Akuoumaru, Luxurious Sea-Lord
  • Artifacts: Emblem of Severed Fate (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ER Sands if needed, Pyro DMG Goblet, CRIT Circlet; balance ER before damage.
  • Teams: Bennett and preferably Xiangling or another Pyro teammate for particles; healer required for Tidal Shadow.
  • When it applies: Tidal Shadow needs healing; Luxurious Sea-Lord is an old limited event weapon.
  • Notes: Lower-constellation Burst damage is modest; prioritize comfortable rotations over perfect CRIT rolls.

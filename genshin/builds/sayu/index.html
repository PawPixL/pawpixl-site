Sayu Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

Sayu build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Favonius Greatsword, Wolf's Gravestone), artifacts (Viridescent Venerer), main stats, talent priority.

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Role: Healer / Support

Sayu is an Anemo healer who combines teamwide Burst healing with Viridescent Venerer RES shred. Her healing scales with ATK, while EM boosts Swirl damage and her active Swirl healing. Build enough Energy Recharge to Burst reliably; C6 makes EM-focused healing more effective.

Best weapons for Sayu

  1. Favonius Greatsword: ER and CRIT-triggered neutral particles help Sayu and the team sustain Burst rotations.
  2. Wolf's Gravestone: High ATK boosts Burst healing.
  3. Skyward Pride: High base ATK supports healing, while its ER substat helps maintain Burst uptime.
  4. Makhaira Aquamarine: Its EM substat supports Swirls, while its passive converts EM into ATK for Sayu and nearby allies.
  5. Katsuragikiri Nagamasa: Craftable Energy Recharge claymore with Skill damage and a delayed flat Energy refund, improving Burst consistency. Skill must hit to activate the passive. Refinements improve Energy refund.
  6. Sacrificial Greatsword: Energy Recharge plus a chance to reset Skill cooldown grants another cast and extra particles. The cooldown reset triggers from Skill damage, notably the finishing kick; extra cast takes field time. Higher refinements improve reset reliability.
  7. Forest Regalia: Craftable ER option; after triggering a Dendro-related reaction, creates a leaf that grants EM to its collector. Only in teams where Sayu can trigger the required Dendro reaction.
  8. Rainslasher: Elemental Mastery improves Swirl and transformative reactions for rolling on-field builds. Prioritize ER first; passive is situational against Hydro/Electro-affected enemies.

Best artifacts for Sayu

  • Viridescent Venerer (4 pcs) · Best: Best overall: Swirl lowers the affected Pyro/Hydro/Cryo/Electro RES by 40% and improves Swirl damage. Main stats: ER/EM/ATK% · EM/ATK% · EM/Healing Bonus/ATK%.
  • Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs): Burst grants allies 20% ATK for 12s; use when no teammate carries Noblesse and Sayu can Burst reliably. Main stats: ER/ATK% · ATK% · Healing Bonus/ATK%.
  • Ocean-Hued Clam (4 pcs): Converts healing into periodic physical damage; a niche option when Viridescent shred is already covered. Main stats: ER/ATK% · ATK% · Healing Bonus.

Main stats

  • Sands: Energy Recharge / ATK% / Elemental Mastery
  • Goblet: ATK% / Elemental Mastery
  • Circlet: Healing Bonus / Elemental Mastery / ATK%

Stat targets

  • ER off-field, every Burst: 200%+
  • ER off-field, alternate Burst: 150% - 170%
  • ER on-field driver: 160% - 180%
  • ER driver with C4: often 10% - 15% less
  • CRIT Rate with Favonius: 35% - 55%

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 6-8
  • Elemental Burst: Level 8-9+

Best teams for Sayu

  • Roly-Poly Taser: Sayu, Sangonomiya Kokomi, Fischl, Sucrose. Sayu rolls with Hydro absorption to Swirl and drive reactions; Kokomi and Fischl apply elements while Sucrose groups and shares EM.
  • Rolling Hyperbloom: Sayu (Driver / Healer), Sangonomiya Kokomi (Hydro Applier), Nahida (Dendro Support), Kuki Shinobu (Hyperbloom Trigger). Sayu absorbs Hydro and spreads it during her roll; Nahida sustains Dendro, and full-EM Kuki triggers Hyperbloom.
  • Sleepy Aggravate: Sayu (VV Healer), Keqing (Electro Main DPS), Fischl (Electro Sub DPS), Nahida (Dendro Support). Sayu heals and Swirls Electro to shred its RES; Nahida maintains Quicken for Keqing and Fischl Aggravates.
  • Firework Ninja: Sayu (VV Healer), Lyney (Pyro Main DPS), Bennett (ATK Buffer), Xiangling (Pyro Sub DPS). Sayu offers healing and Pyro RES shred for Lyney; Bennett buffs and Xiangling adds off-field Pyro damage.

Pros

  • Teamwide Burst healing
  • Viridescent RES shred
  • Flexible team support
  • Fun rolling reaction driver

Cons

  • High Burst energy demands
  • Limited crowd control
  • Rolling uses field time
  • EM healing improves at C6

Build variations

ATK-Focused Healer (Healer)

Use before C6 if you need stronger direct Burst healing and do not prioritize personal Swirl damage.

  • Weapons: Favonius Greatsword, Wolf's Gravestone, Katsuragikiri Nagamasa
  • Artifacts: Viridescent Venerer (4 pcs); Ocean-Hued Clam (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ER/ATK% Sands, ATK% Goblet, Healing Bonus Circlet; ER first, then ATK%.
  • Teams: Works wherever healing plus Anemo utility is needed.
  • When it applies: Very high ER can be needed as solo Anemo.

C6 EM Swirl Healer (Healer / Swirl Support)

At C6, EM increases Daruma damage and healing while enhancing Swirl damage.

  • Weapons: Makhaira Aquamarine, Rainslasher, Favonius Greatsword
  • Artifacts: Viridescent Venerer (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Build EM/EM/EM if Burst uptime permits; ER Sands if needed.
  • Teams: Teams that benefit from Swirl and can support her Energy needs.
  • When it applies: Requires Sayu C6.
  • Notes: C6 does not increase the initial teamwide Burst heal.

On-Field Rolling Driver (On-field Driver)

Roll to absorb an element, spread it, and trigger Swirls/reactions. Sayu Level 90 improves Swirl damage.

  • Weapons: Makhaira Aquamarine, Rainslasher, Katsuragikiri Nagamasa
  • Artifacts: Viridescent Venerer (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Prioritize EM and ER; Skill talent matters more than in off-field builds.
  • Teams: Use persistent off-field application; avoid reliance on Normal Attack-triggered effects.
  • When it applies: Requires substantial on-field time.
  • Notes: Rolling does not trigger Xingqiu/Yelan rain swords or Beidou Burst lightning.

Noblesse Team Buffer (Support)

Give ATK-scaling carries a 20% team ATK buff while retaining healing.

  • Weapons: Favonius Greatsword, Katsuragikiri Nagamasa
  • Artifacts: Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs); Viridescent Venerer (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Prioritize ER and ATK% for consistent Bursts and strong healing.
  • Teams: Use only if another unit already supplies Viridescent shred or it is not needed.
  • When it applies: Needs reliable Burst uptime; avoid duplicating Noblesse.

Accessible ER Healer (F2P Healer)

A budget path that prioritizes consistent Burst healing, then upgrades EM or ATK as Energy improves.

  • Weapons: Katsuragikiri Nagamasa, Forest Regalia, Favonius Greatsword
  • Artifacts: Viridescent Venerer (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ER Sands; ATK% Goblet; Healing Bonus Circlet.
  • Teams: No premium weapon required; crafted sword materials may be needed.
  • When it applies: Forest Regalia passive needs Sayu to trigger a Dendro reaction.

Sayu talents

Normal Attack: Shuumatsuban Ninja Blade

Normal Attack
Performs up to 4 consecutive strikes.

Charged Attack
Drains Stamina over time to perform continuous spinning attacks against all nearby opponents.
At the end of the sequence, performs a more powerful slash.

Plunging Attack
Plunges from mid-air to strike the ground below, damaging opponents along the path and dealing AoE DMG upon impact.

Elemental Skill: Yoohoo Art: Fuuin Dash

Cooldown: 6 to 11s

The special technique of the Yoohoo Ninja Arts!
Sayu curls up into a rolling Fuufuu Windwheel and smashes into opponents at high speed, dealing Anemo DMG. When the duration ends, she unleashes a Fuufuu Whirlwind Kick, dealing AoE Anemo DMG.

Press
Enters the Fuufuu Windwheel state, rolling forward a short distance before using the Fuufuu Whirlwind Kick.

Hold
Rolls about continuously in the Fuufuu Windwheel state, increasing Sayu's resistance to interruption while within that state.
During this time, Sayu can control the direction of her roll, and can use the skill again to end her Windwheel state early and unleash a stronger version of the Fuufuu Whirlwind Kick.
The Hold version of this skill can trigger Elemental Absorption.

This skill has a maximum duration of 10s and enters CD once its effects end. The longer Sayu remains in her Windwheel state, the longer the CD.

Elemental Absorption
If Sayu comes into contact with Hydro/Pyro/Cryo/Electro while in her Windwheel state, she will deal additional elemental DMG of that type.
Elemental Absorption may only occur once per use of this skill.

Elemental Burst: Yoohoo Art: Mujina Flurry

Cooldown: 20s · Energy cost: 80

The other super special technique of the Yoohoo Ninja Arts! It summons a pair of helping hands for Sayu.
Deals Anemo DMG to nearby opponents and heals all nearby party members. The amount of HP restored is based on Sayu's ATK. This skill then summons a Muji-Muji Daruma.

Muji-Muji Daruma
At specific intervals, the Daruma will take one of several actions based on the situation around it:
·If the HP of nearby characters is above 70%, it will attack a nearby opponent, dealing Anemo DMG.
·If there are active characters with 70% or less HP nearby, it will heal the active character with the lowest percentage HP left. If there are no opponents nearby, it will heal active characters nearby even if they have 70% HP or more.

Passive talent: Someone More Capable

When Sayu triggers a Swirl or Stellar Swirl reaction while active, she heals all your characters and nearby allies for 300 HP. She will also heal an additional 1.2 HP for every point of Elemental Mastery she has.
This effect can be triggered once every 2s.

Passive talent: No Work Today!

The Muji-Muji Daruma created by Yoohoo Art: Mujina Flurry gains the following effects:
·When healing a character, it will also heal characters near that healed character for 20% the amount of HP.
·Increases the AoE of its attack against opponents.

Passive talent: Yoohoo Art: Silencer's Secret

When Sayu is in the party, your characters will not startle Crystalflies and certain other animals when getting near them.
Check the "Other" sub-category of the "Living Beings / Wildlife" section in the Archive for creatures this skill works on.

Sayu constellations

C1: Multi-Task no Jutsu

The Muji-Muji Daruma created by Yoohoo Art: Mujina Flurry will ignore HP limits and can simultaneously attack nearby opponents and heal characters.

C2: Egress Prep

Yoohoo Art: Fuuin Dash gains the following effects:
·DMG of Fuufuu Whirlwind Kick in Press Mode increased by 3.3%.
·Every 0.5s in the Fuufuu Windwheel state will increase the DMG of this Fuufuu Whirlwind Kick by 3.3%. The maximum DMG increase possible through this method is 66%.

C3: Eh, the Bunshin Can Handle It

Increases the Level of Yoohoo Art: Mujina Flurry by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C4: Skiving: New and Improved

Sayu recovers 1.2 Energy when she triggers a Swirl or Stellar Swirl reaction while on-field.
This effect occurs once every 2s.

C5: Speed Comes First

Increases the Level of Yoohoo Art: Fuuin Dash by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C6: Sleep O'Clock

The Muji-Muji Daruma created by Sayu's Yoohoo Art: Mujina Flurry will now also benefit from her Elemental Mastery. Each point of Sayu's Elemental Mastery will produce the following effects:
·Increases the damage dealt by the Muji-Muji Daruma's attacks by 0.2% ATK. A maximum DMG increase of 400% ATK can be gained via this method.
·Increases the HP restored by the Muji-Muji Daruma by 3. A maximum of 6,000 additional HP can be restored in this manner.

Role: Healer / Support

Sayu is an Anemo healer who combines teamwide Burst healing with Viridescent Venerer RES shred. Her healing scales with ATK, while EM boosts Swirl damage and her active Swirl healing. Build enough Energy Recharge to Burst reliably; C6 makes EM-focused healing more effective.

  2. Wolf's Gravestone
  3. Skyward Pride
  4. Makhaira Aquamarine
  5. Katsuragikiri Nagamasa
  6. Sacrificial Greatsword
  7. Forest Regalia

Main stats

  • Sands: Energy Recharge / ATK% / Elemental Mastery
  • Goblet: ATK% / Elemental Mastery
  • Circlet: Healing Bonus / Elemental Mastery / ATK%

Stat targets

  • ER off-field, every Burst: 200%+
  • ER off-field, alternate Burst: 150% - 170%
  • ER on-field driver: 160% - 180%
  • ER driver with C4: often 10% - 15% less
  • CRIT Rate with Favonius: 35% - 55%

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 6-8
  • Elemental Burst: Level 8-9+

Best teams

  • Rolling Hyperbloom: Sayu, Sangonomiya Kokomi, Nahida, Kuki Shinobu
  • Sleepy Aggravate: Sayu, Keqing, Fischl, Nahida

Pros

  • Teamwide Burst healing
  • Viridescent RES shred
  • Flexible team support
  • Fun rolling reaction driver

Cons

  • High Burst energy demands
  • Limited crowd control
  • Rolling uses field time
  • EM healing improves at C6

Build variations

  • ATK-Focused Healer (Healer): Use before C6 if you need stronger direct Burst healing and do not prioritize personal Swirl damage.
  • C6 EM Swirl Healer (Healer / Swirl Support): At C6, EM increases Daruma damage and healing while enhancing Swirl damage.
  • On-Field Rolling Driver (On-field Driver): Roll to absorb an element, spread it, and trigger Swirls/reactions. Sayu Level 90 improves Swirl damage.
  • Noblesse Team Buffer (Support): Give ATK-scaling carries a 20% team ATK buff while retaining healing.
  • Accessible ER Healer (F2P Healer): A budget path that prioritizes consistent Burst healing, then upgrades EM or ATK as Energy improves.

Role: DPS / Support

Anastasya switches between two Cryo playstyles. Tap Skill summons an off-field Azure Star Crown for Cryo hits and Stellar Glimmer support; Hold Skill gives her Cryo-infused spear attacks for on-field damage. Her Burst changes with her stance. Pre-release kit; details may change.

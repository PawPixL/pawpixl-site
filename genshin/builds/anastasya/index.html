Anastasya Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

Anastasya build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Silverdawn, The First Great Magic), artifacts (Heart of the Furnace), main stats, talent priority.

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Role: DPS / Support

Anastasya switches between two Cryo playstyles. Tap Skill summons an off-field Azure Star Crown for Cryo hits and Stellar Glimmer support; Hold Skill gives her Cryo-infused spear attacks for on-field damage. Her Burst changes with her stance. Pre-release kit; details may change.

Best weapons for Anastasya

  1. Silverdawn: Likely Anastasya's BiS weapon, providing substantial ATK, CRIT DMG, Stellar reaction dmg, and ER to boost her overall DPS.
  2. The First Great Magic: Provides high CRIT DMG, ATK buffs, and movement speed for Charged Attack-focused builds.
  3. Aqua Simulacra
  4. Polar Star
  5. Jade Vista: A strong CRIT DMG alternative, offering high personal damage but lacking Silver Dawn's ATK and Stellar Glimmer bonuses.
  6. Breezeborne Refrain: Providing CRIT Rate, Energy Recharge, and a teamwide Stellar Glimmer reaction DMG.
  7. King's Squire
  8. Windblume Ode

Best artifacts for Anastasya

  • Heart of the Furnace (4 pcs) · Reaction Support Idea: Consider only if Anastasya can trigger the set’s required Stellar reaction and the party benefits from its buff. Main stats: Provisional · check kit.
  • Disenchantment in Deep Shadow (4 pcs) · Reaction DPS Idea: Potential personal-damage option only if her released kit meets this set’s reaction conditions. Main stats: Provisional · check kit.
  • Gladiator's Finale + Shimenawa's Reminiscence (2 + 2 pcs) · Temporary ATK Mix: A generic ATK placeholder if her final damage scales with ATK; not a confirmed best set. Main stats: ATK · provisional.

Best teams for Anastasya

  • Royal Voltage: Anastasya (On-field DPS), Mitya (Off-field DPS / Enabler), Odette (Cryo Enabler / Buffer), Danica (Shielder/ Buffer). Hold Skill puts Anastasya on field. Mitya supplies Electro, Odette buffs Glimmer, and Danica shields and boosts Stellar-Conduct.
  • Conducting Chaos: Anastasya (Off-field Cryo Enabler), Sandrone (On-field DPS), Yae Miko (Off-field DPS), Danica (Shielder / Buffer). Tap Skill for Anastasya’s off-field Cryo Crown. Sandrone drives the team, Yae adds Electro, and Danica shields and boosts Conduct.
  • Snowball Effect: Anastasya (Cryo Enabler / Buffer), Vesna (On-field DPS), Odette (Cryo Enabler / Buffer), Vodyanitsa (Healer / Buffer). Anastasya’s Crown supplies Cryo and buffs Glimmer. Vesna drives Stellar Swirl; Odette boosts reactions and Vodyanitsa sustains.
  • Cold as Ice: Anastasya (Off-field Cryo Enabler), Wriothesley (On-field DPS), Furina (Off-field DPS / Buffer), Escoffier (Healer / Debuffer). Tap Skill for extra Cryo while Wriothesley stays on field. Furina buffs damage, and Escoffier heals and shreds Cryo/Hydro RES.

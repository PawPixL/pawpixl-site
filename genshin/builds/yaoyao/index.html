Role: Healer / Support

Yaoyao heals and applies Dendro with Yuegui for Quicken, Bloom and Hyperbloom teams. Her Skill heals off-field, while her Burst heals the party but requires field time. Build HP and enough Energy Recharge for your Burst needs.

Best weapons for Yaoyao

  1. Favonius Lance
  2. Dialogues of the Desert Sages
  3. Moonpiercer
  4. Kitain Cross Spear
  5. Black Tassel
  6. Rightful Reward
  7. Skyward Spine
  8. Dragon's Bane

Best artifacts

  • Deepwood Memories (4 pcs)
  • Tenacity of the Millelith (4 pcs)
  • Instructor (4 pcs)

Main stats

  • Sands: HP% / Energy Recharge
  • Goblet: HP%
  • Circlet: Healing Bonus / HP%

Stat targets

  • HP: 30k - 40k
  • ER: 100% - 150%
  • Burst ER: 190%+
  • Fav CR: 40%+

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Not Priority
  • Elemental Skill: Lvl 8 - 10
  • Elemental Burst: Lvl 6 - 9

Best teams

  • Keqing Garden: Yaoyao, Keqing, Fischl, Kaedehara Kazuha
  • Blooming Oasis: Yaoyao, Nilou, Sangonomiya Kokomi, Nahida
  • Seedling Hyperbloom: Yaoyao, Xingqiu, Kuki Shinobu, Nahida
  • Budget Bloom Squad: Yaoyao, Traveler (Dendro), Xingqiu, Kuki Shinobu

Pros

  • Reliable off-field healing
  • Dendro application
  • Flexible reaction teams
  • Useful at C0

Cons

  • Burst takes field time
  • Low personal damage
  • Solo Dendro can need help
  • ER rises with frequent Burst

Build variations

  • Nilou Bloom Burst Healer (Healer): Use for heavy Bountiful Core self-damage. Burst heals the party rapidly but Yaoyao must remain on-field.
  • Bloom Trigger EM (On-field Reaction Driver): Use only if Yaoyao triggers a meaningful share of Bloom reactions. More EM does not improve cores created by other characters.
  • Favonius Team Battery (Support): Swap in for Skill and land a CRIT hit to generate particles, then return to your carry. Burst for emergency healing.
  • Instructor Reaction Buffer (Support): Trigger a reaction while Yaoyao is on-field, then swap to grant team Elemental Mastery.
  • Tenacity ATK Buffer (Healer / Buffer): Yuegui Skill hits refresh Tenacity ATK buffs while Yaoyao heals from off-field.

Yaoyao Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

Yaoyao build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Favonius Lance, Dialogues of the Desert Sages), artifacts (Deepwood Memories), main stats.

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Role: Healer / Support

Yaoyao heals and applies Dendro with Yuegui for Quicken, Bloom and Hyperbloom teams. Her Skill heals off-field, while her Burst heals the party but requires field time. Build HP and enough Energy Recharge for your Burst needs.

Best weapons for Yaoyao

  1. Favonius Lance: CRIT hits generate team particles. Build CRIT Rate; passive procs on-field.
  2. Dialogues of the Desert Sages: HP and Energy from healing. Event weapon; passive needs healing.
  3. Moonpiercer: Reaction leaf buffs ally ATK. Best for ATK-scaling teammates.
  4. Kitain Cross Spear: EM and Skill-based Energy refund. Especially useful for Nilou Bloom.
  5. Black Tassel: Huge HP% for stronger healing.
  6. Rightful Reward: HP% and Energy after healing. Passive needs Yaoyao to receive healing.
  7. Skyward Spine: ER stat; limited passive value.
  8. Dragon's Bane: High EM for Bloom ownership. Use only if Yaoyao triggers Bloom.

Best artifacts for Yaoyao

  • Deepwood Memories (4 pcs): Shreds enemy Dendro RES by 30%. Main stats: ER/HP% · HP% · Heal.
  • Tenacity of the Millelith (4 pcs): Skill hits grant party ATK buffs. Main stats: HP% · HP% · Heal.
  • Instructor (4 pcs): On-field reaction grants team EM. Main stats: ER/HP% · HP% · Heal.

Main stats

  • Sands: HP% / Energy Recharge
  • Goblet: HP%
  • Circlet: Healing Bonus / HP%

Stat targets

  • HP: 30k - 40k
  • ER: 100% - 150%
  • Burst ER: 190%+
  • Fav CR: 40%+

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Not Priority
  • Elemental Skill: Lvl 8 - 10
  • Elemental Burst: Lvl 6 - 9

Best teams for Yaoyao

  • Keqing Garden: Yaoyao, Keqing, Fischl, Kaedehara Kazuha. Yaoyao sustains Quicken and heals. Fischl adds Aggravate damage while Kazuha shreds Electro RES and buffs Keqing.
  • Blooming Oasis: Yaoyao (Dendro Healer), Nilou (Bloom Enabler), Sangonomiya Kokomi (Hydro Driver), Nahida (Dendro Support). Nilou enables Bountiful Cores; Nahida supplies Dendro and Kokomi drives Bloom. Yaoyao adds healing against self-damage.
  • Seedling Hyperbloom: Yaoyao (Dendro Healer), Xingqiu (Hydro Sub DPS), Kuki Shinobu (Hyperbloom Trigger), Nahida (Dendro Support). Xingqiu makes cores with Nahida; full-EM Kuki triggers Hyperbloom. Yaoyao adds Dendro and emergency healing.
  • Budget Bloom Squad: Yaoyao (Dendro Healer), Traveler (Dendro) (Dendro Support), Xingqiu (Hydro Sub DPS), Kuki Shinobu (Hyperbloom Trigger). Traveler maintains Dendro with Yaoyao; Xingqiu creates cores and full-EM Kuki triggers Hyperbloom. Accessible core team.

Pros

  • Reliable off-field healing
  • Dendro application
  • Flexible reaction teams
  • Useful at C0

Cons

  • Burst takes field time
  • Low personal damage
  • Solo Dendro can need help
  • ER rises with frequent Burst

Build variations

Nilou Bloom Burst Healer (Healer)

Use for heavy Bountiful Core self-damage. Burst heals the party rapidly but Yaoyao must remain on-field.

  • Weapons: Dialogues of the Desert Sages, Kitain Cross Spear, Favonius Lance
  • Artifacts: Deepwood Memories (4 pcs); Instructor (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ER/HP% sands, HP% goblet, Healing Bonus circlet; aim to Burst each rotation when necessary.
  • Teams: Nilou plus Hydro and Dendro only; enough teammates to create Bountiful Cores.
  • When it applies: High ER required; C2 helps greatly.

Bloom Trigger EM (On-field Reaction Driver)

Use only if Yaoyao triggers a meaningful share of Bloom reactions. More EM does not improve cores created by other characters.

  • Weapons: Kitain Cross Spear, Dragon's Bane, Moonpiercer
  • Artifacts: Flower of Paradise Lost (4 pcs); Gilded Dreams (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Build enough ER first, then EM/EM/EM when Yaoyao owns substantial Bloom damage.
  • Teams: Consistent Hydro application; ideally Nilou Bloom, with safe healing.
  • When it applies: Reaction ownership determines damage.

Favonius Team Battery (Support)

Swap in for Skill and land a CRIT hit to generate particles, then return to your carry. Burst for emergency healing.

  • Weapons: Favonius Lance, Dialogues of the Desert Sages
  • Artifacts: Deepwood Memories (4 pcs); The Exile (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Use CRIT Rate circlet if needed to trigger Favonius; HP% and ER afterward.
  • Teams: Teams needing extra particles; Yaoyao must hit on-field for Favonius.
  • When it applies: Needs enough CRIT Rate.

Instructor Reaction Buffer (Support)

Trigger a reaction while Yaoyao is on-field, then swap to grant team Elemental Mastery.

  • Weapons: Favonius Lance, Moonpiercer
  • Artifacts: Instructor (4 pcs); Deepwood Memories (4 pcs)
  • Stats: HP%/ER healer stats; some CRIT Rate for Favonius if equipped.
  • Teams: Reaction-based teammates; another unit should hold Deepwood when using Instructor.
  • When it applies: 4-star artifacts have lower main stats.

Tenacity ATK Buffer (Healer / Buffer)

Yuegui Skill hits refresh Tenacity ATK buffs while Yaoyao heals from off-field.

  • Weapons: Black Tassel, Favonius Lance
  • Artifacts: Tenacity of the Millelith (4 pcs); Deepwood Memories (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Focus HP% and Healing Bonus; ER as needed.
  • Teams: ATK-scaling damage dealers; another unit holds Deepwood Memories.
  • When it applies: Not ideal when Dendro RES shred is missing.

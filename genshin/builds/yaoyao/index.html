Yaoyao Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

Yaoyao build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Favonius Lance, Dialogues of the Desert Sages), artifacts (Deepwood Memories), main stats.

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Role: Healer / Support

Yaoyao heals and applies Dendro with Yuegui for Quicken, Bloom and Hyperbloom teams. Her Skill heals off-field, while her Burst heals the party but requires field time. Build HP and enough Energy Recharge for your Burst needs.

Best weapons for Yaoyao

  1. Favonius Lance: CRIT hits generate team particles. Build CRIT Rate; passive procs on-field.
  2. Dialogues of the Desert Sages: HP and Energy from healing. Event weapon; passive needs healing.
  3. Moonpiercer: Reaction leaf buffs ally ATK. Best for ATK-scaling teammates.
  4. Kitain Cross Spear: EM and Skill-based Energy refund. Especially useful for Nilou Bloom.
  5. Black Tassel: Huge HP% for stronger healing.
  6. Rightful Reward: HP% and Energy after healing. Passive needs Yaoyao to receive healing.
  7. Skyward Spine: ER stat; limited passive value.
  8. Dragon's Bane: High EM for Bloom ownership. Use only if Yaoyao triggers Bloom.

Best artifacts for Yaoyao

  • Deepwood Memories (4 pcs): Shreds enemy Dendro RES by 30%. Main stats: ER/HP% · HP% · Heal.
  • Tenacity of the Millelith (4 pcs): Skill hits grant party ATK buffs. Main stats: HP% · HP% · Heal.
  • Instructor (4 pcs): On-field reaction grants team EM. Main stats: ER/HP% · HP% · Heal.

Main stats

  • Sands: HP% / Energy Recharge
  • Goblet: HP%
  • Circlet: Healing Bonus / HP%

Stat targets

  • HP: 30k - 40k
  • ER: 100% - 150%
  • Burst ER: 190%+
  • Fav CR: 40%+

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Not Priority
  • Elemental Skill: Lvl 8 - 10
  • Elemental Burst: Lvl 6 - 9

Best teams for Yaoyao

  • Keqing Garden: Yaoyao (Healer / Dendro Enabler), Keqing (On-field DPS), Fischl (Off-field DPS / Battery), Kaedehara Kazuha (Buffer / Debuffer). Yaoyao sustains Quicken and heals. Fischl adds Aggravate damage while Kazuha shreds Electro RES and buffs Keqing.
  • Blooming Oasis: Yaoyao (Dendro Healer), Nilou (Bloom Enabler), Sangonomiya Kokomi (Hydro Driver), Nahida (Dendro Support). Nilou enables Bountiful Cores; Nahida supplies Dendro and Kokomi drives Bloom. Yaoyao adds healing against self-damage.
  • Seedling Hyperbloom: Yaoyao (Dendro Healer), Xingqiu (Hydro Sub DPS), Kuki Shinobu (Hyperbloom Trigger), Nahida (Dendro Support). Xingqiu makes cores with Nahida; full-EM Kuki triggers Hyperbloom. Yaoyao adds Dendro and emergency healing.
  • Budget Bloom Squad: Yaoyao (Dendro Healer), Traveler (Dendro) (Dendro Support), Xingqiu (Hydro Sub DPS), Kuki Shinobu (Hyperbloom Trigger). Traveler maintains Dendro with Yaoyao; Xingqiu creates cores and full-EM Kuki triggers Hyperbloom. Accessible core team.

Pros

  • Reliable off-field healing
  • Dendro application
  • Flexible reaction teams
  • Useful at C0

Cons

  • Burst takes field time
  • Low personal damage
  • Solo Dendro can need help
  • ER rises with frequent Burst

Build variations

Nilou Bloom Burst Healer (Healer)

Use for heavy Bountiful Core self-damage. Burst heals the party rapidly but Yaoyao must remain on-field.

  • Weapons: Dialogues of the Desert Sages, Kitain Cross Spear, Favonius Lance
  • Artifacts: Deepwood Memories (4 pcs); Instructor (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ER/HP% sands, HP% goblet, Healing Bonus circlet; aim to Burst each rotation when necessary.
  • Teams: Nilou plus Hydro and Dendro only; enough teammates to create Bountiful Cores.
  • When it applies: High ER required; C2 helps greatly.

Bloom Trigger EM (On-field Reaction Driver)

Use only if Yaoyao triggers a meaningful share of Bloom reactions. More EM does not improve cores created by other characters.

  • Weapons: Kitain Cross Spear, Dragon's Bane, Moonpiercer
  • Artifacts: Flower of Paradise Lost (4 pcs); Gilded Dreams (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Build enough ER first, then EM/EM/EM when Yaoyao owns substantial Bloom damage.
  • Teams: Consistent Hydro application; ideally Nilou Bloom, with safe healing.
  • When it applies: Reaction ownership determines damage.

Favonius Team Battery (Support)

Swap in for Skill and land a CRIT hit to generate particles, then return to your carry. Burst for emergency healing.

  • Weapons: Favonius Lance, Dialogues of the Desert Sages
  • Artifacts: Deepwood Memories (4 pcs); The Exile (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Use CRIT Rate circlet if needed to trigger Favonius; HP% and ER afterward.
  • Teams: Teams needing extra particles; Yaoyao must hit on-field for Favonius.
  • When it applies: Needs enough CRIT Rate.

Instructor Reaction Buffer (Support)

Trigger a reaction while Yaoyao is on-field, then swap to grant team Elemental Mastery.

  • Weapons: Favonius Lance, Moonpiercer
  • Artifacts: Instructor (4 pcs); Deepwood Memories (4 pcs)
  • Stats: HP%/ER healer stats; some CRIT Rate for Favonius if equipped.
  • Teams: Reaction-based teammates; another unit should hold Deepwood when using Instructor.
  • When it applies: 4-star artifacts have lower main stats.

Tenacity ATK Buffer (Healer / Buffer)

Yuegui Skill hits refresh Tenacity ATK buffs while Yaoyao heals from off-field.

  • Weapons: Black Tassel, Favonius Lance
  • Artifacts: Tenacity of the Millelith (4 pcs); Deepwood Memories (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Focus HP% and Healing Bonus; ER as needed.
  • Teams: ATK-scaling damage dealers; another unit holds Deepwood Memories.
  • When it applies: Not ideal when Dendro RES shred is missing.

Yaoyao talents

Normal Attack: Toss 'N' Turn Spear

Normal Attack
Performs up to 4 consecutive spear strikes.

Charged Attack
Consumes a certain amount of Stamina to lunge forward, dealing damage to opponents along the way.

Plunging Attack
Plunges from mid-air to strike the ground below, damaging opponents along the path and dealing AoE DMG upon impact.

Elemental Skill: Raphanus Sky Cluster

Cooldown: 15s

Calls upon "Yuegui: Throwing Mode," a special device created by a certain adeptus to help Yaoyao solve her problems.
This skill will be used differently in Holding Mode.

Hold
Enters Aiming Mode to adjust the throw direction.

Yuegui: Throwing Mode
Throws out White Jade Radishes that will explode upon hitting characters or opponents, dealing Dendro DMG to opponents within a certain AoE, and healing characters within that same AoE based on Yaoyao's Max HP. If a radish does not hit either an opponent or a character, the radish will remain where it is and explode on contact with a character or opponent, or will explode after its duration expires.
Yuegui: Throwing Mode will choose its radish-throw targets.
·If all nearby characters have more than 70% HP remaining, then it will throw the radish at a nearby opponent.
·If nearby characters have 70% or less HP remaining, it will throw a radish at the character with the lowest HP percentage remaining. If no opponents exist nearby, Yuegui will also throw White Jade Radishes at characters if they all have more than 70% HP and less than 100% HP remaining. Otherwise, it will throw radishes into the area at random.

A maximum of 2 instances of Yuegui: Throwing Mode can exist at any one time.

Elemental Burst: Moonjade Descent

Cooldown: 20s · Energy cost: 80

At the enjoinment of a certain adeptus, Yuegui's full potential can be unleashed in an emergency, dealing Dendro DMG to nearby opponents and entering an (in some sense) unsurpassed Adeptal Legacy state.

Adeptal Legacy
·White Jade Radishes generated will be changed to heal and deal DMG according to this skill. Explosions will heal all nearby party members, and the Dendro DMG that they deal will be viewed as Elemental Burst DMG instead.
·Summons "Yuegui: Jumping Mode" at intervals until their limit has been reached. The behavior of this version of Yuegui is the same as that of "Yuegui: Throwing Mode" in the Elemental Skill, Raphanus Sky Cluster. A maximum of 3 Yuegui: Jumping Mode can exist at any one time.
·Yaoyao's Movement SPD is increased by 15%.
·Yaoyao's Dendro RES will be increased.

The Adeptal Legacy state will end once Yaoyao is off-field, and all remaining Yuegui: Jumping Mode will be cleared once this state ends.

Passive talent: Starscatter

While affected by the Adeptal Legacy state caused by Moonjade Descent, Yaoyao will constantly throw White Jade Radishes at nearby opponents when she is sprinting, jumping, or running. She can throw 1 White Jade Radish this way once every 0.6s.

Passive talent: In Others' Shoes

When White Jade Radishes explode, active characters within their AoE will regain HP every 1s based on 0.8% of Yaoyao's Max HP. This effect lasts 5s.

Passive talent: Tailing on Tiptoes

When Yaoyao is in the party, your characters will not startle Crystalflies and certain other animals when getting near them.
Check the "Other" sub-category of the "Living Beings / Wildlife" section in the Archive for creatures this skill works on.

Yaoyao constellations

C1: Adeptus' Tutelage

When White Jade Radishes explode, active characters within their AoE will gain 15% Dendro DMG Bonus for 8s and have 15 Stamina restored to them. This form of Stamina Restoration can only be triggered every 5s.

C2: Innocent

While affected by the Adeptal Legacy state caused by Moonjade Descent, if White Jade Radish explosions hit opponents, 3 Energy will be restored to Yaoyao. This form of Energy regeneration can occur once every 0.8s.

C3: Loyal and Kind

Increases the Level of Raphanus Sky Cluster by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C4: Winsome

After using Raphanus Sky Cluster or Moonjade Descent, Yaoyao's Elemental Mastery will be increased based on 0.3% of her Max HP for 8s. The maximum Elemental Mastery she can gain this way is 120.

C5: Compassionate

Increases the Level of Moonjade Descent by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C6: Beneficent

For every 2 White Jade Radishes Yuegui: Throwing Mode throws out, the next throw will also release a Mega Radish that will have a larger AoE than the standard White Jade Radish and have the following effects upon exploding:
·Deals AoE Dendro DMG based on 75% of Yaoyao's ATK.
·Restores HP for the active character based on 7.5% of Yaoyao's Max HP.

Every Yuegui: Throwing Mode can throw out a maximum of 2 Mega Radishes.

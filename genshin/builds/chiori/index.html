Role: Off-Field DPS

Chiori is an off-field Geo DPS whose Tamoto dolls deal damage based on DEF and ATK. At C0, a Geo Construct or compatible Lunar-Crystallize interaction enables her second doll. C1 allows any other Geo teammate. Cast Skill twice for a quick swap and coordinated hits.

  1. Uraku Misugiri
  2. Primordial Jade Cutter
  3. Peak Patrol Song
  4. Harbinger of Dawn
  5. Cinnabar Spindle
  6. Wolf-Fang
  7. Flute of Ezpitzal
  8. Favonius Sword

Best artifacts

  • Husk of Opulent Dreams (4 pcs) · Strong Alternative
  • Golden Troupe + Husk of Opulent Dreams (2 + 2 pcs) · Easy Mix

Main stats

  • Sands: DEF%
  • Goblet: Geo DMG Bonus / DEF%
  • Circlet: CRIT Rate / CRIT DMG

Stat targets

  • CRIT Rate: 65% - 80%
  • CRIT DMG: 140% - 200%+
  • DEF: 2,000 - 2,400+
  • Energy Recharge: 100% - 130% (optional Burst)

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 8-9
  • Elemental Burst: Level 6-8 (optional)

Best teams

  • Oni Fashion Week: Chiori, Arataki Itto, Gorou, Zhongli
  • Runway Ricochet: Chiori, Navia, Furina, Bennett
  • Moonlit Couture: Chiori, Zibai, Linnea, Columbina
  • Budget Beauty Supplies: Chiori, Ningguang, Traveler (Geo), Bennett

Pros

  • Strong off-field Geo damage
  • Low field-time needs
  • Great Geo team synergy
  • Flexible DEF scaling

Cons

  • C0 construct dependency
  • Limited reaction utility
  • Burst often optional
  • C6 for on-field carry

Build variations

  • C0 Double-Tamoto Off-Field (Off-field DPS): Best for C0 Itto, Zhongli, Ningguang, or Geo Traveler setups; summon both dolls before swapping.
  • C1 Navia Double Geo (Off-field DPS): With C1, Chiori brings two dolls to Navia teams without needing a Geo Construct.
  • Budget DEF & CRIT (Off-field DPS): Low-spender setup using 3-star, craftable, or previously available event equipment.
  • Team Buff Utility (Support): A situational utility alternative when Chiori’s damage is not the team priority.
  • C6 On-Field Geo Carry (On-field DPS): For C6 owners using Chiori’s enhanced Normal Attacks as the primary DPS.

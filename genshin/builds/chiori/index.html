Chiori Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams (7.2)

Chiori build guide for Genshin Impact 7.2: best weapons (Uraku Misugiri, Primordial Jade Cutter), artifacts (Golden Troupe), main stats, talent priority.

Last updated: November 12, 2026 · Game version 7.2

Role: Off-Field DPS

Chiori is an off-field Geo DPS whose Tamoto dolls deal damage based on DEF and ATK. At C0, a Geo Construct or compatible Lunar-Crystallize interaction enables her second doll. C1 allows any other Geo teammate. Cast Skill twice for a quick swap and coordinated hits.

Best weapons for Chiori

  1. Uraku Misugiri: CD, Skill DMG and DEF bonuses suit Tamoto; Geo DMG from teammates strengthens its passive.
  2. Primordial Jade Cutter: Huge CR and an HP-based ATK bonus boost her dual-scaling DMG. Avoid excess CR.
  3. Peak Patrol Song: DEF-focused sword that can buff party Elemental DMG, but Chiori needs on-field Normal or Plunging hits to trigger it. Requires extra field time; utility choice.
  4. Harbinger of Dawn: Excellent low-cost CD and CR while her HP stays above 90%. Keep HP above 90%. R5 recommended.
  5. Cinnabar Spindle: DEF% and a DEF-scaling Skill hit bonus make this a powerful option for Tamoto.
  6. Wolf-Fang: CRIT Rate and Skill/Burst DMG bonuses help her personal output; off-field hits do not stack its CRIT passive. Skill/Burst CRIT stacks require on-field hits.
  7. Flute of Ezpitzal: Craftable DEF% weapon that can boost DEF after casting Skill, improving Tamoto damage. Accessible Natlan craftable.
  8. Favonius Sword: Provides Energy Recharge and team particles on CRIT hits; sacrifices personal damage for utility. Passive requires CRIT while Chiori is on-field.

Best artifacts for Chiori

  • Golden Troupe (4 pcs) · Best Off-field: Strong Skill DMG amplification for Tamoto, which deals most of Chiori’s damage when played off-field. Main stats: DEF% / Geo DMG or DEF% / CRIT.
  • Husk of Opulent Dreams (4 pcs) · Strong Alternative: Stacks DEF and Geo DMG for both dolls and Burst; a flexible option with strong substats. Main stats: DEF% / Geo DMG or DEF% / CRIT.
  • Golden Troupe + Husk of Opulent Dreams (2 + 2 pcs) · Easy Mix: Skill DMG plus DEF% is a practical interim set while farming a full 4-piece set. Main stats: DEF% / Geo DMG or DEF% / CRIT.

Main stats

  • Sands: DEF%
  • Goblet: Geo DMG Bonus / DEF%
  • Circlet: CRIT Rate / CRIT DMG

Stat targets

  • CRIT Rate: 65% - 80%
  • CRIT DMG: 140% - 200%+
  • DEF: 2,000 - 2,400+
  • Energy Recharge: 100% - 130% (optional Burst)

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 8-9
  • Elemental Burst: Level 6-8 (optional)

Best teams for Chiori

  • Oni Fashion Week: Chiori (Off-field Geo), Arataki Itto (Main DPS), Gorou (DEF / Geo Buff), Zhongli (Shield / RES Shred). Itto’s Ushi and Zhongli’s pillar are Geo Constructs, enabling two Tamoto dolls at C0; Gorou buffs DEF.
  • Runway Ricochet: Chiori (Off-field DPS), Navia (On-field DPS), Furina (Off-field DPS / Buffer), Bennett (Healer / Buffer). Navia gains Geo damage and Crystallize support; Furina buffs and Bennett heals. Chiori needs C1 for two dolls here.
  • Moonlit Couture: Chiori (Off-field DPS), Zibai (On-field DPS), Linnea (Off-field DPS / Buffer), Columbina (Off-field DPS / Buffer). A Lunar-Crystallize Geo core can enable Chiori’s second Tamoto even at C0; check team sustain before using.
  • Budget Beauty Supplies: Chiori (Off-field DPS), Ningguang (On-field DPS), Traveler (Geo) (Geo Battery / Sub DPS), Bennett (Healer / Buffer). Ningguang and Geo Traveler create constructs for both Tamoto dolls at C0; Bennett adds healing and ATK.

Build variations

C0 Double-Tamoto Off-Field (Off-field DPS)

Best for C0 Itto, Zhongli, Ningguang, or Geo Traveler setups; summon both dolls before swapping.

  • Weapons: Uraku Misugiri, Harbinger of Dawn, Cinnabar Spindle
  • Artifacts: Golden Troupe (4 pcs); Husk of Opulent Dreams (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Prioritize CRIT and DEF%; little ER is needed when Burst is skipped.
  • Teams: Use a Geo Construct teammate or compatible Lunar-Crystallize enabler.
  • When it applies: Needs construct or Lunar-Crystallize interaction.
  • Notes: The extra doll is a major part of Chiori’s personal output.

C1 Navia Double Geo (Off-field DPS)

With C1, Chiori brings two dolls to Navia teams without needing a Geo Construct.

  • Weapons: Uraku Misugiri, Primordial Jade Cutter, Harbinger of Dawn
  • Artifacts: Golden Troupe (4 pcs); Husk of Opulent Dreams (4 pcs)
  • Stats: DEF% / Geo or DEF% / CRIT. Tune ER only if using Burst.
  • Teams: Navia plus a healer or defensive support; another Geo character is required.
  • When it applies: C1 required for second Tamoto without another enabler.
  • Notes: At C0, this exact Navia/Furina/Bennett team produces only one doll.

Budget DEF & CRIT (Off-field DPS)

Low-spender setup using 3-star, craftable, or previously available event equipment.

  • Weapons: Harbinger of Dawn, Flute of Ezpitzal, Cinnabar Spindle
  • Artifacts: Golden Troupe (4 pcs); Golden Troupe + Husk of Opulent Dreams (2 + 2 pcs)
  • Stats: Aim for balanced CRIT and DEF; keep HP above 90% with Harbinger.
  • Teams: A Geo Construct teammate is preferred at C0.
  • When it applies: Cinnabar Spindle is past-event only.
  • Notes: Choose weapons by ownership; a well-refined Harbinger can be especially efficient.

Team Buff Utility (Support)

A situational utility alternative when Chiori’s damage is not the team priority.

  • Weapons: Peak Patrol Song, Favonius Sword
  • Artifacts: Husk of Opulent Dreams (4 pcs); Golden Troupe (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Build DEF for Peak Patrol; build CRIT Rate for Favonius triggering.
  • Teams: Teams that value elemental damage buffs or particles over Chiori’s own damage.
  • When it applies: Peak Patrol requires on-field hits; Favonius must CRIT on-field.
  • Notes: Do not assume the Peak Patrol buff activates from off-field dolls.

C6 On-Field Geo Carry (On-field DPS)

For C6 owners using Chiori’s enhanced Normal Attacks as the primary DPS.

  • Weapons: Uraku Misugiri, Primordial Jade Cutter
  • Artifacts: Husk of Opulent Dreams (4 pcs); Golden Troupe (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Invest in DEF%, Geo DMG, and CRIT; Normal Attack talent matters.
  • Teams: Geo teammates, buffs and sustain for extended field time.
  • When it applies: C6 only.
  • Notes: A distinct playstyle; do not use these priorities for standard off-field Chiori.

Chiori talents

Normal Attack: Weaving Blade

Normal Attack
Performs up to 4 rapid strikes.

Charged Attack
Consumes a certain amount of Stamina to unleash 2 rapid sword strikes.

Plunging Attack
Plunges from mid-air to strike the ground below, damaging opponents along the path and dealing AoE DMG upon impact.

Elemental Skill: Fluttering Hasode

Cooldown: 16s

Dashes nimbly forward with silken steps. Once this dash ends, Chiori will summon the automaton doll "Tamoto" beside her and sweep her blade upward, dealing AoE Geo DMG to nearby opponents based on her ATK and DEF.
Holding the Skill will cause it to behave differently.

Hold
Enter Aiming Mode to adjust the dash direction.

Tamoto
· Will slash at nearby opponents at intervals, dealing AoE Geo DMG based on Chiori's ATK and DEF.
· While active, if there are nearby Geo Construct(s) or Geo Construct(s) are created nearby, an additional Tamoto will be summoned next to your active character. Only 1 additional Tamoto can be summoned in this manner, and its duration is independently counted.

Elemental Burst: Hiyoku: Twin Blades

Cooldown: 13.5s · Energy cost: 50

Twin blades leave their sheaths as Chiori slices with the clean cuts of a master tailor, dealing AoE Geo DMG based on her ATK and DEF.

Passive talent: Tailor-Made

Gain different effects depending on the next action you take within a short duration after using Fluttering Hasode's upward sweep. If you Press the Elemental Skill, you will trigger the Tapestry effect. If you Press your Normal Attack, the Tailoring effect will be triggered instead.

Tapestry
· Switches to the next character in your roster.
· Grants all your party members "Seize the Moment": When your active party member's Normal Attacks, Charged Attacks, and Plunging Attacks hit a nearby opponent, "Tamoto" will execute a coordinated attack, dealing 100% of Fluttering Hasode's upward sweep DMG as AoE Geo DMG at the opponent's location. DMG dealt this way is considered Elemental Skill DMG.
· "Seize the Moment" lasts 8s, and 1 of "Tamoto"'s coordinated attack can be unleashed every 2s. 2 such coordinated attacks can occur per "Seize the Moment" effect duration.

Tailoring
· Chiori gains Geo infusion for 5s.

When on the field, if Chiori does not either Press her Elemental Skill or use a Normal Attack within a short time after using Fluttering Hasode's upward sweep, the Tailoring effect will be triggered by default.

Passive talent: The Finishing Touch

When a nearby party member creates a Geo Construct, Chiori will gain 20% Geo DMG Bonus for 20s.

Passive talent: Brocaded Collar's Beauteous Silhouette

When any party member is wearing an outfit apart from their default outfit, or is wearing a wind glider other than the Wings of First Flight, your party members will obtain the Swift Stride effect: Movement SPD is increased by 10%.
This effect does not take effect in Domains, Trounce Domains, and the Spiral Abyss. Swift Stride does not stack.

Chiori constellations

C1: Six Paths of Sage Silkcraft

The AoE of the automaton doll "Tamoto" summoned by Fluttering Hasode is increased by 50%.
Additionally, if there is a Geo party member other than Chiori, Fluttering Hasode will trigger the following after the dash is completed:
· Summon an additional Tamoto. Only one additional Tamoto can exist at the same time, whether summoned by Chiori this way or through the presence of a Geo Construct.
· Triggers the Ascension Talent "The Finishing Touch." You must first unlock the Ascension Talent "The Finishing Touch."

C2: In Five Colors Dyed

For 10s after using Hiyoku: Twin Blades, a simplified automaton doll, "Kinu," will be summoned next to your active character every 3s. Kinu will attack nearby opponents, dealing AoE Geo DMG equivalent to 170% of Tamoto's DMG. DMG dealt this way is considered Elemental Skill DMG.
Kinu will leave the field after 1 attack or after lasting 3s.

C3: Four Brocade Embellishments

Increases the Level of Fluttering Hasode by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C4: A Tailor's Three Courtesies

For 8s after triggering either follow-up effect of the Ascension Talent "Tailor-Made," when your current active character's Normal, Charged, or Plunging Attacks hit a nearby opponent, a simplified automaton doll, "Kinu," will be summoned near this opponent. You can summon 1 Kinu every 1s in this way, and up to 3 Kinu may be summoned this way during each instance of "Tailor-Made"'s Seize the Moment or Tailoring effect. The above effect can be triggered up to once every 15s.
You must first unlock the Ascension Talent "Tailor-Made."

C5: Two Silken Plumules

Increases the Level of Hiyoku: Twin Blades by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C6: Sole Principle Pursuit

After triggering a follow-up effect of the Ascension Talent "Tailor-Made," Chiori's own Fluttering Hasode's CD is decreased by 12s. You must first unlock the Ascension Talent "Tailor-Made.".
In addition, the DMG dealt by Chiori's own Normal Attacks is increased by an amount equal to 235% of her own DEF.

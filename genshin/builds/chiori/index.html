Chiori Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

Chiori build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Uraku Misugiri, Primordial Jade Cutter), artifacts (Golden Troupe), main stats, talent priority.

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Role: Off-Field DPS

Chiori is an off-field Geo DPS whose Tamoto dolls deal damage based on DEF and ATK. At C0, a Geo Construct or compatible Lunar-Crystallize interaction enables her second doll. C1 allows any other Geo teammate. Cast Skill twice for a quick swap and coordinated hits.

Best weapons for Chiori

  1. Uraku Misugiri: CD, Skill DMG and DEF bonuses suit Tamoto; Geo DMG from teammates strengthens its passive.
  2. Primordial Jade Cutter: Huge CR and an HP-based ATK bonus boost her dual-scaling DMG. Avoid excess CR.
  3. Peak Patrol Song: DEF-focused sword that can buff party Elemental DMG, but Chiori needs on-field Normal or Plunging hits to trigger it. Requires extra field time; utility choice.
  4. Harbinger of Dawn: Excellent low-cost CD and CR while her HP stays above 90%. Keep HP above 90%. R5 recommended.
  5. Cinnabar Spindle: DEF% and a DEF-scaling Skill hit bonus make this a powerful option for Tamoto.
  6. Wolf-Fang: CRIT Rate and Skill/Burst DMG bonuses help her personal output; off-field hits do not stack its CRIT passive. Skill/Burst CRIT stacks require on-field hits.
  7. Flute of Ezpitzal: Craftable DEF% weapon that can boost DEF after casting Skill, improving Tamoto damage. Accessible Natlan craftable.
  8. Favonius Sword: Provides Energy Recharge and team particles on CRIT hits; sacrifices personal damage for utility. Passive requires CRIT while Chiori is on-field.

Best artifacts for Chiori

  • Golden Troupe (4 pcs) · Best Off-field: Strong Skill DMG amplification for Tamoto, which deals most of Chiori’s damage when played off-field. Main stats: DEF% / Geo DMG or DEF% / CRIT.
  • Husk of Opulent Dreams (4 pcs) · Strong Alternative: Stacks DEF and Geo DMG for both dolls and Burst; a flexible option with strong substats. Main stats: DEF% / Geo DMG or DEF% / CRIT.
  • Golden Troupe + Husk of Opulent Dreams (2 + 2 pcs) · Easy Mix: Skill DMG plus DEF% is a practical interim set while farming a full 4-piece set. Main stats: DEF% / Geo DMG or DEF% / CRIT.

Main stats

  • Sands: DEF%
  • Goblet: Geo DMG Bonus / DEF%
  • Circlet: CRIT Rate / CRIT DMG

Stat targets

  • CRIT Rate: 65% - 80%
  • CRIT DMG: 140% - 200%+
  • DEF: 2,000 - 2,400+
  • Energy Recharge: 100% - 130% (optional Burst)

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 8-9
  • Elemental Burst: Level 6-8 (optional)

Best teams for Chiori

  • Oni Fashion Week: Chiori, Arataki Itto, Gorou, Zhongli. Itto’s Ushi and Zhongli’s pillar are Geo Constructs, enabling two Tamoto dolls at C0; Gorou buffs DEF.
  • Runway Ricochet: Chiori, Navia, Furina, Bennett. Navia gains Geo damage and Crystallize support; Furina buffs and Bennett heals. Chiori needs C1 for two dolls here.
  • Moonlit Couture: Chiori, Zibai, Linnea, Columbina. A Lunar-Crystallize Geo core can enable Chiori’s second Tamoto even at C0; check team sustain before using.
  • Budget Beauty Supplies: Chiori, Ningguang, Traveler (Geo), Bennett. Ningguang and Geo Traveler create constructs for both Tamoto dolls at C0; Bennett adds healing and ATK.

Pros

  • Strong off-field Geo damage
  • Low field-time needs
  • Great Geo team synergy
  • Flexible DEF scaling

Cons

  • C0 construct dependency
  • Limited reaction utility
  • Burst often optional
  • C6 for on-field carry

Build variations

C0 Double-Tamoto Off-Field (Off-field DPS)

Best for C0 Itto, Zhongli, Ningguang, or Geo Traveler setups; summon both dolls before swapping.

  • Weapons: Uraku Misugiri, Harbinger of Dawn, Cinnabar Spindle
  • Artifacts: Golden Troupe (4 pcs); Husk of Opulent Dreams (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Prioritize CRIT and DEF%; little ER is needed when Burst is skipped.
  • Teams: Use a Geo Construct teammate or compatible Lunar-Crystallize enabler.
  • When it applies: Needs construct or Lunar-Crystallize interaction.
  • Notes: The extra doll is a major part of Chiori’s personal output.

C1 Navia Double Geo (Off-field DPS)

With C1, Chiori brings two dolls to Navia teams without needing a Geo Construct.

  • Weapons: Uraku Misugiri, Primordial Jade Cutter, Harbinger of Dawn
  • Artifacts: Golden Troupe (4 pcs); Husk of Opulent Dreams (4 pcs)
  • Stats: DEF% / Geo or DEF% / CRIT. Tune ER only if using Burst.
  • Teams: Navia plus a healer or defensive support; another Geo character is required.
  • When it applies: C1 required for second Tamoto without another enabler.
  • Notes: At C0, this exact Navia/Furina/Bennett team produces only one doll.

Budget DEF & CRIT (Off-field DPS)

Low-spender setup using 3-star, craftable, or previously available event equipment.

  • Weapons: Harbinger of Dawn, Flute of Ezpitzal, Cinnabar Spindle
  • Artifacts: Golden Troupe (4 pcs); Golden Troupe + Husk of Opulent Dreams (2 + 2 pcs)
  • Stats: Aim for balanced CRIT and DEF; keep HP above 90% with Harbinger.
  • Teams: A Geo Construct teammate is preferred at C0.
  • When it applies: Cinnabar Spindle is past-event only.
  • Notes: Choose weapons by ownership; a well-refined Harbinger can be especially efficient.

Team Buff Utility (Support)

A situational utility alternative when Chiori’s damage is not the team priority.

  • Weapons: Peak Patrol Song, Favonius Sword
  • Artifacts: Husk of Opulent Dreams (4 pcs); Golden Troupe (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Build DEF for Peak Patrol; build CRIT Rate for Favonius triggering.
  • Teams: Teams that value elemental damage buffs or particles over Chiori’s own damage.
  • When it applies: Peak Patrol requires on-field hits; Favonius must CRIT on-field.
  • Notes: Do not assume the Peak Patrol buff activates from off-field dolls.

C6 On-Field Geo Carry (On-field DPS)

For C6 owners using Chiori’s enhanced Normal Attacks as the primary DPS.

  • Weapons: Uraku Misugiri, Primordial Jade Cutter
  • Artifacts: Husk of Opulent Dreams (4 pcs); Golden Troupe (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Invest in DEF%, Geo DMG, and CRIT; Normal Attack talent matters.
  • Teams: Geo teammates, buffs and sustain for extended field time.
  • When it applies: C6 only.
  • Notes: A distinct playstyle; do not use these priorities for standard off-field Chiori.

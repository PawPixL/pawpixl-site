Lyney Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams (7.2)

Lyney build guide for Genshin Impact 7.2: best weapons (The First Great Magic, Astral Vulture's Crimson Plumage), artifacts (Marechaussee Hunter).

Last updated: November 12, 2026 · Game version 7.2

Role: Main DPS

Lyney is an on-field Pyro DPS who fires Prop Arrows to summon Grin-Malkin Hats, then cashes in Prop Surplus stacks with his Skill. His HP changes make Marechaussee Hunter easy to activate. He shines against Pyro-affected enemies.

Best weapons for Lyney

  1. The First Great Magic: His signature adds CRIT DMG and Charged Attack damage. Multiple Pyro teammates grant ATK stacks that suit his favored teams. Strongest in teams with at least two Pyro characters.
  2. Astral Vulture's Crimson Plumage: Huge CRIT DMG and Charged Attack buffs make it a premium alternative. Its passive favors a different elemental team setup. Value depends on activating its Charged Attack passive.
  3. Aqua Simulacra: Its large CRIT DMG stat and nearby-enemy damage bonus boost his Prop Arrows and Skill without relying on reactions. Stay reasonably close to targets to activate its passive.
  4. Thundering Pulse: Strong base ATK, CRIT DMG and ATK% are useful. Its Normal Attack bonus does not enhance Lyney’s Charged Attacks. Mostly a strong stat stick; Normal Attack stacks are not the goal.
  5. Skyward Harp: High base ATK, CRIT Rate and bonus CRIT DMG provide reliable damage. Watch for CRIT Rate overcap with Marechaussee Hunter. Keep displayed CRIT Rate below about 64% with full Hunter stacks.
  6. Scion of the Blazing Sun: A Charged Attack hit marks a target for extra damage, with an added Charged Attack bonus against that target. Most reliable against one marked enemy. Higher refinements strengthen its marked-target payoff.
  7. Song of Stillness: Fontaine craftable with ATK% and a damage bonus after receiving healing. Bennett can activate it consistently. Bring reliable healing, preferably Bennett. R5 is a strong accessible goal.
  8. Prototype Crescent: An ATK% bow that grants a major ATK boost after a Charged Attack hits an enemy weak point. Excellent when the passive works. Enemy must have a hittable weak point. R5 rewards consistent weak-point hits.

Best artifacts for Lyney

  • Marechaussee Hunter (4 pcs) · Best: His Prop Arrows consume HP and his Skill heals, giving reliable access to 36% CRIT Rate plus 15% Charged Attack DMG. Main stats: ATK% · Pyro DMG · CRIT DMG.
  • Lavawalker (4 pcs) · Mono Pyro alternative: Boosts damage against Pyro-affected enemies. Works in Mono Pyro but is less consistent and generally weaker than Hunter. Main stats: ATK% · Pyro DMG · CRIT.
  • Gladiator's Finale + Shimenawa's Reminiscence (2 + 2 pcs) · Temporary mix: Simple ATK% mix for an interim build while farming Hunter. Choose the pieces with the strongest CRIT stats. Main stats: ATK% · Pyro DMG · CRIT.

Best teams for Lyney

  • Final Curtain: Lyney (Main DPS), Bennett (Healer / ATK Buff), Kaedehara Kazuha (Pyro Buff / Shred), Zhongli (Shield / RES Shred). Bennett heals, buffs ATK and keeps Pyro active. Kazuha Swirls Pyro for damage buffs and RES shred; Zhongli protects Lyney while he aims.
  • House of Flames: Lyney (On-field DPS), Bennett (Healer / Buffer), Xiangling (Off-field DPS / Pyro Enabler), Zhongli (Shielder / Debuffer). Xiangling adds strong off-field Pyro damage and helps maintain Pyro aura. Bennett powers both DPS units, while Zhongli guards charged shots.
  • Smoke and Mirrors: Lyney (Main DPS), Bennett (Healer / ATK Buff), Sucrose (VV / ATK Support), Dehya (Interruption Resistance). Dehya adds Pyro and interruption resistance; Bennett heals and buffs. Sucrose holds Viridescent Venerer and can pass Thrilling Tales ATK.
  • Budget Magic Show: Lyney (Main DPS), Bennett (Healer / ATK Buff), Xiangling (Off-field Pyro DPS), Lynette (VV / Anemo Support). Bennett and Xiangling keep enemies burning with Pyro while Lynette shreds Pyro RES using Viridescent Venerer. Dodge carefully without a shield.

Build variations

Shielded Mono Pyro (On-field Charged Attack DPS)

Best for dangerous enemies that interrupt aimed shots. Bennett heals and buffs; Zhongli or Dehya lets Lyney charge safely.

  • Weapons: The First Great Magic, Aqua Simulacra, Song of Stillness
  • Artifacts: Marechaussee Hunter (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ATK% / Pyro DMG / CRIT. Aim for no more than 64% displayed CRIT Rate with full Hunter stacks.
  • Teams: Bennett plus a shield or interruption resistance source, with reliable Pyro aura.
  • When it applies: Prefer Pyro-affected enemies to maximize his Ascension 4 passive.
  • Notes: Lyney should land several Prop Arrows before using Skill to spend Prop Surplus stacks.

Offensive Mono Pyro (On-field Charged Attack DPS)

For faster clears when dodging is comfortable. Xiangling contributes damage while Anemo support shreds Pyro RES.

  • Weapons: The First Great Magic, Astral Vulture's Crimson Plumage, Thundering Pulse
  • Artifacts: Marechaussee Hunter (4 pcs)
  • Stats: CRIT DMG and ATK% after reaching practical CRIT Rate. ER matters more if bursting each rotation.
  • Teams: Bennett, another Pyro damage source, and Anemo RES shred; no guaranteed shield.
  • When it applies: Less comfortable against aggressive enemies or frequent stagger.
  • Notes: Weapon ranking changes with teams, refinements, and available buffs.

Accessible Craftable (On-field Charged Attack DPS)

An excellent F2P or low-spend setup. Bennett reliably activates Song of Stillness, making it the simpler choice for most fights.

  • Weapons: Song of Stillness, Prototype Crescent
  • Artifacts: Marechaussee Hunter (4 pcs); Gladiator's Finale + Shimenawa's Reminiscence (2 + 2 pcs)
  • Stats: ATK% sands and Pyro goblet. Use the better CRIT circlet while avoiding Hunter CRIT overcap.
  • Teams: Song of Stillness needs healing; Prototype Crescent needs an enemy weak point.
  • When it applies: Prototype Crescent loses value against bosses without weak points.
  • Notes: Refining craftable bows can improve their performance without limited wishes.

Lavawalker Alternative (On-field Charged Attack DPS)

Use an existing well-rolled Lavawalker set before finishing Marechaussee Hunter. Stronger only when its Pyro aura condition holds.

  • Weapons: The First Great Magic, Aqua Simulacra, Song of Stillness
  • Artifacts: Lavawalker (4 pcs); Gladiator's Finale + Shimenawa's Reminiscence (2 + 2 pcs)
  • Stats: Without Hunter stacks, aim for a conventional CRIT ratio with roughly 65%–75% CRIT Rate.
  • Teams: Enemies should remain affected by Pyro; Bennett and Xiangling help maintain aura.
  • When it applies: Lavawalker loses its benefit when the enemy loses Pyro aura.
  • Notes: A mixed ATK set is more universal if Pyro aura is unreliable.

Lyney talents

Normal Attack: Card Force Translocation

Normal Attack
Performs up to 4 consecutive shots with a bow.

Plunging Attack
Fires off a shower of arrows in mid-air before falling and striking the ground, dealing AoE DMG upon impact.

Charged Attack
Performs a more precise Aimed Shot with increased DMG.
While aiming, flames will run across the arrowhead before being fired. Different effects will occur based on the time spent charging.
·Charge Level 1: Fires off a Pyro-infused arrow, dealing Pyro DMG.
·Charge Level 2: Fires off a Prop Arrow that deals Pyro DMG, and upon hit, it will summon a Grin-Malkin Hat.
When firing the Prop Arrow, and when Lyney has more than 60% HP, he will consume a portion of his HP to obtain 1 Prop Surplus stack. Max 5 stacks. The effect will be removed after the character spends 30s out of combat.
The lowest Lyney can drop to through this method is 60% of his Max HP.

Grin-Malkin Hat
·Can taunt nearby opponents and attract their attacks. Each opponent can only be taunted by the Hat once every 5s.
·The Hat will inherit a percentage of Lyney's Max HP.
·If destroyed, or if its duration expires, it will fire off a Pyrotechnic Strike at 1 nearby opponent, dealing Pyro DMG.
Only 1 Hat can exist at any given time.

Arkhe: Pneuma
At certain intervals, the Prop Arrow will cause a Spiritbreath Thorn to descend upon its hit location, dealing Pneuma-aligned Pyro DMG.

Elemental Skill: Bewildering Lights

Cooldown: 15s

Lyney does a flourish with his hat, unleashing a firework surprise!
When used, he will clear all current Prop Surplus stacks and deal AoE Pyro DMG to opponents in front of him. DMG will be increased according to the stacks cleared, and this will also regenerate Lyney's HP based on his Max HP.

When a Grin-Malkin Hat created by Lyney is on the field, the fireworks will cause it to explode, dealing AoE Pyro DMG equal to that of a Pyrotechnic Strike.
The DMG dealt through the Grin-Malkin Hat in this way is considered Charged Attack DMG.

Elemental Burst: Wondrous Trick: Miracle Parade

Cooldown: 15s · Energy cost: 60

Unleashing his magic, Lyney turns himself into a Grin-Malkin Cat that can move around quickly. (Not to be mistaken for the Grin-Malkin Hat. They're two different props!)
When the Grin-Malkin Cat gets close to opponents, it will send flames falling down on them, dealing at most 1 instance of Pyro DMG to each opponent. When the duration ends, he will dismiss the Grin-Malkin Cat and ignite fireworks that deal AoE Pyro DMG, summon 1 Grin-Malkin Hat, and grant himself 1 Prop Surplus stack.

Grin-Malkin Cat can be actively canceled.

Passive talent: Perilous Performance

If Lyney consumes HP when firing off a Prop Arrow, the Grin-Malkin hat summoned by the arrow will, upon hitting an opponent, restore 3 Energy to Lyney and increase DMG dealt by 80% of his ATK.

Passive talent: Conclusive Ovation

The DMG Lyney deals to opponents affected by Pyro will receive the following buffs:
·Increases the DMG dealt by 60%.
·Each Pyro party member other than Lyney will cause the DMG dealt to increase by an additional 20%.
Lyney can deal up to 100% increased DMG to opponents affected by Pyro in this way.

Passive talent: Trivial Observations

Displays the location of nearby resources unique to Fontaine on the mini-map.

Lyney constellations

C1: Whimsical Wonders

Lyney can have 2 Grin-Malkin Hats present at once.
Additionally, Prop Arrows will summon 2 Grin-Malkin Hats and grant Lyney 1 extra stack of Prop Surplus. This effect can occur once every 15s.

C2: Loquacious Cajoling

When Lyney is on the field, he will gain a stack of Crisp Focus every 2s. Each stack will increase his CRIT DMG by 20%. Max 3 stacks. This effect will be canceled when Lyney leaves the field.

C3: Prestidigitation

Increases the Level of Normal Attack: Card Force Translocation by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C4: Well-Versed, Well-Rehearsed

After an opponent is hit by Lyney's Pyro Charged Attack, this opponent's Pyro RES will be decreased by 20% for 6s.

C5: To Pierce Enigmas

Increases the Level of Wondrous Trick: Miracle Parade by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C6: Guarded Smile

When Lyney fires a Prop Arrow, he will fire a Pyrotechnic Strike: Reprised that will deal 80% of a Pyrotechnic Strike's DMG. This DMG is considered Charged Attack DMG.

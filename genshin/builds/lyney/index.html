Lyney Build Guide | Best Weapons, Artifacts & Teams

Lyney build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (The First Great Magic, Astral Vulture's Crimson Plumage), artifacts (Marechaussee Hunter), main stats…

Role: Main DPS

Lyney is an on-field Pyro DPS who fires Prop Arrows to summon Grin-Malkin Hats, then cashes in Prop Surplus stacks with his Skill. His HP changes make Marechaussee Hunter easy to activate. He shines against Pyro-affected enemies.

Best weapons for Lyney

  1. The First Great Magic
  2. Astral Vulture's Crimson Plumage
  3. Aqua Simulacra
  4. Thundering Pulse
  5. Skyward Harp
  6. Scion of the Blazing Sun
  7. Song of Stillness
  8. Prototype Crescent

Best artifacts

  • Marechaussee Hunter (4 pcs) · Best
  • Lavawalker (4 pcs) · Mono Pyro alternative
  • Gladiator's Finale + Shimenawa's Reminiscence (2 + 2 pcs) · Temporary mix

Main stats

Stat targets

  • ATK: 1,800 - 2,300+
  • Energy Recharge: 100% - 150%

Talent priority

  • Elemental Skill: Level 9 - 10
  • Elemental Burst: Level 8 - 9

Best teams

  • Final Curtain: Lyney, Bennett, Kaedehara Kazuha, Zhongli
  • House of Flames: Lyney, Bennett, Xiangling, Zhongli
  • Smoke and Mirrors: Lyney, Bennett, Sucrose, Dehya
  • Budget Magic Show: Lyney, Bennett, Xiangling, Lynette

Pros

  • Powerful Charged Attacks
  • Strong single-target DMG
  • Self-activates Hunter set
  • Excellent Pyro synergy

Cons

  • Aiming takes practice
  • Vulnerable while charging
  • Wants reliable healing
  • Prefers persistent Pyro aura

Build variations

  • Shielded Mono Pyro (On-field Charged Attack DPS): Best for dangerous enemies that interrupt aimed shots. Bennett heals and buffs; Zhongli or Dehya lets Lyney charge safely.
  • Offensive Mono Pyro (On-field Charged Attack DPS): For faster clears when dodging is comfortable. Xiangling contributes damage while Anemo support shreds Pyro RES.
  • Accessible Craftable (On-field Charged Attack DPS): An excellent F2P or low-spend setup. Bennett reliably activates Song of Stillness, making it the simpler choice for most fights.
  • Lavawalker Alternative (On-field Charged Attack DPS): Use an existing well-rolled Lavawalker set before finishing Marechaussee Hunter. Stronger only when its Pyro aura condition holds.

Role: Main DPS / Shield Support

Xinyan is a Physical Claymore DPS who uses her shield to boost Physical damage and her Burst for a powerful hit. Superconduct helps her attacks, while C2 guarantees the Physical Burst hit CRITs. She can also shield Physical carries or play Pyro DPS with C6 Bennett, but her shield is fragile without DEF investment.

Main stats

  • Sands: ATK% / Energy Recharge
  • Goblet: Physical DMG
  • Circlet: Crit Rate / Crit DMG

Stat targets

  • Energy Recharge: 160% - 190% (double Pyro)
  • Energy Recharge: 200% - 230%+ (solo Pyro)
  • Energy Recharge: 130% - 150%+ (solo Pyro + Raiden)
  • Crit Rate: 60% - 75% (on-field)
  • Crit DMG: 120% - 180%+ (gear dependent)

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 8-9
  • Elemental Skill: Level6-8
  • Elemental Burst: Level 8-9

Best teams

  • Rockstar Superconduct: Xinyan, Fischl, Rosaria, Bennett
  • Thunderous Encore: Xinyan, Raiden Shogun, Rosaria, Bennett
  • Rebel Shield Crew: Xinyan, Eula, Raiden Shogun, Mika
  • Pyro Mosh Pit: Xinyan, Bennett, Xiangling, Kaedehara Kazuha

Pros

  • Powerful Physical Burst
  • Superconduct synergy
  • Flexible DPS or shield role
  • C2 guaranteed Burst CRIT

Cons

  • Fragile low-DEF shield
  • Split ATK / DEF scaling
  • High Burst Energy needs
  • Needs careful team setup

Build variations

  • C2+ Physical Burst Nuke (Quickswap Burst DPS): Swap in for Skill then Burst; maximize the upfront Physical strike, rather than long on-field strings.
  • DEF Shield Support (Shield / Physical Buffer): Support a Physical carry with shielding, 15% Physical DMG buff, and C4 Physical RES reduction.
  • C6 Bennett Pyro Driver (On-field Pyro DPS): Use when building Xinyan around Pyro-infused Charged Attacks instead of Physical damage.
  • Accessible Physical DPS (On-field DPS): Practical low-spend option for players without premium Claymores.
  • C6 Physical Spin Driver (Charged Attack DPS): At C6, reduced Charged Attack stamina costs and DEF-to-ATK benefit enable an extended spinning playstyle.

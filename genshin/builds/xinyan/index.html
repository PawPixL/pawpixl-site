Xinyan Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

Xinyan build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Redhorn Stonethresher, Skyward Pride), artifacts (Emblem of Severed Fate), main stats.

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Role: Main DPS / Shield Support

Xinyan is a Physical Claymore DPS who uses her shield to boost Physical damage and her Burst for a powerful hit. Superconduct helps her attacks, while C2 guarantees the Physical Burst hit CRITs. She can also shield Physical carries or play Pyro DPS with C6 Bennett, but her shield is fragile without DEF investment.

Best weapons for Xinyan

  1. Redhorn Stonethresher: High CD and DEF improve Physical DPS builds.
  2. Skyward Pride: Energy Recharge helps costly Bursts, and vacuum blades benefit from Physical DMG bonuses during on-field attacks. Use Burst before on-field attacks.
  3. The Unforged: High ATK potential when shielded.
  4. Song of Broken Pines: Physical DMG and an attack-speed team buff suit on-field Physical combos, though passive uptime varies. On-field Physical DPS.
  5. Wolf's Gravestone: Strong ATK stat stick and occasional party ATK buff; valuable if you can meet Energy needs elsewhere. ATK buffs can dilute its relative value.
  6. Serpent Spine: CRIT Rate and stacking damage bonus are excellent with reliable protection, but its passive increases damage taken. Maintain stacks; protective teammates recommended. R5 is especially strong.
  7. Prototype Archaic: Accessible ATK claymore with an extra hit on Normal/Charged Attacks; a straightforward Physical DPS fallback. General on-field DPS.
  8. Snow-Tombed Starsilver: Physical DMG substat supports this build, but its icicle passive is inconsistent and often weaker than alternatives. Physical DPS; not automatically best against Cryo.

Best artifacts for Xinyan

  • Emblem of Severed Fate (4 pcs) · Support: Reliable unconditional 50% Physical DMG; best all-purpose choice without shield/pulse requirements. Main stats: ATK% / Physical DMG / CRIT.
  • Pale Flame (4 pcs) · Main DPS: Can slightly outperform 2+2 when Skill hits sustain two stacks; unreliable when the shield lacks Pyro pulses. Main stats: ATK% / Physical DMG / CRIT.
  • Tenacity of the Millelith + Bloodstained Chivalry (4 pcs) · Shielder: For C2+ quickswap Burst damage rather than on-field Normal/Charged Attack damage. Main stats: ER or ATK% / Physical DMG / CRIT DMG.

Main stats

  • Sands: ATK% / Energy Recharge
  • Goblet: Physical DMG
  • Circlet: Crit Rate / Crit DMG

Stat targets

  • Energy Recharge: 160% - 190% (double Pyro)
  • Energy Recharge: 200% - 230%+ (solo Pyro)
  • Energy Recharge: 130% - 150%+ (solo Pyro + Raiden)
  • Crit Rate: 60% - 75% (on-field)
  • Crit DMG: 120% - 180%+ (gear dependent)

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 8-9
  • Elemental Skill: Level6-8
  • Elemental Burst: Level 8-9

Best teams for Xinyan

  • Rockstar Superconduct: Xinyan (Shielder / Physical Buffer), Fischl (Off-field DPS / Battery), Rosaria (Off-field DPS / CRIT Buffer), Bennett (Healer / Buffer). Fischl and Rosaria trigger Superconduct for Physical RES shred. Bennett buffs attacks and heals; Xinyan drives with Physical hits.
  • Thunderous Encore: Xinyan (Shielder / Physical Buffer), Raiden Shogun (On-field DPS / Battery), Rosaria (Off-field DPS / CRIT Buffer), Bennett (Healer / Buffer). Raiden and Rosaria enable Superconduct. Raiden restores energy and covers downtime; Bennett amplifies Xinyan’s damage.
  • Rebel Shield Crew: Xinyan (Shielder / Physical Buffer), Eula (On-field DPS), Raiden Shogun (On-field DPS / Battery), Mika (Healer / Physical Buffer). Shield-support Xinyan boosts Physical damage and at C4 shreds Physical RES. Eula carries; Raiden and Mika support her.
  • Pyro Mosh Pit: Xinyan (Shielder / Physical Buffer), Bennett (Healer / Buffer), Xiangling (Off-field DPS / Pyro Enabler), Kaedehara Kazuha (Buffer / Debuffer). Requires Bennett C6 for Pyro-infused attacks. Xiangling adds off-field Pyro while Kazuha buffs Pyro and shreds resistance.

Pros

  • Powerful Physical Burst
  • Superconduct synergy
  • Flexible DPS or shield role
  • C2 guaranteed Burst CRIT

Cons

  • Fragile low-DEF shield
  • Split ATK / DEF scaling
  • High Burst Energy needs
  • Needs careful team setup

Build variations

C2+ Physical Burst Nuke (Quickswap Burst DPS)

Swap in for Skill then Burst; maximize the upfront Physical strike, rather than long on-field strings.

  • Weapons: Redhorn Stonethresher, Skyward Pride, Luxurious Sea-Lord, Akuoumaru
  • Artifacts: Emblem of Severed Fate (4 pcs); Pale Flame + Bloodstained Chivalry (2 + 2 pcs)
  • Stats: Physical Goblet; emphasize CRIT DMG, ATK and enough ER; Burst Physical hit auto-CRITs at C2.
  • Teams: Physical buffers, Superconduct if possible, and adequate particles.
  • When it applies: C2+ for guaranteed Physical Burst CRIT
  • Notes: Only the Physical Burst hit gets the guaranteed CRIT; other attacks still use normal CRIT Rate.

DEF Shield Support (Shield / Physical Buffer)

Support a Physical carry with shielding, 15% Physical DMG buff, and C4 Physical RES reduction.

  • Weapons: Sacrificial Greatsword, Favonius Greatsword, Whiteblind, Skyward Pride
  • Artifacts: Tenacity of the Millelith (4 pcs); Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs)
  • Stats: DEF% / DEF% / DEF%; add ER for regular Burst and CRIT Rate if Favonius.
  • Teams: Best with Physical carry; second defensive source recommended if shield is insufficient.
  • When it applies: C4 improves Physical support value
  • Notes: Tenacity uptime requires shield Pyro pulses and can be inconsistent; Noblesse needs Burst every rotation.

C6 Bennett Pyro Driver (On-field Pyro DPS)

Use when building Xinyan around Pyro-infused Charged Attacks instead of Physical damage.

  • Weapons: Redhorn Stonethresher, Serpent Spine, Wolf's Gravestone, The Unforged
  • Artifacts: Crimson Witch of Flames (4 pcs); Lavawalker (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Pyro Goblet; ATK% or DEF% Sands for C6 spins; standard CRIT ratio.
  • Teams: Requires Bennett C6 to Pyro-infuse Normal and Charged Attacks.
  • When it applies: Bennett C6 required; Xinyan C6 helps spins
  • Notes: Lavawalker is only suitable with stable Pyro aura; do not apply C2 auto-CRIT benefit to Pyro attacks.

Accessible Physical DPS (On-field DPS)

Practical low-spend option for players without premium Claymores.

  • Weapons: Prototype Archaic, Snow-Tombed Starsilver, Blackcliff Slasher, Luxurious Sea-Lord
  • Artifacts: Pale Flame + Bloodstained Chivalry (2 + 2 pcs); Pale Flame (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ATK% Sands, Physical Goblet, CRIT Circlet; prioritize ER before damage substats.
  • Teams: Electro + Cryo for Superconduct, plus healer or buffer.
  • When it applies: 4pc Pale Flame needs sustained Skill hits
  • Notes: Prototype Archaic is craftable; Luxurious Sea-Lord was a limited event reward, not currently obtainable.

C6 Physical Spin Driver (Charged Attack DPS)

At C6, reduced Charged Attack stamina costs and DEF-to-ATK benefit enable an extended spinning playstyle.

  • Weapons: Redhorn Stonethresher, Serpent Spine, Skyward Pride, Prototype Archaic
  • Artifacts: Pale Flame + Bloodstained Chivalry (2 + 2 pcs); Pale Flame (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ATK% Sands for damage; DEF% Sands optionally strengthens shield but usually lowers DPS.
  • Teams: Superconduct support, stamina management and protection.
  • When it applies: Xinyan C6 required
  • Notes: DEF conversion applies to Charged Attacks only; do not assume it buffs her Normal Attacks or Physical Burst.

Xinyan talents

Normal Attack: Dance on Fire

Normal Attack
Performs up to 4 consecutive strikes.

Charged Attack
Drains Stamina over time to perform continuous spinning attacks against all nearby opponents.
At the end of the sequence, performs a more powerful slash.

Plunging Attack
Plunges from mid-air to strike the ground below, damaging opponents along the path and dealing AoE DMG upon impact.

Elemental Skill: Sweeping Fervor

Cooldown: 18s

Xinyan brandishes her instrument, dealing Pyro DMG on nearby opponents, forming a shield made out of her audience's passion.
The shield's DMG Absorption scales based on Xinyan's DEF and on the number of opponents hit.
·Hitting 0–1 opponents grants Shield Level 1: Ad Lib.
·Hitting 2 opponents grants Shield Level 2: Lead-In.
·Hitting 3 or more opponents grants Shield Level 3: Rave, which will also deal intermittent Pyro DMG to nearby opponents.

The shield has the following special properties:
·When unleashed, it infuses Xinyan with Pyro.
·It has 250% DMG Absorption effectiveness against Pyro DMG.

Elemental Burst: Riff Revolution

Cooldown: 15s · Energy cost: 60

Strumming rapidly, Xinyan launches nearby opponents and deals Physical DMG to them, hyping up the crowd.
The sheer intensity of the atmosphere will cause explosions that deal Pyro DMG to nearby opponents.

Passive talent: "The Show Goes On, Even Without an Audience..."

Decreases the number of opponents Sweeping Fervor must hit to trigger each level of shielding.
·Shield Level 2: Lead-In requirement reduced to 1 opponent hit.
·Shield Level 3: Rave requirement reduced to 2 opponents hit or more.

Passive talent: "...Now That's Rock 'N' Roll!"

Characters shielded by Sweeping Fervor deal 15% increased Physical DMG.

Passive talent: A Rad Recipe

When a Perfect Cooking is achieved on a DEF-boosting dish, Xinyan has a 12% chance to obtain double the product.

Xinyan constellations

C1: Fatal Acceleration

Upon scoring a CRIT Hit, increases ATK SPD of Xinyan's Normal and Charged Attacks by 12% for 5s.
Can only occur once every 5s.

C2: Impromptu Opening

Riff Revolution's Physical DMG has its CRIT Rate increased by 100%, and will form a shield at Shield Level 3: Rave when cast.

C3: Double-Stop

Increases the Level of Sweeping Fervor by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C4: Wildfire Rhythm

Sweeping Fervor's swing DMG decreases opponent's Physical RES by 15% for 12s.

C5: Screamin' for an Encore

Increases the Level of Riff Revolution by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C6: Rockin' in a Flaming World

Decreases the Stamina Consumption of Xinyan's Charged Attacks by 30%. Additionally, Xinyan's Charged Attacks gain an ATK Bonus equal to 50% of her DEF.

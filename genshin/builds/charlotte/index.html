Role: Support

Charlotte is a Cryo catalyst healer who provides powerful teamwide healing, off-field Cryo application, and support utility. She excels in Freeze teams and works particularly well alongside Furina.

Best weapons for Charlotte

  1. Favonius Codex
  2. Kagura's Verity
  3. Prototype Amber
  4. Flowing Purity
  5. Thrilling Tales of Dragon Slayers
  6. Oathsworn Eye

Best artifacts

  • Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs) · Support
  • Tenacity of the Millelith (4 pcs) · Support
  • Ocean-Hued Clam (4 pcs) · Healing

Main stats

  • Sands: ATK% / ER
  • Goblet: ATK%
  • Circlet: Healing / CR / ATK%

Stat targets

  • ER: 160–230%+
  • ATK: Healing
  • CR: Favonius only

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 6+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 9+

Best teams

  • Frozen Verdict: Charlotte, Neuvillette, Kaedehara Kazuha, Furina
  • Frozen Assets: Charlotte, Skirk, Escoffier, Furina
  • Shiver Me Timbers: Charlotte, Kamisato Ayaka, Kaedehara Kazuha, Furina
  • Budget Blizzard: Charlotte, Kaeya, Xingqiu, Sucrose

Pros

  • Teamwide healing
  • Off-field Cryo
  • Great with Furina
  • Flexible support

Cons

  • High ER needs
  • Burst-based healing
  • Low personal damage
  • Uneven Cryo uptime

Build variations

  • Furina Team Healer (Healer / Support): Use for strong teamwide healing and Furina synergy while maintaining Burst uptime.
  • Battery / ATK Buff (Healer / Support): Choose Favonius to battery the team or TTDS for a powerful swap-in ATK buff when energy allows.
  • Freeze Utility (Healer / Support): Use for Cryo application, emergency healing and flexible support in Freeze teams.

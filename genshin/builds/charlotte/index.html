Charlotte Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

Charlotte build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Favonius Codex, Kagura's Verity), artifacts (Noblesse Oblige), main stats, talent priority, teams.

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Role: Support

Charlotte is a Cryo catalyst healer who provides powerful teamwide healing, off-field Cryo application, and support utility. She excels in Freeze teams and works particularly well alongside Furina.

Best weapons for Charlotte

  1. Favonius Codex: ER and team particles support her costly Burst. Enough CR to trigger passive.
  2. Kagura's Verity: CRIT DMG and Skill DMG for niche damage builds; meet ER first.
  3. Prototype Amber: Best general healing option, with extra team healing and Energy refund.
  4. Flowing Purity: ATK and conditional DMG; no extra Energy or team buff.
  5. Thrilling Tales of Dragon Slayers: Strong ATK buff for the next teammate, but does not help her ER. Only if Burst uptime is secure.
  6. Oathsworn Eye: Event catalyst: ATK aids healing and Skill use grants ER.

Best artifacts for Charlotte

  • Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs) · Support: Team ATK buff after Burst; best for teams that appreciate ATK. Main stats: ER/ATK% · ATK% · Healing Bonus/ATK%/CR.
  • Tenacity of the Millelith (4 pcs) · Support: Buffs team ATK from off-field Skill hits. Hold Skill helps maintain uptime.
  • Ocean-Hued Clam (4 pcs) · Healing: Turns healing into damage; useful when buffs are already covered.

Main stats

  • Sands: ATK% / ER
  • Goblet: ATK%
  • Circlet: Healing / CR / ATK%

Stat targets

  • ER: 160–230%+
  • ATK: Healing
  • CR: Favonius only

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 6+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 9+

Best teams for Charlotte

  • Frozen Verdict: Charlotte, Neuvillette, Kaedehara Kazuha, Furina. Charlotte provides teamwide healing and Freeze utility while Furina buffs the team's damage.
  • Frozen Assets: Charlotte, Skirk, Escoffier, Furina. Skirk deals Cryo damage while Furina buffs the team.
  • Shiver Me Timbers: Charlotte, Kamisato Ayaka, Kaedehara Kazuha, Furina. Charlotte supplies healing and Cryo Resonance while Furina provides Hydro application and damage buffs.
  • Budget Blizzard: Charlotte, Kaeya, Xingqiu, Sucrose. An accessible Freeze team with Kaeya as the on-field damage dealer.

Pros

  • Teamwide healing
  • Off-field Cryo
  • Great with Furina
  • Flexible support

Cons

  • High ER needs
  • Burst-based healing
  • Low personal damage
  • Uneven Cryo uptime

Build variations

Furina Team Healer (Healer / Support)

Use for strong teamwide healing and Furina synergy while maintaining Burst uptime.

  • Weapons: Prototype Amber, Favonius Codex, Oathsworn Eye
  • Artifacts: Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs); Ocean-Hued Clam (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ER Sands as needed; ATK% Goblet; Healing Bonus or ATK% Circlet.
  • Teams: Furina and an on-field carry; Charlotte’s team heal builds Fanfare.
  • When it applies: Prototype Amber helps energy and adds team healing.

Battery / ATK Buff (Healer / Support)

Choose Favonius to battery the team or TTDS for a powerful swap-in ATK buff when energy allows.

  • Weapons: Favonius Codex, Thrilling Tales of Dragon Slayers, Prototype Amber
  • Artifacts: Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs); Tenacity of the Millelith (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Prioritize ER; add CRIT Rate for Favonius. TTDS needs another source of ER.
  • Teams: An ATK-scaling carry and an appropriate rotation.
  • When it applies: Tenacity needs Skill hits to maintain uptime; TTDS is ER-demanding.

Freeze Utility (Healer / Support)

Use for Cryo application, emergency healing and flexible support in Freeze teams.

  • Weapons: Prototype Amber, Favonius Codex, Oathsworn Eye
  • Artifacts: Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs); Ocean-Hued Clam (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Meet ER needs; prioritize ATK% and Healing Bonus afterward.
  • Teams: Hydro applier plus Cryo carry or driver to sustain Freeze.
  • When it applies: Bosses cannot be frozen; her Burst remains energy-intensive.

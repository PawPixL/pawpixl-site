Charlotte Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts & Teams

Charlotte build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Favonius Codex, Kagura's Verity), artifacts (Noblesse Oblige), main stats (ATK% / Energy Recharge…

Role: Support

Charlotte is a Cryo catalyst healer who provides powerful teamwide healing, off-field Cryo application, and support utility. She excels in Freeze teams and works particularly well alongside Furina.

Best weapons for Charlotte

  1. Favonius Codex
  2. Kagura's Verity
  3. Prototype Amber
  4. Flowing Purity
  5. Thrilling Tales of Dragon Slayers
  6. Oathsworn Eye

Best artifacts

  • Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs) · Support
  • Tenacity of the Millelith (4 pcs) · Support
  • Golden Troupe + Gladiator's Finale (4 pcs) · Sub DPS

Main stats

  • Sands: ATK% / Energy Recharge
  • Goblet: ATK%
  • Circlet: ATK% / CRIT Rate / Healing Bonus

Stat targets

  • Energy Recharge: 180% - 220%
  • ATK: 1,800 - 2,200+

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 6+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 9+

Best teams

  • Frozen Verdict: Charlotte, Neuvillette, Kaedehara Kazuha, Furina
  • Frozen Assets: Charlotte, Skirk, Escoffier, Furina
  • Shiver Me Timbers: Charlotte, Kamisato Ayaka, Kaedehara Kazuha, Furina
  • Budget Blizzard: Charlotte, Kaeya, Xingqiu, Sucrose

Pros

  • Powerful teamwide healing
  • Off-field Cryo application
  • Excellent Furina synergy
  • Flexible support options

Cons

  • High ER needs
  • Burst-dependent healing
  • Limited personal damage
  • Cryo application is inconsistent

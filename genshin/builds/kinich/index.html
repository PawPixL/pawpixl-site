Kinich Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams (7.2)

Kinich build guide for Genshin Impact 7.2: best weapons (Fang of the Mountain King, A Thousand Blazing Suns), artifacts (Obsidian Codex), main stats.

Last updated: November 12, 2026 · Game version 7.2

Role: Main DPS

Kinich is a 5★ Dendro on-field DPS who builds Nightsoul points to fire powerful Scalespiker Cannons. Burning and Burgeon help him generate points faster and maximize his damage.

Best weapons for Kinich

  1. Fang of the Mountain King: His signature boosts Skill and Burst damage as he gains stacks from Skill hits and Burning or Burgeon reactions.
  2. A Thousand Blazing Suns: Grants massive ATK and CRIT DMG buffs during Nightsoul's Blessing, boosting Kinich's first few Scalespiker Cannons. Limited uptime.
  3. Beacon of the Reed Sea: Provides 33.1% CRIT Rate and ATK buffs from Skill hits and taking damage. Best without shields, but watch for CRIT overcap.
  4. Gest of the Mighty Wolf: CRIT Rate stat stick with bonuses best realized in qualifying Hexerei teams; passive uptime varies outside that setup.
  5. Serpent Spine: Battle Pass CRIT Rate and stacking damage bonus; keep stacks by avoiding hits.
  6. Earth Shaker: It's one of Kinich's best accessible weapons.
  7. Tidal Shadow: Craftable ATK weapon with an additional ATK buff after healing; needs a healer.
  8. "Ultimate Overlord's Mega Magic Sword": Energy Recharge and an ATK boost linked to Melusine quest progress; limited past-event weapon.

Best artifacts for Kinich

  • Obsidian Codex (4 pcs) · Main DPS: Provides 15% DMG Bonus and 40% CRIT Rate while Kinich consumes Nightsoul points, making it his best-in-slot artifact set. Main stats: ATK% · Dendro DMG Bonus · CR/CD.
  • Unfinished Reverie (4 pcs) · Main DPS: Provides 18% ATK and up to 50% DMG Bonus while Burning enemies are present, making it a strong alternative for Kinich in Burning teams.
  • Deepwood Memories + Golden Troupe (2 + 2 pcs): Provides 15% Dendro DMG and 20% Elemental Skill DMG, offering a flexible option for boosting Kinich’s Dendro and Skill damage.

Main stats

  • Sands: ATK%
  • Goblet: Dendro DMG Bonus
  • Circlet: Crit Rate / Crit DMG

Stat targets

  • ATK: 2,000+
  • CRIT Rate: 40% - 60% (before Obsidian)
  • CRIT DMG: 170% - 200%+
  • Energy Recharge: 110% - 130% (optional)

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 9+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 9+

Best teams for Kinich

  • Kinich Me Outside: Kinich (DPS), Emilie (Support), Nicole (Support), Durin (Sub DPS). Kinich drives on-field Burning damage, Emilie adds off-field Dendro damage, Nicole provides ATK buffs and shielding, and Durin supplies Pyro application, Dendro RES shred, and off-field damage.
  • Ajaw & Order: Kinich (On-field DPS), Emilie (Off-field DPS), Bennett (Healer / Buffer), Mavuika (Off-field DPS / Pyro Enabler). Kinich deals powerful Skill damage while Emilie adds Dendro damage. Mavuika maintains Pyro for Burning, and Bennett provides healing and ATK buffs.
  • Let Him Cook: Kinich (On-field DPS), Iansan (ATK Buffer), Bennett (Healer / Buffer), Xiangling (Off-field DPS / Pyro Enabler). Kinich drives Burning damage, Xiangling maintains Pyro, and Bennett heals and buffs ATK. Iansan offers extra ATK support, though another Dendro support may offer more.
  • Free to Burn: Kinich (On-field DPS), Traveler (Pyro) (Pyro Enabler / Support), Bennett (Healer / Buffer), Iansan (ATK Buffer). Kinich drives Burning damage, Pyro Traveler provides off-field Pyro and buffs, Bennett boosts ATK and heals, and Iansan adds ATK buffs and DMG Bonus.

Build variations

Burning Cannon DPS (On-field DPS)

Maintain Burning to support consistent Scalespiker cannon cycles.

  • Weapons: Fang of the Mountain King, Gest of the Mighty Wolf, Earth Shaker
  • Artifacts: Obsidian Codex (4 pcs); Unfinished Reverie (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ATK and CRIT take priority; Codex supplies CRIT Rate.
  • Teams: Stable Pyro for Burning; Deepwood on another teammate.
  • When it applies: Avoid CRIT overcap with Obsidian Codex.

Burgeon Cannon DPS (On-field DPS)

Trade the pure Burning setup for Burgeon damage and Nightsoul generation.

  • Weapons: Fang of the Mountain King, Serpent Spine, Earth Shaker
  • Artifacts: Obsidian Codex (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Maintain ATK/CRIT with enough ER for rotation.
  • Teams: Hydro plus controlled Pyro to trigger Burgeon.
  • When it applies: Burgeon trigger should build EM as appropriate.

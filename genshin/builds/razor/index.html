Razor Build Guide | Best Weapons, Artifacts & Teams

Razor build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (A Thousand Blazing Suns, Wolf's Gravestone), artifacts (Hexerei Electro + A Day Carved From Rising…

Role: Main DPS / Hex / Physical

Razor is an on-field Hex/Physical DPS with a fast, Electro-enhanced Burst. Pair him with off-field Cryo for Superconduct to lower Physical RES. His Burst ends on swapping, so set up supports first. Aggravate and C6 Bennett teams need different builds. Make sure to do Witches HW

Best weapons for Razor

  1. A Thousand Blazing Suns
  2. Wolf's Gravestone
  3. Song of Broken Pines
  4. Redhorn Stonethresher
  5. Serpent Spine
  6. Tidal Shadow
  7. Prototype Archaic
  8. Mailed Flower

Best artifacts

  • Hexerei Electro + A Day Carved From Rising Winds (4 pcs)
  • Gladiator's Finale (4 pcs)
  • Pale Flame + Bloodstained Chivalry (2 + 2 pcs)

Main stats

  • Sands: ATK%
  • Goblet: Electro / Physical
  • Circlet: CRIT Rate / CRIT DMG

Stat targets

  • CR: 60% - 75%
  • CD: 120% - 160%
  • ATK: 1700 - 2200
  • ER: 100% - 130%
  • Solo ER: 140%-180%

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Lvl 9 - 10
  • Elemental Skill: Lvl 6 - 8
  • Elemental Burst: Lvl 8 - 9

Best teams

  • Wolvendom Vanguard: Razor, Rosaria, Fischl, Zhongli
  • Lupical Frostguard: Razor, Layla, Yelan, Xingqiu
  • Wildwood Thunder: Razor, Yaoyao, Fischl, Kaedehara Kazuha
  • Thundering Furry: Razor, Nahida, Xingqiu, Bennett

Pros

  • Strong sustained Normal Attacks
  • Easy Superconduct access
  • Self-sufficient Energy kit
  • Accessible early-game DPS

Cons

  • Burst ends on swapping
  • Needs extended field time
  • Physical teams have limits
  • Weak AoE against mobile foes

Build variations

  • Aggravate On-Field (On-field DPS): Trigger Aggravate with Skill and Burst hits while continuing Normal Attacks. Thundering Fury improves Skill frequency and reaction damage.
  • Thundering Furry (C6 Bennett) (On-field Reaction DPS): Bennett gives Pyro infusion while Razor Burst adds Electro, enabling Burgeon, Hyperbloom and Overloaded. Manage reactions within Bennett Burst.
  • Budget Physical Carry (On-field DPS): Accessible alternative when premium weapons and a full Pale Flame set are unavailable.
  • Easy Physical Mixed Set (On-field DPS): Choose the two-piece combination when its substats clearly outperform your four-piece sets.

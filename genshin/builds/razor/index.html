Razor Build Guide | Best Weapons, Artifacts & Teams

Razor build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Wolf's Gravestone, Beacon of the Reed Sea), artifacts (A Day Carved From Rising Winds), main stats…

Role: Main DPS

Witch's Homework boosts Razor's Burst Electro damage. With another unlocked Hexerei ally, overflowing Skill sigils triggers extra Electro strikes and Energy. This main build favors Electro Aggravate; Physical and C6 Bennett reaction builds are alternatives.

Best weapons for Razor

  1. Wolf's Gravestone
  2. Beacon of the Reed Sea
  3. Skyward Pride
  4. Serpent Spine
  5. Mailed Flower
  6. Rainslasher
  7. Tidal Shadow
  8. Prototype Archaic

Best artifacts

  • A Day Carved From Rising Winds (4 pcs)
  • Thundering Fury (4 pcs)
  • Gilded Dreams (4 pcs)

Main stats

  • Sands: ATK% / Elemental Mastery
  • Goblet: Electro DMG Bonus
  • Circlet: CRIT Rate / CRIT DMG

Stat targets

  • CR: 60% - 75%
  • CD: 120% - 170%
  • ER: Team dependent
  • ATK: 1700 - 2200
  • EM: 100 - 250

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Lvl 9 - 10
  • Elemental Skill: Lvl 8 - 9
  • Elemental Burst: Lvl 9 - 10

Best teams

  • Hexerei Thunderpack: Razor, Fischl, Nahida, Baizhu
  • Witchfire Overload: Razor, Durin, Chevreuse, Fischl
  • Hexerei Quickbloom: Razor, Fischl, Nahida, Xingqiu
  • High shelf main boss friends: Razor, Nahida, Xingqiu, Bennett

Pros

  • Hexerei adds Electro strikes
  • Aggravate and AoE potential
  • Fischl boosts consistency
  • Strong free weapon options

Cons

  • Needs Witch Homework
  • Secret Rite needs 2 Hexerei
  • Burst ends on switching
  • Long on-field commitment

Build variations

  • Hexerei Quickbloom (On-field Reaction DPS): Use Burst and Skill taps to overflow Sigils; Hydro enables some Hyperblooms alongside Electro damage. Confirm energy and sustain.
  • Thundering Furry (C6 Bennett) (On-field Reaction DPS): Bennett Pyro infusion plus Razor Burst Electro causes Burgeon, Hyperbloom and Overloaded. Stay in Bennett field.
  • Physical Superconduct DPS (On-field Physical DPS): For traditional Razor gameplay. Keep Superconduct active and stay on-field during his Burst.
  • Hexerei C6 Sigil Carry (On-field Electro DPS): Use C6-enhanced Sigil generation and Skill taps during Burst to trigger overflow strikes and maintain Energy.

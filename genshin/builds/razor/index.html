Razor Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

Razor build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Wolf's Gravestone, Beacon of the Reed Sea), artifacts (A Day Carved From Rising Winds), main stats.

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Role: Main DPS

Witch's Homework boosts Razor's Burst Electro damage. With another unlocked Hexerei ally, overflowing Skill sigils triggers extra Electro strikes and Energy. This main build favors Electro Aggravate; Physical and C6 Bennett reaction builds are alternatives.

Best weapons for Razor

  1. Wolf's Gravestone: High ATK boosts wolf strikes. Strong generalist; low-HP buff situational.
  2. Beacon of the Reed Sea: CRIT Rate and conditional ATK. Keep ATK passive active.
  3. Skyward Pride: ER and Burst vacuum blades. When Burst uptime needs help.
  4. Serpent Spine: CRIT and stackable damage. Shield helps preserve stacks.
  5. Mailed Flower: EM and ATK for Aggravate. Trigger reactions consistently. R5 preferred.
  6. Rainslasher: EM; bonus vs Electro-affected foes. Aggravate or Quickbloom. Higher refinements help.
  7. Tidal Shadow: ATK bonus after healing. Requires reliable healing. R5 boosts ATK bonus.
  8. Prototype Archaic: Accessible ATK and extra hit. Budget generalist.

Best artifacts for Razor

  • A Day Carved From Rising Winds (4 pcs): Hexerei ATK and CRIT gains. Main stats: ATK% · Electro · CRIT.
  • Thundering Fury (4 pcs): Aggravate and faster Skill taps. Main stats: EM/ATK% · Electro · CRIT.
  • Gilded Dreams (4 pcs): EM and ATK for Quicken teams. Main stats: EM/ATK% · Electro · CRIT.

Main stats

  • Sands: ATK% / Elemental Mastery
  • Goblet: Electro DMG Bonus
  • Circlet: CRIT Rate / CRIT DMG

Stat targets

  • CR: 60% - 75%
  • CD: 120% - 170%
  • ER: Team dependent
  • ATK: 1700 - 2200
  • EM: 100 - 250

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Lvl 9 - 10
  • Elemental Skill: Lvl 8 - 9
  • Elemental Burst: Lvl 9 - 10

Best teams for Razor

  • Hexerei Thunderpack: Razor, Fischl, Nahida, Baizhu. Unlock both Hexerei quests. Fischl enables Secret Rite; Nahida sustains Quicken, Baizhu heals, and Razor overflows Sigils during Burst.
  • Witchfire Overload: Razor, Durin (DPS), Chevreuse, Fischl. Unlocked Hexerei teammates activate Secret Rite. Chevreuse enables Electro-Pyro RES shred; Durin and Fischl support Razor damage.
  • Hexerei Quickbloom: Razor, Fischl, Nahida, Xingqiu. Fischl enables Secret Rite; Nahida and Xingqiu generate Dendro Cores. Razor triggers some Hyperblooms; no dedicated healer.
  • High shelf main boss friends: Razor, Nahida, Xingqiu, Bennett. Requires Bennett C6. Razor drives mixed Burgeon and Hyperbloom via Pyro-infused attacks and Burst Electro. No second Hexerei member.

Pros

  • Hexerei adds Electro strikes
  • Aggravate and AoE potential
  • Fischl boosts consistency
  • Strong free weapon options

Cons

  • Needs Witch Homework
  • Secret Rite needs 2 Hexerei
  • Burst ends on switching
  • Long on-field commitment

Build variations

Hexerei Quickbloom (On-field Reaction DPS)

Use Burst and Skill taps to overflow Sigils; Hydro enables some Hyperblooms alongside Electro damage. Confirm energy and sustain.

  • Weapons: Wolf's Gravestone, Mailed Flower, Rainslasher
  • Artifacts: Thundering Fury (4 pcs); Gilded Dreams (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ATK% or EM sands; Electro goblet; prioritize CRIT, ER, then useful EM.
  • Teams: Razor and Fischl with completed Hexerei unlocks; Dendro and Hydro teammates.
  • Notes: No dedicated healer in Razor/Fischl/Nahida/Xingqiu. Reactions vary with elemental auras.

Thundering Furry (C6 Bennett) (On-field Reaction DPS)

Bennett Pyro infusion plus Razor Burst Electro causes Burgeon, Hyperbloom and Overloaded. Stay in Bennett field.

  • Weapons: Mailed Flower, Rainslasher, Bloodtainted Greatsword
  • Artifacts: Gilded Dreams (4 pcs); Flower of Paradise Lost (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Level 90; EM/EM/EM; ER until Burst is reliable.
  • Teams: C6 Bennett, Dendro and Hydro (e.g. Nahida/Xingqiu).
  • When it applies: Bennett C6 required
  • Notes: No Hexerei Secret Rite with this four-character composition.

Physical Superconduct DPS (On-field Physical DPS)

For traditional Razor gameplay. Keep Superconduct active and stay on-field during his Burst.

  • Weapons: Wolf's Gravestone, Song of Broken Pines, Serpent Spine, Prototype Archaic
  • Artifacts: Pale Flame (4 pcs); Gladiator's Finale (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ATK% sands; Physical DMG goblet; CRIT circlet; adjust ER to team.
  • Teams: Cryo applier for Superconduct; Electro battery and sustain preferred.
  • When it applies: Better for players investing in Physical damage
  • Notes: Witch Homework still boosts Wolf Within, but this build prioritizes Physical hits.

Hexerei C6 Sigil Carry (On-field Electro DPS)

Use C6-enhanced Sigil generation and Skill taps during Burst to trigger overflow strikes and maintain Energy.

  • Weapons: Wolf's Gravestone, Beacon of the Reed Sea, Serpent Spine
  • Artifacts: A Day Carved From Rising Winds (4 pcs); Thundering Fury (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ATK%/Electro/CRIT; C6 supplies conditional CRIT after Sigil consumption.
  • Teams: Razor C6, Witch Homework completed, plus another unlocked Hexerei ally.
  • When it applies: Razor C6 and two unlocked Hexerei members
  • Notes: Extra CRIT Rate and CRIT DMG follow Sigil consumption; do not assume they are permanently active.

Citlali Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

Citlali build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Starcaller's Watch, Thrilling Tales of Dragon Slayers).

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Role: Buffer / Support

Citlali shields the team, applies Cryo off-field, and reduces enemy Pyro and Hydro resistance after Melt or Freeze. She excels as a buffer with Scroll and supportive catalysts.

Best weapons for Citlali

  1. Starcaller's Watch: EM strengthens her shield, while its passive buffs the active teammate after shielding.
  2. Thrilling Tales of Dragon Slayers: R5 gives the next active ATK-scaling teammate a large ATK buff. Next character should scale with ATK. R5 preferred.
  3. Etherlight Spindlelute: ER-focused event option for smoother Burst uptime.
  4. Wandering Evenstar: EM with a modest team ATK buff based on her EM.
  5. A Thousand Floating Dreams: Large EM stat and a small team EM buff; best when EM benefits the team.
  6. Sacrificial Fragments: EM and a possible Skill reset; useful for extra shield flexibility.
  7. Mappa Mare: Craftable EM catalyst; its personal damage passive is less useful.
  8. Fruit of Fulfillment: Craftable ER and stacking EM, but lower ATK is unimportant for support.

Best artifacts for Citlali

  • Scroll of the Hero of Cinder City (4 pcs) · Best Support: Large damage buff to elements involved in her reactions. Needs a qualifying reaction. Main stats: EM/ER · EM · EM.
  • Tenacity of the Millelith (4 pcs) · Support: Team ATK buff while her Skill continues hitting. Alternative if Scroll is covered. Main stats: EM/ER · EM · EM.
  • Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs) · Support: Team ATK buff after Burst; use when another ally already holds Scroll. Main stats: EM/ER · EM · EM.

Main stats

  • Sands: EM / ER
  • Goblet: EM
  • Circlet: EM

Stat targets

  • EM: 600–900
  • ER: Team-based

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 9+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 6–8

Best teams for Citlali

  • Frozen Tides: Citlali (Shield / Buffer), Neuvillette (Hydro DPS), Furina (Hydro support), Xilonen (Buffer / Healer). Neuvillette leads Hydro damage with Furina buffs and Xilonen healing and RES shred. Citlali shields, reduces Hydro RES and provides Scroll buffs.
  • Bloodmoon: Citlali (Shield), Arlecchino (DPS), Yelan (Support), Bennett (Support). Arlecchino uses Hydro from Yelan to Vaporize. Citlali shields, shreds Pyro resistance, and enables occasional Melt; Bennett boosts ATK.
  • Blazing Frost: Citlali (Shield), Mavuika (DPS), Xilonen (Support), Bennett (Support). Mavuika can Melt some Pyro hits with Citlali’s Cryo. Xilonen adds buffs and RES shred, while Bennett heals and boosts ATK.
  • Skyfire: Citlali (Shield), Chasca (DPS), Furina (Support), Bennett (Support). Chasca gains Cryo shells and shielding from Citlali. Furina supplies Hydro and buffs; Bennett adds ATK and healing.

Pros

  • Strong shielding
  • Pyro RES shred
  • Hydro RES shred
  • Off-field Cryo
  • Excellent team buffs

Cons

  • Limited Cryo buffs
  • ER can be demanding
  • Freeze limited on bosses
  • Team-dependent RES shred

Build variations

ATK Carry Buffer (Support)

Prioritize team damage buffs and shield strength over personal damage.

  • Weapons: Starcaller's Watch, Thrilling Tales of Dragon Slayers
  • Artifacts: Scroll of the Hero of Cinder City (4 pcs)
  • Stats: EM / EM / EM; add ER as needed.
  • Teams: Pyro or Hydro DPS; TTDS needs the right switch order.
  • When it applies: TTDS benefits ATK-scaling carries

ER Comfort Support (Support)

Use when the team needs more reliable Burst access or flexible shielding.

  • Weapons: Etherlight Spindlelute, Sacrificial Fragments
  • Artifacts: Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs); Tenacity of the Millelith (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ER Sands if Burst uptime is difficult.
  • Teams: Another ally preferably covers Scroll.
  • When it applies: Avoid duplicating the same teamwide artifact buffs

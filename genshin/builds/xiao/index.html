Xiao Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams (7.2)

Xiao build guide for Genshin Impact 7.2: best weapons (Primordial Jade Winged-Spear, Staff of Homa), artifacts (Vermillion Hereafter), main stats.

Last updated: November 12, 2026 · Game version 7.2

Role: Main DPS

Xiao is an on-field Anemo hypercarry who converts his attacks to Anemo during Burst and delivers powerful Plunging Attacks. His Burst drains HP, making healing and Anemo energy support valuable. Build CRIT, ATK, and enough Energy Recharge to Burst every rotation.

Best weapons for Xiao

  1. Primordial Jade Winged-Spear: High base ATK, CRIT Rate, and stacking ATK/DMG buffs suit Xiao’s sustained on-field plunges. Build stacks by hitting enemies.
  2. Staff of Homa: Huge CRIT DMG and HP-based ATK make this an excellent premium alternative, even above half HP. Do not sacrifice healing to force the low-HP bonus.
  3. Lumidouce Elegy: High CRIT Rate and ATK-oriented stats make a strong premium stat stick; much of its passive is irrelevant to Xiao. Avoid overcapping CRIT Rate with Marechaussee Hunter.
  4. Calamity Queller: High base ATK and Elemental DMG Bonus help Xiao, although its off-field-oriented passive is not fully utilized. Best if ATK buffs are limited.
  5. Vortex Vanquisher: Large ATK gains can work with a shield, but it offers no CRIT and its shield-strength bonus applies to Xiao only. Requires reliable shielding to exploit its ATK passive.
  6. Deathmatch: Its CRIT Rate makes Xiao easier to build, and its situational ATK passive works in both single-target and AoE fights. Avoid excess CRIT Rate on Marechaussee Hunter.
  7. Lithic Spear: ATK% and Liyue-party stacks can give strong value in compatible teams, especially at higher refinements. Requires Liyue teammates for its passive. Higher refinements improve performance.
  8. Missive Windspear: An accessible ATK% option; its reaction-triggered ATK buff is situational, and EM contributes little to standard Xiao damage. Event-only; passive depends on triggering a reaction.

Best artifacts for Xiao

  • Vermillion Hereafter (4 pcs) · Best: Burst HP drain quickly stacks up to 48% extra ATK at four stacks; reliable across long plunging rotations, especially in AoE. Main stats: ATK% · Anemo DMG/ATK% · CRIT.
  • Marechaussee Hunter (4 pcs): Burst HP drain activates up to 36% CRIT Rate. Excellent with Xianyun/Furina; avoid overcapping CRIT Rate. Main stats: ATK% · Anemo DMG/ATK% · CRIT.
  • Long Night's Oath (4 pcs): Greatly boosts Plunging Attack DMG through successive plunges. Strong with Bennett; requires consistent plunge timing. Main stats: ATK% · Anemo DMG · CRIT.

Main stats

  • Sands: ATK%
  • Goblet: Anemo DMG Bonus / ATK%
  • Circlet: CRIT Rate / CRIT DMG

Stat targets

  • CRIT Rate: 70% - 85% (Non-MH)
  • CRIT DMG: 150% - 220%+
  • Energy Recharge: 110% - 140%+
  • ATK: 1,800 - 2,400+
  • MH CRIT Rate: 45% - 64% shown

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 8-9+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 6-8
  • Elemental Burst: Level 8-9+

Best teams for Xiao

  • Adeptal Air Force: Xiao (Main DPS), Faruzan (Anemo Buffer), Xianyun (Healer / Buffer), Furina (Sub DPS). Premium plunge hypercarry: Faruzan shreds Anemo RES, Xianyun boosts plunges and heals, and Furina adds damage buffs.
  • Lament on Repeat: Xiao (Main DPS), Faruzan (Anemo Buffer), Zhongli (Shielder), Bennett (ATK Buffer / Healer). A classic Xiao hypercarry. Faruzan boosts Anemo, Zhongli protects and shreds RES, and Bennett grants major ATK buffs.
  • Windborne Remedy: Xiao (Main DPS), Faruzan (Anemo Buffer), Jean (Healer / Battery), Furina (Sub DPS / Buffer). Faruzan buffs Anemo damage, Jean heals and batteries Xiao, and Furina provides off-field damage and Fanfare buffs.
  • Budget Yaksha: Xiao (Main DPS), Faruzan (Anemo Buffer), Sucrose (Anemo Battery), Bennett (ATK Buffer / Healer). A lower-cost triple-Anemo core: Faruzan buffs Xiao, Sucrose funnels particles, and Bennett heals and boosts ATK.

Pros

Cons

Build variations

FFXX Premium Plunge (On-field DPS)

Best for maximum single-target plunge damage and mobile buffs. Xianyun heals through Xiao’s HP drain.

  • Weapons: Primordial Jade Winged-Spear, Staff of Homa, Deathmatch
  • Artifacts: Marechaussee Hunter (4 pcs); Vermillion Hereafter (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Aim for 45–64% displayed CRIT Rate on Marechaussee; invest the rest in CRIT DMG, ATK and sufficient ER.
  • Teams: Xianyun, Furina and ideally C6 Faruzan.
  • When it applies: CRIT Rate cap includes Marechaussee 36%.

Bennett Plunge Hypercarry (On-field DPS)

Use when playing inside Bennett’s field and maintaining consecutive high plunges.

  • Weapons: Primordial Jade Winged-Spear, Staff of Homa, Calamity Queller
  • Artifacts: Long Night's Oath (4 pcs); Vermillion Hereafter (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Anemo DMG Goblet and strong CRIT rolls; ensure Burst uptime before stacking more ATK.
  • Teams: Bennett and Faruzan; add shielding or an Anemo battery.
  • When it applies: Long Night’s Oath loses stacks when plunge timing is interrupted.

Classic AoE Vermillion (On-field DPS)

Reliable general-purpose Xiao for grouped enemies and prolonged Burst rotations.

  • Weapons: Primordial Jade Winged-Spear, Staff of Homa, Vortex Vanquisher
  • Artifacts: Vermillion Hereafter (4 pcs); Marechaussee Hunter (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Balance 70%+ CRIT Rate, CRIT DMG, and ATK; 110–140%+ ER depending on batteries.
  • Teams: Faruzan plus sustain and an Anemo energy source.
  • When it applies: Use Skill charges before Burst to pre-funnel particles.

Accessible Battery Team (On-field DPS)

For players without premium supports or weapons. Consistent Burst uptime outweighs occasional larger plunges.

  • Weapons: Deathmatch, Lithic Spear, Missive Windspear
  • Artifacts: Vermillion Hereafter (4 pcs); Marechaussee Hunter (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ER can rise above 140% without C6 Faruzan; maintain CRIT balance and use ATK% Sands.
  • Teams: Sucrose or other Anemo battery, Bennett and a healer/shielder.
  • When it applies: Lithic Spear needs Liyue teammates for stronger passive.

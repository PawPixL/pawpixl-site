Xiao Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts & Teams

Role: Main DPS

Xiao is an on-field Anemo hypercarry who converts his attacks to Anemo during Burst and delivers powerful Plunging Attacks. His Burst drains HP, making healing and Anemo energy support valuable. Build CRIT, ATK, and enough Energy Recharge to Burst every rotation.

Best weapons for Xiao

  1. Primordial Jade Winged-Spear
  2. Staff of Homa
  3. Lumidouce Elegy
  4. Calamity Queller
  5. Vortex Vanquisher
  6. Deathmatch
  7. Lithic Spear
  8. Missive Windspear

  • Marechaussee Hunter (4 pcs)
  • Long Night's Oath (4 pcs)

Main stats

  • Sands: ATK%
  • Goblet: Anemo DMG Bonus / ATK%
  • Circlet: CRIT Rate / CRIT DMG

Stat targets

  • CRIT Rate: 70% - 85% (Non-MH)
  • CRIT DMG: 150% - 220%+
  • Energy Recharge: 110% - 140%+
  • ATK: 1,800 - 2,400+
  • MH CRIT Rate: 45% - 64% shown

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 8-9+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 6-8
  • Elemental Burst: Level 8-9+

Best teams

  • Adeptal Air Force: Xiao, Faruzan, Xianyun, Furina
  • Lament on Repeat: Xiao, Faruzan, Zhongli, Bennett
  • Windborne Remedy: Xiao, Faruzan, Jean, Furina
  • Budget Yaksha: Xiao, Faruzan, Sucrose, Bennett

Pros

  • Powerful premium supports

Cons

  • Drains own HP

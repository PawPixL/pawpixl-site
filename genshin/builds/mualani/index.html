Mualani Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

Mualani build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Surf's Up, Tome of the Eternal Flow), artifacts (Obsidian Codex), main stats, talent priority, teams.

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Role: Main DPS

Mualani is an HP scaling Hydro DPS built around huge, front loaded Sharky’s Bite damage. She excels in Vaporize teams, trading rapid attacks for fewer, massive hits.

Best weapons for Mualani

  1. Surf's Up: Signature CRIT DMG, HP and Normal Attack buffs that empower Sharky’s Bites and Vaporize.
  2. Tome of the Eternal Flow: High CRIT DMG and HP; its Charged Attack passive does little for Mualani’s Normal Attack bites.
  3. A Thousand Floating Dreams: Large Elemental Mastery stat and modest party EM utility; strong for reaction-focused teams.
  4. Starcaller's Watch: Elemental Mastery catalyst that buffs the active character after creating a shield.
  5. Sacrificial Jade: Sacrificial Jade provides CRIT Rate, a massive HP boost, and EM to strengthen Mualani’s Vaporize damage—but watch for CRIT overcapping with Obsidian Codex.
  6. Ring of Yaxche: Ring of Yaxche provides a hefty HP% boost and increases Mualani’s Normal Attack damage after using her Skill, making it a strong craftable F2P option.
  7. The Widsith: On-switch random buffs include Elemental Mastery or DMG%.
  8. Ash-Graven Drinking Horn: HP% event catalyst with an occasional HP-scaling Physical hit; mostly an HP stat stick for Mualani. HP stat stick; passive does Physical damage.

Best artifacts for Mualani

  • Obsidian Codex (4 pcs) · Main DPS: Obsidian Codex provides 15% increased damage and 40% CRIT Rate, making it Mualani’s best set and freeing up artifact substats for HP%, EM, and CRIT DMG. Main stats: HP%/EM · Hydro DMG Bonus/HP% · CR/CD/HP%.
  • Unfinished Reverie (4 pcs) · Main DPS: Unfinished Reverie boosts Mualani’s damage against Burning enemies, making it a niche option for Burn-Vape teams.
  • Marechaussee Hunter (4 pcs) · Main DPS: Marechaussee Hunter provides 15% Normal Attack DMG and up to 36% CRIT Rate with Furina, making it a solid alternative for Mualani’s Sharky’s Bites. Obsidian Codex remains stronger overall.

Main stats

  • Sands: HP% / Elemental Mastery
  • Goblet: Hydro DMG Bonus / HP%
  • Circlet: Crit Rate / Crit DMG / HP%

Stat targets

  • HP: 32,000-38,000+
  • Crit Rate: 40% - 50% (Codex)
  • Crit DMG: 200%+
  • Elemental Mastery: 100-200

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 9+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 9+

Best teams for Mualani

  • Sharknado: Mualani (Main DPS), Mavuika (Sub DPS), Citlali (Res Shred), Xilonen (Support). Mavuika applies Pyro for Mualani to Vaporize. Xilonen supports her damage, while Citlali offers shielding but can complicate Pyro aura management.
  • Shark Week: Mualani (Main DPS), Mavuika (Sub DPS), Emilie (Burn), Xilonen (Support). Mualani drives Vaporize damage, Mavuika provides off-field Pyro, Emilie adds Burning damage, and Xilonen shreds RES and buffs the team.
  • Jaws & Order: Mualani (Main DPS), Furina (Buffs), Xiangling (Sub DPS), Xilonen (Support). Furina buffs Mualani but her Hydro can compete for the Pyro aura. Xiangling must maintain strong Pyro application, while Xilonen heals and shreds RES.
  • Coupon Cabana: Mualani (Main DPS), Yaoyao (Support), Xiangling (Sub DPS), Sucrose (EM Build). Mualani drives Vaporize damage, Xiangling provides Pyro, Yaoyao heals and supports Bloom reactions, and Sucrose boosts EM and shreds RES.

Pros

  • Great mobility
  • HP-based scaling
  • Strong exploration

Cons

  • Misses feel punishing
  • Vape dependent
  • Unusual playstyle
  • Wants specific supports

Build variations

Vaporize Shark Bites (On-field DPS)

Maximize Mualani’s large forward-Vaporize shark attacks.

  • Weapons: Surf's Up, Sacrificial Jade, Ring of Yaxche
  • Artifacts: Obsidian Codex (4 pcs)
  • Stats: HP% / Hydro DMG / CRIT; Codex supplies CRIT Rate.
  • Teams: Off-field Pyro that maintains aura for bites.
  • When it applies: Avoid excessive CRIT Rate with Obsidian Codex.

Burning Vape (On-field DPS)

Burning can maintain reaction uptime for Mualani’s bite windows.

  • Weapons: Surf's Up, Sacrificial Jade, Ring of Yaxche
  • Artifacts: Obsidian Codex (4 pcs); Unfinished Reverie (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Prioritize HP, CRIT and modest EM for Vaporize.
  • Teams: Pyro plus Dendro to help stabilize Pyro aura.
  • When it applies: Avoid unintended Hydro consumption before bites.

Tartaglia Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

Tartaglia build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Polar Star, Thundering Pulse), artifacts (Nymph's Dream), main stats, talent priority, teams.

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Role: DPS

Tartaglia is a fast paced Hydro DPS who excels at reaction-driven teams and devastating AoE damage through Riptide. If you love chaos ginger energy, he's meant for you.

Best weapons for Tartaglia

  1. Polar Star: Provides CRIT Rate, Skill and Burst damage, and stacking ATK bonuses. Tartaglia's strongest all-around weapon.
  2. Thundering Pulse: Provides CRIT DMG, ATK, and Normal Attack bonuses. Excellent for melee damage, but less effective for Charged Attacks.
  3. Hunter's Path: CRIT Rate and an Elemental DMG bonus; Charged Attack passive scales with EM.
  4. Skyward Harp: High Base ATK with CRIT Rate and CRIT DMG; flexible bow stat stick.
  5. Aqua Simulacra: High CRIT DMG and a damage bonus when enemies are nearby, including while off-field.
  6. The Viridescent Hunt: Provides CRIT Rate and groups enemies for stronger Riptide chains. Especially valuable against multiple targets.
  7. Chain Breaker: Offers ATK buffs based on team elements, plus EM with three qualifying teammates. A dependable craftable option.
  8. Blackcliff Warbow: Provides CRIT DMG and gains ATK after defeating enemies. Less reliable against bosses or single-target encounters.

Best artifacts for Tartaglia

  • Nymph's Dream (4 pcs) · Main DPS: Grants up to 25% ATK and 30% total Hydro DMG Bonus through its set effects. Tartaglia's best general set. Main stats: ATK% · Hydro DMG · CR/CD.
  • Heart of Depth (4 pcs) · Main DPS: Boosts Hydro damage and strengthens Normal and Charged Attacks after using his Skill. A reliable alternative.
  • Night Of The Sky'S Unveiling (4 pcs) · Lunar Charged Only: Niche set only when Lunar Reaction and Moonsign conditions activate its CRIT Rate bonus; not a default International set.

Main stats

  • Sands: ATK%
  • Goblet: Hydro DMG
  • Circlet: Crit Rate / Crit DMG

Stat targets

  • CRIT Rate: 70% - 80%
  • CRIT DMG: 180%+
  • ATK: 1,800 - 2,100+
  • Energy Recharge: 110% - 130%

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 6-8
  • Elemental Skill: Level 9+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 9+

Best teams for Tartaglia

  • Ginger Snaps: Tartaglia, Nicole, Durin, Kaedehara Kazuha. Tartaglia supplies Hydro for Durin's off-field Pyro damage, while Nicole buffs and shields and Kazuha groups enemies.
  • Waterboarding: Tartaglia, Columbina, Ineffa, Citlali. Columbina and Ineffa enable Lunar-Charged damage, while Citlali adds shielding and Hydro resistance shred.
  • Childe, Please: Tartaglia, Furina, Escoffier (Support), Citlali. Furina provides damage and buffs, Escoffier heals and shreds resistance, and Citlali adds shielding and Freeze support.
  • Childe Support: Tartaglia, Bennett, Xiangling, Sucrose. Tartaglia enables Xiangling's Vaporize damage, while Bennett buffs and heals and Sucrose groups enemies and shreds resistance.

  • Flexible teams

Cons

Build variations

International Vaporize (On-field DPS)

Enable Xiangling Vaporize while driving Riptide Hydro damage.

  • Weapons: Polar Star, Skyward Harp, The Viridescent Hunt
  • Artifacts: Nymph's Dream (4 pcs); Heart of Depth (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ATK% / Hydro DMG / CRIT, with enough ER for Burst.
  • Teams: Xiangling plus Bennett and an Anemo buffer.
  • When it applies: Manage Skill duration to avoid excessive cooldown.

Burst Vaporize Nuke (Quickswap DPS)

Prioritize a high-impact Burst over long melee uptime.

  • Weapons: Polar Star, Aqua Simulacra
  • Artifacts: Nymph's Dream (4 pcs); Heart of Depth (4 pcs)
  • Stats: CRIT and ATK, with EM if Burst Vaporizes.
  • Teams: Reliable Pyro aura before a Vaporized Burst.
  • When it applies: Elemental aura timing determines Vaporize.

Riptide AoE (On-field DPS)

Exploit overlapping Riptide hits in multi-target fights.

  • Weapons: Polar Star, The Viridescent Hunt
  • Artifacts: Nymph's Dream (4 pcs); Heart of Depth (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ATK% / Hydro DMG / CRIT.
  • Teams: Grouping and clustered enemies.
  • When it applies: Less effective against isolated targets.

Tartaglia talents

Normal Attack: Cutting Torrent

Normal Attack
Performs up to 6 consecutive shots with a bow.

Charged Attack
Performs a more precise Aimed Shot with increased DMG.
While aiming, the power of Hydro will accumulate on the arrowhead. An arrow fully charged with the torrent will deal Hydro DMG and apply the Riptide status.

Riptide
Opponents affected by Riptide will suffer from AoE Hydro DMG effects when attacked by Tartaglia in various ways. DMG dealt in this way is considered Normal Attack DMG.
·Riptide Flash: A fully-charged Aimed Shot that hits an opponent affected by Riptide deals consecutive bouts of AoE DMG. Can occur once every 0.7s.
·Riptide Burst: Defeating an opponent affected by Riptide creates a Hydro burst that inflicts the Riptide status on nearby opponents hit.

Plunging Attack
Fires off a shower of arrows in mid-air before falling and striking the ground, dealing AoE DMG upon impact.
When Tartaglia is in Foul Legacy: Raging Tide's Melee Stance, he cannot perform a plunging attack.

Elemental Skill: Foul Legacy: Raging Tide

Cooldown: Preemptive: 6-36s / Max: 45s

Unleashes a set of weaponry made of pure water, dealing Hydro DMG to surrounding opponents and entering Melee Stance.
In this Stance, Tartaglia's Normal and Charged Attacks are converted to Hydro DMG that cannot be overridden by any other elemental infusion and change as follows:

Normal Attack
Performs up to 6 consecutive Hydro strikes.

Charged Attack
Consumes a certain amount of Stamina to unleash a cross slash, dealing Hydro DMG.

Riptide Slash
Hitting an opponent affected by Riptide with a melee attack unleashes a Riptide Slash that deals AoE Hydro DMG. DMG dealt in this way is considered Elemental Skill DMG, and can only occur once every 1.5s.

After 30s, or when the ability is unleashed again, this skill will end. Tartaglia will return to his Ranged Stance and this ability will enter CD. The longer Tartaglia stays in his Melee Stance, the longer the CD.
If the return to a ranged stance occurs automatically after 30s, the CD is even longer.

Elemental Burst: Havoc: Obliteration

Cooldown: 15s · Energy cost: 60

Performs different attacks based on what stance Tartaglia is in when casting.

Ranged Stance: Flash of Havoc
Swiftly fires a Hydro-imbued magic arrow, dealing AoE Hydro DMG and applying the Riptide status.
Returns a portion of its Energy Cost after use.

Melee Stance: Light of Obliteration
Performs a slash with a large AoE, dealing massive Hydro DMG to all surrounding opponents, which triggers Riptide Blast.

Riptide Blast
When the obliterating waters hit an opponent affected by Riptide, it clears their Riptide status and triggers a Hydro Explosion that deals AoE Hydro DMG.
DMG dealt in this way is considered Elemental Burst DMG.

Passive talent: Never Ending

Extends Riptide duration by 8s.

Passive talent: Sword of Torrents

When Tartaglia is in Foul Legacy: Raging Tide's Melee Stance, on dealing a CRIT hit, Normal and Charged Attacks apply the Riptide status effect to opponents.

Passive talent: Master of Weaponry

Increases your own party members' Normal Attack Level by 1.

Tartaglia constellations

C1: Foul Legacy: Tide Withholder

Decreases the CD of Foul Legacy: Raging Tide by 20%.

C2: Foul Legacy: Understream

When opponents affected by Riptide are defeated, Tartaglia regenerates 4 Elemental Energy.

C3: Abyssal Mayhem: Vortex of Turmoil

Increases the Level of Foul Legacy: Raging Tide by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C4: Abyssal Mayhem: Hydrospout

If Tartaglia is in Foul Legacy: Raging Tide's Melee Stance, triggers Riptide Slash against opponents on the field affected by Riptide every 4s, otherwise, triggers Riptide Flash.
Riptide Slashes and Riptide Flashes triggered by this Constellation effect are not subject to the time intervals that would typically apply to these two Riptide effects, nor do they have any effect on those time intervals.

C5: Havoc: Formless Blade

Increases the Level of Havoc: Obliteration by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C6: Havoc: Annihilation

When Havoc: Obliteration is cast in Melee Stance, the CD of Foul Legacy: Raging Tide is reset.
This effect will only take place once Tartaglia returns to his Ranged Stance.

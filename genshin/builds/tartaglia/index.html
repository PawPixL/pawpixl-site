Tartaglia Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts & Teams

Role: DPS

Tartaglia is a fast paced Hydro DPS who excels at reaction-driven teams and devastating AoE damage through Riptide. If you love chaos ginger energy, he's meant for you.

Best weapons for Tartaglia

  1. Polar Star
  2. Thundering Pulse
  3. Hunter's Path
  4. Skyward Harp
  5. Aqua Simulacra
  6. The Viridescent Hunt
  7. Chain Breaker
  8. Blackcliff Warbow

Best artifacts

  • Nymph's Dream (4 pcs) · Main DPS
  • Heart of Depth (4 pcs) · Main DPS
  • Night Of The Sky'S Unveiling (4 pcs) · Lunar Charged Only

Main stats

  • Sands: ATK%
  • Goblet: Hydro DMG
  • Circlet: Crit Rate / Crit DMG

Stat targets

  • CRIT Rate: 70% - 80%
  • CRIT DMG: 180%+
  • ATK: 1,800 - 2,100+
  • Energy Recharge: 110% - 130%

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 6-8
  • Elemental Skill: Level 9+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 9+

Best teams

  • Ginger Snaps: Tartaglia, Nicole, Durin, Kaedehara Kazuha
  • Waterboarding: Tartaglia, Columbina, Ineffa, Citlali
  • Childe, Please: Tartaglia, Furina, Escoffier, Citlali
  • Childe Support: Tartaglia, Bennett, Xiangling, Sucrose

  • Flexible teams

Cons

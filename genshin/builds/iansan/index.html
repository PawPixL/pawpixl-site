Iansan Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

Iansan build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Calamity Queller, Engulfing Lightning), artifacts (Scroll of the Hero of Cinder City), main stats.

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Role: Buff Support

Iansan is an Electro support who provides powerful ATK buffs, healing, and off-field utility. She excels alongside mobile DPS characters who can maintain her Nightsoul buffs.

Best weapons for Iansan

  1. Calamity Queller: Provides exceptionally high Base ATK and an ATK% bonus, strengthening Iansan's ATK-scaling buffs while improving her personal damage.
  2. Engulfing Lightning: High Energy Recharge with ER-to-ATK conversion. Best when frequent Bursts matter.
  3. Symphonist of Scents: Provides high Base ATK and an ATK% bonus, strengthening Iansan's teamwide ATK buffs while improving her personal damage.
  4. Tamayuratei no Ohanashi: Provides Energy Recharge and an ATK% bonus after using her Elemental Skill, helping Iansan maintain Burst uptime while strengthening her team buffs.
  5. Skyward Spine: High Base ATK and Energy Recharge; a flexible stat stick when team ER needs are high.
  6. Kitain Cross Spear: Craftable EM and Skill damage; trades Energy briefly for later Energy restoration.
  7. "The Catch": Provides Energy Recharge and increases Elemental Burst damage and CRIT Rate, helping Iansan maintain Burst uptime and improve her personal damage.
  8. Frostbreath: ER polearm granting ATK and team Energy after the wielder triggers a Cryo/Hydro-related reaction; needs a suitable team. Needs Iansan to trigger Cryo/Hydro reaction.

Best artifacts for Iansan

  • Scroll of the Hero of Cinder City (4 pcs) · Best Support: Up to 40% Elemental DMG Bonus to elements in her reactions. Best support set when the desired reaction can trigger it. Main stats: Energy Recharge > ATK%.
  • Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs) · Support: Provides a 20% teamwide ATK buff after using Iansan's Burst, further boosting her support capabilities and strengthening ATK-scaling DPS teammates. Main stats: Energy Recharge > ATK%.
  • Emblem of Severed Fate + Gladiator's Finale (2 + 2 pcs) · Support: Combines 20% Energy Recharge and 18% ATK. Temporary option; does not grant the 4-piece Burst damage bonus. Main stats: Energy Recharge > ATK%.

Main stats

  • Sands: ATK% / Energy Recharge
  • Goblet: ATK%
  • Circlet: ATK%

Stat targets

  • ATK: 2,500+
  • Energy Recharge: 135% to 260% depending on team

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 1+ (optional)
  • Elemental Burst: Level 9+

Best teams for Iansan

  • Overload Express: Iansan, Varesa, Mavuika, Chevreuse. Varesa delivers powerful plunge damage while Mavuika adds Pyro damage and Nightsoul support. Chevreuse provides Overload buffs and resistance reduction, while Iansan boosts team ATK.
  • Ashes to Ashes: Iansan, Mavuika, Ororon, Chevreuse. Mavuika leads the damage while Iansan buffs ATK and supports healing. Ororon adds off field Electro and Chevreuse boosts the Overloaded team.
  • Gravity? Never Heard of Her: Iansan, Varesa, Furina, Xianyun. Varesa takes advantage of Xianyun's plunge buffs and Iansan's ATK support, while Furina provides a powerful teamwide damage bonus. Excellent synergy with Varesa's highly mobile playstyle.
  • Four-Star Fitness: Iansan, Gaming, Xingqiu, Bennett. Gaming delivers powerful Pyro plunges and Vaporize damage with Xingqiu's Hydro application. Iansan and Bennett provide ATK buffs, while Bennett also supplies additional healing.

Pros

  • Powerful ATK buffs
  • Consistent healing
  • Mobile support
  • Excellent team synergy

Cons

  • Energy hungry
  • ATK dependent
  • Movement dependent buffs

Build variations

Scroll Team Buffer (Support)

Maximize team damage with Scroll buffs and Iansan’s ATK support.

  • Weapons: Calamity Queller, Tamayuratei no Ohanashi, Engulfing Lightning
  • Artifacts: Scroll of the Hero of Cinder City (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Prioritize enough ER, then ATK%; CR for Favonius if used.
  • Teams: An active carry that moves frequently; enable an Electro reaction.
  • When it applies: Her buffs depend on movement and Nightsoul mechanics.

Noblesse ATK Support (Support)

Offers a Burst-triggered ATK buff without duplicating Scroll.

  • Weapons: Tamayuratei no Ohanashi, Engulfing Lightning
  • Artifacts: Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Meet ER needs before ATK%; add healing as desired.
  • Teams: Use when another teammate already equips Scroll.
  • When it applies: Needs sufficiently frequent Burst use.

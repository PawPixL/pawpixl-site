Iansan Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts & Teams

Iansan build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Calamity Queller, Engulfing Lightning), artifacts (Scroll of the Hero of Cinder City), main stats…

Role: Buff Support

Iansan is an Electro support who provides powerful ATK buffs, healing, and off-field utility. She excels alongside mobile DPS characters who can maintain her Nightsoul buffs.

Best weapons for Iansan

  1. Calamity Queller
  2. Engulfing Lightning
  3. Symphonist of Scents
  4. Tamayuratei no Ohanashi
  5. Skyward Spine
  6. Kitain Cross Spear
  7. "The Catch"
  8. Frostbreath

Best artifacts

  • Scroll of the Hero of Cinder City (4 pcs) · Best Support
  • Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs) · Support
  • Emblem of Severed Fate + Gladiator's Finale (2 + 2 pcs) · Support

Main stats

  • Sands: ATK% / Energy Recharge
  • Goblet: ATK%
  • Circlet: ATK%

Stat targets

  • Energy Recharge: 180% ~ 200%+
  • ATK: 2500+

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 9+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 9+

Best teams

  • Overload Express: Iansan, Varesa, Mavuika, Chevreuse
  • Ashes to Ashes: Iansan, Mavuika, Ororon, Chevreuse
  • Gravity? Never Heard of Her: Iansan, Varesa, Furina, Xianyun
  • Four-Star Fitness: Iansan, Gaming, Xingqiu, Bennett

Pros

  • Powerful ATK buffs
  • Consistent healing
  • Mobile support
  • Excellent team synergy

Cons

  • Energy hungry
  • ATK dependent
  • Movement dependent buffs

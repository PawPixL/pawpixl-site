Columbina Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

Columbina build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Nocturne's Curtain Call, Reliquary of Truth), artifacts (Silken Moon's Serenade), main stats.

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Role: Support / Sub DPS

Columbina provides off-field Hydro and supports Lunar-Charged, Lunar-Bloom, and Lunar-Crystallize teams. She can focus on team buffs or personal Lunar damage, depending on artifacts and teammates.

Best weapons for Columbina

  1. Nocturne's Curtain Call: Signature CD, HP and Energy support with bonuses to Lunar reaction damage.
  2. Reliquary of Truth: Strong for an on-field Lunar-Bloom build; its brief CRIT passive is harder to use off-field. Primarily for on-field Columbina.
  3. Sacrificial Jade: Strong off-field CR, HP and EM; avoid CR overcap and check Burst Energy needs.
  4. Waveriding Whirl: ER-oriented catalyst for supporting Burst uptime; its on-field Normal Attack effects have limited value. Energy utility over personal damage.
  5. Surf's Up: CD and HP for personal damage; its Normal Attack passive is less useful off-field.
  6. Prototype Amber: Craftable HP, Energy refund and healing after Burst.
  7. Favonius Codex: ER and team particles on CRIT; useful when Energy is tight.
  8. Thrilling Tales of Dragon Slayers: Gives the next active ATK-scaling ally an ATK buff; sacrifices Columbina’s own damage. Only when the next carry benefits from ATK.

Best artifacts for Columbina

  • Silken Moon's Serenade (4 pcs) · Support: ER and team Lunar reaction buffs. Use when the set is not already covered. Main stats: HP%/ER · HP% · CR/CD.
  • Aubade of Morningstar and Moon (4 pcs) · Support: Boosts her personal Lunar damage when another teammate handles Silken Moon. Main stats: HP%/ER · HP% · CR/CD.
  • Tenacity of the Millelith (4 pcs) · Support: HP plus team ATK when her Skill hits. Niche if supporting an ATK carry and Lunar sets are covered. Main stats: ER / HP% · HP% · CR/CD.

Main stats

Best teams for Columbina

  • Watt the Flins: Columbina (Hydro Support), Flins (DPS), Ineffa (Sub DPS), Sucrose (Support). Flins drives Lunar-Charged damage while Ineffa applies Electro. Columbina provides Hydro and Lunar buffs; Sucrose groups and reduces RES.
  • Bloom Goes the Dynamite: Columbina (Hydro Support), Nefer (DPS), Lauma (Sub DPS), Sucrose (Support). Nefer drives Lunar-Bloom with Lauma’s Dendro support. Columbina supplies Hydro and Lunar buffs, while Sucrose groups enemies.
  • Moon Over Matter: Columbina (Hydro Support), Zibai (DPS), Linnea (Sub DPS), Illuga (Support). Zibai deals Geo damage with Lunar-Crystallize enabled by Columbina’s Hydro. Linnea and Illuga add support and damage.
  • MOONIMUM WAGE: Columbina (Hydro Support), Fischl (DPS), Aino (Sub DPS), Sucrose (Support). Columbina and Aino apply Hydro, Fischl supplies Electro, and Sucrose groups enemies and boosts reaction damage.

Build variations

Lunar Team Buffer (Off-field Support)

Prioritize Lunar team buffs and Hydro application over personal damage.

  • Weapons: Nocturne's Curtain Call, Prototype Amber, Favonius Codex
  • Artifacts: Silken Moon's Serenade (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Build enough ER for reliable Burst use, then HP and CRIT.
  • Teams: Lunar reaction team; no duplicate Silken Moon holder.
  • When it applies: Energy needs are higher when solo Hydro

Off-field Lunar Damage (Off-field DPS)

Maximize Columbina’s own Lunar reaction damage with a dedicated damage set.

  • Weapons: Nocturne's Curtain Call, Sacrificial Jade, Reliquary of Truth
  • Artifacts: Aubade of Morningstar and Moon (4 pcs)
  • Stats: HP% / HP% / CRIT; balance ER and CR.
  • Teams: Another character uses Silken Moon.
  • When it applies: Best when another teammate carries Silken Moon and Lunar reactions are frequent.

Budget Utility (Off-field Support)

Trade some personal damage for Energy, healing, or accessible buffs.

  • Weapons: Prototype Amber, Favonius Codex, Thrilling Tales of Dragon Slayers
  • Artifacts: Silken Moon's Serenade (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ER and HP first; CR for Favonius.
  • Teams: Prototype Amber helps sustain; TTDS needs an ATK carry.
  • When it applies: TTDS is not useful for every Lunar DPS

Columbina talents

Normal Attack: Moondew Cascade

Normal Attack
Summons Moonlit Tides, performing up to 3 attacks that deal Hydro DMG.

Charged Attack
Consumes a certain amount of Stamina to deal AoE Hydro DMG after a short casting time.
If you have at least 1 Verdant Dew, Columbina's Charged Attacks will be replaced with the special Charged Attack Moondew Cleanse, which will not consume Stamina: Consume 1 Verdant Dew to deal 3 instances of AoE Dendro DMG to opponents in front. This DMG is considered Lunar-Bloom DMG.

Plunging Attack
Plunges towards the ground from mid-air, damaging all opponents in her path. Deals AoE Hydro DMG upon impact with the ground.

Elemental Skill: Eternal Tides

Cooldown: 17s

Awaken Moonbound Tides, dealing AoE Hydro DMG and summoning Gravity Ripple.
Gravity Ripple will follow the active character on the field, while continuously dealing AoE Hydro DMG to surrounding opponents. While Gravity Ripple is on the field, and nearby party members trigger Lunar reactions or inflict Lunar Reaction DMG, Columbina will accrue a special Gravity. Once Gravity is accumulated to its limit, different types of damage will be dealt to enemies based on the type of Lunar Reaction that has accumulated the most Gravity for Columbina.
Moonsign: Ascendant Gleam: Gravity Ripple inflicts AoE Hydro DMG over a wider area.

Elemental Burst: Moonlit Melancholy

Cooldown: 15s · Energy cost: 60

Hills and tides unite under the silver light of a pristine new moon. Temporarily transforms the surrounding terrain into Lunar Domain, dealing AoE Hydro DMG.

Passive talent: Lunacy's Lure

When triggering Gravity Interference, Columbina gains the Lunacy effect, increasing her CRIT Rate by 5% for 10s. This effect can be stacked up to 3 times.

Passive talent: Law of the New Moon

When characters within the Lunar Domain trigger Lunar reactions, they will gain the following effects respectively:
· Lunar-Charged: When the Thundercloud performs lightning strike(s) against a valid target, it has a 33% chance to perform an additional lightning strike.
· Lunar-Bloom: When triggering Lunar-Bloom reactions, the party gains a special Moonridge Dew. Up to 3 Moonridge Dews can be gained every 18s.
· Lunar-Crystallize: When Moondrift Harmony is triggered, every Moondrift has a 33% chance to inflict an extra attack.

Passive talent: Moonsign Benediction: Moonlight, Lent Unto You

When a party member triggers an Electro-Charged, Bloom, or Hydro-Crystallize reaction, it will be converted into a Lunar-Charged, Lunar-Bloom, or Lunar-Crystallize reaction respectively. Lunar Reaction DMG inflicted by party members gain a Base DMG increase based on Columbina's Max HP. For every 1,000 points of Max HP, the Base DMG of Lunar reactions is increased by 0.2%, up to a maximum of 7%.

Additionally, when Columbina is in the party, the party's Moonsign will increase by 1 level.

Passive talent: Lunar Vigil

If one of your party members is incapacitated while in the Nod-Krai or Frost Moon regions, Columbina will revive this character. The amount of HP the character will have upon revival is based on Columbina's Friendship Level. This effect can occur once every 100s. This effect does not trigger in Domains, Trounce Domains, or the Spiral Abyss.

Columbina constellations

C1: Radiance Over Blossoms and Peaks

Unleashing her Elemental Skill Eternal Tides will immediately trigger an effect equivalent to the effect of Gravity Interference. This effect can be triggered once every 15s.
Moonsign: Ascendant Gleam
When Gravity Interference is triggered, if the Lunar reaction type that has accumulated the most Gravity for Columbina is:
· Lunar-Charged: Your current active character will recover 6 Energy.
· Lunar-Bloom: Your active character's interruption resistance will be increased for 8s.
· Lunar-Crystallize: Summons a Rainsea Shield. The Rainsea Shield's DMG absorption is based on 12% of Columbina's Max HP, and it will absorb Hydro DMG with 250% effectiveness for 8s.

All nearby party members' Lunar Reaction DMG is elevated by 1.5%.

C2: Not in Lone Splendor

Rate of accumulating Gravity increases by 34%.
When Gravity Interference is triggered, Columbina will receive the Lunar Brilliance effect, which increases her Max HP by 40% for 8s.
Moonsign: Ascendant Gleam:
When Lunar Brilliance is active, if, during the current trigger of Gravity Interference, the Lunar reaction type that has accumulated the most Gravity for Columbina is:
· Lunar-Charged: ATK of your current active character increases. Increase in ATK is equal to 1% of Columbina's Max HP.
· Lunar-Bloom: Elemental Mastery of your current active character increases. Increase in Elemental Mastery is equal to 0.35% of Columbina's Max HP.
· Lunar-Crystallize: DEF of your current active character increases. Increase in DEF is equal to 1% of Columbina's Max HP.

All nearby party members' Lunar Reaction DMG is elevated by 7%.

C3: Dreamlike Glow Across Tranquil Waters

Increases the Level of Elemental Skill Eternal Tides by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

All nearby party members' Lunar Reaction DMG is elevated by 1.5%.

C4: Cloudveiled Ridges in Floral Mists

When Gravity Interference is triggered, 4 Elemental Energy will be restored to Columbina;
Additionally, if the Lunar reaction type that accumulates the most Gravity for Columbina is Lunar-Charged, Lunar-Bloom, or Lunar-Crystallize, the Lunar Reaction DMG dealt by this instance of Gravity Interference is increased by 12.5%/2.5%/12.5% of Columbina's Max HP respectively. This effect can be triggered at most once every 15s.

All nearby party members' Lunar Reaction DMG is elevated by 1.5%.

C5: Silence Tending One Lone Song

Increases the Level of Elemental Burst Moonlit Melancholy by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

All nearby party members' Lunar Reaction DMG is elevated by 1.5%.

C6: Through Darkness Led by Moonlight

For 8s after characters in the Lunar Domain trigger a Lunar reaction, based on the elements involved in the reaction, the CRIT DMG of the corresponding Elemental DMG done by all party members is increased by 80%. The CRIT DMG-increasing effects for the same Elemental Type do not stack.

All nearby party members' Lunar Reaction DMG is elevated by 7%.

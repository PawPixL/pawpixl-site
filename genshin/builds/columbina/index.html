Role: Support / Sub DPS

Columbina is a Hydro support/sub DPS who enables and buffs Lunar Reactions. She provides long lasting off field Hydro while boosting team reaction damage.

Best weapons for Columbina

  1. Nocturne's Curtain Call
  2. Reliquary of Truth
  3. Sacrificial Jade
  4. Waveriding Whirl
  5. Surf's Up
  6. Prototype Amber
  7. Favonius Codex
  8. Thrilling Tales of Dragon Slayers

Best artifacts

  • Silken Moon's Serenade (4 pcs) · Support
  • Aubade of Morningstar and Moon (4 pcs) · Support
  • Tenacity of the Millelith (4 pcs) · Support

Main stats

  • Sands: HP% / Energy Recharge
  • Goblet: HP%
  • Circlet: Crit Rate / Crit DMG

Stat targets

  • HP: 30,000-35,000
  • Crit Rate: 60% - 70%
  • Crit DMG: 160% - 200%+
  • Energy Recharge: 160%-200%+

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 9+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 9+

Best teams

  • Watt the Flins: Columbina, Flins, Ineffa, Sucrose
  • Bloom Goes the Dynamite: Columbina, Nefer, Lauma, Sucrose
  • Moon Over Matter: Columbina, Zibai, Linnea, Illuga
  • MOONIMUM WAGE: Columbina, Fischl, Aino, Sucrose

Pros

  • Enables Lunar Reactions
  • Great Hydro application
  • Strong off-field damage
  • Powerful team buffs

Cons

  • High ER needs
  • Lunar-team reliant
  • Wants specific teammates
  • Lower value outside Lunar

Mitya Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

Mitya build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Starsealed Edict, A Thousand Floating Dreams), artifacts (Disenchantment in Deep Shadow), main stats.

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Role: On & Off-field

Flexible Stellar-Conduct unit who can be played as either an on-field DPS or off-field support. His C0 dmg is solid, while his Cons significantly boost both his personal dmg and team utility.

Best weapons for Mitya

  1. Starsealed Edict: Provides CRIT DMG, EM, and powerful Stellar-Conduct CRIT bonuses. Mitya's strongest signature option for both playstyles.
  2. A Thousand Floating Dreams: Provides substantial EM and shares additional EM with teammates. Excellent for off-field Mitya's support role.
  3. Kagura's Verity: Provides high CD and Skill DMG bonuses.
  4. Winter's Heavy Heart: Provides CRIT DMG, EM, and Stellar Glimmer damage bonuses based on Cryo and Electro teammates. An excellent four-star option.
  5. Dawning Frost: CRIT DMG with EM after Charged Attack and Skill hits. Strong for Mitya’s Stellar Glimmer damage.
  6. Clash of Kings: Provides 27.6% CRIT Rate and, after using an Elemental Skill, grants ATK and EM bonuses, making it a strong 4-star option for Mitya.
  7. Sunny Morning Sleep-In: Elemental Mastery and reaction-related damage utility; best suited to reaction-centered builds.
  8. Etherlight Spindlelute: Energy Recharge event catalyst; helps meet Burst needs where personal damage is secondary.

Best artifacts for Mitya

  • Disenchantment in Deep Shadow (4 pcs) · Main DPS: Boosts Stellar-Conduct damage by 40% and grants 16% conditional CRIT Rate. A strong on-field damage set. Main stats: EM · EM · CR/CD.
  • Gilded Dreams (4 pcs) · Main DPS: Provides EM after triggering reactions.
  • Heart of the Furnace (4 pcs) · Off-field: Boosts teamwide Stellar Glimmer damage after triggering its reactions. Best for off-field support when no teammate already carries it.

Best teams for Mitya

  • On-Field Live Wire: Mitya (On-field DPS), Yae Miko (Off-field DPS), Odette (Cryo Enabler / Buffer), Valeriy (Shielder / Buffer). Mitya leads with powerful Stellar-Conduct attacks, while Yae adds Electro damage, Odette boosts reactions, and Valeriy shields and buffs.
  • Off-Field Puppet: Mitya (Off-field DPS / Enabler), Sandrone (On-field DPS), Odette (Cryo Enabler / Buffer), Valeriy (Shielder / Buffer). Sandrone leads the damage while Mitya supplies off-field Stellar-Conduct support. Odette boosts reactions, and Valeriy adds protection.
  • Maximum Security: Mitya (Off-field DPS / Enabler), Wriothesley (On-field DPS), Traveler (Cryo) (Cryo Enabler / Sub DPS), Valeriy (Shielder / Buffer). Wriothesley deals on-field Cryo damage while Mitya supports Stellar-Conduct. Cryo Traveler adds application, and Valeriy provides protection.
  • Economitya F2P: Mitya (On-field DPS), qiqi (Healer), Sucrose (EM Buffer / Debuffer), Valeriy (Shielder / Buffer). Mitya drives Stellar-Conduct while Valeriy shields, Sucrose shares EM and groups enemies, and Qiqi supplies Cryo and healing.

Build variations

On-field Stellar-Conduct (On-field DPS)

Use his damage-focused Skill mode and Beacon-enhanced attacks.

  • Weapons: Starsealed Edict, Kagura's Verity, Dawning Frost
  • Artifacts: Disenchantment in Deep Shadow (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Build EM and CRIT; check ER for Burst uptime.
  • Teams: Cryo and Electro teammates; reliable Stellar-Conduct.
  • When it applies: Protect against interruption while charging.

Off-field Conduct Support (Support)

Use his support Skill mode to buff reaction damage from off-field.

  • Weapons: Starsealed Edict, A Thousand Floating Dreams, Clash of Kings
  • Artifacts: Heart of the Furnace (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Prioritize EM and team utility; Burst often optional.
  • Teams: Stellar-Conduct carry and consistent Cryo/Electro reactions.
  • When it applies: Do not assume on-field damage passives work off-field.

Lower-cost Conduct DPS (On-field DPS)

Accessible route to Mitya’s damage-focused playstyle.

  • Weapons: Dawning Frost, Clash of Kings
  • Artifacts: Disenchantment in Deep Shadow (4 pcs); Gilded Dreams (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Prioritize EM/CRIT and enough ER for rotation.
  • Teams: A functional Stellar-Conduct enabler.
  • When it applies: Check individual weapon passive trigger conditions.

Mitya talents

Normal Attack: Chain Reaction

Normal Attack
Performs a maximum of 3 attacks that deal Electro DMG.

Charged Attack
Consumes a certain amount of Stamina to deal AoE Electro DMG to the area in front after a short casting time.
Mitya will instead perform the special Charged Attack Gradient Detonation, which consumes no Stamina, if a Detection Core: Overload created by Mitya himself is currently on the field and he has at least 1 Gradient Beacon: Deals 1 instance of AoE Electro DMG to the area in front and continuously consumes Gradient Beacons while held. Each Gradient Beacon consumed deals 1 instance of AoE Electro DMG to the area in front that is considered Stellar-Conduct reaction DMG.

Plunging Attack
Plunges from mid-air to strike the ground below, damaging opponents along the path and dealing AoE Electro DMG upon impact.

Elemental Skill: Principle: Variable Detection

Cooldown: 18s

Conducts a theoretically relatively safe experiment at a theoretically relatively safe yield, dealing AoE Electro DMG and creating a Detection Core in different ways depending on whether the Skill is tapped or held.

Tapping the Elemental Skill Principle: Variable Detection again within 6s after using it changes the Detection Core's state. The Detection Core's state can be changed in this way every 1.5s.

Elemental Burst: Analysis: Gradient Theory

Cooldown: 15s · Energy cost: 60

Let the elements burst forth like inspiration itself! Deals AoE Electro DMG and creates 3 Gradient Beacons.

Passive talent: Baseline Stress Test

The Detection Core created by the Elemental Skill Principle: Variable Detection is enhanced. Depending on the state of the Detection Core created:
·Steady State: All nearby party members gain 30 Elemental Mastery for each Gradient Beacon Mitya has;
·Overloaded: After nearby party members trigger Stellar-Conduct reactions, instead obtain 1 Gradient Beacon every 1.5s for 6s.

Passive talent: Explosive Reagent: Safe Limits Exceeded

The duration of Gradient Beacons is increased to 10s. Additionally, Mitya's CRIT Rate increases by 5% for 15s after creating a Gradient Beacon. This effect can stack up to 4 times.

Passive talent: Stellar Jubilee: Top-Secret Protocol

When party members trigger Superconduct, it will be changed to Stellar-Conduct. Stellar-Conduct reactions will no longer create Starlight Prisms, and will instead create 1 Gradient Beacon nearby every 1.5s for 3s. These Beacons can be carried by Mitya and transform the area around them into an even larger Polestar Field. Triggering another Stellar-Conduct reaction will refresh the duration of the aforementioned effects.
Additionally, the Base Stellar-Conduct DMG dealt by all nearby party members will be increased based on Mitya's Elemental Mastery: Every point of Elemental Mastery he has increases Base Stellar-Conduct DMG by 0.028%. The maximum Base DMG increase that can be gained this way is 14%. While within a Polestar Field, Mitya will also enter the Radiance: Stellar-Conduct state.

Passive talent: Energy Catalysis

When destructible rocks are nearby, Detection Core: Steady State can quickly clear them.
Additionally, when Mitya is in the party, creating Prime Ice Construct: Basic Heat Source does not consume Kresnik Energy.

Mitya constellations

C1: The Observer Seeking What Is Missing

The maximum number of Gradient Beacons Mitya can carry is increased to 5. Using the Elemental Skill Principle: Variable Detection additionally creates 1 Gradient Beacon.
While the Detection Core is present, your own active character's CRIT DMG is increased by 40%.
Additionally, Mitya regenerates 10 Elemental Energy when a Gradient Beacon is created. This effect can be triggered once every 8s.

C2: The Matter That Slumbers Beyond Inertness

While the Detection Core is present, all opponents within the Polestar Field have their Cryo RES and Electro RES decreased by 20%.
Additionally, if the Detection Core created is:
·Steady State: All nearby party members gain 123 Elemental Mastery;
·Overloaded: Your own active character gains 234 Elemental Mastery.

C3: The Knotted String of Invariant Topology

Increases the Level of the Elemental Skill Principle: Variable Detection by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C4: The Isotopes Born of Decay

Detection Cores created by Mitya himself deals 1 instance of AoE Electro DMG equal to 300% of Mitya's Elemental Mastery to nearby opponents for every 5 Gradient Beacons consumed while it is on the field. This DMG is considered Stellar-Conduct reaction DMG.

C5: The Periodic Law That Orders All

Increases the Level of Normal Attack: Chain Reaction by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C6: Elemental Transition at the Gate of Reason

Detection Core is enhanced. If the Detection Core:
· Is in the Steady State: Detection Core deals 115% of its original DMG.
· Is Overloaded: There is a 34% chance to create 1 new Gradient Beacon whenever Mitya consumes a Gradient Beacon with the Detection Core.
Additionally, Stellar-Conduct reaction DMG dealt by all nearby party members is Elevated by 20%.

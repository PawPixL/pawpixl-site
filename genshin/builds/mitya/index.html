Mitya Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts & Teams

Mitya build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Starsealed Edict, A Thousand Floating Dreams), artifacts (Disenchantment in Deep Shadow), main stats…

Role: On & Off-field

Flexible Stellar-Conduct unit who can be played as either an on-field DPS or off-field support. His C0 dmg is solid, while his Cons significantly boost both his personal dmg and team utility.

Best weapons for Mitya

  1. Starsealed Edict
  2. A Thousand Floating Dreams
  3. Kagura's Verity
  4. Winter's Heavy Heart
  5. Dawning Frost
  6. Clash of Kings
  7. Sunny Morning Sleep-In
  8. Etherlight Spindlelute

Best artifacts

  • Disenchantment in Deep Shadow (4 pcs) · Main DPS
  • Gilded Dreams (4 pcs) · Main DPS
  • Heart of the Furnace (4 pcs) · Off-field

Main stats

  • Sands: Elemental Mastery
  • Goblet: Elemental Mastery
  • Circlet: Crit Rate / Crit DMG

Stat targets

  • EM: 450-550
  • Crit Rate: 60% - 70%
  • Crit DMG: 200%+
  • ER: 130%-160%

Talent priority

  • Chain Reaction: Level 9+
  • Principle: Variable Detection: Level 6+
  • Analysis: Gradient Theory: Level 9+

Best teams

  • On-Field Live Wire: Mitya, Yae Miko, Odette, Valeriy
  • Off-Field Puppet: Mitya, Sandrone, Odette, Valeriy
  • Maximum Security: Mitya, Wriothesley, Traveler (Cryo), Valeriy
  • Economitya F2P: Mitya, qiqi, Sucrose, Valeriy

Pros

  • On/Off-Field Flexibility
  • Strong Constellations
  • Short Rotations
  • Team EM Buff

Cons

  • C0 is just OK
  • Energy Hungry
  • Easily Interrupted
  • Stellar-Conduct Reliant

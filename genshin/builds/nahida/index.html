Nahida Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams (7.2)

Nahida build guide for Genshin Impact 7.2: best weapons (A Thousand Floating Dreams, Kagura's Verity), artifacts (Deepwood Memories), main stats.

Last updated: November 12, 2026 · Game version 7.2

Role: Support / Sub DPS

Nahida is a powerful Dendro support and DPS, providing consistent application, reaction buffs, and impressive damage through her Elemental Skill.

Best weapons for Nahida

  1. A Thousand Floating Dreams: Boosts Elemental Mastery and provides teamwide buffs.
  2. Kagura's Verity: Boosts Elemental Skill damage and provides CRIT DMG.
  3. The Widsith: Provides CRIT DMG and powerful random buffs that enhance Nahida's damage potential.
  4. Skyward Atlas: High Base ATK and ATK% with an Elemental DMG bonus; strong unconditional offensive stats.
  5. Lost Prayer to the Sacred Winds: High CRIT Rate with an Elemental DMG bonus that stacks during on-field time.
  6. Wandering Evenstar: Provides Elemental Mastery and converts it into additional ATK.
  7. Solar Pearl: Enhances Normal Attack and Skill / Burst damage, plus a modest CRIT Rate substat.
  8. Sacrificial Fragments: Provides high Elemental Mastery and an occasional extra Skill cast. A practical accessible support option.

Best artifacts for Nahida

  • Deepwood Memories (4 pcs) · Best Overall: Shreds enemy Dendro RES, boosting Nahida's damage and the team's output. Main stats: EM · EM/Dendro DMG · EM/CR.
  • Gilded Dreams (4 pcs) · Support: Provides Elemental Mastery and ATK to strengthen reaction damage. Main stats: EM · EM/Dendro DMG · EM/CR.
  • Golden Troupe (4 pcs) · Support: Boosts Elemental Skill damage, enhancing Nahida's off-field DPS. Main stats: EM · EM/Dendro DMG · EM/CR.

Main stats

  • Sands: Elemental Mastery
  • Goblet: Elemental Mastery / Dendro DMG
  • Circlet: Elemental Mastery / Crit Rate

Stat targets

  • Energy Recharge: 120% - 150%
  • Elemental Mastery: 800-1,000

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Not Priority
  • Elemental Skill: Level 9+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 9+

Best teams for Nahida

  • Crit Happens: Nahida (EM Buffs), Nefer (Main DPS), Lauma (Buffs), Columbina (Lunar). Nahida and Lauma provide Dendro application and Lunar-Bloom support, while Nefer and Columbina contribute powerful Lunar-Bloom damage.
  • Touch Grass: Nahida (Dendro Enabler / Buffer), Alhaitham (On-field DPS), Yae Miko (Off-field DPS), Kuki Shinobu (Healer / Reaction Trigger). Double Dendro provides EM resonance, while Yae and Kuki maintain Electro application for Quicken. Nahida and Alhaitham deal substantial Spread damage.
  • Sprout Squad: Nahida (Dendro Enabler / Buffer), Nilou (Bloom Buffer / Hydro Enabler), Lauma (Dendro Buffer / Enabler), Sangonomiya Kokomi (Healer / Hydro Enabler). Nilou enables Bountiful Cores in this Hydro and Dendro team. Kokomi heals and applies Hydro, while Nahida and Lauma supply Dendro and reaction support.
  • Don't Leaf Me: Nahida (Dendro Enabler / Buffer), Fischl (Off-field DPS / Battery), Xingqiu (Off-field DPS / Hydro Enabler), Kuki Shinobu (Healer / Reaction Trigger). Nahida applies Dendro to generate Cores, Xingqiu provides Hydro, and Fischl adds Electro damage. Kuki triggers Hyperbloom while providing healing.

Pros

  • Excellent application
  • Strong reactions
  • EM sharing
  • Flexible builds

Cons

  • Wave content can compete for field-time MDPS.
  • Low Health
  • Burst Reliant

Build variations

Deepwood Support (Support)

Shred enemy Dendro resistance and support reaction damage.

  • Weapons: A Thousand Floating Dreams, Sacrificial Fragments
  • Artifacts: Deepwood Memories (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Build EM; ER only as needed for Burst.
  • Teams: Dendro reaction team; avoid duplicate Deepwood.

Aggravate DPS (Off-field DPS)

Favor personal Spread damage when another ally wears Deepwood.

  • Weapons: A Thousand Floating Dreams, Kagura's Verity
  • Artifacts: Gilded Dreams (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Balance EM, Dendro DMG and CRIT.
  • Teams: Electro and Dendro units to sustain Quicken.

Varesa Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams (7.2)

Varesa build guide for Genshin Impact 7.2: best weapons (Vivid Notions, Kagura's Verity), artifacts (Long Night's Oath), main stats, talent priority.

Last updated: November 12, 2026 · Game version 7.2

Role: Main DPS

Varesa is a 5-star Electro Main DPS built around fast Charged Attacks and powerful Plunging Attacks. Her Skill builds Nightsoul points, unlocking an enhanced state and a special Burst for high Plunge damage.

Best weapons for Varesa

  1. Vivid Notions: Provides 44.1% CRIT DMG, 28% ATK, and up to 68% Plunging Attack CRIT DMG at R1, making it Varesa’s signature and best-in-slot weapon.
  2. Kagura's Verity: Provides 66.2% CRIT DMG and boosts Elemental Skill DMG and overall Elemental DMG, making it a strong 5-star alternative for Varesa.
  3. Lost Prayer to the Sacred Winds: High CRIT Rate with an Elemental DMG bonus that stacks during on-field time.
  4. Crane's Echoing Call: High Base ATK and Plunging Attack team support after the wielder Plunges; value depends on Plunge usage.
  5. The Widsith: Provides 55.1% CRIT DMG and a random ATK, Elemental DMG, or EM buff, offering strong but inconsistent damage boosts for Varesa.
  6. Flowing Purity: A craftable ATK% catalyst that increases Elemental DMG after Skill use. Healing clears its Bond of Life for an extra damage bonus.
  7. Winter's Heavy Heart: CRIT DMG with buffs based on Cryo/Electro allies; specialized for Stellar Glimmer teams, often only a stat stick here. Depends on Cryo/Electro party members.
  8. Blackcliff Agate: CRIT DMG catalyst with an ATK boost after defeating enemies; weak against single bosses.

Best artifacts for Varesa

  • Long Night's Oath (4 pcs) · Main DPS: Greatly increases Plunging Attack damage by building stacks from Varesa’s combat actions. One of her strongest overall sets. Main stats: ATK% · Electro DMG Bonus/ATK% · CR/CD.
  • Obsidian Codex (4 pcs) · Main DPS: Grants 40% CRIT Rate after consuming Nightsoul points, making it a powerful alternative for Varesa. Avoid overcapping CRIT Rate.
  • Gladiator's Finale + A Day Carved From Rising Winds (2 + 2 pcs) · Main DPS: Provides 36% ATK, making it a solid 2-piece combination for boosting Varesa’s Plunging Attack damage.

Best teams for Varesa

  • Plunge & Punishment: Varesa (Main DPS), Mavuika (Sub DPS), Iansan (Buffs), Chevreuse (Support). Varesa drives Plunging Attack damage, Mavuika adds off-field Pyro and buffs, Iansan boosts ATK, and Chevreuse provides healing, ATK buffs, and Pyro/Electro RES shred.
  • Natlan CrossFit: Varesa (Main DPS), Durin (Sub DPS), Iansan (Buffs), Chevreuse (Support). Varesa drives Plunging Attack damage, Durin adds off-field Pyro, Iansan boosts ATK, and Chevreuse heals, buffs ATK, and shreds Pyro/Electro RES.
  • Air Traffic Control: Varesa (Main DPS), Furina (Buffs), Iansan (Buffs), Xianyun (Support). Varesa drives Plunging Attack damage, Furina provides Hydro application and DMG buffs, Iansan boosts ATK, and Xianyun buffs Plunging Attacks and heals.
  • Bat Mobile: Varesa (Main DPS), Ororon (Reaction), Mavuika (Sub DPS), Chevreuse (Support). Varesa drives Plunging Attack damage, Mavuika provides off-field Pyro and buffs, Ororon adds Electro damage and Scroll buffs, and Chevreuse heals, boosts ATK, and shreds Pyro/Electro RES.

Build variations

Plunge Hypercarry (On-field DPS)

Maximize Varesa’s enhanced Plunging Attack windows.

  • Weapons: Vivid Notions, Crane's Echoing Call, The Widsith
  • Artifacts: Long Night's Oath (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ATK% / Electro DMG / CRIT; adjust for set bonuses.
  • Teams: Plunge support or buffers, such as Iansan.
  • When it applies: Maintain the intended plunge rotation.

Nightsoul CRIT Alternative (On-field DPS)

Use a strong existing Codex set before farming Long Night’s Oath.

  • Weapons: Vivid Notions, The Widsith
  • Artifacts: Obsidian Codex (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Avoid overcapping CRIT Rate with Obsidian Codex.
  • Teams: Standard Varesa damage team.
  • When it applies: Long Night’s Oath remains her plunge-focused option.

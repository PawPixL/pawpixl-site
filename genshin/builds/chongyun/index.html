Role: Sub DPS / Support

Chongyun is a quickswap Cryo Burst DPS who grants melee allies Cryo Infusion through his Skill. His low-cost Burst can Reverse Melt for strong damage, while his field enables niche Melt and Freeze teams. His own Normal Attacks are generally not a priority.

Best weapons for Chongyun

  1. Wolf's Gravestone
  2. Beacon of the Reed Sea
  3. Redhorn Stonethresher
  4. Skyward Pride
  5. Serpent Spine
  6. Akuoumaru
  7. Sacrificial Greatsword
  8. Mailed Flower

Best artifacts

  • Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs)
  • Emblem of Severed Fate (4 pcs)
  • Noblesse Oblige + Blizzard Strayer (2 + 2 pcs)

Main stats

  • Sands: ATK% / EM (Melt) / ER
  • Goblet: Cryo DMG Bonus
  • Circlet: CRIT Rate / CRIT DMG

Stat targets

  • ER: 125% - 175%
  • CRIT Rate: 60%+
  • CRIT DMG: 120%+
  • ATK: 1,600+

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 8=9+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 8=9+

Best teams

  • Frostfire Exchange: Chongyun, Rosaria, Xiangling, Bennett
  • Frozen Blades: Chongyun, Kaeya, Xingqiu, Jean
  • Winter Court: Chongyun, Shenhe, Kaeya, Kaedehara Kazuha
  • Budget Melt Workshop: Chongyun, Rosaria, Xiangling, Sucrose

Pros

  • Strong quickswap Burst
  • Melee Cryo infusion
  • Low-cost Burst
  • C2 cooldown reduction

Cons

  • Weak Normal Attacks
  • Infusion can disrupt teams
  • Can Shatter Frozen foes
  • Melt needs Pyro setup

Build variations

  • Reverse Melt Burst (Quickswap Burst DPS): Melt Chongyun’s Burst for high Cryo damage in quickswap teams. Apply Pyro before casting Burst.
  • Cryo Infusion Enabler (Cryo Support): Place Skill field for Cryo-infused melee attacks. C2 reduces Skill and Burst cooldowns cast inside the field.
  • Freeze Cryo Enabler (Freeze Support): Enables Kaeya or Rosaria to use Cryo Normal Attacks while Chongyun adds Burst damage and Cryo particles.
  • Pure Burst Damage (Off-field Burst DPS): Maximize his own Burst damage in quickswap teams without duplicating Noblesse buffs.
  • Budget Cryo Battery (Cryo Support): Accessible build for Cryo application, team Energy, and a Burst-triggered ATK buff.

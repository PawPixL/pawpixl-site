Chongyun Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams (7.2)

Chongyun build guide for Genshin Impact 7.2: best weapons (Wolf's Gravestone, Beacon of the Reed Sea), artifacts (Noblesse Oblige), main stats.

Last updated: November 12, 2026 · Game version 7.2

Role: Sub DPS / Support

Chongyun is a quickswap Cryo Burst DPS who grants melee allies Cryo Infusion through his Skill. His low-cost Burst can Reverse Melt for strong damage, while his field enables niche Melt and Freeze teams. His own Normal Attacks are generally not a priority.

Best weapons for Chongyun

  1. Wolf's Gravestone: High ATK and a team ATK buff.
  2. Beacon of the Reed Sea: CRIT Rate and ATK for Burst damage.
  3. Redhorn Stonethresher: High CRIT DMG; passive adds little.
  4. Skyward Pride: ER helps maintain Burst uptime.
  5. Serpent Spine: Strong CRIT and DMG at full stacks. Keep passive stacks.
  6. Akuoumaru: Boosts Burst with team Energy costs. High team Burst costs.
  7. Sacrificial Greatsword: Extra Skill for Cryo particles. Skill reset is useful.
  8. Mailed Flower: EM and ATK suit Melt Bursts. Reverse Melt only.

Best artifacts for Chongyun

  • Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs): Burst buffs team ATK by 20%. Main stats: ATK/EM · Cryo · CRIT.
  • Emblem of Severed Fate (4 pcs): ER converts into Burst damage. Main stats: ATK/ER · Cryo · CRIT.
  • Noblesse Oblige + Blizzard Strayer (2 + 2 pcs): Simple Burst and Cryo damage. Main stats: ATK/EM · Cryo · CRIT.

Main stats

  • Sands: ATK% / EM (Melt) / ER
  • Goblet: Cryo DMG Bonus
  • Circlet: CRIT Rate / CRIT DMG

Stat targets

  • ER: 125% - 175%
  • CRIT Rate: 60%+
  • CRIT DMG: 120%+
  • ATK: 1,600+

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 8=9+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 8=9+

Best teams for Chongyun

  • Frostfire Exchange: Chongyun (Cryo Enabler / Sub DPS), Rosaria (Off-field DPS / Cryo Enabler), Xiangling (Off-field DPS / Pyro Enabler), Bennett (Healer / Buffer). Xiangling and Bennett maintain Pyro for Chongyun and Rosaria to Reverse Melt; Bennett also boosts their attacks.
  • Frozen Blades: Chongyun (Cryo Enabler / Sub DPS), Kaeya (On-field DPS), Xingqiu (Off-field DPS / Hydro Enabler), Jean (Healer / Debuffer). Chongyun infuses Kaeya with Cryo; Xingqiu helps Freeze enemies while Jean heals and reduces Cryo RES. Claymore hits may Shatter.
  • Winter Court: Chongyun (Cryo Enabler / Sub DPS), Shenhe (Cryo Buffer), Kaeya (On-field DPS), Kaedehara Kazuha (Buffer / Debuffer). Shenhe buffs Cryo hits, Chongyun infuses Kaeya, and Kazuha groups enemies while reducing Cryo RES.
  • Budget Melt Workshop: Chongyun (Cryo Enabler / Sub DPS), Rosaria (Off-field DPS / Cryo Enabler), Xiangling (Off-field DPS / Pyro Enabler), Sucrose (EM Buffer / Debuffer). Xiangling supplies Pyro while Chongyun and Rosaria Reverse Melt. Sucrose shares EM and shreds resistance; lacks dedicated healing.

Pros

Build variations

Reverse Melt Burst (Quickswap Burst DPS)

Melt Chongyun’s Burst for high Cryo damage in quickswap teams. Apply Pyro before casting Burst.

  • Weapons: Mailed Flower, Serpent Spine, Akuoumaru, Wolf's Gravestone
  • Artifacts: Emblem of Severed Fate (4 pcs); Gilded Dreams (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Use EM or ATK% Sands; build CRIT and enough ER to Burst every rotation.
  • Teams: Requires consistent Pyro aura from Bennett and/or Xiangling.
  • When it applies: Burst must hit Pyro aura
  • Notes: EM only raises Chongyun’s own Melt damage; it does not transfer with his Cryo infusion.

Cryo Infusion Enabler (Cryo Support)

Place Skill field for Cryo-infused melee attacks. C2 reduces Skill and Burst cooldowns cast inside the field.

  • Weapons: Sacrificial Greatsword, Favonius Greatsword, Wolf's Gravestone
  • Artifacts: Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs); Instructor (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Prioritize ER and team utility over personal CRIT if lightly invested.
  • Teams: Melee Sword, Claymore, or Polearm ally who benefits from Cryo Infusion.
  • When it applies: Avoid unwanted infusion overrides
  • Notes: Infusion does not pass Chongyun’s EM to teammates.

Freeze Cryo Enabler (Freeze Support)

Enables Kaeya or Rosaria to use Cryo Normal Attacks while Chongyun adds Burst damage and Cryo particles.

  • Weapons: Sacrificial Greatsword, Wolf's Gravestone, Favonius Greatsword
  • Artifacts: Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs); Emblem of Severed Fate (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ATK%/Cryo/CRIT; focus ER if solo Cryo.
  • Teams: Needs Hydro application and Cryo on-field melee attacks.
  • When it applies: Blunt attacks can Shatter Freeze
  • Notes: Avoid Normal Attacking with Chongyun when trying to retain the Frozen aura.

Pure Burst Damage (Off-field Burst DPS)

Maximize his own Burst damage in quickswap teams without duplicating Noblesse buffs.

  • Weapons: Serpent Spine, Beacon of the Reed Sea, Akuoumaru, Skyward Pride
  • Artifacts: Emblem of Severed Fate (4 pcs); Noblesse Oblige + Blizzard Strayer (2 + 2 pcs)
  • Stats: Prioritize CRIT and ATK after meeting ER. EM only if reliably Melting.
  • Teams: Another teammate should carry 4pc Noblesse Oblige.
  • When it applies: Team already has a Noblesse user
  • Notes: His low-cost Burst may make 2pc mixes competitive with Emblem.

Budget Cryo Battery (Cryo Support)

Accessible build for Cryo application, team Energy, and a Burst-triggered ATK buff.

  • Weapons: Favonius Greatsword, Sacrificial Greatsword, Prototype Archaic
  • Artifacts: Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs); The Exile (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Focus ER; add CRIT Rate to trigger Favonius reliably.
  • Teams: Best with Cryo allies needing particles or general team Energy.
  • When it applies: Favonius needs on-field CRIT hits
  • Notes: The Exile is a lower-rarity temporary option.

Chongyun talents

Normal Attack: Demonbane

Normal Attack
Performs up to 4 consecutive strikes.

Charged Attack
Drains Stamina over time to perform continuous spinning attacks against all nearby opponents.
At the end of the sequence, performs a more powerful slash.

Plunging Attack
Plunges from mid-air to strike the ground below, damaging opponents along the path and dealing AoE DMG upon impact.

Elemental Skill: Spirit Blade: Chonghua's Layered Frost

Cooldown: 15s

Chongyun strikes the ground with his greatsword, causing a Cryo explosion in a circular AoE in front of him that deals Cryo DMG.
After a short delay, the cold air created by the Cryo explosion will coalesce into a Chonghua Frost Field, within which all Sword, Claymore and Polearm-wielding characters' weapons will be infused with Cryo.

Elemental Burst: Spirit Blade: Cloud-Parting Star

Cooldown: 12s · Energy cost: 40

Performing the secret hand seals, Chongyun summons 3 giant spirit blades in mid-air that fall to the earth one by one after a short delay, exploding as they hit the ground.
When the spirit blades explode, they will deal AoE Cryo DMG and launch opponents.

Passive talent: Steady Breathing

Sword, Claymore, or Polearm-wielding characters within the field created by Spirit Blade: Chonghua's Layered Frost have their Normal ATK SPD increased by 8%.

Passive talent: Rimechaser Blade

When the field created by Spirit Blade: Chonghua's Layered Frost disappears, another spirit blade will be summoned to strike nearby opponents, dealing 100% of Chonghua's Layered Frost's Skill DMG as AoE Cryo DMG. Opponents hit by this blade will have their Cryo RES decreased by 10% for 8s.

Passive talent: Gallant Journey

When dispatched on an expedition in Liyue, time consumed is reduced by 25%.

Chongyun constellations

C1: Ice Unleashed

The last attack of Chongyun's Normal Attack combo releases 3 ice blades. Each blade deals 50% of Chongyun's ATK as Cryo DMG to all opponents in its path.

C2: Atmospheric Revolution

Elemental Skills and Elemental Bursts cast within the Frost Field created by Spirit Blade: Chonghua's Layered Frost have their CD time decreased by 15%.

C3: Cloudburst

Increases the Level of Spirit Blade: Cloud-Parting Star by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C4: Frozen Skies

Chongyun regenerates 1 Energy every time he hits an opponent affected by Cryo.
This effect can only occur once every 2s.

C5: The True Path

Increases the Level of Spirit Blade: Chonghua's Layered Frost by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C6: Rally of Four Blades

Spirit Blade: Cloud-Parting Star deals 15% more DMG to opponents with a lower percentage of their Max HP remaining than Chongyun.
This skill will also summon 1 additional spirit blade.

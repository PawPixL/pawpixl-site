Kaveh Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

Kaveh build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Favonius Greatsword, Skyward Pride), artifacts (Deepwood Memories), main stats, talent priority, teams.

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Role: Main DPS / Support

Kaveh drives Bloom with on-field Dendro attacks, core ruptures, and self-healing. His Burst buffs regular Bloom, not Hyperbloom or Burgeon. Detonating a core does not change the Bloom trigger whose stats determine its damage.

Best weapons for Kaveh

  1. Favonius Greatsword: Particles ease costly Burst needs. Build CRIT Rate for reliable procs.
  2. Skyward Pride: High base ATK and Energy Recharge. Energy-focused premium option.
  3. Makhaira Aquamarine: EM also grants a small team ATK buff. After meeting Burst ER needs.
  4. Sacrificial Greatsword: Extra Skill cast adds cores and Energy. Needs passive to trigger; refinement helps.
  5. Mailed Flower: Great EM after Skill or reaction. Past event weapon; not always obtainable.
  6. Rainslasher: EM for personal Blooms and healing. Use after meeting ER needs.
  7. Forest Regalia: Craftable ER and team EM leaf. Leaf pickup can interrupt rotations.
  8. Katsuragikiri Nagamasa: Craftable weapon for Energy needs. Skill Energy refund requires timing. Higher refinements help.

Best artifacts for Kaveh

  • Deepwood Memories (4 pcs): Shreds Dendro RES for the team. Main stats: ER / EM · EM · EM/CR.
  • Ocean-Hued Clam (4 pcs): Turns his self-healing into damage. Main stats: ER / EM · EM · HB/EM.
  • Flower of Paradise Lost (4 pcs): For high personal Bloom ownership. Main stats: ER / EM · EM · EM.

Main stats

  • Sands: Energy Recharge / Elemental Mastery
  • Goblet: Elemental Mastery
  • Circlet: Elemental Mastery / CRIT Rate / Healing Bonus

Stat targets

  • ER Nahida: 145-165%
  • ER 2 Dendro: 210%+
  • ER Fav: 160-180%
  • EM: ~700 general
  • EM: ~900 Nilou

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 8-9+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 6-8
  • Elemental Burst: Level 8-9+

Best teams for Kaveh

  • Nilou Bloom Atelier: Kaveh, Nilou, Nahida, Sangonomiya Kokomi. Nilou enhances Bountiful Cores; Nahida supplies Dendro and Energy, Kokomi heals and applies Hydro. Kaveh drives on-field. Dendro/Hydro only.
  • Architects in Bloom: Kaveh, Nilou, Xingqiu, Baizhu. Nilou empowers Bloom cores; Xingqiu supplies Hydro, Baizhu sustains and helps fund Kaveh's Burst. All four satisfy Nilou's team rule.
  • Budget Bloom Workshop: Kaveh, Xingqiu, Barbara, Traveler (Dendro). Xingqiu and Barbara apply Hydro; Dendro Traveler helps Kaveh's Energy. Manage who triggers Bloom and prioritize their EM.
  • Hyperbloom Drafting: Kaveh, Xingqiu, Kuki Shinobu, Nahida. Kaveh drives Dendro; Xingqiu creates cores and full-EM Kuki triggers Hyperbloom. Kaveh's Bloom bonus does not boost Hyperbloom.

Pros

  • Comfortable Bloom driver
  • Self-heals from core hits
  • AoE Dendro application
  • Can rupture Bloom cores

Cons

  • High Burst Energy needs
  • Burst ends on swap
  • Often low Bloom ownership
  • Weak personal Talent DMG

Build variations

Nilou Bloom Driver (On-field Bloom Driver)

Drive Bountiful Core teams safely. Bloom damage is still calculated from the unit who created each core.

  • Weapons: Favonius Greatsword, Makhaira Aquamarine, Mailed Flower
  • Artifacts: Ocean-Hued Clam (4 pcs); Flower of Paradise Lost (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Prioritize enough ER, then EM; ~900 EM at C0 can improve self-healing.
  • Teams: Only Dendro/Hydro allies; preferably Nilou, another Dendro and a Hydro healer.
  • When it applies: Nilou passive requires Dendro/Hydro-only party.
  • Notes: Kaveh can self-heal from core hits; teammates still need healing.

Favonius Team Battery (On-field Support)

Choose when Burst uptime and team energy matter more than Kaveh's personal reaction damage.

  • Weapons: Favonius Greatsword, Sacrificial Greatsword, Forest Regalia
  • Artifacts: Deepwood Memories (4 pcs); Ocean-Hued Clam (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ER to meet Burst uptime; aim for sufficient CRIT Rate to trigger Favonius reliably.
  • Teams: Dendro teammate and Hydro enabler; teammates benefit from generated particles.
  • When it applies: Favonius passive requires a CRIT hit.
  • Notes: Kaveh must stay on-field during Burst; switching early ends its infusion.

Personal Bloom Ownership (On-field Bloom Trigger)

Use when Kaveh applies Dendro onto Hydro often enough to own cores; this is not typical for every Bloom team.

  • Weapons: Makhaira Aquamarine, Mailed Flower, Rainslasher
  • Artifacts: Flower of Paradise Lost (4 pcs); Gilded Dreams (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Level 90, enough ER, then stack EM; EM improves Kaveh-owned Blooms and self-healing.
  • Teams: Hydro application and a rotation that actually lets Kaveh trigger a meaningful share of Bloom reactions.
  • When it applies: Only when Kaveh owns substantial Bloom reactions.
  • Notes: Detonating an existing core does not change its trigger ownership.

Self-Healing Clam Driver (On-field Support)

Comfort-focused Bloom driver when extra personal survival is useful and another unit carries Deepwood.

  • Weapons: Favonius Greatsword, Sacrificial Greatsword, Skyward Pride
  • Artifacts: Ocean-Hued Clam (4 pcs); Deepwood Memories (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ER first; EM and Healing Bonus circlet for personal self-healing.
  • Teams: Bloom cores must damage Kaveh to activate his A1 self-healing; teammates need separate sustain.
  • When it applies: Healing triggers only when core damage hits Kaveh.
  • Notes: Clam damage relies on healing; no team healing from Kaveh.

Hyperbloom Dendro Driver (On-field Reaction Enabler)

Use Kaveh as the on-field Dendro source while Electro triggers Hyperbloom. His Burst Bloom damage bonus does not affect Hyperbloom.

  • Weapons: Favonius Greatsword, Sacrificial Greatsword, Forest Regalia
  • Artifacts: Deepwood Memories (4 pcs); Ocean-Hued Clam (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Build ER and supportive EM; Electro trigger should stack full EM and reach level 90.
  • Teams: Off-field Hydro, EM-built Electro trigger such as Kuki Shinobu, and preferably another Dendro.
  • When it applies: Do not expect Kaveh to own Hyperbloom damage.
  • Notes: Avoid detonating cores unnecessarily before the Electro trigger reaches them.

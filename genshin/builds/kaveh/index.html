Kaveh Build Guide | Best Weapons, Artifacts & Teams

Role: Main DPS / Support

Kaveh drives Bloom with on-field Dendro attacks, core ruptures, and self-healing. His Burst buffs regular Bloom, not Hyperbloom or Burgeon. Detonating a core does not change the Bloom trigger whose stats determine its damage.

Best weapons for Kaveh

  1. Favonius Greatsword
  2. Skyward Pride
  3. Makhaira Aquamarine
  4. Sacrificial Greatsword
  5. Mailed Flower
  6. Rainslasher
  7. Forest Regalia
  8. Katsuragikiri Nagamasa

Best artifacts

  • Deepwood Memories (4 pcs)
  • Ocean-Hued Clam (4 pcs)
  • Flower of Paradise Lost (4 pcs)

Main stats

  • Sands: Energy Recharge / Elemental Mastery
  • Goblet: Elemental Mastery
  • Circlet: Elemental Mastery / CRIT Rate / Healing Bonus

Stat targets

  • ER Nahida: 145-165%
  • ER 2 Dendro: 210%+
  • ER Fav: 160-180%
  • EM: ~700 general
  • EM: ~900 Nilou

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 8-9+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 6-8
  • Elemental Burst: Level 8-9+

Best teams

  • Nilou Bloom Atelier: Kaveh, Nilou, Nahida, Sangonomiya Kokomi
  • Architects in Bloom: Kaveh, Nilou, Xingqiu, Baizhu
  • Budget Bloom Workshop: Kaveh, Xingqiu, Barbara, Traveler (Dendro)
  • Hyperbloom Drafting: Kaveh, Xingqiu, Kuki Shinobu, Nahida

Pros

  • Comfortable Bloom driver
  • Self-heals from core hits
  • AoE Dendro application
  • Can rupture Bloom cores

Cons

  • High Burst Energy needs
  • Burst ends on swap
  • Often low Bloom ownership
  • Weak personal Talent DMG

Build variations

  • Nilou Bloom Driver (On-field Bloom Driver): Drive Bountiful Core teams safely. Bloom damage is still calculated from the unit who created each core.
  • Favonius Team Battery (On-field Support): Choose when Burst uptime and team energy matter more than Kaveh's personal reaction damage.
  • Personal Bloom Ownership (On-field Bloom Trigger): Use when Kaveh applies Dendro onto Hydro often enough to own cores; this is not typical for every Bloom team.
  • Self-Healing Clam Driver (On-field Support): Comfort-focused Bloom driver when extra personal survival is useful and another unit carries Deepwood.
  • Hyperbloom Dendro Driver (On-field Reaction Enabler): Use Kaveh as the on-field Dendro source while Electro triggers Hyperbloom. His Burst Bloom damage bonus does not affect Hyperbloom.

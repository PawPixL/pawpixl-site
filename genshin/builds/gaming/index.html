Gaming Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts & Teams

Gaming build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Gest of the Mighty Wolf, A Thousand Blazing Suns), artifacts (Crimson Witch of Flames), main stats…

Role: Main DPS

Gaming is a Pyro Plunges DPS who repeatedly launches Charmed Cloudstrider using his Skill & Burst. He excels in Melt & Vaporize teams, where his strong DMG & self-healing make him a powerful four-star carry. His Burst needs enough Energy to stay active each rotation.

Best weapons for Gaming

  1. Gest of the Mighty Wolf
  2. A Thousand Blazing Suns
  3. Beacon of the Reed Sea
  4. Redhorn Stonethresher
  5. Fruitful Hook
  6. Serpent Spine
  7. Rainslasher
  8. Blade of Atonement

Best artifacts

  • Crimson Witch of Flames (4 pcs)
  • Marechaussee Hunter (4 pcs)
  • Long Night's Oath (4 pcs)

Main stats

  • Sands: EM / ATK%
  • Goblet: Pyro DMG Bonus
  • Circlet: CRIT Rate / CRIT DMG

Stat targets

  • ER: 100% - 180%
  • CRIT Rate: 45% - 75%
  • CRIT DMG: 140% - 200%
  • EM: 100 - 250

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 9+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 8–9

Best teams

  • Wushou Royal Court: Gaming, Citlali, Nicole, Xianyun
  • Cloudborne Vaporize: Gaming, Furina, Xianyun, Bennett
  • Frostfire Festival: Gaming, Citlali, Bennett, Sucrose
  • Budget Lion Dance: Gaming, Xingqiu, Sucrose, Bennett

Pros

  • Powerful plunge damage
  • Strong Melt and Vaporize
  • Great at C6
  • Self-healing playstyle

Cons

  • Burst dependent
  • Needs careful timing
  • Energy hungry before C4
  • CRIT can overcap

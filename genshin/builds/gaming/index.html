Gaming Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

Gaming build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Gest of the Mighty Wolf, A Thousand Blazing Suns), artifacts (Crimson Witch of Flames), main stats.

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Role: Main DPS

Gaming is a Pyro Plunges DPS who repeatedly launches Charmed Cloudstrider using his Skill & Burst. He excels in Melt & Vaporize teams, where his strong DMG & self-healing make him a powerful four-star carry. His Burst needs enough Energy to stay active each rotation.

Best weapons for Gaming

  1. Gest of the Mighty Wolf: A top premium choice that grants valuable DMG bonuses to his repeated Plunging Attacks.
  2. A Thousand Blazing Suns: High base ATK and CRIT help his DMG, while its combat bonuses suit an on-field Pyro carry.
  3. Beacon of the Reed Sea: Provides CRIT Rate and ATK for strong personal damage. Watch your CRIT cap at C6 or with Marechaussee Hunter. CRIT Rate can overcap.
  4. Redhorn Stonethresher: Its large CRIT DMG stat benefits Gaming, although its DEF-based Normal and Charged Attack passive does little for his plunges. Mostly a CRIT stat stick.
  5. Fruitful Hook: An excellent Plunging Attack weapon with valuable CR and DMG bonuses. Becomes exceptional at high refinements. Its buff may miss the opening plunge.
  6. Serpent Spine: Offers CR and stacking DMG bonuses. Shielding helps preserve its stacks during extended combos. Best when stacks remain active. Excellent at R5.
  7. Rainslasher: Excellent for Vaporize with its Elemental Mastery and bonus damage against Hydro-affected enemies. Vaporize teams only. Strong at R5.
  8. Blade of Atonement: A reliable permanent free option that supports Gaming’s damage without needing limited event weapons. Use at R5 when available. R5 preferred.

Best artifacts for Gaming

  • Crimson Witch of Flames (4 pcs): Best for Melt and Vaporize. Main stats: EM or ATK% / Pyro DMG / CRIT.
  • Marechaussee Hunter (4 pcs): His HP changes activate its 36% CRIT Rate bonus. Avoid exceeding the CRIT cap, especially at C6. Main stats: EM or ATK% / Pyro DMG / CRIT DMG.
  • Long Night's Oath (4 pcs): Boosts Plunging Attack damage. A strong alternative if you already have good pieces. Main stats: EM or ATK% / Pyro DMG / CRIT.

Main stats

  • Goblet: Pyro DMG Bonus
  • Circlet: CRIT Rate / CRIT DMG

Stat targets

  • ER: 100% - 180%
  • CRIT Rate: 45% - 75%
  • CRIT DMG: 140% - 200%

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 9+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 8–9

Best teams for Gaming

  • Wushou Royal Court: Gaming (On-field DPS), Citlali (Shielder / Buffer), Nicole (Shielder / Buffer), Xianyun (Healer / Plunge Buffer). Citlali enables powerful Melt plunges and lowers Pyro resistance. Nicole adds buffs and shielding, while Xianyun boosts plunge damage and heals.
  • Cloudborne Vaporize: Gaming (Main DPS), Furina (Hydro / DMG Buff), Xianyun (Healer / VV), Bennett (ATK Buff). Furina enables Vaporize and boosts damage. Xianyun buffs plunges and heals the team, while Bennett provides ATK and Pyro particles.
  • Frostfire Festival: Gaming (On-field DPS), Citlali (Shielder / Buffer), Bennett (Healer / Buffer), Sucrose (EM Buffer / Debuffer). Citlali supplies Cryo for Melt, Bennett buffs ATK, and Sucrose shares EM and reduces Pyro resistance with Viridescent Venerer.
  • Budget Lion Dance: Gaming (On-field DPS), Xingqiu (Off-field DPS / Hydro Enabler), Sucrose (EM Buffer / Debuffer), Bennett (Healer / Buffer). Use Normal Attacks between plunges to trigger Xingqiu’s Hydro swords. Sucrose supports Vaporize while Bennett buffs and heals.

Build variations

Melt Plunge (On-field DPS)

Use when Gaming can Melt a meaningful share of his plunge hits.

  • Weapons: Gest of the Mighty Wolf, Fruitful Hook, Serpent Spine
  • Artifacts: Crimson Witch of Flames (4 pcs); Long Night's Oath (4 pcs)
  • Stats: EM gains value with consistent Melt; keep CRIT and ATK balanced.
  • Teams: Reliable Cryo aura; healer and plunge support.
  • When it applies: Check aura stability and field-time requirements.

Furina Plunge (On-field DPS)

HP changes enable Marechaussee while supports strengthen plunges.

  • Weapons: Fruitful Hook, Serpent Spine
  • Artifacts: Marechaussee Hunter (4 pcs); Crimson Witch of Flames (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Build around Marechaussee CRIT Rate; avoid CR overcap.
  • Teams: Furina plus steady team healing, ideally Xianyun.
  • When it applies: Requires reliable HP fluctuation.

Budget Plunge DPS (On-field DPS)

Accessible weapon route centered on Gaming’s plunging attacks.

  • Weapons: Fruitful Hook, Serpent Spine
  • Artifacts: Crimson Witch of Flames (4 pcs); Long Night's Oath (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ATK% or EM Sands based on reaction uptime.
  • Teams: A healer and consistent Pyro reaction setup.
  • When it applies: Weapon refinements and reaction consistency matter.

Gaming talents

Normal Attack: Stellar Rend

Normal Attack
Performs up to 4 consecutive strikes.

Charged Attack
Drains Stamina over time to perform continuous spinning attacks against all nearby opponents.
At the end of the sequence, performs a more powerful slash.

Plunging Attack
Plunges from mid-air to strike the ground below, damaging opponents along the path and dealing AoE DMG upon impact.

Elemental Skill: Bestial Ascent

Cooldown: 6s

Pounces forward using the Wushou arts, leaping high into the air after coming into contact with a target or surface.
After Gaming has used Bestial Ascent to rise into the air, if he immediately uses a Plunging Attack, he will use the especially powerful Plunging Attack: Charmed Cloudstrider instead.

Plunging Attack: Charmed Cloudstrider
The DMG from Plunging Attacks caused by Bestial Ascent is converted to Pyro DMG that cannot be overridden by other elemental infusions. Upon landing, Gaming will consume a fixed amount of HP. Gaming's HP cannot be reduced below 10% by this method.
Charmed Cloudstrider DMG is considered Plunging Attack DMG.

Elemental Burst: Suanni's Gilded Dance

Cooldown: 15s · Energy cost: 60

Gaming enters Wushou Stance, briefly applying Pyro to him, recovering a fixed amount of HP, and summons his companion, the Suanni Man Chai, to smash into his target, dealing AoE Pyro DMG.
After bashing its target, Man Chai will roll to a nearby location before moving towards Gaming. When it links up with Gaming, Man Chai will leave the field and reset the CD for Gaming's Elemental Skill, Bestial Ascent.
While Wushou Stance is active, his resistance to interruption is increased, and when Gaming lands with Charmed Cloudstrider attack or completes the forward pounce attack from Bestial Ascent with over 50% HP, he will summon Man Chai again.
Each Gaming can only have 1 Man Chai on the field simultaneously.
This effect will be canceled once Gaming leaves the field.

Passive talent: Dance of Amity

After Bestial Ascent's Plunging Attack: Charmed Cloudstrider hits an opponent, Gaming will regain 1.5% of his Max HP once every 0.2s for 0.8s.

Passive talent: Air of Prosperity

When Gaming has less than 50% HP, he will receive a 20% Incoming Healing Bonus. When Gaming has 50% HP or more, Plunging Attack: Charmed Cloudstrider will deal 20% more DMG.

Passive talent: The Striding Beast

During the day (06:00 – 18:00), your party members gain the Swift Stride effect: Movement SPD increased by 10%.
Does not take effect in Domains, Trounce Domains, or Spiral Abyss. Swift Stride does not stack.

Gaming constellations

C1: Bringer of Blessing

When the Suanni Man Chai from Suanni's Gilded Dance meets back up with Gaming, it will heal 15% of Gaming's HP.

C2: Plum Blossoms Underfoot

When Gaming receives healing and this instance of healing overflows, his ATK will be increased by 20% for 5s.

C3: Awakening Spirit

Increases the Level of Bestial Ascent by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C4: Soar Across Mountains

When Bestial Ascent's Plunging Attack: Charmed Cloudstrider hits an opponent, it will restore 2 Energy to Gaming. This effect can be triggered once every 0.2s.

C5: Evil-Daunting Roar

Increases the Level of Suanni's Gilded Dance by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C6: To Tame All Beasts

Bestial Ascent's Plunging Attack: Charmed Cloudstrider CRIT Rate increased by 20% and CRIT DMG increased by 40%, and its attack radius will be increased.

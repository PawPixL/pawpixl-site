Fischl Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

Fischl build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Polar Star, Aqua Simulacra), artifacts (Golden Troupe), main stats, talent priority, teams.

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Role: On & Off-field

Fischl is an off-field Electro DPS who uses Oz for consistent damage and powerful reactions. She excels in reaction-focused teams and provides strong damage with minimal field time.

Best weapons for Fischl

  1. Polar Star: Provides CRIT Rate, ATK, and increased Oz damage.
  2. Aqua Simulacra: High CRIT DMG and a damage bonus when enemies are nearby, including while off-field.
  3. Skyward Harp: Boosts CRIT stats and Oz's damage.
  4. Thundering Pulse: Provides CRIT DMG and ATK; its Normal Attack stacking passive does not benefit Skill-focused damage.
  5. The Stringless: Boosts Oz's damage and reaction potential.
  6. Song of Stillness: Provides ATK and healing-triggered damage buffs.
  7. The Viridescent Hunt: CRIT Rate and a chance to group enemies with Normal or Charged Attacks; mostly a stat-stick alternative.
  8. Fading Twilight: Energy Recharge and a rotating damage bonus. A usable off-field option, though the buff level varies.

Best artifacts for Fischl

  • Golden Troupe (4 pcs) · Best Overall Set: Boosts Skill damage, massively increasing Oz's off-field damage. Main stats: ATK% · Electro · CR/CD.
  • Golden Troupe + Gladiator's Finale (2 + 2 pcs) · Placeholder Set: Combines Skill damage from Golden Troupe with ATK from Gladiator’s Finale. A flexible placeholder while farming a full Golden Troupe set. Main stats: ATK% · Electro · CR/CD.
  • Gilded Dreams (4 pcs) · Aggravate/Quickbloom: Provides Elemental Mastery and bonus ATK or EM after reactions. A usable Aggravate alternative, though Golden Troupe is usually stronger. Main stats: EM/ATK% · Electro · CR/CD.

Main stats

  • Sands: ATK% / EM
  • Goblet: Electro DMG
  • Circlet: Crit Rate / Crit DMG

Stat targets

  • ATK: 2,000+
  • Crit Rate: 60%+
  • Crit DMG: 140%+
  • Energy Recharge: 120% - 140%

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1
  • Elemental Skill: Level 9+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 9+

Best teams for Fischl

  • Outta Control: Fischl (Off-field DPS / Battery), Clorinde (On-field DPS), Mavuika (Off-field DPS / Pyro Enabler), Chevreuse (Healer / Buffer). Clorinde drives the damage while Fischl and Mavuika trigger Overload. Chevreuse buffs the team and shreds enemy resistance.
  • Boom & Hex: Fischl (Off-field DPS / Battery), Klee (On-field DPS), Emilie (Off-field DPS), Bennett (Healer / Buffer). Klee drives on-field damage, Fischl triggers Overload, Emilie boosts Burning damage, and Bennett provides healing and ATK buffs.
  • Electrolyte Overload: Fischl (Off-field DPS / Battery), Cyno (On-field DPS), Nahida (Dendro Enabler / Buffer), Baizhu (Healer / Shielder). Cyno drives Aggravate while Fischl adds off field Electro damage. Nahida supports Quicken, and Baizhu heals. Cyno’s long rotations can limit Oz uptime.
  • Raiden at Home: Fischl (Off-field DPS / Battery), Xingqiu (Off-field DPS / Hydro Enabler), Beidou (Off-field DPS), Sucrose (On-field Driver). Fischl and Beidou deal off-field Electro damage, Xingqiu enables Electro-Charged, and Sucrose drives reactions with Swirl.

Pros

  • Strong Off-Field Damage
  • Fast Electro Application
  • Strong Battery

Cons

  • Limited AoE
  • Oz Positioning
  • Skill Downtime
  • Burst Reliant Rotation

Build variations

Off-field Oz (Off-field DPS)

Keep Oz active off-field for continuous Skill damage.

  • Weapons: Aqua Simulacra, Fading Twilight
  • Artifacts: Golden Troupe (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ATK% / Electro / CRIT; ER as needed.
  • Teams: An on-field character who attacks frequently and benefits from Oz.

Aggravate (Off-field DPS)

Use when Fischl regularly contributes Aggravate damage.

  • Weapons: Aqua Simulacra, The Viridescent Hunt
  • Artifacts: Gilded Dreams (4 pcs)
  • Stats: EM gains value alongside ATK and CRIT.
  • Teams: Dendro partner for Quicken and Electro driver.

Fischl talents

Normal Attack: Bolts of Downfall

Normal Attack
Performs up to 5 consecutive shots with a bow.

Charged Attack
Performs a more precise Aimed Shot with increased DMG.
While aiming, the dark lightning spirits of Immernachtreich shall heed the call of their Prinzessin and indwell the enchanted arrowhead. When fully indwelt, the Rachsüchtig Blitz shall deal immense Electro DMG.

Plunging Attack
Fires off a shower of arrows in mid-air before falling and striking the ground, dealing AoE DMG upon impact.

Elemental Skill: Nightrider

Cooldown: 25s

Summons Oz. The night raven forged of darkness and lightning descends upon the land, dealing Electro DMG in a small AoE.
For the ability's duration, Oz will continuously attack nearby opponents with Freikugel.

Hold to adjust the location Oz will be summoned to.
Press again any time during the ability's duration to once again summon him to Fischl's side.

Elemental Burst: Midnight Phantasmagoria

Cooldown: 15s · Energy cost: 60

Summons Oz to spread his twin wings of twilight and defend Fischl.
Has the following properties during the ability's duration:
·Fischl takes on Oz's form, greatly increasing her Movement Speed.
·Strikes nearby opponents with lightning, dealing Electro DMG to opponents she comes into contact with. Each opponent can only be struck once.
·Once this ability's effects end, Oz will remain on the battlefield and attack his Prinzessin's foes. If Oz is already on the field, then this will reset the duration of his presence.

Passive talent: Stellar Predator

When Fischl hits Oz with a fully-charged Aimed Shot, Oz brings down Thundering Retribution, dealing AoE Electro DMG equal to 152.7% of the arrow's DMG.

Passive talent: Undone Be Thy Sinful Hex

If your current active character triggers an Electro-related Elemental Reaction when Oz is on the field, the opponent shall be stricken with Thundering Retribution that deals Electro DMG equal to 80% of Fischl's ATK.

Passive talent: Mein Hausgarten

When dispatched on an expedition in Mondstadt, time consumed is reduced by 25%.

Fischl constellations

C1: Gaze of the Deep

Even when Oz is not present in combat, he can still watch over Fischl through his raven eyes. When Fischl performs a Normal Attack against an opponent, Oz fires a coordinated attack, dealing DMG equal to 22% of Fischl's ATK.

C2: Devourer of All Sins

When Nightrider is used, it deals an additional 200% ATK as DMG, and its AoE is increased by 50%.

C3: Wings of Nightmare

Increases the Level of Nightrider by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C4: Her Pilgrimage of Bleak

When Midnight Phantasmagoria is used, it deals 222% of ATK as Electro DMG to surrounding opponents.
When the skill duration ends, Fischl regenerates 20% of her HP.

C5: Against the Fleeing Light

Increases the Level of Midnight Phantasmagoria by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C6: Evernight Raven

Extends the duration of Oz's presence on the field by 2s. Additionally, Oz performs coordinated attacks with your active character when present, dealing 30% of Fischl's ATK as Electro DMG.

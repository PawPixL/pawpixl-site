Fischl Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams (7.2)

Fischl build guide for Genshin Impact 7.2: best weapons (Polar Star, Aqua Simulacra), artifacts (Golden Troupe), main stats, talent priority, teams.

Last updated: November 12, 2026 · Game version 7.2

Role: On & Off-field

Fischl is an off-field Electro DPS who uses Oz for consistent damage and powerful reactions. She excels in reaction-focused teams and provides strong damage with minimal field time.

Best weapons for Fischl

  1. Polar Star: Provides CRIT Rate, ATK, and increased Oz damage.
  2. Aqua Simulacra: High CRIT DMG and a damage bonus when enemies are nearby, including while off-field.
  3. Skyward Harp: Boosts CRIT stats and Oz's damage.
  4. Thundering Pulse: Provides CRIT DMG and ATK; its Normal Attack stacking passive does not benefit Skill-focused damage.
  5. The Stringless: Boosts Oz's damage and reaction potential.
  6. Song of Stillness: Provides ATK and healing-triggered damage buffs.
  7. The Viridescent Hunt: CRIT Rate and a chance to group enemies with Normal or Charged Attacks; mostly a stat-stick alternative.
  8. Fading Twilight: Energy Recharge and a rotating damage bonus. A usable off-field option, though the buff level varies.

Best artifacts for Fischl

  • Golden Troupe (4 pcs) · Best Overall Set: Boosts Skill damage, massively increasing Oz's off-field damage. Main stats: ATK% · Electro · CR/CD.
  • Golden Troupe + Gladiator's Finale (2 + 2 pcs) · Placeholder Set: Combines Skill damage from Golden Troupe with ATK from Gladiator’s Finale. A flexible placeholder while farming a full Golden Troupe set. Main stats: ATK% · Electro · CR/CD.
  • Gilded Dreams (4 pcs) · Aggravate/Quickbloom: Provides Elemental Mastery and bonus ATK or EM after reactions. A usable Aggravate alternative, though Golden Troupe is usually stronger. Main stats: EM/ATK% · Electro · CR/CD.

Main stats

  • Sands: ATK% / EM
  • Goblet: Electro DMG
  • Circlet: Crit Rate / Crit DMG

Stat targets

  • ATK: 2,000+
  • Crit Rate: 60%+
  • Crit DMG: 140%+
  • Energy Recharge: 120% - 140%

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1
  • Elemental Skill: Level 9+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 9+

Best teams for Fischl

  • Outta Control: Fischl (Off-field DPS / Battery), Clorinde (On-field DPS), Mavuika (Off-field DPS / Pyro Enabler), Chevreuse (Healer / Buffer). Clorinde drives the damage while Fischl and Mavuika trigger Overload. Chevreuse buffs the team and shreds enemy resistance.
  • Boom & Hex: Fischl (Off-field DPS / Battery), Klee (On-field DPS), Emilie (Off-field DPS), Bennett (Healer / Buffer). Klee drives on-field damage, Fischl triggers Overload, Emilie boosts Burning damage, and Bennett provides healing and ATK buffs.
  • Electrolyte Overload: Fischl (Off-field DPS / Battery), Cyno (On-field DPS), Nahida (Dendro Enabler / Buffer), Baizhu (Healer / Shielder). Cyno drives Aggravate while Fischl adds off field Electro damage. Nahida supports Quicken, and Baizhu heals. Cyno’s long rotations can limit Oz uptime.
  • Raiden at Home: Fischl (Off-field DPS / Battery), Xingqiu (Off-field DPS / Hydro Enabler), Beidou (Off-field DPS), Sucrose (On-field Driver). Fischl and Beidou deal off-field Electro damage, Xingqiu enables Electro-Charged, and Sucrose drives reactions with Swirl.

Pros

  • Strong Off-Field Damage
  • Fast Electro Application
  • Strong Battery

Cons

  • Limited AoE
  • Oz Positioning
  • Skill Downtime
  • Burst Reliant Rotation

Build variations

Off-field Oz (Off-field DPS)

Keep Oz active off-field for continuous Skill damage.

  • Weapons: Aqua Simulacra, Fading Twilight
  • Artifacts: Golden Troupe (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ATK% / Electro / CRIT; ER as needed.
  • Teams: An on-field character who attacks frequently and benefits from Oz.

Aggravate (Off-field DPS)

Use when Fischl regularly contributes Aggravate damage.

  • Weapons: Aqua Simulacra, The Viridescent Hunt
  • Artifacts: Gilded Dreams (4 pcs)
  • Stats: EM gains value alongside ATK and CRIT.
  • Teams: Dendro partner for Quicken and Electro driver.

Dori Build Guide | Best Weapons, Artifacts & Teams

Dori build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Favonius Greatsword, Wolf's Gravestone), artifacts (Noblesse Oblige), main stats (Energy Recharge / HP%…

Role: Healer, Support

Dori heals and restores Energy to the active character through her 80-Energy Burst. Prioritize Energy Recharge, then HP and Healing Bonus. Her off-field Electro application is limited, but C6 enables an on-field reaction driver.

Best weapons for Dori

  1. Favonius Greatsword
  2. Wolf's Gravestone
  3. Sacrificial Greatsword
  4. Katsuragikiri Nagamasa
  5. Forest Regalia
  6. Makhaira Aquamarine
  7. Rainslasher
  8. Bloodtainted Greatsword

Best artifacts

  • Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs) · Team Buff
  • Deepwood Memories (4 pcs) · Dendro Utility
  • Instructor (4 pcs) · Reaction Buff

Main stats

  • Sands: Energy Recharge / HP%
  • Goblet: HP%
  • Circlet: Healing Bonus / HP%

Stat targets

  • ER solo: 210-260%
  • ER double: 160-180%
  • Fav solo: 180-220%
  • Fav duo: 140-160%
  • CR (Fav): 40-60%

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 1-6
  • Elemental Burst: Level 8-9+

Best teams

  • Claymore Superconduct: Dori, Eula, Rosaria, Shenhe
  • C6 Aggravate Bazaar: Dori, Fischl, Nahida, Sucrose
  • Swirling Jinni Bloom: Dori, Jean, Yelan, Nahida
  • Cyno Energy Caravan: Dori, Cyno, Nahida, Xingqiu

Pros

  • Healing and Energy recovery
  • Flexible support equipment
  • Useful Electro self-application
  • C6 enables reaction driving

Cons

  • High Burst Energy cost
  • Mostly heals active ally
  • Limited off-field Electro
  • Needs careful positioning

Build variations

  • High-HP Burst Healer (Healer): Simple general-purpose healer when damage is handled by teammates.
  • Dendro Resistance Shred (Support): Provides Dendro RES reduction without forcing your Dendro carry to equip Deepwood.
  • C6 Aggravate Driver (On-Field DPS): C6 grants short Electro infusion after Skill, allowing on-field Aggravates and partywide healing from Normal Attacks.
  • Jean Hyperbloom Engine (Reaction Support): Electro self-application from Dori plus Jean’s Burst can Swirl Electro to trigger nearby Dendro cores.
  • Instructor Budget Support (Support): Accessible team Elemental Mastery support when a stronger 5-star artifact set is unavailable.

Dori Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

Dori build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Favonius Greatsword, Wolf's Gravestone), artifacts (Noblesse Oblige), main stats, talent priority.

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Role: Healer, Support

Dori heals and restores Energy to the active character through her 80-Energy Burst. Prioritize Energy Recharge, then HP and Healing Bonus. Her off-field Electro application is limited, but C6 enables an on-field reaction driver.

Best weapons for Dori

  1. Favonius Greatsword: CRIT hits make team Energy particles. Build CRIT Rate. Higher refinements help.
  2. Wolf's Gravestone: High ATK; situational team ATK buff. If Burst uptime is already secure.
  3. Sacrificial Greatsword: Extra Skill gives Energy and particles. Reset must activate. Higher refinements preferred.
  4. Katsuragikiri Nagamasa: Craftable Energy refund from Skill hits. Mind its delayed Energy restoration. Higher refinements help.
  5. Forest Regalia: ER plus a Dendro-reaction EM leaf buff. Use in Dendro teams; collect the leaf.
  6. Makhaira Aquamarine: EM scaling adds an ATK buff for allies. Best on high-EM Hyperbloom variants.
  7. Rainslasher: EM boosts reactions in driver builds. Needs enough ER from other sources.
  8. Bloodtainted Greatsword: Budget EM for reaction-focused builds. Only with sufficient Energy Recharge.

Best artifacts for Dori

  • Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs) · Team Buff: Burst grants teamwide ATK. Main stats: ER/HP% · HP% · Healing Bonus.
  • Deepwood Memories (4 pcs) · Dendro Utility: Reduces enemy Dendro RES. Main stats: Only if nobody else uses Deepwood.
  • Instructor (4 pcs) · Reaction Buff: Reactions grant the team EM. Main stats: 4-star set; prioritize ER sands.

Main stats

  • Sands: Energy Recharge / HP%
  • Goblet: HP%
  • Circlet: Healing Bonus / HP%

Stat targets

  • ER solo: 210-260%
  • ER double: 160-180%
  • Fav solo: 180-220%
  • Fav duo: 140-160%
  • CR (Fav): 40-60%

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 1-6
  • Elemental Burst: Level 8-9+

Best teams for Dori

  • Claymore Superconduct: Dori (Healer / Battery), Eula (Main DPS), Rosaria (Cryo Battery), Shenhe (Cryo Buffer). Dori supplies healing, Energy and Electro for Superconduct; Rosaria and Shenhe support Eula’s Physical damage.
  • C6 Aggravate Bazaar: Dori (On-Field Driver), Fischl (Off-Field DPS), Nahida (Dendro Support), Sucrose (Anemo Support). At C6, Dori drives Aggravate with Electro-infused attacks. Fischl adds damage; Nahida and Sucrose enable and amplify reactions.
  • Swirling Jinni Bloom: Dori (Healer / Electro), Jean (Anemo Driver), Yelan (Hydro DPS), Nahida (Dendro Support). Jean can Swirl Electro from Dori’s self-application to trigger Hyperbloom. Keep both Bursts active and position close to cores.
  • Cyno Energy Caravan: Dori (Healer / Battery), Cyno (On-Field DPS), Nahida (Dendro Support), Xingqiu (Hydro DPS). Dori sustains Cyno during field time and helps with Energy; Nahida and Xingqiu enable Quickbloom reactions.

Pros

  • Healing and Energy recovery
  • Flexible support equipment
  • Useful Electro self-application
  • C6 enables reaction driving

Cons

  • High Burst Energy cost
  • Mostly heals active ally
  • Limited off-field Electro
  • Needs careful positioning

Build variations

High-HP Burst Healer (Healer)

Simple general-purpose healer when damage is handled by teammates.

  • Weapons: Favonius Greatsword, Sacrificial Greatsword, Katsuragikiri Nagamasa
  • Artifacts: Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs); Instructor (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ER/HP% sands, HP% goblet, Healing Bonus circlet. Prioritize ER then HP%.
  • Teams: Teams needing reliable active-character healing and Energy.
  • When it applies: Burst uptime comes first; prioritize CRIT Rate with Favonius.
  • Notes: Only the linked active character gets standard Burst healing.

Dendro Resistance Shred (Support)

Provides Dendro RES reduction without forcing your Dendro carry to equip Deepwood.

  • Weapons: Favonius Greatsword, Forest Regalia
  • Artifacts: Deepwood Memories (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Maintain Burst uptime; use ER/HP%/Healing Bonus.
  • Teams: A Dendro damage dealer and no existing 4pc Deepwood user.
  • When it applies: Needs consistent Dori Skill or Burst contact with enemies.
  • Notes: Burst tether can hit targets, but positioning matters.

C6 Aggravate Driver (On-Field DPS)

C6 grants short Electro infusion after Skill, allowing on-field Aggravates and partywide healing from Normal Attacks.

  • Weapons: Sacrificial Greatsword, Rainslasher, Favonius Greatsword
  • Artifacts: Thundering Fury (4 pcs); Gilded Dreams (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ER to needs; EM or ER sands, Electro goblet, CRIT circlet.
  • Teams: C6 Dori, Dendro enabler, and preferably Fischl.
  • When it applies: C6 required; refresh Skill frequently.
  • Notes: Thundering Fury helps extend infusion uptime via shorter Skill cooldowns.

Jean Hyperbloom Engine (Reaction Support)

Electro self-application from Dori plus Jean’s Burst can Swirl Electro to trigger nearby Dendro cores.

  • Weapons: Makhaira Aquamarine, Rainslasher, Favonius Greatsword
  • Artifacts: Gilded Dreams (4 pcs); Flower of Paradise Lost (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ER first, then EM/EM/EM if Dori triggers reactions.
  • Teams: Jean, off-field Hydro, and a Dendro applier.
  • When it applies: Requires careful Burst overlap and positioning.
  • Notes: Reaction ownership may vary; the actual Hyperbloom trigger should build EM.

Instructor Budget Support (Support)

Accessible team Elemental Mastery support when a stronger 5-star artifact set is unavailable.

  • Weapons: Favonius Greatsword, Forest Regalia, Katsuragikiri Nagamasa
  • Artifacts: Instructor (4 pcs); Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Use a 5-star ER sands with 4pc Instructor; build HP and useful CRIT.
  • Teams: Reaction-based team that can reliably trigger Instructor.
  • When it applies: 4-star pieces reduce total stats and healing.
  • Notes: Instructor requires a reaction triggered while Dori is on field.

Dori talents

Normal Attack: Marvelous Sword-Dance (Modified)

Normal Attack
Performs up to 3 consecutive strikes.

Charged Attack
Drains Stamina over time to perform continuous spinning attacks against all nearby opponents.
At the end of the sequence, performs a more powerful slash.

Plunging Attack
Plunges from mid-air to strike the ground below, damaging opponents along the path and dealing AoE DMG upon impact.

Elemental Skill: Spirit-Warding Lamp: Troubleshooter Cannon

Cooldown: 9s

Directs a Spirit-Warding Lamp to fire off a Troubleshooter Shot at opponents, dealing Electro DMG.
After the Troubleshooter Shot hits, it will create 2 After-Sales Service Rounds that will automatically track nearby opponents and deal Electro DMG.

Elemental Burst: Alcazarzaray's Exactitude

Cooldown: 20s · Energy cost: 80

Summons forth the Jinni within the lamp to give the client various kinds of aid.

Jinni
Connects to a nearby character. The connected character will:
· Continuously restore HP based on Dori's Max HP.
· Continuously regenerate Energy.
· Be affected by Electro.
When the connector between the Jinni and the character touches opponents, it will deal one instance of Electro DMG to them every 0.4s.

Only one Jinni can exist at one time.

Passive talent: An Eye for Gold

After a character connected to the Jinni triggers an Electro-Charged, Lunar-Charged, Superconduct, Stellar-Conduct, Overloaded, Quicken, Aggravate, Hyperbloom, Electro Swirl, or Electro Crystallize reaction, the CD of Spirit-Warding Lamp: Troubleshooter Cannon is decreased by 1s.
This effect can be triggered once every 3s.

Passive talent: Compound Interest

When the Troubleshooter Shots or After-Sales Service Rounds from Spirit-Warding Lamp: Troubleshooter Cannon hit opponents, Dori will restore 5 Elemental Energy for every 100% Energy Recharge possessed.
Per Spirit-Warding Lamp: Troubleshooter Cannon, only one instance of Energy restoration can be triggered and a maximum of 15 Energy can be restored this way.

Passive talent: Unexpected Order

Has a 25% chance to recover some of the materials used when crafting Character and Weapon Materials.

Dori constellations

C1: Additional Investment

The number of After-Sales Service Rounds created by Troubleshooter Shots is increased by 1.

C2: Special Franchise

When you are in combat and the Jinni heals the character it is connected to, it will fire a Jinni Toop from that character's position that deals 50% of Dori's ATK DMG.

C3: Wonders Never Cease

Increases the Level of Alcazarzaray's Exactitude by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C4: Discretionary Supplement

The character connected to the Jinni will obtain the following buffs based on their current HP and Energy:
·When their HP is lower than 50%, they gain 50% Incoming Healing Bonus.
·When their Energy is less than 50%, they gain 30% Energy Recharge.

C5: Value for Mora

Increases the Level of Spirit-Warding Lamp: Troubleshooter Cannon by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C6: Sprinkling Weight

Dori gains the following effects for 3s after using Spirit-Warding Lamp: Troubleshooter Cannon:
·Electro Infusion.
·When Normal Attacks hit opponents, all nearby party members will heal HP equivalent to 4% of Dori's Max HP. This type of healing can occur once every 0.1s.

Alhaitham Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

Alhaitham build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Light of Foliar Incision, Uraku Misugiri), artifacts (Gilded Dreams), main stats, talent priority.

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Role: Main DPS

Alhaitham is an on-field Dendro DPS who manages Chisel-Light Mirrors to deal strong Spread and Quickbloom damage. Skill timing and team energy support matter.

Best weapons for Alhaitham

  1. Light of Foliar Incision: Provides CRIT DMG and increases Normal Attack and Skill damage based on Elemental Mastery.
  2. Uraku Misugiri: Provides CRIT DMG and boosts Normal Attack and Elemental Skill. Especially strong with a Geo teammate.
  3. Primordial Jade Cutter: Excellent CRIT Rate and a consistent ATK bonus; especially competitive in multi-target fights.
  4. Haran Geppaku Futsu: CRIT Rate and Normal Attack bonuses; best when Alhaitham uses more Normal Attacks in his combos.
  5. Freedom-Sworn: EM and a situational team buff. Its support passive can be awkward to activate on Alhaitham. Only when passive activation is reliable.
  6. Toukabou Shigure: Event EM sword with strong single-target damage against a marked enemy. Primarily single-target.
  7. Wolf-Fang: CRIT Rate and Skill damage strengthen his Projection Attacks. Especially valuable at high refinements. Higher refinements help.
  8. Summit Shaper: ATK-based stat stick; its passive is more useful when protected by a shield. Prefer a shield.

Best artifacts for Alhaitham

  • Gilded Dreams (4 pcs) · Best OVERALL: Boosts Elemental Mastery and ATK, strengthening his Spread damage. Main stats: EM · Dendro DMG · CR/CD.
  • Deepwood Memories (4 pcs): Reduces enemy Dendro RES, boosting Alhaitham's personal and team damage. Main stats: EM · Dendro DMG · CR/CD.
  • Gilded Dreams + Wanderer's Troupe (2 + 2 pcs): Combines 160 Elemental Mastery, boosting Spread damage and overall output. Main stats: EM · Dendro DMG · CR/CD.

Main stats

  • Sands: EM
  • Goblet: Dendro DMG
  • Circlet: CR / CD

Stat targets

  • EM: 200–500
  • CR: 70–80%
  • CD: 140–180%+
  • ER: Team-based

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 8–9+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 9+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 8–9+

Best teams for Alhaitham

  • Spread Kings: Alhaitham, Nahida, Yae Miko, Zhongli. Alhaitham drives Spread damage while Nahida buffs EM, Yae adds Electro, and Zhongli shreds resistance.
  • Lunar Bloom: Alhaitham, Lauma, Furina, Kuki Shinobu. Alhaitham drives Dendro damage, Lauma buffs Bloom, Furina boosts damage, and Kuki triggers Hyperbloom.
  • Spread Dread: Alhaitham, Fischl, Yae Miko, Baizhu. Alhaitham drives Spread, Fischl and Yae provide Electro, Baizhu heals and buffs.
  • Budget Dendro: Alhaitham, Xingqiu, Fischl, Kuki Shinobu. Alhaitham drives damage, Xingqiu enables Bloom, Fischl adds Electro, and Kuki triggers Hyperbloom.

Pros

  • Strong Dendro
  • Flexible Teams
  • F2P Friendly

Cons

  • Complex Rotations
  • Energy Hungry

  • Limited Interrupt Resistance

Build variations

Spread / Quicken (On-field DPS)

Great for sustained Dendro damage with Spread reactions and strong three-Mirror uptime.

  • Weapons: Light of Foliar Incision, Primordial Jade Cutter, Wolf-Fang
  • Artifacts: Gilded Dreams (4 pcs)
  • Stats: EM Sands; Dendro DMG Goblet; CRIT Circlet. Reach ER needs, then balance EM and CRIT.
  • Teams: Consistent Electro; ideally a teammate using 4pc Deepwood Memories.
  • When it applies: If no one else holds Deepwood, Alhaitham may need to use it himself.

Quickbloom (On-field DPS)

Combines Alhaitham’s Dendro damage with Bloom cores converted into Hyperbloom damage.

  • Weapons: Light of Foliar Incision, Wolf-Fang, Toukabou Shigure
  • Artifacts: Gilded Dreams (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Keep EM/Dendro DMG/CRIT; prioritize ER for reliable rotations over excessive EM.
  • Teams: Hydro applier plus an Electro unit that reliably triggers Hyperbloom.
  • When it applies: A teammate should hold 4pc Deepwood Memories.

Furina CRIT Build (On-field DPS)

A conditional artifact alternative when Furina enables consistent HP changes.

  • Weapons: Light of Foliar Incision, Primordial Jade Cutter, Wolf-Fang
  • Artifacts: Marechaussee Hunter (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Build around its conditional CRIT Rate; avoid overcapping CRIT while balancing EM and ER.
  • Teams: Furina plus sufficient teamwide healing or reliable HP fluctuation.
  • When it applies: Requires reliable Marechaussee Hunter stacks; not for every team.

Alhaitham build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Light of Foliar Incision, Uraku Misugiri), artifacts (Gilded Dreams), main stats (EM / Dendro DMG…

Role: Main DPS

Alhaitham is an on-field Dendro DPS who manages Chisel-Light Mirrors to deal strong Spread and Quickbloom damage. Skill timing and team energy support matter.

Best weapons for Alhaitham

  1. Light of Foliar Incision
  2. Uraku Misugiri
  3. Primordial Jade Cutter
  4. Haran Geppaku Futsu
  5. Freedom-Sworn
  6. Toukabou Shigure
  7. Wolf-Fang
  8. Summit Shaper

Best artifacts

  • Gilded Dreams (4 pcs) · Best OVERALL
  • Deepwood Memories (4 pcs)
  • Gilded Dreams + Wanderer's Troupe (2 + 2 pcs)

Main stats

  • Sands: EM
  • Goblet: Dendro DMG
  • Circlet: CR / CD

Stat targets

  • EM: 200–500
  • CR: 70–80%
  • CD: 140–180%+
  • ER: Team-based

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 8–9+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 9+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 8–9+

Best teams

  • Spread Kings: Alhaitham, Nahida, Yae Miko, Zhongli
  • Lunar Bloom: Alhaitham, Lauma, Furina, Kuki Shinobu
  • Spread Dread: Alhaitham, Fischl, Yae Miko, Baizhu
  • Budget Dendro: Alhaitham, Xingqiu, Fischl, Kuki Shinobu

Pros

  • Flexible Teams

Cons

Build variations

  • Spread / Quicken (On-field DPS): Great for sustained Dendro damage with Spread reactions and strong three-Mirror uptime.
  • Quickbloom (On-field DPS): Combines Alhaitham’s Dendro damage with Bloom cores converted into Hyperbloom damage.
  • Furina CRIT Build (On-field DPS): A conditional artifact alternative when Furina enables consistent HP changes.

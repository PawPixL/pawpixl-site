Alhaitham Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

Alhaitham build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Light of Foliar Incision, Uraku Misugiri), artifacts (Gilded Dreams), main stats, talent priority.

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Role: Main DPS

Alhaitham is an on-field Dendro DPS who manages Chisel-Light Mirrors to deal strong Spread and Quickbloom damage. Skill timing and team energy support matter.

Best weapons for Alhaitham

  1. Light of Foliar Incision: Provides CRIT DMG and increases Normal Attack and Skill damage based on Elemental Mastery.
  2. Uraku Misugiri: Provides CRIT DMG and boosts Normal Attack and Elemental Skill. Especially strong with a Geo teammate.
  3. Primordial Jade Cutter: Excellent CRIT Rate and a consistent ATK bonus; especially competitive in multi-target fights.
  4. Haran Geppaku Futsu: CRIT Rate and Normal Attack bonuses; best when Alhaitham uses more Normal Attacks in his combos.
  5. Freedom-Sworn: EM and a situational team buff. Its support passive can be awkward to activate on Alhaitham. Only when passive activation is reliable.
  6. Toukabou Shigure: Event EM sword with strong single-target damage against a marked enemy. Primarily single-target.
  7. Wolf-Fang: CRIT Rate and Skill damage strengthen his Projection Attacks. Especially valuable at high refinements. Higher refinements help.
  8. Summit Shaper: ATK-based stat stick; its passive is more useful when protected by a shield. Prefer a shield.

Best artifacts for Alhaitham

  • Gilded Dreams (4 pcs) · Best OVERALL: Boosts Elemental Mastery and ATK, strengthening his Spread damage. Main stats: EM · Dendro DMG · CR/CD.
  • Deepwood Memories (4 pcs): Reduces enemy Dendro RES, boosting Alhaitham's personal and team damage. Main stats: EM · Dendro DMG · CR/CD.
  • Gilded Dreams + Wanderer's Troupe (2 + 2 pcs): Combines 160 Elemental Mastery, boosting Spread damage and overall output. Main stats: EM · Dendro DMG · CR/CD.

Main stats

  • Sands: EM
  • Goblet: Dendro DMG
  • Circlet: CR / CD

Stat targets

  • EM: 200–500
  • CR: 70–80%
  • CD: 140–180%+
  • ER: Team-based

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 8–9+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 9+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 8–9+

Best teams for Alhaitham

  • Spread Kings: Alhaitham, Nahida, Yae Miko, Zhongli. Alhaitham drives Spread damage while Nahida buffs EM, Yae adds Electro, and Zhongli shreds resistance.
  • Lunar Bloom: Alhaitham, Lauma, Furina, Kuki Shinobu. Alhaitham drives Dendro damage, Lauma buffs Bloom, Furina boosts damage, and Kuki triggers Hyperbloom.
  • Spread Dread: Alhaitham, Fischl, Yae Miko, Baizhu. Alhaitham drives Spread, Fischl and Yae provide Electro, Baizhu heals and buffs.
  • Budget Dendro: Alhaitham, Xingqiu, Fischl, Kuki Shinobu. Alhaitham drives damage, Xingqiu enables Bloom, Fischl adds Electro, and Kuki triggers Hyperbloom.

Build variations

Spread / Quicken (On-field DPS)

Great for sustained Dendro damage with Spread reactions and strong three-Mirror uptime.

  • Weapons: Light of Foliar Incision, Primordial Jade Cutter, Wolf-Fang
  • Artifacts: Gilded Dreams (4 pcs)
  • Stats: EM Sands; Dendro DMG Goblet; CRIT Circlet. Reach ER needs, then balance EM and CRIT.
  • Teams: Consistent Electro; ideally a teammate using 4pc Deepwood Memories.
  • When it applies: If no one else holds Deepwood, Alhaitham may need to use it himself.

Quickbloom (On-field DPS)

Combines Alhaitham’s Dendro damage with Bloom cores converted into Hyperbloom damage.

  • Weapons: Light of Foliar Incision, Wolf-Fang, Toukabou Shigure
  • Artifacts: Gilded Dreams (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Keep EM/Dendro DMG/CRIT; prioritize ER for reliable rotations over excessive EM.
  • Teams: Hydro applier plus an Electro unit that reliably triggers Hyperbloom.
  • When it applies: A teammate should hold 4pc Deepwood Memories.

Furina CRIT Build (On-field DPS)

A conditional artifact alternative when Furina enables consistent HP changes.

  • Weapons: Light of Foliar Incision, Primordial Jade Cutter, Wolf-Fang
  • Artifacts: Marechaussee Hunter (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Build around its conditional CRIT Rate; avoid overcapping CRIT while balancing EM and ER.
  • Teams: Furina plus sufficient teamwide healing or reliable HP fluctuation.
  • When it applies: Requires reliable Marechaussee Hunter stacks; not for every team.

Alhaitham talents

Normal Attack: Abductive Reasoning

Normal Attack
Performs up to 5 rapid strikes.

Charged Attack
Consumes a certain amount of Stamina to unleash 2 rapid sword strikes.

Plunging Attack
Plunges from mid-air to strike the ground below, damaging opponents along the path and dealing AoE DMG upon impact.

Elemental Skill: Universality: An Elaboration on Form

Cooldown: 18s

Rushes forward, dealing Dendro DMG to nearby opponents when the rush ends, causing a Chisel-Light Mirror to form.
Holding this skill will cause it to behave differently.

Hold
Enters Aiming Mode to adjust the direction of Alhaitham's rush attack.

Chisel-Light Mirror
When this skill is unleashed, Alhaitham will generate 1 Chisel-Light Mirror. If there are no Mirrors at this time, he will generate 1 additional Mirror. Chisel-Light Mirrors will have the following properties:
·When he possesses Chisel-Light Mirrors, Alhaitham's Normal, Charged, and Plunging Attacks will be converted to Dendro DMG. This cannot be overridden.
·When attacks of the aforementioned kind hit opponents, the Chisel-Light Mirrors will unleash a Projection Attack that deals AoE Dendro DMG based on the number of Mirrors on the field.
·A total of 3 Chisel-Light Mirrors can exist at once.
·The Chisel-Light Mirrors will disappear one after the other over time, and will all disappear when Alhaitham leaves the field.

Elemental Burst: Particular Field: Fetters of Phenomena

Cooldown: 18s · Energy cost: 70

Creates a Particular Binding Field and deals multiple instances of AoE Dendro DMG.
If Chisel-Light Mirrors exist when this ability is unleashed, all such Mirrors will be consumed and increase the number of DMG instances dealt.
2s after this ability is unleashed, if 0/1/2/3 Mirrors were consumed, Alhaitham will generate 3/2/1/0 new Mirrors in turn.

Passive talent: Four-Causal Correction

When Alhaitham's Charged or Plunging Attacks hit opponents, they will generate 1 Chisel-Light Mirror. This effect can be triggered once every 12s.

Passive talent: Mysteries Laid Bare

Each point of Alhaitham's Elemental Mastery will increase the DMG dealt by Projection Attacks and Particular Field: Fetters of Phenomena by 0.1%.
The DMG of both aforementioned abilities can be increased by a maximum of 100% this way.

Passive talent: Law of Reductive Overdetermination

When Alhaitham crafts Weapon Ascension Materials, he has a 10% chance to receive double the product.

Alhaitham constellations

C1: Intuition

When a Projection Attack hits an opponent, Universality: An Elaboration on Form's CD is decreased by 1.2s. This effect can be triggered once every 1s.

C2: Debate

When Alhaitham generates a Chisel-Light Mirror, his Elemental Mastery will be increased by 50 for 8 seconds, max 4 stacks. Each stack's duration is counted independently. This effect can be triggered even when the maximum number of Chisel-Light Mirrors has been reached.

C3: Negation

Increases the Level of Universality: An Elaboration on Form by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C4: Elucidation

When Particular Field: Fetters of Phenomena is unleashed, the following effects will become active based on the number of Chisel-Light Mirrors consumed and created this time around:
·Each Mirror consumed will increase the Elemental Mastery of all other nearby party members by 30 for 15s.
·Each Mirror generated will grant Alhaitham a 10% Dendro DMG Bonus for 15s.

The pre-existing duration of the aforementioned effects will be cleared if you use Particular Field: Fetters of Phenomena again while they are in effect.

C5: Sagacity

Increases the Level of Particular Field: Fetters of Phenomena by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C6: Structuration

Alhaitham gains the following effects:
· 2 seconds after Particular Field: Fetters of Phenomena is unleashed, he will generate 3 Chisel-Light Mirrors regardless of the number of mirrors consumed.
· If Alhaitham generates Chisel-Light Mirrors when their numbers have already maxed out, his CRIT Rate and CRIT DMG will increase by 10% and 70% respectively for 6s. If this effect is triggered again during its initial duration, the duration remaining will be increased by 6s.

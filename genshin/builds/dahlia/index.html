Dahlia Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams (7.2)

Dahlia build guide for Genshin Impact 7.2: best weapons (Favonius Sword, Key of Khaj-Nisut), artifacts (Noblesse Oblige), main stats, talent priority.

Last updated: November 12, 2026 · Game version 7.2

Role: Shield Support / Buffer

Dahlia shields the active character with his Burst and boosts their Normal Attack Speed based on his HP, up to 20% at 40,000 HP (30% at C6). His shield can refresh with Benison stacks, but he needs substantial Energy Recharge to Burst reliably. Best with Normal Attack-focused carries.

Best weapons for Dahlia

  1. Favonius Sword: ER and neutral particles help fund Dahlia’s costly Burst and recharge teammates. Build CRIT Rate to trigger its passive. Requires on-field CRIT hits to generate particles.
  2. Key of Khaj-Nisut: Massive HP boosts his shield and helps reach the 40,000 HP cap for his A4 ATK SPD buff. Only after meeting his Burst ER requirement.
  3. Freedom-Sworn: Can buff team ATK and Normal, Charged.
  4. Skyward Blade: 55.1% ER helps maintain Burst uptime.
  5. Peak Patrol Song: Provides team Elemental DMG buffs when its conditions are met; competes with Dahlia’s preferred HP and ER stats. Only when team buffs outweigh lower HP and tougher ER needs.
  6. Sacrificial Sword: Energy Recharge and a chance for another Skill improve particles and reduce Burst pressure. Passive needs to trigger; extra Skill costs field time.
  7. The Dockhand's Assistant: HP% strengthens the shield and increases his HP-scaling Attack Speed buff. Meet ER needs first; weapon passive is secondary.
  8. Skyrider Sword: Accessible Energy Recharge stat stick until better swords are available. Temporary F2P option; passive offers little support value.

Best artifacts for Dahlia

  • Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs): Reliable team ATK buff when Dahlia uses Burst every rotation. Avoid duplicating with another Noblesse holder. Main stats: ER/HP% · HP% · HP%/CRIT Rate.
  • Scroll of the Hero of Cinder City (4 pcs): Provides a modest reaction-based Elemental DMG buff without Nightsoul; useful for non-ATK scalers or if Noblesse is taken. Main stats: ER/HP% · HP% · HP%/CRIT Rate.
  • Tenacity of the Millelith + Vourukasha's Glow (2 + 2 pcs): Two HP% bonuses help reach 40,000 HP when Burst uptime is already covered; lacks a team-wide set buff. Main stats: ER/HP% · HP% · HP%/CRIT Rate.

Main stats

  • Sands: Energy Recharge / HP%
  • Goblet: HP%
  • Circlet: HP% / CRIT Rate (Favonius)

Stat targets

  • HP: up to 40,000 for full A4 buff
  • ER solo Hydro C0: 240% - 270% (1 Skill)
  • ER double Hydro C0: 170% - 230%
  • ER double Hydro C1: 140% - 165% (1 Skill)
  • CRIT Rate (Favonius): 40% - 60%+

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 1+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 8-9+

Best teams for Dahlia

  • Frostbound Encore: Dahlia (Shield Support), Skirk (Main DPS), Furina (Sub DPS), Escoffier (Healer). Dahlia shields and speeds up Skirk’s Normal Attacks; Furina buffs DMG and Escoffier heals.
  • Windborne Chorus: Dahlia (Shield Support), Wanderer (Main DPS), Faruzan (Anemo Buffer), Bennett (ATK Buffer). Faruzan buffs Wanderer, Bennett supplies ATK and healing.
  • Suprise Baptism: Dahlia (Shield Support), Kamisato Ayato (Main DPS), Furina (Sub DPS), Xilonen (Support). Ayato uses Dahlia’s shield and Attack Speed.
  • Summer Serenade: Dahlia (Shield Support), Yoimiya (Main DPS), Yelan (Sub DPS), Bennett (ATK Buffer). Yoimiya appreciates Dahlia’s shield and Normal Attack Speed.

Pros

  • Up to 30% ATK SPD at C6
  • Refreshable Burst shield
  • Favonius energy utility
  • Supports Normal Attack carries

Cons

  • Very high ER at C0
  • Limited off-field Hydro
  • Shield can break early
  • Little personal damage

Build variations

HP Shield Focus (Shield Support)

When more shield strength and maximum A4 Attack Speed matter more than the 4pc Noblesse team ATK buff.

  • Weapons: Key of Khaj-Nisut, The Dockhand's Assistant, Favonius Sword
  • Artifacts: Tenacity of the Millelith + Vourukasha's Glow (2 + 2 pcs)
  • Stats: Prioritize HP% to approach 40,000 HP after meeting rotation ER. HP% circlet; ER Sands if necessary.
  • Teams: Best when party weapons/particles already cover Dahlia’s high ER needs.
  • When it applies: Burst must remain available every rotation.
  • Notes: Dahlia’s A4 caps at 20% Attack Speed from 40,000 HP.

Elemental Damage Support (Support)

A useful alternative for non-ATK-scaling carries or when 4pc Noblesse would duplicate another teammate.

  • Weapons: Favonius Sword, Skyward Blade, Freedom-Sworn
  • Artifacts: Scroll of the Hero of Cinder City (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Same ER and HP priorities; use CRIT Rate circlet for Favonius as needed.
  • Teams: Teams benefiting from Elemental DMG rather than ATK, or where another teammate carries Noblesse.
  • When it applies: Dahlia must trigger an appropriate elemental reaction.
  • Notes: Without Nightsoul, Dahlia receives only Scroll’s smaller reaction-based bonus, not the full enhanced buff.

C1 Double-Hydro Battery (Shield Support)

At C1+, use energy refunds to invest additional substats into HP and improve the ATK SPD buff.

  • Weapons: Favonius Sword, Sacrificial Sword, Skyward Blade
  • Artifacts: Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ER can drop to roughly 140–165% with C1, double Hydro and one Skill, depending on actual Benison stacks.
  • Teams: Two Hydro characters; sufficient Benison generation; reliable rotations.
  • When it applies: C1 and consistent Benison stacks.
  • Notes: Two Skills or Favonius procs can lower ER further; verify the team’s actual particle generation.

Role: Shield Support / Buffer

Dahlia shields the active character with his Burst and boosts their Normal Attack Speed based on his HP, up to 20% at 40,000 HP (30% at C6). His shield can refresh with Benison stacks, but he needs substantial Energy Recharge to Burst reliably. Best with Normal Attack-focused carries.

Best weapons for Dahlia

  1. Favonius Sword
  2. Key of Khaj-Nisut
  3. Freedom-Sworn
  4. Skyward Blade
  5. Peak Patrol Song
  6. Sacrificial Sword
  7. The Dockhand's Assistant
  8. Skyrider Sword

Best artifacts

  • Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs)
  • Scroll of the Hero of Cinder City (4 pcs)
  • Tenacity of the Millelith + Vourukasha's Glow (2 + 2 pcs)

Main stats

  • Sands: Energy Recharge / HP%
  • Goblet: HP%
  • Circlet: HP% / CRIT Rate (Favonius)

Stat targets

  • HP: up to 40,000 for full A4 buff
  • ER solo Hydro C0: 240% - 270% (1 Skill)
  • ER double Hydro C0: 170% - 230%
  • ER double Hydro C1: 140% - 165% (1 Skill)
  • CRIT Rate (Favonius): 40% - 60%+

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 1+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 8-9+

Best teams

  • Frostbound Encore: Dahlia, Skirk, Furina, Escoffier
  • Windborne Chorus: Dahlia, Wanderer, Faruzan, Bennett
  • Suprise Baptism: Dahlia, Kamisato Ayato, Furina, Xilonen
  • Summer Serenade: Dahlia, Yoimiya, Yelan, Bennett

Pros

  • Up to 30% ATK SPD at C6
  • Refreshable Burst shield
  • Favonius energy utility
  • Supports Normal Attack carries

Cons

  • Very high ER at C0
  • Limited off-field Hydro
  • Shield can break early
  • Little personal damage

Build variations

  • HP Shield Focus (Shield Support): When more shield strength and maximum A4 Attack Speed matter more than the 4pc Noblesse team ATK buff.
  • Elemental Damage Support (Support): A useful alternative for non-ATK-scaling carries or when 4pc Noblesse would duplicate another teammate.
  • C1 Double-Hydro Battery (Shield Support): At C1+, use energy refunds to invest additional substats into HP and improve the ATK SPD buff.

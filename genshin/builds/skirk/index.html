Skirk Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

Skirk build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Azurelight, Haran Geppaku Futsu), artifacts (Finale of the Deep Galleries), main stats.

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Role: Main DPS

Skirk is a powerful Cryo DPS who excels with Cryo and Hydro allies. Her unique resource system lets her unleash devastating attacks without relying on Energy.

Best weapons for Skirk

  1. Azurelight: Signature CRIT Rate sword granting ATK and CRIT DMG after Skill when she has zero Energy.
  2. Haran Geppaku Futsu: CRIT Rate and Elemental DMG with Normal Attack buffs after Skills; strong 5-star alternative.
  3. Primordial Jade Cutter: High CRIT Rate and HP-derived ATK; monitor CRIT Rate overcap.
  4. Mistsplitter Reforged: CRIT DMG and Elemental DMG bonus with stacks from infused attacks and Burst use.
  5. The Black Sword: Battle Pass CRIT Rate and Normal/Charged Attack damage; some healing on CRIT Normal/Charged hits.
  6. Calamity of Eshu: ATK and strong Normal/Charged bonuses while shielded. Best for shielded on-field Skirk.
  7. Finale of the Deep: Craftable ATK sword gaining Bond of Life after Skill; healing clears it for more ATK.
  8. Harbinger of Dawn: Strong CRIT stats at low rarity, but passive CRIT Rate requires staying above 90% HP.

Best artifacts for Skirk

  • Finale of the Deep Galleries (4 pcs) · Main DPS: Her preferred Cryo damage set when building around her unique resource system. It boosts Normal Attack damage and enhances her damage when Energy is at zero. Main stats: ATK% · Cryo DMG Bonus/ATK% · CR/CD.
  • Marechaussee Hunter (4 pcs) · Main DPS: Provides 15% Normal and Charged Attack DMG and up to 36% CRIT Rate when HP changes. Furina reliably activates the full bonus through her HP drain, making this a strong option for on-field Skirk. Watch your CRIT Rate, especially with Azurelight. The set can cause overcapping.
  • Gladiator's Finale (4 pcs) · Main DPS: A temporary ATK and Normal Attack option for on-field Skirk. Farm Finale of the Deep Galleries for a stronger dedicated set.

Main stats

  • Sands: ATK%
  • Goblet: Cryo DMG Bonus / ATK%
  • Circlet: Crit Rate / Crit DMG

Stat targets

  • ATK: 2,100+
  • Crit Rate: 60% - 70%
  • Crit DMG: 180% - 200%+

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 9+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 9+

Best teams for Skirk

  • ABYSS & BOUJE: Skirk (Main DPS), Furina (Buffs), Vodyanitsa (Support), Escoffier (Support). Skirk leads on-field Cryo damage, Furina provides off-field Hydro and a massive teamwide DMG Bonus, Vodyanitsa adds Hydro support, RES shred, and powerful damage buffs, while Escoffier provides Cryo application, additional RES shred, and healing.
  • FROST & FURIOUS: Skirk (Main DPS), Furina (Buffs), Citlali (Support), Escoffier (Support). Skirk deals on-field Cryo damage, Furina buffs and applies Hydro, Citlali shields and shreds RES, and Escoffier heals while boosting Cryo/Hydro damage.
  • COLD HARD FACTS: Skirk (Main DPS), Xingqiu (Sub DPS), Charlotte (Support), Escoffier (Support). Skirk deals on-field Cryo damage, Xingqiu provides off-field Hydro, Charlotte offers teamwide healing and TTDS buffs, and Escoffier heals while shredding Cryo/Hydro RES.
  • BUDGET ABYSS: Skirk (Main DPS), Xingqiu (Sub DPS), Rosaria (Sub DPS), Dahlia (Support). Skirk deals on-field Cryo damage, Xingqiu provides off-field Hydro, Rosaria boosts CRIT Rate and adds Cryo damage, and Dahlia shields Skirk while increasing her Attack SPD.

Pros

  • High damage
  • No Energy needs
  • Flexible rotations
  • High Burst DMG

Cons

  • Restrictive teams
  • Premium supports
  • Cryo/Hydro locked
  • Setup dependent

Build variations

Normal Attack Carry (On-field DPS)

Use Finale of the Deep Galleries for infused Normal Attacks.

  • Weapons: Azurelight, Haran Geppaku Futsu, The Black Sword
  • Artifacts: Finale of the Deep Galleries (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ATK% / Cryo DMG / CRIT; avoid ER investment.
  • Teams: Cryo and Hydro teammates to support her mechanics.
  • When it applies: No conventional Energy requirement.

Furina CRIT Alternative (On-field DPS)

Use an invested Marechaussee set as an alternative.

  • Weapons: Azurelight, Primordial Jade Cutter, The Black Sword
  • Artifacts: Marechaussee Hunter (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Adjust sheet CRIT Rate for Marechaussee stacks.
  • Teams: Furina and steady team healing.
  • When it applies: Finale remains the generally preferred set.

Varka Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

Varka build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Gest of the Mighty Wolf, A Thousand Blazing Suns), artifacts (A Day Carved From Rising Winds).

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Role: Main DPS

Varka is an Anemo on-field DPS built around fast, aggressive combat and reaction driven teams. He shines with strong supports, especially in Stellar Swirl teams.

Best weapons for Varka

  1. Gest of the Mighty Wolf: Provides CRIT Rate, ATK SPD, and stacking damage buffs that perfectly complement Varka's fast attacks and Hexerei synergy.
  2. A Thousand Blazing Suns: Provides CR, ATK, and CD buffs.
  3. Beacon of the Reed Sea: CRIT Rate and ATK boosts from Skill hits or taking damage; HP bonus when unshielded.
  4. Wolf's Gravestone: Very high ATK and a team ATK buff when hitting low-HP enemies; conditional uptime.
  5. Serpent Spine: Provides CR and stacking DMG bonuses.
  6. Prototype Archaic: Offers high Base ATK, ATK%.
  7. Blackcliff Slasher: CRIT DMG with an ATK bonus after defeating enemies; unreliable against single bosses.
  8. Tidal Shadow: Craftable ATK weapon with an additional ATK buff after healing; needs a healer.

Best artifacts for Varka

  • A Day Carved From Rising Winds (4 pcs) · Main DPS: Gives ATK after attacking and extra CRIT Rate once Witch’s Homework is complete; his best general set. Main stats: ATK% · ATK%/Pyro DMG · CR/CD.
  • Shimenawa's Reminiscence (4 pcs) · Main DPS: Boosts Normal and Charged Attack damage by 50% after using his Skill, at the cost of 15 Energy. Main stats: ATK% · ATK%/Pyro DMG · CR/CD.
  • Marechaussee Hunter (4 pcs) · Furina teams: Gain up to 36% CRIT Rate through HP changes. Only effective with reliable HP fluctuation, usually Furina. Main stats: ATK% · DMG% · CD.

Main stats

  • Sands: ATK%
  • Goblet: ATK% / Element DMG
  • Circlet: Crit Rate / Crit DMG

Stat targets

  • ATK: 2,500–3,000+
  • CRIT Rate: 60% - 80%
  • CRIT DMG: 170–200%+

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 6+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 9+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 6–8

Best teams for Varka

  • Knight Shift: Varka, Venti, Nicole, Durin. Venti groups enemies and shreds resistance, Nicole buffs and shields.
  • Prune & Punishment: Varka, Prune, Odette, Escoffier. Prune buffs Varka's DMG, Odette enables Stellar Swirl.
  • Watt the Fischl: Varka, Prune, Ineffa, Fischl. Prune boosts Varka's damage while Ineffa and Fischl provide consistent off-field Electro and Lunar-Charged support.
  • Category Varka: Varka, Prune, Furina, Vodyanitsa. Prune amplifies Varka's DMG, Furina adds Hydro DMG and buffs.

Pros

  • Strong personal damage
  • Fast Gameplay
  • Stellar Swirl synergy
  • Flexible teammates

Cons

  • Wants key supports
  • High Investment
  • Team reliant damage
  • Long field time

Build variations

Furina CRIT (Main DPS)

Trade Rising Winds for an HP-change CRIT setup.

  • Weapons: Gest of the Mighty Wolf, Serpent Spine
  • Artifacts: Marechaussee Hunter (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Avoid overcapping CRIT Rate with artifact stacks.
  • Teams: Furina and reliable HP variation.

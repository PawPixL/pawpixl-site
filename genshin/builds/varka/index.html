Varka Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts & Teams

Role: Main DPS

Varka is an Anemo on-field DPS built around fast, aggressive combat and reaction driven teams. He shines with strong supports, especially in Stellar Swirl teams.

Best weapons for Varka

  1. Gest of the Mighty Wolf
  2. A Thousand Blazing Suns
  3. Beacon of the Reed Sea
  4. Wolf's Gravestone
  5. Serpent Spine
  6. Prototype Archaic
  7. Blackcliff Slasher
  8. Tidal Shadow

Best artifacts

  • A Day Carved From Rising Winds (4 pcs) · Main DPS
  • Desert Pavilion Chronicle (4 pcs) · Main DPS
  • Viridescent Venerer + Crimson Witch of Flames (2 + 2 pcs) · Main DPS

Main stats

  • Sands: ATK%
  • Goblet: ATK% / Pyro DMG
  • Circlet: Crit Rate / Crit DMG

Stat targets

  • ATK: 2,500-3000+
  • Crit Rate: 70% - 80%
  • Crit DMG: 170% - 200%+
  • Energy Recharge: 110% - 130%

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 6+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 9+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 9+

Best teams

  • Knight Shift: Varka, Venti, Nicole, Durin
  • Prune & Punishment: Varka, Prune, Odette, qiqi
  • Watt the Fischl: Varka, Prune, Ineffa, Fischl
  • Category Varka: Varka, Prune, Furina, Vodyanitsa

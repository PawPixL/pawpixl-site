Role: Sub DPS and Buffer

Furina is a universal buffer and Sub-DPS, boosting nearly all forms of team damage while dealing impressive damage herself. She needs strong healers to quickly build Fanfare.

Best weapons for Furina

  1. Splendor of Tranquil Waters
  2. Key of Khaj-Nisut
  3. Primordial Jade Cutter
  4. Favonius Sword
  5. The Dockhand's Assistant
  6. Festering Desire
  7. Fleuve Cendre Ferryman
  8. Wolf-Fang

Best artifacts

  • Golden Troupe (4 pcs) · Sub DPS
  • Tenacity of the Millelith (4 pcs) · Sub DPS
  • Vourukasha's Glow + Heart of Depth (2 + 2 pcs) · Sub DPS

Main stats

  • Sands: Energy Recharge / HP%
  • Goblet: HP% / Hydro DMG Bonus
  • Circlet: Crit Rate / Crit DMG / HP%

Stat targets

  • HP: 35,000 to 40,000
  • CRIT Rate: 60% to 80%
  • CRIT DMG: 140% to 200%
  • Energy Recharge: 130% to 200%+ depending on team

Talent priority

  • Soloist's Solicitation: Level 1+
  • Salon Solitaire: Level 9+
  • Let the People Rejoice: Level 9+

Best teams

  • Fine Dining & Violence: Furina, Skirk, Escoffier, Vodyanitsa
  • Divas & Detonations: Furina, Navia, Columbina, Linnea
  • Furina’s Fitness Club: Furina, Varesa, Xilonen, Iansan
  • Lohen Up Places: Furina, Lohen, Escoffier, Mona

Pros

  • High DMG Buffer
  • Strong Off-field Damage
  • Powerful Cons

Cons

  • Requires Heals
  • Drains Teams HP
  • High ER Needs
  • Fanfare needs stacking

Build variations

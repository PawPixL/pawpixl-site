Furina Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

Furina build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Splendor of Tranquil Waters, Key of Khaj-Nisut), artifacts (Golden Troupe), main stats.

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Role: Sub DPS and Buffer

Furina is a universal buffer and Sub-DPS, boosting nearly all forms of team damage while dealing impressive damage herself. She needs strong healers to quickly build Fanfare.

Best weapons for Furina

  1. Splendor of Tranquil Waters: Signature CRIT DMG and Skill damage. Strong for personal damage when team Energy requirements are met.
  2. Key of Khaj-Nisut: Key of Khaj-Nisut boosts Furina’s HP and provides additional teamwide EM, strengthening her HP-scaling damage and reaction support.
  3. Primordial Jade Cutter: Primordial Jade Cutter provides CRIT Rate and HP, improving Furina’s personal damage and HP-scaling Skill.
  4. Favonius Sword: Favonius provides Energy Recharge and generates teamwide particles, helping Furina maintain Burst uptime and support the entire team.
  5. The Dockhand's Assistant: Provides HP, then grants Elemental Mastery and restores Energy after using a Skill or Burst with healing stacks.
  6. Festering Desire: Budget pick: Energy Recharge plus Skill CRIT Rate and Skill DMG.
  7. Fleuve Cendre Ferryman: Free fishing sword: Energy Recharge substat, Skill CRIT Rate, and bonus ER after casting Skill.
  8. Wolf-Fang: Gives CRIT Rate and boosts Skill damage. Its stacking CRIT passive is awkward for an off field Furina.

Best artifacts for Furina

  • Golden Troupe (4 pcs) · Sub DPS: Boosts off-field Skill damage substantially. Default choice for personal damage. Main stats: ER/HP% · HP%/Hydro DMG Bonus · CR/CD/HP%.
  • Tenacity of the Millelith (4 pcs) · Sub DPS: Tenacity boosts Furina’s HP and provides a teamwide ATK buff, offering support for ATK-scaling carries at the cost of her personal damage.
  • Vourukasha's Glow + Heart of Depth (2 + 2 pcs) · Sub DPS: Combines an HP boost with Hydro DMG Bonus to strengthen Furina’s Skill damage.

Main stats

  • Sands: Energy Recharge / HP%
  • Goblet: HP% / Hydro DMG Bonus
  • Circlet: Crit Rate / Crit DMG / HP%

Stat targets

  • HP: 35,000 to 40,000
  • CRIT Rate: 60% to 80%
  • CRIT DMG: 140% to 200%
  • Energy Recharge: 130% to 200%+ depending on team

Talent priority

  • Soloist's Solicitation: Level 1+
  • Salon Solitaire: Level 9+
  • Let the People Rejoice: Level 9+

Best teams for Furina

  • Fine Dining & Violence: Furina (Buffs), Skirk (Main DPS), Escoffier (Support), Vodyanitsa (Buffs). Skirk drives on-field Cryo damage, Furina provides Hydro application and DMG buffs, Escoffier shreds Cryo/Hydro RES and heals, and Vodyanitsa adds further RES shred and damage buffs.
  • Divas & Detonations: Furina (Buffs), Navia (Main DPS), Columbina (Lunar), Linnea (Support). Navia drives Geo damage, Furina buffs the team and enables Crystallize, Columbina supports Lunar reactions, and Linnea provides healing and Geo buffs.
  • Furina’s Fitness Club: Furina (Buffs), Varesa (Main DPS), Xilonen (Support), Iansan (Buffs). Varesa drives Electro damage, Furina provides off-field Hydro and DMG buffs, Xilonen shreds RES and heals, and Iansan boosts ATK and provides additional support.
  • Lohen Up Places: Furina (Buffs), Lohen (Main DPS), Escoffier (Support), Mona (Buffs). Lohen drives Freeze damage, Furina provides Hydro and DMG buffs, Escoffier shreds Cryo/Hydro RES and heals, and Mona boosts damage with Omen and Hexerei buffs

Pros

  • High DMG Buffer
  • Strong Off-field Damage
  • Powerful Cons

Cons

  • Requires Heals
  • Drains Teams HP
  • High ER Needs
  • Fanfare needs stacking

Build variations

Off-field Skill Damage (Off-field DPS)

Prioritize off-field Skill damage and Burst uptime.

  • Weapons: Splendor of Tranquil Waters, Primordial Jade Cutter
  • Artifacts: Golden Troupe (4 pcs)
  • Stats: HP% / Hydro / CRIT; ER before extra damage.
  • Teams: Reliable healing to maintain Fanfare.

Team Support (Buffer)

Trade personal damage for team buffs and reliable uptime.

  • Weapons: Key of Khaj-Nisut, Favonius Sword
  • Artifacts: Tenacity of the Millelith (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Build ER and HP to maintain Burst.
  • Teams: An ally that benefits from ATK or EM buffs, depending on equipment.

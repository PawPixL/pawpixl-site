Zibai Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

Zibai build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Lightbearing Moonshard, Uraku Misugiri), artifacts (Night Of The Sky'S Unveiling), main stats.

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Role: DPS

Zibai is a Geo on-field DPS focused on Lunar-Crystallize, offering strong sustained damage but favoring dedicated team setups. Also, she's a horse.

Best weapons for Zibai

  1. Lightbearing Moonshard: Signature CRIT DMG and DEF; greatly raises Lunar-Crystallize damage after Skill.
  2. Uraku Misugiri: CRIT DMG, DEF and Skill damage; gains more DEF and Skill damage with nearby Geo hits.
  3. Light of Foliar Incision: CRIT DMG and EM-based damage on Normal and Skill hits. Useful stat stick, but less focused than DEF and Lunar weapons.
  4. Athame Artis: CRIT Rate and a Burst-triggered ATK team buff. Primarily a stat stick; its passive is less valuable for DEF-scaling Zibai.
  5. Peak Patrol Song: Large DEF% stat and team Elemental DMG buffs after Normal/Plunging Attack hits.
  6. Flute of Ezpitzal: Craftable DEF sword with more DEF after Skill. Excellent F2P choice for Zibai.
  7. Cinnabar Spindle: DEF% weapon that adds damage to Skill hits based on DEF; past-event only.
  8. Harbinger of Dawn: Strong CRIT option while HP stays above 90%. A shielder or reliable healing helps maintain its passive.

Best artifacts for Zibai

  • Night Of The Sky'S Unveiling (4 pcs) · Main DPS: Best Lunar set: EM, conditional CRIT Rate and a team Lunar damage bonus at Ascendant Gleam. Main stats: DEF% · DEF% · CR/CD.
  • Husk of Opulent Dreams (4 pcs) · Main DPS: DEF and Geo DMG help as a temporary set, but it trails Night of the Sky’s Unveiling. Main stats: DEF% · DEF% · CR/CD.
  • Husk of Opulent Dreams + Wanderer's Troupe (2 + 2 pcs) · Temporary mixed set: Use 2pc DEF with 2pc EM while farming Night of the Sky’s Unveiling. Main stats: DEF% · DEF% · CR/CD.

Main stats

  • Sands: DEF%
  • Goblet: DEF%
  • Circlet: CRIT Rate / CRIT DMG

Stat targets

  • DEF: 2,500+
  • CRIT Rate: 40–50% with Night of the Sky’s Unveiling
  • CRIT DMG: 170–200%+

Talent priority

  • Elemental Skill: Level 9+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 9+

Best teams for Zibai

  • Crater Expectations: Zibai, Illuga, Columbina, Linnea. Zibai leads on-field Lunar-Crystallize damage, Columbina provides off-field Hydro and Lunar buffs, Linnea adds Geo RES shred, healing, and EM buffs, and Illuga boosts Geo and Lunar-Crystallize damage.
  • Geo Force One: Zibai, Illuga, Columbina, Gorou. Zibai leads on-field Lunar-Crystallize damage, Columbina provides off-field Hydro and Lunar buffs, Illuga boosts Geo and Lunar-Crystallize damage, and Gorou adds DEF and Geo DMG buffs.
  • OSMANTHUS WHINES: Zibai, Illuga, Columbina, Zhongli. Zibai leads on-field Lunar-Crystallize damage, Columbina provides off-field Hydro and Lunar buffs, Illuga boosts Geo and Lunar-Crystallize damage, and Zhongli provides shielding and universal RES shred.
  • Columbina at Home: Zibai, Illuga, Aino, Gorou. Zibai leads on-field Lunar-Crystallize damage, Illuga provides major Geo and Lunar reaction buffs, Aino supplies off-field Hydro application, and Gorou boosts DEF and Geo damage.

Build variations

Lunar-Crystallize DPS (Main DPS)

Stay on-field during Skill to deal direct Lunar-Crystallize damage.

  • Weapons: Lightbearing Moonshard, Uraku Misugiri
  • Artifacts: Night Of The Sky'S Unveiling (4 pcs)
  • Stats: DEF% / DEF% / CRIT; build around set CRIT Rate.
  • Teams: Geo and Hydro teammates with Ascendant Gleam.

Zibai talents

Normal Attack: Golden Blade's Petaled Touch

Normal Attack
Performs up to 4 rapid strikes.

Charged Attack
Consumes a certain amount of Stamina to unleash 2 rapid sword strikes.

Plunging Attack
Plunges from mid-air to strike the ground below, damaging opponents along the path and dealing AoE DMG upon impact.

Elemental Skill: Heaven and Earth Made Manifest

Cooldown: 18s

Summoning a shadow of her former powers, she switches to the Lunar Phase Shift mode.
In this mode, Zibai's Normal Attacks and Charged Attacks will deal Geo DMG that cannot be overridden by other infusions, and she can accrue special Phase Shift Radiance through different methods. Zibai can consume Phase Shift Radiance to unleash the special Elemental Skill Spirit Steed's Stride.

Moonsign: Ascendant Gleam
While in the Lunar Phase Shift mode, when Zibai performs Normal Attacks, the fourth attack will deal an additional instance of Geo DMG, which is considered Lunar-Crystallize Reaction DMG.

Elemental Burst: Tri-Sphere Eminence

Cooldown: 15s · Energy cost: 60

Zibai operates the Jadelight Canopy, dealing two instances of Geo DMG, with the second damage instance being considered Lunar-Crystallize Reaction DMG.
When cast, if Zibai is in the Lunar Phase Shift, the duration of the current Lunar Phase Shift will extend by an additional 1.7s.

Passive talent: The Selenic Adeptus Descends

When casting the Elemental Skill Heaven and Earth Made Manifest, or when nearby party members trigger Moondrift Harmony, Zibai gains the Selenic Descent effect for 4s: The DMG dealt by the 2nd hit of Spirit Steed's Stride is increased by 60% of Zibai's DEF.

Passive talent: Layered Peaks Pierce the Clouds

Other Geo party members increase Zibai's DEF by 15% each. Hydro party members increase her Elemental Mastery by 60 each.

Passive talent: Moonsign Benediction: The Coursing Sun and Moon

When a party member triggers a Hydro Crystallize reaction, it will be converted into the Lunar-Crystallize reaction, with every 100 DEF that Zibai has increasing Lunar-Crystallize's Base DMG by 0.7%, up to a maximum of 14%.

Additionally, when Zibai is in the party, the party's Moonsign will increase by 1 level.

Passive talent: Moonlit Flower Forest

At night (18:00 – 06:00), when not in combat, restore 1 Elemental Energy to Zibai every 2 seconds.
This effect does not work in Domains, Trounce Domains, or the Spiral Abyss.

Zibai constellations

C1: Burst Forth With Vigor, But Enter in Silence

After using the Elemental Skill Heaven and Earth Made Manifest, Zibai will immediately gain 100 Phase Shift Radiance, and the max number of Spirit Steed's Stride usages per Lunar Phase Shift mode is increased to 5 times.
Additionally, each time you switch to the Lunar Phase Shift mode, the first Spirit Steed's Stride's 2nd-hit Lunar-Crystallize Reaction DMG is increased by 220%.

C2: At Birth Are Souls Born, and in Death Leave But Husks

When in the Lunar Phase Shift mode, all nearby party members' Lunar-Crystallize Reaction DMG is increased by 30%.

Moonsign: Ascendant Gleam: Ascension Talent The Selenic Adeptus Descends is enhanced: the DMG dealt by the 2nd hit of Spirit Steed's Stride is further increased by 550% of Zibai's DEF. You must first unlock the Ascension Talent "The Selenic Adeptus Descends."

C3: Free From Constraints and Worldly Ties

Increases the Level of Elemental Skill Heaven and Earth Made Manifest by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C4: The Spirit Passes, Then Form Follows

While in the Lunar Phase Shift mode, Zibai's Normal Attack sequence will not reset, and when Spirit Steed's Stride hits opponents, Zibai will gain the "Scattermoon Splendor" effect: The next time she uses Normal Attacks, the additional attack from her 4th hit will deal 250% of the original damage as Lunar-Crystallize Reaction DMG.

C5: Perceive the Worthless and Debate It Not

Increases the Level of Tri-Sphere Eminence by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C6: The World, A Journey in Passing

While Zibai is in the Lunar Phase Shift mode, her Phase Shift Radiance gain rate is increased by 50%.
Additionally, Spirit Steed's Stride will change such that it will consume all Phase Shift Radiance. This will elevate the DMG dealt by this instance of Spirit Steed's Stride and the Lunar-Crystallize Reaction DMG dealt by Zibai within the next 3s by 1.6% for every point consumed above 70. This effect cannot stack.

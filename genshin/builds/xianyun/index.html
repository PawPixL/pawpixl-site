Xianyun Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts & Teams

Role: Support, Healer

Xianyun heals the whole party and lets allies plunge during her Burst. Her ATK-based passive adds DMG to plunges, making her exceptional with Xiao, Gaming, and other plunge carries. Build enough ER to Burst every rotation, then stack ATK.

Best weapons for Xianyun

  1. Crane's Echoing Call
  2. Skyward Atlas
  3. Favonius Codex
  4. Oathsworn Eye
  5. Flowing Purity
  6. Thrilling Tales of Dragon Slayers
  7. Prototype Amber
  8. Hakushin Ring

Best artifacts

  • Viridescent Venerer (4 pcs) · Elemental Teams
  • Song of Days Past (4 pcs) · Anemo Plunge Teams
  • Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs) · ATK Buff

Main stats

  • Sands: Energy Recharge / ATK%
  • Goblet: ATK%
  • Circlet: ATK% / CRIT Rate (Favonius)

Stat targets

  • ATK: 3,000 - 4,500 (with buffs)
  • Energy Recharge: 180% - 220% (double Anemo)
  • Energy Recharge: 220% - 300% (solo Anemo)
  • CRIT Rate: 30% - 50% (Favonius only)

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 6-8
  • Elemental Burst: Level 8-9+

Best teams

  • Cloud Nine: Xianyun, Xiao, Faruzan, Furina
  • Curtain Call: Xianyun, Gaming, Furina, Bennett
  • Dragon Drop: Xianyun, Diluc, Furina, Bennett
  • Budget Takeoff: Xianyun, Gaming, Xingqiu, Bennett

Pros

  • Enables plunge attacks
  • Strong teamwide healing
  • Powerful plunge buff
  • Great with Furina

Cons

  • High Energy needs
  • Buff favors single target
  • Plunge playstyle required
  • Needs ATK investment

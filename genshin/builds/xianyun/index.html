Xianyun Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

Xianyun build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Crane's Echoing Call, Skyward Atlas), artifacts (Viridescent Venerer), main stats, talent priority.

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Role: Support, Healer

Xianyun heals the whole party and lets allies plunge during her Burst. Her ATK-based passive adds DMG to plunges, making her exceptional with Xiao, Gaming, and other plunge carries. Build enough ER to Burst every rotation, then stack ATK.

Best weapons for Xianyun

  1. Crane's Echoing Call: Very high Base ATK strengthens her plunge buff. A Skill plunge buffs allies’ plunges and their plunges restore her Energy. Strongest in teams that plunge repeatedly.
  2. Skyward Atlas: High Base ATK and ATK% help maximize her healing and plunge buff when Energy Recharge is already covered. Best with adequate ER, especially Xiao plus Faruzan.
  3. Favonius Codex: Energy Recharge and CRIT-triggered particles help Xianyun Burst every rotation and lower team Energy needs. Build some CRIT Rate and hit enemies to trigger its passive. Higher refinements improve consistency.
  4. Oathsworn Eye: ATK% raises healing and plunge buffs.
  5. Flowing Purity: Craftable high Base ATK and ATK% help her buff and healing; its personal damage passive is not the main reason to use it. Use when Burst uptime is already comfortable.
  6. Thrilling Tales of Dragon Slayers: Gives the next character a large ATK buff, useful for Xiao or Gaming, but its low Base ATK weakens Xianyun’s own plunge bonus. Swap directly to an ATK-scaling carry. R5 recommended for the full ATK buff.
  7. Prototype Amber: Restores Xianyun’s Energy and heals the party after Burst. A comfort option, though HP% does not strengthen her ATK-based buff. Prefer when extra sustain and Energy matter. R5 improves Energy and healing.
  8. Hakushin Ring: Energy Recharge and elemental buffs can help Electro teams after she triggers an Electro-related Swirl reaction. Situational Electro teams only; not for Xiao hypercarry.

Best artifacts for Xianyun

  • Viridescent Venerer (4 pcs) · Elemental Teams: Swirling Pyro, Hydro or Electro reduces that element’s enemy RES by 40%. Best for reaction carries when Xianyun can Swirl the right aura. Main stats: ER% or ATK% · ATK% · ATK%.
  • Song of Days Past (4 pcs) · Anemo Plunge Teams: Turns her large Burst healing into an added damage bonus for the active carry, useful when VV cannot shred the carry’s Anemo RES. Main stats: ER% or ATK% · ATK% · ATK%.
  • Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs) · ATK Buff: Burst grants the party 20% ATK for 12 seconds. Main stats: ER% or ATK% · ATK% · ATK%.

Main stats

  • Sands: Energy Recharge / ATK%
  • Goblet: ATK%
  • Circlet: ATK% / CRIT Rate (Favonius)

Stat targets

  • ATK: 3,000 - 4,500 (with buffs)
  • Energy Recharge: 180% - 220% (double Anemo)
  • Energy Recharge: 220% - 300% (solo Anemo)
  • CRIT Rate: 30% - 50% (Favonius only)

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 6-8
  • Elemental Burst: Level 8-9+

Best teams for Xianyun

  • Cloud Nine: Xianyun (Healer / Plunge Buff), Xiao (Main DPS), Faruzan (Anemo Buffer), Furina (Hydro Buffer). Xianyun's healing fuels Furina's Fanfare while her plunge buff amplifies Xiao. Faruzan shreds Anemo RES and boosts his damage.
  • Curtain Call: Xianyun (Healer / VV), Gaming (Main DPS), Furina (Hydro / DMG Buff), Bennett (ATK Buff). Furina supplies Hydro for Gaming's Vaporized plunges. Bennett boosts ATK while Xianyun heals, enables jumps and buffs his plunge hits.
  • Dragon Drop: Xianyun (Healer / VV), Diluc (Main DPS), Furina (Hydro / DMG Buff), Bennett (ATK Buff). Xianyun lets Diluc spam his strong plunges. Furina applies Hydro for Vaporize; Bennett adds ATK and Xianyun provides healing and buffs.
  • Budget Takeoff: Xianyun (Healer / VV), Gaming (Main DPS), Xingqiu (Hydro Sub DPS), Bennett (ATK Buff). Gaming plunges with Xianyun's buff; weave Normal Attacks to trigger Xingqiu's Hydro swords for Vaporize. Bennett buffs ATK and heals.

Build variations

Xiao Plunge Support (Anemo Plunge Buffer)

Use with Xiao when Viridescent Venerer cannot shred the Anemo damage he deals. Her healing also builds Furina Fanfare.

  • Weapons: Crane's Echoing Call, Skyward Atlas, Oathsworn Eye, Favonius Codex
  • Artifacts: Song of Days Past (4 pcs); Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs)
  • Stats: After meeting ER needs, push ATK toward the 4,500 buff cap. Double Anemo and Faruzan reduce required ER.
  • Teams: Xiao and Faruzan; Furina is an excellent fourth teammate.
  • When it applies: Do not duplicate Noblesse on the same team.
  • Notes: C2 is an especially strong support upgrade.

Vaporize Plunge Enabler (Reaction Support)

Choose this setup to enable and buff Pyro plunges while decreasing enemy Pyro resistance.

  • Weapons: Crane's Echoing Call, Favonius Codex, Oathsworn Eye
  • Artifacts: Viridescent Venerer (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ER first, then ATK. Aim for a Pyro Swirl to shred Pyro RES before the carry begins plunging.
  • Teams: Gaming or Diluc; Hydro application from Furina or Xingqiu.
  • When it applies: VV must Swirl Pyro to shred Pyro RES.
  • Notes: Xingqiu needs Normal Attacks between plunges to launch his Burst swords.

High Energy / Budget (Accessible Support)

For teams where her Burst is not ready every rotation. Comfortable healing and jump access outweigh a little extra ATK.

  • Weapons: Favonius Codex, Oathsworn Eye, Prototype Amber, Flowing Purity
  • Artifacts: Viridescent Venerer (4 pcs); Song of Days Past (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Use an ER Sands if needed; add CRIT Rate for Favonius procs, then focus on ATK.
  • Teams: Any plunge carry; team battery or Favonius holders ease the Energy demand.
  • When it applies: Favonius requires a CRIT hit; Prototype Amber gives less ATK.
  • Notes: Check ER per team. Solo Anemo needs substantially more Energy Recharge.

C6 On-field Plunge (On-field Anemo DPS)

Only for players who want Xianyun herself to be the plunge carry instead of supporting another character.

  • Weapons: Skyward Atlas, Crane's Echoing Call
  • Artifacts: Marechaussee Hunter (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Build CRIT and ATK with enough ER; unlike the support build, personal Anemo damage matters.
  • Teams: C6 Xianyun, C6 Faruzan, Furina, and Bennett offer a strong damage shell.
  • When it applies: Requires C6 for the intended offensive playstyle.
  • Notes: Situational constellation build, not the recommended C0 support setup.

Xianyun talents

Normal Attack: Word of Wind and Flower

Normal Attack
Summons swirling winds to perform up to 4 attacks, dealing Anemo DMG.

Charged Attack
Consumes a certain amount of Stamina and launches a Breeze Bolt in a straight line that deals Anemo DMG to opponents along its path.

Plunging Attack
Gathers the power of Anemo and plunges towards the ground from mid-air, damaging all opponents in her path. Deals AoE Anemo DMG upon impact with the ground.

Elemental Skill: White Clouds at Dawn

Cooldown: 12s

Xianyun enters the Cloud Transmogrification state, in which she will not take Fall DMG, and uses Skyladder once.
In this state, her Plunging Attack will be converted into Driftcloud Wave instead, which deals AoE Anemo DMG and ends the Cloud Transmogrification state. This DMG is considered Plunging Attack DMG.
Each use of Skyladder while in this state increases the DMG and AoE of the next Driftcloud Wave used.

Skyladder
Can be used while in mid-air. Xianyun leaps forward, dealing Anemo DMG to targets along her path.
During each Cloud Transmogrification state Xianyun enters, Skyladder may be used up to 3 times and only 1 instance of Skyladder DMG can be dealt to any one opponent.
If Skyladder is not used again in a short period, the Cloud Transmogrification state will be canceled.

If Xianyun does not use Driftcloud Wave while in this state, the next CD of White Clouds at Dawn will be decreased by 3s.

Elemental Burst: Stars Gather at Dusk

Cooldown: 18s · Energy cost: 70

Brings forth a sacred breeze that deals AoE Anemo DMG and heals all nearby characters based on Xianyun's ATK. It will also summon the "Starwicker" mechanism.

Starwicker
·Continuously follows the active character and periodically heals all nearby party members based on Xianyun's ATK.
·Starts with 8 stacks of Adeptal Assistance. While Adeptal Assistance is active, nearby active characters in the party will have their jump height increased.
·When the active character completes a Plunging Attack, Starwicker will consume 1 stack of Adeptal Assistance and deal AoE Anemo DMG.
Only 1 Starwicker can exist simultaneously.

Passive talent: Galefeather Pursuit

Each opponent hit by Driftcloud Waves from White Clouds at Dawn will grant all nearby party members 1 stack of Storm Pinion for 20s. Max 4 stacks. These will cause the characters' Plunging Attack CRIT Rate to increase by 4%/6%/8%/10% respectively.
Each Storm Pinion created by hitting an opponent has an independent duration.

Passive talent: Consider, the Adeptus in Her Realm

When the Starwicker created by Stars Gather at Dusk has Adeptal Assistance stacks, nearby active characters' Plunging Attack shockwave DMG will be increased by 200% of Xianyun's ATK. The maximum DMG increase that can be achieved this way is 9,000.
Each Plunging Attack shockwave DMG instance can only apply this increased DMG effect to a single opponent. Each character can trigger this effect once every 0.4s.

Passive talent: Crane Form

Increases gliding SPD for your own party members by 15%.
Not stackable with Passive Talents that provide the exact same effects.

Xianyun constellations

C1: Purifying Wind

White Clouds at Dawn gains 1 additional charge.

C2: Aloof From the World

After using a Skyladder from White Clouds at Dawn, Xianyun's ATK will be increased by 20% for 15s.
Additionally, the effects of the Ascension Talent "Consider, the Adeptus in Her Realm" will be enhanced: When the Starwicker created by Stars Gather at Dusk has Adeptal Assistance stacks, nearby active characters' Plunging Attack shockwave DMG will be increased by 400% of Xianyun's ATK. The maximum DMG increase that can be achieved this way is 18,000.
Each Plunging Attack shockwave DMG instance can only apply this increased DMG effect to a single opponent. Each character can trigger this effect once every 0.4s.
You must first unlock the Ascension Talent "Consider, the Adeptus in Her Realm."

C3: Creations of Star and Moon

Increases the Level of Stars Gather at Dusk by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C4: Mystery Millet Gourmet

After using Skyladder 1/2/3 times during one White Clouds at Dawn Cloud Transmogrification state, when a Driftcloud Wave unleashed during that instance hits an opponent, it will heal all nearby party members for 50%/80%/150% of Xianyun's ATK. This effect can be triggered once every 5s.

C5: Astride Rose-Colored Clouds

Increases the Level of White Clouds at Dawn by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C6: They Call Her Cloud Retainer

After Xianyun uses 1/2/3 Skyladders within one Cloud Transmogrification caused by White Clouds at Dawn, the CRIT DMG of a Driftcloud Wave created in this instance of Cloud Transmogrification will be increased by 15%/35%/70%.
Within 16s after Xianyun has used Stars Gather at Dusk, White Clouds at Dawn will not enter CD. This effect will be canceled once she has used White Clouds at Dawn 8 times.

Lynette Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

Lynette build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Favonius Sword, Freedom-Sworn), artifacts (Viridescent Venerer), main stats, talent priority, teams.

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Role: Support / Sub DPS

Lynette is an off-field Anemo support whose Burst taunts enemies and deals periodic Anemo damage. Viridescent Venerer lets her reduce Swirled elemental RES, while her passive grants a modest team ATK buff. Her C6 unlocks an Anemo-infused on-field driver playstyle.

Best weapons for Lynette

  1. Favonius Sword: ER and CRIT-triggered neutral particles help fund her costly Burst and support team rotations.
  2. Freedom-Sworn: Elemental Mastery strengthens Swirls; triggering reactions builds teamwide ATK and Normal/Charged/Plunging Attack buffs. Best when teammates use its buffs and Burst energy is covered.
  3. Skyward Blade: A substantial ER substat supports Burst uptime.
  4. Sacrificial Sword: ER plus a possible extra Skill use increases Anemo particles and Swirl opportunities.
  5. Xiphos' Moonlight: Its EM substat improves Swirl damage while converting EM into Energy Recharge for Lynette and her teammates. For EM-focused support or C6 reaction-driver builds. More refinements boost shared ER.
  6. Fleuve Cendre Ferryman: A free fishing sword that adds Energy Recharge, Skill CRIT Rate, and temporary extra ER after using Skill. Excellent free choice if you can fish for it. R5 obtainable through fishing.
  7. Amenoma Kageuchi: ATK% boosts personal damage, while Skill-generated Succession Seeds restore Energy after Burst. Better when multiple Skills are used across rotations; watch ER. Refinement increases Energy restored.
  8. Wolf-Fang: CRIT Rate plus Skill and Burst damage bonuses improve her personal damage when Energy Recharge is already sufficient. Primarily damage-oriented or C6 builds; its stacks need on-field hits.

Best artifacts for Lynette

  • Viridescent Venerer (4 pcs) · Best: Swirling Pyro, Hydro, Electro, or Cryo reduces that element’s enemy RES by 40% for 10s. Main stats: ER/ATK% · Anemo DMG · CRIT.
  • Emblem of Severed Fate (4 pcs) · Personal DMG: Converts Energy Recharge into Burst damage, ideal when another Anemo ally already provides VV shred. Main stats: ER/ATK% · Anemo DMG · CRIT.
  • Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs) · ATK Buffer: Grants the party 20% ATK after Burst; only use if no other teammate already carries 4pc Noblesse. Main stats: ER/ATK% · Anemo DMG · CRIT.

Main stats

  • Sands: Energy Recharge / ATK%
  • Goblet: Anemo DMG Bonus / ATK%
  • Circlet: CRIT Rate / CRIT DMG

Stat targets

  • ER, solo Anemo: 180% - 200%
  • ER, solo Anemo (1 Skill, no Fav): 240%+
  • ER, 2 Skills + Fav: 115% - 160%
  • CRIT Rate, Favonius: 45% - 65%
  • CRIT Rate, damage build: 60% - 75%

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1
  • Elemental Skill: Level 6-8
  • Elemental Burst: Level 8-9

Best teams for Lynette

  • Childe Vaporize: Lynette (Support / Debuffer), Tartaglia (On-field DPS / Hydro Enabler), Xiangling (Off-field DPS / Pyro Enabler), Bennett (Healer / Buffer). Lynette Swirls Hydro or Pyro for VV shred and offers a taunt; Tartaglia enables Xiangling Vaporize while Bennett buffs and heals.
  • Crimson Catwalk: Lynette (Support / Debuffer), Arlecchino (On-field DPS), Yelan (Off-field DPS / Buffer), Bennett (Healer / Buffer). Lynette shreds Pyro or Hydro RES with VV; Yelan supplies off-field Hydro and buffs while Bennett buffs Arlecchino.
  • Electric Masquerade: Lynette (Support / Debuffer), Clorinde (On-field DPS), Fischl (Off-field DPS / Battery), Nahida (Dendro Enabler / Buffer). Lynette Swirls Electro for VV shred, boosting Clorinde and Fischl; Nahida maintains Dendro for Aggravate reactions.
  • C6 Plunge Revue: Lynette (Support / Debuffer), Faruzan (Anemo Buffer / Debuffer), Furina (Off-field DPS / Buffer), Xianyun (Healer / Plunge Buffer). At C6, Lynette uses Anemo-infused attacks and Xianyun-enabled plunges; Faruzan buffs Anemo and Furina provides damage buffs.

Cons

Build variations

C6 Anemo Carry (On-field DPS)

Use the C6 Anemo infusion for on-field attacks and optionally Xianyun-enhanced plunges. Lynette can self-drain/heal HP for Marechaussee.

  • Weapons: Primordial Jade Cutter, Wolf-Fang, Finale of the Deep
  • Artifacts: Marechaussee Hunter (4 pcs); Desert Pavilion Chronicle (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ATK%/Anemo DMG/CRIT; maintain Burst ER; account for Marechaussee CRIT Rate.
  • Teams: C6 required; Faruzan and Furina/Xianyun are strong partners.
  • When it applies: Requires C6
  • Notes: Desert Pavilion is an alternative when its Charged Attack activation fits your rotation.

C6 Hyperbloom Driver (Reaction Driver)

Anemo-infused attacks Swirl Electro into Dendro Cores, triggering Hyperbloom; VV also enhances Hydro and Electro teammate damage.

  • Weapons: Xiphos' Moonlight, Freedom-Sworn, Iron Sting, Favonius Sword
  • Artifacts: Viridescent Venerer (4 pcs); Gilded Dreams (4 pcs)
  • Stats: EM/EM/EM; use ER Sands if Burst uptime is inconsistent. Level 90 matters for reaction damage.
  • Teams: C6; Electro + Hydro + Dendro teammates, e.g. Fischl, Xingqiu, Nahida.
  • When it applies: Requires C6 and Electro aura access
  • Notes: Do not assume Lynette owns every Hyperbloom when Electro teammates also trigger cores.

Burst Damage (Off-field DPS)

Concentrates on her Burst damage rather than boosting Pyro, Hydro, Electro, or Cryo allies.

  • Weapons: Favonius Sword, Skyward Blade, Sacrificial Sword, Fleuve Cendre Ferryman
  • Artifacts: Emblem of Severed Fate (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ER/ATK% Sands, Anemo Goblet, CRIT Circlet; meet Burst ER first.
  • Teams: Best if another Anemo already holds Viridescent Venerer or VV offers little value.
  • When it applies: High Energy Recharge may be needed as solo Anemo
  • Notes: Emblem does not replace VV resistance reduction in reaction-focused teams.

Noblesse Team Buffer (Support)

Use her Burst to provide 20% team ATK. This is a fallback when VV shred is unavailable, redundant, or assigned elsewhere.

  • Weapons: Favonius Sword, Sacrificial Sword, Fleuve Cendre Ferryman
  • Artifacts: Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Prioritize ER; use CRIT Rate for Favonius, then ATK or CRIT as desired.
  • Teams: ATK-scaling main damage dealer; no existing 4pc Noblesse wielder.
  • When it applies: Do not stack duplicate 4pc Noblesse buffs
  • Notes: Her modest built-in ATK buff remains separate from the Noblesse buff.

Accessible VV Support (Support)

Budget build that focuses on reliable Burst and on-field Swirl application instead of expensive weapons.

  • Weapons: Fleuve Cendre Ferryman, Amenoma Kageuchi, Skyrider Sword
  • Artifacts: Viridescent Venerer (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ER Sands if needed; Anemo DMG Goblet and CRIT Circlet.
  • Teams: Pyro, Hydro, Electro, or Cryo carry who benefits from VV shred.
  • When it applies: Solo Anemo and one Skill can need very high ER
  • Notes: Skyrider Sword offers an accessible ER fallback; Amenoma needs a suitable rotation to restore Energy.

Lynette talents

Normal Attack: Rapid Ritesword

Normal Attack
Performs up to 4 rapid strikes.

Charged Attack
Consumes a certain amount of Stamina to unleash 2 rapid sword strikes.

Plunging Attack
Plunges from mid-air to strike the ground below, damaging opponents along the path and dealing AoE DMG upon impact.

Elemental Skill: Enigmatic Feint

Cooldown: 12s

Flicks her mantle and executes an Enigma Thrust, dealing Anemo DMG.
When the Enigma Thrust hits an opponent, it will restore Lynette's HP based on her Max HP, and in the 4s afterward, she will lose a certain amount of HP per second.
Based on whether you press or hold this ability, she will use Enigma Thrust differently.

Press
She swiftly uses an Enigma Thrust.

Hold
Lynette will enter a high-speed Pilfering Shadow state and apply Shadowsign to a nearby opponent. You can control her movement direction during this state, and you can end it prematurely by using this skill again.
When this high-speed state ends, Lynette will unleash her Enigma Thrust. If there is an opponent with Shadowsign applied to them nearby, Lynette will approach them in a flash before using Enigma Thrust.

A maximum of 1 opponent can have Shadowsign at any one time. When this opponent gets too far from Lynette, the Shadowsign will be canceled.

Arkhe: Ousia
At specific intervals, Lynette will unleash a Surging Blade when she uses Enigma Thrust, dealing Ousia-aligned Anemo DMG.

Elemental Burst: Magic Trick: Astonishing Shift

Cooldown: 18s · Energy cost: 70

Lynette raises her mantle high, dealing AoE Anemo DMG, using skillful sleight of hand to make a giant Bogglecat Box appear!

Bogglecat Box
·Taunts nearby opponents, attracting their attacks.
·Deals Anemo DMG to nearby opponents at intervals.
·When the Bogglecat Box comes into contact with Hydro/Pyro/Cryo/Electro, it will gain the corresponding element and additionally fire Vivid Shots that will deal DMG from that element at intervals.
Elemental Absorption of this kind will only occur once during this ability's duration.

Passive talent: Sophisticated Synergy

Within 10s after using Magic Trick: Astonishing Shift, when there are 1/2/3/4 Elemental Types in the party, all party members' ATK will be increased by 8%/12%/16%/20% respectively.

Passive talent: Props Positively Prepped

After the Bogglecat Box summoned by Magic Trick: Astonishing Shift performs Elemental Conversion, Lynette's Elemental Burst will deal 15% more DMG. This effect will persist until the Bogglecat Box's duration ends.

Passive talent: Loci-Based Mnemonics

Shows the location of nearby Recovery Orbs on the minimap. The Aquatic Stamina and HP gained from touching Orbs will be increased by 25%.

Lynette constellations

C1: A Cold Blade Like a Shadow

When Enigmatic Feint's Enigma Thrust hits an opponent with Shadowsign, a vortex will be created at that opponent's position that will pull nearby opponents in.

C2: Endless Mysteries

Whenever the Bogglecat Box summoned by Magic Trick: Astonishing Shift fires a Vivid Shot, it will fire an extra Vivid Shot.

C3: Cognition-Inverting Gaze

Increases the Level of Magic Trick: Astonishing Shift by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C4: Tacit Coordination

Increases Enigmatic Feint's charges by 1.

C5: Obscuring Ambiguity

Increases the Level of Enigmatic Feint by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C6: Watchful Eye

When Lynette uses Enigmatic Feint's Enigma Thrust, she will gain an Anemo Infusion and 20% Anemo DMG Bonus for 6s.

Role: Support / Sub DPS

Lynette is an off-field Anemo support whose Burst taunts enemies and deals periodic Anemo damage. Viridescent Venerer lets her reduce Swirled elemental RES, while her passive grants a modest team ATK buff. Her C6 unlocks an Anemo-infused on-field driver playstyle.

  2. Freedom-Sworn
  3. Skyward Blade
  4. Sacrificial Sword
  5. Xiphos' Moonlight
  6. Fleuve Cendre Ferryman
  7. Amenoma Kageuchi
  8. Wolf-Fang

Main stats

  • Sands: Energy Recharge / ATK%
  • Goblet: Anemo DMG Bonus / ATK%
  • Circlet: CRIT Rate / CRIT DMG

Stat targets

  • ER, solo Anemo: 180% - 200%
  • ER, solo Anemo (1 Skill, no Fav): 240%+
  • ER, 2 Skills + Fav: 115% - 160%
  • CRIT Rate, Favonius: 45% - 65%
  • CRIT Rate, damage build: 60% - 75%

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1
  • Elemental Skill: Level 6-8
  • Elemental Burst: Level 8-9

Best teams

  • Childe Vaporize: Lynette, Tartaglia, Xiangling, Bennett
  • Crimson Catwalk: Lynette, Arlecchino, Yelan, Bennett
  • Electric Masquerade: Lynette, Clorinde, Fischl, Nahida
  • C6 Plunge Revue: Lynette, Faruzan, Furina, Xianyun

Pros

  • VV resistance shred
  • Useful Burst taunt
  • Free story character
  • C6 Anemo infusion

Cons

  • High Burst energy cost
  • Limited crowd control
  • Needs ER or a battery
  • C6 for on-field damage

Build variations

  • C6 Anemo Carry (On-field DPS): Use the C6 Anemo infusion for on-field attacks and optionally Xianyun-enhanced plunges. Lynette can self-drain/heal HP for Marechaussee.
  • C6 Hyperbloom Driver (Reaction Driver): Anemo-infused attacks Swirl Electro into Dendro Cores, triggering Hyperbloom; VV also enhances Hydro and Electro teammate damage.
  • Burst Damage (Off-field DPS): Concentrates on her Burst damage rather than boosting Pyro, Hydro, Electro, or Cryo allies.
  • Noblesse Team Buffer (Support): Use her Burst to provide 20% team ATK. This is a fallback when VV shred is unavailable, redundant, or assigned elsewhere.
  • Accessible VV Support (Support): Budget build that focuses on reliable Burst and on-field Swirl application instead of expensive weapons.

Keqing Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams (7.2)

Keqing build guide for Genshin Impact 7.2: best weapons (Mistsplitter Reforged, Primordial Jade Cutter), artifacts (Thundering Fury), main stats.

Last updated: November 12, 2026 · Game version 7.2

Role: Main DPS

Keqing is a fast Electro DPS who teleports into combat and chains infused attacks with her Burst. She shines in Aggravate teams, where frequent Electro hits trigger reactions and work especially well with Fischl. Her flexible rotations also support Overload and Lunar-Charged teams.

Best weapons for Keqing

  1. Mistsplitter Reforged: High Base ATK, CRIT DMG and Elemental DMG bonuses make this her strongest general sword.
  2. Primordial Jade Cutter: Massive CRIT Rate and a dependable ATK bonus. Watch her Burst passive to avoid excess CRIT Rate.
  3. Haran Geppaku Futsu: CRIT Rate and Elemental DMG with a Normal Attack bonus that needs teammates to cast Skills.
  4. Uraku Misugiri: CRIT DMG and buffs to Normal Attack and Skill damage; stronger when Geo damage triggers its bonus.
  5. Lion's Roar: Boosts damage against enemies affected by Electro. High refinements make it a powerful 4-star option. Maintain Electro aura. Higher refinements help.
  6. The Black Sword: Adds CRIT Rate and Normal Attack damage with a little healing, making her on-field combos more comfortable.
  7. Iron Sting: Craftable EM sword that improves Aggravate damage and grants Elemental DMG bonuses after dealing damage. Best with Dendro reactions. Higher refinements help.
  8. Toukabou Shigure: Event sword with Elemental Mastery and extra damage to a marked target. Useful in Aggravate if you own it. Limited event availability.

Best artifacts for Keqing

  • Thundering Fury (4 pcs) · Aggravate Best: Boosts Electro reactions and shortens Skill cooldown, giving Keqing extra Skill damage and flexible combos. Main stats: ATK% / Electro DMG / CRIT.
  • Gilded Dreams (4 pcs) · Aggravate Alternative: Grants Elemental Mastery and ATK from team composition, strengthening Aggravate damage. Main stats: ATK% or EM / Electro DMG / CRIT.
  • Thundersoother (4 pcs) · Electro Aura Teams: Boosts damage against Electro-affected enemies. Use only when your team keeps Electro applied reliably. Main stats: ATK% / Electro DMG / CRIT.

Main stats

  • Sands: ATK% / Elemental Mastery
  • Goblet: Electro DMG Bonus
  • Circlet: CRIT Rate / CRIT DMG

Stat targets

  • CRIT Rate: 60% - 75%
  • CRIT DMG: 140% - 200%
  • ATK: 1,700 - 2,200
  • Elemental Mastery: 100 - 250
  • Energy Recharge: 100% - 120%

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 9+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 8 - 9
  • Elemental Burst: Level 9+

Best teams for Keqing

  • Lightning Commission: Keqing (On-field DPS), Fischl (Off-field DPS / Battery), Nahida (Dendro Enabler / Buffer), Kaedehara Kazuha (Buffer / Debuffer). Nahida maintains Quicken, Fischl adds powerful off-field Aggravate damage, and Kazuha buffs Electro while reducing enemy resistance. No dedicated healer.
  • Moonlit Voltage: Keqing (On-field DPS), Ineffa (Off-field DPS / Shielder), Furina (Off-field DPS / Buffer), Xilonen (Healer / Debuffer). Ineffa enables Lunar-Charged and protects the team. Furina buffs damage while Xilonen heals and reduces resistance for Keqing.
  • Overload Authority: Keqing (On-field DPS), Chevreuse (Healer / Buffer), Fischl (Off-field DPS / Battery), Xiangling (Off-field DPS / Pyro Enabler). Chevreuse reduces Electro and Pyro resistance after Overload. Fischl helps power Xiangling, whose Burst supplies Pyro. Xiangling needs high ER.
  • Pika...chu?: Keqing (On-field DPS), Fischl (Off-field DPS / Battery), Yaoyao (Healer / Dendro Enabler), Sucrose (EM Buffer / Debuffer). Yaoyao supplies Dendro and healing, Fischl adds Electro damage, and Sucrose shares EM while reducing Electro resistance.

Pros

  • Fast Electro attacks
  • Flexible rotations
  • Strong Aggravate synergy
  • Low Energy needs

Cons

  • Charged Attacks use stamina
  • Can scatter light enemies
  • Weak early constellations
  • Needs good reaction support

Build variations

Aggravate Driver (On-field DPS)

Frequent Electro hits drive Aggravate with reduced Skill cooldown.

  • Weapons: Mistsplitter Reforged, Lion's Roar, The Black Sword
  • Artifacts: Thundering Fury (4 pcs); Gilded Dreams (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Balance CRIT and EM; ATK remains helpful.
  • Teams: Dendro to sustain Quicken; Fischl is a strong partner.
  • When it applies: Ensure stable Dendro application.

Electro-Charged (On-field DPS)

A non-Dendro alternative using sustained Electro application.

  • Weapons: Mistsplitter Reforged, The Black Sword
  • Artifacts: Thundering Fury (4 pcs); Thundersoother (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Prioritize CRIT and ATK; adjust EM for reaction contribution.
  • Teams: Off-field Hydro and Electro support.
  • When it applies: Team performance depends on buffs and reaction ownership.

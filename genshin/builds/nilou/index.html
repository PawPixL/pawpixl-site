Nilou Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams (7.2)

Nilou build guide for Genshin Impact 7.2: best weapons (Key of Khaj-Nisut, Primordial Jade Cutter).

Last updated: November 12, 2026 · Game version 7.2

Role: Support / Sub DPS

Nilou transforms ordinary Bloom into fast, powerful Bountiful Cores when her party uses only Hydro and Dendro. Stack HP to strengthen the whole team’s Bloom damage, and bring a healer for core self-damage. She can also support Lunar-Bloom teams, which require a different setup.

Best weapons for Nilou

  1. Key of Khaj-Nisut: Best overall. Huge HP increases Bountiful Core DMG, while its Skill-triggered passive shares EM.
  2. Primordial Jade Cutter: Its passive adds HP, but the CR secondary stat does not help Bountiful Core DMG. A usable premium option without Key.
  3. Freedom-Sworn: High EM helps Nilou-owned Blooms & its passive can buff teammates. Lacks HP%, so her Bountiful Core bonus will be lower.
  4. The Dockhand's Assistant: Excellent 4-star HP% sword.
  5. Xiphos' Moonlight: Provides EM for Nilou-owned Blooms and shares ER with the team. Still prioritize HP on artifacts.
  6. Serenity's Call: Great craftable HP option when Nilou triggers reactions. Its HP passive is stronger in teams with Ascendant Gleam. Needs a reaction to activate HP buff.
  7. Iron Sting: Accessible craftable sword with EM for Blooms Nilou triggers. Its damage passive does not boost Bountiful Cores.
  8. Sapwood Blade: F2P sword that can create an EM leaf for the teammate triggering Blooms. ER is convenient but not required for Nilou.

Best artifacts for Nilou

  • Tenacity of the Millelith + Vourukasha's Glow (2 + 2 pcs): Best general Bountiful Bloom setup. The combined 40% HP strengthens Nilou’s teamwide core damage bonus. Main stats: HP% / HP% / HP%.
  • Tenacity of the Millelith + Wanderer's Troupe (2 + 2 pcs): Alternative HP and EM mix when pieces have better substats. Prioritize HP until Nilou reaches her Bloom bonus cap. Main stats: HP% / HP% / HP%.
  • Silken Moon's Serenade (4 pcs): Use in Nefer Lunar-Bloom teams if nobody else carries it. Adds team EM and Lunar-Bloom buffs instead of stacking HP. Main stats: HP% / HP% / HP% (flexible).

Main stats

  • Sands: HP%
  • Goblet: HP%
  • Circlet: HP%

Stat targets

  • HP: Aim toward 74,444
  • HP: With Hydro Resonance/buffs
  • EM: After HP (if triggering)
  • ER: Optional for Burst

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 6–8
  • Elemental Burst: Level 1+

Best teams for Nilou

  • Lotus Bloom Royalty: Nilou (Bloom Buffer / Hydro Enabler), Sangonomiya Kokomi (Healer / Hydro Enabler), Lauma (Dendro Buffer / Enabler), Nahida (Dendro Enabler / Buffer). Kokomi drives Blooms and heals core self-DMG.
  • Garden of Deep Waters: Nilou (Bloom Buffer / Hydro Enabler), Xingqiu (Off-field DPS / Hydro Enabler), Nahida (Dendro Enabler / Buffer), Baizhu (Healer / Shielder). Nahida drives Blooms using Xingqiu’s Hydro. Baizhu keeps the team healthy.
  • Moonlit Lotus: Nilou (Bloom Buffer / Hydro Enabler), Nefer (On-field DPS), Lauma (Dendro Buffer / Enabler), Columbina (Off-field DPS / Buffer). Nilou applies Hydro for Nefer’s Lunar-Bloom DMG. Lauma and Columbina provide Lunar buffs.
  • Budget Flower Power: Nilou (Bloom Buffer / Hydro Enabler), Barbara (Healer), Traveler (Dendro) (Dendro Enabler / Battery), Collei (Dendro Enabler). Barbara heals and triggers Blooms on-field. Dendro Traveler and Collei apply Dendro.

Pros

  • Powerful Bountiful Cores
  • Excellent AoE DMG
  • Strong F2P teams
  • Low talent investment
  • Flexible Dendro partners

Cons

  • Hydro/Dendro team lock
  • Needs reliable healing
  • Less effective single-target
  • HP swords are limited

Build variations

Bountiful Bloom (Support)

Maximize team Bountiful Core damage through HP and Bloom support.

  • Weapons: Key of Khaj-Nisut
  • Stats: Prioritize HP to 74,445; add EM after.
  • Teams: Only Hydro and Dendro party members, with dependable healing.

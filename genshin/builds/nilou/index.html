Nilou Build Guide | Best Weapons, Artifacts & Teams

Nilou build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Key of Khaj-Nisut, Primordial Jade Cutter), artifacts (Tenacity of the Millelith + Vourukasha's Glow),…

Role: Support / Sub DPS

Nilou transforms ordinary Bloom into fast, powerful Bountiful Cores when her party uses only Hydro and Dendro. Stack HP to strengthen the whole team’s Bloom damage, and bring a healer for core self-damage. She can also support Lunar-Bloom teams, which require a different setup.

  1. Key of Khaj-Nisut
  2. Primordial Jade Cutter
  3. Freedom-Sworn
  4. The Dockhand's Assistant
  5. Xiphos' Moonlight
  6. Serenity's Call
  7. Iron Sting
  8. Sapwood Blade

Best artifacts

  • Tenacity of the Millelith + Wanderer's Troupe (2 + 2 pcs)
  • Silken Moon's Serenade (4 pcs)

Main stats

  • Sands: HP%
  • Goblet: HP%
  • Circlet: HP%

Stat targets

  • HP: Aim toward 74,444
  • HP: With Hydro Resonance/buffs
  • EM: After HP (if triggering)

Talent priority

  • Elemental Skill: Level 6–8
  • Elemental Burst: Level 1+

Best teams

  • Garden of Deep Waters: Nilou, Xingqiu, Nahida, Baizhu
  • Moonlit Lotus: Nilou, Nefer, Lauma, Columbina

Pros

  • Excellent AoE DMG

Cons

Arlecchino Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

Arlecchino build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Crimson Moon's Semblance, Primordial Jade Winged-Spear), artifacts (Fragment of Harmonic Whimsy).

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Role: DPS

Arlecchino is an on-field Pyro DPS built around Bond of Life and powerful Normal Attacks. She excels in reaction or raw-damage teams but requires careful survival management.

Best weapons for Arlecchino

  1. Crimson Moon's Semblance: CRIT Rate, extra Bond of Life and damage while Bond of Life is active.
  2. Primordial Jade Winged-Spear: Stacks ATK and DMG with hits; excellent for sustained combos.
  3. Staff of the Scarlet Sands: CRIT Rate and EM-based ATK suit Vaporize or Melt teams. Best in reaction teams with EM.
  4. Fractured Halo: Strong CRIT stat stick when its specialized passive is not active.
  5. Staff of Homa: CRIT DMG and an HP-based ATK bonus; still strong above half HP.
  6. Ballad of the Fjords: CRIT Rate and bonus EM improve Vaporize damage. Requires three distinct elements.
  7. Deathmatch: CRIT Rate plus a conditional ATK buff; reliable Battle Pass choice.
  8. White Tassel: Excellent free Normal Attack option at R5, especially with Bennett. R5 preferred.

Best artifacts for Arlecchino

  • Fragment of Harmonic Whimsy (4 pcs) · Main DPS: ATK and stacking DMG whenever Bond of Life changes; best general set. Main stats: ATK%/EM · Pyro DMG Bonus · CR/CD.
  • Echoes of an Offering (4 pcs) · Main DPS: Normal Attack alternative with latency-sensitive performance.
  • Night Of The Sky'S Unveiling (4 pcs) · Main DPS: Situational alternative in qualifying Ascendant Gleam teams.

Main stats

  • Sands: ATK% / EM
  • Goblet: Pyro DMG
  • Circlet: CR / CD

Stat targets

  • CR: 70–80%
  • CD: 160–200%+
  • ATK: 1,800–2,200+
  • EM: 100–150 (Vape)

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 9+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 6–8
  • Elemental Burst: Level 6+

Best teams for Arlecchino

  • Father Figures: Arlecchino (DPS), Nicole (Support), Fischl (Sub DPS), Mona (Nuke). Arlecchino drives Pyro damage; Fischl supplies Electro, Mona grants short Hydro/Omen windows, and Nicole buffs and shields.
  • Heated Rivalry: Arlecchino (DPS), Citlali (Buffs), Yelan (Sub DPS), Xingqiu (Sub DPS). Arlecchino drives Pyro damage, Citlali provides shielding and RES shred, Yelan and Xingqiu enable consistent Vaporize reactions and Hydro application.
  • Moonlighting: Arlecchino (DPS), Ineffa (Support), Columbina (Buffs), Yelan (Sub DPS). Arlecchino supplies Pyro damage while Ineffa and Columbina support Lunar-Charged interactions; Yelan adds Hydro and off-field damage. Reaction ownership matters.
  • Workers' Compensation: Arlecchino (DPS), Bennett (Support), Xingqiu (Sub DPS), Sucrose (EM Build). Arlecchino drives Pyro damage, Xingqiu enables Vaporize, Bennett boosts ATK, and Sucrose provides EM buffs and RES shred.

Pros

  • High single-target DMG
  • Strong Pyro output
  • Strong reaction teams
  • High damage ceiling

Cons

  • Limited outside healing
  • Needs protection
  • HP management
  • Burst consumes Bond

Build variations

Vaporize DPS (On-field DPS)

Use for reaction-driven damage when Hydro uptime supports regular Vaporize hits.

  • Weapons: Crimson Moon's Semblance, Staff of Homa, Ballad of the Fjords, White Tassel
  • Artifacts: Fragment of Harmonic Whimsy (4 pcs)
  • Stats: EM Sands can compete with ATK% when Vaporizing reliably; keep Pyro DMG Goblet and CRIT Circlet.
  • Teams: Reliable Hydro application such as Yelan or Xingqiu; a buffer or shielder helps.
  • When it applies: Ballad of the Fjords requires three distinct team elements.

Raw Pyro / Overload (On-field DPS)

Choose when focusing on strong Normal Attacks and team buffs rather than Vaporize scaling.

  • Weapons: Crimson Moon's Semblance, Primordial Jade Winged-Spear, Deathmatch, White Tassel
  • Artifacts: Fragment of Harmonic Whimsy (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Prefer ATK% Sands over EM when reaction damage is not the main payoff.
  • Teams: Pyro buffers or Chevreuse with only Pyro and Electro teammates.
  • When it applies: Chevreuse team restrictions apply for her full RES reduction.

Budget Normal Attacks (On-field DPS)

Use White Tassel as a low-cost option and prioritize a strong artifact set over expensive weapon upgrades.

  • Weapons: White Tassel, Deathmatch, Ballad of the Fjords
  • Artifacts: Fragment of Harmonic Whimsy (4 pcs); Echoes of an Offering (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Prioritize CRIT and ATK%; EM is useful only when Vaporizing consistently.
  • Teams: A team that supports uninterrupted Normal Attack strings.
  • When it applies: White Tassel at R5; Echoes is latency-sensitive.

Arlecchino talents

Normal Attack: Invitation to a Beheading

Normal Attack
Performs up to 6 consecutive spear strikes.

Charged Attack
Consumes a fixed amount of Stamina, dashing toward a nearby opponent and cleaving once.
Continuously holding this button will cause Arlecchino to consume Stamina and engage in up to 5s of high-speed movement.

Plunging Attack
Plunges from mid-air to strike the ground below, damaging opponents along the path and dealing AoE DMG upon impact.

Masque of the Red Death
When Arlecchino has a Bond of Life equal to or greater than 30% of her Max HP, she will enter the "Masque of the Red Death" state, where her Normal, Charged, and Plunging Attacks will be converted to deal Pyro DMG. This cannot be overridden.
When in the "Masque of the Red Death" state, Arlecchino's Normal Attacks will deal extra DMG to opponents on hit that scales off her ATK multiplied by a certain ratio of her current Bond of Life percentage. This will consume 7.5% of said current Bond of Life. Her Bond of Life can be consumed this way every 0.03s. When her Bond of Life is consumed in this manner, All Is Ash's CD will decrease by 0.8s.

Elemental Skill: All Is Ash

Cooldown: 30s

Summons forth Balemoon Bloodfire, dealing Pyro DMG to multiple nearby opponents and performing a dash-cleave against one of them, dealing AoE Pyro DMG.
Opponents hit by the aforementioned attack will have a Blood-Debt Directive applied to them.

Blood-Debt Directive
·Lasts 30s. Every 5s, it will deal 1 instance of Pyro DMG to the opponent. Max 2 instances. This DMG will be considered Elemental Skill DMG.
·When Arlecchino uses a Charged Attack or her Elemental Burst, Balemoon Rising, she will absorb and clear nearby Blood-Debt Directives. Each Directive absorbed grants her a Bond of Life worth 65% of her Max HP.
·The maximum value of the Bond of Life she can be granted through Blood-Debt Directives within 35s after using her Elemental Skill is 145% of her Max HP. Using the Elemental Skill again during this duration will restart the count on duration and the limit on the value of Bond of Life she may gain from Blood-Debt Directives.

Elemental Burst: Balemoon Rising

Cooldown: 15s · Energy cost: 60

Arlecchino's great wing of Balemoon Bloodfire beats as she absorbs and clears Blood-Debt Directives around her. She deals AoE Pyro DMG before clearing the CD of All Is Ash and healing herself. The healing is based on her Bond of Life value and ATK.

Passive talent: Agony Alone May Be Repaid

Blood-Debt Directives have the following characteristics:
·Arlecchino will be granted a Bond of Life worth 130% of her Max HP when an opponent to which she herself applied a Directive is defeated.
·5s after a Directive is applied, it will be upgraded to a Blood-Debt Due. When absorbed, it will instead grant Arlecchino a Bond of Life worth 130%.
A Bond of Life created in the aforementioned ways cannot exceed the original limit on the value of Bonds of Life obtained through All Is Ash.

Passive talent: Strength Alone Can Defend

Arlecchino gains 1% All Elemental and Physical RES for every 100 ATK she has in excess of 1,000. The maximum RES increase she can gain this way for each is 20%.

Passive talent: The Balemoon Alone May Know

While in combat, Arlecchino gains a 40% Pyro DMG Bonus and can only be healed through Balemoon Rising.

Arlecchino constellations

C1: "All Reprisals and Arrears, Mine to Bear..."

Masque of the Red Death is further enhanced, the value of the increase is 100%. Additionally, Arlecchino's interruption resistance is increased when she performs Normal or Charged Attacks while affected by the Masque of the Red Death.

C2: "All Rewards and Retribution, Mine to Bestow..."

Blood-Debt Directives are now already Blood-Debt Due when first applied.
When Arlecchino absorbs such a Due, she unleashes Balemoon Bloodfire in front of her, dealing 900% of her ATK as AoE Pyro DMG and increasing her All Elemental RES and Physical RES by 20% for 15s. This effect can trigger once every 10s.
You must first unlock the Ascension Talent "Agony Alone May Be Repaid."

C3: "You Shall Become a New Member of Our Family..."

Increases the Level of Normal Attack: Invitation to a Beheading by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C4: "You Shall Love and Protect Each Other Henceforth..."

When Arlecchino successfully absorbs a Blood-Debt Directive, Balemoon Rising's CD will decrease by 2s and 15 Elemental Energy will be restored to her. This effect can occur once every 10s.

C5: "For Alone, We Are as Good as Dead..."

Increases the Level of Balemoon Rising by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C6: "From This Day On, We Shall Delight in New Life Together."

The DMG of Balemoon Rising is increased by Arlecchino's ATK multiplied by 700% of Arlecchino's current Bond of Life percentage.
For 20s after Arlecchino uses All Is Ash, both her Normal Attacks and Elemental Burst gain 10% increased CRIT Rate and 70% increased CRIT DMG. This effect can be triggered up to once every 15s.

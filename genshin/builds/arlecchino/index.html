Arlecchino Build Guide | Best Weapons, Artifacts & Teams

Arlecchino build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Crimson Moon's Semblance, Primordial Jade Winged-Spear), artifacts (Fragment of Harmonic Whimsy),…

Role: DPS

Arlecchino is an on-field Pyro DPS built around Bond of Life and powerful Normal Attacks. She excels in reaction or raw-damage teams but requires careful survival management.

Best weapons for Arlecchino

  1. Crimson Moon's Semblance
  2. Primordial Jade Winged-Spear
  3. Staff of the Scarlet Sands
  4. Fractured Halo
  5. Staff of Homa
  6. Ballad of the Fjords
  7. Deathmatch
  8. White Tassel

Best artifacts

  • Fragment of Harmonic Whimsy (4 pcs) · Main DPS
  • Echoes of an Offering (4 pcs) · Main DPS
  • Night Of The Sky'S Unveiling (4 pcs) · Main DPS

Main stats

  • Sands: ATK% / EM
  • Goblet: Pyro DMG
  • Circlet: CR / CD

Stat targets

  • CR: 70–80%
  • CD: 160–200%+
  • ATK: 1,800–2,200+
  • EM: 100–150 (Vape)

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 9+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 6–8
  • Elemental Burst: Level 6+

Best teams

  • Father Figures: Arlecchino, Nicole, Fischl, Mona
  • Heated Rivalry: Arlecchino, Citlali, Yelan, Xingqiu
  • Moonlighting: Arlecchino, Ineffa, Columbina, Yelan
  • Workers' Compensation: Arlecchino, Bennett, Xingqiu, Sucrose

Pros

  • High single-target DMG
  • Strong Pyro output
  • Strong reaction teams
  • High damage ceiling

Cons

  • Limited outside healing
  • Needs protection
  • HP management
  • Burst consumes Bond

Build variations

  • Vaporize DPS (On-field DPS): Use for reaction-driven damage when Hydro uptime supports regular Vaporize hits.
  • Raw Pyro / Overload (On-field DPS): Choose when focusing on strong Normal Attacks and team buffs rather than Vaporize scaling.
  • Budget Normal Attacks (On-field DPS): Use White Tassel as a low-cost option and prioritize a strong artifact set over expensive weapon upgrades.

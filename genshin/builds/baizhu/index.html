Role: Support

Baizhu is a Dendro healer who provides strong team-wide healing, interruption resistance, and Dendro application. He offers valuable defensive utility while supporting Dendro reactions.

Best weapons for Baizhu

  1. Jadefall's Splendor
  2. Prototype Amber
  3. A Thousand Floating Dreams
  4. Everlasting Moonglow
  5. Favonius Codex
  6. Sacrificial Fragments
  7. Hakushin Ring
  8. Thrilling Tales of Dragon Slayers

Best artifacts

  • Deepwood Memories (4 pcs) · Best Support
  • Song of Days Past (4 pcs) · Support
  • Ocean-Hued Clam (4 pcs) · Support

Main stats

  • Sands: HP% / ER
  • Goblet: HP%
  • Circlet: HP% / Healing

Stat targets

  • HP: Up to 50K
  • ER: Team-based
  • CR: Favonius only

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 9+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 9+

Best teams

  • Nefer Nation: Baizhu, Nefer, Columbina, Lauma
  • Dendro Dominance: Baizhu, Alhaitham, Yae Miko, Fischl
  • Nilou's Garden: Baizhu, Kamisato Ayato, Nilou, Nahida
  • Bloom & Doom: Baizhu, Kaveh, Xingqiu, Nilou

Pros

  • Strong team healing
  • Dendro reaction buffs
  • Teamwide sustain
  • Flexible support

Cons

  • Fragile shields
  • Limited Dendro aura
  • High ER needs
  • Low personal DMG

Build variations

  • Deepwood Dendro Support (Healer / Support): Use Baizhu to heal and shred Dendro RES, enabling more damage from Dendro and Bloom-based teams.
  • Alternate Healing / Buffs (Healer / Support): Choose Song of Days Past for healing-based buffs or Clam for modest extra bubble damage.
  • On-field Dendro Driver (On-field Driver): Use Normal Attacks to apply Dendro for Bloom or Hyperbloom while maintaining team healing.

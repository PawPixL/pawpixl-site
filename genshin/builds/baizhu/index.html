Baizhu Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

Baizhu build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Jadefall's Splendor, Prototype Amber), artifacts (Deepwood Memories), main stats, talent priority.

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Role: Support

Baizhu is a Dendro healer who provides strong team-wide healing, interruption resistance, and Dendro application. He offers valuable defensive utility while supporting Dendro reactions.

Best weapons for Baizhu

  1. Jadefall's Splendor: HP% supports healing; passive refunds Energy for Burst uptime.
  2. Prototype Amber: Craftable HP%, team healing and Energy refund after Burst.
  3. A Thousand Floating Dreams: Supports EM-focused teammates, but only if Baizhu already meets HP and ER needs. Use for EM-focused teams.
  4. Everlasting Moonglow: HP% boosts healing, but its on-field passive offers little support value.
  5. Favonius Codex: ER and team particles on CRIT; needs enough CRIT Rate.
  6. Sacrificial Fragments: Extra Skill casts and EM can help in Bloom teams. Mainly Bloom or driver teams. R3+ preferred.
  7. Hakushin Ring: Short Electro and Dendro damage buff after a reaction. Baizhu must trigger reaction on field.
  8. Thrilling Tales of Dragon Slayers: HP% and a strong ATK buff for the next teammate. ATK-scaling carry; meet ER needs.

Best artifacts for Baizhu

  • Deepwood Memories (4 pcs) · Best Support: Reduces Dendro RES by 30%. Use when another teammate does not already carry it. Main stats: HP%/ER · HP% · HP%.
  • Song of Days Past (4 pcs) · Support: Converts healing into a limited damage buff; most useful in single-target fights. Main stats: HP%/ER · HP% · HP%.
  • Ocean-Hued Clam (4 pcs) · Support: Converts healing to Physical AoE damage; alternative support set. Main stats: HP%/ER · HP% · HP%.

Main stats

  • Sands: HP% / ER
  • Goblet: HP%
  • Circlet: HP% / Healing

Stat targets

  • HP: Up to 50K
  • ER: Team-based
  • CR: Favonius only

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 9+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 9+

Best teams for Baizhu

  • Nefer Nation: Baizhu (Healer / Shielder), Nefer (On-field DPS), Columbina (Off-field DPS / Buffer), Lauma (Dendro Buffer / Enabler). Nefer leads with powerful Lunar-Bloom damage, while Lauma and Columbina provide reaction support and Hydro application. Baizhu keeps the team healthy.
  • Dendro Dominance: Baizhu (Healer / Shielder), Alhaitham (On-field DPS), Yae Miko (Off-field DPS), Fischl (Off-field DPS / Battery). Alhaitham drives Spread damage while Yae and Fischl provide consistent Electro application and off-field damage.
  • Nilou's Garden: Baizhu (Healer / Shielder), Kamisato Ayato (On-field DPS), Nilou (Bloom Buffer / Hydro Enabler), Nahida (Dendro Enabler / Buffer). Nilou transforms Bloom reactions into devastating Bountiful Cores.
  • Bloom & Doom: Baizhu (Healer / Shielder), Kaveh (On-field Driver), Xingqiu (Off-field DPS / Hydro Enabler), Nilou (Bloom Buffer / Hydro Enabler). Nilou enables powerful Bountiful Core reactions, while Kaveh acts as the on-field Dendro driver.

Build variations

Deepwood Dendro Support (Healer / Support)

Use Baizhu to heal and shred Dendro RES, enabling more damage from Dendro and Bloom-based teams.

  • Weapons: Jadefall's Splendor, Prototype Amber, Favonius Codex
  • Artifacts: Deepwood Memories (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Meet ER needs, then build HP% toward 50,000. A CRIT Circlet is optional with Favonius.
  • Teams: Dendro damage teams without another reliable Deepwood holder.
  • When it applies: Deepwood is less valuable if another teammate maintains its RES shred.

Alternate Healing / Buffs (Healer / Support)

Choose Song of Days Past for healing-based buffs or Clam for modest extra bubble damage.

  • Weapons: Prototype Amber, Jadefall's Splendor, Favonius Codex
  • Artifacts: Song of Days Past (4 pcs); Ocean-Hued Clam (4 pcs)
  • Stats: HP% and ER stay the priority; Healing Bonus is an optional comfort choice.
  • Teams: A teammate already carries Deepwood, or Dendro RES shred is not needed.
  • When it applies: Prioritize support uptime over personal damage.

On-field Dendro Driver (On-field Driver)

Use Normal Attacks to apply Dendro for Bloom or Hyperbloom while maintaining team healing.

  • Weapons: Prototype Amber, Sacrificial Fragments, Favonius Codex
  • Artifacts: Deepwood Memories (4 pcs)
  • Stats: EM can help only when Baizhu owns Bloom reactions; otherwise HP and ER usually remain more useful.
  • Teams: Hydro and/or Electro units for Dendro reactions; reliable healing.
  • When it applies: Hyperbloom damage scales with the Electro trigger’s EM, not Baizhu’s.

Baizhu talents

Normal Attack: The Classics of Acupuncture

Normal Attack
Performs up to 4 attacks that deal Dendro DMG to opponents in front of him.

Charged Attack
Consumes a certain amount of Stamina to deal AoE Dendro DMG to opponents in front of him after a short casting time.

Plunging Attack
Calling upon the might of Dendro, Baizhu plunges towards the ground from mid-air, damaging all opponents in his path. Deals AoE Dendro DMG upon impact with the ground.

Elemental Skill: Universal Diagnosis

Cooldown: 10s

Controls a Gossamer Sprite that cruises and attacks nearby opponents, dealing Dendro DMG.
After it performs 3 attacks or if there are no opponents nearby, the Sprite will return, healing all nearby party members based on Baizhu's Max HP.

Elemental Burst: Holistic Revivification

Cooldown: 20s · Energy cost: 80

Enters the Pulsing Clarity state, creating a Seamless Shield that absorbs Dendro DMG with 250% effectiveness.
While in this state, Baizhu will generate a new Seamless Shield every 2.5s.

The Seamless Shield will heal your own active character based on Baizhu's Max HP and attack opponents by unleashing Spiritveins, dealing Dendro DMG under the following circumstances:
·When a character is under the protection of a Seamless Shield and a new Seamless Shield is generated.
·When the Seamless Shield's effects expire, or when it is shattered.

Passive talent: Five Fortunes Forever

Baizhu gains different effects according to the current HP of your current active character:
·When their HP is less than 50%, Baizhu gains 20% Healing Bonus.
·When their HP is equal to or more than 50%, Baizhu gains 25% Dendro DMG Bonus.

Passive talent: All Things Are of the Earth

Characters who are healed by Seamless Shields will gain the Year of Verdant Favor effect: Each 1,000 Max HP that Baizhu possesses that does not exceed 50,000 will increase the Burning, Bloom, Hyperbloom, and Burgeon reaction DMG dealt by these characters by 2%, their Lunar-Bloom DMG by 0.7%, and the DMG Bonus provided by Aggravate and Spread by 0.8%. This effect lasts 6s.

Passive talent: Herbal Nourishment

When Baizhu is in the party, interacting with certain harvestable items will heal your current active character for 2.5% of Baizhu's Max HP.

Baizhu constellations

C1: Attentive Observation

Universal Diagnosis gains 1 additional charge.

C2: Incisive Discernment

When your own active character hits a nearby opponent with their attacks, Baizhu will unleash a Gossamer Sprite: Splice.
Gossamer Sprite: Splice will initiate 1 attack before returning, dealing 250% of Baizhu's ATK as Dendro DMG and healing for 20% of Universal Diagnosis's Gossamer Sprite's normal healing.
DMG dealt this way is considered Elemental Skill DMG.
This effect can be triggered once every 5s.

C3: All Aspects Stabilized

Increases the Level of Holistic Revivification by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C4: Ancient Art of Perception

For 15s after Holistic Revivification is used, Baizhu will increase all nearby party members' Elemental Mastery by 80.

C5: The Hidden Ebb and Flow

Increases the Level of Universal Diagnosis by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C6: Elimination of Malicious Qi

Increases the DMG dealt by Holistic Revivification's Spiritveins by 8% of Baizhu's Max HP.
Additionally, when a Gossamer Sprite or Gossamer Sprite: Splice hits opponents, there is a 100% chance of generating one of Holistic Revivification's Seamless Shields. This effect can only be triggered once by each Gossamer Sprite or Gossamer Sprite: Splice.

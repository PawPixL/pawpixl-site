Zhongli build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Black Tassel, Favonius Lance), artifacts (Tenacity of the Millelith), main stats (HP% / HP% / HP% /…

Role: Shield Support / Sub DPS

Zhongli protects the team with a durable Jade Shield that also reduces nearby enemies' Elemental and Physical RES by 20%. Stack HP for shield strength, or use Favonius Lance for team energy. His Burst offers Petrification, but may cost more time than it is worth in damage-focused rotations.

Main stats

Stat targets

  • HP: 40,000 - 55,000+ (Shield focus)
  • CRIT Rate: 30% - 50% (Favonius only)
  • Energy Recharge: 130% - 160%+ (Burst teams)

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 8-9+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 1 - 6

Best teams

  • Unbreakable Verdict: Zhongli, Neuvillette, Furina, Kaedehara Kazuha
  • Golden Contract: Zhongli, Navia, Fischl, Bennett
  • Pyro-Proof Plan: Zhongli, Arlecchino, Yelan, Bennett
  • Forest Fortress: Zhongli, Tighnari, Yae Miko, Nahida

Pros

  • Excellent shield uptime
  • Universal RES reduction
  • Flexible team slot
  • Comfortable rotations

Cons

  • Low personal DPS
  • Stele placement matters
  • Burst can slow rotations
  • Limited reaction utility

Build variations

  • Favonius Team Battery (Shield Support): Choose when giving allies particles matters more than maximum shield HP.
  • Meteor Burst Support (Burst Sub DPS): For Petrification and extra Geo Burst damage when sacrificing some shield strength is acceptable.
  • Physical Driver (On-field Physical DPS): A fun off-meta playstyle built around fast Normal Attacks and Crescent Pike procs.
  • Dendro RES Shred (Shield Support): Provides Dendro RES reduction while protecting a Dendro damage or reaction team.

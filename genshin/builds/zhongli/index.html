Zhongli Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

Zhongli build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Black Tassel, Favonius Lance), artifacts (Tenacity of the Millelith), main stats, talent priority.

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Role: Shield Support / Sub DPS

Zhongli protects the team with a durable Jade Shield that also reduces nearby enemies' Elemental and Physical RES by 20%. Stack HP for shield strength, or use Favonius Lance for team energy. His Burst offers Petrification, but may cost more time than it is worth in damage-focused rotations.

Best weapons for Zhongli

  1. Black Tassel: Large HP% secondary stat maximizes Jade Shield durability. The slime-damage passive is irrelevant. Best for maximum shield strength.
  2. Favonius Lance: Energy Recharge and on-field CRIT hits generate neutral particles for the team. Build enough CRIT Rate to trigger it. Use when allies need energy; passive cannot trigger off-field.
  3. Staff of Homa: CRIT DMG, bonus HP and HP-to-ATK conversion make it a strong hybrid or Burst-damage option. Burst or hybrid investment; not needed for pure shielding.
  4. Skyward Spine: Energy Recharge and a little CRIT Rate can help a Burst-focused Zhongli cast more frequently. Burst-focused playstyle.
  5. Vortex Vanquisher: His signature grants shield strength to its wielder and stacking ATK, but neither notably improves shields given to allies. On-field niche only; not recommended for shieldbot.
  6. "The Catch": Energy Recharge plus Burst DMG and Burst CRIT Rate supports an affordable meteor-focused setup. Burst damage build. R5 recommended.
  7. Crescent Pike: Physical DMG and extra Normal Attack hits enable a niche on-field Physical Zhongli. Physical driver only; requires particle pickup.
  8. Prototype Starglitter: Craftable Energy Recharge option when Favonius is unavailable; its Normal/Charged Attack passive does little for shield support. Temporary ER option only.

Best artifacts for Zhongli

  • Tenacity of the Millelith (4 pcs) · General support: HP and team ATK buff when Skill hits land. Main stats: HP% · HP% · HP% / CRIT Rate.
  • Archaic Petra (4 pcs): Grants a strong elemental DMG buff after Zhongli personally collects the matching Crystallize shard. Main stats: HP% · HP% · HP% / CRIT Rate.
  • Deepwood Memories (4 pcs): Situational Dendro RES shred for teams lacking another Deepwood holder; Skill hit coverage can be inconsistent. Main stats: HP% · HP% · HP% / CRIT Rate.

Main stats

  • Sands: HP%
  • Goblet: HP%
  • Circlet: HP% / CRIT Rate (Favonius)

Stat targets

  • HP: 40,000 - 55,000+ (Shield focus)
  • CRIT Rate: 30% - 50% (Favonius only)
  • Energy Recharge: 130% - 160%+ (Burst teams)

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 8-9+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 1 - 6

Best teams for Zhongli

  • Unbreakable Verdict: Zhongli (Shield / RES Shred), Neuvillette (Main DPS), Furina (Sub DPS / Buff), Kaedehara Kazuha (Anemo Buff). Zhongli protects C0 Neuvillette from interruption and shreds RES; Furina buffs DMG.
  • Golden Contract: Zhongli (Shield / RES Shred), Navia (Main DPS), Fischl (Sub DPS), Bennett (Healer / Buff). Geo Resonance and Zhongli’s shield help Navia attack safely.
  • Pyro-Proof Plan: Zhongli (Shield / RES Shred), Arlecchino (Main DPS), Yelan (Hydro Sub DPS), Bennett (ATK Buff). Zhongli shields Arlecchino through her combos and shreds RES.
  • Forest Fortress: Zhongli (Shield / RES Shred), Tighnari (Dendro DPS), Yae Miko (Electro Sub DPS), Nahida (Dendro Support). Zhongli safeguards Tighnari’s charged shots; Yae keeps Quicken active and Nahida boosts Dendro DMG.

Build variations

Favonius Team Battery (Shield Support)

Choose when giving allies particles matters more than maximum shield HP.

  • Weapons: Favonius Lance, Black Tassel
  • Artifacts: Tenacity of the Millelith (4 pcs); Archaic Petra (4 pcs)
  • Stats: HP% main stats; use a CRIT Rate circlet or substats to proc Favonius reliably.
  • Teams: Teammates with meaningful Energy Recharge needs.
  • When it applies: Favonius procs only from on-field CRIT hits.
  • Notes: Add a Normal Attack after Hold Skill if needed for a reliable proc.

Meteor Burst Support (Burst Sub DPS)

For Petrification and extra Geo Burst damage when sacrificing some shield strength is acceptable.

  • Weapons: Staff of Homa, "The Catch", Skyward Spine
  • Artifacts: Emblem of Severed Fate (4 pcs); Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs)
  • Stats: HP%/ATK% sands, Geo DMG goblet, CRIT circlet; gain sufficient ER for chosen rotation.
  • Teams: Prefer teams with a window for his long Burst animation.
  • When it applies: Higher investment; often less team DPS than skipping Burst.
  • Notes: Focus Burst and Skill talents; use an HP sands for a sturdier hybrid shield.

Physical Driver (On-field Physical DPS)

A fun off-meta playstyle built around fast Normal Attacks and Crescent Pike procs.

  • Weapons: Crescent Pike
  • Artifacts: Pale Flame (4 pcs); Bloodstained Chivalry + Pale Flame (2 + 2 pcs)
  • Stats: ATK% sands, Physical DMG goblet, CRIT circlet; prioritize Normal Attack talent.
  • Teams: Superconduct support and consistent particle pickups.
  • When it applies: Niche; weaker personal damage than most dedicated carries.
  • Notes: Keep the shield up and use Cryo + Electro teammates for Physical RES reduction.

Dendro RES Shred (Shield Support)

Provides Dendro RES reduction while protecting a Dendro damage or reaction team.

  • Weapons: Favonius Lance, Black Tassel
  • Artifacts: Deepwood Memories (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Keep HP% main stats, adding CRIT Rate if using Favonius.
  • Teams: Only use when no other teammate already carries 4-piece Deepwood.
  • When it applies: Stele placement limits repeated hits.
  • Notes: Deepwood on Nahida or another reliable Dendro applier is typically easier to maintain.

qiqi Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams (7.2)

qiqi build guide for Genshin Impact 7.2: best weapons (Skyward Blade, Primordial Jade Cutter), artifacts (Tenacity of the Millelith), main stats.

Last updated: November 12, 2026 · Game version 7.2

Role: Support

Qiqi is a Cryo healer who provides consistent healing, Cryo application, and Stellar-Conduct support.

Best weapons for qiqi

  1. Skyward Blade: Provides Energy Recharge and CRIT Rate. Using her Burst also grants movement speed, attack speed, and additional Normal Attack damage.
  2. Primordial Jade Cutter: Provides a large CRIT Rate bonus and bonus ATK based on maximum HP. More useful for damage-oriented Qiqi builds than pure healing.
  3. Summit Shaper: ATK% and a stackable ATK bonus. Shielding enhances its passive.
  4. The Flute: ATK% sword with Normal/Charged Attack hit stacks; modest support value.
  5. Favonius Sword: Generates team Energy particles on CRIT hits; needs some CRIT Rate.
  6. Festering Desire: Energy Recharge with Skill damage and Skill CRIT Rate bonuses; past-event weapon.
  7. Amenoma Kageuchi: Its ATK% stat supports healing, while Succession Seeds restore Energy after using her Burst.
  8. Skyrider Sword: Low-rarity Energy Recharge option; Burst grants temporary movement and ATK SPD.

Best artifacts for qiqi

  • Tenacity of the Millelith (4 pcs) · Best Overall: Skill hits can grant a team ATK buff, but uptime varies with Herald rotation and range. Main stats: ATK%/ER · ATK% · Healing/ATK%.
  • Ocean-Hued Clam (4 pcs) · Support Alternative: Converts Qiqi's massive healing into additional physical damage. Main stats: ATK%/ER · ATK% · Healing/ATK%.
  • Song of Days Past (4 pcs) · Support: Turns healing into extra damage for the active character. Team damage option when Clam or Tenacity is less useful. Main stats: ATK%/ER · ATK% · Healing.

Main stats

  • Sands: ATK% / Energy Recharge
  • Goblet: ATK%
  • Circlet: Healing Bonus / ATK% / Crit Rate

Stat targets

  • ATK: 2,300+
  • Energy Recharge: 180% - 200%
  • Crit Rate: 50%+

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 9+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 9+

Best teams for qiqi

  • Weird Science: qiqi (Healer), Mitya (Off-field DPS / Enabler), Yae Miko (Off-field DPS), Sucrose (EM Buffer / Debuffer). Qiqi enables Stellar-Conduct and heals, Yae provides off-field Electro damage, and Sucrose shares EM and shreds resistance.
  • Dolly Pardon?: qiqi (Healer), Sandrone (On-field DPS), Odette (Cryo Enabler / Buffer), Mitya (Off-field DPS / Enabler). Sandrone leads Stellar-Conduct damage while Mitya and Odette provide reaction support, buffs, and additional damage.
  • Night Shift: qiqi (Healer), Cyno (On-field DPS), Odette (Cryo Enabler / Buffer), Traveler (Cryo) (Cryo Enabler / Sub DPS). Cyno drives damage while Cryo Traveler and Odette enable Stellar-Conduct and Qiqi provides healing and buffs.
  • Watt the Fischl: qiqi (Healer), Fischl (Off-field DPS / Battery), Xingqiu (Off-field DPS / Hydro Enabler), Beidou (Off-field DPS). Qiqi drives on-field attacks while Xingqiu, Fischl, and Beidou unleash coordinated damage and Electro-Charged reactions.

Pros

  • Massive Healing
  • Strong Sustain
  • Cryo Application
  • Team Utility

Cons

  • High Energy Needs
  • Limited Battery
  • Low Damage

Build variations

Clam Healing Damage (Healer)

Convert large healing output into Ocean-Hued Clam damage.

  • Weapons: Skyward Blade, Favonius Sword
  • Artifacts: Ocean-Hued Clam (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ATK% and Healing Bonus; ER helps Burst uptime.
  • Teams: Team needing high sustained healing.
  • When it applies: Clam bubble damage is capped and physical.

Tenacity Support (Healer / Support)

Provide a team ATK buff while healing.

  • Weapons: Favonius Sword, Skyward Blade
  • Artifacts: Tenacity of the Millelith (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ATK% and ER; balance healing.
  • Teams: ATK-scaling carry; Skill must hit enemies.
  • When it applies: Tenacity uptime varies with Skill contact.

Song of Days Past (Healer / Support)

Turn healing into extra flat damage for the on-field attacker.

  • Weapons: Favonius Sword, Skyward Blade
  • Artifacts: Song of Days Past (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Prioritize healing output and enough ER.
  • Teams: Team where Noblesse or Tenacity is already covered.
  • When it applies: Buff size depends on accumulated healing.

qiqi Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

qiqi build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Skyward Blade, Primordial Jade Cutter), artifacts (Tenacity of the Millelith), main stats.

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Role: Support

Qiqi is a Cryo healer who provides consistent healing, Cryo application, and Stellar-Conduct support.

Best weapons for qiqi

  1. Skyward Blade: Provides Energy Recharge and CRIT Rate. Using her Burst also grants movement speed, attack speed, and additional Normal Attack damage.
  2. Primordial Jade Cutter: Provides a large CRIT Rate bonus and bonus ATK based on maximum HP. More useful for damage-oriented Qiqi builds than pure healing.
  3. Summit Shaper: ATK% and a stackable ATK bonus. Shielding enhances its passive.
  4. The Flute: ATK% sword with Normal/Charged Attack hit stacks; modest support value.
  5. Favonius Sword: Generates team Energy particles on CRIT hits; needs some CRIT Rate.
  6. Festering Desire: Energy Recharge with Skill damage and Skill CRIT Rate bonuses; past-event weapon.
  7. Amenoma Kageuchi: Its ATK% stat supports healing, while Succession Seeds restore Energy after using her Burst.
  8. Skyrider Sword: Low-rarity Energy Recharge option; Burst grants temporary movement and ATK SPD.

Best artifacts for qiqi

  • Tenacity of the Millelith (4 pcs) · Best Overall: Skill hits can grant a team ATK buff, but uptime varies with Herald rotation and range. Main stats: ATK%/ER · ATK% · Healing/ATK%.
  • Ocean-Hued Clam (4 pcs) · Support Alternative: Converts Qiqi's massive healing into additional physical damage. Main stats: ATK%/ER · ATK% · Healing/ATK%.
  • Song of Days Past (4 pcs) · Support: Turns healing into extra damage for the active character. Team damage option when Clam or Tenacity is less useful. Main stats: ATK%/ER · ATK% · Healing.

Main stats

  • Sands: ATK% / Energy Recharge
  • Goblet: ATK%
  • Circlet: Healing Bonus / ATK% / Crit Rate

Stat targets

  • ATK: 2,300+
  • Energy Recharge: 180% - 200%
  • Crit Rate: 50%+

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 9+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 9+

Best teams for qiqi

  • Weird Science: qiqi, Mitya, Yae Miko, Sucrose. Qiqi enables Stellar-Conduct and heals, Yae provides off-field Electro damage, and Sucrose shares EM and shreds resistance.
  • Dolly Pardon?: qiqi, Sandrone, Odette, Mitya. Sandrone leads Stellar-Conduct damage while Mitya and Odette provide reaction support, buffs, and additional damage.
  • Night Shift: qiqi, Cyno, Odette, Traveler (Cryo). Cyno drives damage while Cryo Traveler and Odette enable Stellar-Conduct and Qiqi provides healing and buffs.
  • Watt the Fischl: qiqi, Fischl, Xingqiu, Beidou. Qiqi drives on-field attacks while Xingqiu, Fischl, and Beidou unleash coordinated damage and Electro-Charged reactions.

Pros

  • Strong Sustain
  • Cryo Application
  • Team Utility

Cons

  • High Energy Needs
  • Limited Battery
  • Low Damage

Build variations

Clam Healing Damage (Healer)

Convert large healing output into Ocean-Hued Clam damage.

  • Weapons: Skyward Blade, Favonius Sword
  • Artifacts: Ocean-Hued Clam (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ATK% and Healing Bonus; ER helps Burst uptime.
  • Teams: Team needing high sustained healing.
  • When it applies: Clam bubble damage is capped and physical.

Tenacity Support (Healer / Support)

Provide a team ATK buff while healing.

  • Weapons: Favonius Sword, Skyward Blade
  • Artifacts: Tenacity of the Millelith (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ATK% and ER; balance healing.
  • Teams: ATK-scaling carry; Skill must hit enemies.
  • When it applies: Tenacity uptime varies with Skill contact.

Song of Days Past (Healer / Support)

Turn healing into extra flat damage for the on-field attacker.

  • Weapons: Favonius Sword, Skyward Blade
  • Artifacts: Song of Days Past (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Prioritize healing output and enough ER.
  • Teams: Team where Noblesse or Tenacity is already covered.
  • When it applies: Buff size depends on accumulated healing.

qiqi talents

Normal Attack: Ancient Sword Art

Normal Attack
Performs up to 5 rapid strikes.

Charged Attack
Consumes a certain amount of Stamina to unleash 2 rapid sword strikes.

Plunging Attack
Plunges from mid-air to strike the ground below, damaging opponents along the path and dealing AoE DMG upon impact.

Elemental Skill: Adeptus Art: Herald of Frost

Cooldown: 30s / Herald of Frost Coordinated Attack: 2.2s / 15s

Using the Icevein Talisman, Qiqi brings forth the Herald of Frost, dealing Cryo DMG to surrounding opponents.

Herald of Frost
·On hit, Qiqi's Normal and Charged Attacks regenerate HP for your own party members and nearby teammates. Healing scales based on Qiqi's ATK.
·Periodically regenerates your active character's HP.
·Follows the character around, dealing Cryo DMG to opponents in their path.

Elemental Burst: Adeptus Art: Preserver of Fortune

Cooldown: 20s · Energy cost: 80

Qiqi releases the adeptus power sealed within her body, marking nearby opponents with a Fortune-Preserving Talisman that deals Cryo DMG.

Fortune-Preserving Talisman
When opponents affected by this Talisman take DMG, the character that dealt this DMG regenerates HP.

Passive talent: Life-Prolonging Methods

When a character under the effects of Adeptus Art: Herald of Frost triggers an Elemental Reaction, their Incoming Healing Bonus is increased by 20% for 8s.

Passive talent: A Glimpse Into Arcanum

When Qiqi hits opponents with her Normal and Charged Attacks, she has a 50% chance to apply a Fortune-Preserving Talisman to them for 6s. This effect can only occur once every 30s.

Passive talent: Former Life Memories

Displays the location of nearby resources unique to Liyue on the mini-map.

qiqi constellations

C1: Ascetics of Frost

When the Herald of Frost hits an opponent marked by a Fortune-Preserving Talisman, Qiqi regenerates 2 Energy.

C2: Frozen to the Bone

Qiqi's Normal and Charged Attack DMG against opponents affected by Cryo is increased by 15%.

C3: Ascendant Praise

Increases the Level of Adeptus Art: Preserver of Fortune by 3.

C4: Divine Suppression

C5: Crimson Lotus Bloom

Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C6: Rite of Resurrection

This effect can only occur once every 15 mins.

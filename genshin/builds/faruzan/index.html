Faruzan Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

Faruzan build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Elegy for the End, Favonius Warbow), artifacts (Tenacity of the Millelith), main stats.

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Role: Support

Faruzan is an Anemo support who shreds resistance, boosts Anemo damage, and offers a new Stellar support niche, expanding her usefulness beyond traditional Anemo teams.

Best weapons for Faruzan

  1. Elegy for the End: Provides Energy Recharge, teamwide ATK and Elemental Mastery buffs.
  2. Favonius Warbow: Provides Energy Recharge and generates particles for her burst.
  3. Breezeborne Refrain: Provides Energy Recharge and boosts Stellar dmg.
  4. Skyward Harp: Provides CRIT Rate and CRIT DMG for DPS focused builds.
  5. End of the Line: Provides Energy Recharge and deals extra area damage after using her Skill. Mostly a fallback when better support bows are unavailable.
  6. Fading Twilight: Energy Recharge and a rotating damage bonus. A usable off-field option, though the buff level varies.
  7. Sacrificial Bow: Energy Recharge and a chance to reset Skill cooldown after Skill damage. More useful for extra casts than raw damage.

Best artifacts for Faruzan

  • Tenacity of the Millelith (4 pcs) · C6 Support: At C6, repeated Skill hits maintain a team ATK buff. Not recommended before C6. Main stats: ER / Flexible / CRIT Rate if Favonius.
  • Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs) · General Support: Provides a team ATK buff after her Burst. Use when another teammate does not already carry it. Main stats: Energy Recharge first.
  • Viridescent Venerer (4 pcs) · Swirl Support: Reduces resistance to Swirled elements, but does not reduce Anemo resistance for hypercarries. Main stats: Energy Recharge first.

Main stats

  • Sands: Energy Recharge / ATK%
  • Goblet: Anemo DMG
  • Circlet: Crit Rate / Crit DMG

Stat targets

  • Energy Recharge: 225% - 245%
  • ATK: 1,400-1,600+
  • Crit Rate: 60% - 75%
  • Crit DMG: 130-150%

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 9+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 9+

Best teams for Faruzan

  • PawPixl's Team: Faruzan, Wanderer, Nicole, Durin. Faruzan provides Anemo buffs and resistance shredding while Nicole and Durin increase team damage.
  • She was a Farie: Faruzan, Vesna, Vodyanitsa, Odette. Faruzan buffs Vesna's Anemo damage and shreds resistance while the team maximizes Stellar-Swirl reactions.
  • Bad Dreams: Faruzan, Yumemizuki Mizuki, Odette, Traveler (Cryo). Faruzan boosts Anemo damage while Mizuki drives Swirl. Odette and Cryo Traveler provide additional reaction support, though Faruzan is less valuable here than for Anemo hypercarries.
  • Terminal Velocity: Faruzan, Xiao, Xianyun, Furina. Faruzan buffs Xiao's Anemo damage while Xianyun boosts plunges and Furina provides powerful DMG buffs.

Pros

  • Powerful buffs
  • RES shredding
  • Stellar synergy
  • C6 grouping

Cons

  • High ER Needs
  • C6 dependent
  • Limited flexibility
  • Low damage

Build variations

Anemo Hypercarry Buffer (Support)

Amplify Wanderer or another Anemo carry with RES reduction and damage buffs.

  • Weapons: Favonius Warbow, Elegy for the End
  • Artifacts: Tenacity of the Millelith (4 pcs); Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Prioritize ER first; add CR for Favonius.
  • Teams: Anemo on-field carry; C6 improves support uptime.
  • When it applies: ER requirements are much higher before C6.

Accessible Burst Support (Support)

A flexible low-investment support setup when premium weapons are unavailable.

  • Weapons: Favonius Warbow, End of the Line
  • Artifacts: Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs); Viridescent Venerer (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ER Sands and enough CR to trigger Favonius.
  • Teams: Anemo DPS and regular Burst uptime.
  • When it applies: Use VV mainly when Swirl shred benefits the team.

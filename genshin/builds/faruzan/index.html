Role: Support

Faruzan is an Anemo support who shreds resistance, boosts Anemo damage, and offers a new Stellar support niche, expanding her usefulness beyond traditional Anemo teams.

Best weapons for Faruzan

  1. Elegy for the End
  2. Favonius Warbow
  3. Breezeborne Refrain
  4. Skyward Harp
  5. End of the Line
  6. Fading Twilight
  7. Sacrificial Bow

Best artifacts

  • Tenacity of the Millelith (4 pcs) · C6 Support
  • Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs) · General Support
  • Viridescent Venerer (4 pcs) · Swirl Support

Main stats

  • Sands: Energy Recharge / ATK%
  • Goblet: Anemo DMG
  • Circlet: Crit Rate / Crit DMG

Stat targets

  • Energy Recharge: 225% - 245%
  • ATK: 1,400-1,600+
  • Crit Rate: 60% - 75%
  • Crit DMG: 130-150%

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 9+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 9+

Best teams

  • PawPixl's Team: Faruzan, Wanderer, Nicole, Durin
  • She was a Farie: Faruzan, Vesna, Vodyanitsa, Odette
  • Bad Dreams: Faruzan, Yumemizuki Mizuki, Odette, Traveler (Cryo)
  • Terminal Velocity: Faruzan, Xiao, Xianyun, Furina

Pros

  • Powerful buffs
  • RES shredding
  • Stellar synergy
  • C6 grouping

Cons

  • High ER Needs
  • C6 dependent
  • Limited flexibility
  • Low damage

Build variations

  • Anemo Hypercarry Buffer (Support): Amplify Wanderer or another Anemo carry with RES reduction and damage buffs.
  • Accessible Burst Support (Support): A flexible low-investment support setup when premium weapons are unavailable.

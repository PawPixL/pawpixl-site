Wriothesley Build Guide: Weapons, Artifacts and Teams (7.2)

Wriothesley build guide for Genshin Impact 7.2: best weapons (Cashflow Supervision, Tulaytullah's Remembrance), artifacts (Disenchantment in Deep Shadow).

Last updated: November 12, 2026 · Game version 7.2

Role: Main DPS

Wriothesley is an on-field Cryo DPS who delivers powerful Normal and Charged Attacks with strong Freeze and Melt potential.

Best weapons for Wriothesley

  1. Cashflow Supervision: Signature CRIT Rate and ATK, with major Normal/Charged bonuses from HP changes.
  2. Tulaytullah's Remembrance: CRIT DMG, attack speed and stacking Normal Attack damage after Skill.
  3. A Thousand Floating Dreams: Offers high Elemental Mastery for Melt teams and useful team buffs, but weaker personal stats than dedicated CRIT weapons.
  4. Lost Prayer to the Sacred Winds: High CRIT Rate with an Elemental DMG bonus that stacks during on-field time.
  5. The Widsith: Huge CRIT DMG and one random ATK, Elemental DMG or EM buff; inconsistent but strong at R5.
  6. Solar Pearl: Battle Pass CRIT Rate with Normal Attack–Skill/Burst damage interactions; needs a suitable rotation.
  7. Ballad of the Boundless Blue: Free ER catalyst stacking Normal and Charged Attack damage through hits.
  8. Mappa Mare: Craftable Elemental Mastery choice; triggering Melt reliably procs its Elemental DMG Bonus.

Best artifacts for Wriothesley

  • Disenchantment in Deep Shadow (4 pcs) · Main DPS: Provides 18% ATK and boosts Stellar-Conduct DMG by 40%, with 16% CR against affected enemies. Wriothesley’s best-in-slot set for Stellar-Conduct teams. Main stats: ATK% - ATK% - CR / CD J.
  • Marechaussee Hunter (4 pcs) · Main DPS: Provides 15% Normal and Charged Attack DMG and up to 36% CR when HP fluctuates. Wriothesley’s best-in-slot set for traditional Freeze and Melt teams.
  • Blizzard Strayer (4 pcs) · Main DPS: Provides 15% Cryo DMG and up to 40% CR against Cryo-affected or Frozen enemies. A strong option for Freeze teams, but Wriothesley’s Charged Attacks can Shatter enemies and reduce Freeze uptime.

Main stats

  • Sands: ATK%
  • Goblet: ATK% / Cryo DMG Bonus
  • Circlet: CRIT Rate / CRIT DMG

Stat targets

  • CRIT DMG: 170–230%
  • ATK: 2,000–2,200+
  • Energy Recharge: 110–130%

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 9+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 9+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 6+

Best teams for Wriothesley

  • Conducted Chaos: Wriothesley (Main DPS), Odette (Stellar), Mitya (Sub DPS), Nicole (Support). Wriothesley leads on-field Stellar-Conduct damage, Mitya adds off-field Electro damage, Odette enables and buffs Stellar-Conduct, and Nicole provides shielding and teamwide ATK buffs.
  • Freeze Frame: Wriothesley (Main DPS), Furina (Buffs), Shenhe (Support), Escoffier (Support). Wriothesley leads on-field Cryo damage, Furina provides Hydro and a massive teamwide DMG Bonus, Shenhe boosts Cryo damage, and Escoffier heals while shredding Cryo/Hydro RES.
  • Fire Meets Frost: Wriothesley (Main DPS), Durin (Sub DPS), Xilonen (Buffs), Nicole (Support). Wriothesley drives on-field Melt damage, Durin provides Pyro application, Nicole delivers teamwide ATK buffs, and Xilonen shreds Cryo/Pyro RES while boosting damage.
  • Frost on a Budget: Wriothesley (Main DPS), Xiangling (Sub DPS), Sucrose (EM Build), Bennett (Support). Wriothesley drives Reverse Melt damage, Xiangling provides off-field Pyro, Sucrose shares EM and shreds Cryo RES, and Bennett supplies ATK buffs, healing, and Pyro energy.

Build variations

Freeze Cryo Carry (On-field DPS)

Sustained Normal and Charged Attacks against Freezeable enemies.

  • Weapons: Cashflow Supervision, Lost Prayer to the Sacred Winds
  • Artifacts: Marechaussee Hunter (4 pcs); Blizzard Strayer (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ATK% / Cryo DMG / CRIT; consider Freeze CRIT buffs.
  • Teams: Reliable Hydro application and Cryo support.
  • When it applies: Freeze value falls against unfreezeable bosses.

Melt / Burnmelt (On-field DPS)

Use amplified Cryo hits with Melt-support teammates.

  • Weapons: Cashflow Supervision, The Widsith
  • Artifacts: Marechaussee Hunter (4 pcs); Disenchantment in Deep Shadow (4 pcs)
  • Stats: EM becomes useful with consistent Melt; keep ATK and CRIT.
  • Teams: Pyro aura, optionally supported by Burning.
  • When it applies: Melt frequency depends on aura management.

Wriothesley talents

Normal Attack: Forceful Fists of Frost

Normal Attack
Coalescing frost about his fist, Wriothesley will unleash powerful Repelling Fists, performing up to 5 rapid attacks that deal Cryo DMG.
Apart from this, Normal Attack combo count will not reset for a short time after using Icefang Rush or sprinting.

Charged Attack
Consumes a fixed amount of Stamina to leap and unleash a Vaulting Fist, dealing AoE Cryo DMG.

Plunging Attack
Plunges from mid-air to strike the ground below, damaging opponents along the path and dealing AoE Cryo DMG upon impact.

Elemental Skill: Icefang Rush

Cooldown: 16s

Adjusting his breathing, rhythm, and pace, Wriothesley sprints forward a short distance, entering the Chilling Penalty state and unleashing more powerful attacks than before.

Chilling Penalty
·Increases Wriothesley's interruption resistance
·When his HP is above 50%, it will enhance the Repelling Fists of Normal Attack: Forceful Fists of Frost and increase its DMG. When such an attack hits, it will consume a fixed amount of Wriothesley's HP. HP can be lost this way once every 0.1s.
This effect will be canceled should Wriothesley leave the field.

Elemental Burst: Darkgold Wolfbite

Cooldown: 15s / Surging Blade: 10s · Energy cost: 60

Activating his boxing gloves, Wriothesley strikes out with an icy straight, then uses Icicle Impact to cause multiple instances of AoE Cryo DMG in a frontal area.

Arkhe: Ousia
After Icicle Impact ends, a Surging Blade will descend upon the opponent's position, dealing Ousia-aligned Cryo DMG.

Passive talent: There Shall Be a Plea for Justice

When Wriothesley's HP is less than 60%, he will obtain a Gracious Rebuke. The next Charged Attack of his Normal Attack: Forceful Fists of Frost will be enhanced to become Rebuke: Vaulting Fist. It will not consume Stamina, will deal 50% increased DMG, and after hitting will restore HP for Wriothesley equal to 30% of his Max HP.
You can gain a Gracious Rebuke this way once every 5s.

Passive talent: There Shall Be a Reckoning for Sin

When Wriothesley's current HP increases or decreases, if he is in the Chilling Penalty state conferred by Icefang Rush, Chilling Penalty will gain one stack of Prosecution Edict. Max 5 stacks. Each stack will increase Wriothesley's ATK by 6%.

Passive talent: The Duke's Grace

When Wriothesley crafts Weapon Ascension Materials, he has a 10% chance to receive double the product.

Wriothesley constellations

C1: Terror for the Evildoers

The Gracious Rebuke from the Ascension Talent "There Shall Be a Plea for Justice" is changed to this:
When Wriothesley's HP is less than 60% or while he is in the Chilling Penalty state caused by Icefang Rush, when the fifth attack of Repelling Fists hits, it will create a Gracious Rebuke. 1 Gracious Rebuke effect can be obtained every 2.5s.

Additionally, Rebuke: Vaulting Fist will obtain the following enhancement:
· The DMG Bonus gained will be further increased to 200%.
· When it hits while Wriothesley is in the Chilling Penalty state, that state's duration is extended by 4s. 1 such extension can occur per 1 Chilling Penalty duration.

You must first unlock the Ascension Talent "There Shall Be a Plea for Justice."

C2: Shackles for the Arrogant

When using Darkgold Wolfbite, each Prosecution Edict stack from the Ascension Talent "There Shall Be a Reckoning for Sin" will increase said ability's DMG dealt by 40%.
You must first unlock the Ascension Talent "There Shall Be a Reckoning for Sin."

C3: Punishment for the Frauds

Increases the Level of Normal Attack: Forceful Fists of Frost by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C4: Redemption for the Suffering

The HP restored to Wriothesley through Rebuke: Vaulting Fist will be increased to 50% of his Max HP. You must first unlock the Ascension Talent "There Shall Be a Plea for Justice."
Additionally, when Wriothesley is healed, if the amount of healing overflows, the following effects will occur depending on whether he is on the field or not. If he is on the field, his ATK SPD will be increased by 20% for 4s. If he is off-field, all of your own party members' ATK SPD will be increased by 10% for 6s. These two methods of increasing ATK SPD cannot stack.

C5: Mercy for the Wronged

Increases the Level of Darkgold Wolfbite by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C6: Esteem for the Innocent

The CRIT Rate of Rebuke: Vaulting Fist will be increased by 10%, and its CRIT DMG by 80%. When unleashed, it will also create an additional icicle that deals 100% of Rebuke: Vaulting Fist's Base DMG as Cryo DMG. DMG dealt this way is regarded as Charged Attack DMG.
You must first unlock the Ascension Talent "There Shall Be a Plea for Justice."

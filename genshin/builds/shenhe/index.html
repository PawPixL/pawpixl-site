Role: Support / Buffer

Shenhe is an ATK-scaling Cryo support whose Icy Quills add damage to allies’ Cryo hits. Her Skill also buffs attack types, while her Burst shreds Cryo and Physical RES. Build enough Energy Recharge to Burst reliably, then stack ATK; she shines beside Cryo damage dealers.

Best weapons for Shenhe

  1. Calamity Queller
  2. Engulfing Lightning
  3. Skyward Spine
  4. Vortex Vanquisher
  5. Favonius Lance
  6. Missive Windspear
  7. Prospector's Drill
  8. Prototype Starglitter

Best artifacts

  • Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs) · Team Buff
  • Gladiator's Finale + Shimenawa's Reminiscence (2 + 2 pcs) · Quill Focus
  • Emblem of Severed Fate (4 pcs) · Personal Damage

Main stats

  • Sands: ATK% / Energy Recharge
  • Goblet: ATK%
  • Circlet: ATK% / CRIT Rate (Favonius)

Stat targets

  • Energy Recharge: 160% - 200% (team dependent)
  • ATK: 2,800 - 3,500+ (after ER needs)
  • CRIT Rate: 30% - 50% (Favonius only)
  • Skill: Tap 5 / Hold 7 Icy Quill uses

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Not Priority
  • Elemental Skill: Level 9 - 10
  • Elemental Burst: Level 8 - 9+

Best teams

  • Father Shenhe: Shenhe, Skirk, Furina, Escoffier
  • Winter Court: Shenhe, Kamisato Ayaka, Sangonomiya Kokomi, Kaedehara Kazuha
  • Cold Front: Shenhe, Wriothesley, Escoffier, Layla
  • Budget Snow Day: Shenhe, Chongyun, Xingqiu, Diona

Pros

  • Powerful Cryo buffs
  • Cryo RES reduction
  • Quick field time
  • Flexible Cryo teams

Cons

  • Energy hungry
  • Needs Cryo teammates
  • Quills run out quickly
  • Low personal damage

Build variations

  • Favonius Battery (Cryo Support): Give Shenhe a brief on-field hit to trigger Favonius, then swap to your Cryo carry.
  • Maximum Icy Quill ATK (Cryo Buffer): Maximize Shenhe’s ATK to amplify Cryo hits from teammates consuming Icy Quills.
  • Hold Skill · Cryo Attacks (Charged / Normal Buffer): Hold Skill before Wriothesley or other attack-focused Cryo carries: 7 Quills each and a 15% attack-type DMG buff.
  • Burst Damage Sidegrade (Sub DPS): Emphasizes her own periodic Cryo Burst damage while keeping RES shred active.

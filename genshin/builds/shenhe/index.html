Shenhe Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

Shenhe build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Calamity Queller, Engulfing Lightning), artifacts (Noblesse Oblige), main stats, talent priority.

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Role: Support / Buffer

Shenhe is an ATK-scaling Cryo support whose Icy Quills add damage to allies’ Cryo hits. Her Skill also buffs attack types, while her Burst shreds Cryo and Physical RES. Build enough Energy Recharge to Burst reliably, then stack ATK; she shines beside Cryo damage dealers.

Best weapons for Shenhe

  1. Calamity Queller: 741 Base ATK, ATK% and a passive that builds more ATK while Shenhe is off-field maximize Icy Quill buffs. Meet Burst Energy needs first.
  2. Engulfing Lightning: Energy Recharge and ER-to-ATK conversion help balance Burst uptime with stronger Icy Quills. Especially useful when ER needs are high.
  3. Skyward Spine: High Base ATK and Energy Recharge help Shenhe maintain Burst uptime without giving up too much buff strength.
  4. Vortex Vanquisher: High Base ATK and an ATK% substat strengthen Icy Quills; the shield-dependent passive is less useful off-field. Value mainly comes from its stats.
  5. Favonius Lance: CRIT hits generate neutral particles for Shenhe and her team, often making this the most practical 4-star choice. Build some CRIT Rate and hit an enemy on-field. Higher refinements improve reliability.
  6. Missive Windspear: ATK% substat and a reaction-triggered ATK boost make it a useful accessible stat stick for Cryo support. Trigger an elemental reaction for the passive. R5 if available.
  7. Prospector's Drill: ATK% and its Liyue-party passive can raise her buffing ATK, but team restrictions reduce its flexibility. Best with several Liyue teammates.
  8. Prototype Starglitter: Craftable Energy Recharge option when better polearms are unavailable; its Normal and Charged Attack passive adds little. Budget stopgap rather than a damage pick.

Best artifacts for Shenhe

  • Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs) · Team Buff: After Burst, gives the team 20% ATK. Main stats: ATK% / ER · ATK% · ATK% / CRIT Rate.
  • Gladiator's Finale + Shimenawa's Reminiscence (2 + 2 pcs) · Quill Focus: Two ATK% set bonuses maximize Icy Quill damage when another teammate already wears Noblesse. Main stats: ATK% / ER · ATK% · ATK%.
  • Emblem of Severed Fate (4 pcs) · Personal Damage: Improves Shenhe’s own Burst DMG with ER. Main stats: ATK% / ER · ATK% / Cryo · ATK% / CRIT.

Main stats

  • Sands: ATK% / Energy Recharge
  • Goblet: ATK%
  • Circlet: ATK% / CRIT Rate (Favonius)

Stat targets

  • Energy Recharge: 160% - 200% (team dependent)
  • ATK: 2,800 - 3,500+ (after ER needs)
  • CRIT Rate: 30% - 50% (Favonius only)
  • Skill: Tap 5 / Hold 7 Icy Quill uses

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Not Priority
  • Elemental Skill: Level 9 - 10
  • Elemental Burst: Level 8 - 9+

Best teams for Shenhe

  • Father Shenhe: Shenhe (Cryo Buffer), Skirk (On-field DPS), Furina (Off-field DPS / Buffer), Escoffier (Healer / Debuffer). Shenhe boosts Skirk’s Cryo hits; Furina supplies DMG buffs and Hydro.
  • Winter Court: Shenhe (Cryo Buffer), Kamisato Ayaka (Main DPS), Sangonomiya Kokomi (Hydro Healer), Kaedehara Kazuha (Anemo Support). Ayaka spends Shenhe’s Icy Quills with her Burst; Kokomi keeps enemies Frozen and heals.
  • Cold Front: Shenhe (Cryo Buffer), Wriothesley (On-field DPS), Escoffier (Healer / Debuffer), Layla (Shielder / Cryo Enabler). Hold Shenhe’s Skill to buff Wriothesley’s attacks. Escoffier adds healing and Cryo shred.
  • Budget Snow Day: Shenhe (Cryo Buffer), Chongyun (Cryo Enabler / Sub DPS), Xingqiu (n), Diona (Shielder / Healer). Chongyun uses Shenhe’s Quills through infused Cryo attacks.

Build variations

Favonius Battery (Cryo Support)

Give Shenhe a brief on-field hit to trigger Favonius, then swap to your Cryo carry.

  • Weapons: Favonius Lance, Skyward Spine
  • Artifacts: Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Build Energy Recharge to Burst every rotation; add CRIT Rate to trigger Favonius.
  • Teams: Teams with high Energy needs or limited Cryo particles.
  • When it applies: Favonius only triggers on on-field CRIT hits.

Maximum Icy Quill ATK (Cryo Buffer)

Maximize Shenhe’s ATK to amplify Cryo hits from teammates consuming Icy Quills.

  • Weapons: Calamity Queller, Vortex Vanquisher
  • Artifacts: Gladiator's Finale + Shimenawa's Reminiscence (2 + 2 pcs)
  • Stats: After reaching required ER, focus on ATK% across Sands, Goblet and Circlet.
  • Teams: Another teammate already wears Noblesse Oblige.
  • When it applies: Calamity Queller still needs enough ER for Burst uptime.

Hold Skill · Cryo Attacks (Charged / Normal Buffer)

Hold Skill before Wriothesley or other attack-focused Cryo carries: 7 Quills each and a 15% attack-type DMG buff.

  • Weapons: Calamity Queller, Favonius Lance
  • Artifacts: Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Similar ATK / ER support stats; prioritize reliable Burst uptime.
  • Teams: Cryo carry relying on Normal, Charged or Plunging Attacks.
  • When it applies: Longer cooldown than Tap Skill.

Burst Damage Sidegrade (Sub DPS)

Emphasizes her own periodic Cryo Burst damage while keeping RES shred active.

  • Weapons: Engulfing Lightning, Skyward Spine
  • Artifacts: Emblem of Severed Fate (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Use ER Sands if needed; seek more personal CRIT after sufficient ATK.
  • Teams: A team already has Noblesse and can support Shenhe’s Burst.
  • When it applies: Usually less team damage than buff-focused builds.

Shenhe talents

Normal Attack: Dawnstar Piercer

Normal Attack
Performs up to 5 consecutive spear strikes.

Charged Attack
Consumes a certain amount of Stamina to lunge forward, dealing damage to opponents along the way.

Plunging Attack
Plunges from mid-air to strike the ground below, damaging opponents along the path and dealing AoE DMG upon impact.

Elemental Skill: Spring Spirit Summoning

Cooldown: Press: 10s / Hold: 15s

The frosted dew, silvery and dense, shall exorcise all demons.
Grants all nearby party members the Icy Quill effect and deals Cryo DMG in different ways based on whether it is pressed or held.

Press
Rushes forward together with a Talisman Spirit, dealing Cryo DMG to opponents along the path.

Hold
Commands the Talisman Spirit to deal AoE Cryo DMG.

Icy Quill
When Normal, Charged, and Plunging Attacks, Elemental Skills, and Elemental Bursts deal Cryo DMG to opponents, the DMG dealt is increased based on Shenhe's current ATK.

The Icy Quill's effects will be cleared once its duration ends or after being triggered a certain number of times. When held rather than pressed, the Icy Quill's effect lasts longer and can be triggered more times.
When one Cryo DMG instance strikes multiple opponents, the effect is triggered multiple times based on the number of opponents hit. The number of times the effect is triggered is calculated independently for each party member with the Icy Quill.

Elemental Burst: Divine Maiden's Deliverance

Cooldown: 20s · Energy cost: 80

Unleashes the power of the Talisman Spirit, allowing it to roam free in this plane, dealing AoE Cryo DMG.
The Talisman Spirit then creates a field that decreases the Cryo RES and Physical RES of opponents within it. It also deals periodic Cryo DMG to opponents within the field.

Passive talent: Deific Embrace

An active character within the field created by Divine Maiden's Deliverance gains 15% Cryo DMG Bonus.

Passive talent: Spirit Communion Seal

After Shenhe uses Spring Spirit Summoning, she will grant all nearby party members the following effects:
· Press: Elemental Skill and Elemental Burst DMG increased by 15% for 10s.
· Hold: Normal, Charged, and Plunging Attack DMG increased by 15% for 15s.

Passive talent: Precise Comings and Goings

Gains 25% more rewards when dispatched on a Liyue Expedition for 20 hours.

Shenhe constellations

C1: Clarity of Heart

Spring Spirit Summoning can be used 1 more time.

C2: Centered Spirit

Divine Maiden's Deliverance lasts for 6 seconds longer. Active characters within the skill's field deal 15% increased Cryo CRIT DMG.

C3: Seclusion

Increases the Level of Spring Spirit Summoning by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C4: Insight

When characters under the effect of Icy Quill applied by Shenhe trigger its DMG Bonus effects, Shenhe will gain a Skyfrost Mantra stack:
· When Shenhe uses Spring Spirit Summoning, she will consume all stacks of Skyfrost Mantra, increasing the DMG of that Spring Spirit Summoning by 5% for each stack consumed.
· Max 50 stacks. Stacks last for 60s.

C5: Divine Attainment

Increases the Level of Divine Maiden's Deliverance by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C6: Mystical Abandon

When characters trigger Icy Quill's effects using Normal and Charged Attack DMG, it does not count toward the Trigger Quota.

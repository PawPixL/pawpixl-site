Xingqiu Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

Xingqiu build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Primordial Jade Cutter, Mistsplitter Reforged), artifacts (Emblem of Severed Fate), main stats.

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Role: Sub DPS

Xingqiu is a premier off-field Hydro DPS whose Burst enables Vaporize, Hyperbloom, and other reactions through coordinated sword rains. He adds DMG reduction, interruption resistance, and minor healing. Prioritize enough ER to Burst every rotation; weapon, team.

Best weapons for Xingqiu

  1. Primordial Jade Cutter: Huge CRIT Rate and an HP-based ATK bonus greatly boost sword-rain damage once Burst uptime is secured. Best in low-ER teams or with C6, Raiden, or strong batteries.
  2. Mistsplitter Reforged: High CD and Element DMG raise his Hydro DMG.
  3. Haran Geppaku Futsu: CRIT Rate and unconditional Elemental DMG Bonus benefit his Burst; the Normal Attack passive has little value off-field. Only after meeting ER requirements.
  4. Skyward Blade: Energy Recharge and high Base ATK help support frequent Bursts while retaining reasonable personal damage. Its on-field Normal Attack passive is mostly wasted.
  5. Sacrificial Sword: ER and a Skill reset produce extra Hydro particles.
  6. Favonius Sword: Energy Recharge and neutral particles help Xingqiu and his entire team; weave a Normal Attack to trigger CRIT reliably. Build enough CRIT Rate and hit an enemy while on-field. Higher refinements improve reliability.
  7. Fleuve Cendre Ferryman: Free Energy Recharge sword with extra Skill CRIT Rate and a brief ER boost after using his Skill. A practical alternative when Favonius or Sacrificial is unavailable. R5 obtainable from Fontaine fishing.
  8. Amenoma Kageuchi: ATK% and an Energy refund after Burst can work in well-batteried teams, but usually require more ER than Sacrificial. More effective with refinements and consistent Skill casts. R3+ preferred.

Best artifacts for Xingqiu

  • Emblem of Severed Fate (4 pcs) · Best: His best general set: 20% ER and Burst DMG scaling with ER directly support his main damage source. Main stats: ER/ATK% · Hydro DMG · CRIT.
  • Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs) · Team Buff: Provides a teamwide ATK buff after Burst if nobody else is using Noblesse, at the cost of personal damage. Main stats: ER/ATK% · Hydro DMG · CRIT.
  • Noblesse Oblige + Heart of Depth (2 + 2 pcs) · Accessible: Mixed Burst and Hydro DMG bonuses are an easy temporary option before farming a strong 4-piece Emblem set. Main stats: ER/ATK% · Hydro DMG · CRIT.

Main stats

  • Sands: Energy Recharge / ATK%
  • Goblet: Hydro DMG Bonus
  • Circlet: CRIT Rate / CRIT DMG

Stat targets

  • ER, Favonius C0-C5: 190% - 220%
  • ER, Sacrificial R3+ C0-C5: 150% - 180%
  • ER, Favonius C6: 150% - 180%
  • ER, Sacrificial R3+ C6: 130% - 150%
  • CRIT Rate: 60% - 75%

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 8-9
  • Elemental Burst: level 8-9

Best teams for Xingqiu

  • National Book Club: Xingqiu (Sub DPS), Xiangling (Off-field DPS), Bennett (Buffer / Healer), Sucrose (Anemo Support). Xingqiu supplies Hydro for Xiangling Vaporize; Bennett buffs and batteries, while Sucrose groups and shares EM.
  • Raiden National: Xingqiu (Sub DPS), Raiden Shogun (Driver / Battery), Xiangling (Off-field DPS), Bennett (Buffer / Healer). Raiden recharges the team and drives Xingqiu sword rains; Xiangling Vaporizes while Bennett buffs both damage dealers.
  • Rain Sword Hyperbloom: Xingqiu (Hydro Enabler), Nahida (Dendro Support), Kuki Shinobu (Hyperbloom Trigger / Healer), Alhaitham (Main DPS). Xingqiu creates Bloom cores with Nahida and Alhaitham; Kuki triggers Hyperbloom while healing the team.
  • Double Hydro Vaporize: Xingqiu (Hydro Enabler), Hu Tao (Main DPS), Yelan (Sub DPS), Zhongli (Shield Support). Xingqiu and Yelan maintain Hydro for Hu Tao Vaporize while Zhongli protects her and shreds resistance.

Pros

  • Excellent off-field Hydro
  • Strong reaction enabler
  • Damage reduction and poise
  • Good single-target damage

Cons

  • High energy needs
  • Normal Attacks trigger Burst
  • Long Skill cooldown
  • Limited teamwide buffs

Build variations

High-ER Off-field Hydro (Off-field DPS)

Sacrificial is most consistent at R3+.

  • Weapons: Sacrificial Sword, Favonius Sword, Fleuve Cendre Ferryman
  • Artifacts: Emblem of Severed Fate (4 pcs); Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ER Sands and CRIT substats; prioritize Burst uptime over raw damage.
  • Teams: Best for solo-Hydro teams, C0-C5, or weak particle generation.
  • When it applies: Approx. 150–180% ER with R3+ Sacrificial or 190–220% with Favonius at C0–C5; team dependent.

C6 Low-ER Damage (Off-field DPS)

Requires C6 for the extra Energy refund.

  • Weapons: Primordial Jade Cutter, Mistsplitter Reforged, Haran Geppaku Futsu
  • Artifacts: Emblem of Severed Fate (4 pcs); Noblesse Oblige + Heart of Depth (2 + 2 pcs)
  • Stats: Use ATK% Sands once ER is covered; focus CRIT and ATK% for Burst damage.
  • Teams: Works best with Raiden, double Hydro, or Favonius teammates.
  • When it applies: C6 lowers ER needs but does not remove them; prioritize reliable Bursts.

Noblesse Team Buffer (Support)

Choose when team ATK matters more than Xingqiu personal damage.

  • Weapons: Favonius Sword, Sacrificial Sword, Skyward Blade
  • Artifacts: Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs); Emblem of Severed Fate (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ER first; aim for sufficient CRIT Rate to trigger Favonius.
  • Teams: ATK-scaling carry; nobody else should wear 4pc Noblesse.
  • When it applies: Avoid duplicate Noblesse buffs.
  • Notes: Best used with stable Burst uptime.

Double-Hydro Off-field DPS (Off-field DPS)

Useful when Hydro resonance and extra particles enable offensive gearing.

  • Weapons: Primordial Jade Cutter, Sacrificial Sword, Favonius Sword
  • Artifacts: Emblem of Severed Fate (4 pcs); Noblesse Oblige + Heart of Depth (2 + 2 pcs)
  • Stats: Reduce ER only as far as partner particles allow; invest leftovers in CRIT/ATK%.
  • Teams: A Hydro partner such as Yelan or Furina plus an active Normal Attack user.
  • When it applies: Energy needs vary with the Hydro partner.
  • Notes: Do not assume double Hydro alone guarantees low ER.

Accessible Budget Build (Off-field DPS)

Practical choice without premium 5-star swords or refined Sacrificial Sword.

  • Weapons: Fleuve Cendre Ferryman, Amenoma Kageuchi, Favonius Sword
  • Artifacts: Emblem of Severed Fate (4 pcs); Noblesse Oblige + Heart of Depth (2 + 2 pcs)
  • Stats: ER Sands if necessary; then build 60%+ CRIT Rate and Hydro DMG.
  • Teams: Any reaction team that can trigger his Burst sword rains.
  • When it applies: Amenoma needs more ER than strong Sacrificial setups.
  • Notes: Fleuve Cendre Ferryman is obtained through fishing; refine when possible.

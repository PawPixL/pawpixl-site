Xingqiu Build Guide | Best Weapons, Artifacts & Teams

Xingqiu build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Primordial Jade Cutter, Mistsplitter Reforged), artifacts (Emblem of Severed Fate), main stats…

Role: Sub DPS

Xingqiu is a premier off-field Hydro DPS whose Burst enables Vaporize, Hyperbloom, and other reactions through coordinated sword rains. He adds DMG reduction, interruption resistance, and minor healing. Prioritize enough ER to Burst every rotation; weapon, team.

  1. Primordial Jade Cutter
  2. Mistsplitter Reforged
  3. Haran Geppaku Futsu
  4. Skyward Blade
  5. Sacrificial Sword
  6. Favonius Sword
  7. Fleuve Cendre Ferryman
  8. Amenoma Kageuchi

Best artifacts

  • Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs) · Team Buff
  • Noblesse Oblige + Heart of Depth (2 + 2 pcs) · Accessible

Main stats

  • ER, Favonius C6: 150% - 180%
  • ER, Sacrificial R3+ C6: 130% - 150%
  • CRIT Rate: 60% - 75%

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 8-9
  • Elemental Burst: level 8-9

Best teams

  • National Book Club: Xingqiu, Xiangling, Bennett, Sucrose
  • Raiden National: Xingqiu, Raiden Shogun, Xiangling, Bennett
  • Rain Sword Hyperbloom: Xingqiu, Nahida, Kuki Shinobu, Alhaitham
  • Double Hydro Vaporize: Xingqiu, Hu Tao, Yelan, Zhongli

Pros

  • Excellent off-field Hydro
  • Strong reaction enabler
  • Damage reduction and poise
  • Good single-target damage

Cons

  • High energy needs
  • Normal Attacks trigger Burst
  • Long Skill cooldown
  • Limited teamwide buffs

Build variations

  • High-ER Off-field Hydro (Off-field DPS): Sacrificial is most consistent at R3+.
  • C6 Low-ER Damage (Off-field DPS): Requires C6 for the extra Energy refund.
  • Noblesse Team Buffer (Support): Choose when team ATK matters more than Xingqiu personal damage.
  • Double-Hydro Off-field DPS (Off-field DPS): Useful when Hydro resonance and extra particles enable offensive gearing.
  • Accessible Budget Build (Off-field DPS): Practical choice without premium 5-star swords or refined Sacrificial Sword.

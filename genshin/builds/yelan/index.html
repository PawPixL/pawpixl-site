Yelan Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

Yelan build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Aqua Simulacra, Elegy for the End), artifacts (Emblem of Severed Fate), main stats, talent priority.

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Role: Sub DPS

Yelan is an HP-scaling off-field Hydro DPS whose Burst follows Normal Attacks to apply Hydro and deal strong single-target damage. She also ramps up the active character’s damage bonus. Prioritize enough Energy Recharge to Burst every rotation; ATK does not increase her Skill or Burst damage.

Best weapons for Yelan

  1. Aqua Simulacra: Huge CRIT DMG, HP bonus, and off-field damage boost. Best for personal damage when her Energy needs are met. Requires sufficient ER from artifacts, teammates, or C1.
  2. Elegy for the End: ER supports Burst uptime while the teamwide EM and ATK buff improves reaction-focused teams. Best when teammates benefit from EM/ATK; trades Yelan damage for team buffs.
  3. Favonius Warbow: Massive ER and neutral particles keep her Burst ready and lower team Energy needs. Use enough CRIT Rate to activate its passive reliably.
  4. Sacrificial Bow: Can reset her Skill for extra Hydro particles and damage, but extends rotations and is less reliable than Favonius. Catch particles from both Skills. R3+ preferred.
  5. Mouun's Moon: Boosts Burst damage based on the team’s combined Burst costs; best with low ER requirements. Needs ER from team and artifacts. R3-R5 preferred.
  6. The Stringless: Boosts Skill and Burst damage; EM is less useful outside reaction-triggering situations. Use when her Burst is already funded. R5 preferred.
  7. Fading Twilight: Provides ER and a rotating damage bonus, making it a workable event alternative. Event-exclusive; cannot currently be obtained.
  8. Slingshot: Large CRIT Rate stat stick; Normal Attack passive does not boost her Burst. Only when energy needs are solved by her team.

Best artifacts for Yelan

  • Emblem of Severed Fate (4 pcs): Best general set: 20% ER plus Burst DMG scaling with ER. Do not overstack ER past her rotation requirement. Main stats: ER%/HP% · Hydro DMG%/HP% · CRIT.
  • Emblem of Severed Fate + Tenacity of the Millelith (2 + 2 pcs): Accessible interim mix of ER and HP for reliable Bursts; weaker damage than 4pc Emblem. Main stats: ER%/HP% · Hydro DMG% · CRIT.
  • Heart of Depth + Tenacity of the Millelith (2 + 2 pcs): Low-ER transition set with Hydro DMG and HP bonuses; mainly when strong Emblem pieces are unavailable. Main stats: HP%/ER% · Hydro DMG% · CRIT.

Main stats

  • Goblet: Hydro DMG% / HP%
  • Circlet: CRIT Rate / CRIT DMG / HP%

Stat targets

  • CRIT DMG: 130% - 180%+
  • ER: 215% - 260%
  • ER (Fav, double Hydro): 110% - 195%

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 8-9
  • Elemental Burst: Level 8-9

Best teams for Yelan

  • Vape and Vanish: Yelan (Sub DPS), Arlecchino (Main DPS), Bennett (Buffer / Healer), Xilonen (Support). Yelan applies off-field Hydro for Arlecchino to Vaporize. Bennett supplies ATK; Xilonen shreds RES and buffs damage.
  • Double Trouble: Yelan (Sub DPS), Hu Tao (Main DPS), Xingqiu (Sub DPS), Zhongli (Shield). Yelan and Xingqiu supply steady Hydro for Hu Tao’s Vaporize attacks; Zhongli protects the team and reduces enemy RES.
  • Bloom Service: Yelan (Sub DPS), Alhaitham (Main DPS), Nahida (Support), Kuki Shinobu (Healer / Trigger). Yelan’s Hydro and Nahida’s Dendro form cores; full-EM Kuki triggers Hyperbloom while Alhaitham drives Dendro damage.
  • National Security: Yelan (Sub DPS), Raiden Shogun (Main DPS / Battery), Xiangling (Sub DPS), Bennett (Buffer / Healer). Raiden restores team Energy and helps stabilize Hydro aura for Xiangling’s Vaporizes; Bennett boosts Xiangling and heals.

Build variations

Team Buff (Elegy) (Off-field Support)

Use Elegy when its teamwide ATK/EM buff adds more damage than Aqua’s personal damage advantage.

  • Weapons: Elegy for the End, Favonius Warbow
  • Artifacts: Emblem of Severed Fate (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Maintain rotation ER first. HP and CRIT remain useful but teammate damage matters more than Yelan’s personal ceiling.
  • Teams: Best with an ATK- or EM-scaling reaction carry such as Arlecchino or Hu Tao.
  • When it applies: Team must benefit from Elegy buffs.
  • Notes: Elegy buffs teammates; it does not make Yelan an ATK-scaling character.

High ER / Solo Hydro (Off-field DPS)

Use when missing even one Burst would cost more damage than upgrading HP or CRIT.

  • Weapons: Favonius Warbow, Sacrificial Bow, Fading Twilight
  • Artifacts: Emblem of Severed Fate (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ER Sands is often needed; with 1 Skill and Favonius, solo-Hydro ER can reach ~215–260%. Build CRIT Rate for Favonius.
  • Teams: Yelan is the only Hydro unit and has limited external battery support.
  • When it applies: ER needs drop with 2 Skill uses, Raiden, and Favonius teammates.
  • Notes: ER numbers are scenario estimates, not universal stat requirements.

Double Hydro / Low ER (Off-field DPS)

Use for stronger personal damage in lower-ER teams, especially with Xingqiu or Furina.

  • Weapons: Aqua Simulacra, Mouun's Moon, The Stringless, Slingshot
  • Artifacts: Emblem of Severed Fate (4 pcs); Heart of Depth + Tenacity of the Millelith (2 + 2 pcs)
  • Stats: Use HP% Sands when ER is met; aim for HP, CRIT and Hydro DMG rather than stacking excess ER.
  • Teams: A second Hydro character, C1, or strong external particles should fund her Burst.
  • When it applies: Only when she can Burst every rotation.
  • Notes: Normal Attack triggers are still necessary for Burst coordinated attacks.

C6 On-field Burst Window (On-field DPS)

For C6 owners using her frontloaded Breakthrough Barb attacks instead of a purely off-field role.

  • Weapons: Aqua Simulacra, Slingshot
  • Artifacts: Emblem of Severed Fate (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Her C6 Breakthrough Barbs make Normal Attack talent investment relevant; balance HP, CRIT and enough ER.
  • Teams: Requires Yelan C6; support buffs should cover her opening on-field damage window.
  • When it applies: C6 only.
  • Notes: Do not recommend upgrading Normal Attack talent for ordinary C0 off-field builds.

Yelan talents

Normal Attack: Stealthy Bowshot

Normal Attack
Performs up to 4 consecutive shots with a bow.

Charged Attack
Performs a more precise Aimed Shot with increased DMG.
While aiming, flowing water will accumulate on the arrowhead. A fully charged torrential arrow will deal Hydro DMG.

Breakthrough
Yelan will enter a "Breakthrough" state after spending 5s out of combat, which will cause her next Charged Aimed Shot to have 80% decreased charge time, and once charged, she can fire a "Breakthrough Barb" that will deal AoE Hydro DMG based on Yelan's Max HP.

Plunging Attack
Fires off a shower of arrows in mid-air before falling and striking the ground, dealing AoE DMG upon impact.

Elemental Skill: Lingering Lifeline

Cooldown: 10s

Fires off a Lifeline that allows her to move rapidly, entangling and marking opponents along its path.
When this rapid movement ends, the Lifeline will explode, dealing Hydro DMG to the marked opponents based on Yelan's Max HP.

Tap
Moves a certain distance forward swiftly.

Hold
Engages in continuous, swift movement, during which Yelan's resistance to interruption is increased.
During this time, Yelan can control this rapid movement and end it by using this Skill again.

Additionally, each opponent marked by the Lifeline when it explodes grants Yelan a 34% chance to reset her Breakthrough state.

Elemental Burst: Depth-Clarion Dice

Cooldown: 18s · Energy cost: 70

Deals AoE Hydro DMG and creates an "Exquisite Throw," which aids her in battle.

Exquisite Throw
Follows the character around and will initiate a coordinated attack under the following circumstances, dealing Hydro DMG based on Yelan's Max HP:
·Can occur once every second when your active character uses a Normal Attack.
·Will occur each time Yelan's Lifeline explodes and hits opponents.

Passive talent: Turn Control

When the party has 1/2/3/4 Elemental Types, Yelan's Max HP is increased by 6%/12%/18%/30%.

Passive talent: Adapt With Ease

So long as an Exquisite Throw is in play, your own active character deals 1% more DMG. This increases by a further 3.5% DMG every second. The maximum increase to DMG dealt is 50%.
The pre-existing effect will be dispelled if Depth-Clarion Dice is recast during its duration.

Passive talent: Necessary Calculation

Gains 25% more rewards when dispatched on a Liyue Expedition for 20 hours.

Yelan constellations

C1: Enter the Plotters

Lingering Lifeline gains 1 additional charge.

C2: Taking All Comers

When Exquisite Throw conducts a coordinated attack, it will fire an additional water arrow that will deal 14% of Yelan's Max HP as Hydro DMG.
This effect can trigger once every 1.8s.

C3: Beware the Trickster's Dice

Increases the Level of Depth-Clarion Dice by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C4: Bait-and-Switch

Increases all party members' Max HP by 10% for 25s for every opponent marked by Lifeline when the Lifeline explodes. A maximum increase of 40% Max HP can be attained in this manner.

C5: Dealer's Sleight

Increases the Level of Lingering Lifeline by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C6: Winner Takes All

After using Depth-Clarion Dice, Yelan will enter the Mastermind state.
In this state, all of Yelan's Normal Attacks will be special Breakthrough Barbs. These Breakthrough Barbs will have similar abilities to normal ones and the DMG dealt will be considered Charged Attack DMG, dealing 156% of a normal Breakthrough Barb's DMG.

The Mastermind state lasts 20s and will be cleared after Yelan fires 5 arrows.

Role: Sub DPS

Yelan is an HP-scaling off-field Hydro DPS whose Burst follows Normal Attacks to apply Hydro and deal strong single-target damage. She also ramps up the active character’s damage bonus. Prioritize enough Energy Recharge to Burst every rotation; ATK does not increase her Skill or Burst damage.

Best weapons for Yelan

  1. Aqua Simulacra
  2. Elegy for the End
  3. Favonius Warbow
  4. Sacrificial Bow
  5. Mouun's Moon
  6. The Stringless
  7. Fading Twilight
  8. Slingshot

Best artifacts

  • Emblem of Severed Fate (4 pcs)
  • Emblem of Severed Fate + Tenacity of the Millelith (2 + 2 pcs)
  • Heart of Depth + Tenacity of the Millelith (2 + 2 pcs)

Main stats

  • Sands: Energy Recharge / HP%
  • Goblet: Hydro DMG% / HP%
  • Circlet: CRIT Rate / CRIT DMG / HP%

Stat targets

  • HP: 30,000 - 40,000+
  • CRIT Rate: 65% - 80%
  • CRIT DMG: 130% - 180%+
  • ER: 215% - 260%
  • ER (Fav, double Hydro): 110% - 195%

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 8-9
  • Elemental Burst: Level 8-9

Best teams

  • Vape and Vanish: Yelan, Arlecchino, Bennett, Xilonen
  • Double Trouble: Yelan, Hu Tao, Xingqiu, Zhongli
  • Bloom Service: Yelan, Alhaitham, Nahida, Kuki Shinobu
  • National Security: Yelan, Raiden Shogun, Xiangling, Bennett

Pros

  • Strong off-field damage
  • Reliable Hydro application
  • Active-character DMG buff
  • Flexible reaction teams

Cons

  • High solo-Hydro ER needs
  • Mostly single-target
  • Needs Normal Attack triggers
  • No defensive utility

Build variations

  • Team Buff (Elegy) (Off-field Support): Use Elegy when its teamwide ATK/EM buff adds more damage than Aqua’s personal damage advantage.
  • High ER / Solo Hydro (Off-field DPS): Use when missing even one Burst would cost more damage than upgrading HP or CRIT.
  • Double Hydro / Low ER (Off-field DPS): Use for stronger personal damage in lower-ER teams, especially with Xingqiu or Furina.
  • C6 On-field Burst Window (On-field DPS): For C6 owners using her frontloaded Breakthrough Barb attacks instead of a purely off-field role.

Thoma Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

Thoma build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Favonius Lance, Kitain Cross Spear), artifacts (Flower of Paradise Lost), main stats, talent priority.

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Role: Reaction DPS / Shield

Thoma triggers Burgeon with coordinated Pyro strikes from his Burst, turning Dendro Cores into powerful AoE explosions. Prioritize Level 90, enough Energy Recharge to Burst consistently, then Elemental Mastery. He also offers a separate HP-focused shield-support build, especially for Normal Attack users.

Best weapons for Thoma

  1. Favonius Lance: Craftable EM polearm that restores Energy after Skill, easing Burst uptime while scaling Burgeon damage. Use Burst before Skill to avoid Kitain draining Energy needed for Burst. R3–R5 preferred.
  2. Kitain Cross Spear: Offers substantial EM for stronger Burgeon explosions, but its damage passive does not boost Burgeon itself. Only if you can meet the higher ER requirement without Kitain.
  3. Dragon's Bane: Provides ER and team particles.
  4. Engulfing Lightning: HP% strengthens the shield and its passive can restore Energy, letting Thoma invest elsewhere. Only useful when he receives healing during rotations.
  5. Rightful Reward: Craftable EM alternative; its leaf can give the on-field ally ATK but does not increase Burgeon reaction damage. Best after solving Burst energy needs.
  6. Moonpiercer: Easy HP% option for stronger shielding, not Burgeon damage or Energy restoration. Shield-support variation only; high ER needed from artifacts.
  7. "The Catch"
  8. Black Tassel

Best artifacts for Thoma

  • Flower of Paradise Lost (4 pcs): Best Burgeon DMG set by a small margin. Main stats: ER or EM / EM / EM.
  • Gilded Dreams (4 pcs): Nearly as strong as Flower and usually more efficient to farm; grants valuable EM in mixed-element teams. Main stats: ER or EM / EM / EM.
  • Gilded Dreams + Wanderer's Troupe (2 + 2 pcs): Simple 160-EM bridge build while farming a complete four-piece set. Main stats: ER or EM / EM / EM.

Main stats

  • Sands: Energy Recharge / Elemental Mastery
  • Goblet: Elemental Mastery
  • Circlet: Elemental Mastery

Stat targets

  • Level: 90 for Burgeon
  • Elemental Mastery: 700 - 950+
  • ER C0-C3 (R3 Kitain): 210% - 240%
  • ER C4+ (R3 Kitain): 165% - 190%

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: L:evel 8-9+
  • Elemental Burst: L:evel 8-9+

Best teams for Thoma

  • Burgeon House Rules: Thoma, Nahida, Xingqiu, Sangonomiya Kokomi. Nahida and Xingqiu create Cores; Kokomi drives Hydro and heals while Thoma’s Burst triggers Burgeon.
  • Garden of Fireworks: Thoma, Kamisato Ayato, Nahida, Yaoyao. Ayato drives Hydro during Thoma’s Burst, Nahida supplies Dendro.
  • Budget Bloom Patrol: Thoma, Traveler (Dendro), Xingqiu, Barbara. Accessible Burgeon core: infuse Dendro Traveler’s Burst with Hydro, not Pyro.
  • Shielded Summer: Thoma, Yoimiya, Xingqiu, Kaedehara Kazuha. Shield-support variation: Yoimiya’s Normal Attacks refresh Thoma’s shield.

Build variations

Shield Support (Shield Support)

Choose for Yoimiya, Wanderer, or another Normal Attack user when protection matters more than Burgeon damage.

  • Weapons: Favonius Lance, Rightful Reward, Black Tassel, Kitain Cross Spear
  • Artifacts: Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs); Tenacity of the Millelith + Emblem of Severed Fate (2 + 2 pcs)
  • Stats: ER or HP% Sands / HP% Goblet / HP% Circlet (CRIT Rate for Favonius). ER first, then HP%.
  • Teams: Normal Attack-focused carry to refresh stacked shields; Burst uptime is essential.
  • When it applies: Single-Pyro teams can need especially high ER.
  • Notes: C4 cuts Energy needs; Favonius requires CRIT. Tenacity + Emblem is a mixed HP/ER option.

Hybrid Burgeon / Shield (Burgeon / Shield)

Use when Thoma is the main defensive unit and you need stronger shields at the cost of some Burgeon damage.

  • Weapons: Kitain Cross Spear, Rightful Reward, Favonius Lance
  • Artifacts: Gilded Dreams (4 pcs); Flower of Paradise Lost (4 pcs)
  • Stats: After meeting ER, replace one EM piece with HP% only if needed for survivability.
  • Teams: Dendro + Hydro Core generation and a driver using Normal Attacks.
  • When it applies: Enough ER for Burst every rotation.
  • Notes: Level 90 remains important for Burgeon damage. Keep another unit on Deepwood Memories.

F2P Burgeon Starter (Burgeon Trigger)

Use craftable equipment and efficiently farmed artifacts to start a Burgeon team without premium weapons.

  • Weapons: Kitain Cross Spear, Moonpiercer
  • Artifacts: Gilded Dreams (4 pcs); Gilded Dreams + Wanderer's Troupe (2 + 2 pcs)
  • Stats: ER Sands if required; otherwise EM/EM/EM. Level Thoma to 90.
  • Teams: Accessible Hydro + Dendro teammates; avoid Pyro infusing Dendro Traveler Burst.
  • When it applies: Kitain refinements reduce ER demand.
  • Notes: Do not assume limited-time event weapons are owned. Favor a healer against Core self-damage.

C4+ Burgeon Efficiency (Burgeon Trigger)

Use when C4 allows improved EM investment while retaining reliable Burgeon rotations.

  • Weapons: Kitain Cross Spear, Dragon's Bane, Favonius Lance
  • Artifacts: Flower of Paradise Lost (4 pcs); Gilded Dreams (4 pcs)
  • Stats: C4 refunds Energy; lower ER only after confirming smooth Burst uptime, then add EM.
  • Teams: C4+ Thoma with Hydro + Dendro and enough team Energy generation.
  • When it applies: Only at C4 or higher.
  • Notes: With Kitain, cast Burst before Skill. The exact ER need depends on team, Skills, and Favonius procs.

C6 Normal Attack Buffer (Shield Support)

Adds offensive utility to a shield-support team when Thoma gains or refreshes his Blazing Barrier.

  • Weapons: Favonius Lance, Black Tassel, Rightful Reward
  • Artifacts: Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs); Tenacity of the Millelith + Emblem of Severed Fate (2 + 2 pcs)
  • Stats: ER and HP% first; CRIT Rate only to support Favonius.
  • Teams: C6 Thoma and on-field carry benefiting from Normal, Charged or Plunging Attack DMG.
  • When it applies: Only at C6.
  • Notes: This is a shield-support variant, not a Burgeon-specific damage upgrade.

Thoma talents

Normal Attack: Swiftshatter Spear

Normal Attack
Performs up to 4 consecutive spear strikes.

Charged Attack
Consumes a certain amount of Stamina to lunge forward, dealing damage to opponents along the way.

Plunging Attack
Plunges from mid-air to strike the ground below, damaging opponents along the path and dealing AoE DMG upon impact.

Elemental Skill: Blazing Blessing

Cooldown: 15s

Thoma vaults forward with his polearm and delivers a flame-filled flying kick that deals AoE Pyro DMG, while also summoning a defensive Blazing Barrier. At the moment of casting, Thoma's Elemental Skill applies Pyro to himself.
The DMG Absorption of the Blazing Barrier scales off Thoma's Max HP.
The Blazing Barrier has the following traits:
·Absorbs Pyro DMG 250% more effectively.
·When a new Blazing Barrier is obtained, the remaining DMG Absorption of an existing Blazing Barrier will stack and its duration will be refreshed.

The maximum DMG Absorption of the Blazing Barrier will not exceed a certain percentage of Thoma's Max HP.

Elemental Burst: Crimson Ooyoroi

Cooldown: 20s · Energy cost: 80

Thoma spins his polearm, slicing at his foes with roaring flames that deal AoE Pyro DMG and weave themselves into a Scorching Ooyoroi.

Scorching Ooyoroi
While Scorching Ooyoroi is in effect, the active character's Normal Attacks will trigger Fiery Collapse, dealing AoE Pyro DMG and summoning a Blazing Barrier.
Fiery Collapse can be triggered once every 1s.

Except for the amount of DMG they can absorb, the Blazing Barriers created in this way are identical to those created by Thoma's Elemental Skill, Blazing Blessing:
·Absorbs Pyro DMG 250% more effectively.
·When a new Blazing Barrier is obtained, the remaining DMG Absorption of an existing Blazing Barrier will stack and its duration will be refreshed.

The maximum DMG Absorption of the Blazing Barrier will not exceed a certain percentage of Thoma's Max HP.

If Thoma falls, the effects of Scorching Ooyoroi will be cleared.

Passive talent: Imbricated Armor

When your current active character obtains or refreshes a Blazing Barrier, this character's Shield Strength will increase by 5% for 6s.
This effect can be triggered once every 0.3s seconds. Max 5 stacks.

Passive talent: Flaming Assault

DMG dealt by Crimson Ooyoroi's Fiery Collapse is increased by 2.2% of Thoma's Max HP.

Passive talent: Snap and Swing

When you fish successfully in Inazuma, Thoma's help grants a 20% chance of scoring a double catch.

Thoma constellations

C1: A Comrade's Duty

When a character protected by Thoma's own Blazing Barrier (Thoma excluded) is attacked, Thoma's own Blazing Blessing CD is decreased by 3s, while his own Crimson Ooyoroi's CD is decreased by 3s.
This effect can be triggered once every 20s.

C2: A Subordinate's Skills

Crimson Ooyoroi's duration is increased by 3s.

C3: Fortified Resolve

Increases the Level of Blazing Blessing by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C4: Long-Term Planning

After using Crimson Ooyoroi, 15 Energy will be restored to Thoma.

C5: Raging Wildfire

Increases the Level of Crimson Ooyoroi by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C6: Burning Heart

When a Blazing Barrier is obtained or refreshed, the DMG dealt by all party members' Normal, Charged, and Plunging Attacks is increased by 15% for 6s.

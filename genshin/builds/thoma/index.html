Thoma build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Favonius Lance, Kitain Cross Spear), artifacts (Flower of Paradise Lost), main stats (Energy Recharge…

Role: Reaction DPS / Shield

Thoma triggers Burgeon with coordinated Pyro strikes from his Burst, turning Dendro Cores into powerful AoE explosions. Prioritize Level 90, enough Energy Recharge to Burst consistently, then Elemental Mastery. He also offers a separate HP-focused shield-support build, especially for Normal Attack users.

Best weapons for Thoma

  1. Favonius Lance
  2. Kitain Cross Spear
  3. Dragon's Bane
  4. Engulfing Lightning
  5. Rightful Reward
  6. Moonpiercer
  7. "The Catch"
  8. Black Tassel

Best artifacts

  • Flower of Paradise Lost (4 pcs)
  • Gilded Dreams (4 pcs)
  • Gilded Dreams + Wanderer's Troupe (2 + 2 pcs)

Main stats

  • Sands: Energy Recharge / Elemental Mastery
  • Goblet: Elemental Mastery
  • Circlet: Elemental Mastery

Stat targets

  • Level: 90 for Burgeon
  • Elemental Mastery: 700 - 950+
  • ER C0-C3 (R3 Kitain): 210% - 240%
  • ER C4+ (R3 Kitain): 165% - 190%

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: L:evel 8-9+
  • Elemental Burst: L:evel 8-9+

Best teams

  • Burgeon House Rules: Thoma, Nahida, Xingqiu, Sangonomiya Kokomi
  • Garden of Fireworks: Thoma, Kamisato Ayato, Nahida, Yaoyao
  • Budget Bloom Patrol: Thoma, Traveler (Dendro), Xingqiu, Barbara
  • Shielded Summer: Thoma, Yoimiya, Xingqiu, Kaedehara Kazuha

Pros

  • Strong AoE Burgeon
  • Craftable top weapon
  • Refreshable shielding
  • C4 eases Energy needs

Cons

  • Very Energy hungry
  • Needs Hydro + Dendro
  • Burst-dependent triggers
  • Shield needs Normal Attacks

Build variations

  • Shield Support (Shield Support): Choose for Yoimiya, Wanderer, or another Normal Attack user when protection matters more than Burgeon damage.
  • Hybrid Burgeon / Shield (Burgeon / Shield): Use when Thoma is the main defensive unit and you need stronger shields at the cost of some Burgeon damage.
  • F2P Burgeon Starter (Burgeon Trigger): Use craftable equipment and efficiently farmed artifacts to start a Burgeon team without premium weapons.
  • C4+ Burgeon Efficiency (Burgeon Trigger): Use when C4 allows improved EM investment while retaining reliable Burgeon rotations.
  • C6 Normal Attack Buffer (Shield Support): Adds offensive utility to a shield-support team when Thoma gains or refreshes his Blazing Barrier.

Jahoda Build Guide | Best Weapons, Artifacts & Teams

Role: Healer, Support

Jahoda is an ATK-scaling Anemo healer who grants the active character 100 EM after healing them above 70% HP. With another Moonsign character, Ascendant Gleam lets her Skill and Burst apply absorbed elements from off-field.

Best weapons for Jahoda

  1. Favonius Warbow
  2. Elegy for the End
  3. Rainbow Serpent's Rain Bow
  4. Snare Hook
  5. End of the Line
  6. Fading Twilight
  7. Jade Vista

Best artifacts

  • Viridescent Venerer (4 pcs) · General
  • Silken Moon's Serenade (4 pcs) · Lunar
  • Tenacity of the Millelith (4 pcs) · ATK Buff

Main stats

  • Sands: Energy Recharge / ATK%
  • Goblet: ATK%
  • Circlet: Healing Bonus / CRIT Rate / ATK%

Stat targets

  • ER (Favonius, C0): 190% - 255%
  • ER (non-Favonius, C0): 225% - 330%
  • ER (Favonius, C4): 150% - 220%
  • CRIT Rate (Favonius): 40% - 60%
  • ATK: maximize after meeting ER

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 6+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 8-9

Best teams

  • Moonlit Lightning: Jahoda, Flins, Mona, Fischl
  • Purrfect Moonstorm: Jahoda, Ineffa, Furina, Ifa
  • Secret Moon Garden: Jahoda, Nefer, Xingqiu, Lauma
  • Budget Moon Circuit: Jahoda, Aino, Fischl, Sucrose

Pros

  • Reliable Burst healing
  • Grants active ally 100 EM
  • Enables Ascendant Gleam
  • Useful off-field application
  • Strong C6 Moonsign buffs

Cons

  • High Energy requirements
  • Skill demands field time
  • VV uptime can be awkward
  • C6 needed for peak buffs

Build variations

  • Lunar EM Buffer (Healer / Support): Use for Flins, Ineffa and other Lunar teams that benefit from team EM and healing.
  • Tenacity ATK Buffer (Support / Healer): Use her repeated infused Skill hits to maintain the Tenacity party ATK buff.
  • Nefer Deepwood Healer (Healer / Dendro Support): Reduce Dendro RES while providing healing and EM utility; particularly relevant for Nefer.
  • Fast Rotation Healer (Healer): Outside Ascendant Gleam, shorten field time by cancelling the Skill charge before casting Burst.
  • C4 Energy-Saving Support (Support / Healer): Use C4 refunds to improve healing stats without sacrificing Burst consistency.

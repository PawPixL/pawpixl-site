Jahoda Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

Jahoda build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Favonius Warbow, Elegy for the End), artifacts (Viridescent Venerer), main stats, talent priority.

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Role: Healer, Support

Jahoda is an ATK-scaling Anemo healer who grants the active character 100 EM after healing them above 70% HP. With another Moonsign character, Ascendant Gleam lets her Skill and Burst apply absorbed elements from off-field.

Best weapons for Jahoda

  1. Favonius Warbow: ER and CRIT-triggered neutral particles help fund her costly Burst and the whole team.
  2. Elegy for the End: ER plus teamwide EM and ATK buffs when Skill and Burst hits build Sigils. Meet ER needs.
  3. Rainbow Serpent's Rain Bow: ER secondary stat and an ATK-boosting passive improve both Burst access and ATK-scaling heals.
  4. Snare Hook: Craftable ER bow that helps maintain Burst healing when Favonius is unavailable.
  5. End of the Line: Fishing-obtainable Energy Recharge bow for dependable Burst access without weapon wishes. Its extra damage contributes little to team utility.
  6. Fading Twilight: Energy Recharge with a rotating personal DMG buff; usable when stronger team-support bows are unavailable. Past limited-time event weapon; personal damage is modest.
  7. Jade Vista

Best artifacts for Jahoda

  • Viridescent Venerer (4 pcs) · General: Swirl reduces the absorbed Pyro, Hydro, Electro or Cryo RES by 40%; long cooldowns can limit uptime. Main stats: ER/ATK% · ATK% · Healing Bonus/CRIT Rate.
  • Silken Moon's Serenade (4 pcs) · Lunar: Provides party EM from Moonsign effects; great for Lunar/reaction teams when no ally already equips it. Main stats: ER/ATK% · ATK% · Healing Bonus/CRIT Rate.
  • Tenacity of the Millelith (4 pcs) · ATK Buff: In Ascendant Gleam, off-field Skill hits can maintain the party ATK buff for ATK-scaling carries. Main stats: Needs Ascendant Gleam and charged Skill flask.

Main stats

  • Sands: Energy Recharge / ATK%
  • Goblet: ATK%
  • Circlet: Healing Bonus / CRIT Rate / ATK%

Stat targets

  • ER (Favonius, C0): 190% - 255%
  • ER (non-Favonius, C0): 225% - 330%
  • ER (Favonius, C4): 150% - 220%
  • CRIT Rate (Favonius): 40% - 60%
  • ATK: maximize after meeting ER

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 6+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 8-9

Best teams for Jahoda

  • Moonlit Lightning: Jahoda (Reaction Enabler), Flins (On-field DPS), Mona (Buffer / Hydro Enabler), Fischl (Off-field DPS / Battery). Jahoda enables Ascendant Gleam and absorbs Hydro for Flins; Mona provides Hydro and Omen, while Fischl adds Electro damage.
  • Purrfect Moonstorm: Jahoda (Healer), Ineffa (Off-field DPS / Shielder), Furina (Off-field DPS / Buffer), Ifa (On-field Driver). Ineffa enables Lunar-Charged, Furina buffs team damage, Jahoda heals for Fanfare, and Ifa drives reactions.
  • Secret Moon Garden: Jahoda (Healer), Nefer (On-field DPS), Xingqiu (Off-field DPS / Hydro Enabler), Lauma (Dendro Buffer / Enabler). Nefer leads Lunar-Bloom with Lauma and Xingqiu. Jahoda heals and shares EM; her C6 adds substantial Moonsign CRIT buffs.
  • Budget Moon Circuit: Jahoda (Reaction Enabler), Aino (Off-field DPS / Enabler), Fischl (Off-field DPS / Battery), Sucrose (On-field Driver). Aino enables Ascendant Gleam with Jahoda; Fischl contributes Electro damage and Sucrose drives reactions and groups enemies.

Pros

  • Grants active ally 100 EM
  • Enables Ascendant Gleam
  • Useful off-field application
  • Strong C6 Moonsign buffs

Cons

Build variations

Lunar EM Buffer (Healer / Support)

Use for Flins, Ineffa and other Lunar teams that benefit from team EM and healing.

  • Weapons: Favonius Warbow, Elegy for the End, Rainbow Serpent's Rain Bow
  • Artifacts: Silken Moon's Serenade (4 pcs); Viridescent Venerer (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ER to Burst each rotation, then ATK%; Healing Bonus or Favonius CRIT circlet.
  • Teams: Another Moonsign teammate; prefer no existing Silken Moon holder.
  • When it applies: Ascendant Gleam for off-field elemental application; C6 needs a full Skill flask.
  • Notes: Do not double-tap Skill before filling the flask if activating C6.

Tenacity ATK Buffer (Support / Healer)

Use her repeated infused Skill hits to maintain the Tenacity party ATK buff.

  • Weapons: Favonius Warbow, Elegy for the End, Snare Hook
  • Artifacts: Tenacity of the Millelith (4 pcs); Viridescent Venerer (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ER first, then ATK% for healing; CRIT Rate with Favonius.
  • Teams: Ascendant Gleam and an ATK-scaling main damage dealer.
  • When it applies: Needs a full Skill flask and Ascendant Gleam to keep hitting off-field.
  • Notes: Weaker outside the infused Skill setup.

Nefer Deepwood Healer (Healer / Dendro Support)

Reduce Dendro RES while providing healing and EM utility; particularly relevant for Nefer.

  • Weapons: Favonius Warbow, Elegy for the End, End of the Line
  • Artifacts: Deepwood Memories (4 pcs); Instructor (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ER first, then ATK% and Healing Bonus; modest CRIT Rate for Favonius.
  • Teams: Nefer Dendro-focused teams with no other Deepwood user.
  • When it applies: Deepwood only when Dendro RES shred is not already supplied.
  • Notes: Skill/Burst hits must connect to maintain Deepwood; C6 improves her value.

Fast Rotation Healer (Healer)

Outside Ascendant Gleam, shorten field time by cancelling the Skill charge before casting Burst.

  • Weapons: Favonius Warbow, End of the Line, Snare Hook
  • Artifacts: Viridescent Venerer (4 pcs); Instructor (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Prioritize Burst ER and ATK%; Healing Bonus for added comfort.
  • Teams: Any team that needs Anemo healing and modest EM sharing.
  • When it applies: No reliable off-field absorbed-element application without Ascendant Gleam.
  • Notes: Anemo Swirl VV shred still requires an on-field Swirl hit.

C4 Energy-Saving Support (Support / Healer)

Use C4 refunds to improve healing stats without sacrificing Burst consistency.

  • Weapons: Favonius Warbow, Elegy for the End, Rainbow Serpent's Rain Bow
  • Artifacts: Silken Moon's Serenade (4 pcs); Viridescent Venerer (4 pcs)
  • Stats: C4 conversion refunds lower ER needs; invest freed stats into ATK% and healing.
  • Teams: An Ascendant Gleam team with Burst robot elemental conversions.
  • When it applies: Requires C4 and elemental conversion; ER needs still vary by team.
  • Notes: At C6 also fully charge the Skill flask to enable Moonsign CRIT buffs.

Jahoda talents

Normal Attack: Strike While the Arrow's Hot

Normal Attack
Performs up to 3 consecutive shots with a bow.

Charged Attack
Performs a more precise Aimed Shot with increased DMG.
While aiming, mighty winds will accumulate on the arrowhead. A fully charged wind arrow will deal Anemo DMG.

Plunging Attack
Fires off a shower of arrows in mid-air before falling and striking the ground, hitting opponents along the path and dealing AoE DMG upon impact.

Elemental Skill: Savvy Strategy: Splitting the Spoils

Cooldown: 15s

Jahoda dashes toward her opponent, entering the Shadow Pursuit state upon contact. While in this state, Jahoda moves continuously and swiftly: If nearby opponents are affected by Pyro, Hydro, Electro, or Cryo, she will quietly convert her Purr-loined Treasure Flask to that element, gradually filling it. When the duration ends or the skill is used again, Jahoda drains the Purr-loined Treasure Flask and deals a single instance of Anemo DMG to nearby opponents. When the Purr-loined Treasure Flask is full, Jahoda immediately drains it, exiting the Shadow Pursuit state and dealing a more powerful instance of Anemo DMG.
If her dash fails to make contact with an opponent, Jahoda instead drops a Smoke Bomb at her location, dealing a single instance of Anemo DMG to nearby opponents.

Moonsign: Ascendant Gleam: When the Purr-loined Treasure Flask becomes full, Jahoda will not immediately discharge it. Instead, for a short duration, she steadily consumes the stored element inside and periodically fires Fluffy Meowballs at nearby opponents, dealing DMG of the corresponding element stored in the Purr-loined Treasure Flask. When a Fluffy Meowball hits an opponent, Jahoda regenerates 2 Elemental Energy. This effect can be triggered once every 3.5s.

Elemental Burst: Hidden Aces: Seven Tools of the Hunter

Cooldown: 18s · Energy cost: 70

Time to reveal those pocket aces! Jahoda retrieves 2 Purrsonal Coordinated Assistance Robots and deals a single instance of Anemo DMG to nearby opponents.

Passive talent: Plan to Get Paid

When your team has at least one Pyro, Hydro, Electro, or Cryo character, the Purrsonal Coordinated Assistance Robots from the Elemental Burst Hidden Aces: Seven Tools of the Hunter will be enhanced based on the number of characters of each corresponding element.
Jahoda secretly keeps track of these characters. Among them, the element with the highest number of characters determines the enhancement effect:
· Pyro: Purrsonal Coordinated Assistance Robots deal 130% of their original DMG.
· Hydro: Purrsonal Coordinated Assistance Robots heal for 120% of their original healing amount.
· Electro: The number of Purrsonal Coordinated Assistance Robots increases by one.
· Cryo: The attack interval of Purrsonal Coordinated Assistance Robots is reduced by 10%.

If there's a tie between elements, the order of priority that determines which enhancement takes effect is: Pyro, Hydro, Electro, then Cryo. Only one elemental effect can initially be triggered.

Passive talent: Sweet Berry Bounty

When a Purrsonal Coordinated Assistance Robot from the Elemental Burst Hidden Aces: Seven Tools of the Hunter triggers healing on an active team member, if that character's HP is above 70%, their Elemental Mastery will be increased by 100 for 6s.

Passive talent: Moonsign Benediction: Rooftop Dash

When Jahoda is in the party, the party's Moonsign will increase by 1 level.

Passive talent: Backstreet Guile

When dispatched on an expedition in Nod-Krai, time consumed is reduced by 25%.

Jahoda constellations

C1: One More Flask!

When a Fluffy Meowball fired by the Purr-loined Treasure Flask from the Elemental Skill Savvy Strategy: Splitting the Spoils hits an enemy, there is a 50% chance for it to bounce, dealing the corresponding Elemental DMG to nearby opponents.

C2: Rogue's Quick Thinking

Moonsign: Ascendant Gleam: In the Ascension Talent "Plan to Get Paid," Jahoda can secretly track, in addition to the element with the highest number of characters, the element with the next highest number of characters among the remaining types. This allows up to two elemental effects to be active at once. You must first unlock the Ascension Talent "Plan to Get Paid."

C3: Desperate Gamble

Increases the Level of Hidden Aces: Seven Tools of the Hunter by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C4: Wild Berry Amid the Dust

When a Purrsonal Coordinated Assistance Robot from the Elemental Burst Hidden Aces: Seven Tools of the Hunter undergoes an Elemental Conversion, Jahoda will recover 4 Elemental Energy.

C5: The Greatest Treasure

Increases the Level of Savvy Strategy: Splitting the Spoils by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C6: The Littlest Luck

Moonsign: Ascendant Gleam: After the Purr-loined Treasure Flask from the Elemental Skill Savvy Strategy: Splitting the Spoils is full, nearby Moonsign characters in your party have their CRIT Rate increased by 5% and CRIT DMG increased by 40% for 20s.

Jean Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts & Teams

Role: Healer / Support

Jean is a flexible Anemo healer who restores the whole party with her Burst and weakens enemy resistance using Viridescent Venerer. She pairs especially well with Furina by quickly building Fanfare. Her Bennett Sunfire combo adds rapid Pyro application for reaction teams.

Best weapons for Jean

  1. Favonius Sword
  2. Freedom-Sworn
  3. Skyward Blade
  4. Primordial Jade Cutter
  5. Xiphos' Moonlight
  6. Amenoma Kageuchi
  7. Fleuve Cendre Ferryman
  8. Sapwood Blade

Best artifacts

  • Viridescent Venerer (4 pcs)
  • Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs)
  • Ocean-Hued Clam (4 pcs)

Main stats

  • Sands: ER / ATK%
  • Goblet: ATK%
  • Circlet: Healing Bonus / CRIT Rate

Stat targets

  • ER: 160% - 200% (Solo Anemo)
  • ER: 140% - 160% (Double Anemo)
  • ATK: 1,800+ for healing
  • CRIT Rate: 40%+ with Favonius
  • EM: 700+ for dedicated Sunfire

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 6–8
  • Elemental Burst: Level 9+

Best teams

  • Tidal Windfall: Jean, Neuvillette, Furina, Xilonen
  • Afterlife Breeze: Jean, Hu Tao, Furina, Yelan
  • Stormfront Sonata: Jean, Raiden Shogun, Furina, Yelan
  • Budget Sunfire: Jean, Bennett, Xiangling, Xingqiu

Pros

  • Strong teamwide healing
  • Viridescent RES shred
  • Flexible support options
  • Useful enemy control

Cons

  • High ER Needs
  • Limited damage buffs
  • Weak Grouping
  • Sunfire locks positioning
  • C4 only buffs Anemo

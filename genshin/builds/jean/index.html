Jean Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams (7.2)

Jean build guide for Genshin Impact 7.2: best weapons (Favonius Sword, Freedom-Sworn), artifacts (Viridescent Venerer), main stats, talent priority, teams.

Last updated: November 12, 2026 · Game version 7.2

Role: Healer / Support

Jean is a flexible Anemo healer who restores the whole party with her Burst and weakens enemy resistance using Viridescent Venerer. She pairs especially well with Furina by quickly building Fanfare. Her Bennett Sunfire combo adds rapid Pyro application for reaction teams.

Best weapons for Jean

  1. Favonius Sword: ER and team particles on CRIT. Helps Jean and allies Burst consistently; build CRIT Rate.
  2. Freedom-Sworn: EM and a team buff after reactions. Especially effective when Jean reliably Swirls in Sunfire.
  3. Skyward Blade: High Base ATK and ER support Jean’s healing, damage, and Burst uptime. A comfortable five-star alternative.
  4. Primordial Jade Cutter: High CRIT Rate improves Jean’s personal damage and Favonius-style stat balance, but provides no team Energy utility.
  5. Xiphos' Moonlight: Great for EM-focused Sunfire Jean. Converts EM into ER and shares a smaller Energy Recharge bonus with teammates. Best with an EM Sunfire build.
  6. Amenoma Kageuchi: Craftable ATK sword that improves healing and refunds Energy after Burst. Refinements make it more comfortable.
  7. Fleuve Cendre Ferryman: Free fishing sword with substantial ER and bonus Skill CRIT Rate. Reliable when Favonius is unavailable.
  8. Sapwood Blade: Craftable ER sword with high Base ATK. Useful for healing and Burst uptime, though its passive is situational.

Best artifacts for Jean

  • Viridescent Venerer (4 pcs): Best general support set. Swirl reduces the affected element’s enemy resistance, improving team damage. Main stats: ER or ATK% / ATK% / Healing Bonus.
  • Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs): Provides a teamwide ATK buff after Burst if no teammate already uses Noblesse and VV shred is less valuable. Main stats: ER or ATK% / ATK% / Healing Bonus.
  • Ocean-Hued Clam (4 pcs): Converts Jean’s large team heal into extra damage when Viridescent and Noblesse bonuses are already covered. Main stats: ER or ATK% / ATK% / Healing Bonus.

Main stats

  • Sands: ER / ATK%
  • Goblet: ATK%
  • Circlet: Healing Bonus / CRIT Rate

Stat targets

  • ER: 160% - 200% (Solo Anemo)
  • ER: 140% - 160% (Double Anemo)
  • ATK: 1,800+ for healing
  • CRIT Rate: 40%+ with Favonius
  • EM: 700+ for dedicated Sunfire

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 6–8
  • Elemental Burst: Level 9+

Best teams for Jean

  • Tidal Windfall: Jean (Healer / Debuffer), Neuvillette (On-field DPS), Furina (Off-field DPS / Buffer), Xilonen (Healer / Debuffer). Jean heals the whole party for Furina’s Fanfare and shreds Hydro RES. Neuvillette leads the damage while Xilonen adds buffs.
  • Afterlife Breeze: Jean (Healer / Debuffer), Hu Tao (On-field DPS), Furina (Off-field DPS / Buffer), Yelan (Off-field DPS / Buffer). Jean heals Furina’s HP drain and shreds Hydro RES. Furina and Yelan enable Hu Tao’s powerful Vaporize attacks.
  • Stormfront Sonata: Jean (Healer / Debuffer), Raiden Shogun (On-field DPS), Furina (Off-field DPS / Buffer), Yelan (Off-field DPS / Buffer). Raiden drives Electro damage and batteries the team. Furina and Yelan add Hydro damage while Jean heals and Swirls.
  • Budget Sunfire: Jean (Healer / Debuffer), Bennett (Healer / Buffer), Xiangling (Off-field DPS / Pyro Enabler), Xingqiu (Off-field DPS / Hydro Enabler). Jean and Bennett create Sunfire Pyro Swirls. Xiangling adds damage, while Xingqiu provides Hydro for Vaporize.

Pros

  • Strong teamwide healing
  • Viridescent RES shred
  • Flexible support options
  • Useful enemy control

Cons

  • High ER Needs
  • Limited damage buffs
  • Weak Grouping
  • Sunfire locks positioning
  • C4 only buffs Anemo

Build variations

Viridescent Healer (Healer)

Provide healing and elemental RES shred with low field time.

  • Weapons: Favonius Sword, Freedom-Sworn
  • Artifacts: Viridescent Venerer (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ER / ATK% / Healing or CRIT for Favonius.
  • Teams: Swirlable damage dealers and party healing demand.

Sunfire Jean (Support)

Jean and Bennett Bursts repeatedly Swirl Pyro for reactions.

  • Weapons: Favonius Sword, Skyward Blade
  • Artifacts: Viridescent Venerer (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Add EM when focusing on self-Swirled Pyro reactions.
  • Teams: Bennett Burst and close positioning.

Jean Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

Jean build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Favonius Sword, Freedom-Sworn), artifacts (Viridescent Venerer), main stats, talent priority, teams.

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Role: Healer / Support

Jean is a flexible Anemo healer who restores the whole party with her Burst and weakens enemy resistance using Viridescent Venerer. She pairs especially well with Furina by quickly building Fanfare. Her Bennett Sunfire combo adds rapid Pyro application for reaction teams.

Best weapons for Jean

  1. Favonius Sword: ER and team particles on CRIT. Helps Jean and allies Burst consistently; build CRIT Rate.
  2. Freedom-Sworn: EM and a team buff after reactions. Especially effective when Jean reliably Swirls in Sunfire.
  3. Skyward Blade: High Base ATK and ER support Jean’s healing, damage, and Burst uptime. A comfortable five-star alternative.
  4. Primordial Jade Cutter: High CRIT Rate improves Jean’s personal damage and Favonius-style stat balance, but provides no team Energy utility.
  5. Xiphos' Moonlight: Great for EM-focused Sunfire Jean. Converts EM into ER and shares a smaller Energy Recharge bonus with teammates. Best with an EM Sunfire build.
  6. Amenoma Kageuchi: Craftable ATK sword that improves healing and refunds Energy after Burst. Refinements make it more comfortable.
  7. Fleuve Cendre Ferryman: Free fishing sword with substantial ER and bonus Skill CRIT Rate. Reliable when Favonius is unavailable.
  8. Sapwood Blade: Craftable ER sword with high Base ATK. Useful for healing and Burst uptime, though its passive is situational.

Best artifacts for Jean

  • Viridescent Venerer (4 pcs): Best general support set. Swirl reduces the affected element’s enemy resistance, improving team damage. Main stats: ER or ATK% / ATK% / Healing Bonus.
  • Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs): Provides a teamwide ATK buff after Burst if no teammate already uses Noblesse and VV shred is less valuable. Main stats: ER or ATK% / ATK% / Healing Bonus.
  • Ocean-Hued Clam (4 pcs): Converts Jean’s large team heal into extra damage when Viridescent and Noblesse bonuses are already covered. Main stats: ER or ATK% / ATK% / Healing Bonus.

Main stats

  • Sands: ER / ATK%
  • Goblet: ATK%
  • Circlet: Healing Bonus / CRIT Rate

Stat targets

  • ER: 160% - 200% (Solo Anemo)
  • ER: 140% - 160% (Double Anemo)
  • ATK: 1,800+ for healing
  • CRIT Rate: 40%+ with Favonius
  • EM: 700+ for dedicated Sunfire

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 6–8
  • Elemental Burst: Level 9+

Best teams for Jean

  • Tidal Windfall: Jean, Neuvillette, Furina, Xilonen. Jean heals the whole party for Furina’s Fanfare and shreds Hydro RES. Neuvillette leads the damage while Xilonen adds buffs.
  • Afterlife Breeze: Jean, Hu Tao, Furina, Yelan. Jean heals Furina’s HP drain and shreds Hydro RES. Furina and Yelan enable Hu Tao’s powerful Vaporize attacks.
  • Stormfront Sonata: Jean, Raiden Shogun, Furina, Yelan. Raiden drives Electro damage and batteries the team. Furina and Yelan add Hydro damage while Jean heals and Swirls.
  • Budget Sunfire: Jean, Bennett, Xiangling, Xingqiu. Jean and Bennett create Sunfire Pyro Swirls. Xiangling adds damage, while Xingqiu provides Hydro for Vaporize.

Pros

  • Strong teamwide healing
  • Viridescent RES shred
  • Flexible support options
  • Useful enemy control

Cons

  • High ER Needs
  • Limited damage buffs
  • Weak Grouping
  • Sunfire locks positioning
  • C4 only buffs Anemo

Build variations

Viridescent Healer (Healer)

Provide healing and elemental RES shred with low field time.

  • Weapons: Favonius Sword, Freedom-Sworn
  • Artifacts: Viridescent Venerer (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ER / ATK% / Healing or CRIT for Favonius.
  • Teams: Swirlable damage dealers and party healing demand.

Sunfire Jean (Support)

Jean and Bennett Bursts repeatedly Swirl Pyro for reactions.

  • Weapons: Favonius Sword, Skyward Blade
  • Artifacts: Viridescent Venerer (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Add EM when focusing on self-Swirled Pyro reactions.
  • Teams: Bennett Burst and close positioning.

Jean talents

Normal Attack: Favonius Bladework

Normal Attack
Performs up to 5 consecutive strikes.

Charged Attack
Consumes a certain amount of stamina to launch an opponent using the power of wind.
Launched opponents will slowly fall to the ground.

Plunging Attack
Plunges from mid-air to strike the ground below, damaging opponents along the path and dealing AoE DMG upon impact.

Elemental Skill: Gale Blade

Cooldown: 6s

Focusing the might of the formless wind around her blade, Jean releases a miniature storm, launching opponents in the direction she aims at, dealing massive Anemo DMG.

Hold
At the cost of continued stamina consumption, Jean can command the whirlwind to pull surrounding opponents and objects towards her front.
Direction can be adjusted.
Character is immobile during skill duration.

Elemental Burst: Dandelion Breeze

Cooldown: 20s · Energy cost: 80

Calling upon the wind's protection, Jean creates a swirling Dandelion Field, launching surrounding opponents and dealing Anemo DMG.
At the same time, she instantly regenerates a large amount of HP for all party members. The amount of HP restored scales off Jean's ATK.

Dandelion Field
·Continuously regenerates HP of characters within the AoE and continuously imbues them with Anemo.
·Deals Anemo DMG to opponents entering or exiting the Dandelion Field.

Passive talent: Wind Companion

On hit, Jean's Normal Attacks have a 50% chance to regenerate HP equal to 15% of Jean's ATK for all party members.

Passive talent: Let the Wind Lead

Using Dandelion Breeze will regenerate 20% of its Energy.

Passive talent: Guiding Breeze

When a Perfect Cooking is achieved on a dish with restorative effects, there is a 12% chance to obtain double the product.

Jean constellations

C1: Spiraling Tempest

Increases the pulling speed of Gale Blade after holding for more than 1s, and increases the DMG dealt by 40%.

C2: People's Aegis

When Jean picks up an Elemental Orb/Particle, all party members have their Movement SPD and ATK SPD increased by 15% for 15s.

C3: When the West Wind Arises

Increases the Level of Dandelion Breeze by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C4: Lands of Dandelion

Within the Field created by Dandelion Breeze, all opponents have their Anemo RES decreased by 40%.

C5: Outbursting Gust

Increases the Level of Gale Blade by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C6: Lion's Fang, Fair Protector of Mondstadt

Incoming DMG is decreased by 35% within the Field created by Dandelion Breeze. Upon leaving the Dandelion Field, this effect lasts for 3 attacks or 10s.

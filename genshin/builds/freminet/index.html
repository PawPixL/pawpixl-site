Role: Main DPS / Physical

Freminet is an on-field Cryo claymore DPS who uses Pers Timer to deliver powerful Physical or Cryo Skill strikes. Physical builds reach Pressure Level 4, while Cryo builds repeatedly use Level 0. Hydro enables Shatter; Electro enables Superconduct.

Best weapons for Freminet

  1. A Thousand Blazing Suns
  2. Fang of the Mountain King
  3. Song of Broken Pines
  4. Skyward Pride
  5. Serpent Spine
  6. Tidal Shadow
  7. Snow-Tombed Starsilver
  8. Prototype Archaic

Best artifacts

  • Pale Flame (4 pcs)
  • Marechaussee Hunter (4 pcs)
  • Pale Flame + Bloodstained Chivalry (2 + 2 pcs)

Main stats

  • Sands: ATK% / Energy Recharge
  • Goblet: Physical DMG Bonus
  • Circlet: Crit Rate / Crit DMG

Stat targets

  • ER C0: 130-180%
  • ER C2+: 110-150%
  • CRIT Rate: 60-75%
  • CRIT DMG: 120%+
  • ATK: 1,600+

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 9+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 9+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 6-8

Best teams

  • Frostbite Circuit: Freminet, Furina, Fischl, Mika
  • Deep-Sea Shatter: Freminet, Xingqiu, Fischl, Diona
  • Clockwork Hyperbloom: Freminet, Nahida, Xingqiu, Kuki Shinobu
  • Cryo Pressure: Freminet, Furina, Escoffier, Shenhe

Pros

  • Flexible Physical/Cryo
  • Strong Skill damage
  • Shatter synergy
  • F2P weapon choices

Cons

  • Needs field time
  • High ER before C2
  • Shatter needs Hydro
  • Combo timing matters

Build variations

  • Cryo Skill DPS (On-field Cryo DPS): During Burst, repeatedly use Level 0 Shattering Pressure instead of building to Level 4.
  • Furina Physical DPS (On-field Physical DPS): Use when Furina reliably changes HP and buffs Freminet; execute Level 4 Physical Skill combos.
  • Budget Physical DPS (On-field Physical DPS): Accessible Physical setup that uses Level 4 Shattering Pressure; works with Xingqiu, Fischl, and Diona.
  • Hyperbloom Driver (Physical Driver): Use Freminet on-field to drive Xingqiu attacks and trigger Level 4 Physical Skill strikes alongside Hyperbloom.
  • Xianyun Plunge DPS (Plunging DPS): Use a plunge-focused rotation instead of his usual Skill combos. C3 boosts Normal Attack talent levels.

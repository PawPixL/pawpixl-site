Freminet Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

Freminet build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (A Thousand Blazing Suns, Fang of the Mountain King), artifacts (Pale Flame), main stats.

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Role: Main DPS / Physical

Freminet is an on-field Cryo claymore DPS who uses Pers Timer to deliver powerful Physical or Cryo Skill strikes. Physical builds reach Pressure Level 4, while Cryo builds repeatedly use Level 0. Hydro enables Shatter; Electro enables Superconduct.

Best weapons for Freminet

  1. A Thousand Blazing Suns: High CRIT and ATK strengthen Skill hits.
  2. Fang of the Mountain King: Strong stats and Skill damage scaling.
  3. Song of Broken Pines: Physical DMG and ATK SPD for his hits. Physical build.
  4. Skyward Pride: ER helps Burst uptime; Physical blades. High ER needs.
  5. Serpent Spine: CRIT Rate and stacking damage bonus. Avoid taking damage.
  6. Tidal Shadow: Large ATK bonus after receiving healing. Needs healing.
  7. Snow-Tombed Starsilver: Physical DMG secondary stat; accessible. Physical build.
  8. Prototype Archaic: Simple ATK-focused free craft option.

Best artifacts for Freminet

  • Pale Flame (4 pcs): Physical DMG and ATK from Skill. Main stats: ATK% · Physical · CRIT.
  • Marechaussee Hunter (4 pcs): CRIT Rate with Furina HP changes. Main stats: ATK% · Physical · CRIT.
  • Pale Flame + Bloodstained Chivalry (2 + 2 pcs): Always-on Physical DMG bonus. Main stats: ATK% · Physical · CRIT.

Main stats

  • Sands: ATK% / Energy Recharge
  • Goblet: Physical DMG Bonus
  • Circlet: Crit Rate / Crit DMG

Stat targets

  • ER C0: 130-180%
  • ER C2+: 110-150%
  • CRIT Rate: 60-75%
  • CRIT DMG: 120%+
  • ATK: 1,600+

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 9+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 9+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 6-8

Best teams for Freminet

  • Frostbite Circuit: Freminet (On-field DPS), Furina (Off-field DPS / Buffer), Fischl (Off-field DPS / Battery), Mika (Healer / Physical Buffer). Furina enables Shatter and buffs damage; Fischl applies Superconduct; Mika heals and boosts Physical damage.
  • Deep-Sea Shatter: Freminet (On-field DPS), Xingqiu (Off-field DPS / Hydro Enabler), Fischl (Off-field DPS / Battery), Diona (Shielder / Healer). Xingqiu enables Freeze and Shatter, Fischl enables Superconduct, and Diona supplies Cryo particles and sustain.
  • Clockwork Hyperbloom: Freminet (On-field DPS), Nahida (Dendro Enabler / Buffer), Xingqiu (Off-field DPS / Hydro Enabler), Kuki Shinobu (Healer / Reaction Trigger). Freminet drives Xingqiu while Nahida produces cores; Kuki triggers Hyperbloom. Use Level 4 Physical strikes.
  • Cryo Pressure: Freminet (On-field DPS), Furina (Off-field DPS / Buffer), Escoffier (Healer / Debuffer), Shenhe (Cryo Buffer). Furina enables Freeze and buffs damage; Escoffier heals and shreds resistance; Shenhe amplifies Cryo Skill strikes.

Pros

  • Flexible Physical/Cryo
  • Strong Skill damage
  • Shatter synergy
  • F2P weapon choices

Cons

  • Needs field time
  • High ER before C2
  • Shatter needs Hydro
  • Combo timing matters

Build variations

Cryo Skill DPS (On-field Cryo DPS)

During Burst, repeatedly use Level 0 Shattering Pressure instead of building to Level 4.

  • Weapons: A Thousand Blazing Suns, Fang of the Mountain King, Serpent Spine
  • Artifacts: Blizzard Strayer (4 pcs); Golden Troupe (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Cryo DMG goblet; ATK% sands. Prioritize ER to requirement, then CRIT.
  • Teams: Hydro applier for Freeze/Shatter; Cryo battery recommended.
  • When it applies: Freeze uptime affects Blizzard Strayer.

Furina Physical DPS (On-field Physical DPS)

Use when Furina reliably changes HP and buffs Freminet; execute Level 4 Physical Skill combos.

  • Weapons: Serpent Spine, Tidal Shadow, Song of Broken Pines
  • Artifacts: Marechaussee Hunter (4 pcs); Pale Flame (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Physical goblet; watch CRIT Rate overcap with Marechaussee Hunter.
  • Teams: Requires Furina and reliable teamwide healing.
  • When it applies: Avoid too much CRIT Rate.

Budget Physical DPS (On-field Physical DPS)

Accessible Physical setup that uses Level 4 Shattering Pressure; works with Xingqiu, Fischl, and Diona.

  • Weapons: Tidal Shadow, Snow-Tombed Starsilver, Prototype Archaic
  • Artifacts: Pale Flame (4 pcs); Pale Flame + Bloodstained Chivalry (2 + 2 pcs)
  • Stats: ATK% / Physical DMG / CRIT; prioritize enough ER for Burst.
  • Teams: Hydro + Electro for Shatter and Superconduct; healer for Tidal Shadow.
  • When it applies: Tidal Shadow requires healing.

Hyperbloom Driver (Physical Driver)

Use Freminet on-field to drive Xingqiu attacks and trigger Level 4 Physical Skill strikes alongside Hyperbloom.

  • Weapons: A Thousand Blazing Suns, Serpent Spine, Tidal Shadow
  • Artifacts: Pale Flame (4 pcs); Marechaussee Hunter (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Build Physical damage and CRIT, not full EM; Kuki owns Hyperbloom damage.
  • Teams: Dendro + Hydro core generation and an EM-built Electro trigger.
  • When it applies: Hyperbloom damage scales with the Electro trigger.

Xianyun Plunge DPS (Plunging DPS)

Use a plunge-focused rotation instead of his usual Skill combos. C3 boosts Normal Attack talent levels.

  • Weapons: A Thousand Blazing Suns, Fang of the Mountain King, Serpent Spine
  • Artifacts: Long Night's Oath (4 pcs); Pale Flame (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Prioritize Normal Attack talent; no Burst ER required. Match goblet to infusion.
  • Teams: Requires Xianyun for repeated plunges; supports vary by Physical or infused build.
  • When it applies: Xianyun required; C3 helps substantially.

Freminet talents

Normal Attack: Flowing Eddies

Normal Attack
Performs up to 4 consecutive strikes.

Charged Attack
Drains Stamina over time to perform continuous spinning attacks against all nearby opponents.
At the end of the sequence, performs a more powerful slash.

Plunging Attack
Plunges from mid-air to strike the ground below, damaging opponents along the path and dealing AoE DMG upon impact.

Elemental Skill: Pressurized Floe

Cooldown: 10s

Performs an upward thrust that deals Cryo DMG and causes Freminet to enter Pers Timer for 10s.
While Pers Timer is active, his Elemental Skill will turn into Shattering Pressure.

Shattering Pressure
Executes different sorts of attacks based on the Pressure Level of Pers Timer, and then cancels Pers Timer.
·Level 0: Unleashes a vertical cut, dealing Cryo DMG.
·Levels 1 to 3: Unleashes a vertical cut alongside Pers, dealing Cryo DMG and Physical DMG. DMG dealt scales based on Pressure Level.
·Level 4: Borrows the power of a fully-pressurized Pers to deal Physical DMG. Meanwhile, Normal Attack: Flowing Eddies will be replaced by Shattering Pressure.

Pers Timer
When Freminet uses Normal Attacks, he will also unleash waves of frost that deal Cryo DMG and increase Pers's Pressure Level.
The accompanying Cryo DMG dealt this way is considered Elemental Skill DMG.

Arkhe: Pneuma
At certain intervals, after using the upward thrust, a Spiritbreath Thorn in the form of another upward thrust will be created, dealing Pneuma-aligned Cryo DMG.

Elemental Burst: Shadowhunter's Ambush

Cooldown: 15s · Energy cost: 60

Unleashes a wave of untouchable cold, dealing AoE Cryo DMG, resetting the CD of the Elemental Skill "Pressurized Floe," and causing Freminet to enter the Subnautical Hunter mode for 10s.

While in Subnautical Hunter mode, Freminet's resistance to interruption will increase, and his Elemental Skill "Pressurized Floe" will obtain the following buffs:
·CD is decreased by 70%.
·Normal Attacks will increase the Pers Timer by 1 additional Pressure Level, and the frost released by his Normal Attacks deal 200% of their original DMG.

These effects will be canceled when Freminet leaves the field.

Passive talent: Saturation Deep Dive

When Freminet unleashes Pressurized Floe: Shattering Pressure, if Pers Timer has yet to reach Pressure Level 4, the CD of Pressurized Floe will be decreased by 1s.

Passive talent: Parallel Condensers

When Freminet triggers Shatter against opponents, the DMG dealt by Pressurized Floe: Shattering Pressure will be increased by 40% for 5s.

Passive talent: Deepwater Navigation

Decreases Aquatic Stamina consumption for your own party members by 35%.
Not stackable with Passive Talents that provide the exact same effects.

Freminet constellations

C1: Dreams of the Foamy Deep

The CRIT Rate of Pressurized Floe: Shattering Pressure will be increased by 15%.

C2: Penguins and the Land of Plenty

Unleashing Pressurized Floe: Shattering Pressure will restore 2 Energy to Freminet. If a Pressure Level 4 Shattering Pressure is unleashed, this will restore 3 Energy.

C3: Song of the Eddies and Bleached Sands

Increases the Level of Normal Attack: Flowing Eddies by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C4: Dance of the Snowy Moon and Flute

After Freminet triggers Frozen, Shatter, Superconduct, or Stellar-Conduct against opponents, his ATK will be increased by 9% for 6s. Max 2 stacks. This can be triggered once every 0.3s.

C5: Nights of Hearth and Happiness

Increases the Level of Pressurized Floe by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C6: Moment of Waking and Resolve

After Freminet triggers Frozen, Shatter, Superconduct, or Stellar-Conduct against opponents, his CRIT DMG will be increased by 12% for 6s. Max 3 stacks. This can be triggered once every 0.3s.

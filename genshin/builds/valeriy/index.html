Valeriy Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts & Teams

Role: Stellar Support

Valeriy is a flexible support who provides shielding and buffs Electro teams, especially those built around SSC. Best in teams that can fully use his buffs. He is a specialized support.

Best weapons for Valeriy

  1. Summit Shaper
  2. Skyward Blade
  3. Calamity of Eshu
  4. Amenoma Kageuchi
  5. Finale of the Deep
  6. Sacrificial Sword
  7. Favonius Sword
  8. Fleuve Cendre Ferryman

Best artifacts

  • Heart of the Furnace (4 pcs) · Support
  • Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs) · Support
  • Gladiator's Finale + Heart of the Furnace (2 + 2 pcs) · Support

Main stats

  • Sands: ATK% / ER%
  • Goblet: ATK%
  • Circlet: ATK% / Crit Rate

Stat targets

  • ATK: 2,500+
  • Energy Recharge: 150% - 170%
  • Crit Rate: 50%+ (Fav only)
  • HP: 15,000+

Talent priority

  • Guard Tactics: Sword and Shield: Level 9+
  • Active Defense: Bastion Charge: Level 9+
  • Focused Fire: Lightning Salvo: Level 6-8

Best teams

  • Current Situation: Valeriy, Odette, Mitya, Sucrose
  • Short Circuit: Valeriy, Odette, Cyno, Sucrose
  • Guilty as Charged: Valeriy, Chevreuse, Clorinde, Durin
  • Budget Conductors: Valeriy, Traveler (Cryo), Mitya, Sucrose

Pros

  • Strong Shield

  • Low Field Time
  • Easy ATK Build

Cons

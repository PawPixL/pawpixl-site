Valeriy Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

Valeriy build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Summit Shaper, Skyward Blade), artifacts (Heart of the Furnace), main stats, talent priority, teams.

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Role: Stellar Support

Valeriy is a flexible support who provides shielding and buffs Electro teams, especially those built around SSC. Best in teams that can fully use his buffs. He is a specialized support.

Best weapons for Valeriy

  1. Summit Shaper: Summit Shaper gives Valeriy massive ATK scaling, strengthening his ATK-based shields and support buffs/increases ATK when shielded.
  2. Skyward Blade: High Base ATK boosts his support scaling, while ER improves Burst uptime.
  3. Calamity of Eshu: ATK strengthens Valeriy’s shield and buffs; its attack passive works only while shielded.
  4. Amenoma Kageuchi: Amenoma Kageuchi provides substantial ATK% and passive Energy restoration, helping Valeriy maintain his Burst uptime.
  5. Finale of the Deep: Provides ATK% and an additional ATK increase after using a Skill. Its Bond of Life mechanic is best handled with reliable healing.
  6. Sacrificial Sword: Energy Recharge and Skill cooldown reset on Skill damage; extra casts depend on proc chance.
  7. Favonius Sword: Energy Recharge and team particles on CRIT; plan enough CRIT Rate for procs.
  8. Fleuve Cendre Ferryman: Free Energy Recharge sword with extra Skill CRIT Rate and temporary ER after Skill.

Best artifacts for Valeriy

  • Heart of the Furnace (4 pcs) · Support: Heart of the Furnace boosts Valeriy's ATK and can provide a massive teamwide Stellar Glimmer DMG bonus when he triggers the reaction. Main stats: ATK%/ER% · ATK% · ATK%/CR.
  • Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs) · Support: Noblesse Oblige is a strong alternative for Valeriy, providing a 20% teamwide ATK buff after his Burst, especially when another teammate already carries Heart of the Furnace.
  • Gladiator's Finale + Heart of the Furnace (2 + 2 pcs) · Support: Combines two ATK bonuses for a simple support build when a suitable four-piece set is unavailable.

Main stats

  • Sands: ATK% / ER%
  • Goblet: ATK%
  • Circlet: ATK% / Crit Rate

Stat targets

  • ATK: 2,500+
  • Energy Recharge: 150% - 170%
  • CRIT Rate: 50%+ (Favonius only)
  • HP: 15,000+

Talent priority

  • Guard Tactics: Sword and Shield: Level 9+
  • Active Defense: Bastion Charge: Level 9+
  • Focused Fire: Lightning Salvo: Level 6-8

Best teams for Valeriy

  • Current Situation: Valeriy (Support), Odette (Buffs), Mitya (Main DPS), Sucrose (EM Build). Mitya drives SSC damage, Odette amplifies reactions, Sucrose boosts EM and shreds RES, and Valeriy provides buffs and team support.
  • Short Circuit: Valeriy (Support), Odette (Buffs), Cyno (Main DPS), Sucrose (EM Build). Cyno drives sustained Electro damage, Odette amplifies reactions, Sucrose boosts EM and shreds RES, and Valeriy provides buffs and team support.
  • Guilty as Charged: Valeriy (Support), Chevreuse (Healer / Buffer), Clorinde (On-field DPS), Durin (Off-field DPS / Buffer). Clorinde drives Electro damage, Durin enables reactions, Chevreuse boosts Overloaded damage and shreds RES, while Valeriy provides buffs and team support.
  • Budget Conductors: Valeriy (Support), Traveler (Cryo) (Buffs), Mitya (Main DPS), Sucrose (EM Build). Mitya drives SSC damage, Cryo Traveler supports Cryo application, Sucrose boosts EM and shreds RES, and Valeriy provides buffs and team support.

Build variations

Stellar Shield Support (Support)

Protect the carry while providing specialized reaction buffs.

  • Weapons: Skyward Blade, Sacrificial Sword
  • Artifacts: Heart of the Furnace (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Prioritize shield scaling, ER and relevant buff stats.
  • Teams: Electro and Cryo teammates using Stellar-Conduct.
  • When it applies: Heart of the Furnace should not be duplicated.

General Burst Support (Support)

A generic support option outside specialized Conduct teams.

  • Weapons: Skyward Blade, Sacrificial Sword
  • Artifacts: Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Meet Burst energy needs before offensive stats.
  • Teams: ATK-scaling DPS and a team without Noblesse.
  • When it applies: Best when the team can use ATK buffs.

Valeriy talents

Normal Attack: Guard Tactics: Sword and Shield

Normal Attack
Performs up to 3 consecutive sword strikes.

Charged Attack
Consumes a certain amount of Stamina to leap forward and slam the ground, dealing Physical DMG to nearby opponents.
When Valeriy has at least 40 Impact Momentum, he will consume all Impact Momentum to instead perform the special Charged Attack Direct-Fire Mortar: Point-Blank Bombardment.

Plunging Attack
Plunges from mid-air to strike the ground below, damaging opponents along the path and dealing AoE DMG upon impact.

Elemental Skill: Active Defense: Bastion Charge

Cooldown: 10s

Valeriy raises his shield cannon and charges forward, dealing AoE Electro DMG, gaining 40 Impact Momentum, and deploying a Supercharged Shield.
The Supercharged Shield's DMG Absorption scales with Valeriy's ATK, and has 250% absorption efficiency against Electro DMG.

Elemental Burst: Focused Fire: Lightning Salvo

Cooldown: 15s · Energy cost: 60

Deals AoE Electro DMG to nearby opponents and fires a Thunderburst Shell high into the air. The Thunderburst Shell disperses into a barrage of projectiles that rain down, dealing AoE Electro DMG to nearby opponents.

Passive talent: Advance With the Creeping Barrage

Valeriy gains 5 Impact Momentum whenever another nearby active character in the party hits an opponent with an Electro attack while the Thunderburst Shells from his Elemental Burst Focused Fire: Lightning Salvo are falling. He can gain up to 80 Impact Momentum in this way after each use of his Elemental Burst.

Passive talent: Into the Breach

Offensive Directive is enhanced based on the amount of Impact Momentum consumed when performing his special Charged Attack Direct-Fire Mortar: Point-Blank Bombardment. Each point consumed further increases the DMG Bonus granted by Offensive Directive. If Valeriy is:
·In the Radiance: Stellar-Conduct state: Each point of Impact Momentum consumed increases this DMG Bonus by an amount equal to 0.9% of Valeriy's ATK;
·Not in the Radiance: Stellar-Conduct state: Each point of Impact Momentum consumed increases this DMG Bonus by an amount equal to 0.3% of Valeriy's ATK.

Passive talent: Subzero Conditioning

Valeriy enters the Radiance: Stellar-Conduct state while within a Polestar Field.

Passive talent: Tundra Operational Intelligence

Gains 25% more rewards when dispatched on a Snezhnaya (not including Nod-Krai) Expedition for 20 hours.

Valeriy constellations

C1: The Cadence of Suppressive Fire

Valeriy regenerates 6 Elemental Energy after performing the special Charged Attack Direct-Fire Mortar: Point-Blank Bombardment. For every 10 Impact Momentum consumed beyond 40 when performing this special Charged Attack, he regenerates an additional 1.5 Elemental Energy. Each time he performs Direct-Fire Mortar: Point-Blank Bombardment, he can regenerate up to 15 Elemental Energy in this way. This effect can trigger once every 15s.
Additionally, each time Valeriy enters combat, the first time he uses his Elemental Skill Active Defense: Bastion Charge, he gains an additional amount of Impact Momentum equal to 150% of the amount normally gained.

C2: Ranks That Charge as One

All nearby party members gain 100 Elemental Mastery for 18s after Valeriy uses his Elemental Skill Active Defense: Bastion Charge.
Additionally, while protected by a Supercharged Shield, if Valeriy is already at maximum Impact Momentum, any further Impact Momentum he would gain is instead converted into increased DMG Absorption for the shield. Each point of excess Impact Momentum increases the Supercharged Shield's DMG Absorption by an amount equal to 8% of Valeriy's ATK. Up to 20 excess Impact Momentum can be converted in this way.

C3: Vigilance at Close Quarters

Increases the Level of Normal Attack Guard Tactics: Sword and Shield by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C4: Fortitude Unshaken by Attrition

Impact Momentum's limit is increased by 30, and its charging efficiency is increased by 30%.
Additionally, the Ascension Talent Advance With the Creeping Barrage is enhanced: The maximum amount of Impact Momentum that can be gained from this Talent after using the Elemental Burst is 104. You must unlock the Ascension Talent Advance With the Creeping Barrage to gain access to this effect.

C5: Resolve to Divide and Destroy

Increases the Level of the Elemental Skill Active Defense: Bastion Charge by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C6: The Strength to Breach Enemy Lines

The special Charged Attack Direct-Fire Mortar: Point-Blank Bombardment and Thunderburst Shells from Elemental Burst Focused Fire: Lightning Salvo are enhanced: Their DMG is increased by 100%, and opponents they hit have the CRIT Rate of Electro DMG they take increased by 10% for 7s. If the Electro DMG taken is Stellar-Conduct reaction DMG, that DMG instance's CRIT DMG is also increased by 40%.

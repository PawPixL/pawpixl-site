Role: Main DPS

Cyno is a Burst-focused Electro DPS who deals sustained on-field damage. He works with Aggravate and Quickbloom, with new Stellar-Conduct options available when supported by qualifying Cryo and Electro allies.

Best weapons for Cyno

  1. Staff of the Scarlet Sands
  2. Staff of Homa
  3. Primordial Jade Winged-Spear
  4. Deathmatch
  5. Ballad of the Fjords
  6. Missive Windspear
  7. Kitain Cross Spear
  8. White Tassel

Best artifacts

  • Disenchantment in Deep Shadow (4 pcs) · Stellar-Conduct
  • Gilded Dreams (4 pcs) · Aggravate / Quickbloom
  • Night Of The Sky'S Unveiling (4 pcs) · Lunar Teams

Main stats

  • Sands: EM / ATK%
  • Goblet: Electro DMG
  • Circlet: CR / CD

Stat targets

  • CR: 65–80%
  • CD: 140–200%+
  • EM: Team-based
  • ER: Team-based

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1
  • Elemental Skill: Level 9+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 9+

Best teams

  • Judgment Day: Cyno, Mitya, qiqi, Yae Miko
  • Conduct Disorder: Cyno, Odette, Traveler (Cryo), Alyosha
  • Moonlit Judgment: Cyno, Columbina, Yelan, Ineffa
  • Bloom & Doom: Cyno, Nahida, Yelan, Baizhu

Pros

  • Strong reaction damage
  • Excellent single-target DPS
  • Great Dendro synergy
  • High investment potential

Cons

  • Long field time
  • Energy management
  • Skill timing matters
  • Team-dependent reactions

Build variations

  • Aggravate Driver (On-field DPS): Focus Cyno’s sustained Electro damage through Quicken and Aggravate.
  • Quickbloom (On-field DPS): Add Hyperbloom damage to Cyno’s Electro attacks without abandoning his own damage.
  • Stellar-Conduct (On-field DPS): A specialized reaction setup when the team consistently enables Stellar-Conduct.

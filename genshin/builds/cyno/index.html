Cyno Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

Cyno build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Staff of the Scarlet Sands, Staff of Homa), artifacts (Disenchantment in Deep Shadow), main stats.

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Role: Main DPS

Cyno is a Burst-focused Electro DPS who deals sustained on-field damage. He works with Aggravate and Quickbloom, with new Stellar-Conduct options available when supported by qualifying Cryo and Electro allies.

Best weapons for Cyno

  1. Staff of the Scarlet Sands: Signature CR weapon that converts EM into ATK, making it especially strong in reaction teams.
  2. Staff of Homa: Provides 66.2% CRIT DMG, 20% HP, and an ATK bonus based on Max HP, making it a powerful 5-star alternative for Cyno.
  3. Primordial Jade Winged-Spear: Situational alternative; compare passive uptime and team needs.
  4. Deathmatch: Gives CRIT Rate and an ATK bonus. The bonus increases in single target fights and is smaller when facing multiple enemies.
  5. Ballad of the Fjords: BP CR and EM with three different party elements. Strong for Quickbloom. Requires three distinct elements. Needs three distinct party elements for its EM bonus.
  6. Missive Windspear: Provides 41.3% ATK and increases ATK and Elemental Mastery after triggering an Elemental Reaction, making it a solid F2P option for Cyno.
  7. Kitain Cross Spear: EM substat helps Hyperbloom, while the passive restores energy and boosts Skill DMG, easing burst uptime.
  8. White Tassel: Accessible CR and Normal Attack damage during Burst. R5 helps offset its low Base ATK. R5 preferred.

Best artifacts for Cyno

  • Disenchantment in Deep Shadow (4 pcs) · Stellar-Conduct: Stellar-Conduct damage and CR against affected enemies. Best in consistent Stellar-Conduct teams. Main stats: EM/ATK% · Electro · CR/CD.
  • Gilded Dreams (4 pcs) · Aggravate / Quickbloom: EM and ATK for Aggravate or Quickbloom. Use outside Stellar-Conduct teams. Main stats: EM/ATK% · Electro · CR/CD.
  • Night Of The Sky'S Unveiling (4 pcs) · Lunar Teams: Situational Lunar option with Moonsign requirements; not the default for Stellar-Conduct. Main stats: EM/ATK% · Electro · CR/CD.

Best teams for Cyno

  • Judgment Day: Cyno (Main DPS), Mitya (Stellar), qiqi (Support), Yae Miko (Off-field). Cyno leads Electro damage, with Mitya and Yae Miko supplying off-field pressure. Qiqi provides healing and Cryo for reaction setups.
  • Conduct Disorder: Cyno (Main DPS), Odette (Buffs), Traveler (Cryo) (Sub DPS), Alyosha (Support). Odette and Cryo Traveler supply Cryo support while Alyosha heals and buffs. Cyno drives Electro damage in Stellar-Conduct setups.
  • Moonlit Judgment: Cyno (Main DPS), Columbina (Lunar), Yelan (Sub DPS), Ineffa (Support). Columbina and Yelan provide Hydro while Ineffa adds Electro, allowing Cyno to lead mixed Electro and Lunar-Charged damage.
  • Bloom & Doom: Cyno (Main DPS), Nahida (Bloom), Yelan (Off-field), Baizhu (Support). Nahida and Baizhu sustain Dendro application, Yelan provides Hydro, and Cyno triggers Hyperbloom during Burst.

Build variations

Aggravate Driver (On-field DPS)

Focus Cyno’s sustained Electro damage through Quicken and Aggravate.

  • Weapons: Staff of the Scarlet Sands, Staff of Homa, Deathmatch
  • Artifacts: Gilded Dreams (4 pcs)
  • Stats: EM/ATK% · Electro · CRIT; meet ER needs.
  • Teams: Consistent Dendro application and Electro support.
  • When it applies: Long field time requires lasting buffs

Quickbloom (On-field DPS)

Add Hyperbloom damage to Cyno’s Electro attacks without abandoning his own damage.

  • Weapons: Staff of the Scarlet Sands, Ballad of the Fjords, White Tassel
  • Artifacts: Gilded Dreams (4 pcs)
  • Stats: EM and CRIT with enough ER for Burst uptime.
  • Teams: Dendro, Hydro and preferably defensive support.
  • When it applies: Hydro must not erase Quicken uptime entirely

Stellar-Conduct (On-field DPS)

A specialized reaction setup when the team consistently enables Stellar-Conduct.

  • Weapons: Staff of the Scarlet Sands, Staff of Homa, Deathmatch
  • Artifacts: Disenchantment in Deep Shadow (4 pcs)
  • Stats: EM / Electro / CRIT; prioritize ER needs.
  • Teams: Qualifying Cryo teammates and Stellar-Conduct support.
  • When it applies: Team-specific; check actual reaction uptime

Cyno talents

Normal Attack: Invoker's Spear

Normal Attack
Performs up to 4 consecutive spear strikes.

Charged Attack
Consumes a certain amount of Stamina to lunge forward, dealing damage to opponents along the way.

Plunging Attack
Plunges from mid-air to strike the ground below, damaging opponents along the path and dealing AoE DMG upon impact.

Elemental Skill: Secret Rite: Chasmic Soulfarer

Cooldown: 7.5s / Mortuary Rite: 3s

Performs a swift thrust, dealing Electro DMG to opponents along the path.

When Cyno is under the Pactsworn Pathclearer state triggered by Sacred Rite: Wolf's Swiftness, he will instead unleash a Mortuary Rite that deals thunderous AoE Electro DMG and extends the duration of Pactsworn Pathclearer.

Elemental Burst: Sacred Rite: Wolf's Swiftness

Cooldown: 20s · Energy cost: 80

Calls upon a divine spirit to indwell him, morphing into the Pactsworn Pathclearer.

Pactsworn Pathclearer
Cyno's Normal, Charged, and Plunging Attacks will be converted to Electro DMG that cannot be overridden.
·Cyno's Elemental Mastery and resistance to interruption will increase, and he gains immunity to Electro-Charged DMG.

This effect will be canceled when Cyno leaves the field and lasts a maximum of 18s.

Passive talent: Featherfall Judgment

When Cyno is in the Pactsworn Pathclearer state activated by Sacred Rite: Wolf's Swiftness, Cyno will enter the Endseer stance at intervals. If he activates Secret Rite: Chasmic Soulfarer while affected by this stance, he will activate the Judication effect, increasing the DMG of this Secret Rite: Chasmic Soulfarer by 35%, and firing off 3 Duststalker Bolts that deal 100% of Cyno's ATK as Electro DMG.
Duststalker Bolt DMG is considered Elemental Skill DMG.

Passive talent: Authority Over the Nine Bows

Cyno's DMG values will be increased based on his Elemental Mastery as follows:
· Pactsworn Pathclearer's Normal Attack DMG is increased by 150% of his Elemental Mastery.
· Duststalker Bolt DMG from his Ascension Talent Featherfall Judgment is increased by 250% of his Elemental Mastery.

Passive talent: The Gift of Silence

Gains 25% more rewards when dispatched on a Sumeru Expedition for 20 hours.

Cyno constellations

C1: Ordinance: Unceasing Vigil

After using Sacred Rite: Wolf's Swiftness, Cyno's Normal Attack SPD will be increased by 20% for 10s. If the Judication effect of his Ascension Talent Featherfall Judgment is triggered during Secret Rite: Chasmic Soulfarer, the duration of this increase will be refreshed.
You must first unlock the Ascension Talent "Featherfall Judgment."

C2: Ceremony: Homecoming of Spirits

When Cyno's Normal Attacks hit opponents, his Electro DMG Bonus will increase by 10% for 4s. This effect can be triggered once every 0.1s. Max 5 stacks.

C3: Precept: Lawful Enforcer

Increases the Level of Sacred Rite: Wolf's Swiftness by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C4: Austerity: Forbidding Guard

When Cyno is in the Pactsworn Pathclearer state triggered by Sacred Rite: Wolf's Swiftness, after he triggers an Electro-Charged, Lunar-Charged, Superconduct, Stellar-Conduct, Overloaded, Quicken, Aggravate, Hyperbloom, or Electro Swirl reaction, he will restore 3 Elemental Energy for all nearby party members (except himself.)
This effect can occur 5 times within one use of Sacred Rite: Wolf's Swiftness.

C5: Funerary Rite: The Passing of Starlight

Increases the Level of Secret Rite: Chasmic Soulfarer by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C6: Raiment: Just Scales

After using Sacred Rite: Wolf's Swiftness or triggering the Judication effect of the Ascension Talent "Featherfall Judgment," Cyno will gain 4 stacks of the "Day of the Jackal" effect. When he hits opponents with Normal Attacks, he will consume 1 stack of "Day of the Jackal" to fire off one Duststalker Bolt.
"Day of the Jackal" lasts for 8s. Max 8 stacks. It will be canceled once Pactsworn Pathclearer ends.
A maximum of 1 Duststalker Bolt can be unleashed this way every 0.4s.
You must first unlock the Ascension Talent "Featherfall Judgment."

Cyno Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams (7.2)

Cyno build guide for Genshin Impact 7.2: best weapons (Staff of the Scarlet Sands, Staff of Homa), artifacts (Disenchantment in Deep Shadow), main stats.

Last updated: November 12, 2026 · Game version 7.2

Role: Main DPS

Cyno is a Burst-focused Electro DPS who deals sustained on-field damage. He works with Aggravate and Quickbloom, with new Stellar-Conduct options available when supported by qualifying Cryo and Electro allies.

Best weapons for Cyno

  1. Staff of the Scarlet Sands: Signature CR weapon that converts EM into ATK, making it especially strong in reaction teams.
  2. Staff of Homa: Provides 66.2% CRIT DMG, 20% HP, and an ATK bonus based on Max HP, making it a powerful 5-star alternative for Cyno.
  3. Primordial Jade Winged-Spear: Situational alternative; compare passive uptime and team needs.
  4. Deathmatch: Gives CRIT Rate and an ATK bonus. The bonus increases in single target fights and is smaller when facing multiple enemies.
  5. Ballad of the Fjords: BP CR and EM with three different party elements. Strong for Quickbloom. Requires three distinct elements. Needs three distinct party elements for its EM bonus.
  6. Missive Windspear: Provides 41.3% ATK and increases ATK and Elemental Mastery after triggering an Elemental Reaction, making it a solid F2P option for Cyno.
  7. Kitain Cross Spear: EM substat helps Hyperbloom, while the passive restores energy and boosts Skill DMG, easing burst uptime.
  8. White Tassel: Accessible CR and Normal Attack damage during Burst. R5 helps offset its low Base ATK. R5 preferred.

Best artifacts for Cyno

  • Disenchantment in Deep Shadow (4 pcs) · Stellar-Conduct: Stellar-Conduct damage and CR against affected enemies. Best in consistent Stellar-Conduct teams. Main stats: EM/ATK% · Electro · CR/CD.
  • Gilded Dreams (4 pcs) · Aggravate / Quickbloom: EM and ATK for Aggravate or Quickbloom. Use outside Stellar-Conduct teams. Main stats: EM/ATK% · Electro · CR/CD.
  • Night Of The Sky'S Unveiling (4 pcs) · Lunar Teams: Situational Lunar option with Moonsign requirements; not the default for Stellar-Conduct. Main stats: EM/ATK% · Electro · CR/CD.

Main stats

  • Sands: EM / ATK%
  • Goblet: Electro DMG
  • Circlet: CR / CD

Stat targets

  • CR: 65–80%
  • CD: 140–200%+
  • EM: Team-based
  • ER: Team-based

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1
  • Elemental Skill: Level 9+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 9+

Best teams for Cyno

  • Judgment Day: Cyno (Main DPS), Mitya (Stellar), qiqi (Support), Yae Miko (Off-field). Cyno leads Electro damage, with Mitya and Yae Miko supplying off-field pressure. Qiqi provides healing and Cryo for reaction setups.
  • Conduct Disorder: Cyno (Main DPS), Odette (Buffs), Traveler (Cryo) (Sub DPS), Alyosha (Support). Odette and Cryo Traveler supply Cryo support while Alyosha heals and buffs. Cyno drives Electro damage in Stellar-Conduct setups.
  • Moonlit Judgment: Cyno (Main DPS), Columbina (Lunar), Yelan (Sub DPS), Ineffa (Support). Columbina and Yelan provide Hydro while Ineffa adds Electro, allowing Cyno to lead mixed Electro and Lunar-Charged damage.
  • Bloom & Doom: Cyno (Main DPS), Nahida (Bloom), Yelan (Off-field), Baizhu (Support). Nahida and Baizhu sustain Dendro application, Yelan provides Hydro, and Cyno triggers Hyperbloom during Burst.

Pros

  • Great Dendro synergy
  • High investment potential

Cons

Build variations

Aggravate Driver (On-field DPS)

Focus Cyno’s sustained Electro damage through Quicken and Aggravate.

  • Weapons: Staff of the Scarlet Sands, Staff of Homa, Deathmatch
  • Artifacts: Gilded Dreams (4 pcs)
  • Stats: EM/ATK% · Electro · CRIT; meet ER needs.
  • Teams: Consistent Dendro application and Electro support.
  • When it applies: Long field time requires lasting buffs

Quickbloom (On-field DPS)

Add Hyperbloom damage to Cyno’s Electro attacks without abandoning his own damage.

  • Weapons: Staff of the Scarlet Sands, Ballad of the Fjords, White Tassel
  • Artifacts: Gilded Dreams (4 pcs)
  • Stats: EM and CRIT with enough ER for Burst uptime.
  • Teams: Dendro, Hydro and preferably defensive support.
  • When it applies: Hydro must not erase Quicken uptime entirely

Stellar-Conduct (On-field DPS)

A specialized reaction setup when the team consistently enables Stellar-Conduct.

  • Weapons: Staff of the Scarlet Sands, Staff of Homa, Deathmatch
  • Artifacts: Disenchantment in Deep Shadow (4 pcs)
  • Stats: EM / Electro / CRIT; prioritize ER needs.
  • Teams: Qualifying Cryo teammates and Stellar-Conduct support.
  • When it applies: Team-specific; check actual reaction uptime

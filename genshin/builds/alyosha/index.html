Alyosha Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts & Teams

Role: Support / Sub-DPS

Alyosha is an Electro support for Stellar-Conduct teams. His Skill improves the active character’s ATK, while his Burst provides off-field Electro, healing for the active character, and reaction support.

Best weapons for Alyosha

  1. Engulfing Lightning
  2. Symphonist of Scents
  3. Favonius Lance
  4. Tamayuratei no Ohanashi
  5. Song of the Vigil
  6. Skyward Spine
  7. "The Catch"
  8. Frostbreath

Best artifacts

  • Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs) · Best Overall Support
  • Heart of the Furnace (4 pcs) · Can use if no one else on it
  • Gladiator's Finale + A Day Carved From Rising Winds (2 + 2 pcs) · Support

Main stats

  • Sands: ATK% / Energy Recharge
  • Goblet: ATK%
  • Circlet: ATK% / CRIT Rate

Stat targets

  • Energy Recharge: 200-230%+
  • ATK: 2,000 - 3000

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 9+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 6–8

Best teams

  • Dogs Gone Wild: Alyosha, Sandrone, Mitya, Odette
  • Frost & Found: Alyosha, Sandrone, qiqi, Odette
  • Thunderstruck: Alyosha, Cyno, Traveler (Cryo), Odette
  • Discount Treats: Alyosha, Beidou, Traveler (Cryo), Diona

Pros

  • Teamwide Utility
  • Consistent Healing
  • Strong Buffs
  • Off-Field Electro

Cons

  • Low Damage
  • Niche Support
  • On-Field Dependent
  • Reaction Reliant

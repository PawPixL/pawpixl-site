Alyosha Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

Alyosha build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Favonius Lance, Symphonist of Scents), artifacts (Noblesse Oblige), main stats, talent priority.

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Role: Support

Alyosha heals the active character and buffs ATK in Electro teams. In Stellar-Conduct teams, his buffs become more specialized. Focus on ER and Skill levels.

Best weapons for Alyosha

  1. Favonius Lance: Reliable ER and team particles make this his default support weapon. Build enough CR to trigger passive.
  2. Symphonist of Scents: Alyosha’s healing activates its ATK buff for the active teammate.
  3. Frostbreath: Restores flat Energy to teammates after qualifying reactions; works against shields. Alyosha must trigger a qualifying reaction.
  4. Engulfing Lightning: Provides substantial ER, but its ATK conversion mainly improves his own damage.
  5. Skyward Spine: ER stat stick with high Base ATK when support options are unavailable.
  6. Tamayuratei no Ohanashi: Event ER option whose Skill-triggered ATK buff supports healing and personal damage.
  7. "The Catch": Free ER polearm. Its Burst damage passive has limited support value.

Best artifacts for Alyosha

  • Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs) · When Furnace Is Covered: Grants a team ATK buff after Burst. Use if another teammate holds Heart of the Furnace. Main stats: ER / ATK / CR.
  • Heart of the Furnace (4 pcs) · Stellar-Conduct Support: Stellar-Conduct team buff if Alyosha can trigger it and no teammate already holds this set. Main stats: ER / ATK / CR.
  • Gladiator's Finale + A Day Carved From Rising Winds (2 + 2 pcs) · Support: Two 2-piece ATK bonuses give 36% ATK. Temporary stat-focused option; no Energy Recharge bonus. Main stats: ER / ATK / CR.

Main stats

  • Sands: ER / ATK%
  • Goblet: ATK%
  • Circlet: ATK% / Healing / CR

Stat targets

  • ER: Team-based
  • ATK: After ER
  • CR: Favonius only

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 9+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 6–8 (lower priority)

Best teams for Alyosha

  • Dogs Gone Wild: Alyosha, Sandrone, Mitya, Odette. Sandrone drives Stellar-Conduct damage while Mitya and Odette provide off-field support, buffs, and reaction synergy.
  • Frost & Found: Alyosha, Sandrone, qiqi, Odette. Sandrone leads Stellar-Conduct damage while Qiqi sustains the team and Odette enables reactions and boosts damage.
  • Thunderstruck: Alyosha, Cyno, Traveler (Cryo), Odette. Cyno drives on-field damage while Cryo Traveler and Odette enable Stellar-Conduct reactions and boost team damage.
  • Discount Treats: Alyosha, Beidou, Traveler (Cryo), Diona. Beidou provides off-field Electro damage while Cryo Traveler enables the relevant reaction setup. Diona offers healing and shields.

Build variations

Stellar-Conduct Buffer (Support)

Use when Alyosha is the team’s Heart of the Furnace holder and the reaction conditions are met.

  • Weapons: Favonius Lance, Symphonist of Scents
  • Artifacts: Heart of the Furnace (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ER Sands when needed; ATK% Goblet; ATK%, Healing Bonus or Favonius CRIT Circlet.
  • Teams: A Stellar-Conduct enabler; Alyosha must be able to trigger the reaction.
  • When it applies: If Odette already holds Heart of the Furnace, use Noblesse instead.

Noblesse / General Support (Support)

Choose for team ATK buffs, healing and off-field Electro; personal CRIT investment is secondary.

  • Weapons: Favonius Lance, Symphonist of Scents, Engulfing Lightning
  • Artifacts: Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Meet ER needs before ATK%. CRIT Rate matters mainly for Favonius; Healing Bonus for sustain.
  • Teams: Works when no other teammate holds Noblesse, including non-Stellar-Conduct teams.
  • When it applies: Favonius is an accessible default; requirements vary greatly by team and constellation.

Low-Investment C2+ (Support)

Use for shorter rotations or resource-efficient builds where every-other-rotation Burst uptime is sufficient.

  • Weapons: Favonius Lance, Tamayuratei no Ohanashi
  • Artifacts: Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ER needs can fall sharply when Bursting every other rotation. Retain enough ER for content requiring faster uptime.
  • Teams: C2 or higher; a team rotation that accommodates alternating Bursts.
  • When it applies: At C0–C1, plan for significantly higher ER if Bursting every rotation.

Alyosha talents

Normal Attack: Skirmishing Spear

Normal Attack
Performs up to 4 consecutive spear strikes and applies the Hunter's Mark to the opponent hit by the final strike.

Charged Attack
Consumes a certain amount of Stamina to lunge forward, dealing damage to opponents in his path.

Plunging Attack
Plunges from mid-air to strike the ground below, damaging opponents along the path and dealing AoE DMG upon impact.

Elemental Skill: Thunderbolt Strike

Cooldown: 15s

Follows his hunter's instincts. Tap or hold to produce different effects as well as apply the Hunter's Mark effect to the hit opponents.

Tapping
Fires at opponents in front of him, dealing AoE Electro DMG.

Holding
Enters aiming mode, then targets opponents within a certain area in front of him. Alyosha's Interruption RES is enhanced when in this mode.
At the end of the button hold, he deals Electro DMG to the targeted opponents.

Elemental Burst: Hunter's Advance

Cooldown: 18s · Energy cost: 70

Summons his trusted companion Tugarin to fight alongside him on the field. This also turns an area in front of him into a Fulgurite Hunting Field for a short time.

Fulgurite Hunting Field
· Continuously taunts nearby opponents to incite them to attack.
· Every 2s, deals an instance of AoE Electro DMG to any opponent that enters the field.

Tugarin
· If there are any opponents nearby, Tugarin will quickly move close to an opponent and maul them every 2s, dealing Electro DMG.
· If an opponent affected by the Hunter's Mark effect is hit, the Hunter's Mark will also be activated.
· Where there are multiple opponents, Tugarin will attack those affected by the Hunter's Mark effect first.

Passive talent: Awakened by the Baying Hounds

When Tugarin attacks, it will also restore HP to nearby active characters at 120% of Alyosha's ATK.

Passive talent: Suffer the Winter Wheat Will

Increases the DMG Alyosha deals with his Elemental Skill and Elemental Burst by 0.35% for every 1% of his Energy Recharge. Up to a 70% increase can be obtained in this way.

Passive talent: Into the Fray

Alyosha will enter the Radiance: Stellar-Conduct state when inside a Polestar Field.
Radiance: Stellar-Conduct: The Hunter's Precision effect obtained when the Hunter's Mark is activated will also increase Stellar-Conduct reaction DMG dealt by the character when on the field by 20%.

Passive talent: Treetop Watch

Displays the location of nearby resources unique to Snezhnaya on the mini-map, and this effect also applies in the Shadow Realm.

Alyosha constellations

C1: Frostvale Thunderclap

Alyosha regenerates 15 Elemental Energy when nearby party members trigger an Electro-related reaction on an opponent. This effect can trigger once every 18s.

C2: Howl From Afar

The duration of the Elemental Burst Hunter's Advance is extended by 6s.
Additionally, each time Tugarin attacks the opponent, he will also apply the Hunter's Mark to a target. This effect does not activate existing Hunter's Marks.

C3: Friendly Call

Increases the Level of the Elemental Skill Thunderbolt Strike by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C4: Harvest the Spoils

When Tugarin attacks, he will also restore 60% of Alyosha's ATK as HP to the nearby party member with the lowest HP by percentage.

C5: When the Nightbird Falls Silent

Increases the Level of the Elemental Burst Hunter's Advance by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C6: Standard Reclaimed

The Hunter's Precision effect obtained from activating a Hunter's Mark can now stack, max 2 stacks. When the stack count reaches 2, characters affected by Hunter's Precision will also have their Elemental Mastery increased by 100 if they are on the field.

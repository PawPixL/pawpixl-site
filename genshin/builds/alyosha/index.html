Alyosha Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts & Teams

Alyosha build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Favonius Lance, Symphonist of Scents), artifacts (Noblesse Oblige), main stats (ER / ATK% / ATK% /…

Role: Support

Alyosha heals the active character and buffs ATK in Electro teams. In Stellar-Conduct teams, his buffs become more specialized. Focus on ER and Skill levels.

Best weapons for Alyosha

  1. Favonius Lance
  2. Symphonist of Scents
  3. Frostbreath
  4. Engulfing Lightning
  5. Skyward Spine
  6. Tamayuratei no Ohanashi
  7. "The Catch"

Best artifacts

  • Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs) · When Furnace Is Covered
  • Heart of the Furnace (4 pcs) · Stellar-Conduct Support
  • Gladiator's Finale + A Day Carved From Rising Winds (2 + 2 pcs) · Support

Main stats

  • Sands: ER / ATK%
  • Goblet: ATK%
  • Circlet: ATK% / Healing / CR

Stat targets

  • ER: Team-based
  • ATK: After ER
  • CR: Favonius only

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 9+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 6–8 (lower priority)

Best teams

  • Dogs Gone Wild: Alyosha, Sandrone, Mitya, Odette
  • Frost & Found: Alyosha, Sandrone, qiqi, Odette
  • Thunderstruck: Alyosha, Cyno, Traveler (Cryo), Odette
  • Discount Treats: Alyosha, Beidou, Traveler (Cryo), Diona

Pros

  • Teamwide Utility
  • Consistent Healing
  • Strong Buffs
  • Off-Field Electro

Cons

  • Low Damage
  • Niche Support
  • On-Field Dependent
  • Reaction Reliant

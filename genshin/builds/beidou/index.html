Beidou Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

Beidou build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Beacon of the Reed Sea, A Thousand Blazing Suns), artifacts (Emblem of Severed Fate), main stats.

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Role: Sub DPS

Beidou deals off-field Electro damage through her bouncing Burst and counters attacks with her Skill. She excels in multi-target fights but often needs an Electro battery and enough ER.

Best weapons for Beidou

  1. Beacon of the Reed Sea: CRIT and ATK work for Burst damage. Shielding limits its damage-taken passive.
  2. A Thousand Blazing Suns: Strong ATK and CRIT stats, but Beidou cannot extend its buff with Nightsoul.
  3. Wolf's Gravestone: High ATK; party buff requires hitting a low-HP enemy.
  4. Verdict: CRIT and ATK are useful; Crystallize-based Skill bonus is situational.
  5. Serpent Spine: Stacked DMG Bonus snapshots into Burst. Often her strongest choice at R5. Maintain stacks before Burst. R5 very strong.
  6. Akuoumaru: Burst DMG increases with teammates’ combined Energy costs. Improves with refinements.
  7. Tidal Shadow: Craftable ATK weapon; passive requires receiving healing.
  8. "Ultimate Overlord's Mega Magic Sword": Event-only ER and ATK; extra ATK depends on Melusine quests.

Best artifacts for Beidou

  • Emblem of Severed Fate (4 pcs) · Best Overall: ER converts to Burst DMG, ideal for Stormbreaker. Main stats: ATK% or ER / Electro DMG / CRIT.
  • Noblesse Oblige + Thundering Fury (2 + 2 pcs) · Flexible Alternative: Burst DMG plus Electro DMG; a flexible 2+2 alternative. Main stats: ATK% or ER / Electro DMG / CRIT.
  • Thundersoother (4 pcs) · Electro Aura Teams: Damage bonus against Electro-affected enemies; aura-dependent. Main stats: ATK% or ER / Electro DMG / CRIT.

Main stats

  • Sands: ATK% / ER
  • Goblet: Electro DMG
  • Circlet: CR / CD

Stat targets

  • ER: 140–180% (battery)
  • CR: 60–75%
  • CD: 120–180%
  • ATK: 1,600–2,200

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Not Priority
  • Elemental Skill: Level 8 - 9
  • Elemental Burst: Level 9+

Best teams for Beidou

  • Thunderous Verdict: Beidou, Clorinde, Nahida, Fischl. Clorinde drives Beidou’s Burst with her Normal Attacks.
  • Overload Armada: Beidou, Arlecchino, Chevreuse, Fischl. Arlecchino drives Pyro damage, Fischl and Beidou provide Electro, and Chevreuse buffs the Pyro/Electro team.
  • Blooming Thunder: Beidou, Nahida, Xingqiu, Kuki Shinobu. Nahida drives Dendro and Xingqiu adds Hydro. Kuki triggers Hyperbloom, while Beidou deals Burst damage without targeting cores.
  • Captain’s Taser: Beidou, Sucrose, Xingqiu, Fischl. Sucrose drives Beidou and Xingqiu with rapid attacks, groups enemies, and shreds resistance.

Pros

  • Strong AoE damage
  • Defensive utility
  • Powerful at C2
  • Flexible teams

Cons

  • High ER needs
  • Weak in single-target
  • Counter timing
  • Needs on-field driver

Build variations

Burst DPS / Multi-target (Off-field DPS)

Best when lightning chains can bounce across at least two enemies.

  • Weapons: Serpent Spine, Beacon of the Reed Sea, Wolf's Gravestone, Akuoumaru
  • Artifacts: Emblem of Severed Fate (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Reach ER needs first, then balance CRIT and ATK%. Electro DMG Goblet.
  • Teams: A Normal Attack driver plus preferably Fischl for energy.
  • When it applies: Serpent Spine is strongest with high stacks and refinements.

Solo Electro Energy (Off-field DPS)

Use when Beidou is the only Electro unit and needs higher ER to Burst every rotation.

  • Weapons: Serpent Spine, "Ultimate Overlord's Mega Magic Sword", Tidal Shadow
  • Artifacts: Emblem of Severed Fate (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Prioritize more ER than in double-Electro teams; ER Sands may be necessary.
  • Teams: A team without an Electro battery; Favonius allies can help.
  • When it applies: Actual ER varies widely; calculate for team and rotation.

Aggravate Flex (Off-field DPS)

Pair her off-field lightning with a Dendro reaction team for additional Aggravate damage.

  • Weapons: Serpent Spine, Beacon of the Reed Sea, Akuoumaru
  • Artifacts: Emblem of Severed Fate (4 pcs)
  • Stats: CRIT and ATK remain strong; EM can gain value with frequent Aggravate.
  • Teams: Dendro teammate maintaining Quicken and an Electro battery.
  • When it applies: Spread damage is not generated by Beidou herself.

Hyperbloom Side Damage (Off-field DPS)

Beidou adds off-field damage while a teammate triggers Hyperbloom cores.

  • Weapons: "Ultimate Overlord's Mega Magic Sword", Serpent Spine, Tidal Shadow
  • Artifacts: Emblem of Severed Fate (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Stay on a normal CRIT / ATK / ER build; do not stack full EM on Beidou for this role.
  • Teams: Hydro, Dendro and a separate reliable Hyperbloom trigger such as Kuki.
  • When it applies: Her Burst lightning does not target Dendro Cores.

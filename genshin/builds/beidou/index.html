Beidou Build Guide | Best Weapons, Artifacts & Teams

Beidou build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Beacon of the Reed Sea, A Thousand Blazing Suns), artifacts (Emblem of Severed Fate), main stats…

Role: Sub DPS

Beidou deals off-field Electro damage through her bouncing Burst and counters attacks with her Skill. She excels in multi-target fights but often needs an Electro battery and enough ER.

Best weapons for Beidou

  1. Beacon of the Reed Sea
  2. A Thousand Blazing Suns
  3. Wolf's Gravestone
  4. Verdict
  5. Serpent Spine
  6. Akuoumaru
  7. Tidal Shadow
  8. "Ultimate Overlord's Mega Magic Sword"

Best artifacts

  • Emblem of Severed Fate (4 pcs) · Best Overall
  • Noblesse Oblige + Thundering Fury (2 + 2 pcs) · Flexible Alternative
  • Thundersoother (4 pcs) · Electro Aura Teams

Main stats

  • Sands: ATK% / ER
  • Goblet: Electro DMG
  • Circlet: CR / CD

Stat targets

  • ER: 140–180% (battery)
  • CR: 60–75%
  • CD: 120–180%
  • ATK: 1,600–2,200

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Not Priority
  • Elemental Skill: Level 8 - 9
  • Elemental Burst: Level 9+

Best teams

  • Thunderous Verdict: Beidou, Clorinde, Nahida, Fischl
  • Overload Armada: Beidou, Arlecchino, Chevreuse, Fischl
  • Blooming Thunder: Beidou, Nahida, Xingqiu, Kuki Shinobu
  • Captain’s Taser: Beidou, Sucrose, Xingqiu, Fischl

Pros

  • Strong AoE damage
  • Defensive utility
  • Powerful at C2
  • Flexible teams

Cons

  • High ER needs
  • Weak in single-target
  • Counter timing
  • Needs on-field driver

Build variations

  • Burst DPS / Multi-target (Off-field DPS): Best when lightning chains can bounce across at least two enemies.
  • Solo Electro Energy (Off-field DPS): Use when Beidou is the only Electro unit and needs higher ER to Burst every rotation.
  • Aggravate Flex (Off-field DPS): Pair her off-field lightning with a Dendro reaction team for additional Aggravate damage.
  • Hyperbloom Side Damage (Off-field DPS): Beidou adds off-field damage while a teammate triggers Hyperbloom cores.

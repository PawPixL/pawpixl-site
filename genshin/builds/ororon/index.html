Ororon Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

Ororon build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Elegy for the End, Polar Star), artifacts (Scroll of the Hero of Cinder City), main stats.

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Role: Sub DPS / Support

Ororon is an off-field Electro damage dealer and team buffer who excels in Electro-Charged and Nightsoul teams. His Hypersense passive deals much of his damage, while Scroll of the Hero of Cinder City buffs teammates. He wants reliable Hydro or Nightsoul damage to trigger his passive.

Best weapons for Ororon

  1. Elegy for the End: Excellent teamwide ATK and EM buffs with helpful ER.
  2. Polar Star: High CR and ATK help Ororon’s passive DMG.
  3. Aqua Simulacra: Large CRIT DMG and a general damage bonus strengthen Hypersense. Stay near enemies to activate its passive.
  4. Skyward Harp: Strong CRIT Rate, CRIT DMG, and Base ATK improve Ororon’s off-field damage without restrictive team conditions.
  5. Alley Hunter: ATK and off-field damage bonuses work well with Hypersense. Builds stacks while Ororon stays off the field. More competitive at R5.
  6. Favonius Warbow: Great team battery that lowers Energy needs. Trade some personal damage for smoother Bursts. Build enough CRIT Rate to activate its passive.
  7. Chain Breaker: Excellent craftable bow. Its ATK buff supports Hypersense, with extra EM for reactions. R5 is a strong F2P goal.
  8. Fading Twilight: Free event bow with ER and a rotating damage bonus. A comfortable option if you already own it.

Best artifacts for Ororon

  • Scroll of the Hero of Cinder City (4 pcs): Best general support set. Main stats: ATK% or ER / Electro DMG / CRIT.
  • Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs): Use when another teammate already carries Scroll and the team benefits from an ATK buff after Burst. Main stats: ATK% or ER / Electro DMG / CRIT.
  • Instructor (4 pcs): Budget team EM support if Scroll is already covered. Requires on-field reaction activation and lowers personal damage. Main stats: ER or ATK% / Electro DMG / CRIT.

Main stats

  • Sands: ATK% / ER
  • Goblet: Electro DMG / ATK%
  • Circlet: CRIT Rate / CRIT DMG

Stat targets

  • ER: 160–195% solo Electro
  • ER: 100–120% double Electro
  • ER: 130–145% with Neuvillette
  • CRIT Rate: 60–70%
  • CRIT DMG: 120–160%+

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 6–8
  • Elemental Burst: Level 8–9

Best teams for Ororon

  • Midnight Tides: Ororon, Neuvillette, Furina, Kaedehara Kazuha. Neuvillette and Furina enable Electro-Charged. Ororon buffs Hydro and Electro with Scroll while Kazuha groups foes and shreds resistance.
  • Moonlit Circuit: Ororon, Columbina, Ifa, Ineffa. Columbina and Ineffa enable Lunar-Charged, while Ifa drives reactions and heals. Ororon adds Electro damage and Scroll buffs.
  • Overload Harvest: Ororon, Mavuika, Chevreuse, Iansan. Mavuika and Iansan provide Pyro and ATK support. Ororon adds Electro and Nightsoul utility, while Chevreuse boosts Overload teams.
  • Budget Night Shift: Ororon, Barbara, Fischl, Sucrose. Barbara drives Electro-Charged and heals. Fischl adds off-field Electro, Sucrose groups and shreds resistance, and Ororon buffs both elements.

Pros

  • Strong off-field damage
  • 40% elemental team buff
  • Excellent Electro-Charged
  • Helps team Energy
  • Useful Burst taunt

Cons

  • Needs specific team triggers
  • Burst can miss mobile foes
  • ER varies by team
  • Limited single-target DMG
  • Long cooldowns

Build variations

Electro-Charged Buffer (Off-field DPS / Support)

Scroll buffs the carry while Ororon contributes off-field Electro.

  • Weapons: Elegy for the End, Favonius Warbow
  • Artifacts: Scroll of the Hero of Cinder City (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ER for Burst uptime and CRIT for personal damage.
  • Teams: Hydro application; consistent Electro-Charged.
  • When it applies: Use qualifying elemental reactions for Scroll.

Favonius Battery (Support)

Trade personal damage for more reliable team Energy.

  • Weapons: Favonius Warbow, Alley Hunter
  • Artifacts: Scroll of the Hero of Cinder City (4 pcs); Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Raise CRIT Rate enough to trigger Favonius; meet ER needs.
  • Teams: Energy-hungry team that can use his particles.
  • When it applies: Favonius must land CRIT hits while Ororon is on-field.

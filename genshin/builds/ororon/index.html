Ororon Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts & Teams

Ororon build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Elegy for the End, Polar Star), artifacts (Scroll of the Hero of Cinder City), main stats (ATK% / ER…

Role: Sub DPS / Support

Ororon is an off-field Electro damage dealer and team buffer who excels in Electro-Charged and Nightsoul teams. His Hypersense passive deals much of his damage, while Scroll of the Hero of Cinder City buffs teammates. He wants reliable Hydro or Nightsoul damage to trigger his passive.

Best weapons for Ororon

  1. Elegy for the End
  2. Polar Star
  3. Aqua Simulacra
  4. Skyward Harp
  5. Alley Hunter
  6. Favonius Warbow
  7. Chain Breaker
  8. Fading Twilight

Best artifacts

  • Scroll of the Hero of Cinder City (4 pcs)
  • Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs)
  • Instructor (4 pcs)

Main stats

  • Sands: ATK% / ER
  • Goblet: Electro DMG / ATK%
  • Circlet: CRIT Rate / CRIT DMG

Stat targets

  • ER: 160–195% solo Electro
  • ER: 100–120% double Electro
  • ER: 130–145% with Neuvillette
  • CRIT Rate: 60–70%
  • CRIT DMG: 120–160%+

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 6–8
  • Elemental Burst: Level 8–9

Best teams

  • Midnight Tides: Ororon, Neuvillette, Furina, Kaedehara Kazuha
  • Moonlit Circuit: Ororon, Columbina, Ifa, Ineffa
  • Overload Harvest: Ororon, Mavuika, Chevreuse, Iansan
  • Budget Night Shift: Ororon, Barbara, Fischl, Sucrose

Pros

  • Strong off-field damage
  • 40% elemental team buff
  • Excellent Electro-Charged
  • Helps team Energy
  • Useful Burst taunt

Cons

  • Needs specific team triggers
  • Burst can miss mobile foes
  • ER varies by team
  • Limited single-target DMG
  • Long cooldowns

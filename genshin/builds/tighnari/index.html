Role: Main DPS

Tighnari is a quick-swap Dendro DPS who excels in Spread teams. His Skill greatly shortens the charge time of his next three Wreath Arrows, while his Burst adds fast Dendro damage. Rotate through Skill, three Charged Attacks, and Burst, then swap to Electro teammates to maintain Quicken.

  2. Aqua Simulacra
  3. Polar Star
  4. The First Great Magic
  5. Skyward Harp
  6. Elegy for the End
  7. Slingshot
  8. Song of Stillness

Main stats

  • Sands: Elemental Mastery / ATK%

Stat targets

  • CRIT Rate: 60% - 75%
  • CRIT DMG: 130% - 200%+
  • Elemental Mastery: 250 - 450+
  • Energy Recharge: 100% - 140% (Team-dependent)
  • ER: 150% - 160% (Solo Dendro, Burst each Skill)

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 8-9
  • Elemental Skill: Level 6-8
  • Elemental Burst: Level 8-9+

Best teams

  • Forest of Thunder: Tighnari, Nahida, Yae Miko, Zhongli
  • Foxfire Circuit: Tighnari, Yae Miko, Fischl, Baizhu
  • Greenhouse Effect: Tighnari, Nahida, Fischl, Kuki Shinobu
  • Budget Botanists: Tighnari, Traveler (Dendro), Fischl, Kuki Shinobu

Pros

  • Fast Charged Attacks
  • Strong Spread damage
  • Flexible rotations
  • Excellent F2P bow

Cons

  • Needs Electro uptime
  • Mostly single-target
  • Weak without Quicken
  • Limited off-field damage

Build variations

  • Hunter’s Path Spread (Quick-Swap DPS): Choose when using his signature bow to maximize EM-based Charged Attack damage.
  • Solo Deepwood Carrier (Quick-Swap DPS): Use when Tighnari must provide the 30% Dendro RES shred himself.
  • F2P Spread (Quick-Swap DPS): Affordable Spread setup built around R5 Slingshot and accessible teammates.
  • Flexible Burst Rotation (Quick-Swap DPS): Trade frequent Bursts for stronger offensive stats in flexible 12-second quick-swap cycles.

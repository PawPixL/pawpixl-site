Tighnari Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

Tighnari build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Hunter's Path, Aqua Simulacra), artifacts (Wanderer's Troupe), main stats, talent priority, teams.

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Role: Main DPS

Tighnari is a quick-swap Dendro DPS who excels in Spread teams. His Skill greatly shortens the charge time of his next three Wreath Arrows, while his Burst adds fast Dendro damage. Rotate through Skill, three Charged Attacks, and Burst, then swap to Electro teammates to maintain Quicken.

Best weapons for Tighnari

  1. Hunter's Path: CRIT Rate and Elemental DMG boost pair with a Charged Attack bonus based on EM. His strongest personal damage weapon. Prioritize an EM Sands.
  2. Aqua Simulacra: Massive CRIT DMG and a nearby-enemy DMG bonus enhance Charged Attacks and Burst. Stay reasonably close to enemies for the passive.
  3. Polar Star: CR and stacking ATK improve DMG.
  4. The First Great Magic: CD and Charged Attack DMG provide strong value.
  5. Skyward Harp: High Base ATK plus CRIT Rate and CRIT DMG make this a reliable all-purpose damage option.
  6. Elegy for the End: Energy Recharge and its teamwide EM and ATK buff benefit Quicken teams, trading some personal damage for team output. Team-focused choice, especially with higher ER needs.
  7. Slingshot: Excellent 3-star option: high CRIT Rate and a large Charged Attack DMG bonus when shots land quickly. Stay close enough to trigger the fast-hit passive. R5 recommended.
  8. Song of Stillness: Craftable ATK% bow that increases damage after receiving healing. Useful when paired with an off-field healer. Needs reliable healing to maintain its passive. R5 recommended.

Best artifacts for Tighnari

  • Wanderer's Troupe (4 pcs) · Best: 80 EM and 35% Charged Attack DMG directly strengthen his main damage source. Excellent general-purpose choice. Main stats: EM/ATK% · Dendro DMG · CRIT.
  • Gilded Dreams (4 pcs): Grants EM and/or ATK after reactions. Main stats: EM/ATK% · Dendro DMG · CRIT.
  • Deepwood Memories (4 pcs): Reduces enemy Dendro RES by 30%. Main stats: EM/ATK% · Dendro DMG · CRIT.

Main stats

  • Sands: Elemental Mastery / ATK%
  • Goblet: Dendro DMG Bonus
  • Circlet: CRIT Rate / CRIT DMG

Stat targets

  • CRIT Rate: 60% - 75%
  • CRIT DMG: 130% - 200%+
  • Elemental Mastery: 250 - 450+
  • Energy Recharge: 100% - 140% (Team-dependent)
  • ER: 150% - 160% (Solo Dendro, Burst each Skill)

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 8-9
  • Elemental Skill: Level 6-8
  • Elemental Burst: Level 8-9+

Best teams for Tighnari

  • Forest of Thunder: Tighnari (Main DPS), Nahida (Dendro Support), Yae Miko (Off-Field DPS), Zhongli (Shield). Nahida supplies EM and Deepwood shred, Yae maintains Quicken, and Zhongli shields Tighnari during aimed shots.
  • Foxfire Circuit: Tighnari (Main DPS), Yae Miko (Off-Field DPS), Fischl (Off-Field DPS), Baizhu (Healer). Double Electro maintains Quicken, while Baizhu heals, protects, and can hold Deepwood to boost Tighnari’s Spread damage.
  • Greenhouse Effect: Tighnari (Main DPS), Nahida (Dendro Support), Fischl (Off-Field DPS), Kuki Shinobu (Healer). Nahida shares EM and shreds Dendro RES, Fischl applies Electro, and Kuki adds healing and Quicken uptime.
  • Budget Botanists: Tighnari (Main DPS), Traveler (Dendro) (Dendro Support), Fischl (Off-Field DPS), Kuki Shinobu (Healer). Dendro Traveler can carry Deepwood; Fischl and Kuki sustain Quicken, provide Electro damage, and keep the team comfortable.

Build variations

Hunter’s Path Spread (Quick-Swap DPS)

Choose when using his signature bow to maximize EM-based Charged Attack damage.

  • Weapons: Hunter's Path, Aqua Simulacra
  • Artifacts: Gilded Dreams (4 pcs); Wanderer's Troupe (4 pcs)
  • Stats: EM Sands strongly preferred; prioritize CRIT and EM after meeting ER needs.
  • Teams: Keep Quicken active with an Electro teammate; another character should hold Deepwood.
  • When it applies: Hunter’s Path equipped
  • Notes: Gilded Dreams can slightly outperform Wanderer’s Troupe with Hunter’s Path.

Solo Deepwood Carrier (Quick-Swap DPS)

Use when Tighnari must provide the 30% Dendro RES shred himself.

  • Weapons: Hunter's Path, Slingshot
  • Artifacts: Deepwood Memories (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Keep normal EM/ATK%, Dendro DMG, CRIT stats; set bonus replaces a stronger personal damage set.
  • Teams: No other reliable Deepwood Memories user.
  • When it applies: No other teammate using 4-piece Deepwood

F2P Spread (Quick-Swap DPS)

Affordable Spread setup built around R5 Slingshot and accessible teammates.

  • Weapons: Slingshot, Song of Stillness
  • Artifacts: Wanderer's Troupe (4 pcs); Deepwood Memories (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Aim for 60–75% CRIT Rate, solid CRIT DMG and roughly 250–450+ EM; adjust for weapon.
  • Teams: Fischl or another consistent Electro applier; healer needed for Song of Stillness.
  • When it applies: Slingshot passive needs close-range hits
  • Notes: Slingshot is a 3-star weapon and can beat many 4-star alternatives.

Flexible Burst Rotation (Quick-Swap DPS)

Trade frequent Bursts for stronger offensive stats in flexible 12-second quick-swap cycles.

  • Weapons: Skyward Harp, Elegy for the End
  • Artifacts: Wanderer's Troupe (4 pcs); Gilded Dreams (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Around 100–110% ER for two Skills per Burst; ~130–160% may be needed for one Skill per Burst.
  • Teams: Maintain Quicken and choose the rotation that fits team energy generation.
  • When it applies: ER depends strongly on particles and Favonius procs

Tighnari talents

Normal Attack: Khanda Barrier-Buster

Normal Attack
Performs up to 4 consecutive shots with a bow.

Charged Attack
Performs a more precise Aimed Shot with increased DMG.
While aiming, the power of Dendro will accumulate on the arrowhead before the arrow is fired. Has different effects based on how long the energy has been charged:
·Charge Level 1: Fires off an arrow carrying the power of flora that deals Dendro DMG.
·Charge Level 2: Fires off a Wreath Arrow that deals Dendro DMG. Upon hit, the Wreath Arrow will create 4 Clusterbloom Arrows that will track nearby opponents automatically and deal Dendro DMG.

Plunging Attack
Fires off a shower of arrows in mid-air before falling and striking the ground, dealing AoE DMG upon impact.

Elemental Skill: Vijnana-Phala Mine

Cooldown: 12s

Tighnari throws a Vijnana Stormheart ahead that deals AoE Dendro DMG, creating a Vijnana-Khanda Field that creates mysterious illusions that taunt opponents and draw their fire.
Additionally, Tighnari gains the Vijnana Suffusion effect, which will decrease the Wreath Arrow's charging time by 2.4s. This effect will dissipate once the skill duration ends or after Tighnari has fired 3 Wreath Arrows.

Elemental Burst: Fashioner's Tanglevine Shaft

Cooldown: 12s · Energy cost: 40

Combines the power of all seeds to fire 6 Tanglevine Shafts that can track opponents and deal Dendro DMG.
After they hit, the Tanglevine Shafts will create a secondary wave of Tanglevine Shafts that can also track nearby opponents and deal Dendro DMG on hit.

Passive talent: Keen Sight

After Tighnari fires a Wreath Arrow, his Elemental Mastery is increased by 50 for 4s.

Passive talent: Scholarly Blade

For every point of Elemental Mastery Tighnari possesses, his Charged Attack and Fashioner's Tanglevine Shaft DMG are increased by 0.06%.
The maximum DMG Bonus obtainable this way is 60%.

Passive talent: Encyclopedic Knowledge

Displays the location of nearby resources unique to Sumeru on the mini-map.

Tighnari constellations

C1: Beginnings Determined at the Roots

Tighnari's Charged Attack CRIT Rate is increased by 15%.

C2: Origins Known From the Stem

When there are opponents within the Vijnana-Khanda Field created by Vijnana-Phala Mine, Tighnari gains 20% Dendro DMG Bonus.
The effect will last up to 6s if the field's duration ends or if it no longer has opponents within it.

C3: Fortunes Read Amongst the Branches

Increases the Level of Fashioner's Tanglevine Shaft by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C4: Withering Glimpsed in the Leaves

When Fashioner's Tanglevine Shaft is unleashed, all nearby party members gain 60 Elemental Mastery for 8s. If the Fashioner's Tanglevine Shaft triggers a Burning, Bloom, Lunar-Bloom, Quicken, or Spread reaction, their Elemental Mastery will be further increased by 60. This latter case will also refresh the buff state's duration.

C5: Comprehension Amidst the Flowers

Increases the Level of Vijnana-Phala Mine by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C6: Karma Adjudged From the Leaden Fruit

Wreath Arrow's charging time is decreased by 0.9s, and will produce 1 additional Clusterbloom Arrow upon hit. This arrow deals 150% of Tighnari's ATK as DMG.

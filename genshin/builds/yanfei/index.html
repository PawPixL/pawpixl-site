Yanfei Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams (7.2)

Yanfei build guide for Genshin Impact 7.2: best weapons (Lost Prayer to the Sacred Winds, Cashflow Supervision), artifacts (Crimson Witch of Flames).

Last updated: November 12, 2026 · Game version 7.2

Role: Main DPS

Yanfei is an on-field Pyro DPS who powers up her Charged Attacks with Scarlet Seals. She excels in Vaporize teams and can also drive Overloaded reactions. Her attacks reach distant enemies, but she appreciates healing or a shield to avoid interruptions.

Best weapons for Yanfei

  1. Lost Prayer to the Sacred Winds: Excellent CRIT Rate and growing Elemental DMG while Yanfei stays on the field. Best for long field time.
  2. Cashflow Supervision: Huge CRIT Rate and Charged Attack bonuses when her HP changes. Best with Furina and consistent healing.
  3. Tome of the Eternal Flow: High CRIT DMG and a powerful Charged Attack bonus when HP changes. Best with Furina and a healer.
  4. Skyward Atlas: Provides high ATK and an unconditional Elemental DMG bonus. A consistent premium option.
  5. The Widsith: Massive short-term buffs make this a top four-star choice. The random passive and downtime affect consistency. Especially strong in Vaporize. R5 is excellent.
  6. Sacrificial Jade: CRIT Rate and EM help Vaporize. Its off-field activation limits longer on-field rotations. Best when rotating her out regularly.
  7. Flowing Purity: Craftable ATK catalyst that boosts Elemental DMG after her Skill. Healing clears its Bond of Life for another bonus. Works best with a strong healer.
  8. Mappa Mare: Craftable EM option that increases Elemental DMG after Yanfei triggers reactions. Use in reaction teams. Refinements improve its bonus.

Best artifacts for Yanfei

  • Crimson Witch of Flames (4 pcs): Best general Vaporize set. Buffs Pyro damage and Pyro reaction damage. Main stats: ATK% / EM, Pyro DMG, CRIT.
  • Marechaussee Hunter (4 pcs): Best with Furina when team HP changes reliably. Provides up to 36% CRIT Rate. Main stats: ATK% / EM, Pyro DMG, CRIT DMG.
  • Wanderer's Troupe (4 pcs): Offers EM and an unconditional Charged Attack damage bonus. Excellent for traditional Yanfei builds. Main stats: ATK% / EM, Pyro DMG, CRIT.

Main stats

  • Sands: ATK% / EM
  • Goblet: Pyro DMG Bonus
  • Circlet: CRIT Rate / CRIT DMG

Stat targets

  • CRIT Rate: 60% - 75%
  • CRIT DMG: 120% - 180%
  • ATK: 1,600 - 2,100
  • EM: 100 - 250 (Vape)
  • ER: 110% - 140% (variable)

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 9+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 6–8
  • Elemental Burst: Level 8–9

Best teams for Yanfei

  • Courtroom Deluge: Yanfei (On-field DPS), Furina (Off-field DPS / Buffer), Xingqiu (Off-field DPS / Hydro Enabler), Xianyun (Healer / Plunge Buffer). Furina buffs the team, Xingqiu sustains Hydro for Vaporize, and Xianyun heals while enabling Yanfei to Vaporize plunges.
  • Crimson Testimony: Yanfei (On-field DPS), Yelan (Off-field DPS / Buffer), Bennett (Healer / Buffer), Kaedehara Kazuha (Buffer / Debuffer). Yelan applies Hydro and buffs Yanfei. Bennett boosts ATK and heals, while Kazuha shreds Pyro resistance and groups enemies.
  • Overload Objection: Yanfei (On-field DPS), Fischl (Off-field DPS / Battery), Beidou (Off-field DPS), Chevreuse (Healer / Buffer). Fischl and Beidou provide off-field Electro damage. Chevreuse buffs Pyro and Electro while Yanfei drives Overloaded.
  • Budget Verdict: Yanfei (On-field DPS), Xingqiu (Off-field DPS / Hydro Enabler), Bennett (Healer / Buffer), Sucrose (EM Buffer / Debuffer). Xingqiu enables Vaporize, Bennett heals and buffs ATK, and Sucrose shares EM while reducing Pyro resistance.

Pros

  • Strong Charged Attacks
  • Reliable Pyro application
  • Great Vaporize damage
  • Accessible weapon options

Cons

  • Charged Attacks use stamina
  • Needs field time
  • Can be interrupted
  • Burst needs Energy

Build variations

Vaporize Charged Attacks (On-field DPS)

Use Scarlet Seal stacks for amplified Charged Attack damage.

  • Weapons: Lost Prayer to the Sacred Winds, The Widsith
  • Artifacts: Crimson Witch of Flames (4 pcs); Wanderer's Troupe (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ATK% or EM Sands; Pyro DMG and CRIT.
  • Teams: Reliable Hydro to Vaporize Charged Attacks.
  • When it applies: Manage stamina and reaction aura.

Furina CRIT Carry (On-field DPS)

Use Furina’s HP changes for a CRIT-heavy alternative.

  • Weapons: Cashflow Supervision, The Widsith
  • Artifacts: Marechaussee Hunter (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Build around Marechaussee CRIT Rate.
  • Teams: Furina plus consistent healing.
  • When it applies: Only when HP fluctuations are reliable.

C4 Shield Support (Support)

Use Yanfei’s Burst shield as a situational support option.

  • Weapons: The Widsith, Sacrificial Jade
  • Artifacts: Wanderer's Troupe (4 pcs); Crimson Witch of Flames (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ER and HP for shield reliability; personal damage secondary.
  • Teams: C4 Yanfei and a team needing protection.
  • When it applies: C4 is required; high ER may be needed.

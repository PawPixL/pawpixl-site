Yanfei Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

Yanfei build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Lost Prayer to the Sacred Winds, Cashflow Supervision), artifacts (Crimson Witch of Flames).

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Role: Main DPS

Yanfei is an on-field Pyro DPS who powers up her Charged Attacks with Scarlet Seals. She excels in Vaporize teams and can also drive Overloaded reactions. Her attacks reach distant enemies, but she appreciates healing or a shield to avoid interruptions.

Best weapons for Yanfei

  1. Lost Prayer to the Sacred Winds: Excellent CRIT Rate and growing Elemental DMG while Yanfei stays on the field. Best for long field time.
  2. Cashflow Supervision: Huge CRIT Rate and Charged Attack bonuses when her HP changes. Best with Furina and consistent healing.
  3. Tome of the Eternal Flow: High CRIT DMG and a powerful Charged Attack bonus when HP changes. Best with Furina and a healer.
  4. Skyward Atlas: Provides high ATK and an unconditional Elemental DMG bonus. A consistent premium option.
  5. The Widsith: Massive short-term buffs make this a top four-star choice. The random passive and downtime affect consistency. Especially strong in Vaporize. R5 is excellent.
  6. Sacrificial Jade: CRIT Rate and EM help Vaporize. Its off-field activation limits longer on-field rotations. Best when rotating her out regularly.
  7. Flowing Purity: Craftable ATK catalyst that boosts Elemental DMG after her Skill. Healing clears its Bond of Life for another bonus. Works best with a strong healer.
  8. Mappa Mare: Craftable EM option that increases Elemental DMG after Yanfei triggers reactions. Use in reaction teams. Refinements improve its bonus.

Best artifacts for Yanfei

  • Crimson Witch of Flames (4 pcs): Best general Vaporize set. Buffs Pyro damage and Pyro reaction damage. Main stats: ATK% / EM, Pyro DMG, CRIT.
  • Marechaussee Hunter (4 pcs): Best with Furina when team HP changes reliably. Provides up to 36% CRIT Rate. Main stats: ATK% / EM, Pyro DMG, CRIT DMG.
  • Wanderer's Troupe (4 pcs): Offers EM and an unconditional Charged Attack damage bonus. Excellent for traditional Yanfei builds. Main stats: ATK% / EM, Pyro DMG, CRIT.

Main stats

  • Sands: ATK% / EM
  • Goblet: Pyro DMG Bonus
  • Circlet: CRIT Rate / CRIT DMG

Stat targets

  • CRIT Rate: 60% - 75%
  • CRIT DMG: 120% - 180%
  • ATK: 1,600 - 2,100
  • EM: 100 - 250 (Vape)
  • ER: 110% - 140% (variable)

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 9+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 6–8
  • Elemental Burst: Level 8–9

Best teams for Yanfei

  • Courtroom Deluge: Yanfei, Furina, Xingqiu, Xianyun. Furina buffs the team, Xingqiu sustains Hydro for Vaporize, and Xianyun heals while enabling Yanfei to Vaporize plunges.
  • Crimson Testimony: Yanfei, Yelan, Bennett, Kaedehara Kazuha. Yelan applies Hydro and buffs Yanfei. Bennett boosts ATK and heals, while Kazuha shreds Pyro resistance and groups enemies.
  • Overload Objection: Yanfei, Fischl, Beidou, Chevreuse. Fischl and Beidou provide off-field Electro damage. Chevreuse buffs Pyro and Electro while Yanfei drives Overloaded.
  • Budget Verdict: Yanfei, Xingqiu, Bennett, Sucrose. Xingqiu enables Vaporize, Bennett heals and buffs ATK, and Sucrose shares EM while reducing Pyro resistance.

Pros

  • Strong Charged Attacks
  • Reliable Pyro application
  • Great Vaporize damage
  • Accessible weapon options

Cons

  • Charged Attacks use stamina
  • Needs field time
  • Can be interrupted
  • Burst needs Energy

Build variations

Vaporize Charged Attacks (On-field DPS)

Use Scarlet Seal stacks for amplified Charged Attack damage.

  • Weapons: Lost Prayer to the Sacred Winds, The Widsith
  • Artifacts: Crimson Witch of Flames (4 pcs); Wanderer's Troupe (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ATK% or EM Sands; Pyro DMG and CRIT.
  • Teams: Reliable Hydro to Vaporize Charged Attacks.
  • When it applies: Manage stamina and reaction aura.

Furina CRIT Carry (On-field DPS)

Use Furina’s HP changes for a CRIT-heavy alternative.

  • Weapons: Cashflow Supervision, The Widsith
  • Artifacts: Marechaussee Hunter (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Build around Marechaussee CRIT Rate.
  • Teams: Furina plus consistent healing.
  • When it applies: Only when HP fluctuations are reliable.

C4 Shield Support (Support)

Use Yanfei’s Burst shield as a situational support option.

  • Weapons: The Widsith, Sacrificial Jade
  • Artifacts: Wanderer's Troupe (4 pcs); Crimson Witch of Flames (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ER and HP for shield reliability; personal damage secondary.
  • Teams: C4 Yanfei and a team needing protection.
  • When it applies: C4 is required; high ER may be needed.

Yanfei talents

Normal Attack: Seal of Approval

Normal Attack
Shoots fireballs that deal up to three counts of Pyro DMG.
When Yanfei's Normal Attacks hit enemies, they will grant her a single Scarlet Seal. Yanfei may possess a maximum of 3 Scarlet Seals, and each time this effect is triggered, the duration of currently possessed Scarlet Seals will refresh.
Each Scarlet Seal will decrease Yanfei's Stamina consumption and will disappear when she leaves the field.

Charged Attack
Consumes Stamina and all Scarlet Seals before dealing AoE Pyro DMG to opponents after a short casting time.
This Charged Attack's AoE and DMG will increase according to the amount of Scarlet Seals consumed.

Plunging Attack
Gathering the power of Pyro, Yanfei plunges towards the ground from mid-air, damaging all opponents in her path. Deals AoE Pyro DMG upon impact with the ground.

Elemental Skill: Signed Edict

Cooldown: 9s

Summons blistering flames that deal AoE Pyro DMG.
Opponents hit by the flames will grant Yanfei the maximum number of Scarlet Seals.

Elemental Burst: Done Deal

Cooldown: 20s · Energy cost: 80

Triggers a spray of intense flames that rush at nearby opponents, dealing AoE Pyro DMG, granting Yanfei the maximum number of Scarlet Seals, and applying Brilliance to her.

Brilliance
Has the following effects:
·Grants Yanfei a Scarlet Seal at fixed intervals.
·Increases the DMG dealt by her Charged Attacks.
The effects of Brilliance will end if Yanfei leaves the field or falls in battle.

Passive talent: Proviso

When Yanfei consumes Scarlet Seals by using a Charged Attack, each Scarlet Seal will increase Yanfei's Pyro DMG Bonus by 5%. This effect lasts for 6s. When a Charged Attack is used again during the effect's duration, it will dispel the previous effect.

Passive talent: Blazing Eye

When Yanfei's Charged Attack deals a CRIT Hit to opponents, she will deal an additional instance of AoE Pyro DMG equal to 80% of her ATK. This DMG counts as Charged Attack DMG.

Passive talent: Encyclopedic Expertise

Displays the location of nearby resources unique to Liyue on the mini-map.

Yanfei constellations

C1: The Law Knows No Kindness

When Yanfei uses her Charged Attack, each existing Scarlet Seal additionally reduces the stamina cost of this Charged Attack by 10% and increases resistance against interruption during its release.

C2: Right of Final Interpretation

Increases Yanfei's Charged Attack CRIT Rate by 20% against enemies below 50% HP.

C3: Samadhi Fire-Forged

Increases the Level of Signed Edict by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C4: Supreme Amnesty

When Done Deal is used:
Creates a shield that absorbs up to 45% of Yanfei's Max HP for 15s.
This shield absorbs Pyro DMG 250% more effectively.

C5: Abiding Affidavit

Increases the Level of Done Deal by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C6: Extra Clause

Increases the maximum number of Scarlet Seals by 1.

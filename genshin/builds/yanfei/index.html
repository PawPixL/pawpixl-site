Yanfei Build Guide | Best Weapons, Artifacts & Teams

Yanfei build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Lost Prayer to the Sacred Winds, Cashflow Supervision), artifacts (Crimson Witch of Flames), main…

Role: Main DPS

Yanfei is an on-field Pyro DPS who powers up her Charged Attacks with Scarlet Seals. She excels in Vaporize teams and can also drive Overloaded reactions. Her attacks reach distant enemies, but she appreciates healing or a shield to avoid interruptions.

Best weapons for Yanfei

  1. Lost Prayer to the Sacred Winds
  2. Cashflow Supervision
  3. Tome of the Eternal Flow
  4. Skyward Atlas
  5. The Widsith
  6. Sacrificial Jade
  7. Flowing Purity
  8. Mappa Mare

Best artifacts

  • Crimson Witch of Flames (4 pcs)
  • Marechaussee Hunter (4 pcs)
  • Wanderer's Troupe (4 pcs)

Main stats

  • Sands: ATK% / EM
  • Goblet: Pyro DMG Bonus
  • Circlet: CRIT Rate / CRIT DMG

Stat targets

  • CRIT Rate: 60% - 75%
  • CRIT DMG: 120% - 180%
  • ATK: 1,600 - 2,100
  • EM: 100 - 250 (Vape)
  • ER: 110% - 140% (variable)

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 9+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 6–8
  • Elemental Burst: Level 8–9

Best teams

  • Courtroom Deluge: Yanfei, Furina, Xingqiu, Xianyun
  • Crimson Testimony: Yanfei, Yelan, Bennett, Kaedehara Kazuha
  • Overload Objection: Yanfei, Fischl, Beidou, Chevreuse
  • Budget Verdict: Yanfei, Xingqiu, Bennett, Sucrose

Pros

  • Strong Charged Attacks
  • Reliable Pyro application
  • Great Vaporize damage
  • Accessible weapon options

Cons

  • Charged Attacks use stamina
  • Needs field time
  • Can be interrupted
  • Burst needs Energy

Build variations

  • Vaporize Charged Attacks (On-field DPS): Use Scarlet Seal stacks for amplified Charged Attack damage.
  • Furina CRIT Carry (On-field DPS): Use Furina’s HP changes for a CRIT-heavy alternative.
  • C4 Shield Support (Support): Use Yanfei’s Burst shield as a situational support option.

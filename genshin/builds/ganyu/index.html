Ganyu Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams (7.2)

Ganyu build guide for Genshin Impact 7.2: best weapons (Astral Vulture's Crimson Plumage, The First Great Magic), artifacts (Wanderer's Troupe).

Last updated: November 12, 2026 · Game version 7.2

Role: Main DPS

Ganyu is a Cryo damage dealer whose Frostflake Charged Shots deliver powerful area damage. She excels in Freeze, Melt, and Burning teams. Freeze helps control groups, while Melt rewards precise aiming and reliable Pyro application. Her Burst also makes her a useful off-field Cryo option.

Best weapons for Ganyu

  1. Astral Vulture's Crimson Plumage: Top premium Melt bow with CRIT DMG and a large Charged Attack bonus after teammates trigger reactions. Strongest in Melt teams.
  2. The First Great Magic: High CRIT DMG and Charged Attack damage. Its ATK bonus improves with additional Cryo teammates. Especially strong in Freeze and Mono Cryo.
  3. Hunter's Path: Provides CRIT Rate and boosts Charged Attack damage using EM, making it a standout Melt option. Best for Melt. Watch CRIT Rate.
  4. Amos' Bow: A classic Ganyu option. Its ATK and Charged Attack bonus grow with arrow travel time, including her Frostflake Bloom. Good in both Melt and Freeze.
  5. Aqua Simulacra: Offers huge CRIT DMG and a damage bonus while Ganyu stays near enemies. Its low Base ATK favors external ATK buffs. Stay within passive range.
  6. Flower-Wreathed Feathers: Gives ATK and strong Charged Attack damage bonuses. A useful four-star gacha bow for both Melt and Freeze. Stronger with refinements. R5 improves its value.
  7. Scion of the Blazing Sun: CRIT Rate and a debuff that increases Charged Attack damage against marked enemies. Avoid excess CRIT in Freeze.
  8. Hamayumi: A reliable craftable Melt bow that buffs Charged Attacks, especially when Ganyu keeps her Burst unused. Best for Melt with full Energy. R5 recommended.

Best artifacts for Ganyu

  • Wanderer's Troupe (4 pcs): Reliable Melt set with 80 EM and a 35% Charged Attack damage bonus. Main stats: EM / Cryo DMG / CRIT.
  • Blizzard Strayer (4 pcs): Best for traditional Freeze against freezable enemies. Adds substantial CRIT Rate against Cryo and Frozen targets. Main stats: ATK% / Cryo DMG / CRIT DMG.
  • Unfinished Reverie (4 pcs): Best for Burnmelt when teammates maintain Burning on enemies. Gives a large conditional damage bonus. Main stats: EM / Cryo DMG / CRIT.

Main stats

  • Goblet: Cryo DMG Bonus
  • Circlet: CRIT Rate / CRIT DMG

Stat targets

  • ATK: 1,800 - 2,200
  • CRIT Rate: 50% - 70% (Melt)
  • CRIT Rate: 25% - 45% (Freeze)
  • CRIT DMG: 160% - 220%
  • EM: 150 - 300 (Melt)

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 9+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 6–8
  • Elemental Burst: Level 8–9

Best teams for Ganyu

  • Frostbound Royalty: Ganyu (On-field DPS), Furina (Off-field DPS / Buffer), Escoffier (Healer / Debuffer), Shenhe (Cryo Buffer). Furina buffs and supplies Hydro, Escoffier heals and lowers Cryo and Hydro resistance, and Shenhe empowers Ganyu’s Cryo damage.
  • Embers in the Snow: Ganyu (On-field DPS), Emilie (Off-field DPS), Dehya (Pyro Enabler / Support), Bennett (Healer / Buffer). Emilie and Dehya maintain Burning for Ganyu’s Melt shots. Bennett buffs ATK and heals, while Dehya provides interruption resistance.
  • Melt the Mountains: Ganyu (On-field DPS), Xiangling (Off-field DPS / Pyro Enabler), Bennett (Healer / Buffer), Zhongli (Shielder / Debuffer). Xiangling applies Pyro for Melt, Bennett boosts ATK, and Zhongli shields Ganyu while she charges her shots.
  • Budget Blizzard: Ganyu (On-field DPS), Xiangling (Off-field DPS / Pyro Enabler), Bennett (Healer / Buffer), Sucrose (EM Buffer / Debuffer). Xiangling enables Melt, Bennett buffs and heals, and Sucrose shares EM and reduces Cryo resistance. Careful aiming is needed without a shield.

Build variations

Melt Charged Shots (On-field DPS)

Use for high-damage Frostflake Arrow Melt hits.

  • Weapons: Astral Vulture's Crimson Plumage, The First Great Magic, Hunter's Path
  • Artifacts: Wanderer's Troupe (4 pcs)
  • Stats: EM Sands can work; prioritize CRIT DMG and charged-shot damage.
  • Teams: Consistent off-field Pyro and interruption protection.
  • When it applies: Needs reliable Pyro application.

Freeze / Mono Cryo (On-field DPS)

Blizzard Strayer offers easy CRIT Rate when enemies stay Cryo-affected.

  • Weapons: Amos' Bow, Aqua Simulacra
  • Artifacts: Blizzard Strayer (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Reduce sheet CRIT Rate to account for Freeze bonuses.
  • Teams: Hydro for Freeze or strong Cryo buffers.
  • When it applies: Freeze is unreliable against some bosses.

Burnmelt (On-field DPS)

Use Burning to sustain Pyro aura for Ganyu’s Melt shots.

  • Weapons: Astral Vulture's Crimson Plumage, The First Great Magic
  • Artifacts: Unfinished Reverie (4 pcs); Wanderer's Troupe (4 pcs)
  • Stats: EM is valuable; still balance ATK and CRIT.
  • Teams: Dendro and Pyro teammates that maintain Burning.
  • When it applies: Unfinished Reverie needs stable Burning uptime.

Ganyu talents

Normal Attack: Liutian Archery

Normal Attack
Performs up to 6 consecutive shots with a bow.

Charged Attack
Performs a more precise Aimed Shot with increased DMG.
While aiming, an icy aura will accumulate on the arrowhead before the arrow is fired. Has different effects based on how long the energy has been charged:
·Charge Level 1: Fires off an icy arrow that deals Cryo DMG.
·Charge Level 2: Fires off a Frostflake Arrow that deals Cryo DMG. The Frostflake Arrow blooms after hitting its target, dealing AoE Cryo DMG.

Plunging Attack
Fires off a shower of arrows in mid-air before falling and striking the ground, dealing AoE DMG upon impact.

Elemental Skill: Trail of the Qilin

Cooldown: 10s

Leaving a single Ice Lotus behind, Ganyu dashes backward, shunning all impurity and dealing AoE Cryo DMG.

Ice Lotus
·Continuously taunts surrounding opponents, attracting them to attack it.
·Endurance scales based on Ganyu's Max HP.
·Blooms profusely when destroyed or once its duration ends, dealing AoE Cryo DMG.

Elemental Burst: Celestial Shower

Cooldown: 15s · Energy cost: 60

Coalesces atmospheric frost and snow to summon a Sacred Cryo Pearl that exorcises evil.
During its ability duration, the Sacred Cryo Pearl will continuously rain down shards of ice, striking opponents within an AoE and dealing Cryo DMG.

Passive talent: Undivided Heart

After firing a Frostflake Arrow, the CRIT Rate of subsequent Frostflake Arrows and their resulting bloom effects is increased by 20% for 5s.

Passive talent: Harmony Between Heaven and Earth

Celestial Shower grants a 20% Cryo DMG Bonus to active party members in the AoE.

Passive talent: Preserved for the Hunt

Refunds 15% of the ore used when crafting Bow-type weapons.

Ganyu constellations

C1: Dew-Drinker

Charge Level 2 Frostflake Arrows or Frostflake Arrow Blooms decrease opponents' Cryo RES by 15% for 6s upon hit.
A hit also regenerates 2 Energy for Ganyu. This effect can only occur once per Charge Level 2 Frostflake Arrow, regardless if Frostflake Arrow itself or its Bloom hits the target.

C2: The Auspicious

Trail of the Qilin gains 1 additional charge.

C3: Cloud-Strider

Increases the Level of Celestial Shower by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C4: Westward Sojourn

Opponents standing within the AoE of Celestial Shower take increased DMG. This effect strengthens over time.
Increased DMG taken begins at 5% and increases by 5% every 3s, up to a maximum of 25%.
The effect lingers for 3s after the opponent leaves the AoE.

C5: The Merciful

Increases the Level of Trail of the Qilin by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C6: The Clement

Using Trail of the Qilin causes the next Frostflake Arrow shot within 30s to not require charging.

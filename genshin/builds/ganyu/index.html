Ganyu Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts & Teams

Ganyu build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Astral Vulture's Crimson Plumage, The First Great Magic), artifacts (Wanderer's Troupe), main stats…

Role: Main DPS

Ganyu is a Cryo damage dealer whose Frostflake Charged Shots deliver powerful area damage. She excels in Freeze, Melt, and Burning teams. Freeze helps control groups, while Melt rewards precise aiming and reliable Pyro application. Her Burst also makes her a useful off-field Cryo option.

Best weapons for Ganyu

  1. Astral Vulture's Crimson Plumage
  2. The First Great Magic
  3. Hunter's Path
  4. Amos' Bow
  5. Aqua Simulacra
  6. Flower-Wreathed Feathers
  7. Scion of the Blazing Sun
  8. Hamayumi

Best artifacts

  • Wanderer's Troupe (4 pcs)
  • Blizzard Strayer (4 pcs)
  • Unfinished Reverie (4 pcs)

Main stats

  • Sands: ATK% / Elemental Mastery
  • Goblet: Cryo DMG Bonus
  • Circlet: CRIT Rate / CRIT DMG

Stat targets

  • ATK: 1,800 - 2,200
  • CRIT Rate: 50% - 70% (Melt)
  • CRIT Rate: 25% - 45% (Freeze)
  • CRIT DMG: 160% - 220%
  • EM: 150 - 300 (Melt)

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 9+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 6–8
  • Elemental Burst: Level 8–9

Best teams

  • Frostbound Royalty: Ganyu, Furina, Escoffier, Shenhe
  • Embers in the Snow: Ganyu, Emilie, Dehya, Bennett
  • Melt the Mountains: Ganyu, Xiangling, Bennett, Zhongli
  • Budget Blizzard: Ganyu, Xiangling, Bennett, Sucrose

Pros

  • Powerful Charged Shots
  • Great area damage
  • Flexible Cryo playstyles
  • Strong off-field Burst

Cons

  • Aiming can be tricky
  • Vulnerable while charging
  • Melt needs steady Pyro
  • Freeze struggles on bosses

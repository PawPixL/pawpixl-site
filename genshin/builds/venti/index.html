Venti Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

Venti build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (The Daybreak Chronicles, Polar Star), artifacts (A Day Carved From Rising Winds), main stats.

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Role: Main DPS / Support

Venti can play on-field HEX Main DPS after completing Witch’s Homework alongside another Hexerei teammate, using Windsunder Arrows for Anemo damage. Alternatively, he groups enemies and supports off-field damage with Swirl, Viridescent Venerer RES shred, and Energy refunds.

Best weapons for Venti

  1. The Daybreak Chronicles: HEX-focused signature bow that enhances Venti’s on-field Windsunder Arrow damage; strongest fit for his HEX DPS playstyle. HEX Main DPS with Witch’s Homework completed.
  2. Polar Star: CRIT Rate and stacking ATK from varied hits boost direct Anemo damage in a HEX DPS build. Requires stack management for best value.
  3. Skyward Harp: CR and CD strengthen direct Anemo DMG.
  4. Elegy for the End: Energy Recharge and a teamwide ATK/EM buff after its effect triggers make it excellent for off-field support. Support / Sub DPS, especially reaction teams.
  5. The Stringless: Elemental Mastery strengthens Swirl while the passive increases Skill and Burst damage; excellent off-field option. Swirl support or off-field damage.
  6. Favonius Warbow: Energy Recharge plus neutral particles from CRIT hits help Venti and energy-hungry teammates Burst reliably. Support; needs some CRIT Rate to trigger.
  7. Song of Stillness: Craftable ATK bow with a damage bonus after healing, offering a lower-spend route for HEX DPS. Best with a healer to activate its passive.
  8. Windblume Ode: Elemental Mastery boosts Swirl; Skill use grants a short ATK buff for a flexible older event alternative. Only if obtained during its event.

Best artifacts for Venti

  • A Day Carved From Rising Winds (4 pcs) · HEX DPS: Best for Venti’s HEX on-field DPS: sustained on-field attacks benefit from its ATK and CRIT bonuses. Main stats: ATK% · Anemo DMG · CRIT.
  • Viridescent Venerer (4 pcs) · Support: Boosts Swirl and reduces enemy RES to the Swirled element by 40%, greatly improving elemental team damage. Main stats: EM · EM · EM (or ATK/Anemo/CRIT).
  • Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs) · Team Buff: Burst grants the party 20% ATK for 12s. Main stats: ER/ATK% · Anemo/ATK% · CRIT.

Main stats

  • Sands: ATK% (HEX) / Elemental Mastery (Support)
  • Goblet: Anemo DMG (HEX) / Elemental Mastery (Support)
  • Circlet: CRIT (HEX) / Elemental Mastery (Support)

Stat targets

  • HEX CRIT Rate: 60% - 75%
  • HEX CRIT DMG: 120% - 180%+
  • Support EM: 700 - 900+
  • Energy Recharge: 140% - 180% (team-dependent)

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 9+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 6-8
  • Elemental Burst: Level 9+

Best teams for Venti

  • Winds of the Witch: Venti, Durin, Faruzan, Bennett. Durin enables HEX and adds off-field damage; Faruzan boosts Anemo damage while Bennett supplies ATK, healing and a Swirlable Pyro aura.
  • Thunderous Encore: Venti, Fischl, Furina, Jean. Fischl activates HEX, Furina adds damage and buffs, and Jean heals. Venti drives Electro-Charged Swirls with infused attacks.
  • Permafrost: Venti, Ganyu, Mona, Diona. Ganyu and Mona freeze grouped enemies while Diona protects and heals. Venti gathers targets and shreds Cryo RES.
  • Swirl and Chill: Venti, Yumemizuki Mizuki, Furina, Ororon. Mizuki drives Swirls; Furina and Ororon supply off-field damage. Venti groups enemies and supports Electro-Charged reactions.

Pros

  • Strong crowd control
  • HEX on-field option
  • Flexible support builds
  • Excellent Swirl utility

Cons

  • Needs HEX partner for DPS
  • Burst misses heavy enemies
  • Energy needs vary
  • Swirl cannot CRIT

Build variations

HEX Main DPS (On-field DPS)

Use Venti as the on-field damage dealer with Windsunder Arrows and HEX buffs.

  • Weapons: The Daybreak Chronicles, Polar Star, Skyward Harp, Song of Stillness
  • Artifacts: A Day Carved From Rising Winds (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ATK% sands, Anemo DMG goblet and CRIT circlet; prioritize CRIT, ATK and sufficient ER.
  • Teams: Witch’s Homework completed and a second Hexerei teammate.
  • When it applies: Requires Hexerei team activation.
  • Notes: This is the main on-field option within the same Studio guide, not a second build ID.

Swirl Support / Sub DPS (Support / Off-field DPS)

Swap to Venti for grouping, Swirl damage, elemental RES shred and team utility rather than extended on-field attacks.

  • Weapons: Elegy for the End, The Stringless, Favonius Warbow, Windblume Ode
  • Artifacts: Viridescent Venerer (4 pcs); Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Prefer EM/EM/EM for Swirl and enough ER for Burst. CRIT Rate matters on Favonius.
  • Teams: Pyro, Hydro, Electro or Cryo teammates who benefit from grouping and RES shred.
  • When it applies: Viridescent only shreds the element actually Swirled.
  • Notes: Noblesse is an option if another teammate already handles Viridescent.

CRIT Off-field DPS (Off-field DPS)

For well-invested Venti builds that favor personal direct damage while still providing grouping.

  • Weapons: Skyward Harp, The Daybreak Chronicles, The Stringless
  • Artifacts: Viridescent Venerer (4 pcs); Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ATK% / Anemo DMG / CRIT with enough ER for Burst.
  • Teams: Teams where direct Burst damage matters and reaction-focused EM is less valuable.
  • When it applies: Needs good CRIT and ATK investment.
  • Notes: Swirl damage itself cannot CRIT.

Accessible HEX DPS (On-field DPS)

A low-spend approach to Venti’s HEX playstyle without his signature bow.

  • Weapons: Song of Stillness, Skyward Harp
  • Artifacts: A Day Carved From Rising Winds (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ATK% / Anemo DMG / CRIT; build enough ER for regular Bursts.
  • Teams: Another Hexerei character plus a healer for Song of Stillness.
  • When it applies: Complete Witch’s Homework and trigger weapon healing passive.
  • Notes: Song of Stillness is craftable; other options depend on owned inventory.

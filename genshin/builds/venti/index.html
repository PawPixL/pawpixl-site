Role: Main DPS / Support

Venti can play on-field HEX Main DPS after completing Witch’s Homework alongside another Hexerei teammate, using Windsunder Arrows for Anemo damage. Alternatively, he groups enemies and supports off-field damage with Swirl, Viridescent Venerer RES shred, and Energy refunds.

Best weapons for Venti

  1. The Daybreak Chronicles
  2. Polar Star
  3. Skyward Harp
  4. Elegy for the End
  5. The Stringless
  6. Favonius Warbow
  7. Song of Stillness
  8. Windblume Ode

Best artifacts

  • A Day Carved From Rising Winds (4 pcs) · HEX DPS
  • Viridescent Venerer (4 pcs) · Support
  • Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs) · Team Buff

Main stats

  • Sands: ATK% (HEX) / Elemental Mastery (Support)
  • Goblet: Anemo DMG (HEX) / Elemental Mastery (Support)
  • Circlet: CRIT (HEX) / Elemental Mastery (Support)

Stat targets

  • HEX CRIT Rate: 60% - 75%
  • HEX CRIT DMG: 120% - 180%+
  • Support EM: 700 - 900+
  • Energy Recharge: 140% - 180% (team-dependent)

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 9+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 6-8
  • Elemental Burst: Level 9+

Best teams

  • Winds of the Witch: Venti, Durin, Faruzan, Bennett
  • Thunderous Encore: Venti, Fischl, Furina, Jean
  • Permafrost: Venti, Ganyu, Mona, Diona
  • Swirl and Chill: Venti, Yumemizuki Mizuki, Furina, Ororon

Pros

  • Strong crowd control
  • HEX on-field option
  • Flexible support builds
  • Excellent Swirl utility

Cons

  • Needs HEX partner for DPS
  • Burst misses heavy enemies
  • Energy needs vary
  • Swirl cannot CRIT

Build variations

  • HEX Main DPS (On-field DPS): Use Venti as the on-field damage dealer with Windsunder Arrows and HEX buffs.
  • Swirl Support / Sub DPS (Support / Off-field DPS): Swap to Venti for grouping, Swirl damage, elemental RES shred and team utility rather than extended on-field attacks.
  • CRIT Off-field DPS (Off-field DPS): For well-invested Venti builds that favor personal direct damage while still providing grouping.
  • Accessible HEX DPS (On-field DPS): A low-spend approach to Venti’s HEX playstyle without his signature bow.

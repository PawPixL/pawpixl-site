Sethos Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

Sethos build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Hunter's Path, Aqua Simulacra), artifacts (Wanderer's Troupe), main stats, talent priority, teams.

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Role: Main DPS

Sethos is an Electro DPS built for Aggravate. His Burst turns Normal Attacks into piercing Dusk Bolts that deal Charged Attack damage. Alternatively, spend Energy on rapid Shadowpiercing Shots without using Burst.

Best weapons for Sethos

  1. Hunter's Path: CRIT and EM-scaled Charged DMG. Premium best-in-slot; marginal over Slingshot.
  2. Aqua Simulacra: CRIT DMG and proximity-based damage. Stay close enough for its passive.
  3. The First Great Magic: CRIT DMG and Charged Attack bonus. Passive varies with party elements.
  4. Skyward Harp: CRIT Rate and CRIT DMG stat stick. Solid alternative if already owned.
  5. Slingshot: Big close-range Charged Attack buff. Stay close for passive; excellent 3-star. R5 recommended.
  6. Scion of the Blazing Sun: CRIT Rate and Charged Attack mark. Mark requires Charged Attack to hit.
  7. Ibis Piercer: Gains EM after Charged Attacks. Event-limited; stack the EM passive.
  8. Cloudforged: EM bow; weaker than R5 Slingshot. Use only if stronger bows are missing.

Best artifacts for Sethos

  • Wanderer's Troupe (4 pcs) · Best overall: EM and Charged Attack damage. Main stats: EM · Electro · CRIT.
  • Gilded Dreams (4 pcs) · Alternative: EM for Quicken teams. Main stats: EM · Electro · CRIT.
  • Desert Pavilion Chronicle (4 pcs) · Burst variant: Faster Burst Dusk Bolt attacks. Main stats: EM · Electro · CRIT.

Main stats

  • Sands: Elemental Mastery / Energy Recharge
  • Goblet: Electro DMG Bonus
  • Circlet: Crit Rate / Crit DMG

Stat targets

  • CRIT Rate: 60-75%
  • CRIT DMG: 120-160%
  • EM: 250-450
  • ER: 100-160%
  • Level: 90

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 8-9+
  • Elemental Skill: LEvel 1-6
  • Elemental Burst: Level 8-9+

Best teams for Sethos

  • Sandstorm Aggravate: Sethos (Electro DPS), Fischl (Battery / Sub DPS), Kirara (Dendro / Shield), Sucrose (VV / EM Buffer). Fischl batteries Sethos, Kirara enables Quicken and shields, and Sucrose adds EM while shredding Electro RES. Swirl Electro before Dendro if needed.
  • Desert Thunder: Sethos (Electro DPS), Fischl (Battery / Sub DPS), Nahida (Dendro / EM Buffer), Zhongli (Shield / RES Shred). Nahida supplies durable Quicken and EM; Fischl provides Energy, while Zhongli shields and shreds RES without relying on difficult Electro Swirls.
  • Twin Desert Archers: Sethos (Charged Shot DPS), Fischl (Battery), Tighnari (Quickswap DPS), Baizhu (Dendro / Healer). Alternate Sethos Shadowpiercing Shots with Tighnari Charged Shots. Fischl fuels Energy, and Baizhu keeps Quicken active with healing.
  • Budget Sandstorm: Sethos (On-field DPS), Fischl (Off-field DPS / Battery), Yaoyao (Healer / Dendro Enabler), Sucrose (EM Buffer / Debuffer). Accessible Aggravate core: Fischl batteries Sethos, Yaoyao heals and applies Dendro, and Sucrose shreds Electro RES and shares EM.

Pros

Cons

  • Wants Electro battery
  • Energy-sensitive shots
  • Needs Dendro teammate

Build variations

Shadowpiercing Quickswap (Charged Shot DPS)

Do not use Burst. Fire charged Shadowpiercing Shots, use Skill to regenerate Energy by triggering a reaction, then resume shooting.

  • Weapons: Hunter's Path, Slingshot, Aqua Simulacra, Ibis Piercer
  • Artifacts: Wanderer's Troupe (4 pcs); Gilded Dreams (4 pcs)
  • Stats: EM/Electro/CRIT; budget Energy for 3-4 enhanced shots. ER varies sharply by Skill count.
  • Teams: Strong Electro battery, preferably Fischl; Dendro for Quicken.
  • When it applies: Energy-hungry at C0; extra Skill or fewer shots improves consistency.
  • Notes: At C0, 1-Skill/Fischl Charged Shot cycles can need ~140-150% ER; 2 Skills can be near 100-110%.

C6 Charged Shot Economy (Charged Shot DPS)

C6 enables a more forgiving 4-shot sequence or an extra fifth shot at some damage loss.

  • Weapons: Slingshot, Hunter's Path, Aqua Simulacra
  • Artifacts: Wanderer's Troupe (4 pcs); Gilded Dreams (4 pcs)
  • Stats: EM/Electro/CRIT. C6 refunds Energy for one enhanced Shot every 15s.
  • Teams: Electro battery and Dendro support; stable reaction triggers on Skill.
  • When it applies: Requires C6.
  • Notes: Only the first four Shadowpiercing Shots can benefit from one A4 buff cycle.

Tighnari Dual Carry (Quickswap DPS)

Alternate short Sethos charged-shot windows with Tighnari combos in a Quicken rotation.

  • Weapons: Slingshot, Hunter's Path, The First Great Magic
  • Artifacts: Wanderer's Troupe (4 pcs); Gilded Dreams (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Favor rapid shots and shorter field time; adjust ER for repeated swaps.
  • Teams: Tighnari, Fischl and a Dendro sustain such as Baizhu.
  • When it applies: Not for extended Burst Dusk Bolt field time.
  • Notes: Avoid firing Burst during Sethos charged-shot windows.

Desert Pavilion Dusk Bolts (On-field DPS)

Use Burst to convert Normal Attacks into fast, piercing Dusk Bolts; cancel long third-attack animation.

  • Weapons: Slingshot, Hunter's Path, Aqua Simulacra
  • Artifacts: Desert Pavilion Chronicle (4 pcs); Wanderer's Troupe (4 pcs)
  • Stats: EM/Electro/CRIT; prioritize Burst talent and consistent Energy.
  • Teams: Fischl battery and a Dendro applicator; preserve full on-field Burst window.
  • When it applies: Burst stance ends if Sethos switches out.
  • Notes: Dusk Bolts deal Charged Attack damage but retain Normal Attack action triggers.

Sethos talents

Normal Attack: Royal Reed Archery

Normal Attack
Performs up to 3 consecutive shots with a bow.

Charged Attack
Performs a more precise Aimed Shot with increased DMG.
While aiming, the power of Electro will accumulate on the arrowhead before the arrow is fired. Has different effects based on how long the energy has been charged:
·Charge Level 1: Fires off an arrow carrying the power of lightning that deals Electro DMG.
·Charge Level 2: Fires off a Shadowpiercing Shot which can pierce enemies, dealing Electro DMG to enemies along its path. After the Shadowpiercing Shot is fully charged, Sethos cannot move around.

Plunging Attack
Fires off a shower of arrows in mid-air before falling and striking the ground, dealing AoE DMG upon impact.

Elemental Skill: Ancient Rite: The Thundering Sands

Cooldown: 8s

Gathers the might of thunder, dealing AoE Electro DMG and quickly retreating. If this attack triggers Electro-Charged, Lunar-Charged, Superconduct, Stellar-Conduct, Overloaded, Quicken, Aggravate, Hyperbloom, or Electro Swirl reactions, Sethos recovers a certain amount of Elemental Energy.

Elemental Burst: Secret Rite: Twilight Shadowpiercer

Cooldown: 15s · Energy cost: 60

Perform a secret rite, entering the "Twilight Meditation" state, during which Sethos's Normal Attacks will be converted into enemy-piercing Dusk Bolts: Deal Electro DMG to opponents in its path, with DMG increased based on Sethos's Elemental Mastery.
Sethos cannot perform Aimed Shots while in this state.
DMG dealt by Dusk Bolts is considered Charged Attack DMG.
This effect will be canceled when Sethos leaves the field.

Passive talent: Black Kite's Enigma

When Aiming, the charging time is decreased by 0.285s based on each point of Sethos's current Elemental Energy. Charging time can be reduced to a minimum of 0.3s through this method and a maximum of 20 Energy can be tallied. If a Shadowpiercing Shot is fired, consume the tallied amount of Elemental Energy; if it is a Charge Level 1 shot, then consume 50% of the tallied amount of Elemental Energy.

Passive talent: The Sand King's Boon

Sethos gains the "Scorching Sandshade" effect, increasing the DMG dealt by Shadowpiercing Shots by 700% of Sethos's Elemental Mastery.
The Scorching Sandshade effect will be canceled when any of the following conditions are met:
· 5s after a Shadowpiercing Shot first hits an opponent.
· After 4 Shadowpiercing Shots strike opponents.

When a Shadowpiercing Shot affected by Scorching Sandshade first hits an opponent, Sethos will regain Scorching Sandshade after 15s.

Passive talent: Thoth's Revelation

Displays the location of nearby resources unique to Sumeru on the mini-map.

Sethos constellations

C1: Sealed Shrine's Spiritsong

The CRIT Rate of Shadowpiercing Shot is increased by 15%.

C2: Papyrus Scripture of Silent Secrets

When any of the following conditions are met, Sethos gains a 15% Electro DMG Bonus for 10s that may stack twice, with each stack duration counted independently:
· Consuming Elemental Energy through Aimed Shots. You must first unlock the Ascension Talent "Black Kite's Enigma" to trigger this condition.
· Regaining Elemental Energy by triggering Elemental Reactions using Ancient Rite: The Thundering Sands.
· Using Secret Rite: Twilight Shadowpiercer.

C3: Ode to the Moonrise Sage

Increases the Level of Normal Attack: Royal Reed Archery by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C4: Beneficent Plumage

When a Shadowpiercing Shot or Dusk Bolt strikes 2 or more opponents, all nearby party members gain 80 Elemental Mastery for 10s.

C5: Record of the Desolate God's Burning Sands

Increases the Level of Secret Rite: Twilight Shadowpiercer by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C6: Pylon of the Sojourning Sun Temple

After Shadowpiercing Shot strikes an opponent, the Elemental Energy consumed by the Ascension Talent "Black Kite's Enigma" will be returned. This effect can be triggered up to once every 15s. You must first unlock the Ascension Talent "Black Kite's Enigma."

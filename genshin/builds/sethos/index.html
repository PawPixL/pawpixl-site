Role: Main DPS

Sethos is an Electro DPS built for Aggravate. His Burst turns Normal Attacks into piercing Dusk Bolts that deal Charged Attack damage. Alternatively, spend Energy on rapid Shadowpiercing Shots without using Burst.

Best weapons for Sethos

  2. Aqua Simulacra
  3. The First Great Magic
  4. Skyward Harp
  5. Slingshot
  6. Scion of the Blazing Sun
  7. Ibis Piercer
  8. Cloudforged

Best artifacts

  • Gilded Dreams (4 pcs) · Alternative
  • Desert Pavilion Chronicle (4 pcs) · Burst variant

  • Sandstorm Aggravate: Sethos, Fischl, Kirara, Sucrose

  • Twin Desert Archers: Sethos, Fischl, Tighnari, Baizhu
  • Budget Sandstorm: Sethos, Fischl, Yaoyao, Sucrose

Pros

  • Strong Aggravate synergy
  • Two distinct playstyles
  • Great low-rarity weapon
  • Piercing Electro attacks

Cons

  • Wants Electro battery
  • Energy-sensitive shots
  • Needs Dendro teammate
  • Field time limits teams

Build variations

  • Shadowpiercing Quickswap (Charged Shot DPS): Do not use Burst. Fire charged Shadowpiercing Shots, use Skill to regenerate Energy by triggering a reaction, then resume shooting.
  • C6 Charged Shot Economy (Charged Shot DPS): C6 enables a more forgiving 4-shot sequence or an extra fifth shot at some damage loss.
  • Tighnari Dual Carry (Quickswap DPS): Alternate short Sethos charged-shot windows with Tighnari combos in a Quicken rotation.
  • Desert Pavilion Dusk Bolts (On-field DPS): Use Burst to convert Normal Attacks into fast, piercing Dusk Bolts; cancel long third-attack animation.

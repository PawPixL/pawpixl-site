Barbara Build Guide | Best Weapons, Artifacts & Teams

Barbara build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Thrilling Tales of Dragon Slayers, Everlasting Moonglow), artifacts (Ocean-Hued Clam), main stats…

Role: Healer / Support

Barbara is an accessible Hydro healer who keeps teams healthy through her Skill and emergency teamwide Burst. She can buff an ally with Thrilling Tales or drive Bloom and reaction teams with on-field Hydro attacks. Her Skill's self-Wet application and limited off-field Hydro reach require care.

Best weapons for Barbara

  1. Thrilling Tales of Dragon Slayers
  2. Everlasting Moonglow
  3. Prototype Amber
  4. Favonius Codex
  5. Sacrificial Fragments
  6. A Thousand Floating Dreams
  7. Hakushin Ring

Best artifacts

  • Ocean-Hued Clam (4 pcs) · Best overall
  • Song of Days Past (4 pcs) · Buff alternative
  • Maiden Beloved (4 pcs) · Maximum healing

Main stats

  • Sands: HP%
  • Goblet: HP%
  • Circlet: Healing Bonus / HP%

Stat targets

  • HP: 28,000 - 38,000+ (Healer goal)
  • Energy Recharge: Team-dependent; Burst is optional
  • CRIT Rate: 40% - 60% (Favonius only)

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 8-9
  • Elemental Burst: Level 8-9

Best teams

  • Idol Bloom Brigade: Barbara, Nilou, Traveler (Dendro), Collei
  • Hyperbloom Harmony: Barbara, Nahida, Kuki Shinobu, Xingqiu
  • Hydro Healthcare: Barbara, Furina, Yelan, Kaedehara Kazuha
  • Electro Idol Tour: Barbara, Fischl, Beidou, Sucrose

Pros

  • Strong teamwide healing
  • Excellent F2P catalyst
  • On-field Hydro driver
  • Emergency Burst healing

Cons

  • Skill can self-apply Wet
  • Weak off-field Hydro reach
  • Long Skill cooldown
  • Burst has high Energy cost

Build variations

  • Nilou Bloom Driver (On-field Hydro driver / Healer): Use Barbara on-field to apply Hydro, trigger Bountiful Blooms, and heal through self-damage.
  • Hyperbloom Hydro Driver (On-field Hydro driver / Healer): Drive with Barbara’s attacks while an Electro teammate triggers Hyperblooms and Barbara maintains healing.
  • Electro-Charged Clam Driver (On-field Hydro driver / Healer): Keep Barbara on-field using Normal and Charged Attacks for continuous healing, Hydro application, and Clam damage.
  • Charged Attack Vaporize DPS (On-field DPS): A niche high-investment playstyle for players who enjoy carefully timed Hydro Charged Attack Vaporizes.
  • Accessible Team Healer (Off-field Healer / Support): Low-investment setup focused on steady Skill healing, emergency Burst healing, and an easy ATK buff.

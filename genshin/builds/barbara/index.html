Barbara Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

Barbara build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Thrilling Tales of Dragon Slayers, Everlasting Moonglow), artifacts (Ocean-Hued Clam), main stats.

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Role: Healer / Support

Barbara is an accessible Hydro healer who keeps teams healthy through her Skill and emergency teamwide Burst. She can buff an ally with Thrilling Tales or drive Bloom and reaction teams with on-field Hydro attacks. Her Skill's self-Wet application and limited off-field Hydro reach require care.

Best weapons for Barbara

  1. Thrilling Tales of Dragon Slayers: HP% improves healing, while switching directly to your damage dealer grants a large ATK buff at R5. Best when the next character benefits from ATK. R5 strongly recommended.
  2. Everlasting Moonglow: HP% and Healing Bonus increase healing.
  3. Prototype Amber: Craftable HP% catalyst that restores party HP and refunds Barbara Energy after using Burst. Useful for frequent emergency healing; passive only triggers on Burst.
  4. Favonius Codex: Creates neutral particles after an on-field CRIT, helping the team generate Energy. Needs enough CRIT Rate and an on-field hit to trigger.
  5. Sacrificial Fragments: EM helps Bloom damage; Skill damage can reset her long cooldown, improving uptime. On-field Bloom driver; Skill must hit enemies for reset.
  6. A Thousand Floating Dreams: Large EM stat supports Barbara-triggered Bloom and provides a small team EM benefit. Bloom-focused driver only; poor value for pure healing.
  7. Hakushin Ring: Craftable ER option that can buff reaction elements when on-field Barbara triggers Electro-Charged. Only in teams consistently triggering Electro reactions.

Best artifacts for Barbara

  • Ocean-Hued Clam (4 pcs) · Best overall: Converts Barbara’s healing into extra Physical bubble damage, adding value beyond pure healing. Main stats: HP% · HP% · Healing Bonus.
  • Song of Days Past (4 pcs) · Buff alternative: Converts healing into a limited damage bonus for an active attacker; useful when the team can exploit it. Main stats: HP% · HP% · Healing Bonus.
  • Maiden Beloved (4 pcs) · Maximum healing: Raises healing output, but often overheals without contributing extra team damage. Main stats: HP% · HP% · Healing Bonus.

Main stats

  • Sands: HP%
  • Goblet: HP%
  • Circlet: Healing Bonus / HP%

Stat targets

  • HP: 28,000 - 38,000+ (Healer goal)
  • Energy Recharge: Team-dependent; Burst is optional
  • CRIT Rate: 40% - 60% (Favonius only)

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 8-9
  • Elemental Burst: Level 8-9

Best teams for Barbara

  • Idol Bloom Brigade: Barbara, Nilou, Traveler (Dendro), Collei. Barbara drives Bountiful Blooms with on-field Hydro while healing their self-damage. Nilou enables enhanced cores; the Dendro duo supplies aura.
  • Hyperbloom Harmony: Barbara, Nahida, Kuki Shinobu, Xingqiu. Barbara drives with Hydro attacks, Nahida sustains Dendro, and Kuki triggers Hyperblooms. Xingqiu adds reliable off-field Hydro.
  • Hydro Healthcare: Barbara, Furina, Yelan, Kaedehara Kazuha. On-field Barbara heals the team to build Furina Fanfare while driving Yelan’s Burst; Kazuha buffs and shreds Hydro resistance.
  • Electro Idol Tour: Barbara, Fischl, Beidou, Sucrose. Barbara drives Electro-Charged and team healing, Fischl and Beidou add off-field Electro, and Sucrose groups foes and shreds resistance.

Pros

  • Strong teamwide healing
  • Excellent F2P catalyst
  • On-field Hydro driver
  • Emergency Burst healing

Cons

  • Skill can self-apply Wet
  • Weak off-field Hydro reach
  • Long Skill cooldown
  • Burst has high Energy cost

Build variations

Nilou Bloom Driver (On-field Hydro driver / Healer)

Use Barbara on-field to apply Hydro, trigger Bountiful Blooms, and heal through self-damage.

  • Weapons: Sacrificial Fragments, A Thousand Floating Dreams, Prototype Amber
  • Artifacts: Flower of Paradise Lost (4 pcs); Gilded Dreams (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Build EM/EM/EM for Bloom damage; replace one or more pieces with HP% or Healing Bonus if healing is insufficient.
  • Teams: Nilou plus two Dendro or Hydro allies; only Hydro and Dendro party members for Nilou passive.
  • When it applies: Barbara must trigger Blooms to benefit from her EM.
  • Notes: Sacrificial Fragments reset requires Skill damage to hit an enemy.

Hyperbloom Hydro Driver (On-field Hydro driver / Healer)

Drive with Barbara’s attacks while an Electro teammate triggers Hyperblooms and Barbara maintains healing.

  • Weapons: Prototype Amber, Thrilling Tales of Dragon Slayers, Favonius Codex
  • Artifacts: Ocean-Hued Clam (4 pcs); Maiden Beloved (4 pcs)
  • Stats: HP%/HP%/Healing Bonus; Barbara usually does not need EM because the Electro unit triggers Hyperbloom.
  • Teams: Dendro source, reliable Electro trigger built with EM, and enough Hydro application.
  • When it applies: EM belongs primarily on the Electro Hyperbloom trigger.
  • Notes: On-field Barbara helps supplement her limited off-field Hydro application.

Electro-Charged Clam Driver (On-field Hydro driver / Healer)

Keep Barbara on-field using Normal and Charged Attacks for continuous healing, Hydro application, and Clam damage.

  • Weapons: Prototype Amber, Favonius Codex, Hakushin Ring
  • Artifacts: Ocean-Hued Clam (4 pcs); Song of Days Past (4 pcs)
  • Stats: HP%/HP%/Healing Bonus; prioritize enough healing to support Ocean-Hued Clam bubble damage.
  • Teams: Off-field Electro damage sources such as Fischl and Beidou.
  • When it applies: Hakushin Ring requires Barbara to trigger an Electro-related reaction.
  • Notes: Clam bubble damage is Physical and does not scale with Hydro DMG Bonus.

Charged Attack Vaporize DPS (On-field DPS)

A niche high-investment playstyle for players who enjoy carefully timed Hydro Charged Attack Vaporizes.

  • Weapons: A Thousand Floating Dreams, Lost Prayer to the Sacred Winds, The Widsith
  • Artifacts: Wanderer's Troupe (4 pcs); Heart of Depth (4 pcs)
  • Stats: EM or ATK% Sands / Hydro DMG Goblet / CRIT Circlet; prioritize CRIT, EM, ATK and sufficient stamina.
  • Teams: Strong off-field Pyro application and buffs to Vaporize Barbara’s Charged Attacks.
  • When it applies: Requires reliable Pyro aura and Charged Attack stamina management.
  • Notes: Normal Attack talent becomes highest priority for this variation.

Accessible Team Healer (Off-field Healer / Support)

Low-investment setup focused on steady Skill healing, emergency Burst healing, and an easy ATK buff.

  • Weapons: Thrilling Tales of Dragon Slayers, Prototype Amber
  • Artifacts: Maiden Beloved (4 pcs); Ocean-Hued Clam (4 pcs)
  • Stats: HP%/HP%/Healing Bonus; ER is optional if Burst remains an emergency heal.
  • Teams: Works best with an ATK-scaling carry who can immediately follow Barbara for Thrilling Tales buff.
  • When it applies: R5 Thrilling Tales is ideal; its buff applies only to the next character switched in.
  • Notes: Barbara’s Skill does not generate particles and can apply Wet to the active character.

Barbara talents

Normal Attack: Whisper of Water

Normal Attack
Performs up to 4 water splash attacks that deal Hydro DMG.

Charged Attack
Consumes a certain amount of Stamina to deal AoE Hydro DMG after a short casting time.

Plunging Attack
Gathering the might of Hydro, Barbara plunges towards the ground from mid-air, damaging all opponents in her path. Deals AoE Hydro DMG upon impact with the ground.

Elemental Skill: Let the Show Begin♪

Cooldown: 32s

Summons water droplets resembling musical notes that form a Melody Loop, dealing Hydro DMG to surrounding opponents and applying the Wet status to them.

Melody Loop
·On hit, Barbara's Normal Attacks heal your own party members and nearby teammates for a certain amount of HP, which scales with Barbara's Max HP.
·On hit, Barbara's Charged Attack generates 4 times the amount of healing.
·Periodically regenerates your own active character's HP.
·Applies the Wet status to the character and to opponents who come in contact with them.

Elemental Burst: Shining Miracle♪

Cooldown: 20s · Energy cost: 80

Heals your own party members and nearby teammates for a large amount of HP that scales with Barbara's Max HP.

Passive talent: Glorious Season

The Stamina Consumption of characters within Let the Show Begin♪'s Melody Loop is reduced by 12%.

Passive talent: Encore

When your active character gains an Elemental Orb/Particle, the duration of the Melody Loop of Let the Show Begin♪ is extended by 1s.
The maximum extension is 5s.

Passive talent: With My Whole Heart♪

When a Perfect Cooking is achieved on a dish with restorative effects, there is a 12% chance to obtain double the product.

Role: Main DPS

Mizuki drives Swirl and Stellar Swirl from the field. Stellar Swirl can CRIT, favoring EM plus CRIT; traditional Swirl favors EM and ER instead. Her Burst also provides healing.

Main stats

  • Sands: EM
  • Goblet: EM
  • Circlet: CR / CD (Stellar); EM (Swirl)

Stat targets

  • EM: ~800–1,000+
  • CR/CD: Stellar Swirl only
  • ER: As needed for healing
  • Swirl: No CRIT needed

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 9+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 9+

Best teams

  • Wind Chill Factor: Yumemizuki Mizuki, Odette, Vodyanitsa, Faruzan
  • Swirl Problems: Yumemizuki Mizuki, Traveler (Cryo), qiqi, Faruzan
  • Lunar-tics: Yumemizuki Mizuki, Columbina, Ineffa, Sucrose
  • Discount Draft: Yumemizuki Mizuki, Traveler (Cryo), Diona, Sucrose

Pros

  • Strong Stellar Swirl DMG
  • Excellent team support
  • Built-in healing
  • Great AoE potential

Cons

  • Needs high EM investment
  • Limited team flexibility
  • Requires Cryo/SSW enablers

Build variations

  • Stellar Swirl DPS (On-field DPS): For Stellar Swirl teams where the upgraded reaction can CRIT.
  • Traditional Swirl (On-field Driver): For ordinary Swirl teams without a Stellar Swirl enabler.

Yumemizuki Mizuki Build Guide: Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

Yumemizuki Mizuki build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Sunny Morning Sleep-In, Reliquary of Truth), artifacts (Gilded Dreams), main stats.

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Role: Main DPS

Mizuki drives Swirl and Stellar Swirl from the field. Stellar Swirl can CRIT, favoring EM plus CRIT; traditional Swirl favors EM and ER instead. Her Burst also provides healing.

Best weapons for Yumemizuki Mizuki

  1. Sunny Morning Sleep-In: Signature EM catalyst gaining more EM through Swirl, Skill hits and Burst hits.
  2. Reliquary of Truth: Strong CRIT-oriented catalyst with bonuses for its intended reaction and team setup.
  3. A Thousand Floating Dreams: Provides 265 EM at Level 90, up to 30% Elemental DMG Bonus from diverse teammates, and 40 teamwide EM at R1, boosting Mizuki’s Swirl damage and team reactions.
  4. The Widsith: Provides 55.1% CRIT DMG and a random ATK, Elemental DMG, or EM buff, offering strong but inconsistent damage boosts for Mizuki.
  5. Favonius Codex: High Energy Recharge and particle generation keep the burst ready on demand.
  6. Fruit of Fulfillment: Provides 45.9% Energy Recharge and increases EM after using an Elemental Reaction, making it a solid F2P option for Mizuki’s Swirl damage and Burst uptime.
  7. Hakushin Ring: When Mizuki triggers an Electro-related reaction, she can increase elemental damage for the participating elements.
  8. Etherlight Spindlelute: Energy Recharge event catalyst; helps meet Burst needs where personal damage is secondary.

Best artifacts for Yumemizuki Mizuki

  • Gilded Dreams (4 pcs) · Main DPS: Strong EM-focused set for Swirl and competitive Stellar Swirl damage; best when reaction uptime is consistent. Main stats: EM · EM · CR/CD.
  • Scarlet Proof (4 pcs) · Main DPS: Stellar Swirl option: grants 16% CRIT Rate and 40% Stellar Swirl DMG after triggering it. The 2pc ATK is less useful.
  • Wanderer's Troupe + Gilded Dreams (2 + 2 pcs) · Main DPS: Two EM bonuses grant 160 EM. A flexible fallback for Stellar Swirl or traditional Swirl builds.

Main stats

  • Sands: EM
  • Goblet: EM
  • Circlet: CR / CD (Stellar); EM (Swirl)

Stat targets

  • EM: ~800–1,000+
  • CR/CD: Stellar Swirl only
  • ER: As needed for healing
  • Swirl: No CRIT needed

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 9+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 9+

Best teams for Yumemizuki Mizuki

  • Wind Chill Factor: Yumemizuki Mizuki (Main DPS), Odette (Stellar), Vodyanitsa (Support), Faruzan (Res Shred). Mizuki drives on-field Swirl damage, Odette enables Stellar Swirl and provides Cryo support, Vodyanitsa heals and shreds RES, and Faruzan buffs Anemo damage and shreds Anemo RES.
  • Swirl Problems: Yumemizuki Mizuki (Main DPS), Traveler (Cryo) (Stellar), qiqi (Support), Faruzan (Res Shred). Mizuki drives on-field Swirl damage, Cryo Traveler provides off-field Cryo application, Qiqi heals and adds Cryo support, and Faruzan buffs Anemo damage and shreds Anemo RES.
  • Lunar-tics: Yumemizuki Mizuki (Main DPS), Columbina (Lunar), Ineffa (Support), Sucrose (EM Build). Mizuki drives on-field Swirl damage, Columbina buffs Lunar reactions, Ineffa provides off-field Electro and Lunar-Charged damage, and Sucrose boosts EM and shreds RES.
  • Discount Draft: Yumemizuki Mizuki (Main DPS), Traveler (Cryo) (Stellar), Diona (Support), Sucrose (EM Build). Mizuki drives Swirl damage, Cryo Traveler provides off-field Cryo application, Diona shields and heals, and Sucrose boosts EM and shreds RES.

Pros

  • Excellent team support
  • Built-in healing
  • Great AoE potential

Cons

  • Needs high EM investment
  • Limited team flexibility
  • Requires Cryo/SSW enablers

Build variations

Stellar Swirl DPS (On-field DPS)

For Stellar Swirl teams where the upgraded reaction can CRIT.

  • Weapons: Sunny Morning Sleep-In, A Thousand Floating Dreams, The Widsith
  • Artifacts: Scarlet Proof (4 pcs); Gilded Dreams (4 pcs)
  • Stats: EM Sands and Goblet; CRIT Circlet. Balance CRIT and EM; the 4pc Scarlet Proof supplies 16% CRIT Rate.
  • Teams: Cryo ally who enables Stellar Swirl, such as Odette or Traveler (Cryo).
  • When it applies: Stellar Swirl setup, not regular Swirl.
  • Notes: Recheck CRIT totals after artifact and team buffs.

Traditional Swirl (On-field Driver)

For ordinary Swirl teams without a Stellar Swirl enabler.

  • Weapons: Sunny Morning Sleep-In, A Thousand Floating Dreams, Favonius Codex
  • Artifacts: Gilded Dreams (4 pcs); Viridescent Venerer (4 pcs)
  • Stats: EM Sands, EM Goblet, EM Circlet; ER for healing/Burst if needed.
  • Teams: Swirlable elements; use VV if no other teammate applies the desired RES shred.
  • When it applies: Regular Swirl does not CRIT.
  • Notes: CRIT Rate only matters here when Favonius procs are needed.

Yumemizuki Mizuki talents

Normal Attack: Pure Heart, Pure Dreams

Normal Attack
Performs up to 3 attacks, dealing Anemo DMG.

Charged Attack
Consumes a certain amount of Stamina and deals AoE Anemo DMG after a short casting time.

Plunging Attack
Calling upon the power of Anemo, she plunges towards the ground from mid-air, damaging all opponents in her path. Deals AoE Anemo DMG upon impact with the ground.

Elemental Skill: Aisa Utamakura Pilgrimage

Cooldown: 15s

Weaves memories of lovely dreams, entering a Dreamdrifter state where Yumemizuki Mizuki floats above the ground, and dealing 1 instance of AoE Anemo DMG to nearby opponents.

Dreamdrifter
·While in the Dreamdrifter state, Yumemizuki Mizuki will continuously drift forward, dealing AoE Anemo DMG to nearby opponents at regular intervals.
·During this time, Yumemizuki Mizuki can control her direction of drift, and the pick-up distance of Yumemi Style Special Snacks from the Elemental Burst Anraku Secret Spring Therapy will be increased.
·Increases the Swirl DMG and Stellar Swirl reaction DMG that nearby party members deal based on Yumemizuki Mizuki's Elemental Mastery.
·Dreamdrifter will end when Mizuki leaves the field or uses her Elemental Skill again.

Elemental Burst: Anraku Secret Spring Therapy

Cooldown: 15s · Energy cost: 60

Summons forth countless lovely dreams and nightmares that pull in nearby objects and opponents, dealing AoE Anemo DMG and summoning a Mini Baku.

Mini Baku:
Follows the current active character. At regular intervals, the Mini Baku will create 1 Yumemi Style Special Snack nearby. After one of your own party members picks up a Special Snack, if their HP is higher than 70%, the Snack will explode and unleash a Munen Shockwave, dealing 1 instance of AoE Anemo DMG to nearby opponents. Otherwise, it will heal the character who picked it up based on Yumemizuki Mizuki's Elemental Mastery. When Yumemizuki Mizuki triggers healing by picking up a Yumemi Style Special Snack, the amount of healing is increased by 100%. If a Snack is not picked up after a period of time, it will explode and release a Munen Shockwave, dealing 1 instance of AoE Anemo DMG to surrounding opponents.

Passive talent: Bright Moon's Restless Voice

When Yumemizuki Mizuki triggers a Swirl or Stellar Swirl reaction while in her Dreamdrifter state, Dreamdrifter's duration increases by 2.5s. This effect can trigger once every 0.3s for a maximum of 2 times per Dreamdrifter state.

Passive talent: Thoughts by Day Bring Dreams by Night

While Yumemizuki Mizuki is in the Dreamdrifter state, when other nearby party members hit opponents with Pyro, Hydro, Cryo, or Electro attacks, her Elemental Mastery will increase by 100 for 4s.

Passive talent: All Ailments Banished

When Yumemizuki Mizuki is in the party, party members have a chance to recover 30% additional HP when using healing foods that do not have reviving properties. The trigger chance for this effect increases with the corresponding character's Friendship Level. (This effect will always trigger if the Traveler is the character consuming healing food.)

Yumemizuki Mizuki constellations

C1: In Mist-Like Waters

When Yumemizuki Mizuki is in the Dreamdrifter state, she will continuously apply the "Twenty-Three Nights' Awaiting" effect to nearby opponents for 3s every 3.5s. When an opponent has a Swirl or Stellar Swirl reaction triggered against them by Anemo DMG while the aforementioned effect is active, the effect will be canceled, and this Swirl or Stellar Swirl instance deals DMG against this opponent increased by 1,100% and 550% of Mizuki's Elemental Mastery, respectively.

C2: Your Echo I Meet in Dreams

When Yumemizuki Mizuki enters the Dreamdrifter state, every Elemental Mastery point she has will increase all nearby party members' Pyro, Hydro, Cryo, and Electro DMG Bonuses by 0.04% until the Dreamdrifter state ends.

C3: Till Dawn's Moon Ends Night

Increases the Level of Aisa Utamakura Pilgrimage by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C4: Buds Warm Lucid Springs

Picking up a Yumemi Style Special Snack from the Elemental Burst Anraku Secret Spring Therapy will both deal DMG and heal, and will restore 5 Energy to Yumemizuki Mizuki. Energy can be restored this way 4 times per Anraku Secret Spring Therapy duration.

C5: As Setting Moon Brings Year's End

Increases the Level of Anraku Secret Spring Therapy by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C6: The Heart Lingers Long

While Yumemizuki Mizuki is in the Dreamdrifter state, Swirl reaction DMG dealt by nearby party members can score CRIT Hits, with CRIT Rate fixed at 30%, and CRIT DMG fixed at 100%. Any Stellar Swirl reaction DMG dealt by these characters will also have its CRIT Rate and CRIT DMG increased by 10% and 20%, respectively.

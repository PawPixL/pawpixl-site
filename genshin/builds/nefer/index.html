Nefer Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts & Teams

Nefer build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Reliquary of Truth, Nightweaver's Looking Glass), artifacts (Night Of The Sky'S Unveiling), main stats…

Role: Main DPS

Nefer is a Dendro DPS who specializes in powerful Lunar Bloom damage. She thrives in dedicated teams and scales heavily with Elemental Mastery.

Best weapons for Nefer

  1. Reliquary of Truth
  2. Nightweaver's Looking Glass
  3. Cashflow Supervision
  4. Sunny Morning Sleep-In
  5. Dawning Frost
  6. Sacrificial Jade
  7. Blackmarrow Lantern
  8. Mappa Mare

Best artifacts

  • Night Of The Sky'S Unveiling (4 pcs) · Main DPS
  • Gilded Dreams (4 pcs) · Main DPS
  • Gilded Dreams + Wanderer's Troupe (2 + 2 pcs) · Main DPS

Main stats

  • Sands: Elemental Mastery
  • Goblet: Elemental Mastery
  • Circlet: CRIT Rate / CRIT DMG

Stat targets

  • EM: 500+
  • Crit Rate: 40% - 50%
  • Crit DMG: 200% - 220%+
  • Energy Recharge: 100–110%+

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 9+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 9+

Best teams

  • Petal to the Meta: Nefer, Lauma, Nahida, Columbina
  • Petty Petals: Nefer, Columbina, Sangonomiya Kokomi, Nahida
  • Bloom With a View: Nefer, Columbina, Lauma, Ineffa
  • Broke But Blooming: Nefer, Aino, Kirara, Sucrose

Pros

  • Massive damage
  • Strong single target DPS
  • Excellent EM scaling
  • Powerful reaction synergy

Cons

  • Limited team flexibility
  • Relies on Lunar Bloom
  • Wants Lauma
  • Struggles with Pyro aura

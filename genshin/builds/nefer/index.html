Nefer Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams (7.2)

Nefer build guide for Genshin Impact 7.2: best weapons (Reliquary of Truth, Nightweaver's Looking Glass), artifacts (Night Of The Sky'S Unveiling).

Last updated: November 12, 2026 · Game version 7.2

Role: Main DPS

Nefer is a Dendro DPS who specializes in powerful Lunar Bloom damage. She thrives in dedicated teams and scales heavily with Elemental Mastery.

Best weapons for Nefer

  1. Reliquary of Truth: Provides 88.2% CRIT DMG, 8% CRIT Rate, 120 EM, and up to 36% additional CRIT DMG at R1, making it Nefer’s signature and best-in-slot weapon.
  2. Nightweaver's Looking Glass: Provides 265 EM and boosts Lunar-Bloom DMG by 40% at R1, making it Nefer’s second-best weapon behind Reliquary of Truth.
  3. Cashflow Supervision: CRIT Rate and ATK; Normal/Charged damage and Stellar-Conduct bonuses need reliable HP changes.
  4. Sunny Morning Sleep-In: Elemental Mastery and reaction-related damage utility; best suited to reaction-centered builds.
  5. Dawning Frost: Provides 55.1% CRIT DMG and up to 216 EM at R1 after hitting with Charged Attacks and Elemental Skills, making it a strong 4-star option for Nefer
  6. Sacrificial Jade: Battle Pass CRIT Rate, HP and EM when off-field. Its buff expires after 10 seconds on-field.
  7. Blackmarrow Lantern: Offers Elemental Mastery and reaction bonuses. Check its acquisition source before listing it as a craftable F2P recommendation.
  8. Mappa Mare: Craftable EM catalyst with Elemental DMG stacks after triggering a reaction.

Best artifacts for Nefer

  • Night Of The Sky'S Unveiling (4 pcs) · Main DPS: Provides 80 EM and up to 30% CRIT Rate, while increasing teamwide Lunar Reaction DMG by 10%. Nefer’s best-in-slot set for Lunar-Bloom teams. Main stats: EM · EM · CR/CD.
  • Gilded Dreams (4 pcs) · Main DPS: Provides 80 EM and up to 150 additional EM or 28% ATK, boosting Nefer’s Lunar-Bloom and reaction damage.
  • Gilded Dreams + Wanderer's Troupe (2 + 2 pcs) · Main DPS: Provides 160 EM, boosting Nefer’s Lunar-Bloom damage and reaction scaling. A solid transitional option while farming her 4-piece set.

Main stats

  • Goblet: Elemental Mastery
  • Circlet: CRIT Rate / CRIT DMG

Stat targets

  • EM: 500+
  • Crit Rate: 40% - 50%
  • Crit DMG: 200% - 220%+
  • Energy Recharge: 100–110%+

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 9+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 9+

Best teams for Nefer

  • Petal to the Meta: Nefer (Main DPS), Lauma (Buffs), Nahida (EM Buffs), Columbina (Lunar). Nefer drives Lunar-Bloom damage, Lauma provides major Lunar-Bloom buffs and Dendro RES shred, Nahida boosts EM, and Columbina supplies off-field Hydro and Lunar-Bloom buffs.
  • Petty Petals: Nefer (Main DPS), Columbina (Lunar), Sangonomiya Kokomi (Support), Nahida (EM Buffs). Nefer drives Lunar-Bloom damage while Columbina supplies Hydro and reaction buffs. Kokomi adds healing and Hydro application, and Nahida shares EM.
  • Bloom With a View: Nefer (Main DPS), Columbina (Lunar), Lauma (Buffs), Ineffa (Support). Nefer drives Lunar-Bloom damage, Columbina provides Hydro and Lunar buffs, Lauma amplifies Lunar-Bloom, and Ineffa adds shielding, off-field damage, and extra reaction support.
  • Broke But Blooming: Nefer (Main DPS), Aino (Hydro), Kirara (Support), Sucrose (EM Build). Nefer drives Lunar-Bloom damage, Aino provides Hydro application and Lunar buffs, Kirara supplies Dendro application and shielding, and Sucrose boosts EM.

Pros

  • Massive damage
  • Strong single target DPS
  • Excellent EM scaling
  • Powerful reaction synergy

Cons

  • Limited team flexibility
  • Relies on Lunar Bloom
  • Wants Lauma
  • Struggles with Pyro aura

Build variations

Lunar-Bloom Carry (On-field DPS)

Focus on Nefer’s on-field Lunar-Bloom damage windows.

  • Weapons: Reliquary of Truth, Nightweaver's Looking Glass, Sacrificial Jade
  • Artifacts: Night Of The Sky'S Unveiling (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Build EM and CRIT in line with the Lunar damage setup.
  • Teams: Dedicated Lunar-Bloom enabler, preferably Lauma.
  • When it applies: Reaction support is required for peak value.

Accessible Lunar-Bloom (On-field DPS)

Lower-cost alternative preserving Nefer’s reaction-based playstyle.

  • Weapons: Dawning Frost, Sacrificial Jade
  • Artifacts: Night Of The Sky'S Unveiling (4 pcs); Gilded Dreams (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Balance EM and CRIT; check artifact-provided CRIT.
  • Teams: Lunar-Bloom team with accessible catalyst choices.
  • When it applies: Do not assume generic Bloom scaling applies unchanged.

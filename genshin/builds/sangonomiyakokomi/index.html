Sangonomiya Kokomi Build Guide | Weapons, Artifacts & Teams

Sangonomiya Kokomi build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Everlasting Moonglow, A Thousand Floating Dreams), artifacts (Tenacity of the Millelith),…

Role: Healer / Support

Kokomi is a versatile Hydro healer whose jellyfish applies Hydro and sustains teammates off-field. Her Burst refreshes the jellyfish and lets her heal the entire team while attacking on-field. Build HP and enough ER for your rotation.

  • Elemental Mastery (Bloom): 600 - 900+

Talent priority

  • Elemental Skill: Level 8-9
  • Elemental Burst: Level 8-9

Pros

  • Excellent team healing
  • Reliable Hydro application
  • Flexible reaction teams
  • Strong accessible weapons

Cons

  • Cannot normally CRIT
  • Stationary jellyfish
  • Burst can need high ER
  • Modest personal damage

Build variations

  • On-field Hydro Driver (On-field Healer): Use Kokomi on-field after Burst to heal the entire team and drive Hydro reactions; Ocean-Hued Clam converts her healing into damage.
  • Nilou Bloom Trigger (Bloom DPS): Use when Kokomi owns many Bloom triggers. Prioritize EM while retaining enough healing to survive Bountiful Core damage.
  • Electro-Charged Driver (On-field Healer): Drive Electro-Charged with Hydro Normal Attacks while healing and enabling Electro teammates.
  • Short-Rotation Skill Support (Off-field Support): Use jellyfish healing and Hydro application without relying on an every-rotation Burst.

Sangonomiya Kokomi Build Guide: Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

Sangonomiya Kokomi build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Everlasting Moonglow, A Thousand Floating Dreams), artifacts (Tenacity of the Millelith).

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Role: Healer / Support

Kokomi is a versatile Hydro healer whose jellyfish applies Hydro and sustains teammates off-field. Her Burst refreshes the jellyfish and lets her heal the entire team while attacking on-field. Build HP and enough ER for your rotation.

Best weapons for Sangonomiya Kokomi

  1. Everlasting Moonglow: HP% and Healing Bonus improve sustain; Normal Attacks during Burst gain damage and refund Energy. Mostly for on-field Kokomi. On-field Burst driver.
  2. A Thousand Floating Dreams: Large Elemental Mastery for Kokomi-owned Bloom reactions and a small team EM share. Not a standard healing-support upgrade. Bloom-trigger build.
  3. Thrilling Tales of Dragon Slayers: At R5, swapping from Kokomi grants the next character a large ATK buff. Outstanding for teams with an ATK-scaling carry. Swap directly into the main ATK DPS. R5 recommended.
  4. Prototype Amber: HP% boosts healing; casting Burst restores Energy and heals the team. Comfortable craftable choice for on-field or refresh builds. R5 improves Energy return.
  5. Hakushin Ring: After Kokomi triggers an Electro reaction, grants relevant Elemental DMG Bonuses to teammates. Niche but useful in Electro-Charged teams. Kokomi must trigger an Electro-related reaction.
  6. Sacrificial Fragments: Elemental Mastery improves Bloom damage; Skill cooldown reset can reposition the jellyfish, though it is chance-based. Bloom teams with mobile enemies. Higher refinements improve consistency.
  7. Fruit of Fulfillment: Energy Recharge substat helps Burst uptime; its passive trades ATK for Elemental Mastery, especially useful when triggering Bloom. Bloom-focused use.
  8. Magic Guide: Cheap Elemental Mastery option for Bloom reactions; its damage passive is inconsistent and does not boost reaction damage. Budget Bloom builds.

Best artifacts for Sangonomiya Kokomi

  • Tenacity of the Millelith (4 pcs) · Best support: Jellyfish hits can maintain the partywide ATK buff. Ideal for off-field Kokomi supporting an ATK-scaling carry. Main stats: ER/HP% · HP% · Healing Bonus.
  • Ocean-Hued Clam (4 pcs): Converts healing into extra Physical bubble damage. Excellent for Kokomi driving on-field during Burst. Main stats: HP%/ER · Hydro DMG · Healing Bonus.
  • Flower of Paradise Lost (4 pcs): Boosts Bloom damage when Kokomi triggers cores; only choose it when her Bloom ownership justifies an EM build. Main stats: EM/ER · EM/HP% · EM/Healing Bonus.

Main stats

  • Sands: HP% / Energy Recharge
  • Goblet: HP%
  • Circlet: Healing Bonus / HP%

Stat targets

  • HP: 35,000 - 45,000+
  • Energy Recharge (Skill-only): 100% - 140%
  • Energy Recharge (Burst refresh): 160% - 220%+
  • Healing Bonus: Circlet preferred
  • Elemental Mastery (Bloom): 600 - 900+

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1-6
  • Elemental Skill: Level 8-9
  • Elemental Burst: Level 8-9

Best teams for Sangonomiya Kokomi

  • Watatsumi Winter: Sangonomiya Kokomi (Hydro Healer), Kamisato Ayaka (Main DPS), Shenhe (Cryo Buffer), Kaedehara Kazuha (Anemo Support). Kokomi maintains Freeze and healing with her jellyfish; Shenhe buffs Ayaka while Kazuha groups and shreds Cryo resistance.
  • Blooming Tides: Sangonomiya Kokomi (Bloom Driver), Nilou (Bloom Support), Nahida (Dendro Support), Collei (Dendro Support). Kokomi drives Nilou Bountiful Blooms and heals self-damage. Build EM on Kokomi; keep Deepwood on a Dendro teammate.
  • Storm-Surf Society: Sangonomiya Kokomi (Hydro Driver), Fischl (Electro Sub DPS), Beidou (Electro Sub DPS), Sucrose (Anemo Support). Kokomi drives Electro-Charged while sustaining the team; Fischl and Beidou deal off-field damage, and Sucrose groups and shreds.
  • Fanfare by the Sea: Sangonomiya Kokomi (Hydro Driver), Furina (Hydro Buffer), Yelan (Hydro Sub DPS), Kaedehara Kazuha (Anemo Support). On-field Kokomi heals the team to build Furina Fanfare while Yelan deals off-field damage and Kazuha buffs Hydro.

Pros

  • Excellent team healing
  • Reliable Hydro application
  • Flexible reaction teams
  • Strong accessible weapons

Cons

  • Cannot normally CRIT
  • Stationary jellyfish
  • Burst can need high ER
  • Modest personal damage

Build variations

On-field Hydro Driver (On-field Healer)

Use Kokomi on-field after Burst to heal the entire team and drive Hydro reactions; Ocean-Hued Clam converts her healing into damage.

  • Weapons: Everlasting Moonglow, Prototype Amber, Hakushin Ring
  • Artifacts: Ocean-Hued Clam (4 pcs); Tenacity of the Millelith (4 pcs)
  • Stats: HP% or ER Sands / Hydro DMG Goblet / Healing Bonus Circlet; prioritize Burst uptime, HP and healing.
  • Teams: Off-field damage teammates who benefit from Kokomi attacking during Burst.
  • When it applies: Burst must be available consistently.

Nilou Bloom Trigger (Bloom DPS)

Use when Kokomi owns many Bloom triggers. Prioritize EM while retaining enough healing to survive Bountiful Core damage.

  • Weapons: A Thousand Floating Dreams, Sacrificial Fragments, Fruit of Fulfillment, Magic Guide
  • Artifacts: Flower of Paradise Lost (4 pcs); Ocean-Hued Clam (4 pcs)
  • Stats: EM Sands / EM or HP% Goblet / EM or Healing Bonus Circlet; level Kokomi to 90.
  • Teams: Nilou with Dendro and Hydro only; a Dendro ally should carry Deepwood Memories.
  • When it applies: Only for Nilou Bountiful Bloom teams.
  • Notes: Bloom ownership varies by team and rotation; do not force full EM if healing becomes inadequate.

Electro-Charged Driver (On-field Healer)

Drive Electro-Charged with Hydro Normal Attacks while healing and enabling Electro teammates.

  • Weapons: Hakushin Ring, Prototype Amber, Thrilling Tales of Dragon Slayers
  • Artifacts: Ocean-Hued Clam (4 pcs); Tenacity of the Millelith (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Prioritize HP, Healing Bonus and ER; Hakushin requires Kokomi to trigger an Electro-related reaction.
  • Teams: Off-field Electro teammates such as Fischl and Beidou.
  • When it applies: Hakushin buff depends on Kokomi triggering the reaction.

Short-Rotation Skill Support (Off-field Support)

Use jellyfish healing and Hydro application without relying on an every-rotation Burst.

  • Weapons: Thrilling Tales of Dragon Slayers, Prototype Amber, Sacrificial Fragments
  • Artifacts: Tenacity of the Millelith (4 pcs)
  • Stats: HP% Sands / HP% Goblet / Healing Bonus Circlet; minimal ER if skipping Burst.
  • Teams: Carry that appreciates Hydro application and ATK buffs; ideally stationary enemies.
  • When it applies: Skill uptime is not continuous without a Burst refresh or other timing plan.
  • Notes: Jellyfish cannot follow moving opponents.

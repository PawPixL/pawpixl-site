Role: Support

Xilonen is a versatile Geo support who shreds enemy RES while providing strong healing and team buffs. She fits into a huge variety of teams thanks to her low field time and excellent support utility.

Best weapons for Xilonen

  1. Peak Patrol Song
  2. Favonius Sword
  3. Freedom-Sworn
  4. Flute of Ezpitzal
  5. Skyrider Sword

Best artifacts

  • Scroll of the Hero of Cinder City (4 pcs) · Support
  • Archaic Petra (4 pcs) · Support
  • Instructor (4 pcs) · Support

Main stats

  • Sands: ER% / DEF %
  • Goblet: DEF%
  • Circlet: Healing% / DEF%

Stat targets

  • DEF: 2,000–2,500+ (3,200 with signature)
  • Energy Recharge: 160–200% as needed

Talent priority

  • Ehecatl's Roa: Level 1+
  • Yohual's Scratch: Level 9+
  • Ocelotlicue Point!: Level 9+

Best teams

  • Rock & Burn: Xilonen, Mavuika, Citlali, Bennett
  • Water Works: Xilonen, Neuvillette, Furina, Kaedehara Kazuha
  • Rock Bottom: Xilonen, Navia, Mavuika, Bennett
  • Budget Impact: Xilonen, Gaming, Kaeya, Bennett

Pros

  • Strong RES Shred
  • Powerful Healing
  • Low Field Time
  • Flexible Teammate
  • Easy to Build

Cons

  • Team Comp Restrictions
  • Limited Mono-Geo Synergy
  • Healing Burst Reliant

Xilonen Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams (7.2)

Xilonen build guide for Genshin Impact 7.2: best weapons (Peak Patrol Song, Favonius Sword), artifacts (Scroll of the Hero of Cinder City), main stats.

Last updated: November 12, 2026 · Game version 7.2

Role: Support

Xilonen is a versatile Geo support who shreds enemy RES while providing strong healing and team buffs. She fits into a huge variety of teams thanks to her low field time and excellent support utility.

Best weapons for Xilonen

  1. Peak Patrol Song: Her signature boosts DEF and grants a strong teamwide Elemental DMG Bonus after Normal or Plunging Attacks.
  2. Favonius Sword: Generates particles for the team when she lands CRIT hits. Build enough CRIT Rate to trigger its passive reliably.
  3. Freedom-Sworn: Triggering a reaction grants the party 20% DMG Bonus and 20% ATK for 12s.
  4. Flute of Ezpitzal: Craftable DEF% sword and using Skill raises DEF further.
  5. Skyrider Sword: Budget Energy Recharge stat stick.

Best artifacts for Xilonen

  • Scroll of the Hero of Cinder City (4 pcs) · Support: Her strongest general support set. Grants a large Elemental DMG Bonus when she triggers the right Crystallize reaction. Main stats: ER%/DEF % · DEF% · Healing%/DEF%.
  • Archaic Petra (4 pcs) · Support: A situational alternative when another teammate already uses Scroll of the Hero of Cinder City.
  • Instructor (4 pcs) · Support: Cheap 4-star option giving the team 120 Elemental Mastery after a reaction.

Main stats

  • Sands: ER% / DEF %
  • Goblet: DEF%
  • Circlet: Healing% / DEF%

Stat targets

  • DEF: 2,000–2,500+ (3,200 with signature)
  • Energy Recharge: 160–200% as needed

Talent priority

  • Ehecatl's Roa: Level 1+
  • Yohual's Scratch: Level 9+
  • Ocelotlicue Point!: Level 9+

Best teams for Xilonen

  • Rock & Burn: Xilonen (Support), Mavuika (DPS), Citlali (Shield), Bennett (Support). Xilonen reduces enemy resistance and buffs damage. Mavuika deals damage while Citlali supports reactions and Bennett boosts ATK.
  • Water Works: Xilonen (Healer / Shred), Neuvillette (Main DPS), Furina (Hydro Buff), Kaedehara Kazuha (Anemo Buff). Neuvillette leads while Furina boosts damage. Xilonen reduces resistance and heals, and Kazuha adds grouping and Hydro buffs.
  • Rock Bottom: Xilonen (Healer / Debuffer), Navia (On-field DPS), Mavuika (Off-field DPS / Pyro Enabler), Bennett (Healer / Buffer). Navia is the main damage dealer. Xilonen provides Geo support, Mavuika supplies Pyro and damage, and Bennett heals and buffs ATK.
  • Budget Impact: Xilonen (Healer / Debuffer), Gaming (On-field DPS), Kaeya (Off-field DPS / Cryo Enabler), Bennett (Healer / Buffer). Gaming drives damage with Pyro Plunging Attacks. Kaeya provides Cryo, Bennett heals and buffs ATK, and Xilonen adds resistance reduction.

Cons

Build variations

Scroll Buffer + Healer (Support)

Provide RES shred, Scroll buffs and healing to elemental carries.

  • Weapons: Peak Patrol Song, Favonius Sword, Flute of Ezpitzal
  • Artifacts: Scroll of the Hero of Cinder City (4 pcs)
  • Stats: DEF% and ER; Healing Bonus optional.
  • Teams: Two or more eligible Pyro/Hydro/Electro/Cryo samples for healing.
  • When it applies: Healing requirements depend on team element samples.

Favonius Team Battery (Support)

Trade signature buffing for extra team particles.

  • Weapons: Favonius Sword, Skyrider Sword
  • Artifacts: Scroll of the Hero of Cinder City (4 pcs); Archaic Petra (4 pcs)
  • Stats: CRIT Rate for Favonius, enough ER and DEF.
  • Teams: Energy-hungry teams with relevant elemental reactions.
  • When it applies: Favonius triggers from CRIT hits while on-field.

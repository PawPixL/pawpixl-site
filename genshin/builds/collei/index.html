Collei Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

Collei build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Elegy for the End, Polar Star), artifacts (Deepwood Memories), main stats, talent priority, teams.

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Role: Support / Sub DPS

Collei is a quickswap Dendro support who enables Bloom, Hyperbloom, Quicken and Spread with her boomerang Skill and off-field Burst. Prioritize enough Energy Recharge to maintain Dendro application; Elegy and support sets add team buffs.

Best weapons for Collei

  1. Elegy for the End: ER plus teamwide EM and ATK buffs.
  2. Polar Star: CRIT and Skill/Burst damage. Personal damage; needs extra attack inputs.
  3. Favonius Warbow: CRIT hits generate team Energy. Build CRIT Rate to trigger passive.
  4. Sacrificial Bow: Second Skill aids Dendro and Energy. Needs passive proc; adds field time.
  5. The Stringless: EM plus Skill and Burst damage. Meet ER needs first.
  6. Fading Twilight: ER and rotating damage bonus.
  7. End of the Line: Fishable ER option for Burst uptime.
  8. Raven Bow: Budget EM for Bloom reactions. ER requirement already met.

Best artifacts for Collei

  • Deepwood Memories (4 pcs): Shreds 30% Dendro RES on hits. Main stats: ER · Dendro · CRIT.
  • Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs): Burst grants 20% team ATK. Main stats: ER · Dendro · CRIT.
  • Instructor (4 pcs): Grants team EM after reactions. Main stats: ER · Dendro · CRIT.

Main stats

  • Sands: Energy Recharge / ATK% / EM
  • Goblet: Dendro DMG Bonus / EM
  • Circlet: CRIT Rate / CRIT DMG / EM

Stat targets

  • ER: 160%-200%
  • Fav CR: 30%+
  • CR: 50%-70%
  • CD: 100%-150%

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 8-9
  • Elemental Burst: Level 8-9

Best teams for Collei

  • Hyperbloom Garden: Collei (Dendro Support), Sangonomiya Kokomi (Hydro Driver/Healer), Raiden Shogun (Hyperbloom Trigger), Traveler (Dendro) (Dendro Battery). Kokomi creates Bloom cores; full-EM Raiden triggers Hyperbloom. Both Dendro units maintain the aura. Raiden Skill should hit cores.
  • Tighnari Forestcall: Collei (Dendro Support), Tighnari (Spread DPS), Yae Miko (Electro Sub DPS), Zhongli (Shield). Yae sustains Quicken for Tighnari Spread; Collei adds Dendro, energy, and support buffs while Zhongli protects. Collei can hold Deepwood.
  • Aggravate Sprouts: Collei (Dendro Support), Keqing (Electro DPS), Fischl (Electro Sub DPS), Jean (Healer/VV). Collei keeps Quicken active for Keqing and Fischl Aggravates. Jean heals and shreds Electro RES with Viridescent Venerer.
  • Budget Bloom Circuit: Collei (Dendro Support), Xingqiu (Hydro Sub DPS), Kuki Shinobu (Hyperbloom/Healer), Traveler (Dendro) (Dendro Battery). Xingqiu supplies Hydro, Dendro teammates make cores, and full-EM Kuki heals and triggers Hyperbloom with her Skill.

Pros

  • Fast Dendro application
  • Strong reaction enabler
  • Great free bow option
  • Short field time

Cons

  • High Energy needs
  • Small fixed Burst area
  • Skill needs positioning
  • Limited personal damage

Build variations

Quicken Team Buffer (Dendro Support)

Use for Keqing, Clorinde, or other Aggravate teams when a second unit already carries Deepwood. Noblesse supports ATK-scaling Electro units.

  • Weapons: Elegy for the End, Favonius Warbow
  • Artifacts: Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs); Instructor (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ER sands; CRIT Rate with Favonius, then EM.
  • Teams: Electro damage dealer; ideally another Dendro RES shred source if Dendro damage matters.
  • When it applies: Another unit must hold Deepwood if team needs Dendro RES shred.

Pure Bloom Enabler (Dendro Support)

Use in Bloom teams to sustain Dendro and boost reaction damage. Do not assume Collei owns every Bloom; EM value depends on ownership.

  • Weapons: Elegy for the End, Sacrificial Bow, Favonius Warbow
  • Artifacts: Deepwood Memories (4 pcs); Instructor (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ER first; EM sands/goblet/circlet only if Collei owns significant Bloom reactions.
  • Teams: Reliable Hydro, and enough Dendro application for continuous cores.
  • When it applies: ER and reaction ownership vary by team.

Personal Burst Damage (Off-field DPS)

Use when your team already provides Dendro RES shred and you want more from Collei’s Skill and Burst. Polar Star needs added attack inputs for stacks.

  • Weapons: Polar Star, The Stringless, Fading Twilight
  • Artifacts: Emblem of Severed Fate (4 pcs); Gilded Dreams (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ER to Burst consistently, then Dendro DMG, CRIT, ATK% and EM in Quicken.
  • Teams: Another character carrying Deepwood; Quicken helps personal Spread damage.
  • When it applies: Not her default support build.

F2P Energy Support (Dendro Support)

Use a free or accessible ER bow to keep Burst available each rotation and maintain reaction support without premium equipment.

  • Weapons: Favonius Warbow, End of the Line, Fading Twilight
  • Artifacts: Deepwood Memories (4 pcs); Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ER sands and CRIT Rate circlet with Favonius; otherwise flexible defensive/offensive stats.
  • Teams: Any Dendro reaction team; double Dendro or a battery lowers ER needs.
  • When it applies: Favonius particles require a CRIT hit on-field.

C2 Sprout Uptime (Dendro Support)

Use C2 to extend her off-field Sprout effect after Skill returns when a qualifying reaction occurs. Stay close enough for the ring to hit.

  • Weapons: Elegy for the End, Sacrificial Bow, Favonius Warbow
  • Artifacts: Deepwood Memories (4 pcs); Instructor (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ER requirement first; personal damage remains secondary.
  • Teams: C2 Collei and a team that repeatedly triggers qualifying Dendro reactions.
  • When it applies: Requires Collei C2; extension can occur only once per Skill.

Collei talents

Normal Attack: Supplicant's Bowmanship

Normal Attack
Performs up to 4 consecutive shots with a bow.

Charged Attack
Performs a more precise Aimed Shot with increased DMG.
While aiming, Dendro energy will accumulate on the arrowhead. A fully charged arrow will deal Dendro DMG.

Plunging Attack
Fires off a shower of arrows in mid-air before falling and striking the ground, dealing AoE DMG upon impact.

Elemental Skill: Floral Brush

Cooldown: 12s

Throws out a Floral Ring that deals 1 instance of Dendro DMG to targets it comes into contact with.
The Floral Ring will return after a set time, dealing Dendro DMG once again.

Elemental Burst: Trump-Card Kitty

Cooldown: 15s · Energy cost: 60

Trusty Cuilein-Anbar comes to save the day!
Throws the doll named Cuilein-Anbar, causing an explosion that deals AoE Dendro DMG, creating a Cuilein-Anbar Zone. Cuilein-Anbar will bounce around within this zone, dealing AoE Dendro DMG.

Passive talent: Floral Sidewinder

If one of your party members has triggered Burning, Quicken, Aggravate, Spread, Bloom, Hyperbloom, Burgeon, or Lunar-Bloom reactions before the Floral Ring returns, it will grant the character the Sprout effect upon return, which will continuously deal Dendro DMG equivalent to 40% of Collei's ATK to nearby opponents for 3s.
If another Sprout effect is triggered during its initial duration, the initial effect will be removed. DMG dealt by Sprout is considered Elemental Skill DMG.

Passive talent: The Languid Wood

When a character within the Cuilein-Anbar Zone triggers Burning, Quicken, Aggravate, Spread, Bloom, Hyperbloom, Burgeon, or Lunar-Bloom reactions, the Zone's duration will be increased by 1s.
A single Trump-Card Kitty can be extended this way by up to 3s.

Passive talent: Gliding Champion of Sumeru

Decreases gliding Stamina consumption for your own party members by 20%.
Not stackable with Passive Talents that provide the exact same effects.

Collei constellations

C1: Deepwood Patrol

When in the party and not on the field, Collei's Energy Recharge is increased by 20%.

C2: Through Hill and Copse

The Ascension Talent Floral Sidewinder is changed to this:
The Floral Ring will grant the character the Sprout effect from Floral Sidewinder upon return, dealing 40% of Collei's ATK as Dendro DMG to nearby opponents for 3s.
From the moment of using Floral Brush to the moment when this instance of Sprout effect ends, if any of your party members triggers Burning, Quicken, Aggravate, Spread, Bloom, Hyperbloom, Lunar-Bloom, or Burgeon reactions, the Sprout effect will be extended by 3s.
The Sprout effect can only be extended this way once. If another Sprout effect is triggered during its initial duration, the initial effect will be removed. DMG dealt by Sprout is considered Elemental Skill DMG.
You must first unlock the Ascension Talent "Floral Sidewinder."

C3: Scent of Summer

Increases the Level of Floral Brush by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C4: Gift of the Woods

Using Trump-Card Kitty will increase all nearby characters' Elemental Mastery by 60 for 12s (not including Collei herself).

C5: All Embers

Increases the Level of Trump-Card Kitty by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C6: Forest of Falling Arrows

When the Floral Ring hits, it will create a miniature Cuilein-Anbar that will deal 200% of Collei's ATK as Dendro DMG.
Each Floral Brush can only create one such miniature Cuilein-Anbar.

Collei Build Guide | Best Weapons, Artifacts & Teams

Collei build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Elegy for the End, Polar Star), artifacts (Deepwood Memories), main stats (Energy Recharge / ATK% /…

Role: Support / Sub DPS

Collei is a quickswap Dendro support who enables Bloom, Hyperbloom, Quicken and Spread with her boomerang Skill and off-field Burst. Prioritize enough Energy Recharge to maintain Dendro application; Elegy and support sets add team buffs.

Best weapons for Collei

  1. Elegy for the End
  2. Polar Star
  3. Favonius Warbow
  4. Sacrificial Bow
  5. The Stringless
  6. Fading Twilight
  7. End of the Line
  8. Raven Bow

Best artifacts

  • Deepwood Memories (4 pcs)
  • Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs)
  • Instructor (4 pcs)

Main stats

  • Sands: Energy Recharge / ATK% / EM
  • Goblet: Dendro DMG Bonus / EM
  • Circlet: CRIT Rate / CRIT DMG / EM

Stat targets

  • ER: 160%-200%
  • Fav CR: 30%+
  • CR: 50%-70%
  • CD: 100%-150%

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 8-9
  • Elemental Burst: Level 8-9

Best teams

  • Hyperbloom Garden: Collei, Sangonomiya Kokomi, Raiden Shogun, Traveler (Dendro)
  • Tighnari Forestcall: Collei, Tighnari, Yae Miko, Zhongli
  • Aggravate Sprouts: Collei, Keqing, Fischl, Jean
  • Budget Bloom Circuit: Collei, Xingqiu, Kuki Shinobu, Traveler (Dendro)

Pros

  • Fast Dendro application
  • Strong reaction enabler
  • Great free bow option
  • Short field time

Cons

  • High Energy needs
  • Small fixed Burst area
  • Skill needs positioning
  • Limited personal damage

Build variations

  • Quicken Team Buffer (Dendro Support): Use for Keqing, Clorinde, or other Aggravate teams when a second unit already carries Deepwood. Noblesse supports ATK-scaling Electro units.
  • Pure Bloom Enabler (Dendro Support): Use in Bloom teams to sustain Dendro and boost reaction damage. Do not assume Collei owns every Bloom; EM value depends on ownership.
  • Personal Burst Damage (Off-field DPS): Use when your team already provides Dendro RES shred and you want more from Collei’s Skill and Burst. Polar Star needs added attack inputs for stacks.
  • F2P Energy Support (Dendro Support): Use a free or accessible ER bow to keep Burst available each rotation and maintain reaction support without premium equipment.
  • C2 Sprout Uptime (Dendro Support): Use C2 to extend her off-field Sprout effect after Skill returns when a qualifying reaction occurs. Stay close enough for the ring to hit.

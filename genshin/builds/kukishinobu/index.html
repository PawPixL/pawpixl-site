Kuki Shinobu Build Guide: Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

Kuki Shinobu build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Freedom-Sworn, Key of Khaj-Nisut), artifacts (Flower of Paradise Lost), main stats.

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Role: Hyperbloom / Healer

Kuki Shinobu heals and triggers Hyperbloom with off-field Electro Skill pulses. Build triple EM and level 90 for reaction damage; her Burst is optional. Outside Hyperbloom, HP-focused Tenacity support can improve healing and team buffs.

Best weapons for Kuki Shinobu

  1. Freedom-Sworn: High EM plus a team buff. Trigger reactions consistently; team buff needs its passive to activate.
  2. Key of Khaj-Nisut: HP scaling and shareable EM. Best in HP/EM hybrid builds; requires Skill-hit passive stacks.
  3. Xiphos' Moonlight: EM and teamwide Energy Recharge. Useful when teammates value its ER conversion. More ER at higher refinements.
  4. Iron Sting: Craftable EM for Hyperbloom. Weapon passive does not boost Hyperbloom damage. R1 works.
  5. Toukabou Shigure: EM substat boosts Hyperbloom. Event-limited; damage passive does not buff Hyperbloom.
  6. Favonius Sword: Team particles on CRIT hits. Support only: build CRIT Rate to activate passive while on-field. Higher refinements improve consistency.
  7. Dark Iron Sword: Free EM starter sword. Low-cost Hyperbloom option; smaller EM stat than 4-star alternatives.
  8. Sapwood Blade: Craftable team EM leaf buff. Dendro reaction teams; an ally must collect the leaf.

Best artifacts for Kuki Shinobu

  • Flower of Paradise Lost (4 pcs) · Best Hyperbloom: Maximizes Hyperbloom damage. Main stats: EM / EM / EM; prioritize level 90.
  • Gilded Dreams (4 pcs) · Farm-efficient: Large EM bonus for Hyperbloom. Main stats: EM / EM / EM; pair farming with Deepwood.
  • Tenacity of the Millelith (4 pcs) · Support: Skill maintains team ATK buff. Main stats: HP% / HP% / Healing Bonus; support-only.

Best teams for Kuki Shinobu

  • Dendro Rainstorm: Kuki Shinobu (Hyperbloom / Heal), Nahida (Dendro / Deepwood), Xingqiu (Hydro Applicator), Yelan (Hydro Sub DPS). Nahida and double Hydro generate Dendro Cores; Kuki triggers Hyperbloom and heals. Keep Deepwood on Nahida.
  • Scholar Hyperbloom: Kuki Shinobu (Hyperbloom / Heal), Alhaitham (On-field Dendro), Nahida (Dendro / Deepwood), Xingqiu (Hydro Applicator). Alhaitham drives Dendro damage; Xingqiu makes cores and Kuki triggers them while healing.
  • Budget Bloom Circuit: Kuki Shinobu (Hyperbloom / Heal), Traveler (Dendro) (Dendro Applicator), Xingqiu (Hydro Applicator), Collei (Dendro Support). Accessible Hyperbloom core: Dendro Traveler and Collei enable cores, Xingqiu supplies Hydro and Kuki detonates them.
  • Coffee Needed: Kuki Shinobu (Healer / Buffer), Keqing (On-field Electro), Fischl (Electro Sub DPS), Nahida (Dendro / Deepwood). Kuki sustains Quicken and heals while Keqing and Fischl deal Aggravate damage; switch to Tenacity support stats.

Build variations

Gilded Hyperbloom (Off-field Reaction DPS)

Choose the efficient Gilded/Deepwood domain instead of farming Flower of Paradise Lost directly.

  • Weapons: Iron Sting, Xiphos' Moonlight, Dark Iron Sword
  • Artifacts: Gilded Dreams (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Triple EM; prioritize level 90. Burst and ER are optional.
  • Teams: Hydro and Dendro applications; a Deepwood holder.
  • When it applies: Kuki should trigger cores herself.
  • Notes: Set effectiveness depends on teammate elements.

Aggravate Team Healer (Healer / Buffer)

Keep Skill active for healing, Electro application and Tenacity ATK buff; do not treat this as her Hyperbloom damage build.

  • Weapons: Key of Khaj-Nisut, Favonius Sword, Freedom-Sworn
  • Artifacts: Tenacity of the Millelith (4 pcs)
  • Stats: HP%/HP%/Healing Bonus for healing; mix EM if comfortable.
  • Teams: Electro DPS and Dendro applier in a Quicken team.
  • Notes: If on Favonius, get enough CRIT Rate to trigger its passive on-field.

Energy Utility Support (Healer / Battery)

Trade reaction damage for energy support. Favonius particles require a CRIT hit while Kuki is on-field.

  • Weapons: Favonius Sword, Sapwood Blade
  • Artifacts: Tenacity of the Millelith (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ER as needed for Burst; HP% and CRIT Rate for healing/Favonius.
  • Teams: Team that values particles or a Dendro-reaction EM leaf.
  • Notes: Sapwood leaf is collected by a teammate, not automatically granted.

Key HP / EM Hybrid (Healer / Team Buffer)

Hybrid setup for additional healing and team buffs while retaining some reaction contribution.

  • Weapons: Key of Khaj-Nisut, Freedom-Sworn
  • Artifacts: Tenacity of the Millelith (4 pcs); Gilded Dreams (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Balance HP and EM; HP boosts healing and Key passive.
  • Teams: Able to stack Key passive with Skill hits; supportive teammates.
  • When it applies: Key passive stacks required.
  • Notes: Key can share EM; evaluate full-EM Hyperbloom separately.

F2P Full-EM Trigger (Off-field Reaction DPS)

Accessible Hyperbloom damage with low weapon investment. Iron Sting R1 is sufficient.

  • Weapons: Iron Sting, Dark Iron Sword
  • Artifacts: Flower of Paradise Lost (4 pcs); Gilded Dreams (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Triple EM; aim for level 90 before chasing CRIT or ER.
  • Teams: At least one Dendro and one Hydro unit making cores.
  • Notes: Weapon damage bonuses do not increase Hyperbloom reaction damage.

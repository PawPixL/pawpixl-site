Role: Hyperbloom / Healer

Kuki Shinobu heals and triggers Hyperbloom with off-field Electro Skill pulses. Build triple EM and level 90 for reaction damage; her Burst is optional. Outside Hyperbloom, HP-focused Tenacity support can improve healing and team buffs.

Pros

  • Useful from C0

Cons

  • Skill drains her HP
  • Single-target healing
  • Limited personal damage
  • C0 ring has downtime

Build variations

  • Gilded Hyperbloom (Off-field Reaction DPS): Choose the efficient Gilded/Deepwood domain instead of farming Flower of Paradise Lost directly.
  • Aggravate Team Healer (Healer / Buffer): Keep Skill active for healing, Electro application and Tenacity ATK buff; do not treat this as her Hyperbloom damage build.
  • Energy Utility Support (Healer / Battery): Trade reaction damage for energy support. Favonius particles require a CRIT hit while Kuki is on-field.
  • Key HP / EM Hybrid (Healer / Team Buffer): Hybrid setup for additional healing and team buffs while retaining some reaction contribution.
  • F2P Full-EM Trigger (Off-field Reaction DPS): Accessible Hyperbloom damage with low weapon investment. Iron Sting R1 is sufficient.

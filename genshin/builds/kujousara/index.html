Role: Support / Sub DPS

Kujou Sara is an Electro quickswap buffer whose short Crowfeather ATK boost scales with her base ATK and Skill level. Her Burst adds strong damage, while C6 grants buffed allies 60% Electro CRIT DMG. She shines with fast, Electro-focused carries.

  2. Skyward Harp
  3. Polar Star
  4. Mouun's Moon

  • CRIT DMG: 120%+

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 8-9+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 8-9+

Best teams

  • Shogun Thunder Court: Kujou Sara, Raiden Shogun, Kaedehara Kazuha, Bennett
  • Aggravate Edict: Kujou Sara, Keqing, Fischl, Yaoyao

Pros

  • Strong base-ATK buff
  • C6 Electro CRIT boost
  • High Burst damage
  • Excellent with Raiden

Cons

  • Very short buff window
  • High Energy needs
  • Awkward setup before C2
  • Needs an Electro carry

Build variations

  • C6 Electro Hypercarry Buffer (Support): Apply Sara’s Crowfeather or Burst buff just before Raiden/Keqing attacks. C6 adds 60% Electro CRIT DMG that cannot be snapshotted.
  • Personal Burst Damage (Sub DPS): Use when Sara contributes meaningful personal damage as well as buffs; snapshot her own ATK buff onto her Burst when possible.
  • Noblesse Team Buffer (Support): Choose when the team benefits more from an extra 20% ATK than Sara’s personal Burst damage. Sara’s Skill still sets her own ATK buff strength.
  • Favonius Team Battery (Support): Favonius reduces Sara’s ATK-buff strength due to its low base ATK, but can help stabilize the party’s rotation.
  • Accessible Buff Support (Support): Fading Twilight is excellent if owned from its past event. Otherwise craft Prototype Crescent or use Sacrificial Bow for energy utility.

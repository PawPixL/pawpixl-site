Kujou Sara Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

Kujou Sara build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Elegy for the End, Skyward Harp), artifacts (Emblem of Severed Fate), main stats, talent priority.

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Role: Support / Sub DPS

Kujou Sara is an Electro quickswap buffer whose short Crowfeather ATK boost scales with her base ATK and Skill level. Her Burst adds strong damage, while C6 grants buffed allies 60% Electro CRIT DMG. She shines with fast, Electro-focused carries.

Best weapons for Kujou Sara

  1. Elegy for the End: ER and extra team ATK/EM when triggered.
  2. Skyward Harp: 674 base ATK; strong buff and Burst DMG.
  3. Polar Star: Strong personal Burst DMG and CRIT.
  4. Mouun's Moon: High base ATK; boosts Burst DMG. Best in high-total-Energy teams.
  5. Fading Twilight: 565 base ATK, ER and damage bonus. Event-exclusive; unavailable to new players. R5 from original event.
  6. Sacrificial Bow: ER and 565 base ATK; extra Skill use. Use a Crowfeather aimed shot for particles. Higher refinements ease resets.
  7. Favonius Warbow: ER and team particles; weaker ATK buff. Lower base ATK; build CRIT Rate. More consistent at high refinement.
  8. Prototype Crescent: Craftable choice for her ATK buff. Lower base ATK than Fading Twilight; aimed shots are situational.

Best artifacts for Kujou Sara

  • Emblem of Severed Fate (4 pcs) · Best: ER plus amplified Burst damage. Main stats: ER/ATK% · Electro · CRIT.
  • Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs) · Team: Burst grants allies 20% ATK. Main stats: ER/ATK% · Electro · CRIT.
  • Noblesse Oblige + Thundering Fury (2 + 2 pcs) · Interim: Burst damage and Electro DMG. Main stats: ER/ATK% · Electro · CRIT.

Main stats

  • Sands: Energy Recharge / ATK%
  • Goblet: Electro DMG Bonus
  • Circlet: CRIT Rate / CRIT DMG

Stat targets

  • Raiden ER: 170%+
  • 2 Electro: 230%+
  • 3 Electro: 130-160%
  • CRIT Rate: 60-70%
  • CRIT DMG: 120%+

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 8-9+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 8-9+

Best teams for Kujou Sara

  • Shogun Thunder Court: Kujou Sara (Buffer), Raiden Shogun (Main DPS), Kaedehara Kazuha (Support), Bennett (Healer / Buffer). Sara buffs Raiden before her Burst; Kazuha shreds Electro RES and Bennett adds healing and ATK. C6 Sara greatly amplifies Electro CRIT hits.
  • Aggravate Edict: Kujou Sara (ATK Buffer), Keqing (On-field DPS), Fischl (Off-field DPS / Battery), Yaoyao (Healer / Dendro Enabler). Yaoyao enables Quicken; Fischl supplies off-field Electro and energy. Sara buffs Keqing’s short damage window, especially at C6.
  • Clorinde Lightning: Kujou Sara (ATK Buffer), Clorinde (On-field DPS), Fischl (Off-field DPS / Battery), Kirara (Shielder / Dendro Enabler). Kirara enables Quicken and protection; Fischl batteries the team. Sara buffs Clorinde’s Electro damage, but her short buff limits uptime.
  • Overload Commission: Kujou Sara (ATK Buffer), Raiden Shogun (On-field DPS / Battery), Chevreuse (Healer / Buffer), Xiangling (Off-field DPS / Pyro Enabler). Only Electro/Pyro teammates preserve Chevreuse’s Overloaded support. Sara buffs Raiden, while Xiangling provides Pyro and off-field damage.

Pros

  • Strong base-ATK buff
  • C6 Electro CRIT boost
  • High Burst damage
  • Excellent with Raiden

Cons

  • Very short buff window
  • High Energy needs
  • Awkward setup before C2
  • Needs an Electro carry

Build variations

C6 Electro Hypercarry Buffer (Support)

Apply Sara’s Crowfeather or Burst buff just before Raiden/Keqing attacks. C6 adds 60% Electro CRIT DMG that cannot be snapshotted.

  • Weapons: Elegy for the End, Skyward Harp, Fading Twilight
  • Artifacts: Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs); Emblem of Severed Fate (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Meet ER needs first; high weapon base ATK determines her ATK buff.
  • Teams: Electro damage dealer with a brief, frontloaded attack window.
  • When it applies: Requires C6 for CRIT DMG bonus.
  • Notes: Skill talent increases the base-ATK-scaling ATK buff; artifact ATK% does not.

Personal Burst Damage (Sub DPS)

Use when Sara contributes meaningful personal damage as well as buffs; snapshot her own ATK buff onto her Burst when possible.

  • Weapons: Polar Star, Skyward Harp, Mouun's Moon, Fading Twilight
  • Artifacts: Emblem of Severed Fate (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ER to Burst each rotation, then Electro DMG, CRIT and ATK%.
  • Teams: Team supports Sara’s Burst and provides ample Energy.
  • When it applies: Polar Star stacks vary by rotation.
  • Notes: Mouun’s Moon performs well with high party Burst Energy costs.

Noblesse Team Buffer (Support)

Choose when the team benefits more from an extra 20% ATK than Sara’s personal Burst damage. Sara’s Skill still sets her own ATK buff strength.

  • Weapons: Elegy for the End, Skyward Harp, Sacrificial Bow
  • Artifacts: Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Increase ER until she can Burst reliably before prioritizing damage.
  • Teams: No other teammate should carry 4pc Noblesse Oblige.
  • When it applies: Not useful if another 4pc Noblesse user is present.
  • Notes: Aim Crowfeather explosion at enemy to generate Electro particles.

Favonius Team Battery (Support)

Favonius reduces Sara’s ATK-buff strength due to its low base ATK, but can help stabilize the party’s rotation.

  • Weapons: Favonius Warbow, Sacrificial Bow
  • Artifacts: Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs); Emblem of Severed Fate (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Build CRIT Rate to trigger Favonius; retain enough ER.
  • Teams: Energy-hungry teams that value particles above a maximum ATK buff.
  • When it applies: Favonius passive must CRIT while Sara is on field.
  • Notes: C2’s extra Crowfeather does not produce Skill particles; an aimed Crowfeather explosion does.

Accessible Buff Support (Support)

Fading Twilight is excellent if owned from its past event. Otherwise craft Prototype Crescent or use Sacrificial Bow for energy utility.

  • Weapons: Fading Twilight, Prototype Crescent, Sacrificial Bow
  • Artifacts: Emblem of Severed Fate (4 pcs); Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ER first; use ATK% Sands only if Burst is already consistent.
  • Teams: Prefer double Electro with Raiden or Fischl for smoother energy.
  • When it applies: Fading Twilight is no longer obtainable.
  • Notes: Level the bow for base ATK; ATK% artifacts do not raise Sara’s Crowfeather buff.

Kujou Sara talents

Normal Attack: Tengu Bowmanship

Normal Attack
Performs up to 5 consecutive shots with a bow.

Charged Attack
Performs a more precise Aimed Shot with increased DMG.
While aiming, crackling lightning will accumulate on the arrowhead. An arrow fully charged with the storm's might will deal Electro DMG.
When in the Crowfeather Cover state, a fully-charged arrow will leave a Crowfeather behind after it hits.

Plunging Attack
Fires off a shower of arrows in mid-air before falling and striking the ground, dealing AoE DMG upon impact.

Elemental Skill: Tengu Stormcall

Cooldown: 10s

Retreats rapidly with the speed of a tengu, summoning the protection of the Crowfeather.
Gains Crowfeather Cover for 18s, and when Kujou Sara fires a fully-charged Aimed Shot, Crowfeather Cover will be consumed, and will leave a Crowfeather at the target location.
Crowfeathers will trigger Tengu Juurai: Ambush after a short time, dealing Electro DMG and granting the active character within its AoE an ATK Bonus based on Kujou Sara's Base ATK.

The ATK Bonuses from different Tengu Juurai will not stack, and their effects and duration will be determined by the last Tengu Juurai to take effect.

Elemental Burst: Subjugation: Koukou Sendou

Cooldown: 20s · Energy cost: 80

Casts down Tengu Juurai: Titanbreaker, dealing AoE Electro DMG. Afterwards, Tengu Juurai: Titanbreaker spreads out into 4 consecutive bouts of Tengu Juurai: Stormcluster, dealing AoE Electro DMG.
Tengu Juurai: Titanbreaker and Tengu Juurai: Stormcluster can provide the active character within their AoE with the same ATK Bonus as given by the Elemental Skill, Tengu Stormcall.

The ATK Bonus provided by various kinds of Tengu Juurai will not stack, and their effects and duration will be determined by the last Tengu Juurai to take effect.

Passive talent: Immovable Will

While in the Crowfeather Cover state provided by Tengu Stormcall, Aimed Shot charge times are decreased by 60%.

Passive talent: Decorum

When Tengu Juurai: Ambush hits opponents, Kujou Sara will restore 1.2 Energy to all party members for every 100% Energy Recharge she has. This effect can be triggered once every 3s.

Passive talent: Land Survey

When dispatched on an expedition in Inazuma, time consumed is reduced by 25%.

Kujou Sara constellations

C1: Crow's Eye

When Tengu Juurai grants characters ATK Bonuses or hits opponents, the CD of Tengu Stormcall is decreased by 1s.
This effect can be triggered once every 3s.

C2: Dark Wings

Unleashing Tengu Stormcall will leave a weaker Crowfeather at Kujou Sara's original position that will deal 30% of its original DMG.

C3: The War Within

Increases the Level of Subjugation: Koukou Sendou by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C4: Conclusive Proof

The number of Tengu Juurai: Stormcluster released by Subjugation: Koukou Sendou is increased to 6.

C5: Spellsinger

Increases the Level of Tengu Stormcall by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C6: Sin of Pride

The Electro DMG of characters who have had their ATK increased by Tengu Juurai has its CRIT DMG increased by 60%.

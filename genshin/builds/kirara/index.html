Kirara Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams (7.2)

Kirara build guide for Genshin Impact 7.2: best weapons (Key of Khaj-Nisut, The Dockhand's Assistant).

Last updated: November 12, 2026 · Game version 7.2

Role: Support / Shielder

Kirara is a Dendro shielder who protects teammates with her HP-scaling Skill. She excels in quickswap Quicken teams & can support Bloom teams with a second Dendro applier. Her shield is especially effective against Dendro DMG, & her parcel form makes exploration easier.

Best weapons for Kirara

  1. Key of Khaj-Nisut: Massive HP boosts shield strength. Skill hits can grant the team an EM buff based on Kirara’s HP.
  2. The Dockhand's Assistant: Its HP secondary stat strengthens Kirara’s shield.
  3. Freedom-Sworn: Can provide teamwide Normal, Charged, and Plunging Attack DMG and ATK buffs after Kirara triggers reactions.
  4. Primordial Jade Cutter: Provides CRIT Rate and a modest HP bonus. Better for personal damage than pure shield utility. Use for a hybrid damage build.
  5. Favonius Sword: Generates neutral Energy particles to help the whole team recharge. Build enough CR to trigger its passive. Favonius must CRIT while Kirara is on field.
  6. Sacrificial Sword: Can reset her Skill cooldown for another shield refresh and extra Dendro particles. Passive needs a Skill hit to trigger.
  7. Xiphos' Moonlight: Offers team Energy Recharge based on Kirara’s EM, though a shield build usually stacks HP instead. More useful with some EM investment.
  8. Sapwood Blade: Craftable sword with ER. Dendro reactions create a leaf that one teammate can collect for an EM buff. A teammate must pick up the leaf.

Best artifacts for Kirara

  • Tenacity of the Millelith + Vourukasha's Glow (2 + 2 pcs) · Shield Strength: Two HP bonuses strengthen Kirara’s shield without relying on reaction timing or Burst uptime. Main stats: HP% / HP% / HP%.
  • Instructor (4 pcs) · Team EM: Triggering a reaction grants teammates EM. Main stats: HP% / HP% / HP%.
  • Deepwood Memories (4 pcs) · Dendro Support: Reduces enemy Dendro resistance after Skill or Burst hits. Main stats: HP% / HP% / HP%.

Main stats

  • Sands: HP%
  • Goblet: HP%
  • Circlet: HP% / CRIT Rate (Favonius)

Stat targets

  • HP: 35,000 - 45,000
  • ER: 100% - 120% (Double Dendro)
  • ER: 120% - 160% (Solo Dendro)
  • CRIT Rate: 30% - 50% (Favonius)

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 9+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 6–8

Best teams for Kirara

  • Lightning Delivery: Kirara (Shielder / Dendro Enabler), Keqing (On-field DPS), Fischl (Off-field DPS / Battery), Kaedehara Kazuha (Buffer / Debuffer). Keqing and Fischl trigger Aggravate while Kazuha groups and buffs Electro damage. Kirara provides shielding and Quicken support.
  • Forest Express: Kirara (Shielder / Dendro Enabler), Tighnari (Quickswap DPS), Yae Miko (Off-field DPS), Nahida (Dendro Enabler / Buffer). Tighnari deals Spread damage with support from Nahida and Yae Miko. Kirara shields him and generates extra Dendro particles.
  • Bloom Delivery: Kirara (Shielder / Dendro Enabler), Nilou (Bloom Buffer / Hydro Enabler), Nahida (Dendro Enabler / Buffer), Sangonomiya Kokomi (Healer / Hydro Enabler). Nilou enables Bountiful Cores. Nahida supplies Dendro, Kokomi applies Hydro and heals, and Kirara shields against Bloom damage.
  • Budget Courier: Kirara (Shielder / Dendro Enabler), Lisa (Electro DPS / Debuffer), Fischl (Off-field DPS / Battery), Traveler (Dendro) (Dendro Enabler / Battery). Lisa and Fischl deal Aggravate damage. Dendro Traveler maintains Quicken while Kirara protects the team with her shield.

Pros

  • Strong Dendro shield
  • Short Skill cooldown
  • Great for Quicken
  • Excellent exploration

Cons

  • No healing
  • Limited off-field Dendro
  • Needs HP for shielding
  • Burst damage is modest

Build variations

Maximum Shield (Support)

Choose this when shield strength matters more than damage.

  • Weapons: Key of Khaj-Nisut, The Dockhand's Assistant
  • Artifacts: Tenacity of the Millelith + Vourukasha's Glow (2 + 2 pcs)
  • Stats: Stack HP%; ER only if using Burst regularly.
  • Teams: Teams needing interruption protection.
  • When it applies: Skill hold can extend field time.

Instructor Quicken Buffer (Support)

Trade some shield strength for an EM team buff.

  • Weapons: Favonius Sword, Sacrificial Sword
  • Artifacts: Instructor (4 pcs)
  • Stats: HP% main stats; sufficient ER and Favonius CR.
  • Teams: Quicken team that can reliably trigger a reaction.
  • When it applies: Instructor is a four-star set with lower stat ceilings.

Deepwood Shield Support (Support)

Combine protection with Dendro resistance reduction.

  • Weapons: Favonius Sword, The Dockhand's Assistant
  • Artifacts: Deepwood Memories (4 pcs)
  • Stats: HP% first; ER/CR for utility.
  • Teams: Dendro damage team without another Deepwood holder.
  • When it applies: Needs Skill hits to maintain shred.

Role: Healer / Support

Sigewinne is an HP-scaling Hydro healer whose Skill restores the team and boosts limited instances of off-field Elemental Skill damage. She pairs especially well with Furina. Her Hydro application is modest, and most healer builds skip her costly Burst to save field time.

Best weapons for Sigewinne

  1. Silvershower Heartstrings
  2. Aqua Simulacra
  3. Elegy for the End
  4. Sequence of Solitude
  5. Recurve Bow
  6. Favonius Warbow
  7. Sacrificial Bow
  8. Jade Vista

Best artifacts

  • Ocean-Hued Clam (4 pcs) · Best
  • Song of Days Past (4 pcs)
  • Tenacity of the Millelith (4 pcs)

Main stats

  • Sands: HP% / Energy Recharge
  • Goblet: HP%
  • Circlet: HP%

Stat targets

  • HP: 50,000 - 65,000 (65k caps A1)
  • Energy Recharge: 100% - 120% (no Burst)
  • Energy Recharge: 155% - 220% (Burst)
  • CRIT Rate: 45% - 60% (Favonius)

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 9-10
  • Elemental Burst: Level 1-6 (Optional)

Best teams

  • Bubble Taser: Sigewinne, Furina, Fischl, Kaedehara Kazuha
  • House Call: Sigewinne, Kamisato Ayato, Furina, Kaedehara Kazuha
  • Bloom Ward: Sigewinne, Nilou, Nahida, Xingqiu
  • Fireworks Clinic: Sigewinne, Yoimiya, Furina, Zhongli

Pros

  • Strong teamwide healing
  • Boosts off-field Skill hits
  • Excellent Furina partner
  • Simple healer rotation

Cons

  • Limited Hydro application
  • Buff stacks are limited
  • High HP goal for full buff
  • Burst takes field time

Build variations

  • Furina Fanfare Partner (Healer): Use Sigewinne’s Skill to restore team HP after Furina drains it and support Fanfare generation.
  • Quickswap Burst Damage (Sub DPS): Add her Burst during downtime for personal Hydro damage, rather than spending all her field time on healing.
  • Tenacity ATK Buffer (Support): Use Skill hits to trigger Tenacity’s team ATK buff; less useful for HP-scaling or reaction-damage carries.
  • Accessible HP Healer (Healer): A low-cost build that gets strong team healing and off-field Skill buffs without her signature weapon.

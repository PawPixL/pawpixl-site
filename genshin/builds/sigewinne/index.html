Sigewinne Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

Sigewinne build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Silvershower Heartstrings, Aqua Simulacra), artifacts (Ocean-Hued Clam), main stats.

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Role: Healer / Support

Sigewinne is an HP-scaling Hydro healer whose Skill restores the team and boosts limited instances of off-field Elemental Skill damage. She pairs especially well with Furina. Her Hydro application is modest, and most healer builds skip her costly Burst to save field time.

Best weapons for Sigewinne

  1. Silvershower Heartstrings: Huge HP% boosts healing and her off-field Skill buff; the passive aids her Burst DMG.
  2. Aqua Simulacra: HP% passive and CD boost her personal DMG, especially when her Burst is used.
  3. Elegy for the End: ER plus team ATK and EM buffs once its sigils are stacked.
  4. Sequence of Solitude: 41.3% HP at level 90 boosts healing and her Skill buff.
  5. Recurve Bow: 46.9% HP at level 90 is excellent for healing and her Skill buff; its defeat-heal passive is minor.
  6. Favonius Warbow: Provides Energy Recharge and team particles from on-field CRIT hits. When allies need energy; build enough CRIT Rate to trigger it.
  7. Sacrificial Bow: Can reset her Skill for extra healing, Hydro application, and another set of buff stacks. Costs additional field time; reset requires a Skill hit. Higher refinements improve consistency.
  8. Jade Vista

Best artifacts for Sigewinne

  • Ocean-Hued Clam (4 pcs) · Best: Converts her frequent healing into extra Physical bubble DMG, even when teammates are healthy. Main stats: HP% · HP% · HP%.
  • Song of Days Past (4 pcs): Converts healing into a flat DMG bonus for teammates. Main stats: HP% · HP% · HP%.
  • Tenacity of the Millelith (4 pcs): Her Skill hits can provide an ATK buff for several seconds. Main stats: HP% · HP% · HP%.

Main stats

  • Sands: HP% / Energy Recharge
  • Goblet: HP%
  • Circlet: HP%

Stat targets

  • HP: 50,000 - 65,000 (65k caps A1)
  • Energy Recharge: 100% - 120% (no Burst)
  • Energy Recharge: 155% - 220% (Burst)
  • CRIT Rate: 45% - 60% (Favonius)

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 9-10
  • Elemental Burst: Level 1-6 (Optional)

Best teams for Sigewinne

  • Bubble Taser: Sigewinne, Furina, Fischl, Kaedehara Kazuha. Sigewinne heals for Furina Fanfare and buffs off-field Skill hits from Furina and Fischl; Kazuha groups and shreds resistance.
  • House Call: Sigewinne, Kamisato Ayato, Furina, Kaedehara Kazuha. Ayato drives Hydro damage while Furina buffs and attacks off-field. Sigewinne sustains Fanfare and buffs Furina’s Skill hits.
  • Bloom Ward: Sigewinne, Nilou, Nahida, Xingqiu. Nilou enables Bountiful Cores; Nahida supplies Dendro while Xingqiu drives Hydro application. Sigewinne keeps the team healthy.
  • Fireworks Clinic: Sigewinne, Yoimiya, Furina, Zhongli. Furina and Sigewinne provide Hydro for some Vaporizes; Sigewinne heals Furina’s HP drain while Zhongli protects Yoimiya.

Pros

  • Strong teamwide healing
  • Boosts off-field Skill hits
  • Excellent Furina partner
  • Simple healer rotation

Cons

  • Limited Hydro application
  • Buff stacks are limited
  • High HP goal for full buff
  • Burst takes field time

Build variations

Furina Fanfare Partner (Healer)

Use Sigewinne’s Skill to restore team HP after Furina drains it and support Fanfare generation.

  • Weapons: Silvershower Heartstrings, Sequence of Solitude, Recurve Bow
  • Artifacts: Song of Days Past (4 pcs); Ocean-Hued Clam (4 pcs)
  • Stats: HP% / HP% / HP%; ignore Burst ER if not casting Burst.
  • Teams: Furina plus off-field Skill damage dealers.
  • When it applies: Furina healing synergy; off-field Skill damage buff has limited stacks.

Quickswap Burst Damage (Sub DPS)

Add her Burst during downtime for personal Hydro damage, rather than spending all her field time on healing.

  • Weapons: Aqua Simulacra, Silvershower Heartstrings, Elegy for the End
  • Artifacts: Emblem of Severed Fate (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Prioritize enough ER for Burst, then HP%, Hydro DMG and CRIT.
  • Teams: A quickswap team with time for her Burst; preferably double Hydro.
  • When it applies: High energy needs; generally weaker than healer-focused builds at C0.

Tenacity ATK Buffer (Support)

Use Skill hits to trigger Tenacity’s team ATK buff; less useful for HP-scaling or reaction-damage carries.

  • Weapons: Silvershower Heartstrings, Sequence of Solitude, Recurve Bow
  • Artifacts: Tenacity of the Millelith (4 pcs)
  • Stats: HP% / HP% / HP%; Energy Recharge only if Burst is used.
  • Teams: An ATK-scaling carry; no competing Tenacity holder.
  • When it applies: Buff is not permanent and depends on Skill hits.

Accessible HP Healer (Healer)

A low-cost build that gets strong team healing and off-field Skill buffs without her signature weapon.

  • Weapons: Recurve Bow, Sequence of Solitude, Favonius Warbow
  • Artifacts: Ocean-Hued Clam (4 pcs); Song of Days Past (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Stack HP%; with Favonius, add CRIT Rate and consider ER.
  • Teams: Any team needing reliable healing; especially useful alongside Furina.
  • When it applies: Sequence of Solitude requires ownership from a past event.

Sigewinne talents

Normal Attack: Targeted Treatment

Normal Attack
Performs up to 3 consecutive attacks.

Charged Attack
Performs a more precise Aimed Shot with increased DMG.
While aiming, the power of Hydro will continually accumulate on the arrow. After fully charging, Sigewinne will periodically fire slow-moving Mini-Stration Bubbles toward the target, dealing Hydro DMG. When released, an arrow imbued with torrential energy will deal Hydro DMG to the opponent hit.

Plunging Attack
Fires off a shower of arrows in mid-air before falling and striking the ground, dealing AoE DMG upon impact.

Elemental Skill: Rebound Hydrotherapy

Cooldown: 18s

Blows a Bolstering Bubblebalm that can stimulate blood flow and help patients drift off to sleep using a bubblegun specially made in the Fortress of Meropide.
Bolstering Bubblebalms will bounce between nearby opponents, dealing Hydro DMG based on Sigewinne's max HP to opponents it hits. When it bounces, it will restore HP to all nearby party members except Sigewinne herself. The amount healed is based on Sigewinne's max HP.
After bouncing 5 times, the Bolstering Bubblebalm will disappear and restore HP to Sigewinne based on her max HP value.
When no opponents are present, the Bolstering Bubblebalm will bounce nearby. Only one Bolstering Bubblebalm created by a Sigewinne may exist at once.
When Held, the Bolstering Bubblebalm can be boosted to an even bigger size.

Hold
Enter Aiming Mode to begin blowing an even bigger, even more breathtaking Bolstering Bubblebalm!
The Bolstering Bubblebalm will grow bigger the longer the skill is Held, until it grows up to two tiers in size. Each tier it grows increases its DMG by 5% and healing by 5%. When a Bolstering Bubblebalm bounces, it will drop down a tier in size, until it returns to being a normal Bolstering Bubblebalm.
After weaker opponents are hit by big Bolstering Bubblebalms, they will be imprisoned and will be unable to move.

In addition, 2 Sourcewater Droplets will be created near Sigewinne when she uses this Skill. Each Sourcewater Droplet Sigewinne collects will grant her a Bond of Life worth 10% of her Max HP.
When Sigewinne's Bond of Life is cleared, she regains 1 Elemental Energy for every 2,000 HP worth of the Bond of Life that was cleared. Sigewinne can regain up to 5 Elemental Energy in this way.

Arkhe: Ousia
Bolstering Bubblebalms periodically call down a Surging Blade on the position they hit, dealing Ousia-aligned Hydro DMG based on Sigewinne's max HP.

Elemental Burst: Super Saturated Syringing

Cooldown: 18s · Energy cost: 70

Takes out a special Fortress of Meropide-made syringe and assault the area in front with waves of kindness and medicine, dealing AoE Hydro DMG based on Sigewinne's max HP.
In addition, Sigewinne absorbs up to 2 nearby Sourcewater Droplets within a certain range when she uses this skill.

Passive talent: Requires Appropriate Rest

Sigewinne grants herself the "Semi-Strict Bedrest" effect for 18s after using Rebound Hydrotherapy: Sigewinne gains an 8% Hydro DMG Bonus and 10 stacks of Convalescence. When the Elemental Skills of your nearby off-field party members other than Sigewinne deal DMG, consume 1 stack of Convalescence, and increase the DMG dealt by this instance of Elemental Skill DMG. Every 1,000 Max HP Sigewinne has above 30,000 increases the DMG by 80. The maximum DMG increase for Elemental Skills that can be gained in this way is 2,800.

Passive talent: Detailed Diagnosis, Thorough Treatment

When Sigewinne performs healing, the amount healed for this instance is increased based on the total current value of the Bonds of Life on all party members: For each 1,000 HP worth of Bonds of Life, the outgoing healing is increased by 3%. The amount of healing provided can be increased by up to 30% in this way.

Passive talent: Emergency Dose

While underwater, heals your active character over 2.5s when their HP falls under 50%. The amount healed is equal to 50% of their Max HP, and their All Elemental and Physical RES will be decreased by 10% for 10s. This effect can be triggered up to once every 20s.

Sigewinne constellations

C1: "Can the Happiest of Spirits Understand Anxiety?"

Rebound Hydrotherapy's Bolstering Bubblebalm can bounce 3 extra times, and the first 3 bounces will not cause big Bubblebalms to become smaller.
The Ascension Talent "Requires Appropriate Rest" is enhanced: Each Bubblebalm bounce adds 1 stack to her Convalescence tally, and the values for the Convalescence stacks' DMG bonus will be modified to: Every 1,000 max HP Sigewinne has above 30,000 increases the DMG by 100. The maximum DMG increase for Elemental Skills that can be gained in this way is 3,500. You must first unlock the Ascension Talent "Requires Appropriate Rest."

C2: "Can the Most Merciful of Spirits Defeat Its Foes?"

When using Rebound Hydrotherapy and Super Saturated Syringing, Sigewinne can create a Bubbly Shield worth 30% of her Max HP that absorbs Hydro DMG with 250% efficiency. The Bubbly Shield will persist until Sigewinne finishes using relevant skills.
Additionally, after Rebound Hydrotherapy's Bolstering Bubblebalm or Super Saturated Syringing hits an opponent, that opponent's Hydro RES will be decreased by 35% for 8s.

C3: "Can the Healthiest of Spirits Cure Fevers?"

Increases the Level of Rebound Hydrotherapy by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C4: "Can the Loveliest of Spirits Keep Decay at Bay?"

The duration of Super Saturated Syringing is extended by 3s.

C5: "Can the Most Joyful of Spirits Alleviate Agony?"

Increases the Level of Super Saturated Syringing by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C6: "Can the Most Radiant of Spirits Pray For Me?"

When Sigewinne performs healing, she will increase the CRIT Rate and CRIT DMG of her Super Saturated Syringing based on her Max HP. Every 1,000 Max HP she has will increase CRIT Rate by 0.4% and CRIT DMG by 2.2% for 15s. The maximum increase achievable this way is 20% CRIT Rate and 110% CRIT DMG.

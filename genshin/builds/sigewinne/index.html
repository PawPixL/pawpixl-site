Sigewinne Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams (7.2)

Sigewinne build guide for Genshin Impact 7.2: best weapons (Silvershower Heartstrings, Aqua Simulacra), artifacts (Ocean-Hued Clam), main stats.

Last updated: November 12, 2026 · Game version 7.2

Role: Healer / Support

Sigewinne is an HP-scaling Hydro healer whose Skill restores the team and boosts limited instances of off-field Elemental Skill damage. She pairs especially well with Furina. Her Hydro application is modest, and most healer builds skip her costly Burst to save field time.

Best weapons for Sigewinne

  1. Silvershower Heartstrings: Huge HP% boosts healing and her off-field Skill buff; the passive aids her Burst DMG.
  2. Aqua Simulacra: HP% passive and CD boost her personal DMG, especially when her Burst is used.
  3. Elegy for the End: ER plus team ATK and EM buffs once its sigils are stacked.
  4. Sequence of Solitude: 41.3% HP at level 90 boosts healing and her Skill buff.
  5. Recurve Bow: 46.9% HP at level 90 is excellent for healing and her Skill buff; its defeat-heal passive is minor.
  6. Favonius Warbow: Provides Energy Recharge and team particles from on-field CRIT hits. When allies need energy; build enough CRIT Rate to trigger it.
  7. Sacrificial Bow: Can reset her Skill for extra healing, Hydro application, and another set of buff stacks. Costs additional field time; reset requires a Skill hit. Higher refinements improve consistency.
  8. Jade Vista

Best artifacts for Sigewinne

  • Ocean-Hued Clam (4 pcs) · Best: Converts her frequent healing into extra Physical bubble DMG, even when teammates are healthy. Main stats: HP% · HP% · HP%.
  • Song of Days Past (4 pcs): Converts healing into a flat DMG bonus for teammates. Main stats: HP% · HP% · HP%.
  • Tenacity of the Millelith (4 pcs): Her Skill hits can provide an ATK buff for several seconds. Main stats: HP% · HP% · HP%.

Main stats

  • Sands: HP% / Energy Recharge
  • Goblet: HP%
  • Circlet: HP%

Stat targets

  • HP: 50,000 - 65,000 (65k caps A1)
  • Energy Recharge: 100% - 120% (no Burst)
  • Energy Recharge: 155% - 220% (Burst)
  • CRIT Rate: 45% - 60% (Favonius)

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 9-10
  • Elemental Burst: Level 1-6 (Optional)

Best teams for Sigewinne

  • Bubble Taser: Sigewinne (Healer / Buffer), Furina (Off-field DPS / Buffer), Fischl (Off-field DPS / Battery), Kaedehara Kazuha (Buffer / Debuffer). Sigewinne heals for Furina Fanfare and buffs off-field Skill hits from Furina and Fischl; Kazuha groups and shreds resistance.
  • House Call: Sigewinne (Healer / Buffer), Kamisato Ayato (On-field DPS), Furina (Off-field DPS / Buffer), Kaedehara Kazuha (Buffer / Debuffer). Ayato drives Hydro damage while Furina buffs and attacks off-field. Sigewinne sustains Fanfare and buffs Furina’s Skill hits.
  • Bloom Ward: Sigewinne (Healer / Buffer), Nilou (Bloom Buffer / Hydro Enabler), Nahida (Dendro Enabler / Buffer), Xingqiu (Off-field DPS / Hydro Enabler). Nilou enables Bountiful Cores; Nahida supplies Dendro while Xingqiu drives Hydro application. Sigewinne keeps the team healthy.
  • Fireworks Clinic: Sigewinne (Healer / Buffer), Yoimiya (On-field DPS), Furina (Off-field DPS / Buffer), Zhongli (Shielder / Debuffer). Furina and Sigewinne provide Hydro for some Vaporizes; Sigewinne heals Furina’s HP drain while Zhongli protects Yoimiya.

Pros

  • Simple healer rotation

Cons

  • Limited Hydro application
  • Buff stacks are limited

Build variations

Furina Fanfare Partner (Healer)

Use Sigewinne’s Skill to restore team HP after Furina drains it and support Fanfare generation.

  • Weapons: Silvershower Heartstrings, Sequence of Solitude, Recurve Bow
  • Artifacts: Song of Days Past (4 pcs); Ocean-Hued Clam (4 pcs)
  • Stats: HP% / HP% / HP%; ignore Burst ER if not casting Burst.
  • Teams: Furina plus off-field Skill damage dealers.
  • When it applies: Furina healing synergy; off-field Skill damage buff has limited stacks.

Quickswap Burst Damage (Sub DPS)

Add her Burst during downtime for personal Hydro damage, rather than spending all her field time on healing.

  • Weapons: Aqua Simulacra, Silvershower Heartstrings, Elegy for the End
  • Artifacts: Emblem of Severed Fate (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Prioritize enough ER for Burst, then HP%, Hydro DMG and CRIT.
  • Teams: A quickswap team with time for her Burst; preferably double Hydro.
  • When it applies: High energy needs; generally weaker than healer-focused builds at C0.

Tenacity ATK Buffer (Support)

Use Skill hits to trigger Tenacity’s team ATK buff; less useful for HP-scaling or reaction-damage carries.

  • Weapons: Silvershower Heartstrings, Sequence of Solitude, Recurve Bow
  • Artifacts: Tenacity of the Millelith (4 pcs)
  • Stats: HP% / HP% / HP%; Energy Recharge only if Burst is used.
  • Teams: An ATK-scaling carry; no competing Tenacity holder.
  • When it applies: Buff is not permanent and depends on Skill hits.

Accessible HP Healer (Healer)

A low-cost build that gets strong team healing and off-field Skill buffs without her signature weapon.

  • Weapons: Recurve Bow, Sequence of Solitude, Favonius Warbow
  • Artifacts: Ocean-Hued Clam (4 pcs); Song of Days Past (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Stack HP%; with Favonius, add CRIT Rate and consider ER.
  • Teams: Any team needing reliable healing; especially useful alongside Furina.
  • When it applies: Sequence of Solitude requires ownership from a past event.

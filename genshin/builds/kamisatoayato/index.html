Role: Main DPS

Ayato is an on-field Hydro DPS whose Elemental Skill unleashes rapid AoE Shunsuiken slashes. His Burst adds off-field Hydro application and Normal Attack damage buffs. He fits Hyperbloom, Burgeon, Electro-Charged and carry teams, with flexible rotations and gear.

Pros

  • Consistent AoE Hydro
  • Flexible reaction teams
  • Strong at C0
  • Easy Skill combo

Cons

  • Lengthy Skill cooldown
  • Burst needs Energy
  • Limited burst damage
  • F2P swords are weaker

Build variations

  • Furina Hypercarry (On-field DPS): Use this when Furina enables frequent HP changes to activate Marechaussee Hunter.
  • Off-Field Burst Support (Off-field DPS): Use Ayato for his Burst droplets and Normal Attack buff when a different unit needs field time.
  • Shielded Normal Attack DPS (On-field DPS): Use an accessible event sword that shines while the shield stays active.
  • Electro-Charged Cooldown (On-field DPS): Try 4pc Thundering Fury to reduce Skill cooldown and enable back-to-back Shunsuiken windows.
  • Accessible Hydro Carry (On-field DPS): Choose readily available equipment while saving premium weapons for other characters.

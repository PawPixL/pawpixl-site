Kamisato Ayato Build Guide: Weapons, Artifacts and Teams (7.2)

Kamisato Ayato build guide for Genshin Impact 7.2: best weapons (Haran Geppaku Futsu, Primordial Jade Cutter), artifacts (Heart of Depth), main stats.

Last updated: November 12, 2026 · Game version 7.2

Role: Main DPS

Ayato is an on-field Hydro DPS whose Elemental Skill unleashes rapid AoE Shunsuiken slashes. His Burst adds off-field Hydro application and Normal Attack damage buffs. He fits Hyperbloom, Burgeon, Electro-Charged and carry teams, with flexible rotations and gear.

Best weapons for Kamisato Ayato

  1. Haran Geppaku Futsu: Signature CRIT Rate sword. Its Elemental DMG and Normal Attack bonuses strongly enhance Skill-empowered Shunsuiken. Use teammate Skills before Ayato Skill.
  2. Primordial Jade Cutter: Excellent CR and HP-to-ATK conversion.
  3. Mistsplitter Reforged: High CD and Elemental DMG bonuses.
  4. Uraku Misugiri: High CD and Normal Attack bonuses.
  5. The Black Sword: Reliable CRIT Rate and Normal Attack DMG, plus a small heal during Shunsuiken attacks. One of his best 4-star swords.
  6. Calamity of Eshu: Boosts Normal Attack DMG and CRIT Rate while shielded; can outperform Black Sword in shield teams. Only while protected by a shield.
  7. Kagotsurube Isshin: Free Story Quest sword with ATK and a useful hit-triggered ATK buff. An accessible option for on-field Ayato.
  8. Lion's Roar: Increases damage against Electro-affected foes, making it viable in Electro-Charged teams only. Electro aura must remain on targets. Higher refinement improves this niche.

Best artifacts for Kamisato Ayato

  • Heart of Depth (4 pcs) · Best: A top general-purpose set: Hydro DMG plus a Normal Attack damage buff after Skill use. Main stats: ATK% · Hydro DMG · CRIT.
  • Gladiator's Finale (4 pcs): Another top set; its ATK% and Normal Attack bonus perform close to Heart of Depth. Choose better substats. Main stats: ATK% · Hydro DMG · CRIT.
  • Echoes of an Offering (4 pcs): Competitive Normal Attack proc set. Main stats: ATK% · Hydro DMG · CRIT.

Main stats

  • Sands: ATK%
  • Goblet: Hydro DMG
  • Circlet: Crit Rate / Crit DMG

Stat targets

  • CRIT Rate: 65% - 80%
  • CRIT DMG: 130% - 190%
  • ATK: 1,700 - 2,200
  • ER Solo Hydro: 130% - 160%
  • ER Double Hydro: 120% - 135%

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 8-9+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 8-9+

Best teams for Kamisato Ayato

  • Boba Hyperbloom: Kamisato Ayato (On-field DPS), Nahida (Dendro Enabler / Buffer), Kuki Shinobu (Healer / Reaction Trigger), Fischl (Off-field DPS / Battery). Ayato creates Dendro Cores with Nahida; full-EM Kuki triggers Hyperbloom and heals. Fischl adds damage and Electro energy.
  • Blooming Boba: Kamisato Ayato (On-field DPS), Nilou (Bloom Buffer / Hydro Enabler), Nahida (Dendro Enabler / Buffer), Baizhu (Healer / Shielder). Nilou enables Bountiful Cores, Nahida provides Dendro, Baizhu heals, and Ayato applies AoE Hydro. Manage Bloom self-damage.
  • Burgeon Boba: Kamisato Ayato (On-field DPS), Thoma (Burgeon Trigger / Shielder), Nahida (Dendro Enabler / Buffer), Fischl (Off-field DPS / Battery). Ayato makes cores with Nahida; EM Thoma detonates them. Fischl adds damage and reduces Burning. Shielding may need extra sustain.
  • Sparkle Princess: Kamisato Ayato (On-field DPS), Fischl (Off-field DPS / Battery), Beidou (Off-field DPS), Sucrose (EM Buffer / Debuffer). Ayato drives Fischl and Beidou while applying Hydro; Sucrose groups foes and shreds resistances. Beidou appreciates Electro energy.

Build variations

Furina Hypercarry (On-field DPS)

Use this when Furina enables frequent HP changes to activate Marechaussee Hunter.

  • Weapons: Haran Geppaku Futsu, Primordial Jade Cutter, The Black Sword
  • Artifacts: Marechaussee Hunter (4 pcs); Heart of Depth (4 pcs)
  • Stats: CRIT Rate can be lower with 4pc Marechaussee; keep ATK%/Hydro/CRIT and enough ER.
  • Teams: Furina plus a reliable team healer such as Xilonen or Jean.
  • When it applies: Requires consistent Furina HP fluctuation.
  • Notes: Be mindful that Ayato Burst can snapshot before Marechaussee CRIT stacks build.

Off-Field Burst Support (Off-field DPS)

Use Ayato for his Burst droplets and Normal Attack buff when a different unit needs field time.

  • Weapons: Favonius Sword, Skyward Blade, Mistsplitter Reforged
  • Artifacts: Emblem of Severed Fate (4 pcs); Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Use ER Sands as needed; solo-Hydro, one-Skill rotations may need 155–175% ER or more.
  • Teams: An on-field carry that values extra Hydro application; account for cooldown and Energy.
  • When it applies: One Skill per Burst; more demanding Energy requirements.
  • Notes: Favonius sacrifices personal damage for team particles. Noblesse only if no teammate already carries it.

Shielded Normal Attack DPS (On-field DPS)

Use an accessible event sword that shines while the shield stays active.

  • Weapons: Calamity of Eshu, Haran Geppaku Futsu, The Black Sword
  • Artifacts: Heart of Depth (4 pcs); Gladiator's Finale (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Standard ATK%/Hydro/CRIT; balance CRIT with Eshu shielded bonuses.
  • Teams: Reliable shield such as Thoma, Kirara, Citlali, or Dahlia.
  • When it applies: Shield uptime required for its damage and CRIT passive.

Electro-Charged Cooldown (On-field DPS)

Try 4pc Thundering Fury to reduce Skill cooldown and enable back-to-back Shunsuiken windows.

  • Weapons: Lion's Roar, The Black Sword, Haran Geppaku Futsu
  • Artifacts: Thundering Fury (4 pcs); Heart of Depth (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Normal offensive stats; meet ER. Lion’s Roar needs a consistent Electro aura.
  • Teams: Two Electro units, ideally Fischl and Beidou, with reliable reactions.
  • When it applies: Requires regular Electro-Charged procs.
  • Notes: The cooldown-compression gain is situational rather than a universal best build.

Accessible Hydro Carry (On-field DPS)

Choose readily available equipment while saving premium weapons for other characters.

  • Weapons: Kagotsurube Isshin, The Black Sword, Calamity of Eshu
  • Artifacts: Gladiator's Finale (4 pcs); Heart of Depth (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ATK% Sands, Hydro Goblet, CRIT Circlet; prioritize strong substats and enough ER.
  • Teams: Any suitable on-field team; Eshu needs a shield.
  • When it applies: Story sword is free; Black Sword requires paid Battle Pass.
  • Notes: Kagotsurube Isshin is obtained from Kazuha’s Story Quest; Calamity of Eshu is a past event weapon.

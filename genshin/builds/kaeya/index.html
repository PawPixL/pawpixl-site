Kaeya Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

Kaeya build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Mistsplitter Reforged, Light of Foliar Incision), artifacts (Blizzard Strayer), main stats.

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Role: Main DPS, Sub DPS

Kaeya is a flexible Cryo damage dealer who excels in Freeze and quickswap teams. His Burst deals off-field Cryo damage, while his Skill generates energy. Use Blizzard Strayer against freezable enemies; use Emblem or Melt builds when Freeze is unreliable.

Best weapons for Kaeya

  1. Mistsplitter Reforged: High CRIT DMG and elemental damage.
  2. Light of Foliar Incision: Strong CRIT DMG stat stick.
  3. Haran Geppaku Futsu: CRIT Rate and Normal Attack boosts. Avoid CRIT overcap in Freeze.
  4. Primordial Jade Cutter: Huge CRIT Rate and an ATK boost. Avoid CRIT overcap in Freeze.
  5. Wolf-Fang: Boosts Skill and Burst damage. Better in Melt builds.
  6. Kagotsurube Isshin: Free ATK-focused damage option.
  7. Favonius Sword: Energy for Kaeya and his team. Build CRIT Rate to trigger passive.
  8. Amenoma Kageuchi: Craftable ATK and energy refund.

Best artifacts for Kaeya

  • Blizzard Strayer (4 pcs) · Freeze DPS: Best against Frozen enemies; grants substantial CRIT Rate. Main stats: ATK% · Cryo DMG · CRIT DMG.
  • Emblem of Severed Fate (4 pcs) · General Burst DPS: Reliable Burst damage outside Freeze, especially with high ER. Main stats: ATK% / ER · Cryo DMG · CRIT.
  • Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs) · Team Support: Boosts team ATK after Kaeya uses his Burst. Main stats: ER · Cryo DMG · CRIT Rate.

Main stats

  • Sands: ATK%
  • Goblet: Cryo DMG
  • Circlet: Crit DMG

Stat targets

  • CR: 25% - 45%
  • CD: 150% - 220%
  • ATK: 1600 - 2100
  • ER: 120% - 160%

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 6+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 8-9+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 8-9+

Best teams for Kaeya

  • Frostbound Knights: Kaeya (On-field Cryo DPS), Chongyun (Cryo infusion), Xingqiu (Hydro / Off-field DPS), Sucrose (VV / Grouping). Chongyun infuses Kaeya’s attacks, Xingqiu enables Freeze, and Sucrose groups enemies and shreds Cryo resistance.
  • Winter’s Court: Kaeya (Cryo DPS), Rosaria (Cryo DPS / Battery), Furina (Hydro / Buffer), Jean (Healer / VV). Double Cryo sustains damage and energy; Furina buffs the team while Jean heals and shreds Cryo resistance.
  • Melting Point: Kaeya (Off-field Cryo DPS), Rosaria (Cryo DPS / Battery), Xiangling (Pyro application), Bennett (ATK buffer / Healer). Xiangling maintains Pyro for Kaeya and Rosaria to Reverse Melt. Bennett boosts ATK and batteries Xiangling.
  • Glacial Assist: Kaeya (Cryo battery / Support), Kamisato Ayaka (Main DPS), Sangonomiya Kokomi (Hydro / Healer), Kaedehara Kazuha (VV / Grouping). Kaeya batteries Ayaka and contributes Cryo damage; Kokomi enables Freeze and Kazuha groups and buffs.

Pros

  • Reliable Cryo application
  • Strong Freeze battery
  • Flexible quickswap teams
  • Free starter character

Cons

  • Short Burst duration
  • Close-range Burst
  • Freeze-resistant bosses
  • Constellations hard to obtain

Build variations

Reverse Melt DPS (Off-field DPS)

Choose this against bosses or enemies that cannot be Frozen. Melt some of Kaeya’s Burst hits while rotating through teammates.

  • Weapons: Primordial Jade Cutter, Mistsplitter Reforged, Wolf-Fang, Lion's Roar
  • Artifacts: Emblem of Severed Fate (4 pcs); Gilded Dreams (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Build more ER than Freeze (often 150–190% with double Cryo); EM becomes useful. Use CRIT Rate or DMG circlet.
  • Teams: Consistent Pyro application, ideally Xiangling + Bennett and a second Cryo damage dealer.
  • When it applies: Only some Burst hits can trigger Melt.
  • Notes: ER needs vary widely with teammates and Skill uses.

Off-field Cryo Support (Support)

Use Kaeya as a low-field-time Cryo battery and Burst support rather than a dedicated carry.

  • Weapons: Favonius Sword, Skyward Blade, Amenoma Kageuchi
  • Artifacts: Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs); Emblem of Severed Fate (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ER first for Burst uptime, then CRIT Rate for Favonius; personal damage is secondary.
  • Teams: A Cryo or Pyro on-field carry who values extra Cryo application, particles, or Noblesse buffs.
  • When it applies: Favonius requires CRIT hits; Noblesse should not duplicate another holder.
  • Notes: Solo Cryo teams may require substantially more ER.

Role: Stellar Support / Enabler

Odette is a versatile Cryo support who enhances Stellar reactions with consistent off-field application, team buffs, and strong synergy with Anemo drivers.

Best weapons for Odette

  1. Whitelake Frostfeather
  2. Azurelight
  3. Freedom-Sworn
  4. Aquila Favonia
  5. Emberwell
  6. Finale of the Deep
  7. Xiphos' Moonlight
  8. Harbinger of Dawn

Best artifacts

  • Heart of the Furnace (4 pcs) · Sub DPS
  • Disenchantment in Deep Shadow (4 pcs) · Sub DPS
  • Gladiator's Finale + A Day Carved From Rising Winds (2 + 2 pcs) · Sub DPS

Main stats

  • Sands: ATK%
  • Goblet: ATK%
  • Circlet: CRIT Rate / CRIT DMG

Stat targets

  • ATK: 2,000-3,000
  • Crit Rate: 50% - 60%
  • Crit DMG: 180%+
  • Energy Recharge: 120% - 140%

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 9+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 9+

Best teams

  • Puppet Masters: Odette, Sandrone, Yae Miko, Nicole
  • Icebreaker: Odette, Wriothesley, Yae Miko, Nicole
  • Slumber Party: Odette, Yumemizuki Mizuki, Traveler (Cryo), Sucrose
  • Discount Detectives: Odette, Shikanoin Heizou, Barbara, Fischl

Pros

  • Powerful Stellar reaction buffs
  • Reliable off-field Cryo
  • Enables strong team synergies
  • Flexible team compositions

Cons

  • Requires Stellar teammates
  • Limited non-Stellar value
  • Needs consistent reaction triggers

Build variations

  • Stellar-Conduct Enabler (Support): Enable and buff reaction damage while applying Cryo off-field.
  • Personal Cryo Damage (Off-field DPS): Use a damage-focused artifact setup instead of maximum team buffs.

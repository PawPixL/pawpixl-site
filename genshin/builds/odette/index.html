Odette Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

Odette build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Whitelake Frostfeather, Azurelight), artifacts (Heart of the Furnace), main stats, talent priority.

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Role: Stellar Support / Enabler

Odette is a versatile Cryo support who enhances Stellar reactions with consistent off-field application, team buffs, and strong synergy with Anemo drivers.

Best weapons for Odette

  1. Whitelake Frostfeather: Provides 22.1% CRIT Rate, up to 24% ATK, and 50% Stellar Glimmer CRIT DMG at R1, plus Energy restoration. Odette’s signature and best-in-slot weapon.
  2. Azurelight: Provides 22.1% CRIT Rate, 24% ATK, and up to an additional 24% ATK and 40% CRIT DMG when Odette has zero Energy, making it a strong 5-star alternative.
  3. Freedom-Sworn: EM and a team buff after triggering reactions; on-field triggering may be required.
  4. Aquila Favonia: High Base ATK and Physical DMG; mainly a stat stick for non-physical support builds.
  5. Emberwell: Provides 165 EM and up to 32% ATK plus 32% Stellar Glimmer reaction DMG at R5, making it Odette’s strongest craftable 4-star option for personal damage
  6. Finale of the Deep: Provides 27.6% ATK and up to 24% additional ATK at R5, plus up to 300 flat ATK when its Bond of Life is cleared. A strong craftable 4-star option for Odette.
  7. Xiphos' Moonlight: R 1 + Elemental Mastery Small ATK and Energy Recharge support option.
  8. Harbinger of Dawn: R 1 + CRIT DMG Effective F2P CRIT DMG sword; keep HP above 90% to gain the 14% CRIT Rate bonus.

Best artifacts for Odette

  • Heart of the Furnace (4 pcs) · Sub DPS: Provides 18% ATK and boosts teamwide Stellar Glimmer DMG by 50% when the wearer triggers or deals Stellar Glimmer damage, making it one of Odette’s best-in-slot support sets. Main stats: ATK% · ATK% · CR/CD.
  • Disenchantment in Deep Shadow (4 pcs) · Sub DPS: Provides 18% ATK and boosts Stellar-Conduct DMG by 40%, with 16% CR against affected enemies. A strong personal-DMG set for Odette in Stellar-Conduct teams, especially when another teammate carries Heart of the Furnace.
  • Gladiator's Finale + A Day Carved From Rising Winds (2 + 2 pcs) · Sub DPS: Provides 36% ATK, making it a flexible option for boosting Odette’s personal damage.

Best teams for Odette

  • Puppet Masters: Odette (Stellar), Sandrone (Main DPS), Yae Miko (Sub DPS), Nicole (Support). Odette enables and buffs Stellar-Conduct, Sandrone drives on-field damage, Yae adds off-field Electro damage, and Nicole provides ATK buffs and shielding.
  • Icebreaker: Odette (Stellar), Wriothesley (Main DPS), Yae Miko (Off-Sub DPS), Nicole (Support). Wriothesley drives on-field Stellar-Conduct damage, Odette enables and buffs Stellar-Conduct, Yae provides off-field Electro damage, and Nicole supplies ATK buffs and shielding.
  • Slumber Party: Odette (Stellar), Yumemizuki Mizuki (Main DPS), Traveler (Cryo) (Cryo), Sucrose (EM Build). Mizuki drives on-field Stellar Swirl damage, Odette enables and buffs Stellar Swirl, Cryo Traveler provides off-field Cryo and reaction buffs, and Sucrose boosts EM and shreds RES.
  • Discount Detectives: Odette (Stellar Support / Enabler), Shikanoin Heizou (Anemo DPS), Barbara (Support), Fischl (Off-field). Heizou drives on-field Stellar Swirl damage, Odette provides off-field Cryo and enables Stellar Swirl, Fischl adds off-field Electro damage, and Barbara provides healing and Hydro application.

Build variations

Stellar-Conduct Enabler (Support)

Enable and buff reaction damage while applying Cryo off-field.

  • Weapons: Whitelake Frostfeather, Emberwell
  • Artifacts: Heart of the Furnace (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Prioritize ER and support stats for rotation.
  • Teams: Electro ally triggering Stellar-Conduct.
  • When it applies: Avoid duplicate Heart of the Furnace buffs.

Personal Cryo Damage (Off-field DPS)

Use a damage-focused artifact setup instead of maximum team buffs.

  • Weapons: Whitelake Frostfeather, Azurelight
  • Artifacts: Disenchantment in Deep Shadow (4 pcs); Gladiator's Finale + A Day Carved From Rising Winds (2 + 2 pcs)
  • Stats: Balance ATK, CRIT and ER.
  • Teams: Team that sustains Cryo reactions and needs extra damage.
  • When it applies: More personal damage means fewer team utility bonuses.

Odette talents

Normal Attack: Snow Swan Variation

Normal Attack
Unleashes a sword attack of up to 5 consecutive strikes.

Charged Attack
Consumes a certain amount of Stamina to unleash a dazzling slash on opponents in front of her.

Plunging Attack
Attacks opponents in her path while plunging from mid-air, then deals AoE DMG upon landing.

Elemental Skill: Adagio: Phantom Night Dancers

Cooldown: Coda at Dawn's Tolling: 15s / 15s

With slow, graceful dance steps, Odette deals AoE Cryo DMG to the opponent, and also summons her Solo Dance Double to the field.
If a Dance Double summoned by Odette is already on the field, this will re-summon the Dance Double and reset its duration.

Solo Dance Double
Alternates between the Plume and Wing dance moves, periodically attacking nearby opponents and dealing to them AoE Cryo DMG.
Additionally, for 6s after unleashing the Elemental Skill Adagio: Phantom Night Dancers, Odette's Elemental Skill Adagio: Phantom Night Dancers will become the special Elemental Skill Adagio: Coda at Dawn's Tolling instead, where a dance duet deals AoE Cryo DMG to nearby opponents over time. Then, when the duet ends, she deals another instance of AoE Cryo DMG that is considered Stellar-Conduct or Stellar Swirl DMG.

Elemental Burst: Presto: Bluebird Finale

Cooldown: 15s · Energy cost: 60

With quick, lively dance steps, Odette deals multiple instances of AoE Cryo DMG, and summons her Solo Dance Double. She also gains Snow Swan's Dream, which increases the Stellar Glimmer reaction DMG Odette deals.
Additionally, for 6s after unleashing the Elemental Burst Presto: Bluebird Finale, Odette's Elemental Skill Adagio: Phantom Night Dancers will be replaced with the special Elemental Skill Adagio: Coda at Dawn's Tolling instead.

If there is a Solo Dance Double summoned by Odette on the field, it will be summoned to her side with its duration refreshed.

Passive talent: Spring Rite of the Chosen One

When Odette summons her Solo Dance Double, she also obtains 4 stacks of Marvelous Splendor.

Passive talent: Pathetique of Pateticheskaya

For every 100 ATK Odette has over 1,000, her Stellar Glimmer DMG is additionally increased by 1.5% of the original DMG. She can deal up to 30% more additional DMG in this way.

Passive talent: Stellar Jubilee: Dance of Aurore

Odette will enter the Radiance: Stellar-Conduct state when she is inside a Polestar Field, or the Radiance: Stellar Swirl state for 8s after a nearby party member triggers a Stellar Swirl reaction.
When a party member triggers a Superconduct or Cryo Swirl reaction, it becomes a Stellar-Conduct or Stellar Swirl reaction instead, and the Base DMG of said reaction is also increased by 0.7% for every 100 points of Odette's ATK. A maximum increase of 14% can be obtained in this way.

Passive talent: Echo of Winter Daydreams

Shows the locations of Snezhnaya's Local Specialties on the mini-map, and this effect also applies in the Shadow Realm.
Practicing dance in one's spare time might even trigger other effects...

Odette constellations

C1: "On This Danceless Morn, She Gazes at Her Reflection"

After unleashing the special Elemental Skill Adagio: Coda at Dawn's Tolling, at the dance duet's end Odette will deal an additional instance of Cryo AoE DMG to nearby opponents that is considered:
Radiance: Stellar-Conduct or when not in a Radiance state: Stellar-Conduct reaction DMG at 300% of Odette's ATK;
Radiance: Stellar Swirl: Stellar Swirl reaction DMG at 450% of Odette's ATK.

Additionally, the Ascension Talent "Spring Rite of the Chosen One" is also enhanced: now, when the Solo Dance Double is summoned, Odette also gains 2 stacks of Marvelous Splendor. When Odette is off-field, the rate at which Marvelous Splendor is removed is sped up to 2 stacks per second.

C2: "I Must See the Snow Swan's Unseen Dream for Myself, She Thought"

The Ascension Talent "Spring Rite of the Chosen One" is enhanced as follows: every stack of Marvelous Splendor active also increases the character's ATK by 7%.
Additionally, if Odette is in the Radiance: Stellar Glimmer state when there is a Solo Dance Double on the field, opponents near the Dance Double will also have their corresponding Elemental RES lowered by 20%.
·Radiance: Stellar-Conduct: Cryo and Electro.
·Radiance: Stellar Swirl: Cryo and Anemo.

C3: "I'll Chase the Shouting Wind Along, Climbing Alone As I Go"

Increases the Level of Adagio: Phantom Night Dancers by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C4: "Up, Up the Long, Delirious, Burning Blue"

The Elemental Burst Presto: Bluebird Finale is enhanced as follows: when Odette obtains Snow Swan's Dream, Stellar Glimmer reaction DMG dealt by other nearby party members is increased by 50% of Snow Swan Dream's effects.

Additionally, when a party member deals Stellar Glimmer reaction DMG to an opponent, Odette will also join in with a coordinated attack, dealing an instance of AoE Cryo DMG. This effect, which can trigger once every 3.5s, will be considered:
·Radiance: Stellar-Conduct or when not in a Radiance state: Stellar-Conduct reaction DMG at 66% of Odette's ATK.
·Radiance: Stellar Swirl: Stellar Swirl reaction DMG at 99% of Odette's ATK.

C5: "Oh! I Have Slipped the Surly Bonds of Earth"

Increases the Level of Presto: Bluebird Finale by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C6: "Put Out My Hand, and Touched the Face of the Divine"

The Ascension Talent "Spring Rite of the Chosen One" is enhanced as follows: When Odette grants Marvelous Splendor to all nearby party members, her own Marvelous Splendor stacks will no longer decrease.
Additionally, characters affected by Marvelous Splendor have their Stellar Glimmer reaction DMG dealt to opponents elevated by 25%, and Stellar Glimmer reaction DMG dealt by Odette is elevated by an additional 20%.

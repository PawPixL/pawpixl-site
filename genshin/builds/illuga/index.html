Illuga Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams (7.2)

Illuga build guide for Genshin Impact 7.2: best weapons (Favonius Lance, Dragon's Bane), artifacts (Silken Moon's Serenade), main stats, talent priority.

Last updated: November 12, 2026 · Game version 7.2

Role: Support / Buffer

Illuga is an off-field Geo buffer who strengthens Geo and direct Lunar-Crystallize damage through his Burst and passives. Build enough ER to Burst each rotation, then stack EM. He cannot enable Lunar-Crystallize by himself.

Best weapons for Illuga

  1. Favonius Lance: Team particles; steady Bursts. Build CRIT Rate to trigger passive.
  2. Dragon's Bane: High EM boosts his Burst buffs. Best when ER is already sufficient.
  3. Engulfing Lightning: Large ER helps Burst uptime. Premium fallback; ATK adds little.
  4. Kitain Cross Spear: EM and self Energy refund. Use Skill before Burst; mind Energy drain. R5 helps.
  5. Frostbreath: ER plus team energy support. Needs Hydro or Lunar-Crystallize triggers.
  6. Prototype Starglitter: Craftable ER for consistent Bursts. Passive offers little to his support role.

Best artifacts for Illuga

  • Silken Moon's Serenade (4 pcs) · Best: Team EM buff; limited before C2. Main stats: EM/ER · EM · EM/CR.
  • Instructor (4 pcs): Reaction EM buff; weaker stats. Main stats: EM/ER · EM · EM/CR.
  • Scroll of the Hero of Cinder City (4 pcs): Buffs Geo and Hydro damage. Main stats: EM/ER · EM · EM/CR.

Main stats

  • Sands: Elemental Mastery / Energy Recharge
  • Goblet: Elemental Mastery
  • Circlet: Elemental Mastery / Crit Rate

Stat targets

  • ER: 160% - 200%
  • EM: 600 - 900
  • CR: 25% - 45%

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 6+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 9+

Best teams for Illuga

  • Moonlit Vanguard: Illuga (Geo Buffer), Zibai (On-field DPS), Columbina (Off-field DPS / Buffer), Gorou (DEF Buffer). Zibai deals direct Lunar-Crystallize damage; Columbina enables reactions, while Gorou and Illuga boost Geo output.
  • Lunar Bulwark: Illuga (Geo Buffer), Zibai (On-field DPS), Aino (Off-field DPS / Enabler), Zhongli (Shielder / Debuffer). Aino provides Hydro for Zibai; Zhongli supplies shielding and RES shred as Illuga amplifies direct Geo damage.
  • Nightingale Chorus: Illuga (Geo Buffer), Linnea (On-field DPS), Columbina (Off-field DPS / Buffer), Gorou (DEF Buffer). Linnea drives Geo damage and enables Lunar-Crystallize; Columbina and Gorou support her alongside Illuga.
  • Probably Hopeless: Illuga (Geo Buffer), Noelle (On-field DPS), Furina (Off-field DPS / Buffer), Gorou (DEF Buffer). Noelle heals and drives Geo damage, enabling Furina buffs while Gorou and Illuga bolster her damage.

Pros

  • Strong Geo buffs
  • Excellent Zibai synergy
  • Simple rotations
  • Short cooldowns

Cons

  • Needs Lunar enabler
  • High ER demands
  • Low personal damage
  • C2 improves set uptime

Build variations

C0–C1 Alternative Support (Off-field Support)

Before C2, Silken Moon has shorter buff uptime. Use Instructor for team EM or Scroll for Geo/Hydro DMG bonuses.

  • Weapons: Favonius Lance, Dragon's Bane, Kitain Cross Spear
  • Artifacts: Instructor (4 pcs); Scroll of the Hero of Cinder City (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Reach ER needs first, then stack EM. Build CRIT Rate only for Favonius.
  • Teams: Lunar-Crystallize enabler such as Zibai, Linnea, or Columbina.
  • When it applies: Set buffs depend on reaction triggers and team composition.
  • Notes: Avoid duplicating an artifact set already supplied by a teammate.

Traditional Geo DPS Support (Off-field Support)

Support Noelle, Itto, or Navia without relying on direct Lunar-Crystallize damage. His buffs are weaker outside Lunar teams.

  • Weapons: Favonius Lance, Dragon's Bane, Kitain Cross Spear
  • Artifacts: Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs); The Exile (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Prioritize Burst uptime and EM; Favonius needs some CRIT Rate.
  • Teams: An ATK-scaling Geo carry for Noblesse, or energy-hungry allies for Exile.
  • When it applies: Do not duplicate Noblesse on another teammate.
  • Notes: Noblesse is less valuable for DEF-scaling carries; Exile helps energy.

Low-Energy Accessible Build (Off-field Support)

For limited weapon rosters or rotations where Illuga cannot Burst reliably. Kitain is the accessible default.

  • Weapons: Kitain Cross Spear, Favonius Lance, Prototype Starglitter
  • Artifacts: Silken Moon's Serenade (4 pcs); The Exile (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Use an ER Sands if needed. Swap to EM Sands only after reliable Burst uptime.
  • Teams: Geo teammates or Favonius users help lower his energy requirements.
  • When it applies: Kitain refinements improve energy refund; ER depends on team and C1.
  • Notes: Do not prioritize raw ATK or personal CRIT damage over Burst uptime.

Illuga talents

Normal Attack: Oathkeeper's Spear

Normal Attack
Performs up to 4 consecutive spear strikes.

Charged Attack
Consumes a certain amount of Stamina to lunge forward, dealing damage to opponents along the way.

Plunging Attack
Plunges from mid-air to strike the ground below, damaging opponents along the path and dealing AoE DMG upon impact.

Elemental Skill: Dawnbearing Songbird

Cooldown: 15s

Summons the trusty messenger bird, "Aedon." Different effects are produced depending on whether the skill is Tapped or Held.

Tap
Aedon dashes towards enemies in front, dealing Geo DMG to enemies it comes into contact with.

Hold
Enter aiming mode, and command Aedon to dash towards opponents being aimed at, dealing Geo DMG to opponents it comes into contact with.

Elemental Burst: Shadowless Reflection

Cooldown: 15s · Energy cost: 60

Light the lamp, dealing AoE Geo DMG, and gain Haunted Night's Oriole-Song effect for 20s. When this effect is active, Illuga gains 21 stacks of Nightingale's Song. When Normal Attacks, Charged Attacks, Plunging Attacks, Elemental Skills, and Elemental Bursts of nearby active party members deal Geo DMG to opponents, 1 stack of Nightingale's Song is consumed to increase the DMG dealt. Increase in DMG is based on Illuga's Elemental Mastery. If DMG is inflicted by Lunar-Crystallize, DMG dealt will increase further.
When Geo DMG dealt in this manner hits multiple opponents, multiple stacks of Nightingale's Song will be consumed, depending on how many opponents are hit.

Additionally, if there are Geo Constructs created by nearby party members on the battlefield while Illuga unleashes his Elemental Burst, or when nearby party members create Geo Constructs while Haunted Night's Oriole-Song is active, every Geo Construct on the battlefield will allow Illuga to gain 5 additional stacks of Nightingale's Song. Within 20s after unleashing his Elemental Burst, Illuga can gain a maximum of 15 additional stacks of Nightingale's Song this way.

When all stacks of Nightingale's Song have been consumed, or when the state's duration ends, the Haunted Night's Oriole-Song effect will end.

Passive talent: Torchforger's Covenant

After unleashing Elemental Skill Dawnbearing Songbird or Elemental Burst Shadowless Reflection, other nearby party members will gain the Lightkeeper's Oath effect for 20s: For Geo DMG dealt to opponents, CRIT Rate increases by 5% and CRIT DMG increases by 10%.

Moonsign: Ascendant Gleam
Party members affected by the Lightkeeper's Oath effect have their Elemental Mastery increased by 50.

Passive talent: Demonhunter's Dusk

Nightingale's Song's effects are enhanced: When there are 1/2/3 Hydro or Geo characters in the party, Nightingale's Song will further increase DMG. Increase in DMG is equal to 7%/14%/24% of Illuga's Elemental Mastery. If this aforementioned DMG is inflicted by a Lunar-Crystallize reaction, increase in DMG is equal to 48%/96%/160% of Illuga's Elemental Mastery.

Passive talent: Moonsign Benediction: Unwithering in Winter

When Illuga is in the party, the party's Moonsign will increase by 1 level.

Passive talent: Night Warden's Stride

At night (18:00 – 06:00), your party members gain the Swift Stride effect: Movement SPD increased by 10%.
Does not take effect in Domains, Trounce Domains, or Spiral Abyss. Swift Stride does not stack.
Additionally, Illuga appears to be able to decipher the whispers of the Wild Hunt...

Illuga constellations

C1: Vigilant Sentinel

After Illuga triggers a Geo-related Reaction on the field, he will regain 12 Elemental Energy. This effect can be triggered at most once every 15s.

C2: Elk With Fanged Antlers

When Elemental Burst Shadowless Reflection's Haunted Night's Oriole-Song effect is active, for every 7 stacks of Nightingale's Song consumed, Illuga will summon Aedon, dealing a single instance of Geo DMG to 1 nearby opponent. The DMG dealt is based on 400% of Illuga's Elemental Mastery and 200% of his DEF. This DMG is considered Elemental Burst DMG.

C3: Earthshaking Maw

Increases the Level of Elemental Burst Shadowless Reflection by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C4: Solarhunting Wolf

When Elemental Burst Shadowless Reflection's Haunted Night's Oriole-Song effect is active, nearby active party members will have their DEF increased by 200.

C5: Hurricane Steed

Increases the Level of Elemental Skill Dawnbearing Songbird by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C6: Nightmare Orioles

Enhances the Lightkeeper's Oath effect of the Ascension Talent Torchforger's Covenant: For Geo DMG dealt to opponents, CRIT Rate is increased by 10%, CRIT DMG is increased by 30%.

Moonsign: Ascendant Gleam:
When characters are affected by Lightkeeper's Oath, Elemental Mastery is increased by 80.
You must first unlock the Ascension Talent "Torchforger's Covenant."

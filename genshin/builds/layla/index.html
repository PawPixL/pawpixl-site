Layla Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams (7.2)

Layla build guide for Genshin Impact 7.2: best weapons (Key of Khaj-Nisut, Primordial Jade Cutter), artifacts (Tenacity of the Millelith), main stats.

Last updated: November 12, 2026 · Game version 7.2

Role: Shielder / Support

Layla is an HP-scaling Cryo shielder providing strong protection, off-field Cryo, and Tenacity buffs. Her shield needs no Energy, while her Burst improves Cryo application but can be skipped if ER is too costly.

Best weapons for Layla

  1. Key of Khaj-Nisut: Massive HP% strengthens her shield.
  2. Primordial Jade Cutter: HP bonus and CRIT for hybrid builds. Hybrid shield and damage.
  3. Freedom-Sworn: Team buffs if she triggers reactions. Reliable reaction triggers needed.
  4. The Dockhand's Assistant: HP% greatly boosts shield strength.
  5. Favonius Sword: Generates team Energy on CRIT hits. Build some CRIT Rate.
  6. Sacrificial Sword: ER and a situational Skill reset. Shield refresh or extra ER.
  7. Harbinger of Dawn: Cheap CRIT option for personal damage. Off-field damage, HP above 90%.
  8. Skyrider Sword: Accessible ER when Burst is needed. No better ER sword available.

Best artifacts for Layla

  • Tenacity of the Millelith (4 pcs): Best shields and frequent ATK buff. Main stats: HP% · HP% · HP%.
  • Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs): Party ATK buff after Burst. Main stats: HP%/ER · HP% · HP%.
  • Tenacity of the Millelith + Vourukasha's Glow (2 + 2 pcs): Easy HP-focused shield fallback. Main stats: HP% · HP% · HP%.

Main stats

  • Sands: HP% / Energy Recharge
  • Goblet: HP%
  • Circlet: HP% / Crit Rate (Favonius)

Stat targets

  • HP: 35k - 45k
  • ER: Team dependent
  • CRIT: Favonius only

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 9+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 8-9+

Best teams for Layla

  • Frostbound Court: Layla (Shielder / Cryo Enabler), Kamisato Ayaka (On-field DPS), Mona (Buffer / Hydro Enabler), Kaedehara Kazuha (Buffer / Debuffer). Ayaka gets Cryo Resonance and a secure Freeze setup; Mona amplifies her Burst, while Kazuha groups and shreds Cryo resistance.
  • Dreamguard: Layla (Shielder / Cryo Enabler), Wanderer (On-field DPS), Faruzan (Anemo Buffer / Debuffer), Bennett (Healer / Buffer). Layla protects Wanderer from interruption; Faruzan and Bennett amplify his damage. Cryo absorption can grant extra CRIT Rate.
  • Tidal Starlight: Layla (Shielder / Cryo Enabler), Neuvillette (On-field DPS), Fischl (Off-field DPS / Battery), Kaedehara Kazuha (Buffer / Debuffer). Layla shields C0 Neuvillette against interruption and enables Freeze; Fischl supplies off-field damage and Kazuha shreds Hydro RES.
  • Starlit Arrows: Layla (Shielder / Cryo Enabler), Yoimiya (On-field DPS), Yelan (Off-field DPS / Buffer), Bennett (Healer / Buffer). Layla protects Yoimiya’s attack string, while Yelan enables Vaporize and Bennett provides ATK and healing. Melt is situational.

Pros

  • Strong HP shield
  • Off-field Cryo
  • Tenacity buffs
  • Low field time

Cons

  • No healing
  • 12s shield duration
  • Shield loss stops particles
  • Limited personal damage

Build variations

Off-field Cryo Damage (Off-field DPS)

For invested Layla builds, especially C6, when additional off-field Cryo damage is worth some shield strength.

  • Weapons: Primordial Jade Cutter, Key of Khaj-Nisut, Harbinger of Dawn
  • Artifacts: Tenacity of the Millelith (4 pcs); Blizzard Strayer (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Use a Cryo DMG goblet and a CRIT circlet, with enough ER to Burst and enough HP to keep the shield intact.
  • Teams: Best with Cryo damage buffs or Freeze support; Shenhe can boost her personal damage.
  • When it applies: C6 is preferred, not required. Blizzard Strayer is Freeze-dependent.
  • Notes: Tenacity usually remains better for total team support.

Shield Only (Low ER) (Shielder)

Use when she is solo Cryo, Burst costs too much ER, or her shielding is the only priority.

  • Weapons: Key of Khaj-Nisut, The Dockhand's Assistant, Favonius Sword
  • Artifacts: Tenacity of the Millelith (4 pcs); Tenacity of the Millelith + Vourukasha's Glow (2 + 2 pcs)
  • Stats: Stack HP% on sands, goblet, and circlet; do not chase Burst ER.
  • Teams: Teams mainly need her shield rather than extra Cryo application from Burst.
  • When it applies: Without Burst, Cryo application and Shooting Star generation are reduced.
  • Notes: Cast Skill shortly before your on-field DPS to maximize its 12s protection.

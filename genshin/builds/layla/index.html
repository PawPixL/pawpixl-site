Layla Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

Layla build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Key of Khaj-Nisut, Primordial Jade Cutter), artifacts (Tenacity of the Millelith), main stats.

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Role: Shielder / Support

Layla is an HP-scaling Cryo shielder providing strong protection, off-field Cryo, and Tenacity buffs. Her shield needs no Energy, while her Burst improves Cryo application but can be skipped if ER is too costly.

Best weapons for Layla

  1. Key of Khaj-Nisut: Massive HP% strengthens her shield.
  2. Primordial Jade Cutter: HP bonus and CRIT for hybrid builds. Hybrid shield and damage.
  3. Freedom-Sworn: Team buffs if she triggers reactions. Reliable reaction triggers needed.
  4. The Dockhand's Assistant: HP% greatly boosts shield strength.
  5. Favonius Sword: Generates team Energy on CRIT hits. Build some CRIT Rate.
  6. Sacrificial Sword: ER and a situational Skill reset. Shield refresh or extra ER.
  7. Harbinger of Dawn: Cheap CRIT option for personal damage. Off-field damage, HP above 90%.
  8. Skyrider Sword: Accessible ER when Burst is needed. No better ER sword available.

Best artifacts for Layla

  • Tenacity of the Millelith (4 pcs): Best shields and frequent ATK buff. Main stats: HP% · HP% · HP%.
  • Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs): Party ATK buff after Burst. Main stats: HP%/ER · HP% · HP%.
  • Tenacity of the Millelith + Vourukasha's Glow (2 + 2 pcs): Easy HP-focused shield fallback. Main stats: HP% · HP% · HP%.

Main stats

  • Sands: HP% / Energy Recharge
  • Goblet: HP%
  • Circlet: HP% / Crit Rate (Favonius)

Stat targets

  • HP: 35k - 45k
  • ER: Team dependent
  • CRIT: Favonius only

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 9+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 8-9+

Best teams for Layla

  • Frostbound Court: Layla, Kamisato Ayaka, Mona, Kaedehara Kazuha. Ayaka gets Cryo Resonance and a secure Freeze setup; Mona amplifies her Burst, while Kazuha groups and shreds Cryo resistance.
  • Dreamguard: Layla, Wanderer, Faruzan, Bennett. Layla protects Wanderer from interruption; Faruzan and Bennett amplify his damage. Cryo absorption can grant extra CRIT Rate.
  • Tidal Starlight: Layla, Neuvillette, Fischl, Kaedehara Kazuha. Layla shields C0 Neuvillette against interruption and enables Freeze; Fischl supplies off-field damage and Kazuha shreds Hydro RES.
  • Starlit Arrows: Layla, Yoimiya, Yelan, Bennett. Layla protects Yoimiya’s attack string, while Yelan enables Vaporize and Bennett provides ATK and healing. Melt is situational.

Pros

  • Strong HP shield
  • Off-field Cryo
  • Tenacity buffs
  • Low field time

Cons

  • No healing
  • 12s shield duration
  • Shield loss stops particles
  • Limited personal damage

Build variations

Off-field Cryo Damage (Off-field DPS)

For invested Layla builds, especially C6, when additional off-field Cryo damage is worth some shield strength.

  • Weapons: Primordial Jade Cutter, Key of Khaj-Nisut, Harbinger of Dawn
  • Artifacts: Tenacity of the Millelith (4 pcs); Blizzard Strayer (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Use a Cryo DMG goblet and a CRIT circlet, with enough ER to Burst and enough HP to keep the shield intact.
  • Teams: Best with Cryo damage buffs or Freeze support; Shenhe can boost her personal damage.
  • When it applies: C6 is preferred, not required. Blizzard Strayer is Freeze-dependent.
  • Notes: Tenacity usually remains better for total team support.

Shield Only (Low ER) (Shielder)

Use when she is solo Cryo, Burst costs too much ER, or her shielding is the only priority.

  • Weapons: Key of Khaj-Nisut, The Dockhand's Assistant, Favonius Sword
  • Artifacts: Tenacity of the Millelith (4 pcs); Tenacity of the Millelith + Vourukasha's Glow (2 + 2 pcs)
  • Stats: Stack HP% on sands, goblet, and circlet; do not chase Burst ER.
  • Teams: Teams mainly need her shield rather than extra Cryo application from Burst.
  • When it applies: Without Burst, Cryo application and Shooting Star generation are reduced.
  • Notes: Cast Skill shortly before your on-field DPS to maximize its 12s protection.

Layla talents

Normal Attack: Sword of the Radiant Path

Normal Attack
Performs up to 3 rapid strikes.

Charged Attack
Consumes a certain amount of Stamina to unleash 2 rapid sword strikes.

Plunging Attack
Plunges from mid-air to strike the ground below, damaging opponents along the path and dealing AoE DMG upon impact.

Elemental Skill: Nights of Formal Focus

Cooldown: 12s

Puts forth a shield known as the Curtain of Slumber, dealing AoE Cryo DMG.
The Curtain of Slumber's DMG Absorption is based on Layla's Max HP and absorbs Cryo DMG with 250% effectiveness. When the shield is deployed, Layla will have Cryo applied to her briefly.

Night Stars and Shooting Stars
While the Curtain of Slumber is active, it will create 1 Night Star that will be attached to it every 1.5s. When a character protected by this shield uses an Elemental Skill, 2 Night Stars will be created. Night Stars can be created once every 0.3s in this way. A maximum of 4 Night Stars can be accumulated at any one time.
Once the Curtain of Slumber has accumulated 4 Night Stars and there are opponents nearby, these Night Stars will transform into homing Shooting Stars that will be fired off in sequence, dealing Cryo DMG to any opponents hit.
If the Curtain of Slumber's duration ends or it is destroyed, the Night Stars will disappear. If they are already being fired off as Shooting Stars, these Shooting Stars will last until this wave of shots ends.

New Night Stars cannot be created until the previous wave of Shooting Stars has been fired completely.

Elemental Burst: Dream of the Star-Stream Shaker

Cooldown: 12s · Energy cost: 40

Unleashes a Celestial Dreamsphere that constantly fires Starlight Slugs at opponents within its AoE, dealing Cryo DMG.
When a Starlight Slug hits, it will generate 1 Night Star for nearby Curtains of Slumber. Each Curtain of Slumber can gain 1 Night Star this way every 0.5s.

Passive talent: Like Nascent Light

While the Curtain of Slumber is active, the Deep Sleep effect will activate each time the Curtain gains 1 Night Star:
·The Shield Strength of a character under the effect of the Curtain of Slumber increases by 6%.
·This effect can have a maximum of 4 stacks and persists until the Curtain of Slumber disappears.

Passive talent: Sweet Slumber Undisturbed

The DMG dealt by the Shooting Stars fired by Nights of Formal Focus is increased by 1.5% of Layla's Max HP.

Passive talent: Shadowy Dream-Signs

When Layla crafts Character Talent Materials, she has a 10% chance to receive double the product.

Layla constellations

C1: Fortress of Fantasy

The Shield Absorption of the Curtain of Slumber generated by Nights of Formal Focus is increased by 20%.
Additionally, when unleashing Nights of Formal Focus, she will generate a shield for any nearby party members who are not being protected by a Curtain of Slumber. This shield will have 35% of the absorption of a Curtain of Slumber, will last for 12s, and will absorb Cryo DMG with 250% effectiveness.

C2: Light's Remit

When Shooting Stars from Nights of Formal Focus strike opponents, they will each restore 1 Energy to Layla. Each Shooting Star can restore Energy to her in this manner once.

C3: Secrets of the Night

Increases the Level of Nights of Formal Focus by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C4: Starry Illumination

When Nights of Formal Focus starts to fire off Shooting Stars, it will grant all nearby party members the Dawn Star effect, causing their Normal and Charged Attack DMG to increase based on 5% of Layla's Max HP.
Dawn Star can last up to 3s and will be removed 0.05s after dealing Normal or Charged Attack DMG.

C5: Stream of Consciousness

Increases the Level of Dream of the Star-Stream Shaker by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C6: Radiant Soulfire

Shooting Stars from Nights of Formal Focus deal 40% increased DMG, and Starlight Slugs from Dream of the Star-Stream Shaker deal 40% increased DMG.
Additionally, the interval between the creation of Night Stars via Nights of Formal Focus is decreased by 20%.

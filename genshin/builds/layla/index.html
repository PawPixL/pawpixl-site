Layla Build Guide | Best Weapons, Artifacts & Teams

Layla build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Key of Khaj-Nisut, Primordial Jade Cutter), artifacts (Tenacity of the Millelith), main stats (HP% /…

Role: Shielder / Support

Layla is an HP-scaling Cryo shielder providing strong protection, off-field Cryo, and Tenacity buffs. Her shield needs no Energy, while her Burst improves Cryo application but can be skipped if ER is too costly.

Best weapons for Layla

  1. Key of Khaj-Nisut
  2. Primordial Jade Cutter
  3. Freedom-Sworn
  4. The Dockhand's Assistant
  5. Favonius Sword
  6. Sacrificial Sword
  7. Harbinger of Dawn
  8. Skyrider Sword

Best artifacts

  • Tenacity of the Millelith (4 pcs)
  • Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs)
  • Tenacity of the Millelith + Vourukasha's Glow (2 + 2 pcs)

Main stats

  • Sands: HP% / Energy Recharge
  • Goblet: HP%
  • Circlet: HP% / Crit Rate (Favonius)

Stat targets

  • HP: 35k - 45k
  • ER: Team dependent
  • CRIT: Favonius only

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 9+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 8-9+

Best teams

  • Frostbound Court: Layla, Kamisato Ayaka, Mona, Kaedehara Kazuha
  • Dreamguard: Layla, Wanderer, Faruzan, Bennett
  • Tidal Starlight: Layla, Neuvillette, Fischl, Kaedehara Kazuha
  • Starlit Arrows: Layla, Yoimiya, Yelan, Bennett

Pros

  • Strong HP shield
  • Off-field Cryo
  • Tenacity buffs
  • Low field time

Cons

  • No healing
  • 12s shield duration
  • Shield loss stops particles
  • Limited personal damage

Build variations

  • Off-field Cryo Damage (Off-field DPS): For invested Layla builds, especially C6, when additional off-field Cryo damage is worth some shield strength.
  • Shield Only (Low ER) (Shielder): Use when she is solo Cryo, Burst costs too much ER, or her shielding is the only priority.

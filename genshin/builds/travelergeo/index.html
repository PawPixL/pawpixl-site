Traveler (Geo) Build Guide | Weapons, Artifacts & Teams

Traveler (Geo) build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Exaiphanes Blade, Primordial Jade Cutter), artifacts (Archaic Petra + Noblesse Oblige), main…

Role: Sub DPS / Support

Geo Traveler is a quickswap Geo damage dealer and battery with strong Skill and Burst hits. Their Burst grants nearby allies CRIT Rate at C1, while Geo constructs offer utility and synergy with Zhongli. Positioning matters: boulders can obstruct movement.

Best weapons for Traveler (Geo)

  1. Exaiphanes Blade
  2. Primordial Jade Cutter
  3. Haran Geppaku Futsu
  4. Mistsplitter Reforged
  5. Wolf-Fang
  6. Favonius Sword
  7. Amenoma Kageuchi
  8. Festering Desire

Best artifacts

  • Archaic Petra + Noblesse Oblige (2 + 2 pcs)
  • Emblem of Severed Fate (4 pcs)
  • Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs)

Main stats

  • Sands: ATK% / Energy Recharge
  • Goblet: Geo DMG Bonus
  • Circlet: Crit Rate / Crit DMG

Stat targets

  • CRIT Rate: 60-75%
  • CRIT DMG: 120-180%
  • ATK: 1,500-2,000
  • ER: 120-150%

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 9+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 9+

Best teams

  • Meteorite Resonance: Traveler (Geo), Zhongli, Albedo, Bennett
  • Oni Support Squad: Traveler (Geo), Arataki Itto, Gorou, Zhongli
  • Geo Powerhouse: Traveler (Geo), Noelle, Gorou, Furina
  • Budget Rockslide: Traveler (Geo), Ningguang, Bennett, Xiangling

Pros

  • Strong quickswap damage
  • Geo particle generation
  • C1 team CRIT buff
  • Flexible support builds

Cons

  • Constructs obstruct allies
  • Requires careful aim
  • Low base stats
  • Best with Geo resonance

Build variations

  • Geo Battery Support (Support): Choose when team Energy and buff uptime matter more than Traveler personal damage. Pick up matching Crystallize shards for Petra buff.
  • Quickswap Geo Damage (Off-field DPS): Swap in for Starfell Sword and Wake of Earth, then return to the main damage dealer.

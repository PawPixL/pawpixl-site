Traveler (Geo) Build Guide: Weapons, Artifacts and Teams (7.2)

Traveler (Geo) build guide for Genshin Impact 7.2: best weapons (Exaiphanes Blade, Primordial Jade Cutter), artifacts (Archaic Petra + Noblesse Oblige).

Last updated: November 12, 2026 · Game version 7.2

Role: Sub DPS / Support

Geo Traveler is a quickswap Geo damage dealer and battery with strong Skill and Burst hits. Their Burst grants nearby allies CRIT Rate at C1, while Geo constructs offer utility and synergy with Zhongli. Positioning matters: boulders can obstruct movement.

Best weapons for Traveler (Geo)

  1. Exaiphanes Blade: Exaiphanes Blade is excellent for Geo Traveler because it provides CRIT Rate, ATK buffs, and Energy regeneration,all of which improve their Skill and Burst damage.
  2. Primordial Jade Cutter: Provides a huge CRIT Rate stat stick and converts HP to ATK.
  3. Haran Geppaku Futsu: Provides CRIT Rate and increases Normal Attack damage through its Wavespike passive.
  4. Mistsplitter Reforged: Gives CRIT DMG and up to 12% Elemental DMG Bonus across its stacks.
  5. Wolf-Fang: Battle Pass sword giving CRIT Rate and boosting both Skill and Burst damage.
  6. Favonius Sword: Energy battery for the team; low base ATK but high ER, best for support roles rather than raw damage.
  7. Amenoma Kageuchi: ATK% sword that restores energy after Burst, letting you spam Burst without heavy ER investment.
  8. Festering Desire: Event sword that boosts Skill damage and CRIT Rate on Skill hit.

Best artifacts for Traveler (Geo)

  • Archaic Petra + Noblesse Oblige (2 + 2 pcs): Balanced Geo and Burst damage. Main stats: ATK% · Geo DMG · CRIT.
  • Emblem of Severed Fate (4 pcs): Converts ER into Burst damage. Main stats: ATK%/ER · Geo DMG · CRIT.
  • Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs): Team ATK buff after Burst. Main stats: ER/ATK% · Geo DMG · CRIT.

Best teams for Traveler (Geo)

  • Meteorite Resonance: Traveler (Geo) (Geo Battery / Sub DPS), Zhongli (Shielder / Debuffer), Albedo (Off-field DPS), Bennett (Healer / Buffer). Zhongli resonates with Traveler constructs, Albedo adds Geo damage, and Bennett boosts attacks.
  • Oni Support Squad: Traveler (Geo) (Geo Battery / Sub DPS), Arataki Itto (On-field DPS), Gorou (DEF Buffer), Zhongli (Shielder / Debuffer). Traveler supplies Geo particles and C1 CRIT Rate; Gorou buffs Itto while Zhongli shields.
  • Geo Powerhouse: Traveler (Geo) (Geo Battery / Sub DPS), Noelle (On-field DPS / Healer), Gorou (DEF Buffer), Furina (Off-field DPS / Buffer). Traveler batteries Noelle and grants CRIT Rate; Gorou buffs DEF while Furina raises damage.
  • Budget Rockslide: Traveler (Geo) (Geo Battery / Sub DPS), Ningguang (On-field DPS), Bennett (Healer / Buffer), Xiangling (Off-field DPS / Pyro Enabler). Geo resonance boosts Geo damage while Bennett and Xiangling add Pyro pressure.

Build variations

Geo Battery Support (Support)

Choose when team Energy and buff uptime matter more than Traveler personal damage. Pick up matching Crystallize shards for Petra buff.

  • Weapons: Favonius Sword, Amenoma Kageuchi
  • Artifacts: Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs); Archaic Petra (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Prioritize ER and Favonius CRIT Rate. ER Sands may be needed when funneling particles to a Geo carry.
  • Teams: Best alongside an Energy-hungry Geo damage dealer; use Burst for C1 CRIT Rate.
  • When it applies: C1 required for Burst CRIT Rate buff.
  • Notes: Archaic Petra buffs the element of the shard picked up, not Geo damage itself.

Quickswap Geo Damage (Off-field DPS)

Swap in for Starfell Sword and Wake of Earth, then return to the main damage dealer.

  • Weapons: Primordial Jade Cutter, Haran Geppaku Futsu, Mistsplitter Reforged, Wolf-Fang
  • Artifacts: Archaic Petra + Noblesse Oblige (2 + 2 pcs); Emblem of Severed Fate (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ATK% Sands, Geo DMG Goblet and CRIT Circlet. Seek enough ER to Burst on rotation.
  • Teams: Another Geo ally is recommended for resonance and particle support.
  • When it applies: Avoid placing constructs where they obstruct allies.
  • Notes: This is the preferred personal-damage playstyle.

Traveler (Geo) talents

Normal Attack: Foreign Rockblade

Normal Attack
Performs up to 5 rapid strikes.

Charged Attack
Consumes a certain amount of Stamina to unleash 2 rapid sword strikes.

Plunging Attack
Plunges from mid-air to strike the ground below, damaging opponents along the path and dealing AoE DMG upon impact.

Elemental Skill: Starfell Sword

Cooldown: 8s

You disgorge a meteorite from the depths of the earth, dealing AoE Geo DMG.
The meteorite is considered a Geo Construct, and can be climbed or used to block attacks.

Hold
This skill's positioning may be adjusted.

Elemental Burst: Wake of Earth

Cooldown: 15s · Energy cost: 60

Energizing the Geo deep underground, you set off expanding shockwaves.
Launches surrounding opponents back and deals AoE Geo DMG.
A stone wall is erected at the edges of the shockwave.
The stone wall is considered a Geo Construct, and may be used to block attacks.

Passive talent: Shattered Darkrock

Reduces Starfell Sword's CD by 2s.

Passive talent: Frenzied Rockslide

The final hit of a Normal Attack combo triggers a collapse, dealing 60% of ATK as AoE Geo DMG.

Traveler (Geo) constellations

C1: Invincible Stonewall

Party members within the radius of Wake of Earth have their CRIT Rate increased by 10% and have increased resistance against interruption.

C2: Rockcore Meltdown

When the meteorite created by Starfell Sword is destroyed, it will also explode, dealing additional AoE Geo DMG equal to the amount of damage dealt by Starfell Sword.

C3: Will of the Rock

Increases the Level of Wake of Earth by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C4: Reaction Force

The shockwave triggered by Wake of Earth regenerates 5 Energy for every opponent hit.
A maximum of 25 Energy can be regenerated in this manner at any one time.

C5: Meteorite Impact

Increases the Level of Starfell Sword by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C6: Everlasting Boulder

The barrier created by Wake of Earth lasts 5s longer.
The meteorite created by Starfell Sword lasts 10s longer.

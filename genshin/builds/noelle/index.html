Noelle Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

Noelle build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Redhorn Stonethresher, Gest of the Mighty Wolf), artifacts (Husk of Opulent Dreams), main stats.

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Role: Main DPS & Support

Noelle combines strong Geo AoE damage with shielding and healing. At C6, she becomes a powerful DEF-scaling Main DPS with excellent survivability.

Best weapons for Noelle

  1. Redhorn Stonethresher: Best personal DPS weapon: huge CRIT DMG, DEF and DEF-based Normal/Charged Attack damage.
  2. Gest of the Mighty Wolf: CRIT Rate and strong offensive bonuses. Particularly effective in qualifying Hexerei teams; less specialized outside them.
  3. A Thousand Blazing Suns: High Base ATK, CRIT Rate and a Skill/Burst-triggered ATK and CRIT DMG buff; less DEF synergy than Redhorn. ATK passive is less useful than DEF scaling.
  4. Serpent Spine: Serpent Spine is an excellent 4-star alternative, offering valuable CRIT Rate and stacking DMG bonuses that significantly boost Noelle's overall damage.
  5. Skyward Pride: Skyward Pride provides valuable Energy Recharge for Burst uptime, along with increased damage and additional vacuum blade damage after using her Burst.
  6. Favonius Greatsword: Favonius Greatsword provides Energy Recharge and generates extra particles through CRIT hits, helping Noelle and her teammates maintain consistent Burst uptime.
  7. Whiteblind: Craftable DEF% claymore; Normal/Charged hits grant extra DEF and ATK.
  8. Sacrificial Greatsword: Energy Recharge and a chance to reset Skill cooldown. Better for extra casts than personal damage.

Best artifacts for Noelle

  • Husk of Opulent Dreams (4 pcs) · Best Overall Set: 4pc Husk of Opulent Dreams is Noelle's best overall artifact set, providing DEF% and Geo DMG bonuses that directly enhance her C6 damage, shielding, and healing. Main stats: DEF% · Geo DMG · CR/CD.
  • Marechaussee Hunter (4 pcs) · Main DPS with Furina: 4pc Marechaussee Hunter is a strong alternative when paired with Furina, providing up to 36% CRIT Rate through HP fluctuations. This allows Noelle to prioritize CRIT DMG while maintaining excellent damage output. Main stats: DEF% · Geo DMG · CR/CD.
  • Archaic Petra (4 pcs) · Support / Healer: 4pc Archaic Petra makes Noelle a team support option, granting a 35% Elemental DMG Bonus to the element corresponding to the Crystallize shard she collects, benefiting elemental-focused teammates. Main stats: DEF%/ER · DEF% · DEF%/Healing Bonus.

Best teams for Noelle

  • Geo Moonrise: Noelle (On-field DPS), Columbina (Off-field DPS / Buffer), Ineffa (Off-field DPS / Shielder), Linnea (Off-field DPS / Buffer). Noelle leads with Geo damage and healing. Columbina supports Lunar reactions, Ineffa adds off-field Electro damage, and Linnea provides Geo support.
  • Hopeless Impact: Noelle (On-field DPS), Columbina (Off-field DPS / Buffer), Illuga (Geo Buffer), Linnea (Off-field DPS / Buffer). Noelle leads as the on-field DPS, while Columbina enables Lunar-Crystallize reactions. Linnea provides Geo RES shred and off-field damage, while Illuga boosts Noelle's Geo damage and CRIT potential for stronger overall output.
  • Heavenly Deluge: Noelle (On-field DPS), Columbina (Off-field DPS / Buffer), Furina (Off-field DPS / Buffer), Linnea (Off-field DPS / Buffer). Noelle drives the team with Geo damage and healing, while Furina buffs overall damage, Columbina boosts Lunar reactions, and Linnea provides Geo RES shred and off-field damage.
  • Big Boom: Noelle (On-field DPS), Nicole (Shielder / Buffer), Durin (Off-field DPS / Buffer), Xilonen (Healer / Debuffer). Noelle leads with Geo damage while Durin provides off-field Pyro damage, Nicole boosts team performance through Hexerei synergy, and Xilonen adds buffs and resistance shred.

Cons

Build variations

On-field Geo (Main DPS)

Focus on Burst-infused attacks and team healing.

  • Weapons: Whiteblind, Serpent Spine
  • Artifacts: Husk of Opulent Dreams (4 pcs)
  • Stats: DEF% / Geo DMG / CRIT; manage ER.
  • Teams: Geo battery and off-field damage allies.

Furina Driver (Main DPS)

Use Noelle’s healing and attacks to enable Furina’s Fanfare.

  • Weapons: Whiteblind, Serpent Spine
  • Artifacts: Marechaussee Hunter (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Build around artifact CRIT Rate; retain DEF.
  • Teams: Furina and enough ER for Noelle Burst.

Noelle talents

Normal Attack: Favonius Bladework - Maid

Normal Attack
Performs up to 4 consecutive strikes.

Charged Attack
Drains Stamina over time to perform continuous spinning attacks against all nearby opponents.
At the end of the sequence, performs a more powerful slash.

Plunging Attack
Plunges from mid-air to strike the ground below, damaging opponents along the path and dealing AoE DMG upon impact.

Elemental Skill: Breastplate

Cooldown: 24s

Summons protective stone armor, dealing Geo DMG to surrounding opponents and creating a shield. The shield's DMG Absorption scales based on Noelle's DEF.
The shield has the following properties:
·When Noelle's Normal and Charged Attacks hit a target, they have a certain chance to regenerate HP for all characters.
·Possesses 150% DMG Absorption efficiency against all Elemental and Physical DMG.

The amount of HP healed when regeneration is triggered scales based on Noelle's DEF.

Elemental Burst: Sweeping Time

Cooldown: 15s · Energy cost: 60

Gathering the strength of stone around her weapon, Noelle strikes the opponents surrounding her within a large AoE, dealing Geo DMG.
Afterwards, Noelle gains the following effects:
·Larger attack AoE.
·Converts attack DMG to Geo DMG that cannot be overridden by any other elemental infusion.
·Increased ATK that scales based on her DEF.

Passive talent: Devotion

When Noelle is in the party but not on the field, this ability triggers automatically when your active character's HP falls below 30%:
Creates a shield for your active character that lasts for 20s and absorbs DMG equal to 400% of Noelle's DEF.
The shield has a 150% DMG Absorption effectiveness against all Elemental and Physical DMG.
This effect can only occur once every 60s.

Passive talent: Nice and Clean

Noelle will decrease the CD of Breastplate by 1s for every 4 Normal or Charged Attack hits she scores on opponents.
One hit may be counted every 0.1s.

Passive talent: Maid's Knighthood

When a Perfect Cooking is achieved on a DEF-boosting dish, Noelle has a 12% chance to obtain double the product.

Noelle constellations

C1: I Got Your Back

While Sweeping Time and Breastplate are both in effect, the chance of Breastplate's healing effects activating is increased to 100%.

C2: Combat Maid

Decreases the Stamina Consumption of Noelle's Charged Attacks by 20% and increases her Charged Attack DMG by 15%.

C3: Invulnerable Maid

Increases the Level of Breastplate by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C4: To Be Cleaned

When Breastplate's duration expires or it is destroyed by DMG, it will deal 400% of Noelle's ATK as Geo DMG to surrounding opponents.

C5: Favonius Sweeper Master

Increases the Level of Sweeping Time by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C6: Must Be Spotless

Sweeping Time increases Noelle's ATK by an additional 50% of her DEF.
Additionally, every opponent defeated during the skill's duration adds 1s to the duration, up to 10s.

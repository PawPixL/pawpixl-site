Sandrone Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

Sandrone build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (A Teaspoon of Transcendence, A Thousand Blazing Suns), artifacts (Disenchantment in Deep Shadow).

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Role: Main DPS

Sandrone is an on-field DPS specializing in consistent damage and Stellar Conduct reactions, rewarding teams built around her unique mechanics.

Best weapons for Sandrone

  1. A Teaspoon of Transcendence: Provides 44.1% CRIT DMG and 28% ATK, plus up to 48% Stellar-Conduct DMG at R1 through Charged Attack stacks. Sandrone’s signature and best-in-slot weapon.
  2. A Thousand Blazing Suns: CRIT Rate plus ATK and CRIT DMG after Skill or Burst. Powerful general offensive option.
  3. Redhorn Stonethresher: High CRIT DMG and DEF; Normal/Charged Attack bonus rarely helps non-DEF carries.
  4. Verdict: CRIT Rate and ATK with Skill damage bonuses tied to collecting Crystallize shards.
  5. Mailed Flower: Event EM claymore that adds ATK and EM after a reaction or Skill hit; useful for reaction damage.
  6. Tidal Shadow: Craftable ATK weapon with an additional ATK buff after healing; needs a healer.
  7. Blade of Atonement: Craftable ATK claymore with EM and ATK after reactions; best when Sandrone triggers Stellar-Conduct.
  8. Forged by the Golden Melody: Battle Pass CRIT Rate. Cycles ATK, EM and Stellar Glimmer buffs; a reaction can add a second active buff. Needs Electro aura.

Best artifacts for Sandrone

  • Disenchantment in Deep Shadow (4 pcs) · Main DPS: Provides 18% ATK and boosts Stellar-Conduct DMG by 40%, with 16% CRIT Rate against affected enemies. Sandrone’s best-in-slot artifact set for Stellar-Conduct teams. Main stats: ATK% · ATK% · CD/CR.
  • Gilded Dreams (4 pcs) · Main DPS: Provides 80 EM and, at 4-piece, grants up to 42% ATK and 150 EM based on team composition, making it a strong alternative for Sandrone’s Stellar-Conduct damage.
  • Gladiator's Finale + A Day Carved From Rising Winds (2 + 2 pcs) · Main DPS: Provides 36% ATK, making it a solid transitional set for boosting Sandrone’s personal damage.

Main stats

  • Sands: ATK%
  • Goblet: ATK%
  • Circlet: CRIT DMG / CRIT Rate

Stat targets

  • ATK: 2,400-2,600+
  • Crit Rate: 65% - 80%
  • Crit DMG: 170% - 200%+
  • Energy Recharge: 120% - 140%

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 9+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 1+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 9+

Best teams for Sandrone

  • Marionette Mayhem: Sandrone (Main DPS), Mitya (Sub DPS), Nicole (Support), Odette (Stellar). Sandrone drives Stellar-Conduct with teammates enabling Cryo/Electro reactions, off-field hits and buffs.
  • The Puppet Protocol: Sandrone (Main DPS), Yae Miko (Sub DPS), qiqi (Support), Odette (Stellar). Sandrone leads the damage while Yae Miko adds off-field Electro. Odette supports Stellar reactions and Qiqi provides healing.
  • Strings Attached: Sandrone (Main DPS), Yae Miko (Sub DPS), Escoffier (Support), Sucrose (EM Build). Sandrone drives Stellar-Conduct while her teammates support Cryo/Electro application and team damage.
  • Spare Parts Squad: Sandrone (Main DPS), Beidou (Off-field), qiqi (Support), Sucrose (EM Build). Sandrone leads Stellar-Conduct damage; teammates provide Electro/Cryo coverage, support and sustain.

Pros

  • Strong DMG
  • Excellent Reaction synergy
  • Consistent damage output
  • Scales well with investment

Cons

  • Team dependent damage
  • Limited flexibility
  • Wants premium supports
  • High investment ceiling

Build variations

Stellar-Conduct Hypercarry (On-field DPS)

Maximize damage from her on-field Stellar-Conduct attacks.

  • Weapons: A Teaspoon of Transcendence, A Thousand Blazing Suns, Verdict
  • Artifacts: Disenchantment in Deep Shadow (4 pcs)
  • Stats: CRIT and relevant scaling stats; meet rotation ER.
  • Teams: Electro enabler and Stellar-Conduct support, such as Mitya.
  • When it applies: Needs reliable Cryo and Electro reaction uptime.

Accessible Conduct Carry (On-field DPS)

Lower-cost weapon alternatives to her signature.

  • Weapons: Mailed Flower, Tidal Shadow
  • Artifacts: Disenchantment in Deep Shadow (4 pcs); Gladiator's Finale + A Day Carved From Rising Winds (2 + 2 pcs)
  • Stats: Maintain CRIT and applicable offensive stats.
  • Teams: A budget Stellar-Conduct team with stable application.
  • When it applies: Damage depends heavily on enabling supports.

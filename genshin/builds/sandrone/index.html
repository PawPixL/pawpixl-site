Sandrone Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

Sandrone build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (A Teaspoon of Transcendence, A Thousand Blazing Suns), artifacts (Disenchantment in Deep Shadow).

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Role: Main DPS

Sandrone is an on-field DPS specializing in consistent damage and Stellar Conduct reactions, rewarding teams built around her unique mechanics.

Best weapons for Sandrone

  1. A Teaspoon of Transcendence: Provides 44.1% CRIT DMG and 28% ATK, plus up to 48% Stellar-Conduct DMG at R1 through Charged Attack stacks. Sandrone’s signature and best-in-slot weapon.
  2. A Thousand Blazing Suns: CRIT Rate plus ATK and CRIT DMG after Skill or Burst. Powerful general offensive option.
  3. Redhorn Stonethresher: High CRIT DMG and DEF; Normal/Charged Attack bonus rarely helps non-DEF carries.
  4. Verdict: CRIT Rate and ATK with Skill damage bonuses tied to collecting Crystallize shards.
  5. Mailed Flower: Event EM claymore that adds ATK and EM after a reaction or Skill hit; useful for reaction damage.
  6. Tidal Shadow: Craftable ATK weapon with an additional ATK buff after healing; needs a healer.
  7. Blade of Atonement: Craftable ATK claymore with EM and ATK after reactions; best when Sandrone triggers Stellar-Conduct.
  8. Forged by the Golden Melody: Battle Pass CRIT Rate. Cycles ATK, EM and Stellar Glimmer buffs; a reaction can add a second active buff. Needs Electro aura.

Best artifacts for Sandrone

  • Disenchantment in Deep Shadow (4 pcs) · Main DPS: Provides 18% ATK and boosts Stellar-Conduct DMG by 40%, with 16% CRIT Rate against affected enemies. Sandrone’s best-in-slot artifact set for Stellar-Conduct teams. Main stats: ATK% · ATK% · CD/CR.
  • Gilded Dreams (4 pcs) · Main DPS: Provides 80 EM and, at 4-piece, grants up to 42% ATK and 150 EM based on team composition, making it a strong alternative for Sandrone’s Stellar-Conduct damage.
  • Gladiator's Finale + A Day Carved From Rising Winds (2 + 2 pcs) · Main DPS: Provides 36% ATK, making it a solid transitional set for boosting Sandrone’s personal damage.

Main stats

  • Sands: ATK%
  • Goblet: ATK%
  • Circlet: CRIT DMG / CRIT Rate

Stat targets

  • ATK: 2,400-2,600+
  • Crit Rate: 65% - 80%
  • Crit DMG: 170% - 200%+
  • Energy Recharge: 120% - 140%

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 9+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 1+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 9+

Best teams for Sandrone

  • Marionette Mayhem: Sandrone (Main DPS), Mitya (Sub DPS), Nicole (Support), Odette (Stellar). Sandrone drives Stellar-Conduct with teammates enabling Cryo/Electro reactions, off-field hits and buffs.
  • The Puppet Protocol: Sandrone (Main DPS), Yae Miko (Sub DPS), qiqi (Support), Odette (Stellar). Sandrone leads the damage while Yae Miko adds off-field Electro. Odette supports Stellar reactions and Qiqi provides healing.
  • Strings Attached: Sandrone (Main DPS), Yae Miko (Sub DPS), Escoffier (Support), Sucrose (EM Build). Sandrone drives Stellar-Conduct while her teammates support Cryo/Electro application and team damage.
  • Spare Parts Squad: Sandrone (Main DPS), Beidou (Off-field), qiqi (Support), Sucrose (EM Build). Sandrone leads Stellar-Conduct damage; teammates provide Electro/Cryo coverage, support and sustain.

Pros

  • Strong DMG
  • Excellent Reaction synergy
  • Consistent damage output
  • Scales well with investment

Cons

  • Team dependent damage
  • Limited flexibility
  • Wants premium supports
  • High investment ceiling

Build variations

Stellar-Conduct Hypercarry (On-field DPS)

Maximize damage from her on-field Stellar-Conduct attacks.

  • Weapons: A Teaspoon of Transcendence, A Thousand Blazing Suns, Verdict
  • Artifacts: Disenchantment in Deep Shadow (4 pcs)
  • Stats: CRIT and relevant scaling stats; meet rotation ER.
  • Teams: Electro enabler and Stellar-Conduct support, such as Mitya.
  • When it applies: Needs reliable Cryo and Electro reaction uptime.

Accessible Conduct Carry (On-field DPS)

Lower-cost weapon alternatives to her signature.

  • Weapons: Mailed Flower, Tidal Shadow
  • Artifacts: Disenchantment in Deep Shadow (4 pcs); Gladiator's Finale + A Day Carved From Rising Winds (2 + 2 pcs)
  • Stats: Maintain CRIT and applicable offensive stats.
  • Teams: A budget Stellar-Conduct team with stable application.
  • When it applies: Damage depends heavily on enabling supports.

Sandrone talents

Normal Attack: Formule Phenomenale: Self-Evident Proposition

Normal Attack
Generates threads that control her weapon using formulae, performing up to 3 consecutive strikes.

Charged Attack
Summons the Facilitatrice Automatisee Generaliste Interactive et Omnitache, or "Fagio," and switches it to Decoding mode: Unleashes a sweeping fire attack on enemies in front, as well as periodically fires condensed beams, dealing AoE Cryo DMG.
Radiance: Stellar Glimmer: The condensed beams will instead deal AoE Cryo DMG that is considered the corresponding Stellar Glimmer reaction DMG.
While in Decoding mode, Fagio's Decoding Power will continuously increase. Firing condensed beams will also further increase its Decoding Power.
When Fagio's Decoding Power reaches the maximum of 100 points, it switches to Power Overdrive mode, in which it fires at opponents with longer intervals, dealing Cryo DMG. It will be unable to switch to Decoding mode at this time.
When not in Decoding mode, Fagio's Decoding Power gradually decreases. When Sandrone is off-field, she will focus on maintaining Fagio, causing its Decoding Power to decrease at 300% the original rate.
Fagio exits Power Overdrive mode when its Decoding Power has decreased to under 50.

Plunging Attack
Plunges from midair to strike the ground below, dealing DMG to opponents along their path and causing AoE DMG on landing.

Elemental Skill: Formule Phenomenale: Differential Analysis

Cooldown: 4s

With her impeccable, ladylike poise, Sandrone boards the Tea Party Tactical Assault Hovermech and hovers forward for 6s. During this time, Sandrone can control the travel direction of the craft. Pressing Sprint consumes 10 Stamina and launches her into an elegant high-speed hover mode, in which she will move even faster at the cost of 15 Stamina per second.

When Sandrone starts hovering, if there are opponents nearby, she will also summon a Prismatic Resonance Cannon and fire 2 Prism Shots at any opponents who have yet to cool down, dealing Cryo DMG.
Radiance: Stellar Glimmer: The second Prism Shot will instead deal Cryo DMG that is considered the corresponding Stellar Glimmer reaction DMG.

When on the Tea Party Tactical Assault Hovermech, Sandrone will also elegantly and quietly repair Fagio, causing Decoding Power to swiftly decrease.

Elemental Burst: Formule Phenomenale: Q.E.D.

Cooldown: 15s · Energy cost: 60

In line with established ladylike behavior, Sandrone summons a large number of Prismatic Resonance Cannons for a frontal bombardment before firing on her foes with a Convective Inhibition Ray, dealing AoE Cryo DMG.
Radiance: Stellar Glimmer: The Convective Inhibition Ray instead deals AoE Cryo DMG that is considered the corresponding Stellar Glimmer reaction DMG.

Passive talent: Eternal Speculation Engine

Radiance: Stellar Glimmer: When using the Elemental Skill Formule Phenomenale: Differential Analysis, if Fagio's Decoding Power is greater than 50, the second Prism Shot fired by Fagio at nearby opponents will deal 400% of its original DMG as Fagio's Decoding Power is decreased.
Additionally, for every 10 points of Decoding Power Fagio loses, it gains 1 stack of Refined Tactics for 60s (max 10 stacks). When Sandrone uses the Elemental Burst Formule Phenomenale: Q.E.D. while in the Radiance: Stellar Glimmer state, all stacks of Refined Tactics are cleared, causing the Convective Inhibition Ray to deal 100% + (number of stacks cleared) × 10% of its original DMG.

Passive talent: A Lady's Code of Conduct

Increases Sandrone's Elemental Mastery based on her ATK. Every 100 ATK will increase her Elemental Mastery by 8. The maximum increase she can gain this way is 160.

Passive talent: Stellar Jubilee: Light of Rationalisme

Sandrone will enter the Radiance: Stellar-Conduct state when she is inside a Polestar Field, or the Radiance: Stellar Swirl state for 8s after a nearby party member triggers a Stellar Swirl reaction.
When a party member triggers a Superconduct or Cryo Swirl reaction, it becomes a Stellar-Conduct or Stellar Swirl reaction instead, and the Base DMG of the aforementioned reaction is also increased by 0.7% for every 100 points of Sandrone's ATK. A maximum increase of 14% can be obtained in this way.

Passive talent: A Caucus Prelude and a Long Tale

When Sandrone is in the party, you will gain additional snack rewards after turning in your Daily Commissions with Katheryne.

Sandrone constellations

C1: Morrow After the Golden Dusk

When in Decoding mode, Fagio's Decoding Power increases at a 50% lower rate, and all party members deal 30% increased Stellar Glimmer reaction DMG.

C2: An Heiress Gazed Into the Looking-Glass

Radiance: Stellar Glimmer: Increases CRIT DMG from Charged Attack condensed beams by 40%. Also, each time a beam is fired, CRIT DMG dealt by all condensed beams fired during this Decoding mode period will be further increased by 20%. Max 3 stacks.

C3: Refuse the Wake of Dusk, the Moonlit Yoke

Increases the Level of Formule Phenomenale: Self-Evident Proposition by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C4: In Knowledge Lies the World's True Ground

When Sandrone deals Stellar-Conduct or Stellar Swirl DMG to an opponent, she will also summon an extra Prismatic Resonance Cannon to fire off a coordinated attack which deals Cryo DMG at 125% or 187.5% of her ATK, respectively. This DMG is considered the corresponding Stellar Glimmer reaction DMG, and the effect can occur once every 4s.

C5: Of All Beside, She Takes No Part

Increases the Level of Formule Phenomenale: Q.E.D. by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C6: Narcissus Wakes, Her Eyes Upon the Dawn

When Fagio fires a condensed beam for the third time while in Decoding mode, the beam becomes a more powerful Condensed Cluster Beam that fires continuously. This beam will deal up to 4 additional instances of AoE Cryo DMG at 100% of Sandrone's ATK on top of any subsequent original beam fired.
Radiance: Stellar Glimmer: The Condensed Cluster Beam attack deals 4 additional instances of AoE Cryo DMG. The DMG dealt with this attack, which is considered the corresponding Stellar Glimmer reaction DMG, is equal to:
Radiance: Stellar-Conduct: 80% of Sandrone's ATK.
Radiance: Stellar Swirl: 120% of Sandrone's ATK.
Additionally, all Stellar Glimmer reaction DMG dealt by Sandrone is elevated by 20%.

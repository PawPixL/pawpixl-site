Lauma Build Guide | Best Weapons, Artifacts & Teams

Lauma build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Nightweaver's Looking Glass, Etherlight Spindlelute), artifacts (Silken Moon's Serenade), main stats…

Role: Buff Support

Lauma is a powerful Dendro support specializing in Lunar-Bloom, offering strong reaction buffs, excellent team synergy, and solid off-field utility.

Best weapons for Lauma

  1. Nightweaver's Looking Glass
  2. Etherlight Spindlelute
  3. A Thousand Floating Dreams
  4. Sunny Morning Sleep-In
  5. Starcaller's Watch
  6. Blackmarrow Lantern
  7. Sacrificial Fragments
  8. Fruit of Fulfillment

Best artifacts

  • Silken Moon's Serenade (4 pcs) · Best for Lunar
  • Deepwood Memories (4 pcs) · Support
  • Gilded Dreams + Wanderer's Troupe (2 + 2 pcs) · Support

Main stats

  • Sands: Energy Recharge / Elemental Mastery
  • Goblet: EM
  • Circlet: EM / Crit

Stat targets

  • Elemental Mastery: 1,000 (with buffs)
  • Energy Recharge: 160% - 200%
  • CRIT Rate: 40% - 50% (if Favonius)
  • CRIT DMG: 100%+ (optional)

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 9+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 9+

Best teams

  • Celestial Bloom: Lauma, Nefer, Columbina, Nahida
  • Mossy Water: Lauma, Neuvillette, Furina, Ineffa
  • Leaf It Alone: Lauma, Alhaitham, Xingqiu, Kuki Shinobu
  • Four-Star Garden: Lauma, Aino, Xingqiu, Kuki Shinobu

Pros

  • Massive Lunar-Bloom buffs
  • Strong Dendro application
  • Excellent off-field support
  • Flexible team utility

Cons

  • Less specialized outside Bloom teams
  • Restricted team synergies
  • Healing locked behind C1

Build variations

  • Lunar-Bloom Team Buffer (Support): Carry the Lunar team buff while supporting reaction damage.
  • Deepwood Dendro Support (Support): Provide Dendro RES shred while maintaining support utility.

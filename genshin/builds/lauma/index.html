Lauma Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

Lauma build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Nightweaver's Looking Glass, Etherlight Spindlelute), artifacts (Silken Moon's Serenade), main stats.

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Role: Buff Support

Lauma is a powerful Dendro support specializing in Lunar-Bloom, offering strong reaction buffs, excellent team synergy, and solid off-field utility.

Best weapons for Lauma

  1. Nightweaver's Looking Glass: Signature EM catalyst granting powerful party bonuses to Bloom, Hyperbloom, Burgeon and Lunar-Bloom.
  2. Etherlight Spindlelute: Event ER catalyst with EM after Skill. Good F2P option balancing Burst uptime and reaction support.
  3. A Thousand Floating Dreams: Provides 265 EM, with up to 96 additional EM or 30% Element DMG depending on team composition. grants nearby teammates 40 EM.
  4. Sunny Morning Sleep-In: Elemental Mastery and reaction-related damage utility; best suited to reaction-centered builds.
  5. Starcaller's Watch: Elemental Mastery catalyst that buffs the active character after creating a shield.
  6. Blackmarrow Lantern: EM and strong Bloom/Lunar-Bloom bonuses; especially valuable with Ascendant Gleam active.
  7. Sacrificial Fragments: Elemental Mastery and a chance to reset Skill cooldown; extra casts depend on Skill hits.
  8. Fruit of Fulfillment: Craftable Energy Recharge catalyst that gains EM through reactions while reducing ATK.

Best artifacts for Lauma

  • Silken Moon's Serenade (4 pcs) · Best for Lunar: Grants 20% ER from its 2-piece bonus. The 4-piece effect provides 60 EM to the entire team and increases Lunar Reaction DMG by 10% per different Gleaming Moon effect. Lauma's best artifact set for supporting Lunar-Bloom teams. Main stats: ER/EM · EM · EM.
  • Deepwood Memories (4 pcs) · Support: Provides 15% Dendro DMG Bonus and reduces enemies' Dendro RES by 30% for 8s after Lauma's Skill or Burst hits, making it a strong support option for boosting team Dendro damage, particularly when no other teammate carries the set. Main stats: ER/EM · EM · EM.
  • Gilded Dreams + Wanderer's Troupe (2 + 2 pcs) · Support: Provides 160 EM, making this an excellent alternative for maximizing Lauma's Lunar-Bloom buffs and reaction DMG. A strong, flexible combination when farming a dedicated 4-piece set isn't practical. Main stats: ER/EM · EM · EM.

Best teams for Lauma

  • Celestial Bloom: Lauma (Buffs), Nefer (Main DPS), Columbina (Lunar), Nahida (EM Buffs). Lauma and Nahida supply Dendro and buffs; Columbina enables Lunar-Bloom. The on-field carry capitalizes on their reactions.
  • Mossy Water: Lauma (Dendro Buffer / Enabler), Neuvillette (On-field DPS), Furina (Off-field DPS / Buffer), Ineffa (Off-field DPS / Shielder). Neuvillette and Furina provide Hydro damage, while Lauma adds Dendro and reaction support. Ineffa shields and triggers Hyperbloom and Lunar-Charged damage.
  • Leaf It Alone: Lauma (Dendro Buffer / Enabler), Alhaitham (On-field DPS), Xingqiu (Off-field DPS / Hydro Enabler), Kuki Shinobu (Healer / Reaction Trigger). Alhaitham deals on-field Dendro damage, Xingqiu applies Hydro, and Kuki heals while triggering Hyperbloom. Lauma adds Dendro and reaction support.
  • Four-Star Garden: Lauma (Dendro Buffer / Enabler), Aino (Off-field DPS / Enabler), Xingqiu (Off-field DPS / Hydro Enabler), Kuki Shinobu (Healer / Reaction Trigger). Lauma supplies Dendro and buffs, Aino and Xingqiu apply Hydro, and Kuki heals and triggers Hyperbloom. Accessible teammates support a premium Lauma core.

Pros

  • Massive Lunar-Bloom buffs
  • Strong Dendro application
  • Excellent off-field support
  • Flexible team utility

Cons

  • Less specialized outside Bloom teams
  • Restricted team synergies
  • Healing locked behind C1

Build variations

Lunar-Bloom Team Buffer (Support)

Carry the Lunar team buff while supporting reaction damage.

  • Weapons: Nightweaver's Looking Glass, Etherlight Spindlelute, A Thousand Floating Dreams
  • Artifacts: Silken Moon's Serenade (4 pcs)
  • Stats: EM and enough ER to maintain abilities.
  • Teams: Hydro and Dendro teammates able to trigger Lunar-Bloom.
  • When it applies: Avoid a second Silken Moon holder.

Deepwood Dendro Support (Support)

Provide Dendro RES shred while maintaining support utility.

  • Weapons: Etherlight Spindlelute, A Thousand Floating Dreams, Blackmarrow Lantern
  • Artifacts: Deepwood Memories (4 pcs)
  • Stats: EM first; adjust ER for Burst uptime.
  • Teams: Dendro damage team with no other Deepwood carrier.
  • When it applies: Use only when Deepwood is not already covered.

Lauma talents

Normal Attack: Peregrination of Linnunrata

Cooldown: Spirit Envoy Metamorphosis: 4s

Normal Attack
Performs up to 3 attacks that deal Dendro DMG.

Charged Attack
Lauma undergoes Spirit Envoy Metamorphosis, transforming into an envoy of the tranquil forest and consuming Stamina to charge forward for up to 10s. While in this state, Lauma's interruption resistance is increased, and she can consume additional Stamina to perform up to two consecutive jumps.
When exiting the Spirit Envoy state, Spirit Envoy Metamorphosis will enter cooldown, during which time, Lauma's Charged Attack will be transformed into Spiritcall Prayer: Consumes a certain amount of Stamina to deal AoE Dendro DMG to opponents in front of her after a short casting time.

Plunging Attack
Calling upon the might of Dendro, Lauma plunges towards the ground from mid-air, damaging all opponents in her path and dealing AoE Dendro DMG upon impact with the ground.

Elemental Skill: Runo: Dawnless Rest of Karsikko

Cooldown: 12s

Silently incants an ancient hymn, summoning a Frostgrove Sanctuary with different effects depending on whether you Tap or Hold.

Tap
Intone a Hymn of Hunting, dealing AoE Dendro DMG.

Hold
Can be unleashed when you have at least 1 Verdant Dew. Lauma consumes all Verdant Dew and intones a Hymn of Eternal Rest, dealing one regular instance of AoE Dendro DMG and another instance of AoE Dendro DMG that is considered Lunar-Bloom DMG. Each Verdant Dew consumed will give Lauma one stack of Moon Song. Each time you Hold to cast an Elemental Skill, a maximum of 3 Verdant Dew can be consumed in this way.

Additionally, when Lauma's Elemental Skill or attacks from Frostgrove Sanctuary hit an opponent, that opponent's Dendro RES and Hydro RES will be decreased for 10s.

Elemental Burst: Runo: All Hearts Become the Beating Moon

Cooldown: 15s · Energy cost: 60

Let the wishes of all beings become as moonlight that bathes the earth. Lauma incants a sacred song of the far north, gaining 18 stacks of Pale Hymn.
Additionally, if Lauma uses her Elemental Burst while she has Moon Song, or she gains Moon Song within 15s of using her Elemental Burst, she will consume all Moon Song stacks and gain 6 stacks of Pale Hymn for every Moon Song stack consumed. This effect can only be triggered once for each Elemental Burst used, including the 15 seconds following its use.

Pale Hymn
When nearby party members deal Bloom, Hyperbloom, Burgeon, or Lunar-Bloom DMG, 1 stack of Pale Hymn will be consumed and the DMG dealt will be increased based on Lauma's Elemental Mastery. If this DMG hits multiple opponents at once, then multiple stacks of Pale Hymn will be consumed, depending on how many opponents are hit.
The duration for each stack of Pale Hymn is counted independently.

Passive talent: Light for the Frosty Night

For the next 20s after Lauma uses her Elemental Skill Runo: Dawnless Rest of Karsikko, corresponding differing buff effects will be granted depending on the party's Moonsign. The buffs provided by different Moonsign levels cannot stack.

Moonsign: Nascent Gleam
Bloom, Hyperbloom, and Burgeon DMG dealt by all nearby party members can score CRIT Hits, with CRIT Rate fixed at 15%, and CRIT DMG fixed at 100%. CRIT Rate from this effect stacks with CRIT Rate from similar effects that allow these Elemental Reactions to CRIT.

Moonsign: Ascendant Gleam
All nearby party members' Lunar-Bloom DMG CRIT Rate +10%, CRIT DMG +20%.

Passive talent: Cleansing for the Spring

Each point of Elemental Mastery Lauma has will give her the following bonuses:
· DMG dealt by her Elemental Skill is increased by 0.04%. The maximum increase obtainable this way is 32%.
· The cooldown of her Charged Attack Spirit Envoy Metamorphosis is reduced by 0.02%. The maximum reduction obtainable this way is 20%.

Passive talent: Moonsign Benediction: Nature's Chorus

When a Bloom reaction is triggered by a party member, it will be converted into the Lunar-Bloom reaction, with every point of Elemental Mastery that Lauma has increasing Lunar-Bloom's Base DMG by 0.0175%, up to a maximum of 14%.

Additionally, when Lauma is in the party, the party's Moonsign will increase by 1 level.

Passive talent: Prayers for the Forest

Displays the location of nearby resources unique to Nod-Krai on the mini-map.
Additionally, Lauma appears to have a special affinity with some small animals affected by kuuvahki...

Lauma constellations

C1: "O Lips, Weave Me Songs and Psalms"

After Lauma uses her Elemental Skill Runo: Dawnless Rest of Karsikko or her Elemental Burst Runo: All Hearts Become the Beating Moon, she will gain Threads of Life for 20s.
During this time, when nearby party members trigger Lunar-Bloom reactions, nearby active characters will recover HP equal to 500% of Lauma's Elemental Mastery. This effect can be triggered once every 1.9s.
Additionally, the Stamina cost for Lauma's Spirit Envoy Form will be reduced by 40%, and its Max Duration will be extended by 5s.

C2: "Twine Warnings and Tales From the North"

The Elemental Burst Runo: All Hearts Become the Beating Moon is enhanced:
· Pale Hymn effects are increased: All nearby party members' Bloom, Hyperbloom, and Burgeon DMG is further increased by 500% of Lauma's Elemental Mastery, and their Lunar-Bloom DMG is further increased by 400% of Lauma's Elemental Mastery.

Moonsign: Ascendant Gleam: All nearby party members' Lunar-Bloom DMG is increased by 40%.

C3: "Seek Not to Tread the Sly Fox's Path"

Increases the Level of Runo: All Hearts Become the Beating Moon by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C4: "Nor Yearn for the Great Bear's Might"

When attacks from the Frostgrove Sanctuary summoned by her Elemental Skill Runo: Dawnless Rest of Karsikko hit opponents, Lauma will regain 4 Elemental Energy. This effect can be triggered once every 5s.

C5: "If Truth May Be Subject to Witness"

Increases the Level of Runo: Dawnless Rest of Karsikko by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C6: "I Offer Blood and Tears to the Moonlight"

When the Frostgrove Sanctuary attacks opponents, it will deal 1 additional instance of AoE Dendro DMG equal to 185% of Lauma's Elemental Mastery. This DMG is considered Lunar-Bloom DMG. This instance of DMG will not consume any Pale Hymn stacks and will provide Lauma with 2 stacks of Pale Hymn, as well as refreshing the duration of Pale Hymn stacks gained in this manner.
This effect can occur up to 8 times during each Frostgrove Sanctuary. When using the Elemental Skill Runo: Dawnless Rest of Karsikko, all Pale Hymn stacks gained in this manner will be removed.

Additionally, when Lauma uses a Normal Attack while she has Pale Hymn stacks, she will consume 1 stack to convert this to deal Dendro DMG equal to 150% of her Elemental Mastery. This DMG is considered Lunar-Bloom DMG.

Moonsign: Ascendant Gleam: All nearby party members' Lunar-Bloom DMG is elevated by 25%.

Amber Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

Amber build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Elegy for the End, Favonius Warbow), artifacts (Noblesse Oblige), main stats, talent priority, teams.

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Role: Support / Sub DPS

Amber is a flexible Pyro support whose rapid-hit Burst enables Pyro Swirls, shield breaking, and buffs from Elegy or support artifacts. She needs substantial Energy Recharge for frequent Bursts. Her alternate playstyles include C0 Melt Charged Shots, C2+ Baron Bunny detonations, and EM-based Burgeon.

Best weapons for Amber

  1. Elegy for the End: Her rapid-hit Burst stacks Elegy to grant team EM and ATK; its ER substat helps meet demanding Burst needs. Ideal in reaction teams benefiting from EM and ATK.
  2. Favonius Warbow: ER and neutral particles help Amber and her team cycle Bursts. Build CRIT Rate and land a field-time CRIT to trigger it. Requires CRIT hits while Amber is on-field. Higher refinements improve proc consistency.
  3. The First Great Magic: Provides substantial ER, but the Skill reset is inconsistent because Baron Bunny may explode after Amber has left the field. Use mainly for its ER substat; do not rely on the reset.
  4. Skyward Harp: Event bow with ER and a rotating DMG bonus.
  5. Sacrificial Bow: Fishing bow with ER and modest extra damage. Practical permanent-access fallback for Burst uptime. Purchased through Sumeru fishing exchange.
  6. Fading Twilight: Normal Attacks may group smaller enemies briefly; its CRIT Rate can help Fav-like stat goals, but lacks ER. Paid Battle Pass; only a niche utility choice.
  7. End of the Line
  8. The Viridescent Hunt

Best artifacts for Amber

  • Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs): Reliable 20% party ATK buff after Burst. Main stats: ER · flexible goblet · CRIT Rate (Fav) / flexible.
  • Instructor (4 pcs): Reaction-triggered team EM buff. Main stats: ER · flexible · CRIT Rate (Fav).
  • Scroll of the Hero of Cinder City (4 pcs): Situational elemental DMG buff if Amber triggers a relevant reaction; less potent without Nightsoul mechanics. Main stats: ER · flexible · CRIT Rate (Fav).

Main stats

  • Sands: Energy Recharge
  • Goblet: Pyro DMG / HP% (utility)
  • Circlet: Crit Rate (Favonius) / Flexible

  • Energy Recharge (Elegy): 225% - 240% solo baseline
  • Energy Recharge (Favonius): 165% - 175% solo baseline
  • Crit Rate (Favonius): ~40% - 60%
  • Level: 70+ utility / 90 for Burgeon

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 6-8
  • Elemental Burst: Level 8-9

Best teams for Amber

  • Outrider VV Vape: Amber (Pyro buffer), Hu Tao (Main DPS), Xingqiu (Hydro enabler), Kaedehara Kazuha (VV support). Amber sets up Pyro for Kazuha to Swirl, enabling Pyro RES shred before Hu Tao attacks. Xingqiu enables Vaporize; no dedicated sustain.
  • Firework Support Crew: Amber (Burst buffer), Lyney (Main DPS), Bennett (ATK buffer / healer), Zhongli (Shielder). Triple Pyro supports Lyney. Amber offers Pyro Resonance, Burst utility and possible Elegy/Noblesse buffs; Zhongli protects charged shots.
  • Outrider Frostfire: Amber (Melt Charged DPS), Citlali (Cryo support), Xilonen (RES shred / healer), Bennett (ATK buffer). Alternate Melt DPS build: Citlali offers Cryo and protection while Xilonen and Bennett boost Amber’s Charged Shots.
  • Bunny Bloom Patrol: Amber (Burgeon trigger), Collei (Dendro applier), Sangonomiya Kokomi (Hydro / healer), Rosaria (Cryo support). Alternate EM Burgeon build: Collei and Kokomi form Dendro Cores; Rosaria adds Freeze. Aim Pyro shots at cores, not enemies.

Build variations

Melt Charged Shot (On-field DPS)

For C0+ Amber fans who enjoy aimed-shot Melt; a balanced CRIT build is safer against weak-spotless bosses.

  • Weapons: Astral Vulture's Crimson Plumage, The First Great Magic, Amos' Bow, Hamayumi
  • Artifacts: Wanderer's Troupe (4 pcs); Shimenawa's Reminiscence (4 pcs)
  • Stats: EM or ATK% / Pyro DMG / CRIT; 150–300 EM as a starting aim; level Normal Attack first.
  • Teams: Reliable Cryo aura, protection against interruption, and offensive buffs.
  • When it applies: Accuracy and Cryo uptime matter.
  • Notes: Shimenawa consumes energy. Hamayumi rewards saving Burst; do not combine those assumptions indiscriminately.

C2+ Bunny Bomber (Skill DPS)

Use when you want big manual Baron Bunny detonation damage and AoE.

  • Weapons: Astral Vulture's Crimson Plumage, The First Great Magic, Amos' Bow, Hamayumi
  • Artifacts: Crimson Witch of Flames (4 pcs); Gilded Dreams (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Build EM/ATK%, Pyro DMG and CRIT; prioritize Skill over Normal Attack as C4+ becomes available.
  • Teams: Pyro-friendly Melt setup and time to detonate Baron Bunny with fully charged shots at its feet.
  • When it applies: C2 required; C4 strongly preferred.
  • Notes: C2 enables manual foot detonation, and C4 adds a second charge and lowers cooldown.

Burgeon Trigger (Reaction DPS)

Aim Amber’s Charged Shots at Dendro Cores to trigger controlled AoE Burgeon.

  • Weapons: The Stringless, Cloudforged, King's Squire, Raven Bow
  • Artifacts: Flower of Paradise Lost (4 pcs); Gilded Dreams (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Level 90; EM/EM/EM with maximum EM. Talent investment is optional; Burst is not part of standard rotation.
  • Teams: Reliable Dendro Core generation from Hydro + Dendro, plus interruption resistance or Freeze.
  • When it applies: Needs generated cores; avoid aiming at enemies and causing Burning.
  • Notes: Avoid Burst because heavy Pyro application may prevent Dendro Core production.

Accessible Burst Utility (Support)

Use when you want Amber’s quick Pyro setup and team buffs without premium weapons.

  • Weapons: Favonius Warbow, End of the Line, Sacrificial Bow
  • Artifacts: Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs); Instructor (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Prioritize ER; add CRIT Rate if using Favonius. Offensive stats are optional.
  • Teams: ATK-scaling carry for Noblesse, or reaction-oriented carry for Instructor.
  • When it applies: Instructor must trigger a reaction while Amber is on-field.
  • Notes: ER varies widely with Favonius procs; do not assume Burst uptime at low ER.

Reverse Melt Enabler (Support)

Use Burst to help set up specific reverse Melt actions or break shields.

  • Weapons: Elegy for the End, Favonius Warbow
  • Artifacts: Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs); Instructor (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Prioritize Burst access with ER; personal damage is secondary.
  • Teams: Cryo characters that can use a short, rapid Pyro application window.
  • When it applies: Short Pyro window; not a substitute for sustained off-field Pyro.
  • Notes: Check rotation timing and aura consistency for the exact team.

Amber talents

Normal Attack: Sharpshooter

Normal Attack
Performs up to 5 consecutive shots with a bow.

Charged Attack
Performs a more precise Aimed Shot with increased DMG.
While aiming, flames will accumulate on the arrowhead. A fully charged flaming arrow will deal Pyro DMG.

Plunging Attack
Fires off a shower of arrows in mid-air before falling and striking the ground, dealing AoE DMG upon impact.

Elemental Skill: Explosive Puppet

Cooldown: 15s

The ever-reliable Baron Bunny takes the stage.

Baron Bunny
·Continuously taunts the enemy, drawing their fire.
·Baron Bunny's HP scales with Amber's Max HP.
·When destroyed or when its timer expires, Baron Bunny explodes, dealing AoE Pyro DMG.

Hold
Adjusts the throwing direction of Baron Bunny.
The longer the button is held, the further the throw.

Cooldown: 12s · Energy cost: 40

Fires off a shower of arrows, dealing continuous AoE Pyro DMG.

Passive talent: Every Arrow Finds Its Target

Increases the CRIT Rate of Fiery Rain by 10% and widens its AoE by 30%.

Passive talent: Precise Shot

Aimed Shot hits on weak points increase ATK by 15% for 10s.

Passive talent: Gliding Champion

Decreases gliding Stamina consumption for your own party members by 20%.
Not stackable with Passive Talents that provide the exact same effects.

Role: Support / Sub DPS

Amber is a flexible Pyro support whose rapid-hit Burst enables Pyro Swirls, shield breaking, and buffs from Elegy or support artifacts. She needs substantial Energy Recharge for frequent Bursts. Her alternate playstyles include C0 Melt Charged Shots, C2+ Baron Bunny detonations, and EM-based Burgeon.

Best weapons for Amber

  1. Elegy for the End
  2. Favonius Warbow
  3. The First Great Magic
  4. Skyward Harp
  5. Sacrificial Bow
  6. Fading Twilight
  7. End of the Line
  8. The Viridescent Hunt

Best artifacts

  • Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs)
  • Instructor (4 pcs)
  • Scroll of the Hero of Cinder City (4 pcs)

Main stats

  • Sands: Energy Recharge
  • Goblet: Pyro DMG / HP% (utility)
  • Circlet: Crit Rate (Favonius) / Flexible

Stat targets

  • Energy Recharge (Elegy): 225% - 240% solo baseline
  • Energy Recharge (Favonius): 165% - 175% solo baseline
  • Crit Rate (Favonius): ~40% - 60%
  • Level: 70+ utility / 90 for Burgeon

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 6-8
  • Elemental Burst: Level 8-9

Best teams

  • Outrider VV Vape: Amber, Hu Tao, Xingqiu, Kaedehara Kazuha
  • Firework Support Crew: Amber, Lyney, Bennett, Zhongli
  • Outrider Frostfire: Amber, Citlali, Xilonen, Bennett
  • Bunny Bloom Patrol: Amber, Collei, Sangonomiya Kokomi, Rosaria

Pros

  • Fast Burst Pyro application
  • Great shield breaking
  • Free starter character
  • Useful buff options

Cons

  • High Burst ER needs
  • Short buff setup window
  • Unreliable Skill particles
  • Low personal support damage

Build variations

  • Melt Charged Shot (On-field DPS): For C0+ Amber fans who enjoy aimed-shot Melt; a balanced CRIT build is safer against weak-spotless bosses.
  • C2+ Bunny Bomber (Skill DPS): Use when you want big manual Baron Bunny detonation damage and AoE.
  • Burgeon Trigger (Reaction DPS): Aim Amber’s Charged Shots at Dendro Cores to trigger controlled AoE Burgeon.
  • Accessible Burst Utility (Support): Use when you want Amber’s quick Pyro setup and team buffs without premium weapons.
  • Reverse Melt Enabler (Support): Use Burst to help set up specific reverse Melt actions or break shields.

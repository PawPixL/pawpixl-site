Role: Support, Buffer

Gorou buffs DEF and Geo DMG for Geo-focused teams, with stronger effects when the party has three Geo members. His Skill supplies the core buff; his Burst adds mobile buffs and extra DEF. Prioritize Energy Recharge and Favonius consistency over damage.

Best weapons for Gorou

  1. Favonius Warbow
  2. Elegy for the End
  3. Sacrificial Bow
  4. End of the Line
  5. Snare Hook

Best artifacts

  • Scroll of the Hero of Cinder City (4 pcs)
  • The Exile (4 pcs)
  • Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs)

Main stats

  • Sands: Energy Recharge
  • Goblet: DEF% / Geo DMG
  • Circlet: Crit Rate / DEF%

Stat targets

  • ER: 200% - 220%
  • CR: 45% - 65%
  • DEF: Situational

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 9+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 6-8

Best teams

  • Oni Command: Gorou, Arataki Itto, Albedo, Durin
  • Maid of Honor: Gorou, Noelle, Furina, Yelan
  • Moonstone Guard: Gorou, Zibai, Illuga, Columbina
  • Geo on a Budget: Gorou, Noelle, Yun Jin, Fischl

Pros

  • Strong DEF buffs
  • Low field time
  • Geo team synergy
  • Free best weapon

Cons

  • Geo-focused teams
  • High ER needs
  • Low personal damage
  • C6 boosts value

Build variations

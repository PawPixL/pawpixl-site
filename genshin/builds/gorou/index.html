Gorou Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

Gorou build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Favonius Warbow, Elegy for the End), artifacts (Scroll of the Hero of Cinder City), main stats.

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Role: Support, Buffer

Gorou buffs DEF and Geo DMG for Geo-focused teams, with stronger effects when the party has three Geo members. His Skill supplies the core buff; his Burst adds mobile buffs and extra DEF. Prioritize Energy Recharge and Favonius consistency over damage.

Best weapons for Gorou

  1. Favonius Warbow: Generates team energy on CRIT hits. Build CRIT Rate for procs.
  2. Elegy for the End: Offers ATK and EM buffs, but procs vary. Only when allies benefit.
  3. Sacrificial Bow: Extra Skill use can battery Geo allies. Longer field time. High refinements help.
  4. End of the Line: Free ER stat stick for Burst uptime.
  5. Snare Hook: Backup ER option without Favonius.

Best artifacts for Gorou

  • Scroll of the Hero of Cinder City (4 pcs): Crystallize boosts Geo damage. Main stats: ER · Any · CR.
  • The Exile (4 pcs): Restores team energy after Burst. Main stats: ER · Any · CR.
  • Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs): ATK buff; weaker for DEF carries. Main stats: ER · Any · CR.

Main stats

  • Sands: Energy Recharge
  • Goblet: DEF% / Geo DMG
  • Circlet: Crit Rate / DEF%

Stat targets

  • ER: 200% - 220%
  • CR: 45% - 65%
  • DEF: Situational

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 9+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 6-8

Best teams for Gorou

  • Oni Command: Gorou, Arataki Itto, Albedo, Durin. Gorou boosts Itto and Albedo; Durin adds damage and resistance shred.
  • Maid of Honor: Gorou, Noelle, Furina, Yelan. Gorou boosts Noelle, while Furina and Yelan add powerful Hydro damage and buffs.
  • Moonstone Guard: Gorou, Zibai, Illuga, Columbina. Gorou boosts Zibai’s DEF; Illuga and Columbina support Lunar-Crystallize damage.
  • Geo on a Budget: Gorou, Noelle, Yun Jin, Fischl. Three Geo allies maximize Gorou’s buffs; Fischl brings off-field damage and Crystallize.

Pros

  • Strong DEF buffs
  • Low field time
  • Geo team synergy
  • Free best weapon

Cons

  • Geo-focused teams
  • High ER needs
  • Low personal damage
  • C6 boosts value

Build variations

Geo Battery (Support)

Choose this when team energy is more valuable than an additional damage buff.

  • Weapons: Favonius Warbow, Sacrificial Bow
  • Artifacts: The Exile (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Prioritize ER and Favonius CRIT Rate; use a 5-star off-set piece if needed.
  • Teams: Geo carry with demanding Burst energy needs.
  • When it applies: Exile pieces are 4-star only.
  • Notes: Favonius requires a CRIT hit while Gorou is on field.

Lunar-Crystallize Support (Support)

Instructor shares EM to strengthen Lunar-Crystallize damage.

  • Weapons: Favonius Warbow
  • Artifacts: Instructor (4 pcs); Silken Moon's Serenade (4 pcs)
  • Stats: In short Zibai rotations, Burst every other rotation; invest more in DEF.
  • Teams: Zibai and consistent Lunar-Crystallize teammates.
  • When it applies: A team-specific setup, not general Geo support.
  • Notes: Other teammates may already hold Silken Moon’s Serenade.

C4 Healing Utility (Support)

Add light healing without changing Gorou’s core support role.

  • Weapons: Favonius Warbow
  • Artifacts: Scroll of the Hero of Cinder City (4 pcs); Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs)
  • Stats: After ER and Favonius CRIT needs, build DEF% for stronger C4 healing.
  • Teams: Gorou at C4+ in a DEF-based Geo team.
  • When it applies: C4 required; healing is not a full sustain replacement.
  • Notes: C4 heals based on DEF while Burst is active.

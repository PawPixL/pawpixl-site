Emilie Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams (7.2)

Emilie build guide for Genshin Impact 7.2: best weapons (Lumidouce Elegy, Calamity Queller), artifacts (Unfinished Reverie), main stats, talent priority.

Last updated: November 12, 2026 · Game version 7.2

Role: Off-Field DPS

Emilie is an off-field Dendro DPS who thrives when enemies are Burning. Her damage comes largely from her Skill and Burst, so prioritize ATK, Dendro damage, and CRIT rather than stacking EM.

Best weapons for Emilie

  1. Lumidouce Elegy: Signature CR, ATK and damage bonuses that synergize with Burning teams.
  2. Calamity Queller: High Base ATK, elemental damage bonus and an ATK passive that gains value off-field.
  3. Symphonist of Scents: CD stat stick and ATK; its healing-based team buff is normally inactive on Emilie. For personal stats, not its healing passive.
  4. Staff of Homa: CD and HP-based ATK; a solid general damage alternative without Burning-specific buffs.
  5. Deathmatch: BP CR with conditional ATK; convenient for balancing CRIT.
  6. Tamayuratei no Ohanashi: Provides Energy Recharge and an ATK bonus after using her Skill. A usable event weapon when stronger damage options are unavailable.
  7. Kitain Cross Spear: Craftable EM and Skill-based Energy return; not ideal for her ATK-focused damage.
  8. "The Catch": Free ER and Burst bonuses; Skill damage does not benefit from its passive.

Best artifacts for Emilie

  • Unfinished Reverie (4 pcs) · Best: Best general personal damage set when Burning is maintained. Main stats: ATK% · Dendro · CR/CD.
  • Deepwood Memories (4 pcs): Use when no teammate carries Deepwood and Dendro RES shred is needed. Main stats: ATK% · Dendro · CR/CD.
  • Golden Troupe (4 pcs): Boosts off-field Skill damage, but not all of Emilie’s damage sources. Main stats: ATK% · Dendro · CR/CD.

Best teams for Emilie

  • pixl Burn: Emilie (Sub DPS), Kinich (DPS), Nicole (Support), Durin (Sub DPS). Kinich deals on-field damage while Emilie benefits from Burning. Durin maintains Pyro, and Nicole adds buffs and protection.
  • Bombing Mondstat: Emilie (Sub DPS), Klee (DPS), Nicole (Support), Durin (Sub DPS). Klee supplies sustained Pyro for Emilie’s Burning damage. Durin adds off-field Pyro and Nicole provides buffs and protection.
  • Beach Day: Emilie (Sub DPS), Mualani (DPS), Mavuika (Support), Nahida (Sub DPS). Mavuika maintains Pyro for Burning, Emilie deals off-field Dendro damage, and Mualani contributes Hydro damage. Nahida supports Dendro.
  • Burning at home: Emilie (Sub DPS), Bennett (DPS), Xiangling (Support), Sucrose (Sub DPS). Xiangling maintains Burning while Emilie adds off-field Dendro damage. Bennett boosts ATK and heals; Sucrose groups enemies.

Build variations

Burning Off-field DPS (Off-field DPS)

Default damage-focused setup for teams that maintain Burning.

  • Weapons: Lumidouce Elegy, Calamity Queller, Deathmatch
  • Artifacts: Unfinished Reverie (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ATK% / Dendro / CRIT; ER as needed.
  • Teams: Sustained Pyro application to keep Burning active.
  • When it applies: Avoid overinvesting in EM

Deepwood Utility (Off-field DPS)

Trade some personal damage to supply Dendro resistance reduction.

  • Weapons: Lumidouce Elegy, Deathmatch, Tamayuratei no Ohanashi
  • Artifacts: Deepwood Memories (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ATK% / Dendro / CRIT.
  • Teams: No other unit holds Deepwood.
  • When it applies: More valuable when teammates deal Dendro damage

Accessible Weapons (Off-field DPS)

Lower-cost weapon alternatives while retaining her Burning-focused playstyle.

  • Weapons: Deathmatch, Tamayuratei no Ohanashi, Kitain Cross Spear
  • Artifacts: Unfinished Reverie (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ATK% / Dendro / CRIT; use ER if needed.
  • Teams: Burning team; monitor Burst uptime.
  • When it applies: Kitain EM is not a primary damage stat

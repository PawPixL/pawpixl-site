Emilie Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

Emilie build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Lumidouce Elegy, Calamity Queller), artifacts (Unfinished Reverie), main stats, talent priority.

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Role: Off-Field DPS

Emilie is an off-field Dendro DPS who thrives when enemies are Burning. Her damage comes largely from her Skill and Burst, so prioritize ATK, Dendro damage, and CRIT rather than stacking EM.

Best weapons for Emilie

  1. Lumidouce Elegy: Signature CR, ATK and damage bonuses that synergize with Burning teams.
  2. Calamity Queller: High Base ATK, elemental damage bonus and an ATK passive that gains value off-field.
  3. Symphonist of Scents: CD stat stick and ATK; its healing-based team buff is normally inactive on Emilie. For personal stats, not its healing passive.
  4. Staff of Homa: CD and HP-based ATK; a solid general damage alternative without Burning-specific buffs.
  5. Deathmatch: BP CR with conditional ATK; convenient for balancing CRIT.
  6. Tamayuratei no Ohanashi: Provides Energy Recharge and an ATK bonus after using her Skill. A usable event weapon when stronger damage options are unavailable.
  7. Kitain Cross Spear: Craftable EM and Skill-based Energy return; not ideal for her ATK-focused damage.
  8. "The Catch": Free ER and Burst bonuses; Skill damage does not benefit from its passive.

Best artifacts for Emilie

  • Unfinished Reverie (4 pcs) · Best: Best general personal damage set when Burning is maintained. Main stats: ATK% · Dendro · CR/CD.
  • Deepwood Memories (4 pcs): Use when no teammate carries Deepwood and Dendro RES shred is needed. Main stats: ATK% · Dendro · CR/CD.
  • Golden Troupe (4 pcs): Boosts off-field Skill damage, but not all of Emilie’s damage sources. Main stats: ATK% · Dendro · CR/CD.

Best teams for Emilie

  • pixl Burn: Emilie (Sub DPS), Kinich (DPS), Nicole (Support), Durin (Sub DPS). Kinich deals on-field damage while Emilie benefits from Burning. Durin maintains Pyro, and Nicole adds buffs and protection.
  • Bombing Mondstat: Emilie (Sub DPS), Klee (DPS), Nicole (Support), Durin (Sub DPS). Klee supplies sustained Pyro for Emilie’s Burning damage. Durin adds off-field Pyro and Nicole provides buffs and protection.
  • Beach Day: Emilie (Sub DPS), Mualani (DPS), Mavuika (Support), Nahida (Sub DPS). Mavuika maintains Pyro for Burning, Emilie deals off-field Dendro damage, and Mualani contributes Hydro damage. Nahida supports Dendro.
  • Burning at home: Emilie (Sub DPS), Bennett (DPS), Xiangling (Support), Sucrose (Sub DPS). Xiangling maintains Burning while Emilie adds off-field Dendro damage. Bennett boosts ATK and heals; Sucrose groups enemies.

Build variations

Burning Off-field DPS (Off-field DPS)

Default damage-focused setup for teams that maintain Burning.

  • Weapons: Lumidouce Elegy, Calamity Queller, Deathmatch
  • Artifacts: Unfinished Reverie (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ATK% / Dendro / CRIT; ER as needed.
  • Teams: Sustained Pyro application to keep Burning active.
  • When it applies: Avoid overinvesting in EM

Deepwood Utility (Off-field DPS)

Trade some personal damage to supply Dendro resistance reduction.

  • Weapons: Lumidouce Elegy, Deathmatch, Tamayuratei no Ohanashi
  • Artifacts: Deepwood Memories (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ATK% / Dendro / CRIT.
  • Teams: No other unit holds Deepwood.
  • When it applies: More valuable when teammates deal Dendro damage

Accessible Weapons (Off-field DPS)

Lower-cost weapon alternatives while retaining her Burning-focused playstyle.

  • Weapons: Deathmatch, Tamayuratei no Ohanashi, Kitain Cross Spear
  • Artifacts: Unfinished Reverie (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ATK% / Dendro / CRIT; use ER if needed.
  • Teams: Burning team; monitor Burst uptime.
  • When it applies: Kitain EM is not a primary damage stat

Emilie talents

Normal Attack: Shadow-Hunting Spear (Custom)

Normal Attack
Performs up to 4 consecutive spear strikes.

Charged Attack
Consumes a certain amount of Stamina to perform an upward slash.

Plunging Attack
Plunges from mid-air to strike the ground below, damaging opponents along the path and dealing AoE DMG upon impact.

Elemental Skill: Fragrance Extraction

Cooldown: Skill: 14s

Creates a Lumidouce Case that deals AoE Dendro DMG.

Lumidouce Case
·Fires Puffs of Puredew at nearby opponents at intervals, dealing Dendro DMG.
·When nearby opponents are affected by Burning, they will give off Scents at intervals, and 1 Scent can be created this way every 2s. The Lumidouce Case collects nearby Scents. The Lumidouce Case will level up after gathering 2 of them, after which it will fire 1 extra Puff of Puredew when firing, while the DMG dealt by and DMG AoE of the above attack will also be increased.
·1 Lumidouce Case created by Emilie herself can exist at any one time. The Case starts at Level 1 and can go up to Level 2. If the Case does not collect any Scents for 8s while it is on the field, it will go back to Level 1.

Arkhe: Pneuma
At intervals, after Emilie creates a Lumidouce Case this way, a Spiritbreath Thorn will descend in front of her and pierce her opponent, dealing Pneuma-aligned Dendro DMG.

Elemental Burst: Aromatic Explication

Cooldown: 13.5s · Energy cost: 50

Guiding the fragrances collected within the Case, Emilie converts them into pure Dendro energy, creating a Level 3 Lumidouce Case and stowing existing Cases away.
While it exists, the Level 3 Lumidouce Case will not gather nearby Scents, but it will continuously cause Scented Dew to descend, attacking opponents within range and dealing Dendro DMG. During this time, 1 drop of Scented Dew will descend every 0.3 seconds, and 1 opponent can become the target every 0.7 seconds.
When the duration ends, a Level 1 Lumidouce Case will be recreated. If a Lumidouce Case was stowed away when using Aromatic Explication, then the Case that was stowed away will be deployed instead, and its duration will be reset.
While Aromatic Explication is active, the Elemental Skill "Fragrance Extraction" will not create a Lumidouce Case.

Passive talent: Lingering Fragrance

Each time it collects 2 Scents, the Level 2 Lumidouce Case will consume Scents and release Cleardew Cologne that deals AoE Dendro DMG equal to 600% of Emilie's ATK to opponents. This DMG is not considered Elemental Skill DMG.

Passive talent: Rectification

Emilie deals increased DMG to Burning opponents based on her ATK, with every 1,000 ATK increasing DMG dealt by 15%. The maximum DMG bonus that can be gained this way is 36%.

Passive talent: Headspace Capture

When the Lumidouce Case created by Emilie is on the field, all party members gain 85% Pyro RES against Burning DMG.

Emilie constellations

C1: Light Fragrance Leaching

Increases the DMG dealt by Fragrance Extraction and her Ascension Talent's Cleardew Cologne "Lingering Fragrance" by 20%. The latter requires unlocking said Ascension Talent first.
Additionally, when nearby party members trigger the Burning reaction on opponents or deal Dendro DMG to Burning opponents, they will generate an additional Scent. This effect can be triggered once every 2.9s.

C2: Lakelight Top Note

When Fragrance Extraction, Aromatic Explication, or Cleardew Cologne produced by the Ascension Talent "Lingering Fragrance" (the last of which requires Ascension Talent activation) hits opponents, those opponents' Dendro RES is decreased by 30% for 10s.

C3: Exquisite Essence

Increases the Level of Fragrance Extraction by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C4: Lumidouce Heart Note

Aromatic Explication's duration is increased by 2s. The interval between opponents being selected as the target for Scented Dew is decreased by 0.3s.

C5: Puredew Aroma

Increases the Level of Aromatic Explication by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C6: Marcotte Sillage

When using Fragrance Extraction or Aromatic Explication, Emilie will gain Abiding Fragrance for 5s.
While this is active, after Emilie uses Normal or Charged Attacks, she will generate 1 Scent, and her Normal and Charged Attack DMG will be converted into Dendro DMG that cannot be overridden, and the DMG dealt will be increased by 300% of Emilie's ATK.

The Abiding Fragrance effect will be removed after 4 Scents are created this way or after its duration expires. Abiding Fragrance can be triggered once every 12s.

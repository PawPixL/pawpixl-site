Linnea Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

Linnea build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Golden Frostbound Oath, Aqua Simulacra), artifacts (Aubade of Morningstar and Moon), main stats.

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Role: Support / Sub DPS

Linnea is a Geo support specializing in Lunar Crystallize, boosting team damage and enabling powerful Lunar focused comps.

Best weapons for Linnea

  1. Golden Frostbound Oath: Provides 88.2% CRIT DMG and, at R1, grants 16% DEF, 40% Geo DMG, and 40% Lunar-Crystallize DMG to Linnea. When Moondrifts are nearby, teammates also gain 20% Geo and Lunar-Crystallize DMG. Linnea’s signature weapon and best-in-slot choice.
  2. Aqua Simulacra: Provides 88.2% CRIT DMG and, at R1, increases damage by 20% when enemies are nearby, even while Linnea is off-field. An excellent 5-star alternative for maximizing her personal damage, though it lacks the DEF and team buffs of her signature.
  3. Polar Star: Stacks ATK and DMG via Skill / Burst hits; gives CRIT Rate and rewards frequent off-field casts while…
  4. Slingshot: Provides 31.2% CRIT Rate and, at R5, grants 60% Normal and Charged Attack DMG when hits land within 0.3 seconds of firing. A budget option for Linnea, but its low Base ATK and conditional passive make it a considerable step down from her 5-star choices.
  5. The Stringless
  6. Favonius Warbow
  7. Covenant of Frost and Snow: Provides 51.7% DEF and, at R5, grants 240 Elemental Mastery for 12 seconds after using an Elemental Skill. A strong craftable option for Linnea, boosting her Lunar-Crystallize reaction damage, though it lacks the CRIT DMG and team buffs of her signature.
  8. Snare Hook

Best artifacts for Linnea

  • Aubade of Morningstar and Moon (4 pcs) · Main DPS: Provides 80 EM and increases the wearer’s Lunar Reaction DMG by 20% while off-field, rising to 60% with Ascendant Gleam. An excellent set for boosting Linnea’s personal Lunar-Crystallize damage. Main stats: DEF % · DEF % · CR/CD.
  • Silken Moon's Serenade (4 pcs) · Main DPS: Provides 20% Energy Recharge and, at 4-piece, grants the team up to 120 EM and a 10% Lunar Reaction DMG Bonus. Linnea can activate the effect off-field, making this a strong support option when no other teammate carries the set.
  • Husk of Opulent Dreams + Golden Troupe (4 pcs) · Main DPS: Provides 30% DEF and up to 24% additional DEF and 24% Geo DMG Bonus at max stacks. A strong option for Linnea, boosting both her DEF scaling and personal Geo damage, though Aubade is generally better for Lunar-Crystallize damage.

Best teams for Linnea

  • Crater Expectations: Linnea, Zibai, Columbina, Illuga. Zibai leads on-field Lunar-Crystallize damage, Columbina provides off-field Hydro and Lunar buffs, Linnea adds Geo RES shred, healing, and EM buffs, and Illuga boosts Geo and Lunar-Crystallize damage.
  • Guns & Rosés: Linnea, Navia, Columbina, Furina. Navia leads on-field Geo damage, Columbina provides Hydro application and Lunar reaction buffs, Linnea adds Geo RES shred, healing, and EM buffs, and Furina supplies off-field Hydro and a powerful teamwide DMG Bonus.
  • OniFans: Linnea, Arataki Itto, Columbina, Illuga. Itto leads on-field Geo damage, Linnea provides off-field damage, healing, and Geo RES shred, Columbina enables Lunar-Crystallize with Hydro application, and Illuga boosts Geo and Lunar-Crystallize damage.
  • Hopeless Impact: Linnea, Noelle, Aino, Illuga. Noelle leads on-field Geo damage and provides healing, Linnea adds off-field damage, Geo RES shred, and healing, Aino enables Hydro application and Lunar-Crystallize, and Illuga boosts Geo and Lunar reaction damage.

Linnea talents

Normal Attack: Capture Protocol

Normal Attack
Performs up to 3 consecutive shots with a bow.

Charged Attack
Performs a more precise Aimed Shot with increased DMG.
While aiming, stone crystals will accumulate on the arrowhead. A fully charged crystalline arrow will deal Geo DMG.

Plunging Attack
Fires off a shower of arrows in mid-air before falling and striking the ground, dealing AoE DMG upon impact.

Elemental Skill: Countermeasure: Lumi's Battle Cry!

Cooldown: 18s

Adventure with Lumi! Depending on how you Tap, Lumi attacks in different forms.

Tap
Lumi strikes in a Super Power Form, attacking nearby enemies continuously, dealing AoE Geo DMG. If there are Moondrifts nearby, Lumi will also deal AoE Geo DMG to nearby enemies, which will be considered Lunar-Crystallize Reaction DMG.

Continuous Tapping
Time to show what we're really capable of! After using her Elemental Skill, Linnea will feed Lumi sparkling gemstones when her Elemental Skill or Normal Attack is Tapped repeatedly. A full-bellied Lumi will strike in her Ultimate Power Form, dealing an especially powerful instance of AoE Geo DMG that is considered Lunar-Crystallize Reaction DMG, switching to Standard Power Form.
Additionally, tapping the Elemental Skill increases Linnea's interruption resistance.

Elemental Burst: Memo: Survival Guide in Extreme Conditions

Cooldown: 15s · Energy cost: 60

Even master adventurers need proper rest! Linnea summons Lumi to strike in Super Power Form, healing nearby party members. For a short duration, she will continuously heal nearby active party members based on Linnea's DEF.
If Lumi is already on the field when Linnea unleashes Elemental Burst, Lumi's active duration will be reset instead and her Strike Form will not change.

Passive talent: Field Observation Notes

When Lumi is present on the field, the Geo RES of opponents near Lumi will decrease by 15%.

Moonsign: Ascendant Gleam: Linnea's Elemental Skill Countermeasure: Lumi's Battle Cry! and Elemental Burst Memo: Survival Guide in Extreme Conditions are enhanced. After summoning Lumi, the Geo RES of opponents near Lumi will be further decreased by 15%.

Passive talent: Universal Naturalist Archive

Linnea will increase the Elemental Mastery of certain characters in your party according to your current active character. Increase in Elemental Mastery is based on 5% of Linnea's DEF. If your current active character:
· Is a Moonsign character: Increase this character's Elemental Mastery.
· Is not a Moonsign character: Increase Linnea's own Elemental Mastery.

Passive talent: Moonsign Benediction: Habitat Survey

When a party member triggers a Hydro Crystallize reaction, it will be converted into the Lunar-Crystallize reaction, with every 100 DEF that Linnea has increasing Lunar-Crystallize's Base DMG by 0.7%, up to a maximum of 14%.

Additionally, when Linnea is in the party, the party's Moonsign will increase by 1 level.

Passive talent: Master Adventurer

When Linnea is not in combat, the charge time of her Aimed Shot is greatly decreased, and she will shoot an Ultra Convenient Quick Arrow instead. Different effects will occur depending on the targets hit:
· Specific animals: Ultra Convenient Quick Arrow will be transformed into a special "Net" that can capture the essence and form of these animals, using them as a medium to manifest the animal's physical form again. The "Net" can only be used for a limited number of times each week.
· Ore veins used in forging: Linnea will dispatch Lumi, who will quickly strike at the ore marked by the Ultra Convenient Quick Arrow, then collect the dislodged ore. If Lumi is not on the field, Linnea will summon her for a short duration to help obtain the ore.

The DMG dealt to opponents by Linnea's Ultra Convenient Quick Arrow is reduced to 20% of her original Charged Arrow. Additionally, after firing a certain number of Ultra Convenient Quick Arrows within a short period of time, Linnea needs to take a temporary break before she can continue using them.

Linnea constellations

C1: Provisional Classification

When unleashing Elemental Skill Countermeasure: Lumi's Battle Cry!, or when triggering Moondrift Harmony, Linnea gains 6 stacks of the Field Catalog effect for 10s. Max 18 stacks. When nearby party members deal Lunar-Crystallize Reaction DMG, consume 1 stack of Field Catalog to increase the DMG dealt. The increase in DMG is equal to 75% of Linnea's DEF.
Additionally, when Lumi uses Million Ton Crush in her Ultimate Power Form, Linnea can consume up to 5 stacks of Field Catalog. Each stack will increase the DMG dealt by 150% of Linnea's DEF.

C2: Tidings of Joy and Sorrow

Within 8s after triggering Moondrift Harmony, all Hydro and Geo party members have their CRIT DMG increased by 40%. Additionally, when Lumi uses Million Ton Crush in her Ultimate Power Form, the CRIT DMG of that attack increases by an additional 150%.

Moonsign: Ascendant Gleam: When Lumi uses Heavy Overdrive Hammer in her Super Power Form, or when she uses Million Ton Crush in her Ultimate Power Form, an instance of Moondrift Harmony will be triggered. For this instance of Moondrift Harmony, all Hydro and Geo characters in the party will be considered to have applied their Elements in the reaction.

C3: Eventful Log Page

Increases the Level of Countermeasure: Lumi's Battle Cry! by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C4: Expert Instinct

Within 5s after Moondrift Harmony is triggered, increase the DEF of Linnea and your current active character by 25% respectively. When Linnea is on field, the DEF-increasing effect can be stacked.

C5: Fairyland's Farewell Gift

Elemental Burst Memo: Survival Guide in Extreme Conditions increases by 3 Levels.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C6: Golden Beagle's Dream

The effects of the Constellation, Provisional Classification, are enhanced: When unleashing Elemental Skill Countermeasure: Lumi's Battle Cry!, or when triggering Moondrift Harmony, Linnea will directly gain the maximum number of Field Catalog stacks. When consuming Field Catalog, consume twice the original number of stacks, such that the increase in DMG will be boosted to 150% of the original value.

Moonsign: Ascendant Gleam: Lunar-Crystallize Reaction DMG dealt by nearby party members is elevated by 25%.

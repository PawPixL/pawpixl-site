Linnea Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts & Teams

Linnea build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Fang of the Mountain King, A Thousand Blazing Suns), artifacts (Husk of Opulent Dreams), main stats…

Role: Sub DPS, Healer

Linnea is a DEF-scaling Geo bow support who deals off-field Lunar-Crystallize damage through Lumi, reduces Geo RES, and heals with her Burst. She can focus on personal damage or team buffs.

Best weapons for Linnea

  1. Fang of the Mountain King
  2. A Thousand Blazing Suns
  3. Serpent Spine
  4. Beacon of the Reed Sea
  5. A Teaspoon of Transcendence
  6. Earth Shaker
  7. Tidal Shadow
  8. "Ultimate Overlord's Mega Magic Sword"

Best artifacts

  • Husk of Opulent Dreams (4 pcs) · DEF / Geo
  • Aubade of Morningstar and Moon (4 pcs) · Lunar damage
  • Silken Moon's Serenade (4 pcs) · Team support

Main stats

  • Sands: DEF%
  • Goblet: DEF%
  • Circlet: CR / CD

Stat targets

  • DEF: 2,000+
  • CRIT: Balance ratio
  • ER: Only as needed

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 9+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 6–8 if healing

Best teams

  • Crater Expectations: Linnea, Zibai, Columbina, Illuga
  • Guns & Rosés: Linnea, Navia, Columbina, Furina
  • ONIFANS: Linnea, Illuga, Columbina, Zibai
  • Hopeless Impact: Linnea, Noelle, Aino, Illuga

Pros

  • Off-field Geo damage
  • Lunar-Crystallize buffs
  • Teamwide healing
  • Geo RES reduction

Cons

  • Niche team choices
  • Burst raises ER needs
  • Needs Lunar synergy

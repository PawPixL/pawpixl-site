Linnea Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

Linnea build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Golden Frostbound Oath, Aqua Simulacra), artifacts (Aubade of Morningstar and Moon), main stats.

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Role: Support / Sub DPS

Linnea is a Geo support specializing in Lunar Crystallize, boosting team damage and enabling powerful Lunar focused comps.

Best weapons for Linnea

  1. Golden Frostbound Oath: Provides 88.2% CRIT DMG and, at R1, grants 16% DEF, 40% Geo DMG, and 40% Lunar-Crystallize DMG to Linnea. When Moondrifts are nearby, teammates also gain 20% Geo and Lunar-Crystallize DMG. Linnea’s signature weapon and best-in-slot choice.
  2. Aqua Simulacra: Provides 88.2% CRIT DMG and, at R1, increases damage by 20% when enemies are nearby, even while Linnea is off-field. An excellent 5-star alternative for maximizing her personal damage, though it lacks the DEF and team buffs of her signature.
  3. Polar Star: Stacks ATK and DMG via Skill / Burst hits; gives CRIT Rate and rewards frequent off-field casts while…
  4. Slingshot: Provides 31.2% CRIT Rate and, at R5, grants 60% Normal and Charged Attack DMG when hits land within 0.3 seconds of firing. A budget option for Linnea, but its low Base ATK and conditional passive make it a considerable step down from her 5-star choices.
  5. The Stringless
  6. Favonius Warbow
  7. Covenant of Frost and Snow: Provides 51.7% DEF and, at R5, grants 240 Elemental Mastery for 12 seconds after using an Elemental Skill. A strong craftable option for Linnea, boosting her Lunar-Crystallize reaction damage, though it lacks the CRIT DMG and team buffs of her signature.
  8. Snare Hook

Best artifacts for Linnea

  • Aubade of Morningstar and Moon (4 pcs) · Main DPS: Provides 80 EM and increases the wearer’s Lunar Reaction DMG by 20% while off-field, rising to 60% with Ascendant Gleam. An excellent set for boosting Linnea’s personal Lunar-Crystallize damage. Main stats: DEF % · DEF % · CR/CD.
  • Silken Moon's Serenade (4 pcs) · Main DPS: Provides 20% Energy Recharge and, at 4-piece, grants the team up to 120 EM and a 10% Lunar Reaction DMG Bonus. Linnea can activate the effect off-field, making this a strong support option when no other teammate carries the set.
  • Husk of Opulent Dreams + Golden Troupe (4 pcs) · Main DPS: Provides 30% DEF and up to 24% additional DEF and 24% Geo DMG Bonus at max stacks. A strong option for Linnea, boosting both her DEF scaling and personal Geo damage, though Aubade is generally better for Lunar-Crystallize damage.

Main stats

  • Sands: DEF %
  • Goblet: DEF %
  • Circlet: Crit Rate / Crit DMG

Stat targets

  • DEF: 2000+
  • Crit Rate: 70–80%
  • Crit DMG: 150%+
  • Energy Recharge: 110% - 130%

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 9+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 9+

Best teams for Linnea

  • Crater Expectations: Linnea, Zibai, Columbina, Illuga. Zibai leads on-field Lunar-Crystallize damage, Columbina provides off-field Hydro and Lunar buffs, Linnea adds Geo RES shred, healing, and EM buffs, and Illuga boosts Geo and Lunar-Crystallize damage.
  • Guns & Rosés: Linnea, Navia, Columbina, Furina. Navia leads on-field Geo damage, Columbina provides Hydro application and Lunar reaction buffs, Linnea adds Geo RES shred, healing, and EM buffs, and Furina supplies off-field Hydro and a powerful teamwide DMG Bonus.
  • OniFans: Linnea, Arataki Itto, Columbina, Illuga. Itto leads on-field Geo damage, Linnea provides off-field damage, healing, and Geo RES shred, Columbina enables Lunar-Crystallize with Hydro application, and Illuga boosts Geo and Lunar-Crystallize damage.
  • Hopeless Impact: Linnea, Noelle, Aino, Illuga. Noelle leads on-field Geo damage and provides healing, Linnea adds off-field damage, Geo RES shred, and healing, Aino enables Hydro application and Lunar-Crystallize, and Illuga boosts Geo and Lunar reaction damage.

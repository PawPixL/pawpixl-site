Role: Support / Buffer

Prune is an off-field Anemo buffer whose converted hammer grants teammates Tolling Rally after a Swirl, providing up to 50% DMG Bonus based on her ATK above 2,000. Her Hexerei passive further boosts her ATK and Hexerei Anemo allies when two Hexerei units are present. Prioritize Burst uptime before ATK.

  2. Crane's Echoing Call

Main stats

  • Sands: Energy Recharge / ATK%

Stat targets

  • ATK (buff active): up to 4,000
  • ER C0 double Anemo: 185% - 205%
  • ER C1-C5 double Anemo: 150% - 170%
  • ER C0 solo Anemo: 245% - 285%
  • ER C6 double Anemo: 140% - 160%

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1
  • Elemental Skill: Level 1-6
  • Elemental Burst: Level 8-9

Best teams

  • Witchhunter Vanguard: Prune, Varka, Durin, Nicole
  • Witchwind Encore: Prune, Venti, Durin, Nicole
  • Chimes of Frost: Prune, Lohen, Durin, Nicole
  • Keqing-A-Ling: Prune, Keqing, Fischl, Kirara

Pros

  • Up to 50% team DMG Bonus
  • Strong Hexerei Anemo synergy
  • Can equip Viridescent
  • C6 grants flat ATK

Cons

  • High Energy needs at C0
  • Needs reliable Swirl aura
  • 4,000 ATK can be difficult
  • Limited personal damage

Build variations

  • Hexerei Anemo Buffer (Off-field Support): For Varka/Venti-style teams where Prune grants 30% ATK to a Hexerei Anemo ally on qualifying Swirl.
  • Electro VV Support (Off-field Buffer): For Keqing/Fischl and similar teams that value Electro RES shred and teamwide DMG Bonus.
  • Anemo Carry Artifact Flex (Off-field Buffer): Choose Celestial Gift when the primary damage is Anemo; use Noblesse if Celestial Gift already has a holder.
  • High-ER Solo Anemo (Off-field Support): When her high Burst cost prevents every-rotation uptime; lower ATK is preferable to skipping Burst.
  • TTDS Heavy-Buff Setup (Off-field Buffer): Niche setup that layers TTDS onto Tolling Rally only when its low base ATK does not meaningfully weaken her own buff.

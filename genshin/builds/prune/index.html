Prune Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams (7.2)

Prune build guide for Genshin Impact 7.2: best weapons (Angelos' Heptades, Crane's Echoing Call), artifacts (Viridescent Venerer), main stats.

Last updated: November 12, 2026 · Game version 7.2

Role: Support / Buffer

Prune is an off-field Anemo buffer whose converted hammer grants teammates Tolling Rally after a Swirl, providing up to 50% DMG Bonus based on her ATK above 2,000. Her Hexerei passive further boosts her ATK and Hexerei Anemo allies when two Hexerei units are present. Prioritize Burst uptime before ATK.

Best weapons for Prune

  1. Angelos' Heptades: Exceptional base ATK helps reach the 4,000 ATK Tolling Rally cap. Its other effects are secondary to the stat value. Premium ATK option; do not pull just for Prune.
  2. Crane's Echoing Call: High base ATK and ATK% help strengthen Tolling Rally; its Plunging Attack buff is mostly unused on typical Prune teams. Use if already owned; ER must be covered.
  3. Skyward Atlas: High base ATK plus ATK% makes reaching the 4,000 ATK buff cap easier without relying on niche passive triggers. Enough Energy Recharge from artifacts and team.
  4. Memory of Dust: ATK% stat and stackable ATK can improve Tolling Rally; gaining passive stacks needs field time, so do not assume maximum stacks. Lower value without time to build stacks.
  5. Oathsworn Eye: High base ATK and ATK% improve her buffs.
  6. Flowing Purity: Craftable high-base-ATK catalyst with ATK% for stronger Tolling Rally; its Skill damage passive is not why Prune uses it. Best when Energy Recharge is already sufficient.
  7. Hakushin Ring: Energy Recharge eases Burst requirements; Electro-related reactions may provide a brief elemental DMG buff in Electro teams. Electro team; Prune must trigger a qualifying reaction.
  8. Favonius Codex: Energy Recharge and CRIT-triggered neutral particles reduce steep Burst costs and help allies, at the expense of Prune's ATK buff. High-ER teams or teammates needing particles. Higher refinements improve consistency.

Best artifacts for Prune

  • Viridescent Venerer (4 pcs) · Best: Shreds 40% RES of the Swirled Pyro/Hydro/Electro/Cryo element when Prune triggers Swirl on-field; ideal for elemental carries. Main stats: ER/ATK% · ATK% · ATK% / CR (Fav).
  • Celestial Gift (4 pcs): Alternative for Anemo-focused Hexerei teams or when Prune cannot Swirl the primary carry's element; check for an existing set holder. Main stats: ER/ATK% · ATK% · ATK%.
  • Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs): After Burst, buffs party ATK by 20%. Main stats: ER/ATK% · ATK% · ATK%.

Main stats

  • Sands: Energy Recharge / ATK%
  • Goblet: ATK%
  • Circlet: ATK% / CRIT Rate (Favonius)

Stat targets

  • ATK (buff active): up to 4,000
  • ER C0 double Anemo: 185% - 205%
  • ER C1-C5 double Anemo: 150% - 170%
  • ER C0 solo Anemo: 245% - 285%
  • ER C6 double Anemo: 140% - 160%

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1
  • Elemental Skill: Level 1-6
  • Elemental Burst: Level 8-9

Best teams for Prune

  • Witchhunter Vanguard: Prune (Buffer), Varka (Main DPS), Durin (Sub DPS), Nicole (Support). Prune enables Varka's Hexerei Anemo bonuses and teamwide DMG buffs; Durin supplies elemental application and Nicole buffs Varka.
  • Witchwind Encore: Prune (Buffer), Venti (Main DPS), Durin (Sub DPS), Nicole (Support). Venti activates Hexerei Anemo synergy and helps fund Prune's Burst; Durin maintains a Swirlable aura. Use an appropriate set holder.
  • Chimes of Frost: Prune (Buffer), Lohen (Main DPS), Durin (Sub DPS), Nicole (Support). Prune grants Lohen teamwide DMG Bonus and supports Durin's off-field damage. Swirlable elemental application sustains Tolling Rally.
  • Keqing-A-Ling: Prune (Anemo Buffer), Keqing (On-field DPS), Fischl (Off-field DPS / Battery), Kirara (Shielder / Dendro Enabler). Accessible Aggravate team: Prune Swirls Electro for VV and buffs damage, Fischl supports Keqing, and Kirara supplies Dendro and shielding.

Pros

  • Up to 50% team DMG Bonus
  • Strong Hexerei Anemo synergy
  • Can equip Viridescent
  • C6 grants flat ATK

Cons

Build variations

Hexerei Anemo Buffer (Off-field Support)

For Varka/Venti-style teams where Prune grants 30% ATK to a Hexerei Anemo ally on qualifying Swirl.

  • Weapons: Angelos' Heptades, Oathsworn Eye, Skyward Atlas
  • Artifacts: Celestial Gift (4 pcs); Viridescent Venerer (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ER to Burst every rotation, then ATK; count Hexerei-triggered 60% ATK toward the 4,000 cap.
  • Teams: Two Hexerei characters, an Anemo Hexerei carry and consistent Swirlable aura.
  • When it applies: Hexerei homework completed; two Hexerei in party.
  • Notes: 4pc VV still suits a team with important Pyro/Hydro/Electro/Cryo damage.

Electro VV Support (Off-field Buffer)

For Keqing/Fischl and similar teams that value Electro RES shred and teamwide DMG Bonus.

  • Weapons: Hakushin Ring, Oathsworn Eye, Favonius Codex
  • Artifacts: Viridescent Venerer (4 pcs); Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ER first; ATK% afterward. CRIT Rate only if using Favonius.
  • Teams: Electro aura available for on-field Swirl; Electro damage dealers.
  • When it applies: Hakushin Ring requires Prune to trigger an Electro-related reaction.
  • Notes: Cast or hit with Anemo while on-field to activate VV RES shred.

Anemo Carry Artifact Flex (Off-field Buffer)

Choose Celestial Gift when the primary damage is Anemo; use Noblesse if Celestial Gift already has a holder.

  • Weapons: Skyward Atlas, Crane's Echoing Call, Flowing Purity
  • Artifacts: Celestial Gift (4 pcs); Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Use ATK/ATK/ATK if ER met; ER Sands otherwise.
  • Teams: A primarily Anemo DPS; another character can already hold VV or Celestial Gift.
  • When it applies: Do not duplicate team-wide artifact buffs unnecessarily.
  • Notes: 4pc Celestial Gift is conditional; check team artifact ownership.

High-ER Solo Anemo (Off-field Support)

When her high Burst cost prevents every-rotation uptime; lower ATK is preferable to skipping Burst.

  • Weapons: Favonius Codex, Oathsworn Eye, Hakushin Ring
  • Artifacts: Viridescent Venerer (4 pcs); Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs)
  • Stats: At C0 solo Anemo, start around 245-285% ER before weapon/particle reductions.
  • Teams: Solo Anemo team with a reliable Swirlable aura; Favonius helps energy.
  • When it applies: C1 and Favonius lower Energy needs substantially.
  • Notes: Aim for CRIT Rate if using Favonius; team ER needs vary.

TTDS Heavy-Buff Setup (Off-field Buffer)

Niche setup that layers TTDS onto Tolling Rally only when its low base ATK does not meaningfully weaken her own buff.

  • Weapons: Thrilling Tales of Dragon Slayers, Skyward Atlas, Flowing Purity
  • Artifacts: Viridescent Venerer (4 pcs); Celestial Gift (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Requires strong external ATK buffs; verify Prune approaches 4,000 ATK when Tolling Rally applies.
  • Teams: ATK-scaling on-field carry plus enough ATK boosts for Prune; swap order matters.
  • When it applies: Use TTDS only if A4 buff remains near cap; R5 advised.
  • Notes: TTDS is generally worse without exceptional external ATK.

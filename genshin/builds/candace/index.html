Candace Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

Candace build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Engulfing Lightning, Favonius Lance), artifacts (Noblesse Oblige), main stats, talent priority.

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Role: Support / Buffer

Candace is a Hydro support who buffs Elemental Normal Attacks and grants Hydro Infusion to Sword, Claymore, and Polearm allies. Her Burst enables creative Bloom and Normal Attack teams, while C6 adds off-field Hydro waves. Prioritize enough Energy Recharge to use her Burst reliably.

Best weapons for Candace

  1. Engulfing Lightning: Generates teamwide particles on CRIT and helps meet her demanding Burst Energy needs. Needs CRIT Rate and an on-field CRIT hit.
  2. Favonius Lance: Very high ER helps sustain her Burst.
  3. Rightful Reward: HP% supports Candace’s buff.
  4. Kitain Cross Spear: Energy restoration and EM help her Bloom setup, especially with refinements. Best for Bloom-focused builds or when the Skill passive fits rotations. R5 improves Energy refund.
  5. Dragon's Bane: Large EM stat boosts Bloom damage when Candace herself triggers Dendro Cores. Bloom variation only; not a general HP/ER support weapon.
  6. Prototype Starglitter: Craftable Energy Recharge stat stick that supports consistent Bursts. Normal/Charged Attack passive rarely matters off-field.
  7. Black Tassel: Large HP% stat increases Candace’s HP-based Normal Attack buff. Only after meeting Energy Recharge needs from artifacts and team.

Best artifacts for Candace

  • Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs) · General: Burst activation grants the party ATK. Main stats: ER/HP% · HP% · HP%/CR.
  • Scroll of the Hero of Cinder City (4 pcs): Provides reaction-based Elemental DMG buffs. Main stats: ER/HP% · HP% · HP%/CR.
  • Emblem of Severed Fate (4 pcs): Increases ER and Burst DMG. Main stats: ER · HP% · HP%/CR.

Main stats

  • Sands: Energy Recharge / HP%
  • Goblet: HP% / Hydro DMG
  • Circlet: HP% / Crit Rate

Stat targets

  • Energy Recharge: 180% - 200% (2 Hydro, 2 Skills)
  • Energy Recharge: 230% - 260% (solo Hydro, 2 Skills)
  • Energy Recharge: 260% - 280% (solo Hydro, 1 Skill)
  • CRIT Rate: 40% - 60% (Favonius, practical target)
  • HP: 30,000 - 40,000+ (after ER needs)

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 8-9
  • Elemental Skill: Level 8-9
  • Elemental Burst: Level 8-9

Best teams for Candace

  • Bloom Oasis: Candace, Nilou, Yaoyao, Traveler (Dendro). Candace infuses Yaoyao or Dendro Traveler with Hydro to create Bountiful Cores. Give Bloom trigger characters EM; C6 adds Hydro waves.
  • Hyperbloom Guard: Candace, Kuki Shinobu, Nahida, Fischl. Candace grants Kuki Hydro-infused attacks to make Cores, while Kuki triggers Hyperbloom. Nahida supplies Dendro; Fischl adds damage.
  • Crimson Tide: Candace, Arlecchino, Bennett, Kaedehara Kazuha. C6 Candace supplies AoE Hydro waves for Arlecchino Vaporize; Bennett buffs and Kazuha groups and reduces resistance. Requires C6.
  • Frozen Crown: Candace, Skirk, Furina, Escoffier. Candace buffs Skirk’s Elemental Normal Attacks and adds Hydro Resonance. Furina boosts damage; Escoffier shreds resistance and heals.

Pros

  • Hydro infusion utility
  • Normal Attack buffs
  • Flexible reaction teams
  • Accessible equipment

Cons

  • High Energy needs
  • Low personal damage
  • C6 improves Hydro uptime
  • Limited infusion demand

Build variations

Nilou Bloom Trigger (Bloom DPS)

Use when Candace owns meaningful Bloom reactions, including her swap waves and C6. Other Hydro-infused teammates use their own EM.

  • Weapons: Dragon's Bane, Kitain Cross Spear, Favonius Lance
  • Artifacts: Flower of Paradise Lost (4 pcs); Gilded Dreams (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ER/EM Sands, EM Goblet, EM Circlet; level 90 is important for Blooms.
  • Teams: Nilou plus Dendro, with Candace actually triggering Dendro Cores.
  • When it applies: Only Candace-owned Blooms scale with her EM.

C6 Arlecchino Vaporize (Support)

C6 Hydro waves add application for Arlecchino to Vaporize in clustered fights.

  • Weapons: Favonius Lance, Engulfing Lightning, Rightful Reward
  • Artifacts: Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs); Scroll of the Hero of Cinder City (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Prioritize Burst uptime, then HP%; Favonius needs some CRIT Rate.
  • Teams: Arlecchino, a buffer, and an Anemo or Hydro-compatible support.
  • When it applies: Requires Candace C6.
  • Notes: Avoid presenting this as a reliable solo-Hydro Vaporize setup before C6.

Hyperbloom Infusion (Support)

Candace creates Hydro access; an Electro character triggers the Hyperblooms and needs EM.

  • Weapons: Favonius Lance, Prototype Starglitter, Black Tassel
  • Artifacts: Instructor (4 pcs); Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ER first, then HP%; do not stack EM for Candace.
  • Teams: An on-field melee Hydro-infused attacker, Dendro applier and EM Electro trigger.
  • When it applies: Instructor requires Candace to trigger an on-field reaction.

C6 Burst Damage (Sub DPS)

Only for investment-focused players seeking more personal C6 Hydro damage.

  • Weapons: Engulfing Lightning, Staff of Homa, Favonius Lance
  • Artifacts: Emblem of Severed Fate (4 pcs); Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Meet ER, then HP%, Hydro DMG and CRIT; her waves scale with HP.
  • Teams: Another on-field character whose Elemental Normal Attacks activate C6.
  • When it applies: Candace C6; her own attacks cannot trigger C6.

Accessible ER Support (Support)

Use obtainable weapons and mixed ER pieces while farming better support equipment.

  • Weapons: Prototype Starglitter, Rightful Reward, Black Tassel
  • Artifacts: Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs); Emblem of Severed Fate + Scroll of the Hero of Cinder City (2 + 2 pcs)
  • Stats: ER Sands as needed; HP% Goblet/Circlet once Burst is consistent.
  • Teams: Best with a second Hydro, enough team particles, or a healer for Rightful Reward.
  • When it applies: 2pc Scroll ER effect needs a Natlan teammate.

Role: Support / Buffer

Candace is a Hydro support who buffs Elemental Normal Attacks and grants Hydro Infusion to Sword, Claymore, and Polearm allies. Her Burst enables creative Bloom and Normal Attack teams, while C6 adds off-field Hydro waves. Prioritize enough Energy Recharge to use her Burst reliably.

Best weapons for Candace

  1. Engulfing Lightning
  2. Favonius Lance
  3. Rightful Reward
  4. Kitain Cross Spear
  5. Dragon's Bane
  6. Prototype Starglitter
  7. Black Tassel

Best artifacts

  • Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs) · General
  • Scroll of the Hero of Cinder City (4 pcs)
  • Emblem of Severed Fate (4 pcs)

Main stats

  • Sands: Energy Recharge / HP%
  • Goblet: HP% / Hydro DMG
  • Circlet: HP% / Crit Rate

Stat targets

  • Energy Recharge: 180% - 200% (2 Hydro, 2 Skills)
  • Energy Recharge: 230% - 260% (solo Hydro, 2 Skills)
  • Energy Recharge: 260% - 280% (solo Hydro, 1 Skill)
  • CRIT Rate: 40% - 60% (Favonius, practical target)
  • HP: 30,000 - 40,000+ (after ER needs)

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 8-9
  • Elemental Skill: Level 8-9
  • Elemental Burst: Level 8-9

Best teams

  • Bloom Oasis: Candace, Nilou, Yaoyao, Traveler (Dendro)
  • Hyperbloom Guard: Candace, Kuki Shinobu, Nahida, Fischl
  • Crimson Tide: Candace, Arlecchino, Bennett, Kaedehara Kazuha
  • Frozen Crown: Candace, Skirk, Furina, Escoffier

Pros

  • Hydro infusion utility
  • Normal Attack buffs
  • Flexible reaction teams
  • Accessible equipment

Cons

  • High Energy needs
  • Low personal damage
  • C6 improves Hydro uptime
  • Limited infusion demand

Build variations

  • Nilou Bloom Trigger (Bloom DPS): Use when Candace owns meaningful Bloom reactions, including her swap waves and C6. Other Hydro-infused teammates use their own EM.
  • C6 Arlecchino Vaporize (Support): C6 Hydro waves add application for Arlecchino to Vaporize in clustered fights.
  • Hyperbloom Infusion (Support): Candace creates Hydro access; an Electro character triggers the Hyperblooms and needs EM.
  • C6 Burst Damage (Sub DPS): Only for investment-focused players seeking more personal C6 Hydro damage.
  • Accessible ER Support (Support): Use obtainable weapons and mixed ER pieces while farming better support equipment.

Diluc Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams (7.2)

Diluc build guide for Genshin Impact 7.2: best weapons (Wolf's Gravestone, Beacon of the Reed Sea), artifacts (Crimson Witch of Flames), main stats.

Last updated: November 12, 2026 · Game version 7.2

Role: DPS

Diluc is an on-field Pyro claymore DPS with flexible Vaporize and Melt teams. He can also use Xianyun’s plunge support for strong plunging attacks. Build ATK, Pyro damage, and CRIT, with EM when reactions are reliable.

Best weapons for Diluc

  1. Wolf's Gravestone: High ATK and a conditional team ATK buff. A strong general option.
  2. Beacon of the Reed Sea: CR and ATK after taking damage or using Skill. Excellent damage stat stick for Diluc. Shielding may limit part of its passive.
  3. Redhorn Stonethresher: Large CD stat stick. Its DEF-based Normal Attack passive is weak on Diluc; use for CRIT stats.
  4. Serpent Spine: BP CR and stacking damage with field time. Protect stacks from hits. Stronger at high refinements.
  5. Blackcliff Slasher: Provides CRIT DMG and stacking ATK after defeating enemies. Best against waves rather than single bosses.
  6. Rainslasher: EM and damage against Hydro-affected enemies; strong in Vaporize. Requires Hydro uptime.
  7. Prototype Archaic: Craftable ATK claymore with occasional bonus Physical damage. Solid budget fallback.
  8. Mailed Flower: Gives Elemental Mastery and grants ATK and more EM after using his Skill or triggering a reaction. Good for Vaporize teams.

Best artifacts for Diluc

  • Crimson Witch of Flames (4 pcs) · Main DPS: Strong general set for Pyro reaction damage and Skill-based rotations. Main stats: ATK%/EM · Pyro · CR/CD.
  • Marechaussee Hunter (4 pcs) · Main DPS: CRIT alternative when Furina enables consistent HP changes. Main stats: ATK%/EM · Pyro · CD.
  • Gilded Dreams (4 pcs) · Main DPS: EM-focused reaction alternative, generally weaker than Crimson Witch. Main stats: EM/ATK% · Pyro · CR/CD.

Main stats

  • Sands: ATK% / EM
  • Goblet: Pyro DMG
  • Circlet: CR / CD

Stat targets

  • CR: 65–80%
  • CD: 140–200%+
  • ATK: 1,800–2,400
  • EM: Vape/Melt only

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 9+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 9+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 9+

Best teams for Diluc

  • Melt Force One: Diluc (DPS), Xianyun (Support), Citlali (Support), Bennett (Support). Xianyun enables plunging attacks while Citlali shields and supplies Cryo for Melt. Bennett provides healing and ATK.
  • Splash Landing: Diluc (DPS), Xianyun (Support), Furina (Support), Bennett (Support). Xianyun turns Diluc’s attacks into plunges, Furina supplies Hydro and damage buffs, and Bennett boosts ATK and heals.
  • Dawn & Tonic: Diluc (DPS), Kaedehara Kazuha (Support), Xianyun (Support), Bennett (Support). Xianyun enables plunges while Kazuha groups and shreds Pyro resistance. Bennett adds ATK and healing; this focuses on raw Pyro damage.
  • F2P-Wan Kenobi: Diluc (DPS), Sucrose (Support), Xingqiu (Support), Bennett (Support). Xingqiu enables Vaporize, Sucrose boosts EM and reduces Pyro resistance, and Bennett heals and increases ATK.

Cons

Build variations

Vaporize DPS (On-field DPS)

A flexible reaction build that amplifies Diluc’s Skill and infused attacks.

  • Weapons: Wolf's Gravestone, Rainslasher, Serpent Spine
  • Artifacts: Crimson Witch of Flames (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ATK% or EM / Pyro / CRIT.
  • Teams: Reliable Hydro such as Xingqiu.
  • When it applies: EM has value only with dependable reactions

Furina Plunge (On-field DPS)

Use high-multiplier plunges and Furina’s HP changes to enable a CRIT-focused set.

  • Weapons: Serpent Spine, Wolf's Gravestone, Redhorn Stonethresher
  • Artifacts: Marechaussee Hunter (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ATK% or EM / Pyro / CD; avoid CR overcap.
  • Teams: Xianyun plunge support and Furina with healing.
  • When it applies: Requires reliable HP fluctuation

Melt Plunge (On-field DPS)

Focus on Melt-enhanced plunges with Citlali or other Cryo enablers.

  • Weapons: Wolf's Gravestone, Serpent Spine, Mailed Flower
  • Artifacts: Crimson Witch of Flames (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ATK% or EM / Pyro / CRIT.
  • Teams: Xianyun and sufficient Cryo application.
  • When it applies: Melt frequency depends on Cryo uptime

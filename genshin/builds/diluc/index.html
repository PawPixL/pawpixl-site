Role: DPS

Diluc is an on-field Pyro claymore DPS with flexible Vaporize and Melt teams. He can also use Xianyun’s plunge support for strong plunging attacks. Build ATK, Pyro damage, and CRIT, with EM when reactions are reliable.

Best weapons for Diluc

  1. Wolf's Gravestone
  2. Beacon of the Reed Sea
  3. Redhorn Stonethresher
  4. Serpent Spine
  5. Blackcliff Slasher
  6. Rainslasher
  7. Prototype Archaic
  8. Mailed Flower

Best artifacts

  • Crimson Witch of Flames (4 pcs) · Main DPS
  • Marechaussee Hunter (4 pcs) · Main DPS
  • Gilded Dreams (4 pcs) · Main DPS

Main stats

  • Sands: ATK% / EM
  • Goblet: Pyro DMG
  • Circlet: CR / CD

Stat targets

  • CR: 65–80%
  • CD: 140–200%+
  • ATK: 1,800–2,400
  • EM: Vape/Melt only

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 9+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 9+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 9+

Best teams

  • Melt Force One: Diluc, Xianyun, Citlali, Bennett
  • Splash Landing: Diluc, Xianyun, Furina, Bennett
  • Dawn & Tonic: Diluc, Kaedehara Kazuha, Xianyun, Bennett
  • F2P-Wan Kenobi: Diluc, Sucrose, Xingqiu, Bennett

Pros

  • Easy to play
  • Flexible combos
  • Strong reactions
  • Low-cost Burst

Cons

  • Lower damage ceiling
  • Wants reactions
  • Support reliant
  • Plunge wants Xianyun

Build variations

  • Vaporize DPS (On-field DPS): A flexible reaction build that amplifies Diluc’s Skill and infused attacks.
  • Furina Plunge (On-field DPS): Use high-multiplier plunges and Furina’s HP changes to enable a CRIT-focused set.
  • Melt Plunge (On-field DPS): Focus on Melt-enhanced plunges with Citlali or other Cryo enablers.

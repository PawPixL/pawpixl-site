Diluc Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts & Teams

Diluc build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Wolf's Gravestone, Gest of the Mighty Wolf), artifacts (Crimson Witch of Flames), main stats…

Role: DPS

Diluc is a 5-star Pyro Claymore DPS with straightforward combos and strong reaction damage. His flexible Skill and low-cost Burst make him easy to play, especially in Vaporize and Plunge teams.

  2. Gest of the Mighty Wolf
  3. Redhorn Stonethresher
  4. Serpent Spine
  5. Blackcliff Slasher
  6. Rainslasher
  7. Prototype Archaic
  8. Mailed Flower

  • Marechaussee Hunter (4 pcs) · Main DPS
  • Gilded Dreams (4 pcs) · Main DPS

Talent priority

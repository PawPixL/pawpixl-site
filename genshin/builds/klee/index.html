Klee Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

Klee build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Reliquary of Truth, Cashflow Supervision), artifacts (A Day Carved From Rising Winds), main stats.

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Role: Main DPS

Klee is an on-field Pyro DPS who floods enemies with explosive attacks. Complete her Witch’s Homework and pair her with another Hexerei character to unlock powerful Spark-enhanced attacks. Overload and Furina teams are excellent, while her C2 also offers DEF shred support.

Best weapons for Klee

  1. Reliquary of Truth: Best general-purpose premium choice. CRIT stats and EM work especially well in reaction teams.
  2. Cashflow Supervision: Excellent in Furina teams. HP changes activate Normal and Charged Attack bonuses, plus ATK Speed.
  3. Tome of the Eternal Flow: A premium Furina option with high CD, Charged Attack buffs, and Energy recovery. Requires HP fluctuations.
  4. The Widsith: Excellent four-star DMG with CD and powerful temporary buffs. Performance varies with the random buff and cooldown.
  5. Dawning Frost: Strong reaction-focused option with a reliable EM bonus when Bennett is on the team. Best with Bennett in reaction teams.
  6. Solar Pearl: CRIT Rate and Normal Attack, Skill, and Burst damage support her on-field rotation. Charged Attacks are not directly buffed.
  7. Flowing Purity: Craftable ATK and Elemental DMG option. A healer must clear its Bond of Life to unlock the full bonus. Use a reliable healer.
  8. Dodoco Tales: Themed event catalyst that improves Charged Attacks after Normal Attacks. Useful if owned, but weaker than modern options. Past limited-time event weapon.

Best artifacts for Klee

  • A Day Carved From Rising Winds (4 pcs): Best all-around set after completing Witch’s Homework. Strong ATK and CRIT Rate bonuses reward her upgraded kit. Main stats: ATK% / Pyro / CRIT.
  • Crimson Witch of Flames (4 pcs): Good reaction-focused alternative that buffs Vaporize and Overload damage and benefits from her Skill use. Main stats: ATK% / Pyro / CRIT.
  • Marechaussee Hunter (4 pcs): Strong in Furina teams as HP fluctuations grant CR. Main stats: ATK% / Pyro / CRIT.

Main stats

  • Sands: ATK%
  • Goblet: Pyro DMG Bonus / ATK%
  • Circlet: CRIT Rate / CRIT DMG

Stat targets

  • ER: 125% - 140% (double Pyro)
  • ER: 140% - 160% (solo Pyro)
  • CRIT Rate: 60% - 75% before set buffs
  • CRIT DMG: 140% - 200%
  • ATK: 1,800 - 2,200 before buffs

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 9+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 8–9
  • Elemental Burst: Level 8–9

Best teams for Klee

  • Kloverload Royalty: Klee (On-field DPS), Chevreuse (Healer / Buffer), Durin (Off-field DPS / Buffer), Fischl (Off-field DPS / Battery). Klee drives Overload while Chevreuse buffs and heals. Durin shreds resistance, and Fischl supplies Electro and Hexerei synergy.
  • Explosive Encore: Klee (On-field DPS), Furina (Off-field DPS / Buffer), Fischl (Off-field DPS / Battery), Bennett (Healer / Buffer). Furina adds off-field Hydro damage, Fischl activates Hexerei and boosts damage, and Bennett heals and buffs Klee.
  • Burning Playdate: Klee (On-field DPS), Emilie (Off-field DPS), Durin (Off-field DPS / Buffer), Bennett (Healer / Buffer). Klee maintains Burning for Emilie. Durin provides Pyro support and Hexerei synergy, while Bennett heals and buffs.
  • Fish Blasting: Klee (On-field DPS), Bennett (Healer / Buffer), Xiangling (Off-field DPS / Pyro Enabler), Sucrose (EM Buffer / Debuffer). Bennett buffs and heals, Xiangling adds Pyro damage, and Sucrose groups foes and activates Hexerei after Witch’s Homework.

Pros

  • Strong Hexerei upgrades
  • Great team Energy support
  • Versatile team options

Cons

  • Needs Hexerei teammate

  • Burst needs consistent ER
  • Overload scatters enemies

Build variations

Hexerei On-field DPS (On-field DPS)

Use her updated synergy for high personal Pyro damage.

  • Weapons: Reliquary of Truth, Cashflow Supervision, The Widsith
  • Artifacts: A Day Carved From Rising Winds (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Balance CRIT and ATK; EM with frequent reactions.
  • Teams: Qualifying Hexerei team and sustained field time.
  • When it applies: Damage depends on relevant Hexerei conditions.

Furina Vape Carry (On-field DPS)

Furina enables an HP-change artifact route for Klee.

  • Weapons: Cashflow Supervision, The Widsith
  • Artifacts: Marechaussee Hunter (4 pcs); Crimson Witch of Flames (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Account for Marechaussee CRIT Rate; EM helps if Vaporizing.
  • Teams: Furina and strong team healing.
  • When it applies: Vaporize consistency varies with Hydro application.

C2 Resistance Support (Support)

Quickswap Klee can provide utility rather than occupying long field time.

  • Weapons: The Widsith, Solar Pearl
  • Artifacts: Crimson Witch of Flames (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Build enough ER and utility; personal damage secondary.
  • Teams: Requires C2; use a team that benefits from DEF reduction.
  • When it applies: C2 required for DEF reduction.

Klee talents

Normal Attack: Kaboom!

Normal Attack
Throws things that go boom when they hit things! Performs up to 3 explosive attacks, dealing AoE Pyro DMG.

Charged Attack
Consumes a certain amount of Stamina and deals AoE Pyro DMG to opponents after a short casting time.

Plunging Attack
Gathering the power of Pyro, Klee plunges towards the ground from mid-air, damaging all opponents in her path. Deals AoE Pyro DMG upon impact with the ground.

Elemental Skill: Jumpy Dumpty

Cooldown: 20s

Jumpy Dumpty is tons of boom-bang-fun!
When thrown, Jumpy Dumpty bounces thrice, igniting and dealing AoE Pyro DMG with every bounce.

On the third bounce, the bomb splits into many mines.
The mines will explode upon contact with opponents, or after a short period of time, dealing AoE Pyro DMG.

Starts with 2 charges.

Elemental Burst: Sparks 'n' Splash

Cooldown: 15s · Energy cost: 60

Klee's Blazing Delight! For the duration of this ability, continuously summons Sparks 'n' Splash to attack nearby opponents, dealing AoE Pyro DMG.

Passive talent: Pounding Surprise

When Jumpy Dumpty and Normal Attacks deal DMG, Klee has a 50% chance to obtain an Explosive Spark.
This Explosive Spark is consumed by the next Charged Attack, which costs no Stamina and deals 50% increased DMG.

Passive talent: Sparkling Burst

When Klee's Charged Attack results in a CRIT Hit, all party members gain 2 Elemental Energy.

Passive talent: All Of My Treasures!

Displays the location of nearby resources unique to Mondstadt on the mini-map.

Klee constellations

C1: Chained Reactions

Attacks and Skills have a certain chance to summon sparks that bombard opponents, dealing DMG equal to 120% of Sparks 'n' Splash's DMG.

C2: Explosive Frags

Being hit by Jumpy Dumpty's mines decreases opponents' DEF by 23% for 10s.

C3: Exquisite Compound

Increases the Level of Jumpy Dumpty by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C4: Sparkly Explosion

If Klee leaves the field during the duration of Sparks 'n' Splash, her departure triggers an explosion that deals 555% of her ATK as AoE Pyro DMG.

C5: Nova Burst

Increases the Level of Sparks 'n' Splash by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C6: Blazing Delight

While under the effects of Sparks 'n' Splash, Klee will regenerate 3 Energy for all members of the party (excluding Klee) every 3s.
When Sparks 'n' Splash is used, all party members will gain a 10% Pyro DMG Bonus for 25s.

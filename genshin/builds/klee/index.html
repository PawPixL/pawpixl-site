Klee Build Guide | Best Weapons, Artifacts & Teams

Klee build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Reliquary of Truth, Cashflow Supervision), artifacts (A Day Carved From Rising Winds), main stats (ATK%…

Role: Main DPS

Klee is an on-field Pyro DPS who floods enemies with explosive attacks. Complete her Witch’s Homework and pair her with another Hexerei character to unlock powerful Spark-enhanced attacks. Overload and Furina teams are excellent, while her C2 also offers DEF shred support.

Best weapons for Klee

  1. Reliquary of Truth
  2. Cashflow Supervision
  3. Tome of the Eternal Flow
  4. The Widsith
  5. Dawning Frost
  6. Solar Pearl
  7. Flowing Purity
  8. Dodoco Tales

Best artifacts

  • A Day Carved From Rising Winds (4 pcs)
  • Crimson Witch of Flames (4 pcs)
  • Marechaussee Hunter (4 pcs)

Main stats

  • Sands: ATK%
  • Goblet: Pyro DMG Bonus / ATK%
  • Circlet: CRIT Rate / CRIT DMG

Stat targets

  • ER: 125% - 140% (double Pyro)
  • ER: 140% - 160% (solo Pyro)
  • CRIT Rate: 60% - 75% before set buffs
  • CRIT DMG: 140% - 200%
  • ATK: 1,800 - 2,200 before buffs

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 9+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 8–9
  • Elemental Burst: Level 8–9

Best teams

  • Kloverload Royalty: Klee, Chevreuse, Durin, Fischl
  • Explosive Encore: Klee, Furina, Fischl, Bennett
  • Burning Playdate: Klee, Emilie, Durin, Bennett
  • Fish Blasting: Klee, Bennett, Xiangling, Sucrose

Pros

  • Powerful Pyro damage
  • Strong Hexerei upgrades
  • Great team Energy support
  • Versatile team options

Cons

  • Needs Hexerei teammate
  • Can be interrupted
  • Burst needs consistent ER
  • Overload scatters enemies

Build variations

  • Hexerei On-field DPS (On-field DPS): Use her updated synergy for high personal Pyro damage.
  • Furina Vape Carry (On-field DPS): Furina enables an HP-change artifact route for Klee.
  • C2 Resistance Support (Support): Quickswap Klee can provide utility rather than occupying long field time.

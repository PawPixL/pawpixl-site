Role: Support / ATK Buffer

Bennett is a powerful support who provides massive ATK buffs, reliable healing, and Pyro application. He's one of the most versatile supports in the game.

Best weapons for Bennett

  1. Mistsplitter Reforged
  2. Aquila Favonia
  3. Skyward Blade
  4. Sacrificial Sword
  5. Favonius Sword
  6. Sapwood Blade
  7. Blackcliff Longsword
  8. Fleuve Cendre Ferryman

Best artifacts

  • Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs) · Support
  • Emblem of Severed Fate (4 pcs) · Support
  • Scroll of the Hero of Cinder City (4 pcs) · Support

Main stats

  • Sands: Energy Recharge
  • Goblet: HP%
  • Circlet: Healing Bonus or HP%

Stat targets

  • Energy Recharge: 200% ~ 230%+
  • CRIT Rate: 50 ~ 60%
  • CRIT DMG: 150%+

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 9+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 9+

Best teams

  • Phoenix Dive: Bennett, Gaming, Furina, Xianyun
  • National Overload: Bennett, Raiden Shogun, Kujou Sara, Kaedehara Kazuha
  • Crimson Reign: Bennett, Arlecchino, Kaedehara Kazuha, Yelan
  • Crimson Tide: Bennett, Tartaglia, Xiangling, Kaedehara Kazuha

Pros

  • Massive ATK buff.
  • Strong healing.
  • Great energy generation.
  • Highly versatile.

Cons

  • Burst circle restricted.
  • Healing limited to below 70% HP.
  • Less effective for mobile teams.

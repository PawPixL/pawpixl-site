Bennett Build Guide | Best Weapons, Artifacts & Teams

Bennett build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Mistsplitter Reforged, Aquila Favonia), artifacts (Noblesse Oblige), main stats (ER / HP% / Healing…

Role: Support / ATK Buffer

Bennett is a powerful support who provides massive ATK buffs, reliable healing, and Pyro application. He's one of the most versatile supports in the game.

Best weapons for Bennett

  1. Mistsplitter Reforged
  2. Aquila Favonia
  3. Skyward Blade
  4. Sacrificial Sword
  5. Favonius Sword
  6. Sapwood Blade
  7. Blackcliff Longsword
  8. Fleuve Cendre Ferryman

Best artifacts

  • Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs) · Support
  • Emblem of Severed Fate (4 pcs) · Personal Burst Damage
  • Scroll of the Hero of Cinder City (4 pcs) · Support

Main stats

  • Sands: ER
  • Goblet: HP%
  • Circlet: Healing / HP%

Stat targets

  • ER: 200–230%+
  • HP: For healing
  • CR: Favonius only

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 6–8
  • Elemental Burst: Level 9+

Best teams

  • Phoenix Dive: Bennett, Gaming, Furina, Xianyun
  • Raiden Hypercarry: Bennett, Raiden Shogun, Kujou Sara, Kaedehara Kazuha
  • Crimson Reign: Bennett, Arlecchino, Kaedehara Kazuha, Yelan
  • Crimson Tide: Bennett, Tartaglia, Xiangling, Kaedehara Kazuha

Pros

  • Huge ATK buff
  • Strong healing
  • Energy generation
  • Versatile support

Cons

  • Circle-bound Burst
  • Healing has HP limit
  • Mobile teams suffer

Build variations

  • Standard Burst Buffer (Support / Healer): Use for his strongest general team ATK buffs and reliable healing.
  • F2P Battery Support (Support / Healer): Choose Sapwood for stronger base-ATK buffs or Favonius when team energy matters more.
  • Personal Pyro Damage (On-field / Burst DPS): A niche build for players using Bennett for personal Pyro damage, not just his team buff.

Bennett Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

Bennett build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Mistsplitter Reforged, Aquila Favonia), artifacts (Noblesse Oblige), main stats, talent priority.

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Role: Support / ATK Buffer

Bennett is a powerful support who provides massive ATK buffs, reliable healing, and Pyro application. He's one of the most versatile supports in the game.

Best weapons for Bennett

  1. Mistsplitter Reforged: 674 Base ATK maximizes his Burst buff; CRIT DMG benefits personal damage.
  2. Aquila Favonia: 674 Base ATK maximizes his Burst buff, although the Physical stat adds little.
  3. Skyward Blade: Strong Base ATK with ER helps maintain his Burst.
  4. Sacrificial Sword: ER and Skill reset; lower Base ATK weakens his Burst buff. Only if extra energy matters more than buff strength.
  5. Favonius Sword: Team particles and ER help battery the party at the cost of a smaller ATK buff. Enough CR to trigger passive.
  6. Sapwood Blade: Craftable sword with good Base ATK and ER. Excellent accessible support choice.
  7. Blackcliff Longsword: 565 Base ATK; CRIT DMG suits personal damage more than support.
  8. Fleuve Cendre Ferryman: Free ER option; lower Base ATK and little team-buff benefit.

Best artifacts for Bennett

  • Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs) · Support: 20% team ATK after Burst; his best general support set. Main stats: ER · HP% · Healing Bonus or HP%.
  • Emblem of Severed Fate (4 pcs) · Personal Burst Damage: ER and personal Burst DMG; use when Noblesse is covered. Main stats: ER/ATK% · Pyro DMG · CR/CD.
  • Scroll of the Hero of Cinder City (4 pcs) · Support: Minor elemental buff without Nightsoul; generally weaker than Noblesse. Main stats: ATK%/ER · Pyro DMG · CR/CD.

Main stats

  • Sands: ER
  • Goblet: HP%
  • Circlet: Healing / HP%

Stat targets

  • ER: 200–230%+
  • HP: For healing
  • CR: Favonius only

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 6–8
  • Elemental Burst: Level 9+

Best teams for Bennett

  • Phoenix Dive: Bennett, Gaming, Furina, Xianyun. Gaming delivers powerful Pyro plunging attacks, supported by Bennett's ATK buff, Furina's damage bonus, and Xianyun's plunging support and healing.
  • Raiden Hypercarry: Bennett, Raiden Shogun, Kujou Sara, Kaedehara Kazuha. Raiden Shogun deals on-field Electro damage while Sara and Bennett provide ATK buffs.
  • Crimson Reign: Bennett, Arlecchino, Kaedehara Kazuha, Yelan. Bennett boosts Arlecchino's ATK while Yelan provides off-field Hydro damage for Vaporize.
  • Crimson Tide: Bennett, Tartaglia, Xiangling, Kaedehara Kazuha. Tartaglia enables powerful Vaporize reactions with Xiangling's Pyronado.

Pros

  • Huge ATK buff
  • Strong healing
  • Energy generation
  • Versatile support

Cons

  • Circle-bound Burst
  • Healing has HP limit
  • Mobile teams suffer

Build variations

Standard Burst Buffer (Support / Healer)

Use for his strongest general team ATK buffs and reliable healing.

  • Weapons: Mistsplitter Reforged, Aquila Favonia, Skyward Blade, Sapwood Blade
  • Artifacts: Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ER Sands, HP% Goblet and Healing Bonus Circlet; meet ER needs before extra healing.
  • Teams: ATK-scaling damage dealer able to stand in Bennett’s Burst field.
  • When it applies: The team buff depends on Base ATK, not artifact ATK%.

F2P Battery Support (Support / Healer)

Choose Sapwood for stronger base-ATK buffs or Favonius when team energy matters more.

  • Weapons: Sapwood Blade, Favonius Sword, Fleuve Cendre Ferryman
  • Artifacts: Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ER is first; CRIT Rate needed when using Favonius Sword.
  • Teams: Teams struggling to recharge multiple Bursts.
  • When it applies: Favonius has lower Base ATK; CRIT hits required for its passive.

Personal Pyro Damage (On-field / Burst DPS)

A niche build for players using Bennett for personal Pyro damage, not just his team buff.

  • Weapons: Mistsplitter Reforged, Skyward Blade, Blackcliff Longsword
  • Artifacts: Emblem of Severed Fate (4 pcs); Crimson Witch of Flames (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ER to requirement, then Pyro DMG, CRIT and ATK/EM based on reactions.
  • Teams: Dedicated Vaporize, Melt or reaction-supporting teammates.
  • When it applies: Crimson Witch favors frequent Skill-triggered reactions.

Bennett talents

Normal Attack: Strike of Fortune

Normal Attack
Performs up to 5 rapid strikes.

Charged Attack
Consumes a certain amount of Stamina to unleash 2 rapid sword strikes.

Plunging Attack
Plunges from mid-air to strike the ground below, damaging opponents along the path and dealing AoE DMG upon impact.

Elemental Skill: Passion Overload

Cooldown: 5/7.5/10s

Bennett puts all his fire and passion for adventuring into his sword. Results may vary based on how fired up he is...

Press
A single, swift flame strike that deals Pyro DMG.

Hold (Short)
Charges up, resulting in different effects when unleashed based on the Charge Level.
·Level 1: Strikes twice, dealing Pyro DMG and launching opponents.
·Level 2: Unleashes 3 consecutive attacks that deal impressive Pyro DMG, but the last attack triggers an explosion that launches both Bennett and the enemy.
Bennett takes no damage from being launched.

Elemental Burst: Fantastic Voyage

Cooldown: 15s · Energy cost: 60

Bennett performs a jumping attack that deals Pyro DMG, creating an Inspiration Field.

Inspiration Field
·If the health of a character within the AoE is equal to or falls below 70%, their health will continuously regenerate. The amount of HP restored scales off Bennett's Max HP.
·If the health of a character within the AoE is higher than 70%, they gain an ATK Bonus that is based on Bennett's Base ATK.
·Imbues characters within the AoE with Pyro.

Passive talent: Rekindle

Decreases Passion Overload's CD by 20%.

Passive talent: Fearnaught

Within the area created by Fantastic Voyage, Passion Overload takes on the following effects:
·CD is reduced by 50%.
·Bennett will not be launched by the effects of Charge Level 2.

Passive talent: It Should Be Safe...

When dispatched on an expedition in Mondstadt, time consumed is reduced by 25%.

Bennett constellations

C1: Grand Expectation

Fantastic Voyage's ATK increase no longer has an HP restriction, and gains an additional 20% of Bennett's Base ATK.

C2: Impasse Conqueror

When Bennett's HP falls below 70%, his Energy Recharge is increased by 30%.

C3: Unstoppable Fervor

Increases the Level of Passion Overload by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C4: Unexpected Odyssey

Using a Normal Attack as the second attack of Passion Overload's Charge Level 1 will perform a follow-up attack.
This additional attack does 135% of the second attack's DMG.

C5: True Explorer

Increases the Level of Fantastic Voyage by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C6: Fire Ventures With Me

Sword, Claymore, or Polearm-wielding characters inside Fantastic Voyage's radius gain a 15% Pyro DMG Bonus and their weapons are infused with Pyro.

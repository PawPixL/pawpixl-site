Clorinde Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

Clorinde build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Absolution, Mistsplitter Reforged), artifacts (Fragment of Harmonic Whimsy), main stats.

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Role: Main DPS

Clorinde is an on-field Electro DPS who converts Bond of Life into rapid pistol shots and lunges. Her Skill supplies self-healing, and she performs well in Aggravate, Overloaded, and Electro-Charged teams.

Best weapons for Clorinde

  1. Absolution: Her signature gives CRIT DMG and increases damage whenever her Bond of Life rises. Best overall damage weapon.
  2. Mistsplitter Reforged: Provides CRIT DMG and Elemental DMG bonuses that Clorinde can use during her Electro attacks. Passive stacks depend on rotation.
  3. Haran Geppaku Futsu: Boosts Normal Attack damage and Elemental DMG, which suit her Skill attacks. Watch for excess CRIT Rate.
  4. Light of Foliar Incision: Huge CRIT DMG with extra Normal Attack damage that scales with Elemental Mastery. Best in Aggravate teams with some EM.
  5. The Black Sword: CR and Normal Attack damage. Its healing is secondary to Clorinde’s own sustain; avoid excess CR.
  6. Lion's Roar: Increases damage against enemies affected by Electro or Pyro. Needs reliable aura uptime. Higher refinements help.
  7. Finale of the Deep: Craftable ATK sword with a Bond of Life passive Clorinde can clear using her own healing.
  8. Calamity of Eshu: Event sword boosting Normal Attacks when shielded. Requires a consistent shield.

Best artifacts for Clorinde

  • Fragment of Harmonic Whimsy (4 pcs) · Best Overall: Bond of Life changes grant damage bonuses that Clorinde stacks quickly during her Skill. Main stats: ATK% / Electro DMG / CRIT.
  • Thundering Fury (4 pcs) · Aggravate Rotations: Electro reactions reduce Skill cooldown. Use with an adjusted rotation, especially Aggravate. Main stats: ATK%/EM · Electro · CR/CD.
  • Gladiator's Finale (4 pcs) · Accessible Alternative: Boosts her Normal Attack damage during Skill, making a good set while farming Whimsy. Main stats: ATK% / Electro DMG / CRIT.

Main stats

  • Sands: ATK% / EM
  • Goblet: Electro DMG
  • Circlet: CR / CD

Stat targets

  • CR: 55–75%
  • CD: 140–200%+
  • ATK: 1,800–2,400
  • ER: Team-based

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 9+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 8 - 9

Best teams for Clorinde

  • Duelist’s Verdict: Clorinde, Fischl, Nahida, Kaedehara Kazuha. Nahida enables Quicken, Fischl adds Electro damage, and Kazuha shreds Electro resistance. Clorinde drives Aggravate.
  • Overload Authority: Clorinde, Chevreuse, Kujou Sara, Mavuika. Chevreuse shreds Electro and Pyro resistance after Overload. Sara buffs Clorinde while Mavuika adds Pyro damage.
  • Charged Justice: Clorinde, Furina, Xilonen, Ororon. Furina buffs team damage, Ororon adds Electro damage, and Xilonen reduces resistance. Clorinde heals herself while attacking.
  • Headed to jail: Clorinde, Fischl, Kirara, Sucrose. Kirara provides Dendro and a shield. Fischl adds Electro damage, while Sucrose shares EM and shreds Electro resistance.

Pros

  • Fast Electro damage
  • Strong self-healing
  • Flexible team options
  • Mobile combat style

Cons

  • Vulnerable to interruption
  • Long Skill cooldown
  • Timing matters
  • Needs reaction support

Build variations

Aggravate Driver (On-field DPS)

Choose for frequent Aggravate reactions and flexible Skill rotations.

  • Weapons: Absolution, Light of Foliar Incision, Lion's Roar
  • Artifacts: Thundering Fury (4 pcs); Fragment of Harmonic Whimsy (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ATK% or EM / Electro / CRIT.
  • Teams: Dendro application and Electro support; account for her CR buff.
  • When it applies: EM matters more than in raw Electro teams

Overloaded Duelist (On-field DPS)

Use Chevreuse’s resistance reduction and buffs for consistent Electro damage.

  • Weapons: Absolution, Finale of the Deep, The Black Sword
  • Artifacts: Fragment of Harmonic Whimsy (4 pcs); Thundering Fury (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ATK% / Electro / CRIT.
  • Teams: Chevreuse and only Pyro/Electro teammates.
  • When it applies: Requires Overloaded to maintain Chevreuse shred

Shielded Budget (On-field DPS)

A lower-cost build that keeps her fast gun-and-lunge gameplay.

  • Weapons: Calamity of Eshu, Finale of the Deep, Lion's Roar
  • Artifacts: Fragment of Harmonic Whimsy (4 pcs); Gladiator's Finale (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ATK% / Electro / CRIT.
  • Teams: Reliable shielder for Calamity of Eshu.
  • When it applies: Calamity passive only works while shielded

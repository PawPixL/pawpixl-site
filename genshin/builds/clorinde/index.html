Role: Main DPS

Clorinde is an on-field Electro DPS who uses Bond of Life to power rapid pistol shots and lunging attacks. Her Skill provides self-healing, while Electro reactions strengthen her damage. She excels in Aggravate, Overload, and Electro-Charged teams.

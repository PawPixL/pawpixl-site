Flins Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

Flins build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Bloodsoaked Ruins, Staff of the Scarlet Sands), artifacts (Night Of The Sky'S Unveiling), main stats.

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Role: Main DPS

Flins is an Electro DPS who unleashes devastating Lunar Charged damage through his Elemental Bursts. He’s easy to play and hits hard, but his best performance depends on the right teammates.

Best weapons for Flins

  1. Bloodsoaked Ruins: Signature CRIT weapon improving Lunar-Charged damage, CRIT DMG and Energy after triggering Lunar-Charged.
  2. Staff of the Scarlet Sands: Provides 44.1% CRIT Rate and converts EM into bonus ATK, making it Flins’s second-best 5-star option for Lunar-Charged damage.
  3. Fractured Halo: CRIT DMG and ATK after Skill or Burst. Its Lunar-Charged team buff requires the wielder to create a shield, so Flins cannot normally activate it. Shield-creation passive unavailable.
  4. Staff of Homa: High CRIT DMG and HP-scaling ATK. Strong general offensive stats even above half HP.
  5. Primordial Jade Winged-Spear: CRIT Rate and stacking ATK with hits; needs field time to reach full stacks.
  6. Deathmatch: Battle Pass CRIT Rate with an ATK bonus based on nearby enemy count.
  7. Tamayuratei no Ohanashi: Provides 30.6% Energy Recharge and increases ATK after triggering an Elemental Skill, helping Flins maintain Burst uptime and damage.
  8. Prospector's Shovel: Provides 41.3% ATK and up to 48% Lunar-Charged DMG Bonus at R5, making it Flins’s best craftable F2P option.

Best artifacts for Flins

  • Night Of The Sky'S Unveiling (4 pcs) · Main DPS: Grants EM and extra CRIT Rate during Lunar reactions. The higher bonus depends on Moonsign level, making it ideal for Flins teams. Main stats: ATK% · ATK% · CR/CD.
  • Thundering Fury (4 pcs) · Main DPS: Provides 15% Electro DMG and 20% Lunar-Charged DMG, with Skill cooldown reduction. A strong alternative for Flins, especially in teams with Fischl.
  • Gladiator's Finale + Gilded Dreams (2 + 2 pcs) · Main DPS: Provides 18% ATK and 80 EM, offering a flexible alternative for boosting Flins’s ATK and reaction damage.

Main stats

  • Sands: ATK%
  • Goblet: ATK%
  • Circlet: CRIT Rate / CRIT DMG

Stat targets

  • ATK: 2500+
  • Crit Rate: 40% - 50%
  • Crit DMG: 180%+
  • Energy Recharge: 130% - 140%

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 9+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 9+

Best teams for Flins

  • Thunder Down Under: Flins (Main DPS), Columbina (Lunar), Sucrose (EM Build), Ineffa (Support). Flins drives Lunar-Charged damage, Columbina buffs Lunar reactions, Sucrose boosts EM and shreds RES, and Ineffa adds off-field Electro and shielding.
  • Watt the Flins: Flins (Main DPS), Aino (Reaction), Sucrose (EM Build), Ineffa (Support). Flins deals Lunar Charged damage while Aino supplies Hydro. Ineffa adds off field Electro damage and shielding, and Sucrose groups enemies and shares EM.
  • Birds of Prey: Flins (Main DPS), Columbina (Lunar), Ineffa (Support), Fischl (Sub DPS). Flins leads the Lunar Charged damage while Columbina supports Lunar reactions. Ineffa adds shielding and Electro damage, and Fischl contributes Oz damage.
  • Low Current Balance: Flins (Main DPS), Aino (Reaction), Sucrose (EM Build), Fischl (Sub DPS). Flins drives Lunar Charged reactions with Aino’s Hydro application. Sucrose shares EM and groups enemies, while Fischl adds off field Electro damage.

Pros

  • Massive Burst damage
  • Strong AoE DMG
  • Straightforward gameplay
  • Good damage even at C0

Cons

  • Relies heavily on Lunar Charged
  • Needs the right team setup
  • Damage drops without proper team synergy

Build variations

Premium Lunar-Charged (On-field DPS)

Prioritize Lunar-Charged damage in teams that maintain their Moonsign bonuses.

  • Weapons: Bloodsoaked Ruins, Staff of the Scarlet Sands, Staff of Homa
  • Artifacts: Night Of The Sky'S Unveiling (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Balance CRIT, ATK and EM; avoid excess set-granted CRIT Rate.
  • Teams: Hydro application and Lunar-Charged enabler; ideally Ineffa.
  • When it applies: Set bonus requires on-field Lunar reactions.

Accessible Lunar Carry (On-field DPS)

Lower-cost weapon route using the same Lunar-Charged game plan.

  • Weapons: Deathmatch, Primordial Jade Winged-Spear
  • Artifacts: Night Of The Sky'S Unveiling (4 pcs); Gladiator's Finale + Gilded Dreams (2 + 2 pcs)
  • Stats: Favor CRIT and sufficient EM; adjust ATK based on buffs.
  • Teams: Hydro teammate and reliable Lunar-Charged reactions.
  • When it applies: Performance depends on reaction uptime.

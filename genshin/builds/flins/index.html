Flins Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

Flins build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Bloodsoaked Ruins, Staff of the Scarlet Sands), artifacts (Night Of The Sky'S Unveiling), main stats.

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Role: Main DPS

Flins is an Electro DPS who unleashes devastating Lunar Charged damage through his Elemental Bursts. He’s easy to play and hits hard, but his best performance depends on the right teammates.

Best weapons for Flins

  1. Bloodsoaked Ruins: Signature CRIT weapon improving Lunar-Charged damage, CRIT DMG and Energy after triggering Lunar-Charged.
  2. Staff of the Scarlet Sands: Provides 44.1% CRIT Rate and converts EM into bonus ATK, making it Flins’s second-best 5-star option for Lunar-Charged damage.
  3. Fractured Halo: CRIT DMG and ATK after Skill or Burst. Its Lunar-Charged team buff requires the wielder to create a shield, so Flins cannot normally activate it. Shield-creation passive unavailable.
  4. Staff of Homa: High CRIT DMG and HP-scaling ATK. Strong general offensive stats even above half HP.
  5. Primordial Jade Winged-Spear: CRIT Rate and stacking ATK with hits; needs field time to reach full stacks.
  6. Deathmatch: Battle Pass CRIT Rate with an ATK bonus based on nearby enemy count.
  7. Tamayuratei no Ohanashi: Provides 30.6% Energy Recharge and increases ATK after triggering an Elemental Skill, helping Flins maintain Burst uptime and damage.
  8. Prospector's Shovel: Provides 41.3% ATK and up to 48% Lunar-Charged DMG Bonus at R5, making it Flins’s best craftable F2P option.

Best artifacts for Flins

  • Night Of The Sky'S Unveiling (4 pcs) · Main DPS: Grants EM and extra CRIT Rate during Lunar reactions. The higher bonus depends on Moonsign level, making it ideal for Flins teams. Main stats: ATK% · ATK% · CR/CD.
  • Thundering Fury (4 pcs) · Main DPS: Provides 15% Electro DMG and 20% Lunar-Charged DMG, with Skill cooldown reduction. A strong alternative for Flins, especially in teams with Fischl.
  • Gladiator's Finale + Gilded Dreams (2 + 2 pcs) · Main DPS: Provides 18% ATK and 80 EM, offering a flexible alternative for boosting Flins’s ATK and reaction damage.

Best teams for Flins

  • Thunder Down Under: Flins (Main DPS), Columbina (Lunar), Sucrose (EM Build), Ineffa (Support). Flins drives Lunar-Charged damage, Columbina buffs Lunar reactions, Sucrose boosts EM and shreds RES, and Ineffa adds off-field Electro and shielding.
  • Watt the Flins: Flins (Main DPS), Aino (Reaction), Sucrose (EM Build), Ineffa (Support). Flins deals Lunar Charged damage while Aino supplies Hydro. Ineffa adds off field Electro damage and shielding, and Sucrose groups enemies and shares EM.
  • Birds of Prey: Flins (Main DPS), Columbina (Lunar), Ineffa (Support), Fischl (Sub DPS). Flins leads the Lunar Charged damage while Columbina supports Lunar reactions. Ineffa adds shielding and Electro damage, and Fischl contributes Oz damage.
  • Low Current Balance: Flins (Main DPS), Aino (Reaction), Sucrose (EM Build), Fischl (Sub DPS). Flins drives Lunar Charged reactions with Aino’s Hydro application. Sucrose shares EM and groups enemies, while Fischl adds off field Electro damage.

Build variations

Premium Lunar-Charged (On-field DPS)

Prioritize Lunar-Charged damage in teams that maintain their Moonsign bonuses.

  • Weapons: Bloodsoaked Ruins, Staff of the Scarlet Sands, Staff of Homa
  • Artifacts: Night Of The Sky'S Unveiling (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Balance CRIT, ATK and EM; avoid excess set-granted CRIT Rate.
  • Teams: Hydro application and Lunar-Charged enabler; ideally Ineffa.
  • When it applies: Set bonus requires on-field Lunar reactions.

Accessible Lunar Carry (On-field DPS)

Lower-cost weapon route using the same Lunar-Charged game plan.

  • Weapons: Deathmatch, Primordial Jade Winged-Spear
  • Artifacts: Night Of The Sky'S Unveiling (4 pcs); Gladiator's Finale + Gilded Dreams (2 + 2 pcs)
  • Stats: Favor CRIT and sufficient EM; adjust ATK based on buffs.
  • Teams: Hydro teammate and reliable Lunar-Charged reactions.
  • When it applies: Performance depends on reaction uptime.

Flins talents

Normal Attack: Pocztowy Demonspear

Normal Attack
Performs up to 5 consecutive spear strikes.

Charged Attack
Consumes a certain amount of Stamina to perform a forward spear throw.

Plunging Attack
Plunges from mid-air to strike the ground below, damaging opponents along the path and dealing AoE DMG upon impact.

Elemental Skill: Ancient Rite: Arcane Light

Cooldown: Northland Spearstorm: 6s / Skill: 16s

Flins summons forth the ancient power concealed within his lamp and switches to his Manifest Flame form. This form has the following characteristics:
· Flins's Normal and Charged Attacks deal Electro DMG that cannot be overridden by other Elemental infusions, and he is unable to perform Plunging Attacks.
· His interruption resistance is increased.
· His Elemental Skill Ancient Rite: Arcane Light is replaced with the special Elemental Skill Northland Spearstorm.

Northland Spearstorm
Flins summons a flurry of spears in front, dealing AoE Electro DMG and causing his Elemental Burst Ancient Ritual: Cometh the Night to be replaced with the special Elemental Burst Thunderous Symphony for the next 6s.
The base CD of Northland Spearstorm is 6s and will not be affected by other effects.

Elemental Burst: Ancient Ritual: Cometh the Night

Cooldown: 20s · Energy cost: 80

Flins unleashes the true power of his lamp, dealing AoE Electro DMG and, after a short delay, dealing 2 instances of middle-phase and 1 instance of final-phase AoE Electro DMG, all of which are considered Lunar-Charged DMG.
Moonsign: Ascendant Gleam: Flins's Skill is enhanced: If there are thunderclouds nearby, Flins will deal an additional 2 instances of middle-phase Lunar-Charged AoE Electro DMG.

After using the special Elemental Skill Northland Spearstorm, Flins's Elemental Burst Ancient Ritual: Cometh the Night will be replaced with the special Elemental Burst Thunderous Symphony for the next 6s.

Thunderous Symphony
Consume less Elemental Energy to unleash a special Elemental Burst. Flins deals a single instance of AoE Electro DMG that is considered Lunar-Charged DMG.
Moonsign: Ascendant Gleam: Flins's Skill is enhanced: If there are thunderclouds nearby, Flins will deal an additional instance of Lunar-Charged AoE Electro DMG.

Passive talent: Symphony of Winter

Flins will gain a corresponding buff effect based on the party's Moonsign.

Moonsign: Ascendant Gleam: Lunar-Charged reactions triggered by Flins will deal an additional 20% DMG.

Passive talent: Whispering Flame

Flins's Elemental Mastery is increased by 8% of his ATK. The maximum increase obtainable this way is 160.

Passive talent: Moonsign Benediction: Old World Secrets

When a party member triggers an Electro-Charged reaction, it will be converted into the Lunar-Charged reaction, with every 100 ATK that Flins has increasing Lunar-Charged's Base DMG by 0.7%, up to a maximum of 14%.

Additionally, when Flins is in the party, the party's Moonsign will increase by 1 level.

Passive talent: A Light in the Dark

Displays the location of nearby resources unique to Nod-Krai on the mini-map.
Additionally, Flins appears to be able to decipher the whispers of the Wild Hunt...

Flins constellations

C1: Part the Veil of Snow

The basic cooldown of the special Elemental Skill Northland Spearstorm is reduced to 4s.
Additionally, when party members trigger Lunar-Charged reactions, Flins will recover 8 Elemental Energy. This effect can occur once every 5.5s.

C2: The Devil's Wall

For the next 6s after using the special Elemental Skill Northland Spearstorm, when Flins's next Normal Attack hits an opponent, it will deal an additional 50% of Flins's ATK as AoE Electro DMG. This DMG is considered Lunar-Charged DMG.
Moonsign: Ascendant Gleam: While Flins is on the field, after his Electro attacks hit an opponent, that opponent's Electro RES will be decreased by 25% for 7s.

C3: Stranger in the Night

Increases the Level of Ancient Ritual: Cometh the Night by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C4: Night on Bald Mountain

Flins's ATK is increased by 20%.
Additionally, his Ascension Talent Whispering Flame is enhanced: Flins's Elemental Mastery is increased by 10% of his ATK. The maximum increase obtainable this way is 220.

C5: Exile's Shadow

Increases the Level of Ancient Rite: Arcane Light by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C6: Songs and Dances of Death

The DMG dealt to opponents by Flins's Lunar-Charged reactions is elevated by 35%.
Moonsign: Ascendant Gleam: All nearby party members' Lunar-Charged DMG is elevated by 10%.

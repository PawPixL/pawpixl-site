Flins Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts & Teams

Flins build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Bloodsoaked Ruins, Staff of the Scarlet Sands), artifacts (Night Of The Sky'S Unveiling), main stats…

Role: Main DPS

Flins is an Electro DPS who unleashes devastating Lunar Charged damage through his Elemental Bursts. He’s easy to play and hits hard, but his best performance depends on the right teammates.

Best weapons for Flins

  1. Bloodsoaked Ruins
  2. Staff of the Scarlet Sands
  3. Fractured Halo
  4. Staff of Homa
  5. Primordial Jade Winged-Spear
  6. Deathmatch
  7. Tamayuratei no Ohanashi
  8. Prospector's Shovel

Best artifacts

  • Night Of The Sky'S Unveiling (4 pcs) · Main DPS
  • Thundering Fury (4 pcs) · Main DPS
  • Gladiator's Finale + Gilded Dreams (2 + 2 pcs) · Main DPS

Main stats

  • Sands: ATK%
  • Goblet: ATK%
  • Circlet: CRIT Rate / CRIT DMG

Stat targets

  • ATK: 2500+
  • Crit Rate: 40% - 50%
  • Crit DMG: 180%+
  • Energy Recharge: 130% - 140%

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 9+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 9+

Best teams

  • Thunder Down Under: Flins, Columbina, Sucrose, Ineffa
  • Watt the Flins: Flins, Aino, Sucrose, Ineffa
  • Birds of Prey: Flins, Columbina, Ineffa, Fischl
  • Low Current Balance: Flins, Aino, Sucrose, Fischl

Pros

  • Massive Burst damage
  • Strong AoE DMG
  • Straightforward gameplay
  • Good damage even at C0

Cons

  • Relies heavily on Lunar Charged
  • Needs the right team setup
  • Damage drops without proper team synergy

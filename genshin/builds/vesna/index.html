Vesna Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

Vesna build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Beyond the Chrysalis, Whitelake Frostfeather), artifacts (Scarlet Proof), main stats, talent priority.

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Role: Main DPS

Vesna is an Anemo DPS who excels in stellar-swirl teams with powerful skill based damage. She rewards frequent reactions and strong off-field teammates.

Best weapons for Vesna

  1. Beyond the Chrysalis: Provides 44.1% CRIT DMG, up to 56% additional CRIT DMG, 36% Stellar Swirl DMG, and 5 Energy
  2. Whitelake Frostfeather: Provides 22.1% CRIT Rate, up to 24% ATK, 50% Stellar Glimmer CRIT DMG, and 4 Energy at R1. A strong 5-star alternative for Vesna.
  3. Azurelight: CRIT Rate and ATK after Skill. The extra bonus needs zero Energy, so it depends on Vesna’s rotation.
  4. Absolution: CRIT DMG and extra damage when Bond of Life rises; mainly a stat stick without consistent Bond of Life.
  5. New Bough: Provides 55.1% CRIT DMG, up to 18% ATK, and 24% Stellar Glimmer DMG at R1, making it a strong 4-star option for Vesna.
  6. Heretic's Molten Blade: Provides 165 EM, 16% ATK, and 16% Stellar Glimmer DMG at R1, making it a strong craftable F2P option for Vesna.
  7. Emberwell: An accessible Elemental Mastery option when your stronger damage weapons are unavailable.
  8. The Black Sword: Battle Pass CRIT Rate and Normal/Charged Attack damage; some healing on CRIT Normal/Charged hits.

Best artifacts for Vesna

  • Scarlet Proof (4 pcs) · Main DPS: Scarlet Proof is Vesna’s BiS because it gives her 18% ATK, plus 16% CRIT Rate and 40% Stellar Swirl DMG for 10 seconds after triggering Stellar Swirl. Main stats: ATK% · ATK%/Anemo DMG% · CR/CD.
  • Viridescent Venerer (4 pcs) · Main DPS: Boosts regular Swirl and shreds the Swirled element’s RES; lower direct Stellar Swirl damage than Scarlet Proof.
  • Gladiator's Finale + A Day Carved From Rising Winds (2 + 2 pcs) · Main DPS: Combines 36% ATK from both 2-piece bonuses, providing a strong placeholder build while farming Scarlet Proof.

Main stats

  • Sands: ATK%
  • Goblet: ATK% / Anemo DMG%
  • Circlet: Crit Rate / Crit DMG

Stat targets

  • ATK: 2,000-2,500+
  • Crit Rate: 50% - 60%
  • Crit DMG: 180%+
  • Energy Recharge: 110% - 130%

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 9+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 9+

Best teams for Vesna

  • Category Five: Vesna, Odette, Vodyanitsa, Faruzan. Vesna deals Stellar Swirl damage while Odette supplies Cryo and reaction support. Vodyanitsa heals and buffs, and Faruzan strengthens Anemo damage.
  • Celestial Spending: Vesna, Odette, Vodyanitsa, Nicole. Odette enables Stellar Swirl and adds Cryo support. Vodyanitsa heals and buffs, while Nicole protects and strengthens Vesna’s damage.
  • WEATHER ADVISORY: Vesna, Furina, Vodyanitsa, Faruzan. Furina buffs damage and applies Hydro, Vodyanitsa heals and shreds RES, and Faruzan boosts Anemo damage and shreds Anemo RES.
  • TEMU TORNADO: Vesna, Traveler (Cryo), Xingqiu, Sucrose. Cryo Traveler enables Stellar Swirl. Xingqiu adds Hydro through Normal Attack triggers, while Sucrose offers grouping and Elemental Mastery support.

Build variations

Stellar Swirl Carry (On-field DPS)

Use Scarlet Proof for Vesna’s Stellar Swirl-focused damage.

  • Weapons: Beyond the Chrysalis, Whitelake Frostfeather, Absolution
  • Artifacts: Scarlet Proof (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Balance CRIT and damage stats for Stellar Swirl.
  • Teams: Cryo and Electro reaction setup plus relevant buffs.
  • When it applies: Needs correct reaction support.

VV Team Utility (Anemo Support)

Trade Vesna’s personal output for Viridescent Venerer support.

  • Weapons: Whitelake Frostfeather, New Bough
  • Artifacts: Viridescent Venerer (4 pcs)
  • Stats: EM/ER can be useful for Swirl utility; balance personal damage loss.
  • Teams: Teammates benefiting from Pyro, Hydro, Electro or Cryo RES shred.
  • When it applies: VV does not shred Anemo RES.

Vesna talents

Normal Attack: Vila Blade Dance

Normal Attack
Performs up to 6 consecutive strikes.

Charged Attack
Consumes a certain amount of Stamina to trample opponents in front of her.

Plunging Attack
Plunges from mid-air to strike the ground below, damaging opponents along her path and dealing AoE DMG upon impact.

Elemental Skill: The Art of Victory

Cooldown: 18s

Calls forth Spirit Blades passed down through generations of the Vila to deal AoE Anemo DMG, then switches to the Armed for Action mode and gains 2 stacks of Sword Essence.

Armed for Action
DMG dealt by Vesna with her Normal Attacks, Charged Attacks, and Plunging Attacks become Anemo DMG that cannot be overridden by another elemental infusion instead. She will also summon a wind pinion that attacks the opponent at the same time, dealing additional Anemo DMG. Each time the wind pinion is summoned, Vesna will regenerate a certain amount of Sword Essence.
Vesna can consume Sword Essence to unleash the special Elemental Skill Windborne Sword, which deals to the opponent Anemo DMG.
Each use of the special Elemental Skill Windborne Sword in the same instance of Armed for Action will increase the level of Windborne Sword, beginning from Lv. 1 to Lv. 3 max, for more Anemo DMG. Using Windborne Sword at Lv. 2 and Lv. 3 will also summon Spirit Blades which deal Anemo DMG.
Vesna will exit the mode when its duration ends or when she has used Windborne Sword at the highest level 3 times. Exiting will clear all remaining Sword Essence she has accrued.
Additionally, when in this mode, Vesna's resistance to interruption is increased.
Radiance: Stellar Swirl: DMG dealt with the Spirit Blades becomes Anemo DMG that is also considered Stellar Swirl reaction DMG.

Elemental Burst: For the Tsaritsa!

Cooldown: 15s · Energy cost: 60

Summons multiple Spirit Blades, dealing AoE Anemo DMG. Also gains a stack of Sword Essence.

Passive talent: Rite of Spring's Procession

After Vesna uses the special Elemental Skill Windborne Sword or the Elemental Burst For the Tsaritsa!, she also gains 1 stack of Disciplinary Action which lasts 20s. Max 6 stacks, and the duration of each stack is independent of the others'.
When she has Disciplinary Action stacks active, the Spirit Blades summoned by Vesna will deal DMG equal to (100% + 10% × Disciplinary Action stack count) of the original DMG instead. All active stacks of Disciplinary Action are cleared when Vesna uses the Elemental Skill The Art of Victory or when she leaves the field.

Passive talent: Truth Prevails

Radiance: Stellar Swirl: Vesna will gain the corresponding effect based on the Elemental Type of her party members:
· For every Cryo or Anemo character in her current party, Vesna's ATK increases by 6%; and
· For every character of all other Elemental Types in her current party, Vesna's Elemental Mastery increases by 25 instead.

Passive talent: Stellar Jubilee: Splendid Prelude

When party members trigger Cryo Swirl, it will be changed to a Stellar Swirl reaction, and party members' Base DMG for the Stellar Swirl will be increased based on Vesna's ATK. Every 100 ATK she has increases the Base Stellar Swirl DMG by 0.7%. The maximum DMG increase that can be gained this way is 14%.
After a party member triggers a Stellar Swirl reaction nearby, Vesna enters the Radiance: Stellar Swirl state for 8s.

Passive talent: The Grand Circuit

Holding on Sprint or Jump when Vesna is not in combat will cause her to enter the Coursing state.

Coursing
Continuously consumes Stamina to hover in mid-air. Sprinting or tapping Jump to go higher expends additional Stamina. Including the hold on Jump that triggers Coursing, Vesna can go higher up to 3 times in each instance of the Coursing state.

Additionally, in Snezhnaya (excluding Nod-Krai), when Vesna is switched onto the field while the currently active party member is in mid-air and not in combat, she will immediately enter the Coursing state and regenerate 50 Stamina. This effect can trigger once every 10s, and does not take effect in Domains, Trounce Domains, or the Spiral Abyss.

Vesna constellations

C1: Winter's Farewell Feast

When in the Armed for Action mode, Vesna can unleash Windborne Sword at the highest level 4 times instead of 3. Also, each time she enters the Armed for Action mode, she will not consume Sword Essence the first time she uses Windborne Sword at the highest level.
Additionally, Vesna deals 20% increased Stellar Swirl reaction DMG when in the Armed for Action mode.

C2: Kolo of Spring's Arrival

The Ascension Talent Rite of Spring's Procession is enhanced: Vesna gains the maximum number of Disciplinary Action stacks when she enters Armed for Action mode. Vesna's ATK is increased by 40% when she has maximum Disciplinary Action stacks.
You must unlock the Ascension Talent "Rite of Spring's Procession" to gain access to the above effects.

C3: Winter's Last Offering

Increases the Level of the Elemental Skill The Art of Victory by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C4: Glory to Our Forebears

The Ascension Talent Truth Prevails is enhanced: Its ATK and Elemental Mastery bonuses increase to 3 times their original values.

C5: The Nobleborn's Charge

Increases the Level of the Elemental Burst For the Tsaritsa! by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C6: Unwavering Ardor

Tapping on Normal Attack or Elemental Skill in the 5s after Vesna uses Windborne Sword at its highest level will unleash the secret Vila ability Windborne Sword: Transpose, dealing Anemo DMG at 150% of Vesna's ATK as well as summoning Spirit Blades which deal an additional instance of Anemo DMG at 200% of Vesna's ATK. If Vesna is in the Armed for Action mode, she will also summon a wind pinion to perform Coordinated Attacks against opponents.
Additionally, Vesna's Stellar Swirl reaction DMG dealt is elevated by 20%.

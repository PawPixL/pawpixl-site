Aloy Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams (7.2)

Aloy build guide for Genshin Impact 7.2: best weapons (Polar Star, Skyward Harp), artifacts (Noblesse Oblige), main stats, talent priority, teams.

Last updated: November 12, 2026 · Game version 7.2

Role: Sub DPS / Support

Aloy is a quickswap Cryo Burst DPS and battery whose Skill produces Cryo particles and whose 40-Energy Burst can Melt for a strong hit. Her 4 Coil stacks unlock Cryo-infused attacks, but inconsistent Bomblet hits make on-field play situational.

Best weapons for Aloy

  1. Polar Star: CRIT and ATK boost Skill/Burst. Best with attack stack setup.
  2. Skyward Harp: High base ATK and double CRIT stats.
  3. Elegy for the End: ER and occasional team ATK/EM buffs. Buff may take multiple rotations.
  4. Sacrificial Bow: Extra Skill for particles and damage. Strong at high refinements. R4+ preferred.
  5. Favonius Warbow: Team particles and abundant ER. Needs enough CRIT Rate.
  6. The Stringless: EM boosts Melt; buffs Skill/Burst. Best for Reverse Melt.
  7. Prototype Crescent: ATK bonus after weak-point hits. Requires accessible weak points.
  8. Song of Stillness: DMG bonus after healing. Needs a healer to trigger passive.

Best artifacts for Aloy

  • Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs): Burst gives team +20% ATK. Main stats: ER/ATK% · Cryo · CRIT.
  • Emblem of Severed Fate (4 pcs): ER converts into Burst damage. Main stats: ER/EM/ATK · Cryo · CRIT.
  • Blizzard Strayer (4 pcs): High CRIT in Freeze teams. Main stats: ATK% · Cryo · CRIT DMG.

Main stats

  • Sands: ATK% / Energy Recharge / EM (Melt)
  • Goblet: Cryo DMG Bonus
  • Circlet: CRIT Rate / CRIT DMG

Stat targets

  • Solo ER: 140-165%
  • Duo ER: 100-125%
  • CR: 55-70%
  • CD: 110-160%
  • EM Melt: 100-250

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1
  • Elemental Skill: Level 8-9+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 8-9+

Best teams for Aloy

  • Dawnfire Detonation: Aloy (Burst DPS), Bennett (Healer/Buffer), Kaedehara Kazuha (Anemo Support), Xiangling (Pyro Sub DPS). Bennett buffs; Xiangling maintains Pyro; Kazuha groups Bomblets and shreds RES so Aloy can Reverse Melt.
  • Frozen Horizon: Aloy (Burst DPS / Battery), Kamisato Ayaka (On-field DPS), Sangonomiya Kokomi (Healer / Hydro Enabler), Kaedehara Kazuha (Buffer / Debuffer). Aloy batteries Ayaka and adds Burst damage; Kokomi maintains Freeze while Kazuha groups and shreds Cryo RES.
  • Glacial Dawn: Aloy (Burst DPS / Battery), Shenhe (Cryo Buffer), Kamisato Ayaka (On-field DPS), Jean (Healer / Debuffer). Mono Cryo quickswap: Shenhe boosts Cryo damage, Aloy supplies particles, Ayaka deals damage, and Jean heals and shreds RES.
  • Where have all the good cons gone?: Aloy (Burst DPS / Battery), Bennett (Healer/Buffer), Xiangling (Pyro Sub DPS), Sucrose (Anemo Support). Accessible Reverse Melt core: Bennett and Xiangling apply Pyro while Sucrose shares EM and groups Bomblets.

Pros

Cons

Build variations

Reverse Melt Burst DPS (Quickswap DPS)

Set up Pyro aura and buffs, then Melt Aloy Skill and Burst. The Burst is a single powerful Cryo hit.

  • Weapons: Polar Star, The Stringless, Sacrificial Bow
  • Artifacts: Emblem of Severed Fate (4 pcs); Gilded Dreams (4 pcs)
  • Stats: EM/ATK% or ER sands, Cryo goblet, CRIT circlet. Balance EM with enough ER.
  • Teams: Needs Bennett plus sustained Pyro and preferably Anemo grouping.

Freeze Quickswap DPS (Cryo Sub DPS)

Use Aloy for Cryo particles and a short Burst window while your Cryo carry handles field time.

  • Weapons: Skyward Harp, Polar Star, Favonius Warbow
  • Artifacts: Blizzard Strayer (4 pcs); Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ATK% sands; Cryo goblet; favor CRIT DMG due to Freeze/Blizzard CRIT Rate.
  • Teams: Hydro applier, Cryo teammate, and preferably Anemo RES shred.

Cryo Battery / Team Buffer (Support)

Cast Skill for five Cryo particles, swap to the carry to catch particles, and Burst to refresh Noblesse.

  • Weapons: Sacrificial Bow, Favonius Warbow, Elegy for the End
  • Artifacts: Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs); Instructor (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ER first; CRIT Rate for Favonius. Personal DPS is secondary.
  • Teams: A Cryo carry who benefits from particles and team buffs.
  • When it applies: Noblesse only if no teammate already carries it.

Rushing Ice On-Field (On-field Cryo DPS)

Get 4 Coil stacks from spaced Bomblet hits to unlock 10s Cryo Normal Attacks. This setup is inconsistent.

  • Weapons: Thundering Pulse, Polar Star, Skyward Harp
  • Artifacts: Blizzard Strayer (4 pcs); Marechaussee Hunter (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ATK%/Cryo/CRIT DMG; less CRIT Rate with Freeze. Normal Attack becomes a high-priority talent.
  • Teams: Anemo Bomblet grouping, Hydro for Freeze, and preferably another Cryo unit.
  • When it applies: Coil stack acquisition has a 0.1s interval.
  • Notes: Marechaussee Hunter requires Furina HP fluctuation.

Accessible Melt Burst (Quickswap DPS)

An accessible burst-oriented build that still prioritizes reactions and consistent Energy.

  • Weapons: Prototype Crescent, Song of Stillness, The Stringless
  • Artifacts: Emblem of Severed Fate (4 pcs); Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ER to requirement; ATK% or EM sands depending on Pyro/Bennett buffs.
  • Teams: Pyro application; weak-point target for Prototype Crescent or healer for Song of Stillness.
  • When it applies: Weapon passives require their stated triggers.

Aloy talents

Normal Attack: Rapid Fire

Normal Attack
Performs up to 4 consecutive shots with a bow.

Charged Attack
Performs a more precise Aimed Shot with increased DMG.
While aiming, biting frost will accumulate on the arrowhead. A fully charged frost arrow will deal Cryo DMG.

Plunging Attack
Fires off a shower of arrows in mid-air before falling and striking the ground, dealing AoE DMG upon impact.

Elemental Skill: Frozen Wilds

Cooldown: 20s

Aloy throws a Freeze Bomb in the targeted direction that explodes on impact, dealing Cryo DMG. After it explodes, the Freeze Bomb will split up into many Chillwater Bomblets that explode on contact with opponents or after a short delay, dealing Cryo DMG.
When a Freeze Bomb or Chillwater Bomblet hits an opponent, the opponent's ATK is decreased and Aloy receives 1 Coil stack.
Aloy can gain up to 1 Coil stack every 0.1s.

Coil
·Each stack increases Aloy's Normal Attack DMG.
·When Aloy has 4 Coil stacks, all stacks of Coil are cleared. She then enters the Rushing Ice state, which further increases the DMG dealt by her Normal Attacks and converts her Normal Attack DMG to Cryo DMG.

While in the Rushing Ice state, Aloy cannot obtain new Coil stacks.
Coil effects will be cleared 30s after Aloy leaves combat.

Elemental Burst: Prophecies of Dawn

Cooldown: 12s · Energy cost: 40

Aloy throws a Power Cell filled with Cryo in the targeted direction, then detonates it with an arrow, dealing AoE Cryo DMG.

Passive talent: Combat Override

When Aloy receives the Coil effect from Frozen Wilds, her ATK is increased by 16%, while nearby party members' ATK is increased by 8%. This effect lasts 10s.

Passive talent: Strong Strike

When Aloy is in the Rushing Ice state conferred by Frozen Wilds, her Cryo DMG Bonus increases by 3.5% every 1s. A maximum Cryo DMG Bonus increase of 35% can be gained in this way.

Passive talent: Easy Does It

When Aloy is in the party, animals who produce Fowl, Raw Meat, or Chilled Meat will not be startled when party members approach them.

Aloy Build Guide | Best Weapons, Artifacts & Teams

Aloy build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Polar Star, Skyward Harp), artifacts (Noblesse Oblige), main stats (ATK% / Energy Recharge / EM (Melt)…

Role: Sub DPS / Support

Aloy is a quickswap Cryo Burst DPS and battery whose Skill produces Cryo particles and whose 40-Energy Burst can Melt for a strong hit. Her 4 Coil stacks unlock Cryo-infused attacks, but inconsistent Bomblet hits make on-field play situational.

Best weapons for Aloy

  1. Polar Star
  2. Skyward Harp
  3. Elegy for the End
  4. Sacrificial Bow
  5. Favonius Warbow
  6. The Stringless
  7. Prototype Crescent
  8. Song of Stillness

Best artifacts

  • Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs)
  • Emblem of Severed Fate (4 pcs)
  • Blizzard Strayer (4 pcs)

Main stats

  • Sands: ATK% / Energy Recharge / EM (Melt)
  • Goblet: Cryo DMG Bonus
  • Circlet: CRIT Rate / CRIT DMG

Stat targets

  • Solo ER: 140-165%
  • Duo ER: 100-125%
  • CR: 55-70%
  • CD: 110-160%
  • EM Melt: 100-250

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1
  • Elemental Skill: Level 8-9+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 8-9+

Best teams

  • Dawnfire Detonation: Aloy, Bennett, Kaedehara Kazuha, Xiangling
  • Frozen Horizon: Aloy, Kamisato Ayaka, Sangonomiya Kokomi, Kaedehara Kazuha
  • Glacial Dawn: Aloy, Shenhe, Kamisato Ayaka, Jean
  • Where have all the good cons gone?: Aloy, Bennett, Xiangling, Sucrose

Pros

  • Low-cost Cryo Burst
  • Strong Melt burst potential
  • Generates Cryo particles
  • Supports Freeze teams

Cons

  • Unreliable Coil stacks
  • Long Skill cooldown
  • No constellations
  • Limited sustained damage

Build variations

  • Reverse Melt Burst DPS (Quickswap DPS): Set up Pyro aura and buffs, then Melt Aloy Skill and Burst. The Burst is a single powerful Cryo hit.
  • Freeze Quickswap DPS (Cryo Sub DPS): Use Aloy for Cryo particles and a short Burst window while your Cryo carry handles field time.
  • Cryo Battery / Team Buffer (Support): Cast Skill for five Cryo particles, swap to the carry to catch particles, and Burst to refresh Noblesse.
  • Rushing Ice On-Field (On-field Cryo DPS): Get 4 Coil stacks from spaced Bomblet hits to unlock 10s Cryo Normal Attacks. This setup is inconsistent.
  • Accessible Melt Burst (Quickswap DPS): An accessible burst-oriented build that still prioritizes reactions and consistent Energy.

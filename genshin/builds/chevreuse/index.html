Chevreuse Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

Chevreuse build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Symphonist of Scents, Favonius Lance), artifacts (Noblesse Oblige), main stats, talent priority.

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Role: Support / Sub DPS

Chevreuse buffs Pyro and Electro teams by reducing enemy resistance after Overloaded. She also provides ATK buffs and healing. Aim for 40,000 HP on support builds; a damage-focused build is optional.

Best weapons for Chevreuse

  1. Symphonist of Scents: Premium ATK option that buffs a teammate when she heals. Best for ATK-scaling carries; balance HP and ER.
  2. Favonius Lance: ER and team particles on CRIT. A strong default for energy-hungry teams.
  3. Black Tassel: Easy HP% option to help reach her 40,000 HP buff cap.
  4. Dialogues of the Desert Sages: Event HP weapon that restores Energy when healing a teammate.
  5. Rightful Reward: Craftable HP% and Energy after she receives healing. Needs external healing; her own Skill may not trigger it.
  6. Staff of Homa: CD and HP for optional personal damage; not her default support weapon. Personal damage build.
  7. Staff of the Scarlet Sands: CR and EM-scaling ATK for a niche damage build. Personal damage build. Niche personal damage build only, not a standard support weapon.
  8. Prototype Starglitter: ER stat stick when stronger support options are unavailable.

Best artifacts for Chevreuse

  • Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs) · Best overall: Team ATK buff after Burst. Choose when she can Burst consistently. Main stats: HP%/ER · HP% · HP%.
  • Song of Days Past (4 pcs) · Support: Converts healing into extra damage; useful when Noblesse is covered. Main stats: HP%/ER · HP% · HP%.
  • Crimson Witch of Flames (4 pcs) · Sub DPS: Personal Pyro and reaction damage set. Only for a dedicated quickswap damage build. Main stats: ATK% · Pyro DMG · CR/CD.

Main stats

  • Sands: HP% / ER
  • Goblet: HP%
  • Circlet: HP% / Healing / CR

Stat targets

  • HP: Up to 40K
  • ER: If using Burst
  • CR: Favonius only

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 8–9+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 1–6

Best teams for Chevreuse

  • Shock and Awe: Chevreuse (Support), Cyno (DPS), Durin (Sub DPS), Fischl (Support). Cyno leads Electro damage, Fischl adds off-field Electro, and Durin supplies Pyro. Chevreuse provides healing, ATK buffs, and RES shred.
  • Emotional Overload: Chevreuse (Support), Varesa (DPS), Mavuika (Sub DPS), Iansan (Support). Varesa drives Electro damage with Mavuika’s Pyro and Iansan’s ATK buff. Chevreuse heals and reduces Pyro and Electro resistance.
  • Shots Fired: Chevreuse (Support), Arlecchino (DPS), Fischl (Sub DPS), Durin (Support). Arlecchino leads Pyro damage while Fischl and Durin add off-field pressure. Chevreuse supplies buffs, healing, and RES shred.
  • Inazu-watt?: Chevreuse (Support), Raiden Shogun (DPS), Xiangling (Sub DPS), Bennett (Support). Raiden batteries Xiangling’s Pyro Burst. Bennett boosts ATK and heals, while Chevreuse adds Overloaded-based RES shred.

Build variations

40K HP Support (Support)

Reach up to 40,000 HP to maximize her ATK buff while providing healing.

  • Weapons: Favonius Lance, Black Tassel, Dialogues of the Desert Sages
  • Artifacts: Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs); Song of Days Past (4 pcs)
  • Stats: HP% / HP% / HP%; ER if Bursting; CR with Favonius.
  • Teams: Only Pyro and Electro; trigger Overloaded for RES shred.
  • When it applies: No need to Burst every rotation

Quickswap Pyro Damage (Sub DPS)

Trade some HP-based support strength for personal Skill and Burst damage.

  • Weapons: Staff of Homa, Staff of the Scarlet Sands, Favonius Lance
  • Artifacts: Crimson Witch of Flames (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ATK% / Pyro DMG / CRIT; balance ER.
  • Teams: Pyro and Electro team that can spare field time for her.
  • When it applies: Below 40K HP reduces her ATK buff

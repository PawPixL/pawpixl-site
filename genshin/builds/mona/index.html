Mona Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams (7.2)

Mona build guide for Genshin Impact 7.2: best weapons (The Widsith, Skyward Atlas), artifacts (Emblem of Severed Fate), main stats, talent priority, teams.

Last updated: November 12, 2026 · Game version 7.2

Role: Burst DPS / Buffer

Mona is a Hydro Burst nuker and Omen buffer whose Burst amplifies damage enemies take. Her Skill briefly taunts and applies Hydro, but cannot sustain most Vaporize carries alone. With another Hexerei teammate and Witch's Homework completed, her attacks can extend Omen and enhance Vaporize.

Best weapons for Mona

  1. The Widsith: CRIT DMG and huge short-window buffs suit her Burst nuke; its random buff and 30s cooldown make damage inconsistent. Burst-nuke DPS; less reliable for repeated rotations.
  2. Skyward Atlas: High base ATK and ATK% provide consistent personal damage without depending on a random buff. Personal-damage builds.
  3. Lost Prayer to the Sacred Winds: CRIT Rate is useful, but its stacking Elemental DMG passive needs extended field time. Primarily an on-field Mona build.
  4. Favonius Codex: Energy Recharge and CRIT-triggered particles help fund her costly Burst and support the entire team. Land on-field CRIT hits; build adequate CRIT Rate.
  5. Thrilling Tales of Dragon Slayers: R5 grants a major ATK buff to the character switched in next. Lacks ER, so Burst uptime can be difficult. ATK-scaling carry; 20s buff cooldown. R5 strongly preferred.
  6. Prototype Amber: Refunds Energy after Burst and adds team healing, substantially lowering her demanding ER needs. Support or healer-lite setups. Higher refinements improve energy and healing.
  7. Solar Pearl: CRIT Rate and a Normal Attack-activated Burst DMG buff help damage-focused builds. Use a Normal Attack before Burst to activate its passive.
  8. Mappa Mare: Craftable EM weapon with reaction-triggered Elemental DMG stacks; useful for budget Vaporize nukes. Mona must trigger reactions; less useful for pure Omen support.

Best artifacts for Mona

  • Emblem of Severed Fate (4 pcs) · Best: Strong Burst-nuke set: converts required Energy Recharge into Burst DMG. Main stats: ATK%/ER · Hydro DMG · CRIT.
  • Noblesse Oblige + Heart of Depth (2 + 2 pcs): 2pc Burst DMG plus 2pc Hydro DMG for a balanced nuke set; no 4pc effects are active. Main stats: ATK%/EM · Hydro DMG · CRIT.
  • Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs) · Support: Support option: using Burst grants the whole party 20% ATK for 12s; avoid duplicate Noblesse holders. Main stats: ER · Hydro DMG · CRIT/ATK%.

Main stats

  • Sands: Energy Recharge / ATK% / Elemental Mastery
  • Goblet: Hydro DMG
  • Circlet: CRIT Rate / CRIT DMG

Stat targets

  • CRIT Rate: 60% - 75% (DPS)
  • CRIT DMG: 120% - 180%+ (DPS)
  • Energy Recharge: 180% - 300%+ (support varies)
  • Energy Recharge: 150% - 230% (Favonius; varies)
  • Energy Recharge: 120% - 180% (Amber; varies)

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1 (Level 8+ at C6 DPS)
  • Elemental Skill: Level 8-9+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 8-9+

Best teams for Mona

  • Frostbound Prophecy: Mona (Buffer / Hydro Enabler), Skirk (On-field DPS), Furina (Off-field DPS / Buffer), Escoffier (Healer / Debuffer). Mona's Omen amplifies Skirk's damage; Furina adds Hydro and buffs while Escoffier heals and shreds Cryo/Hydro RES. ER needs can be high.
  • Morgana Starfall: Mona (t), Ganyu (On-field DPS), Venti (Crowd Control / Debuffer), Diona (Shielder / Healer). Classic Freeze AoE team. Cryo and Mona's Hydro help sustain Freeze and prolong Omen; Venti groups foes and Diona sustains.
  • Hexerei Flamecraft: Mona (Buffer / Hydro Enabler), Durin (Off-field DPS / Buffer), Xingqiu (Off-field DPS / Hydro Enabler), Sucrose (EM Buffer / Debuffer). Mona drives Hydro for Durin's off-field Pyro Vaporize; Xingqiu stabilizes Hydro. With Witch's Homework, Mona and Sucrose enable Hexerei synergy.
  • Moonlit Divination: Mona (Buffer / Hydro Enabler), Columbina (Off-field DPS / Buffer), Ineffa (Off-field DPS / Shielder), Lauma (Dendro Buffer / Enabler). A Lunar-reaction setup: Mona's Omen and support gear help Columbina, while Ineffa enables Lunar-Charged and Lauma adds reaction support.

Pros

  • Powerful Omen damage buff
  • High Burst nuke potential
  • Hexerei synergy
  • Flexible support weapons

Cons

  • Very high Burst ER needs
  • Limited off-field Hydro
  • Short base Omen duration
  • Setup-sensitive Vaporize

Build variations

Omen Support (Support)

Prioritize teamwide damage rather than Mona's Bubble pop. Swap to the carry after setting up Omen.

  • Weapons: Favonius Codex, Thrilling Tales of Dragon Slayers, Prototype Amber
  • Artifacts: Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs); Tenacity of the Millelith (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ER until Burst every rotation; use CRIT Rate with Favonius. Personal damage is secondary.
  • Teams: Bring a carry who deals damage during Omen; TTDS works best with an ATK-scaling carry.
  • When it applies: TTDS needs sufficient ER and proper swap order; do not duplicate Noblesse.
  • Notes: ER can exceed 250% in solo-Hydro teams. Prototype Amber reduces requirements.

Hexerei Omen Buffer (Support)

Use Normal/Charged hits during the setup to extend Omen and, in Vaporize teams, build Astral Glow stacks.

  • Weapons: Favonius Codex, Thrilling Tales of Dragon Slayers, Prototype Amber
  • Artifacts: Celestial Gift (4 pcs); Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ER first, then Favonius CRIT Rate; Hydro DMG and CRIT are optional personal-damage stats.
  • Teams: Needs another Hexerei teammate and completion of Witch's Homework to activate Mona's extra passive.
  • When it applies: Requires Witch's Homework and at least one other Hexerei character.
  • Notes: Celestial Gift is usually her preferred Hexerei support set unless another teammate already carries it.

Freeze Omen Support (Support)

Apply Cryo before Mona's Burst when attempting Freeze-based Bubble extension. Shatter breaks the setup.

  • Weapons: Favonius Codex, Thrilling Tales of Dragon Slayers, Prototype Amber
  • Artifacts: Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs); Tenacity of the Millelith (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Prioritize enough ER for every Burst; TTDS can increase ER demands.
  • Teams: Use strong Cryo application; pair a Freeze carry such as Ganyu or Skirk with sustain/energy support.
  • When it applies: Best against freezable enemies; bosses and Shatter limit extended Omen.
  • Notes: Mona's Skill alone cannot replace a sustained Hydro applicator in every team.

C6 Charged Attack DPS (On-field DPS)

Use Omen windows to unleash amplified Charged Attacks; this is an investment-heavy alternative to support Mona.

  • Weapons: The Widsith, Skyward Atlas, Lost Prayer to the Sacred Winds, Solar Pearl
  • Artifacts: A Day Carved From Rising Winds (4 pcs); Heart of Depth (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ATK% or EM Sands / Hydro DMG / CRIT. Level Normal Attacks for Charged Attack damage.
  • Teams: Needs C6; strong off-field Pyro for Vaporize and teammates supporting her on-field time.
  • When it applies: C6 strongly recommended; Witch's Homework/Hexerei rules are separate.
  • Notes: Her new on-field recommendations continue to receive theorycrafting updates.

Lunar-Charged Flex (Hydro Support)

Mona provides Omen and Hydro support in Lunar-Charged teams, but is not necessarily the best artifact holder.

  • Weapons: Favonius Codex, Hakushin Ring, Prototype Amber
  • Artifacts: Silken Moon's Serenade (4 pcs); Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ER to Burst reliably; invest EM only if Mona actually triggers relevant reactions.
  • Teams: Use an Electro Lunar-Charged enabler such as Ineffa plus a reaction-focused Hydro carry.
  • When it applies: Hakushin Ring needs Mona to trigger a reaction on-field; prioritize team-set coverage.
  • Notes: Silken Moon may be inferior if another ally can use its full bonus.

Budget Burst Support (Support)

Choose craftable Prototype Amber when ER is difficult; TTDS is a free buff option once energy is solved.

  • Weapons: Prototype Amber, Thrilling Tales of Dragon Slayers, Mappa Mare
  • Artifacts: Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs); Emblem of Severed Fate (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ER Sands often required; do not force damage stats at the expense of Burst uptime.
  • Teams: Pairs well with accessible teams that can funnel particles or use Favonius weapons.
  • When it applies: Crafting and free catalyst access vary by account.
  • Notes: A dedicated healer may still be needed; Amber's healing is modest.

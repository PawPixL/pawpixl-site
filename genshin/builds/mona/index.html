Role: Burst DPS / Buffer

Mona is a Hydro Burst nuker and Omen buffer whose Burst amplifies damage enemies take. Her Skill briefly taunts and applies Hydro, but cannot sustain most Vaporize carries alone. With another Hexerei teammate and Witch's Homework completed, her attacks can extend Omen and enhance Vaporize.

Pros

  • Powerful Omen damage buff
  • High Burst nuke potential
  • Hexerei synergy
  • Flexible support weapons

Cons

  • Very high Burst ER needs
  • Limited off-field Hydro
  • Short base Omen duration
  • Setup-sensitive Vaporize

Build variations

  • Omen Support (Support): Prioritize teamwide damage rather than Mona's Bubble pop. Swap to the carry after setting up Omen.
  • Hexerei Omen Buffer (Support): Use Normal/Charged hits during the setup to extend Omen and, in Vaporize teams, build Astral Glow stacks.
  • Freeze Omen Support (Support): Apply Cryo before Mona's Burst when attempting Freeze-based Bubble extension. Shatter breaks the setup.
  • C6 Charged Attack DPS (On-field DPS): Use Omen windows to unleash amplified Charged Attacks; this is an investment-heavy alternative to support Mona.
  • Lunar-Charged Flex (Hydro Support): Mona provides Omen and Hydro support in Lunar-Charged teams, but is not necessarily the best artifact holder.
  • Budget Burst Support (Support): Choose craftable Prototype Amber when ER is difficult; TTDS is a free buff option once energy is solved.

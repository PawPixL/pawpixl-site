Role: Support

Escoffier heals with her Burst, deals Cryo damage off-field, and reduces Cryo and Hydro resistance. Her full shred is strongest in teams made entirely of Cryo and Hydro characters; other teams sacrifice some of that bonus.

Best weapons for Escoffier

  1. Symphonist of Scents
  2. Engulfing Lightning
  3. Calamity Queller
  4. Fractured Halo
  5. Staff of Homa
  6. Favonius Lance
  7. Tamayuratei no Ohanashi
  8. Deathmatch

Best artifacts

  • Golden Troupe (4 pcs) · Sub DPS
  • Tenacity of the Millelith (4 pcs) · Support
  • Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs) · Support

Main stats

  • Sands: ATK% / ER
  • Goblet: Cryo DMG / ATK%
  • Circlet: CR / CD

Stat targets

  • ER: Team-based
  • CR: 60–75%
  • CD: 140–200%+
  • ATK: Team-based

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 9+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 9+

Best teams

  • Hell's Kitchenette: Escoffier, Skirk, Furina, Vodyanitsa
  • Grand MasterChef: Escoffier, Varka, Prune, Shenhe
  • Ice Guys Finish Last: Escoffier, Wriothesley, Xiangling, Bennett
  • We Have Skirk at Home: Escoffier, Kaeya, Xingqiu, Rosaria

Pros

  • Strong RES shred
  • Reliable healing
  • Good off-field damage
  • Excellent Cryo support

Cons

  • Team restrictive
  • Cryo/Hydro focused
  • Wants some ER
  • Restricted RES shred

Build variations

  • Pure Cryo / Hydro (Healer / Sub DPS): Combine strong off-field Cryo damage, healing, and full Cryo/Hydro resistance reduction.
  • Team ATK Buffer (Support): Sacrifice some personal damage to increase the active damage dealer’s output.
  • Melt Flex (Off-field DPS): A flexible Cryo application and healing option outside pure Cryo/Hydro teams.

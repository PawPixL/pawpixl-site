Escoffier Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

Escoffier build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Symphonist of Scents, Engulfing Lightning), artifacts (Golden Troupe), main stats, talent priority.

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Role: Support

Escoffier heals with her Burst, deals Cryo damage off-field, and reduces Cryo and Hydro resistance. Her full shred is strongest in teams made entirely of Cryo and Hydro characters; other teams sacrifice some of that bonus.

Best weapons for Escoffier

  1. Symphonist of Scents: Signature CD and ATK buffs after healing. Use Burst to activate the healing synergy.
  2. Engulfing Lightning: ER and ER-to-ATK conversion. Best when she needs substantial ER to Burst often.
  3. Calamity Queller: High Base ATK, elemental damage and increasing ATK while off-field.
  4. Fractured Halo: Strong offensive stat stick for her personal damage.
  5. Staff of Homa: Provides CRIT DMG, increases HP, and converts Max HP into bonus ATK for stronger damage.
  6. Favonius Lance: ER and team particles on CRIT. Choose when the whole team needs Energy. Requires CR and an on-field hit to trigger.
  7. Tamayuratei no Ohanashi: Event ER weapon for smoother Burst uptime and some ATK.
  8. Deathmatch: BP CR option for personal Skill damage.

Best artifacts for Escoffier

  • Golden Troupe (4 pcs) · Sub DPS: Boosts her off-field Skill damage; strongest when personal damage is the priority. Main stats: ATK%/ER · Cryo · CR/CD.
  • Tenacity of the Millelith (4 pcs) · Support: Team ATK buff from recurring Skill hits. Good when supporting an ATK carry. Main stats: ATK%/ER · Cryo · CR/CD.
  • Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs) · Support: Team ATK buff after Burst; choose when no ally holds this set. Main stats: ATK%/ER · Cryo · CR/CD.

Main stats

  • Circlet: CR / CD

Stat targets

  • ER: Team-based

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 9+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 9+

Best teams for Escoffier

  • Hell's Kitchenette: Escoffier (Healer), Skirk (DPS), Furina (Sub DPS), Vodyanitsa (Support). Skirk leads Cryo damage while Furina and Vodyanitsa provide Hydro support. Escoffier heals and provides strong Cryo/Hydro RES shred.
  • Grand MasterChef: Escoffier (Healer), Varka (DPS), Prune (Sub DPS), Shenhe (Support). Varka leads with Prune and Shenhe supporting. Escoffier heals and applies Cryo, but mixed elements reduce her RES shred.
  • Ice Guys Finish Last: Escoffier (Healer), Wriothesley (DPS), Xiangling (Sub DPS), Bennett (Support). Wriothesley drives Cryo damage while Xiangling enables Melt. Bennett buffs ATK; Escoffier heals and adds off-field Cryo.
  • We Have Skirk at Home: Escoffier (Healer / Sub DPS), Kaeya (On-field Cryo), Xingqiu (Hydro support), Rosaria (Cryo Sub DPS). Kaeya drives Cryo damage, Xingqiu provides Hydro and Rosaria adds Cryo. Escoffier heals and maintains full Cryo/Hydro RES shred.

Build variations

Pure Cryo / Hydro (Healer / Sub DPS)

Combine strong off-field Cryo damage, healing, and full Cryo/Hydro resistance reduction.

  • Weapons: Symphonist of Scents, Calamity Queller, Fractured Halo
  • Artifacts: Golden Troupe (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ATK% or ER / Cryo / CRIT.
  • Teams: All teammates Cryo or Hydro for maximum shred.
  • When it applies: Full shred requires only Cryo/Hydro party members

Team ATK Buffer (Support)

Sacrifice some personal damage to increase the active damage dealer’s output.

  • Weapons: Symphonist of Scents, Engulfing Lightning, Tamayuratei no Ohanashi
  • Artifacts: Tenacity of the Millelith (4 pcs); Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Meet ER to use Burst, then ATK and CRIT.
  • Teams: ATK-scaling carry; do not duplicate support sets.
  • When it applies: Tenacity favors recurring Skill hits

Melt Flex (Off-field DPS)

A flexible Cryo application and healing option outside pure Cryo/Hydro teams.

  • Weapons: Symphonist of Scents, Staff of Homa, Deathmatch
  • Artifacts: Golden Troupe (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ATK% / Cryo / CRIT with enough ER.
  • Teams: Pyro damage dealer; accept reduced RES shred.
  • When it applies: Less Cryo/Hydro RES shred in mixed teams

Escoffier talents

Normal Attack: Kitchen Skills

Normal Attack
Performs up to 3 consecutive spear strikes.

Charged Attack
Consumes a certain amount of Stamina to perform an upward thrust.

Plunging Attack
Plunges from mid-air to strike the ground below, damaging opponents along the path and dealing AoE DMG upon impact.

Elemental Skill: Low-Temperature Cooking

Cooldown: Press: 15s / Hold: 6s

Behold the true meaning of "cooking"! Escoffier will demonstrate different cooking techniques when you Tap or Hold this skill, but rest assured, each of them is Fontaine's pride and joy.

Tap
Activates her All-Spectrum Multi-Use Cooking Mek in its Cold Storage mode and deals AoE Cryo DMG to nearby opponents.

Cooking Mek: Cold Storage Mode
Follows the current active character and fires Frosty Parfaits at nearby opponents at intervals, dealing Cryo DMG.

Arkhe: Ousia
At intervals, Escoffier will summon a Surging Blade when activating her Cooking Mek in Cold Storage mode, dealing Ousia-aligned AoE Cryo DMG.

Hold
Activates her All-Spectrum Multi-Use Cooking Mek's Off-the-Cuff Cookery Mode.

Elemental Burst: Scoring Cuts

Cooldown: 15s · Energy cost: 60

Displaying her exceptional knife skills, Escoffier deals AoE Cryo DMG and restores HP to all nearby party members based on her ATK.

Passive talent: Better to Salivate Than Medicate

After using the Elemental Burst Scoring Cuts, Escoffier will obtain the Rehab Diet effect for 9s: Restores HP for nearby active characters every 1s based on 138.24% of Escoffier's ATK.

Passive talent: Inspiration-Immersed Seasoning

When there are 1/2/3/4 Hydro or Cryo characters in the party, Escoffier will decrease the Hydro RES and Cryo RES of any opponents hit by her Elemental Skill, Low-Temperature Cooking, or her Elemental Burst, Scoring Cuts, by 5%/10%/15%/55% for 12s.

Passive talent: Constant Off-the-Cuff Cookery

When using the Hold form of her Elemental Skill Low-Temperature Cooking, Escoffier will activate her All-Spectrum Multi-Use Cooking Mek in its Off-the-Cuff Cookery mode.

Cooking Mek: Off-the-Cuff Cookery Mode
· Places the Cooking Mek on the field. The Cooking Mek can absorb elemental attacks. When the absorbed elemental energy hits a certain limit, Escoffier can convert the ingredients placed within it into wonderful food.
· Escoffier needs time to purchase new ingredients, so she can only make a certain number of dishes like this each week. The number of dishes Escoffier can make resets every Monday at 4:00 AM .

Escoffier constellations

C1: Pre-Dinner Dance for Your Taste Buds

When 4 party members are Hydro or Cryo, all nearby party members will have their Cryo DMG CRIT DMG increased by 60% for 15s after Escoffier uses her Elemental Skill Low-Temperature Cooking or Elemental Burst Scoring Cuts.
You must first unlock the Ascension Talent "Inspiration-Immersed Seasoning."

C2: Fresh, Fragrant Stew Is an Art

When Escoffier activates her Cooking Mek in its Cold Storage Mode, she will gain the "Freshly-Prepped Delicacy" effect for 15s. During this time, Escoffier will gain 5 Cold Dish stacks. When nearby active characters apart from Escoffier deal Cryo DMG with a Normal, Charged, or Plunging Attack, or an Elemental Skill or Elemental Burst, they will expend 1 Cold Dish stack to increase their DMG dealt by 240% of Escoffier's ATK.
If you hit multiple opponents with a single instance of Cryo DMG, Cold Dish stacks will be consumed based on the number of opponents hit.

C3: The Bakery Magic of Caramel Browning

Increases the Level of Low-Temperature Cooking by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C4: Secret Rosemary Recipe

Rehab Diet lasts 6 more seconds. While it is active, you have a chance of healing 100% extra HP and restoring 2 Energy to Escoffier when you trigger healing through Rehab Diet. This chance corresponds to Escoffier's CRIT Rate. You can trigger this effect 7 times during any one Rehab Diet's duration.
You must first unlock the Ascension Talent "Better to Salivate Than Medicate."

C5: Symphony of a Thousand Sauces

Increases the Level of Scoring Cuts by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C6: Tea Parties Bursting With Color

Cooking Mek: Cold Storage Mode is enhanced:
· When your party's active character's Normal, Charged, or Plunging Attacks hit opponents, Cooking Mek: Cold Storage Mode will fire off another Special-Grade Frosty Parfait, dealing 500% of Escoffier's ATK as AoE Cryo DMG. This DMG is considered Elemental Skill DMG.
This effect can be triggered once every 0.5s, and this effect can be triggered 6 times within the duration of one Cooking Mek: Cold Storage Mode.

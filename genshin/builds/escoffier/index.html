Role: Support

Escoffier is a Cryo sub-DPS and healer who excels in Cryo/Hydro teams. She provides strong off field damage while shredding enemy RES. Also, she cooks ice.

Best weapons for Escoffier

  1. Symphonist of Scents
  2. Engulfing Lightning
  3. Calamity Queller
  4. Fractured Halo
  5. Staff of Homa
  6. Sacrificer's Staff
  7. Tamayuratei no Ohanashi
  8. Deathmatch

Best artifacts

  • Golden Troupe (4 pcs) · Sub DPS
  • Tenacity of the Millelith (4 pcs) · Support
  • Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs) · Support

Main stats

  • Sands: ER% / ATK%
  • Goblet: Cryo DMG Bonus / ATK%
  • Circlet: Crit Rate / Crit DMG

Stat targets

  • ATK: 2,000+
  • Crit Rate: 60% - 70%
  • Crit DMG: 120%+
  • Energy Recharge: 160% - 180%

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 9+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 9+

Best teams

  • Hell's Kitchenette: Escoffier, Skirk, Furina, Vodyanitsa
  • Grand MasterChef: Escoffier, Varka, Prune, Shenhe
  • Ice Guys Finish Last: Escoffier, Wriothesley, Xiangling, Bennett
  • We Have Skirk at Home: Escoffier, Freminet, Xingqiu, Rosaria

Pros

  • Strong RES shred
  • Reliable healing
  • Good off-field damage
  • Excellent Cryo support

Cons

  • Team restrictive
  • Cryo/Hydro focused
  • Wants some ER
  • Restricted RES shred

Traveler (Cryo) Build Guide: Weapons, Artifacts and Teams (7.2)

Traveler (Cryo) build guide for Genshin Impact 7.2: best weapons (Exaiphanes Blade, Whitelake Frostfeather), artifacts (Disenchantment in Deep Shadow).

Last updated: November 12, 2026 · Game version 7.2

Role: Main DPS / Sub DPS

Cryo Traveler is a flexible Cryo DMG dealer who powers Stellar-Conduct & Stellar Swirl teams. They fight on the field in Stellar-Conduct & offer off-field DMG in Stellar Swirl. Their strongest attacks rely on building Frostglow stacks before using their Burst.

Best weapons for Traveler (Cryo)

  1. Exaiphanes Blade: The Traveler’s free exclusive sword adds CR, ATK & Energy restoration.
  2. Whitelake Frostfeather: Provides strong offensive stats & excellent Stellar DMG potential, although the free Exaiphanes Blade competes closely.
  3. Primordial Jade Cutter: Grants a large amount of CR & converts some HP into ATK. Watch for CR overcapping.
  4. Uraku Misugiri: Offers abundant CRIT DMG, but much of its passive is less useful for the Traveler’s main Stellar damage.
  5. Emberwell: Craftable sword that grants EM & improves ATK & Stellar DMG after triggering reactions. Use in Stellar reaction teams. Higher refinements improve its value.
  6. Harbinger of Dawn: An inexpensive CRIT stat option when health stays high, but its low Base ATK limits Stellar damage. Keep HP above the passive threshold.
  7. Favonius Sword: ER and team particles on CRIT. Sacrifices personal damage for team Energy support.

Best artifacts for Traveler (Cryo)

  • Disenchantment in Deep Shadow (4 pcs) · Best Stellar-Conduct: Best personal DMG set for on-field Stellar-Conduct. Boosts reaction DMG & grants conditional CR. Main stats: ATK% / ATK% / CRIT.
  • Heart of the Furnace (4 pcs) · Stellar Swirl Support: Boosts team Stellar DMG after the wearer deals Stellar DMG. Useful when another teammate is not already wearing it. Main stats: ATK% / ATK% / CRIT.
  • Tenacity of the Millelith (4 pcs) · Team Support: Off-field Skill hits grant teammates an ATK buff. A practical support alternative in Stellar Swirl teams. Main stats: ATK% / ATK% / CRIT.

Best teams for Traveler (Cryo)

  • Frostbound Scholars: Traveler (Cryo) (Main DPS), Yae Miko (Sub DPS), Odette (Support), Escoffier (Healer). Yae supplies Electro for Stellar-Conduct, Odette raises Stellar damage, and Qiqi keeps the team healthy while Traveler stays on the field.
  • Frost and Thunder: Traveler (Cryo) (Main DPS), Yae Miko (Sub DPS), Odette (Support), Alyosha (Healer). Yae and Odette boost Stellar-Conduct damage while Alyosha offers healing and extra buffs. Traveler uses field time to build Frostglow.
  • Swirl Society: Traveler (Cryo) (Sub DPS), Yumemizuki Mizuki (Main DPS), Sucrose (Support), Odette (Support). Mizuki drives Stellar Swirl while Traveler supplies Cryo and Burst damage. Odette boosts Stellar reactions and Sucrose adds grouping and EM.
  • Budget Blizzard: Traveler (Cryo) (Main DPS), Fischl (Sub DPS), Rosaria (Sub DPS), Diona (Healer). Fischl applies Electro for Stellar-Conduct while Rosaria adds Cryo damage. Diona provides shields and healing for a budget option.

Build variations

On-field Conduct DPS (On-field DPS)

Use on-field Cryo attacks in a dedicated Conduct team.

  • Weapons: Exaiphanes Blade, Primordial Jade Cutter
  • Artifacts: Disenchantment in Deep Shadow (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Balance CRIT and relevant scaling; meet Burst needs.
  • Teams: Electro teammate to enable Stellar-Conduct.
  • When it applies: Specific reaction setup required.

Off-field Conduct Utility (Off-field DPS / Support)

Provide Cryo application and team utility rather than on-field time.

  • Weapons: Whitelake Frostfeather, Emberwell
  • Artifacts: Heart of the Furnace (4 pcs); Tenacity of the Millelith (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ER to maintain off-field application; personal damage secondary.
  • Teams: Electro teammate and another active carry.
  • When it applies: Set choice depends on whether another ally holds the buff.

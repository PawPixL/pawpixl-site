Traveler (Cryo) Build Guide: Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

Traveler (Cryo) build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Exaiphanes Blade, Whitelake Frostfeather), artifacts (Disenchantment in Deep Shadow).

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Role: Main DPS / Sub DPS

Cryo Traveler is a flexible Cryo DMG dealer who powers Stellar-Conduct & Stellar Swirl teams. They fight on the field in Stellar-Conduct & offer off-field DMG in Stellar Swirl. Their strongest attacks rely on building Frostglow stacks before using their Burst.

Best weapons for Traveler (Cryo)

  1. Exaiphanes Blade: The Traveler’s free exclusive sword adds CR, ATK & Energy restoration.
  2. Whitelake Frostfeather: Provides strong offensive stats & excellent Stellar DMG potential, although the free Exaiphanes Blade competes closely.
  3. Primordial Jade Cutter: Grants a large amount of CR & converts some HP into ATK. Watch for CR overcapping.
  4. Uraku Misugiri: Offers abundant CRIT DMG, but much of its passive is less useful for the Traveler’s main Stellar damage.
  5. Emberwell: Craftable sword that grants EM & improves ATK & Stellar DMG after triggering reactions. Use in Stellar reaction teams. Higher refinements improve its value.
  6. Harbinger of Dawn: An inexpensive CRIT stat option when health stays high, but its low Base ATK limits Stellar damage. Keep HP above the passive threshold.
  7. Favonius Sword: ER and team particles on CRIT. Sacrifices personal damage for team Energy support.

Best artifacts for Traveler (Cryo)

  • Disenchantment in Deep Shadow (4 pcs) · Best Stellar-Conduct: Best personal DMG set for on-field Stellar-Conduct. Boosts reaction DMG & grants conditional CR. Main stats: ATK% / ATK% / CRIT.
  • Heart of the Furnace (4 pcs) · Stellar Swirl Support: Boosts team Stellar DMG after the wearer deals Stellar DMG. Useful when another teammate is not already wearing it. Main stats: ATK% / ATK% / CRIT.
  • Tenacity of the Millelith (4 pcs) · Team Support: Off-field Skill hits grant teammates an ATK buff. A practical support alternative in Stellar Swirl teams. Main stats: ATK% / ATK% / CRIT.

Best teams for Traveler (Cryo)

  • Frostbound Scholars: Traveler (Cryo) (Main DPS), Yae Miko (Sub DPS), Odette (Support), Escoffier (Healer). Yae supplies Electro for Stellar-Conduct, Odette raises Stellar damage, and Qiqi keeps the team healthy while Traveler stays on the field.
  • Frost and Thunder: Traveler (Cryo) (Main DPS), Yae Miko (Sub DPS), Odette (Support), Alyosha (Healer). Yae and Odette boost Stellar-Conduct damage while Alyosha offers healing and extra buffs. Traveler uses field time to build Frostglow.
  • Swirl Society: Traveler (Cryo) (Sub DPS), Yumemizuki Mizuki (Main DPS), Sucrose (Support), Odette (Support). Mizuki drives Stellar Swirl while Traveler supplies Cryo and Burst damage. Odette boosts Stellar reactions and Sucrose adds grouping and EM.
  • Budget Blizzard: Traveler (Cryo) (Main DPS), Fischl (Sub DPS), Rosaria (Sub DPS), Diona (Healer). Fischl applies Electro for Stellar-Conduct while Rosaria adds Cryo damage. Diona provides shields and healing for a budget option.

Build variations

On-field Conduct DPS (On-field DPS)

Use on-field Cryo attacks in a dedicated Conduct team.

  • Weapons: Exaiphanes Blade, Primordial Jade Cutter
  • Artifacts: Disenchantment in Deep Shadow (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Balance CRIT and relevant scaling; meet Burst needs.
  • Teams: Electro teammate to enable Stellar-Conduct.
  • When it applies: Specific reaction setup required.

Off-field Conduct Utility (Off-field DPS / Support)

Provide Cryo application and team utility rather than on-field time.

  • Weapons: Whitelake Frostfeather, Emberwell
  • Artifacts: Heart of the Furnace (4 pcs); Tenacity of the Millelith (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ER to maintain off-field application; personal damage secondary.
  • Teams: Electro teammate and another active carry.
  • When it applies: Set choice depends on whether another ally holds the buff.

Traveler (Cryo) talents

Normal Attack: Foreign Frostglint

Normal Attack
Performs up to 5 rapid strikes.

Charged Attack
Consumes a certain amount of Stamina to unleash 2 rapid sword strikes.

Plunging Attack
Plunges from mid-air to strike the ground below, damaging opponents along the path and dealing AoE DMG upon impact.

Elemental Skill: Ice Fog Piercer

Cooldown: 15s

Stabs at the opponent with the Traveler's weapon, which releases an ice-cold fog. This deals Cryo DMG to opponents up ahead and forms a Frostpierce Star next to the Traveler.

Frostpierce Star
· Follows your own party members around the field and periodically fires ice crystals at nearby opponents, dealing Cryo DMG.
Radiance: Stellar-Conduct: The Frostpierce Star will no longer fire ice crystals at opponents at intervals but will instead fire an ice crystal at opponents in a coordinated attack when the Traveler hits an opponent with a Normal Attack, Charged Attack, or Plunging Attack. This effect can trigger once every 0.2s.
· When an opponent is hit with an ice crystal, the Traveler gains 1 stack of Frostglow (max 8 stacks).
· When not in combat, Frostglow expires after 30s.

Elemental Burst: Frostbound Javelin

Cooldown: 15s · Energy cost: 60

Generates an ice javelin with the power of Cryo, then directs it at enemies to deal multiple instances of Cryo DMG.
When cast, the Traveler consumes all existing stacks of Frostglow, which in turn increases the DMG dealt by this Elemental Burst. When 8 stacks of Frostglow are consumed, the number of DMG instances caused by the javelins is also increased.

Radiance: Stellar Glimmer: DMG from the current Elemental Burst is changed to Cryo DMG of the corresponding Stellar Glimmer reaction type.

Passive talent: Ever-Keen Frost

Radiance: Stellar-Conduct: When a Frostpierce Star is on the field, DMG from the Traveler's Normal Attacks, Charged Attacks, and Plunging Attacks are converted to Cryo DMG that cannot be overridden by another elemental infusion, and DMG dealt is also increased by 80% of the Traveler's ATK.

Passive talent: Lucent Ice

The Traveler's Elemental Mastery is increased by 8% of their ATK. Up to 160 Elemental Mastery can be gained in this way.

Passive talent: Stellar Jubilee: Illusory Frostmirror

The Traveler will enter the Radiance: Stellar-Conduct state when inside a Polestar Field, or the Radiance: Stellar Swirl state for 8s after a nearby party member triggers a Stellar Swirl reaction.
When a party member triggers a Superconduct or Cryo Swirl reaction, it becomes a Stellar-Conduct or Stellar Swirl reaction instead, and the Base DMG of said reaction is also increased by 0.35% for every 100 points of the Traveler's ATK. A maximum 7% increase can be obtained in this way.

Traveler (Cryo) constellations

C1: Somber Freeze

The Traveler regenerates 5 Elemental Energy when they deal Stellar Glimmer DMG. This effect can trigger once every 0.5s.

C2: Frostfall Reverberation

The active character's Elemental Mastery is increased by 60 for 5s when an opponent is hit by an ice crystal fired by the Elemental Skill Ice Fog Piercer.
Additionally, when a character affected by the aforementioned effect triggers a Stellar Glimmer reaction or deals Stellar Glimmer reaction DMG, the Elemental Mastery boost described above will be increased to 120 for its duration.

C3: Glacial Shard

Increases the Level of the Elemental Burst Frostbound Javelin by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C4: Enduring Ice

The duration of the Frostpierce Star created by the Elemental Skill Ice Fog Piercer is increased by 25%.

C5: Bittercold Fog

Increases the Level of the Elemental Skill Ice Fog Piercer by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C6: Brumal Grimfrost

Every stack of Frostglow consumed when using the Elemental Burst Frostbound Javelin increases Stellar Glimmer reaction DMG dealt by other party members by 5% for 15s. A maximum 40% increase can be obtained in this way.

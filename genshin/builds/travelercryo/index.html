Traveler (Cryo) Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts & Teams

Traveler (Cryo) build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Exaiphanes Blade, Whitelake Frostfeather), artifacts (Disenchantment in Deep Shadow), main…

Role: Main DPS / Sub DPS

Cryo Traveler is a flexible Cryo DMG dealer who powers Stellar-Conduct & Stellar Swirl teams. They fight on the field in Stellar-Conduct & offer off-field DMG in Stellar Swirl. Their strongest attacks rely on building Frostglow stacks before using their Burst.

Best weapons for Traveler (Cryo)

  1. Exaiphanes Blade
  2. Whitelake Frostfeather
  3. Primordial Jade Cutter
  4. Uraku Misugiri
  5. Emberwell
  6. Harbinger of Dawn
  7. Favonius Sword

Best artifacts

  • Disenchantment in Deep Shadow (4 pcs) · Best Stellar-Conduct
  • Heart of the Furnace (4 pcs) · Stellar Swirl Support
  • Tenacity of the Millelith (4 pcs) · Team Support

Talent priority

Best teams

  • Frostbound Scholars: Traveler (Cryo), Yae Miko, Odette, Escoffier
  • Frost and Thunder: Traveler (Cryo), Yae Miko, Odette, Alyosha

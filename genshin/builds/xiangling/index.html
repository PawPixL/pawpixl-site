Xiangling Build Guide | Best Weapons, Artifacts & Teams

Xiangling build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Staff of the Scarlet Sands, Engulfing Lightning), artifacts (Emblem of Severed Fate), main stats…

Role: Sub DPS / Off-Field DPS

Xiangling is an off-field Pyro DMG dealer whose Pyronado delivers powerful AoE DMG and reaction application. Cast it inside Bennett’s Burst to snapshot his ATK buff, then use a Hydro or Electro teammate to enable reactions.

Best weapons for Xiangling

  1. Staff of the Scarlet Sands
  2. Engulfing Lightning
  3. Staff of Homa
  4. "The Catch"
  5. Wavebreaker's Fin
  6. Favonius Lance
  7. Dragon's Bane
  8. Kitain Cross Spear

Best artifacts

  • Emblem of Severed Fate (4 pcs)
  • Crimson Witch of Flames (4 pcs)
  • Noblesse Oblige + Crimson Witch of Flames (2 + 2 pcs)

Main stats

  • Sands: Energy Recharge / Elemental Mastery / ATK%
  • Goblet: Pyro DMG Bonus
  • Circlet: CRIT Rate / CRIT DMG

Stat targets

  • Energy Recharge: 190% - 250% (team-dependent)
  • CRIT Rate: 60% - 75%
  • CRIT DMG: 120% - 170%+
  • Elemental Mastery: 100 - 250 (Vaporize)
  • ATK: varies with Bennett buffs

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 6-8
  • Elemental Burst: Level 8-9

Best teams

  • National Kitchen: Xiangling, Xingqiu, Bennett, Sucrose
  • Raiden Hot Pot: Xiangling, Raiden Shogun, Xingqiu, Bennett
  • International Heat: Xiangling, Tartaglia, Kaedehara Kazuha, Bennett
  • Overload Express: Xiangling, Bennett, Fischl, Chevreuse

Pros

  • Strong off-field AoE
  • Excellent Pyro application
  • Powerful Vaporize teams
  • Outstanding free weapon

Cons

  • High Energy needs
  • Bennett often preferred
  • Needs particle funneling
  • Guoba positioning matters

Build variations

  • Accessible National (Off-field Pyro DPS): An accessible way to learn Vaporize Xiangling without premium weapons. Catch R5 is a standout option.
  • Chevreuse Overloaded (Off-field Pyro DPS): Use in Pyro/Electro damage teams where Chevreuse improves damage and RES shred.
  • No-Bennett Pyronado (Off-field Pyro DPS): For teams using Bennett on the other side, accepting much more demanding Burst uptime.
  • Burgeon Trigger (Reaction DPS): Triggers Burgeon via off-field Pyro; best suited to encounters where AoE cores are available.
  • Early-Game Mixed Set (Off-field Pyro DPS): A transitional setup while farming a proper 4pc Emblem set.

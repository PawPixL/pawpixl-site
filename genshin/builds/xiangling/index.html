Xiangling Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

Xiangling build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Staff of the Scarlet Sands, Engulfing Lightning), artifacts (Emblem of Severed Fate), main stats.

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Role: Sub DPS / Off-Field DPS

Xiangling is an off-field Pyro DMG dealer whose Pyronado delivers powerful AoE DMG and reaction application. Cast it inside Bennett’s Burst to snapshot his ATK buff, then use a Hydro or Electro teammate to enable reactions.

Best weapons for Xiangling

  1. Staff of the Scarlet Sands: Premium CRIT Rate and EM-to-ATK conversion benefit reaction-focused Pyronado. Do not waste field time stacking its passive. Best when reactions and EM investment matter.
  2. Engulfing Lightning: Converts ER into ATK and buffs Burst DMG.
  3. Staff of Homa: Large CRIT DMG and HP-based ATK make it a strong damage option, though its low-HP bonus is not always active. Meet ER needs through artifacts and teammates.
  4. "The Catch": Outstanding free choice: ER.
  5. Wavebreaker's Fin: Boosts Burst damage based on the team’s combined Energy costs; competitive with high refinements and strong CRIT artifacts. Best with high-cost Bursts. R5 preferred.
  6. Favonius Lance: Generates neutral particles on an on-field CRIT hit, reducing team Energy pressure; lower personal damage than offensive options. Build enough CRIT Rate and trigger passive on field. Higher refinements improve consistency.
  7. Dragon's Bane: Provides EM and bonus damage against Hydro/Pyro-affected enemies, making it strong in Vaporize and Overvape teams. Use in reaction teams; solve ER separately. Refinements improve passive damage.
  8. Kitain Cross Spear: Craftable EM polearm that restores Energy through its Skill passive; good in energy-hungry reaction teams. Use Skill after Burst to manage its initial Energy cost. R5 preferred.

Best artifacts for Xiangling

  • Emblem of Severed Fate (4 pcs): Best general set: 20% ER and Burst DMG scaling with ER strengthen Pyronado while helping meet Energy requirements. Main stats: ER / EM / ATK% · Pyro DMG · CRIT.
  • Crimson Witch of Flames (4 pcs): Good in reaction-heavy teams, especially Overvape. Main stats: ER / EM / ATK% · Pyro DMG · CRIT.
  • Noblesse Oblige + Crimson Witch of Flames (2 + 2 pcs): Flexible interim 2-piece Burst DMG and 2-piece Pyro DMG while farming a complete Emblem set. Main stats: ER / EM / ATK% · Pyro DMG · CRIT.

Main stats

  • Sands: Energy Recharge / Elemental Mastery / ATK%
  • Goblet: Pyro DMG Bonus
  • Circlet: CRIT Rate / CRIT DMG

Stat targets

  • Energy Recharge: 190% - 250% (team-dependent)
  • CRIT Rate: 60% - 75%
  • CRIT DMG: 120% - 170%+
  • Elemental Mastery: 100 - 250 (Vaporize)
  • ATK: varies with Bennett buffs

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 6-8
  • Elemental Burst: Level 8-9

Best teams for Xiangling

  • National Kitchen: Xiangling (Off-Field DPS), Xingqiu (Hydro Sub DPS), Bennett (Healer / Buffer), Sucrose (Anemo Support). Xingqiu enables Pyronado Vaporize, Bennett fuels and buffs Xiangling, and Sucrose shares EM and shreds resistance with Viridescent Venerer.
  • Raiden Hot Pot: Xiangling (Off-Field DPS), Raiden Shogun (On-Field DPS), Xingqiu (Hydro Sub DPS), Bennett (Healer / Buffer). Raiden helps recharge the team while Xingqiu enables Vaporize. Cast Pyronado inside Bennett’s Burst to snapshot his ATK buff.
  • International Heat: Xiangling (Off-Field DPS), Tartaglia (Hydro Driver), Kaedehara Kazuha (Anemo Buffer), Bennett (Healer / Buffer). Tartaglia applies Hydro for frequent Pyronado Vaporizes; Kazuha buffs and groups while Bennett supplies ATK and Pyro particles.
  • Overload Express: Xiangling (Off-Field DPS), Bennett (Healer / Buffer), Fischl (Electro Sub DPS), Chevreuse (Overloaded Support). Chevreuse supports a Pyro/Electro-only Overloaded team; Fischl adds off-field Electro and Bennett strengthens Xiangling’s Pyronado.

Build variations

Accessible National (Off-field Pyro DPS)

An accessible way to learn Vaporize Xiangling without premium weapons. Catch R5 is a standout option.

  • Weapons: "The Catch", Favonius Lance, Kitain Cross Spear
  • Artifacts: Emblem of Severed Fate (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Meet team ER first; then CRIT, EM and ATK%.
  • Teams: Bennett plus a Hydro applier such as Xingqiu; Anemo flex works well.
  • When it applies: Funnel Bennett Skill particles to Xiangling.
  • Notes: Bennett buffs should be active when Pyronado is cast.

Chevreuse Overloaded (Off-field Pyro DPS)

Use in Pyro/Electro damage teams where Chevreuse improves damage and RES shred.

  • Weapons: "The Catch", Deathmatch, Wavebreaker's Fin
  • Artifacts: Emblem of Severed Fate (4 pcs); Crimson Witch of Flames (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Prioritize ER and CRIT; do not stack full EM solely for Overloaded.
  • Teams: Only Pyro and Electro teammates when relying on Chevreuse’s team passive.
  • When it applies: Requires Chevreuse and compatible Pyro/Electro-only party.
  • Notes: Xiangling snapshots applicable ATK buffs when casting Burst.

No-Bennett Pyronado (Off-field Pyro DPS)

For teams using Bennett on the other side, accepting much more demanding Burst uptime.

  • Weapons: Engulfing Lightning, Favonius Lance, "The Catch"
  • Artifacts: Emblem of Severed Fate (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Raise ER significantly; ATK% can become more valuable without Bennett.
  • Teams: Needs strong battery or multiple Favonius procs; consider Raiden.
  • When it applies: Energy requirements vary widely by team.
  • Notes: Do not imply Pyronado is consistently available without sufficient Energy.

Burgeon Trigger (Reaction DPS)

Triggers Burgeon via off-field Pyro; best suited to encounters where AoE cores are available.

  • Weapons: Kitain Cross Spear, Dragon's Bane, Favonius Lance
  • Artifacts: Flower of Paradise Lost (4 pcs); Gilded Dreams (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Level 90; prioritize ER until Burst is consistent, then as much EM as practical.
  • Teams: Consistent Hydro + Dendro for Bloom cores, plus healing or shielding.
  • When it applies: Requires a stable Bloom-core supply and sufficient Burst Energy.
  • Notes: Burgeon scales with level and EM; avoid assuming standard CRIT benefits reaction damage.

Early-Game Mixed Set (Off-field Pyro DPS)

A transitional setup while farming a proper 4pc Emblem set.

  • Weapons: "The Catch", Favonius Lance, Kitain Cross Spear
  • Artifacts: Emblem of Severed Fate (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Use an ER Sands if needed; 2pc Noblesse + 2pc Crimson Witch is a transition.
  • Teams: Any team that can battery her Burst; Bennett helps considerably.
  • When it applies: Alternative mixed pieces are temporary; listed 4pc Emblem is the final target.
  • Notes: Do not farm weak 4pc pieces at the expense of functioning ER and CRIT.

Xiangling talents

Normal Attack: Dough-Fu

Normal Attack
Performs up to 5 consecutive spear strikes.

Charged Attack
Consumes a certain amount of Stamina to lunge forward, dealing damage to opponents along the way.

Plunging Attack
Plunges from mid-air to strike the ground below, damaging opponents along the path and dealing AoE DMG upon impact.

Elemental Skill: Guoba Attack

Cooldown: 12s

Summons Guoba, who will continuously breathe fire at opponents, dealing AoE Pyro DMG.

Elemental Burst: Pyronado

Cooldown: 20s · Energy cost: 80

Displaying her mastery over both fire and polearms, Xiangling sends a Pyronado whirling around her.
The Pyronado will move with your character for the ability's duration, dealing Pyro DMG to all opponents in its path.

Passive talent: Crossfire

Increases the flame range of Guoba by 20%.

Passive talent: Beware, It's Super Hot!

When Guoba Attack's effect ends, Guoba leaves a chili pepper on the spot where it disappeared. Picking up a chili pepper increases ATK by 10% for 10s.

Passive talent: Chef de Cuisine

When Xiangling cooks an ATK-boosting dish perfectly, she has a 12% chance to receive double the product.

Xiangling constellations

C1: Crispy Outside, Tender Inside

Opponents hit by Guoba's attacks have their Pyro RES reduced by 15% for 6s.

C2: Oil Meets Fire

The last attack in a Normal Attack sequence applies the Implode status onto the opponent for 2s. An explosion will occur once this duration ends, dealing 75% of Xiangling's ATK as AoE Pyro DMG.

C3: Deepfry

Increases the Level of Pyronado by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C4: Slowbake

Pyronado's duration is increased by 40%.

C5: Guoba Mad

Increases the Level of Guoba Attack by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C6: Condensed Pyronado

For the duration of Pyronado, all party members receive a 15% Pyro DMG Bonus.

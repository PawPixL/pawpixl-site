Ifa Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

Ifa build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Sunny Morning Sleep-In, A Thousand Floating Dreams), artifacts (Viridescent Venerer), main stats.

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Role: Reaction DPS / Healer

Ifa is an on-field Anemo driver who fires Tonicshots during Nightsoul's Blessing, healing the team based on his EM. His passive boosts Swirl and Electro-Charged DMG using party Nightsoul points, making Ororon a valuable partner. Build EM for reactions and healing.

Best weapons for Ifa

  1. Sunny Morning Sleep-In: Huge EM boosts Swirl DMG and team healing.
  2. A Thousand Floating Dreams: Massive EM for reaction DMG and healing.
  3. Starcaller's Watch: Very high EM makes it a strong stat stick for Swirl and healing; its shield-based team buff is usually inactive. No reliable shield application from Ifa.
  4. Wandering Evenstar: EM secondary stat enhances reactions and healing.
  5. Sacrificial Fragments: High EM supports healing and Swirl. Its Skill-reset passive is unreliable because casting Ifa’s Skill does not hit. Use for EM, not the reset.
  6. Favonius Codex: Energy Recharge and neutral particles help Burst uptime and team rotations; build CRIT Rate to trigger its passive. Needs on-field CRIT hits.
  7. Mappa Mare: Craftable EM option that supports Swirl and healing; its reaction-triggered Elemental DMG bonus mainly helps personal damage. F2P craftable choice.
  8. Thrilling Tales of Dragon Slayers: Provides a large ATK buff to the next character after swapping, sacrificing personal EM and healing. Swap directly into an ATK-scaling carry. R5 preferred.

Best artifacts for Ifa

  • Viridescent Venerer (4 pcs) · Best: Best overall for an EM Swirl driver: increases Swirl damage and lowers the RES of swirled Pyro, Hydro, Electro or Cryo. Main stats: EM / EM / EM.
  • Scroll of the Hero of Cinder City (4 pcs) · Team Buff: Team-buff alternative if someone else already wears Viridescent Venerer; trigger an applicable reaction for elemental DMG buffs. Main stats: EM / EM / EM.
  • Gilded Dreams (4 pcs) · EM Alternative: EM-focused alternative that improves Swirl damage and healing when another Anemo teammate already supplies VV resistance shred. Main stats: EM / EM / EM.

Main stats

  • Sands: Elemental Mastery / Energy Recharge
  • Goblet: Elemental Mastery
  • Circlet: Elemental Mastery / Healing Bonus

Stat targets

  • Elemental Mastery: 750 - 1,000+
  • Energy Recharge: 110% - 150%
  • CRIT Rate: 30% - 50% (Favonius only)

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 8+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 6-8

Best teams for Ifa

  • Cacucu Air Control: Ifa, Ororon, Furina, Sayu. Ororon adds Nightsoul and Electro; Furina supplies Hydro and buffs. Sayu adds healing and can hold VV for a CRIT Ifa build.
  • Storm Chasers: Ifa, Ororon, Xingqiu, Fischl. Ifa drives Xingqiu and Fischl while Ororon contributes off-field Electro and Nightsoul points, strengthening Electro-Charged reactions.
  • Moonlit Medevac: Ifa, Ororon, Aino, Ineffa. Ororon contributes Nightsoul points; Aino and Ineffa enable Lunar-Charged while Ifa drives, heals and Swirls.
  • Budget Air Ambulance: Ifa, Fischl, Xingqiu, Beidou. Accessible Taser-style team: Ifa drives off-field attacks and shreds RES. Without another Nightsoul unit, his reaction buff is weaker.

Pros

  • Teamwide EM healing
  • Strong reaction driving
  • Mobile airborne attacks
  • VV resistance shred

Cons

  • Needs on-field time
  • Weak personal grouping
  • Healing can be modest
  • Best buffs need Nightsoul

Build variations

Anemo CRIT DPS (On-field Anemo DPS)

Use for personal Tonicshot damage, particularly single-target. Obsidian Codex boosts CRIT Rate after Nightsoul-point consumption.

  • Weapons: Cashflow Supervision, Tulaytullah's Remembrance, The Widsith, Flowing Purity
  • Artifacts: Obsidian Codex (4 pcs); Viridescent Venerer (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ATK% / Anemo DMG / CRIT; prioritize CRIT, ATK and enough ER rather than triple EM.
  • Teams: Best with a second Anemo VV holder and damage buffers (e.g. Faruzan).
  • When it applies: Team damage may fall without VV on someone else.
  • Notes: Cashflow performs best with consistent HP changes, such as Furina.

Extra Healing / Furina (On-field Healer)

Use when survival or Fanfare generation is a problem rather than maximizing Swirl damage.

  • Weapons: Sunny Morning Sleep-In, Sacrificial Fragments, Prototype Amber
  • Artifacts: Viridescent Venerer (4 pcs); Gilded Dreams (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Use Healing Bonus circlet if needed; retain enough EM for reaction damage and healing.
  • Teams: Furina teams benefit from an additional healer if Ifa cannot keep pace.
  • When it applies: Prototype Amber requires using Burst.
  • Notes: Ifa heals teammates on Tonicshot hits, not passively off-field.

Team Buffer / Scroll (On-field Support)

Use when your main damage dealers benefit more from buffs or particle support than from Ifa’s own Swirl damage.

  • Weapons: Thrilling Tales of Dragon Slayers, Favonius Codex, Wandering Evenstar
  • Artifacts: Scroll of the Hero of Cinder City (4 pcs); Viridescent Venerer (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Prioritize ER and EM; add CRIT Rate if Favonius is equipped.
  • Teams: Prefer another Anemo unit holding VV if choosing Scroll; TTDS needs an ATK-scaling swap target.
  • When it applies: Scroll bonus requires relevant reactions.
  • Notes: Avoid duplicating VV on two units unless the team benefits from their separate coverage.

F2P Electro-Charged Driver (On-field Swirl DPS)

Low-spend setup focused on reactions and teamwide healing; Xingqiu, Fischl and Beidou all work.

  • Weapons: Mappa Mare, Sacrificial Fragments, Favonius Codex
  • Artifacts: Viridescent Venerer (4 pcs); Gilded Dreams (4 pcs)
  • Stats: EM / EM / EM; prioritize Level 90 and enough ER to use Burst as desired.
  • Teams: Bring strong off-field Hydro and Electro; Ororon improves Nightsoul-based buff when available.
  • When it applies: Without other Natlan Nightsoul characters, his passive buff is smaller.
  • Notes: Mappa Mare is craftable; Sacrificial Fragments is gacha.

Lunar-Charged Driver (On-field Reaction Driver)

Use to drive Lunar-Charged teams while Swirling and healing; Ororon boosts Ifa’s passive through Nightsoul points.

  • Weapons: Sunny Morning Sleep-In, A Thousand Floating Dreams, Wandering Evenstar
  • Artifacts: Viridescent Venerer (4 pcs); Gilded Dreams (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Prioritize EM and ER; use CRIT-focused build only when teammates strongly buff personal damage.
  • Teams: Requires compatible Hydro and Electro Lunar-Charged partners such as Aino and Ineffa.
  • When it applies: Exact best stats depend on team damage distribution.
  • Notes: C2 increases the Rescue Essentials cap and makes stacking easier.

Ifa Build Guide | Best Weapons, Artifacts & Teams

Ifa build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Sunny Morning Sleep-In, A Thousand Floating Dreams), artifacts (Viridescent Venerer), main stats…

Role: Reaction DPS / Healer

Ifa is an on-field Anemo driver who fires Tonicshots during Nightsoul's Blessing, healing the team based on his EM. His passive boosts Swirl and Electro-Charged DMG using party Nightsoul points, making Ororon a valuable partner. Build EM for reactions and healing.

Stat targets

  • Energy Recharge: 110% - 150%

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+

Best teams

  • Cacucu Air Control: Ifa, Ororon, Furina, Sayu
  • Storm Chasers: Ifa, Ororon, Xingqiu, Fischl
  • Moonlit Medevac: Ifa, Ororon, Aino, Ineffa
  • Budget Air Ambulance: Ifa, Fischl, Xingqiu, Beidou

Pros

  • Teamwide EM healing
  • Strong reaction driving
  • Mobile airborne attacks
  • VV resistance shred

Cons

  • Needs on-field time
  • Weak personal grouping
  • Healing can be modest
  • Best buffs need Nightsoul

Build variations

  • Anemo CRIT DPS (On-field Anemo DPS): Use for personal Tonicshot damage, particularly single-target. Obsidian Codex boosts CRIT Rate after Nightsoul-point consumption.
  • Extra Healing / Furina (On-field Healer): Use when survival or Fanfare generation is a problem rather than maximizing Swirl damage.
  • Team Buffer / Scroll (On-field Support): Use when your main damage dealers benefit more from buffs or particle support than from Ifa’s own Swirl damage.
  • F2P Electro-Charged Driver (On-field Swirl DPS): Low-spend setup focused on reactions and teamwide healing; Xingqiu, Fischl and Beidou all work.
  • Lunar-Charged Driver (On-field Reaction Driver): Use to drive Lunar-Charged teams while Swirling and healing; Ororon boosts Ifa’s passive through Nightsoul points.

Role: Support / Battery

Electro Traveler is a universal Energy battery who gives allies flat Energy and Energy Recharge through Skill amulets and Burst follow-up strikes. Let the intended teammate collect amulets; the Traveler needs substantial ER to sustain their own costly Burst.

Best weapons for Traveler (Electro)

  1. Favonius Sword
  2. Skyward Blade
  3. Freedom-Sworn
  4. Sacrificial Sword
  5. Festering Desire
  6. Amenoma Kageuchi
  7. Fleuve Cendre Ferryman
  8. Skyrider Sword

Best artifacts

  • Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs)
  • The Exile (4 pcs)
  • Emblem of Severed Fate (4 pcs)

Main stats

  • Sands: Energy Recharge
  • Goblet: Electro DMG / ER
  • Circlet: Crit Rate / Crit DMG

Stat targets

  • ER: 200% - 300%
  • Fav CR: 50%+

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 8-9
  • Elemental Burst: Level 8-9

Best teams

  • Stormcaller Battery: Traveler (Electro), Beidou, Fischl, Xingqiu
  • Xiao Energy Relay: Traveler (Electro), Xiao, Faruzan, Bennett
  • Eula Thunder Battery: Traveler (Electro), Eula, Rosaria, Mika
  • Broken Toaster: Traveler (Electro), Yoimiya, Chevreuse, Bennett

Pros

  • Energy for any element
  • Shares Energy Recharge
  • Accessible support options
  • Electro RES shred at C2

Cons

  • 80-cost Burst
  • Low personal damage
  • Amulets need pickup
  • Limited Electro particles

Build variations

  • Noblesse Team Battery (Support): Burst before the main damage window; switch to the carry to collect amulets and restore Energy.
  • Exile Budget Energy (Support): Use Burst to grant extra team Energy, then Skill to place amulets for the selected teammate.
  • Beidou Energy Partner (Battery): Let Beidou collect Skill amulets. Burst follow-up strikes need the active character to hit with Normal or Charged Attacks.
  • Emblem Burst Damage (Sub DPS): Use when Energy needs are covered and you want incidental Burst damage rather than maximum battery utility.
  • Skill Reset Utility (Support): Sacrificial reset enables another amulet cycle; new Skill erases any uncollected amulets.

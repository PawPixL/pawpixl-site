Traveler (Electro) Build Guide: Weapons, Artifacts and Teams (7.2)

Traveler (Electro) build guide for Genshin Impact 7.2: best weapons (Favonius Sword, Skyward Blade), artifacts (Noblesse Oblige), main stats.

Last updated: November 12, 2026 · Game version 7.2

Role: Support / Battery

Electro Traveler is a universal Energy battery who gives allies flat Energy and Energy Recharge through Skill amulets and Burst follow-up strikes. Let the intended teammate collect amulets; the Traveler needs substantial ER to sustain their own costly Burst.

Best weapons for Traveler (Electro)

  1. Favonius Sword: ER and teamwide particles. Requires on-field CRIT hits. R3+ helps rotations.
  2. Skyward Blade: High ER for reliable Burst uptime.
  3. Freedom-Sworn: Team buffs if reactions trigger. Only when Energy is already covered.
  4. Sacrificial Sword: Extra Skill after amulet collection. Collect old amulets before recasting. R3+ preferred.
  5. Festering Desire: ER substat; modest Skill damage.
  6. Amenoma Kageuchi: Self Energy after planned Skill casts. Requires multiple Skill uses before Burst. R3+ preferred.
  7. Fleuve Cendre Ferryman: F2P ER and Skill CRIT Rate.
  8. Skyrider Sword: Starter option with ER substat.

Best artifacts for Traveler (Electro)

  • Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs): Buffs team ATK after Burst. Main stats: ER · flexible · CRIT.
  • The Exile (4 pcs): Energy support on a budget. Main stats: ER · flexible · CRIT.
  • Emblem of Severed Fate (4 pcs): ER plus some Burst damage. Main stats: ER · Electro · CRIT.

Main stats

  • Sands: Energy Recharge
  • Goblet: Electro DMG / ER
  • Circlet: Crit Rate / Crit DMG

Stat targets

  • ER: 200% - 300%
  • Fav CR: 50%+

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 8-9
  • Elemental Burst: Level 8-9

Best teams for Traveler (Electro)

  • Stormcaller Battery: Traveler (Electro) (Energy Support), Beidou (Electro DPS), Fischl (Electro Battery), Xingqiu (Hydro Driver Support). Traveler feeds Beidou Energy and shreds Electro RES at C2. Fischl generates particles; Xingqiu drives reactions with normal attacks.
  • Xiao Energy Relay: Traveler (Electro) (Energy Support), Xiao (Anemo DPS), Faruzan (Anemo Support), Bennett (Healer/Buffer). Traveler supplies Xiao with flat Energy and ER from amulets. Faruzan boosts Anemo damage; Bennett heals and buffs.
  • Eula Thunder Battery: Traveler (Electro) (Energy Support), Eula (Physical DPS), Rosaria (Cryo Battery), Mika (Healer/Buffer). Electro Traveler enables Superconduct and transfers Energy to Eula; Rosaria batteries Cryo and Mika provides sustain.
  • Broken Toaster: Traveler (Electro) (Energy Support), Yoimiya (Pyro DPS), Chevreuse (Overload Support), Bennett (Healer/Buffer). Burst follow-up strikes from Yoimiya normal hits can trigger Overloaded. Chevreuse buffs Pyro/Electro teams; time amulet pickup.

Pros

  • Energy for any element
  • Shares Energy Recharge
  • Accessible support options
  • Electro RES shred at C2

Cons

  • 80-cost Burst
  • Low personal damage
  • Amulets need pickup
  • Limited Electro particles

Build variations

Noblesse Team Battery (Support)

Burst before the main damage window; switch to the carry to collect amulets and restore Energy.

  • Weapons: Favonius Sword, Skyward Blade
  • Artifacts: Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ER sands; prioritize ER and Favonius CRIT Rate over personal damage.
  • Teams: Best when no other teammate uses Noblesse Oblige.

Exile Budget Energy (Support)

Use Burst to grant extra team Energy, then Skill to place amulets for the selected teammate.

  • Weapons: Favonius Sword, Skyrider Sword
  • Artifacts: The Exile (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Prioritize ER; 4-star artifact stats are naturally lower.
  • Teams: Teams needing cheap Energy support; suitable for early progression.

Beidou Energy Partner (Battery)

Let Beidou collect Skill amulets. Burst follow-up strikes need the active character to hit with Normal or Charged Attacks.

  • Weapons: Favonius Sword, Sacrificial Sword
  • Artifacts: Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs); Emblem of Severed Fate (4 pcs)
  • Stats: High ER for Traveler; CRIT Rate if holding Favonius.
  • Teams: Beidou plus a normal-attack driver; Fischl helps battery Traveler.

Emblem Burst Damage (Sub DPS)

Use when Energy needs are covered and you want incidental Burst damage rather than maximum battery utility.

  • Weapons: Skyward Blade, Festering Desire
  • Artifacts: Emblem of Severed Fate (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ER sands, Electro goblet, CRIT circlet; meet Burst ER before offensive stats.
  • Teams: Needs external particles or Electro resonance to sustain Traveler Burst.

Skill Reset Utility (Support)

Sacrificial reset enables another amulet cycle; new Skill erases any uncollected amulets.

  • Weapons: Sacrificial Sword, Favonius Sword
  • Artifacts: Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ER and on-field CRIT Rate for Favonius.
  • Teams: Teammates can pick up Skill amulets before a second Skill cast.
  • When it applies: Sacrificial passive proc and cooldown
  • Notes: A second Skill is not an automatic Energy gain unless amulets are actually collected.

Danica Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

Danica build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Torrents of Spring, Wolf's Gravestone), artifacts (Heart of the Furnace), main stats, talent priority.

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Role: Support / DPS

Danica is an Electro claymore user with two reported Anchor modes. Guardian Maid gives an ATK-scaling shield, off-field Electro hits and team buffs. Combat Maid keeps her on field for Electro attacks and Stellar-Conduct damage. Her modes cannot run together. Pre-release kit.

Best weapons for Danica

  1. Torrents of Spring: At R1, grants 16% ATK. Electro hits grant Royal Decree: 20% active-character Stellar-Conduct DMG, enhanced to 40% with Complete. Stellar-Conduct teams.
  2. Wolf's Gravestone: High ATK strengthens an ATK-scaling shield and can provide a conditional party ATK buff. Provisional.
  3. A Thousand Blazing Suns: High offensive stats for an on-field Combat Maid build; less suitable for pure shield support. Provisional.
  4. Gest of the Mighty Wolf: DMG-focused alternative for on-field Combat Maid; value depends on her final attack sequence.
  5. Blade of Atonement: Potential offensive alternative if her released rotation can reliably activate its passive. Provisional; check released kit.
  6. Favonius Greatsword: Provides Energy Recharge and team particles when Danica can CRIT. Reliable support choice. Build CRIT Rate for particle generation.
  7. "Ultimate Overlord's Mega Magic Sword": Energy Recharge and ATK make this a practical event weapon for support or hybrid builds. Provisional; check released kit.
  8. Blackcliff Slasher: CRIT DMG for Combat Maid, but its kill-based passive is unreliable against bosses. Provisional; check released kit.

Best artifacts for Danica

  • Heart of the Furnace (4 pcs) · Reaction support idea: Consider for Guardian Maid only if she can activate the required Stellar reaction and team buff. Main stats: ATK% / ER · provisional.
  • Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs) · Team Burst buff: Supports party ATK after Burst when no other teammate is using this set. Main stats: ATK% / ER · support.
  • Disenchantment in Deep Shadow (4 pcs) · Combat Maid concept: Possible personal damage set if the release kit meets the reaction requirements. Main stats: CRIT / ATK · provisional.

Best teams for Danica

  • Royal Protection: Danica (Shield / Buff), Anastasya (Cryo DPS), Odette (Glimmer Buff), Mitya (Electro). Guardian Maid shields Anastasya during her on-field Cryo stance. Danica’s Anchor adds Electro and buffs Conduct; Odette and Mitya support.
  • Palace Conduct: Danica (Shield / Buff), Sandrone (DPS), Odette (Glimmer Buff), Yae Miko (Electro DPS). Sandrone drives Stellar-Conduct while Guardian Maid shields and buffs her. Yae adds Electro hits and Odette strengthens Glimmer.
  • Cryo Bodyguard: Danica (Shield / Buff), Wriothesley (Cryo DPS), Escoffier (Healer), Odette (Glimmer Buff). Danica’s shield protects Wriothesley while the Anchor supplies Electro. Escoffier heals; Odette supports the reaction.
  • Combat Maid: Danica (Electro DPS), Anastasya (Cryo Support), Odette (Glimmer Buff), Alyosha (Electro Support). Danica takes the field in Combat Maid for Electro attacks. Anastasya uses her off-field Crown; Odette and Alyosha support Conduct.

Build variations

Guardian Maid Shield (Electro Support)

Use Entry Skill to enter Guardian Maid, refresh the Anchor, shield the active carry and support Stellar-Conduct.

  • Weapons: Torrents of Spring, Wolf's Gravestone, Favonius Greatsword
  • Artifacts: Heart of the Furnace (4 pcs); Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ATK% for shield strength and reaction buffs; enough ER for Burst. CRIT Rate only if using Favonius.
  • Teams: An on-field Cryo or Electro attacker; build Excitement before switching Danica in.
  • When it applies: Guardian Maid and Combat Maid are mutually exclusive.
  • Notes: Torrents of Spring adds 16% ATK and boosts active-character Stellar-Conduct damage by 20% or 40% at R1. Pre-release weapon info.

Combat Maid Electro (On-field Electro DPS)

Stay on field to spend Excitement on repeated special Skills and Electro Anchor attacks, rather than shielding a teammate.

  • Weapons: Torrents of Spring, A Thousand Blazing Suns, Wolf's Gravestone, Serpent Spine
  • Artifacts: Disenchantment in Deep Shadow (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ATK% / Electro DMG / CRIT; meet energy needs. Exact best set is unverified.
  • Teams: Off-field Cryo for Stellar-Conduct; pre-cast buffs before Danica takes the field.
  • When it applies: Only Combat Maid mode; shield support mode is unavailable.
  • Notes: Torrents of Spring buffs Electro Stellar-Conduct damage and can work off-field. Combat Maid interactions need release testing.

Noblesse Burst Buffer (Electro Support)

Choose Noblesse when a second Furnace set would duplicate the team buff and Danica can Burst in rotation.

  • Weapons: Favonius Greatsword, "Ultimate Overlord's Mega Magic Sword"
  • Artifacts: Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ER to Burst consistently, then ATK for shield and buffs; CRIT Rate for Favonius.
  • Teams: An ATK-scaling carry; another teammate already holds Heart of the Furnace.
  • When it applies: Support setup, not the Combat Maid damage build.
  • Notes: Pre-release; Burst cadence and buff values may change.

Role: Support / DPS

Danica is an Electro claymore user with two reported Anchor modes. Guardian Maid gives an ATK-scaling shield, off-field Electro hits and team buffs. Combat Maid keeps her on field for Electro attacks and Stellar-Conduct damage. Her modes cannot run together. Pre-release kit.

Build variations

  • Guardian Maid Shield (Electro Support): Use Entry Skill to enter Guardian Maid, refresh the Anchor, shield the active carry and support Stellar-Conduct.
  • Combat Maid Electro (On-field Electro DPS): Stay on field to spend Excitement on repeated special Skills and Electro Anchor attacks, rather than shielding a teammate.
  • Noblesse Burst Buffer (Electro Support): Choose Noblesse when a second Furnace set would duplicate the team buff and Danica can Burst in rotation.

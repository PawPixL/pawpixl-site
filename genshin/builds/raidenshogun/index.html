Raiden Shogun Build Guide | Weapons, Artifacts & Teams

Raiden Shogun build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Engulfing Lightning, Sacrificer's Staff), artifacts (Emblem of Severed Fate), main stats (ER%…

Role: Main DPS, Battery

Raiden is an Electro Burst DPS who deals powerful damage while restoring Energy to her team. She can also trigger Hyperbloom off-field with a separate, full-EM build.

Best weapons for Raiden Shogun

  1. Engulfing Lightning
  2. Sacrificer's Staff
  3. Primordial Jade Winged-Spear
  4. Staff of Homa
  5. "The Catch"
  6. Deathmatch
  7. Skyward Spine
  8. Vortex Vanquisher

Best artifacts

  • Emblem of Severed Fate (4 pcs) · Burst DPS
  • Flower of Paradise Lost (4 pcs) · Hyperbloom
  • Gilded Dreams (4 pcs) · Hyperbloom alt

Main stats

  • Sands: ER% / ATK% (Burst); EM (Bloom)
  • Goblet: Electro% / ATK% (Burst); EM (Bloom)
  • Circlet: CRIT (Burst); EM (Bloom)

Stat targets

  • Burst ER: ~200–270% by team/weapon
  • Burst CR: ~60–75%
  • Burst CD: ~120–160%+
  • Hyperbloom EM: ~900–1,000+
  • Hyperbloom: level 90

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 8–9
  • Elemental Burst: Level 8-9

Best teams

  • Thunderstruck Royalty: Raiden Shogun, Durin, Chevreuse, Fischl
  • The Shogun's Court: Raiden Shogun, Kujou Sara, Kaedehara Kazuha, Bennett
  • National Emergency: Raiden Shogun, Xiangling, Xingqiu, Bennett
  • Bloom Daycare: Raiden Shogun, Nahida, Xingqiu, Yaoyao

Pros

  • Team Battery
  • Strong Burst
  • Flexible Builds
  • Easy Hyperbloom

Cons

  • Burst Reliant
  • Field Time
  • ER Demands
  • Investment Scaling

Build variations

  • Burst Hypercarry (On-field DPS): Choose when Raiden is the primary on-field damage dealer.
  • Off-field Hyperbloom (Reaction Trigger): Raiden stays off-field and triggers Hyperbloom with Skill hits.
  • Lunar-Charged Driver (On-field Driver): Raiden drives Electro reactions while supporting team Energy.
  • Aggravate DPS (On-field DPS): Use Raiden Burst damage within an Aggravate team.

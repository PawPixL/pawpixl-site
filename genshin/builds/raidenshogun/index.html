Raiden Shogun Build Guide: Weapons, Artifacts and Teams (7.2)

Raiden Shogun build guide for Genshin Impact 7.2: best weapons (Engulfing Lightning, Sacrificer's Staff), artifacts (Emblem of Severed Fate), main stats.

Last updated: November 12, 2026 · Game version 7.2

Role: Main DPS / Battery

Raiden is an Electro Burst DPS who deals powerful damage while restoring Energy to her team. She can also trigger Hyperbloom off-field with a separate, full-EM build.

Best weapons for Raiden Shogun

  1. Engulfing Lightning: Converts Energy Recharge into ATK and adds ER after Burst, improving damage and battery utility.
  2. Sacrificer's Staff: Burst-oriented premium alternative; performance depends on its passive and refinement. Compare with Engulfing for your team.
  3. Primordial Jade Winged-Spear: CRIT Rate and stacking ATK help her on-field Burst damage. Stack uptime matters.
  4. Staff of Homa: High CRIT DMG and an ATK bonus make it a strong general DPS option. HP-based bonus is situational.
  5. "The Catch": Free R5 option with ER, Burst DMG and Burst CRIT Rate. Best at R5. R5 recommended.
  6. Deathmatch: CRIT Rate and conditional ATK help balance her stats. Meet Energy needs first.
  7. Skyward Spine
  8. Vortex Vanquisher

Best artifacts for Raiden Shogun

  • Emblem of Severed Fate (4 pcs) · Burst DPS: Her standard Burst build: 20% ER and Burst DMG scaling from ER. Main stats: ER% or ATK% / Electro% or ATK% / CRIT.
  • Flower of Paradise Lost (4 pcs) · Hyperbloom: Raises the damage of Hyperblooms Raiden triggers with her off-field Skill. Main stats: EM / EM / EM.
  • Gilded Dreams (4 pcs) · Hyperbloom alt: EM-focused alternative for Hyperbloom; efficient if farming Deepwood too. Main stats: EM / EM / EM.

Main stats

  • Sands: ER% / ATK% (Burst); EM (Bloom)
  • Goblet: Electro% / ATK% (Burst); EM (Bloom)
  • Circlet: CRIT (Burst); EM (Bloom)

Stat targets

  • Burst ER: ~200–270% by team/weapon
  • Burst CR: ~60–75%
  • Burst CD: ~120–160%+
  • Hyperbloom EM: ~900–1,000+
  • Hyperbloom: level 90

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 8–9
  • Elemental Burst: Level 8-9

Best teams for Raiden Shogun

  • Thunderstruck Royalty: Raiden Shogun (Main DPS), Durin (Sub DPS), Chevreuse (Buffer / Healer), Fischl (Sub DPS). Raiden drives Overloaded, Durin supplies Pyro damage, Fischl adds Electro, and Chevreuse buffs ATK and shreds resistance.
  • The Shogun's Court: Raiden Shogun (Main DPS), Kujou Sara (Buffer), Kaedehara Kazuha (Support), Bennett (Healer / Buffer). Sara boosts Raiden, especially at C6; Kazuha shreds Electro RES and Bennett supplies ATK and healing.
  • National Emergency: Raiden Shogun (Main DPS / Battery), Xiangling (Sub DPS), Xingqiu (Sub DPS), Bennett (Healer / Buffer). Xiangling Vaporizes Xingqiu’s Hydro, Bennett buffs and heals, and Raiden refills their Bursts.
  • Bloom Daycare: Raiden Shogun (Hyperbloom Trigger), Nahida (Dendro Support), Xingqiu (Hydro Support), Yaoyao (Healer). Nahida and Yaoyao provide Dendro, Xingqiu creates cores, and full-EM Raiden triggers Hyperbloom off-field.

Build variations

Burst Hypercarry (On-field DPS)

Choose when Raiden is the primary on-field damage dealer.

  • Weapons: Engulfing Lightning, "The Catch"
  • Artifacts: Emblem of Severed Fate (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ER to team needs, then CRIT and ATK.
  • Teams: Buffers such as Sara, Kazuha and Bennett.

Off-field Hyperbloom (Reaction Trigger)

Raiden stays off-field and triggers Hyperbloom with Skill hits.

  • Weapons: Dragon's Bane, Moonpiercer, Kitain Cross Spear
  • Artifacts: Flower of Paradise Lost (4 pcs); Gilded Dreams (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Full EM on sands, goblet and circlet; level 90.
  • Teams: Dendro and Hydro teammates to create cores.
  • When it applies: Burst/CRIT not required for this role.

Lunar-Charged Driver (On-field Driver)

Raiden drives Electro reactions while supporting team Energy.

  • Weapons: Engulfing Lightning, "The Catch"
  • Artifacts: Emblem of Severed Fate (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Adjust ER, EM and CRIT to team needs.
  • Teams: Columbina and Ineffa supply Lunar-Charged synergy.
  • When it applies: Do not assume niche sets beat Emblem without team testing.

Aggravate DPS (On-field DPS)

Use Raiden Burst damage within an Aggravate team.

  • Weapons: Engulfing Lightning, "The Catch"
  • Artifacts: Emblem of Severed Fate (4 pcs); Gilded Dreams (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ER and CRIT remain important; EM gains value.
  • Teams: Reliable Dendro application plus Electro supports.
  • When it applies: EM is not a reason to abandon Burst needs.

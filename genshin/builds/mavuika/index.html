Role: On & Off-field

Mavuika is a powerful Pyro DPS who delivers massive damage through her motorcycle attacks and Elemental Burst, with flexible on-field and off-field roles.

Best weapons for Mavuika

  1. A Thousand Blazing Suns
  2. Gest of the Mighty Wolf
  3. Redhorn Stonethresher
  4. Verdict
  5. Serpent Spine
  6. Mailed Flower
  7. Blackcliff Slasher
  8. Earth Shaker

Best artifacts

  • Obsidian Codex (4 pcs) · Main DPS
  • Marechaussee Hunter (4 pcs) · Main DPS
  • Scroll of the Hero of Cinder City (4 pcs) · Off-field

Main stats

  • Sands: ATK% / EM
  • Goblet: Pyro DMG Bonus
  • Circlet: CRIT Rate / CRIT DMG

Stat targets

  • ATK: 2,000 - 2,500
  • CRIT Rate: 40% - 60% (before Obsidian)
  • CRIT DMG: 170% - 200%+
  • EM: 150 - 250 (reactions)

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 9+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 9+

Best teams

  • Melt Down: Mavuika, Xilonen, Nicole, Citlali
  • Steam Powered: Mavuika, Xilonen, Nicole, Furina
  • Beach, Please!: Mavuika, Mualani, Kaedehara Kazuha, Xilonen
  • Budget Biker Gang: Mavuika, Bennett, Xingqiu, Kachina

Pros

  • Massive Pyro damage
  • Flexible team roles
  • Strong AoE and single-target

Cons

  • Needs Nightsoul support
  • Burst depends on Fighting Spirit
  • Competes for teammates

Build variations

  • On-field Burst Carry (On-field DPS): Use her motorcycle attacks and Burst for high personal damage.
  • Off-field Pyro Buffer (Off-field DPS / Support): Use off-field Pyro application while Scroll boosts team damage.
  • Furina DPS Alternative (On-field DPS): Use Marechaussee when already invested and HP changes are consistent.

Mavuika Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

Mavuika build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (A Thousand Blazing Suns, Gest of the Mighty Wolf), artifacts (Obsidian Codex), main stats.

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Role: On & Off-field

Mavuika is a powerful Pyro DPS who delivers massive damage through her motorcycle attacks and Elemental Burst, with flexible on-field and off-field roles.

Best weapons for Mavuika

  1. A Thousand Blazing Suns: Her signature has a CRIT Rate substat and grants ATK and CRIT DMG after her Skill or Burst, with stronger benefits during Nightsoul’s Blessing.
  2. Gest of the Mighty Wolf: Huge base ATK plus a teamwide flat ATK buff on hit.
  3. Redhorn Stonethresher: stat stick with a Normal/Charged Attack bonus that applies to the charged-slash combo.
  4. Verdict: Provides CRIT Rate and ATK. Its Skill damage bonus requires collecting Crystallize shards, so it is less consistent in teams without Geo support.
  5. Serpent Spine: Provides CRIT Rate and up to 30% damage bonus at R1 when fully stacked. Taking damage removes stacks.
  6. Mailed Flower: main stat with ATK gained from Skill and Normal Attacks.
  7. Blackcliff Slasher: CRIT DMG with an ATK bonus after defeating enemies; unreliable against single bosses.
  8. Earth Shaker: Craftable ATK claymore that boosts Skill damage after a Pyro-related reaction.

Best artifacts for Mavuika

  • Obsidian Codex (4 pcs) · Main DPS: Obsidian Codex provides 15% increased damage and up to 40% CRIT Rate, maximizing Mavuika's on-field damage and making CRIT building easier. Main stats: ATK%/EM · Pyro DMG Bonus · CR/CD.
  • Marechaussee Hunter (4 pcs) · Main DPS: Marechaussee Hunter pairs with Furina's HP fluctuations to provide Mavuika up to 36% CRIT Rate, easing stat requirements while maximizing her damage potential.
  • Scroll of the Hero of Cinder City (4 pcs) · Off-field: Scroll of the Hero of Cinder City boosts Mavuika's off-field damage while providing teamwide Elemental DMG bonuses to amplify her teammates.

Main stats

  • Sands: ATK% / EM
  • Goblet: Pyro DMG Bonus
  • Circlet: CRIT Rate / CRIT DMG

Stat targets

  • ATK: 2,000 - 2,500
  • CRIT Rate: 40% - 60% (before Obsidian)
  • CRIT DMG: 170% - 200%+
  • EM: 150 - 250 (reactions)

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 9+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 9+

Best teams for Mavuika

  • Melt Down: Mavuika (Main DPS), Xilonen (Support), Nicole (Buffs), Citlali (Res Shred). Mavuika drives Pyro damage, Xilonen shreds RES, Citlali provides shielding and Melt support, and Nicole boosts team damage and reaction potential.
  • Steam Powered: Mavuika (Main DPS), Xilonen (Support), Nicole (Buffs), Furina (Buffs). Mavuika drives Pyro damage, Furina enables Vaporize and boosts damage, Xilonen shreds RES, and Nicole provides buffs and reaction support.
  • Beach, Please!: Mavuika (Sub DPS), Mualani (Main DPS), Kaedehara Kazuha (EM Build), Xilonen (Support). Mualani drives massive Vaporize hits, Mavuika provides off-field Pyro application, Kazuha boosts elemental damage and shreds RES, and Xilonen further amplifies team damage.
  • Budget Biker Gang: Mavuika (Main DPS), Bennett (Support), Xingqiu (Sub DPS), Kachina. Mavuika deals on-field Pyro damage while Xingqiu enables Vaporize. Bennett heals and buffs ATK, and Kachina can provide Geo application and Scroll set buffs.

Pros

  • Massive Pyro damage
  • Flexible team roles
  • Strong AoE and single-target

Cons

  • Needs Nightsoul support
  • Burst depends on Fighting Spirit
  • Competes for teammates

Build variations

On-field Burst Carry (On-field DPS)

Use her motorcycle attacks and Burst for high personal damage.

  • Weapons: A Thousand Blazing Suns, Gest of the Mighty Wolf, Serpent Spine
  • Artifacts: Obsidian Codex (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ATK% or EM Sands; Pyro Goblet, CRIT Circlet. No ER needed.
  • Teams: Nightsoul teammates build Fighting Spirit; Pyro reactions help.
  • When it applies: Obsidian Codex grants CRIT Rate; avoid overcapping.

Off-field Pyro Buffer (Off-field DPS / Support)

Use off-field Pyro application while Scroll boosts team damage.

  • Weapons: A Thousand Blazing Suns, Mailed Flower
  • Artifacts: Scroll of the Hero of Cinder City (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ATK% and CRIT for damage; ER is not useful.
  • Teams: An on-field carry benefiting from Pyro and Scroll buffs.
  • When it applies: If Xilonen already holds Scroll, avoid duplicating it.

Furina DPS Alternative (On-field DPS)

Use Marechaussee when already invested and HP changes are consistent.

  • Weapons: A Thousand Blazing Suns, Gest of the Mighty Wolf
  • Artifacts: Marechaussee Hunter (4 pcs); Obsidian Codex (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Account for Marechaussee CRIT Rate; maintain ATK/EM balance.
  • Teams: Furina and reliable HP fluctuations.
  • When it applies: Obsidian Codex remains the default on-field set.

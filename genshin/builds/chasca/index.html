Role: Main DPS

Chasca is an aerial on-field DPS who fires Charged Attack shells converted to Pyro, Hydro, Electro, or Cryo. Her best teams supply these elements and amplify her multi-element damage.

  2. Aqua Simulacra
  3. The First Great Magic
  4. Thundering Pulse
  5. Flower-Wreathed Feathers
  6. Scion of the Blazing Sun
  7. Song of Stillness
  8. Blackcliff Warbow

Talent priority

  • Spirit Reins, Shadow Hunt: Level 9+
  • Soul Reaper's Fatal Round: Level 6–8

Best teams

  • Hexecutioners: Chasca, Iansan, Nicole, Durin
  • Starstruck: Chasca, Odette, Nicole, Yae Miko
  • Moonstruck: Chasca, Columbina, Nicole, Ineffa
  • Budget Airlines: Chasca, Ororon, Bennett, Diona

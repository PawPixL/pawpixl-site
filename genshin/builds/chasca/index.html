Role: Main DPS

Chasca is a 5-star Anemo Bow DPS who uses her Skill to enter Nightsoul’s Blessing, taking flight and loads six Charged Attack shells that convert to her teams’ elements. She excels with PHEC teammates.

Best weapons for Chasca

  1. Astral Vulture's Crimson Plumage
  2. Aqua Simulacra
  3. The First Great Magic
  4. Thundering Pulse
  5. Flower-Wreathed Feathers
  6. Scion of the Blazing Sun
  7. Song of Stillness
  8. Blackcliff Warbow

Best artifacts

  • Obsidian Codex (4 pcs) · Main DPS
  • Marechaussee Hunter (4 pcs) · Main DPS
  • Viridescent Venerer (4 pcs) · Support

Main stats

  • Sands: ATK%
  • Goblet: ATK% >= DMG% > EM
  • Circlet: Crit Rate / Crit DMG

Stat targets

  • Crit Rate: 40% - 50%
  • Crit DMG: 170% - 200%+
  • ATK: 1,700 - 2,000+
  • Energy Recharge: 110% - 130%

Talent priority

  • Phantom Feather Flurry: Level 6
  • Spirit Reins, Shadow Hunt: Level 9+
  • Soul Reaper's Fatal Round: Level 8-9+

Best teams

  • Hexecutioners: Chasca, Iansan, Nicole, Durin
  • Starstruck: Chasca, Odette, Nicole, Yae Miko
  • Moonstruck: Chasca, Columbina, Nicole, Ineffa
  • Budget Airlines: Chasca, Ororon, Bennett, Diona

Pros

  • Elite Exploration
  • Scales well with constellations
  • Flexible Team Reactions
  • F2P Friendly

Cons

  • Wants to have a shielder
  • Needs C2 for AoE Bullets
  • Damage can be RNG

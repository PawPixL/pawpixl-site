Traveler (Anemo) Build Guide | Weapons, Artifacts & Teams

Traveler (Anemo) build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Freedom-Sworn, Primordial Jade Cutter), artifacts (Viridescent Venerer), main stats…

Role: Support / Sub DPS

Anemo Traveler is a flexible quickswap support who uses Palm Vortex and Gust Surge to trigger Swirl and reduce enemy resistance with Viridescent Venerer. Their C6 adds resistance reduction to Burst-affected enemies. The traveling tornado can push small foes away, so positioning matters.

Best teams

  • Frostwind Freeze: Traveler (Anemo), Kamisato Ayaka, Sangonomiya Kokomi, Rosaria
  • Rainstorm Taser: Traveler (Anemo), Beidou, Fischl, Xingqiu
  • Windswept Xiao: Traveler (Anemo), Xiao, Zhongli, Bennett

Pros

  • Free C6 constellations
  • Useful VV RES shred
  • Flexible quickswap support
  • Skill generates Anemo energy

Cons

  • Burst carries small enemies away
  • Weak against immovable bosses
  • Limited grouping control
  • Needs ER for Burst uptime

Build variations

  • Hybrid Quickswap Damage (Sub DPS): Use for more personal Skill/Burst damage when already meeting energy needs. Four-piece VV still benefits the team most.
  • C6 Xiao Anemo Battery (Support): Support Xiao with Anemo particles and Burst-based Anemo resistance reduction. Noblesse can buff ATK when no teammate already carries it.
  • Noblesse Team Buffer (Support): Choose Noblesse for an ATK-scaling Anemo, Geo, or Dendro carry when VV elemental shred is less useful.
  • Accessible EM Swirl (Support): A budget-friendly way to provide VV shred and Swirl damage. The craftable sword works well if ER is already covered.

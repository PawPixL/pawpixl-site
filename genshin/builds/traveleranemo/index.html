Traveler (Anemo) Build Guide: Weapons, Artifacts and Teams (7.2)

Traveler (Anemo) build guide for Genshin Impact 7.2: best weapons (Freedom-Sworn, Primordial Jade Cutter), artifacts (Viridescent Venerer), main stats.

Last updated: November 12, 2026 · Game version 7.2

Role: Support / Sub DPS

Anemo Traveler is a flexible quickswap support who uses Palm Vortex and Gust Surge to trigger Swirl and reduce enemy resistance with Viridescent Venerer. Their C6 adds resistance reduction to Burst-affected enemies. The traveling tornado can push small foes away, so positioning matters.

Best weapons for Traveler (Anemo)

  1. Freedom-Sworn: EM improves Swirl; reaction triggers can buff ally ATK and attack damage. Needs reliable reaction triggers; premium team buff.
  2. Primordial Jade Cutter: High CRIT Rate boosts Anemo Skill and Burst damage in hybrid builds. Best when energy and CRIT balance are covered.
  3. Mistsplitter Reforged: Strong CRIT DMG and elemental damage; some stacks are hard to maintain. Personal damage build, not pure EM Swirl.
  4. Skyward Blade: Energy Recharge helps Burst uptime; extra passive damage needs on-field hits. ER-focused hybrid build.
  5. Favonius Sword: CRIT hits generate neutral particles, helping Traveler and teammates. Build enough CRIT Rate to trigger passive. Refinements improve consistency.
  6. Sacrificial Sword: Skill cooldown reset adds Anemo particles and another chance to Swirl. Cooldown reset must trigger. Higher refinements preferred.
  7. Xiphos' Moonlight: EM supports Swirl while passive converts EM into team Energy Recharge. EM-focused support; gacha-limited.
  8. Iron Sting: Craftable EM option that strengthens Swirl; passive also adds personal damage. Accessible EM support.

Best artifacts for Traveler (Anemo)

  • Viridescent Venerer (4 pcs) · Best: Swirl reduces enemy resistance to the Swirled element by 40%. Main stats: EM / ER · EM · EM.
  • Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs): Burst grants party ATK; use only when no teammate already equips Noblesse. Main stats: ER / ATK% · Anemo DMG · CRIT.
  • Viridescent Venerer + Gladiator's Finale (2 + 2 pcs): Temporary damage mix; weaker team utility than 4pc Viridescent Venerer. Main stats: ATK% / ER · Anemo DMG · CRIT.

Best teams for Traveler (Anemo)

  • Childe Vaporize: Traveler (Anemo) (Support / Debuffer), Tartaglia (On-field DPS / Hydro Enabler), Xiangling (Off-field DPS / Pyro Enabler), Bennett (Healer / Buffer). Traveler Swirls Hydro or Pyro for VV shred, while Tartaglia enables Xiangling Vaporize. Position the tornado carefully to avoid scattering small enemies.
  • Frostwind Freeze: Traveler (Anemo) (Support / Debuffer), Kamisato Ayaka (On-field DPS), Sangonomiya Kokomi (Healer / Hydro Enabler), Rosaria (Off-field DPS / CRIT Buffer). Traveler shreds Cryo resistance and helps group lighter foes. Kokomi sustains Freeze, while Rosaria supplies Cryo energy and damage.
  • Rainstorm Taser: Traveler (Anemo) (Support / Debuffer), Beidou (Off-field DPS), Fischl (Off-field DPS / Battery), Xingqiu (Off-field DPS / Hydro Enabler). Traveler Swirls Electro or Hydro to reduce resistance as Beidou, Fischl, and Xingqiu deal off-field damage. Defensive utility comes from their kits.
  • Windswept Xiao: Traveler (Anemo) (Support / Debuffer), Xiao (On-field DPS), Zhongli (Shielder / Debuffer), Bennett (Healer / Buffer). Traveler provides Anemo particles and C6 Anemo RES shred for Xiao; Zhongli protects him and Bennett boosts damage. Burst can displace light enemies.

Build variations

Hybrid Quickswap Damage (Sub DPS)

Use for more personal Skill/Burst damage when already meeting energy needs. Four-piece VV still benefits the team most.

  • Weapons: Primordial Jade Cutter, Mistsplitter Reforged, Skyward Blade, Sacrificial Sword
  • Artifacts: Viridescent Venerer (4 pcs); Viridescent Venerer + Gladiator's Finale (2 + 2 pcs)
  • Stats: ATK% or ER Sands, Anemo DMG Goblet, CRIT Circlet; balance CRIT and ATK after meeting ER.
  • Teams: An allied Pyro, Hydro, Electro, or Cryo applier allows Swirl.
  • When it applies: Best in teams with regular swaps.
  • Notes: Physical Normal Attack builds remain a low-damage novelty; this variant is Anemo-focused.

C6 Xiao Anemo Battery (Support)

Support Xiao with Anemo particles and Burst-based Anemo resistance reduction. Noblesse can buff ATK when no teammate already carries it.

  • Weapons: Favonius Sword, Sacrificial Sword, Freedom-Sworn
  • Artifacts: Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs); Viridescent Venerer (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Prioritize reliable Skill particles and enough ER for Burst; build CRIT Rate with Favonius.
  • Teams: Requires Xiao as main carry; C6 Traveler for Anemo RES shred.
  • When it applies: C6 needed for extra Anemo RES shred.
  • Notes: Anemo RES shred from C6 works on Burst-hit targets. Avoid letting the tornado drag enemies beyond Xiao reach.

Noblesse Team Buffer (Support)

Choose Noblesse for an ATK-scaling Anemo, Geo, or Dendro carry when VV elemental shred is less useful.

  • Weapons: Favonius Sword, Sacrificial Sword, Skyward Blade
  • Artifacts: Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs); Viridescent Venerer (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ER first; optional ATK/Anemo/CRIT stats for personal damage.
  • Teams: No other Noblesse holder; consistent Burst access.
  • When it applies: Team-specific; Noblesse buffs do not stack.
  • Notes: Noblesse requires Burst activation; positioning remains important.

Accessible EM Swirl (Support)

A budget-friendly way to provide VV shred and Swirl damage. The craftable sword works well if ER is already covered.

  • Weapons: Iron Sting, Favonius Sword, Sacrificial Sword
  • Artifacts: Viridescent Venerer (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Use ER Sands until Burst is consistent; otherwise aim for EM main stats.
  • Teams: Bring at least one Swirlable Pyro, Hydro, Electro, or Cryo source.
  • When it applies: Level 90 increases Swirl damage.
  • Notes: Iron Sting is craftable; Favonius/Sacrificial are gacha alternatives. Prioritize energy before extra EM.

Ineffa Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts & Teams

Ineffa build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Fractured Halo, Staff of the Scarlet Sands), artifacts (Aubade of Morningstar and Moon), main stats…

Role: Sub DPS / Buffer / Shielder

Ineffa is a versatile off-field Electro DPS and Lunar-Charged enabler who provides shielding, strong reaction damage, and valuable team support.

Best weapons for Ineffa

  1. Fractured Halo
  2. Staff of the Scarlet Sands
  3. Lumidouce Elegy
  4. Symphonist of Scents
  5. Deathmatch
  6. Prospector's Shovel
  7. "The Catch"
  8. Dialogues of the Desert Sages

Best artifacts

  • Aubade of Morningstar and Moon (4 pcs) · Best
  • Silken Moon's Serenade (4 pcs) · Support
  • Gilded Dreams (4 pcs) · Support

Main stats

  • Sands: ATK%
  • Goblet: ATK%
  • Circlet: CRIT Rate / CRIT DMG

Stat targets

  • ATK: 2,000
  • CRIT Rate: 60% ~ 70%
  • CRIT DMG: 150%+
  • Elemental Mastery: 300+

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 9+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 9+

Best teams

  • Moonfall: Ineffa, Flins, Columbina, Sucrose
  • Storm Surge: Ineffa, Neuvillette, Furina, Xilonen
  • Electric Bubbles: Ineffa, Clorinde, Furina, Xilonen
  • Short Circuit: Ineffa, Ifa, Aino, Ororon

Pros

  • Strong Lunar DMG.
  • Strong shielding and EM buffs.
  • Flexible teams.
  • Excellent off-field damage.

Cons

  • Requires Hydro teammates.
  • Lunar-Charged teams work best.
  • DMG potential depends on team synergy.

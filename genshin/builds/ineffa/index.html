Ineffa Build Guide: Best Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

Ineffa build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Fractured Halo, Staff of the Scarlet Sands), artifacts (Aubade of Morningstar and Moon), main stats.

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Role: Sub DPS / Buffer / Shielder

Ineffa is a versatile off-field Electro DPS and Lunar-Charged enabler who provides shielding, strong reaction damage, and valuable team support.

Best weapons for Ineffa

  1. Fractured Halo: Provides valuable CRIT stats and boosts Ineffa's damage, making it a strong option for improving her personal DPS and overall Lunar-Charged damage output.
  2. Staff of the Scarlet Sands: Provides high CRIT Rate and converts Elemental Mastery into additional ATK, boosting Ineffa's personal damage while benefiting her reaction-focused builds.
  3. Lumidouce Elegy: CRIT Rate with an ATK passive suited to Burning-related teams; conditional outside that niche.
  4. Symphonist of Scents: Premium CRIT DMG and ATK option with team buff utility after healing.
  5. Deathmatch: Battle Pass CRIT Rate with an ATK bonus based on nearby enemy count.
  6. Prospector's Shovel: Provides ATK and a Lunar Charged damage bonus under its passive conditions. A useful accessible option for her reaction damage.
  7. "The Catch": Provides Energy Recharge and increases Elemental Burst damage and CRIT Rate, helping Ineffa maintain Burst uptime and improve her Burst damage.
  8. Dialogues of the Desert Sages: HP% event polearm that restores Energy after healing a teammate.

Best artifacts for Ineffa

  • Aubade of Morningstar and Moon (4 pcs) · Best: Boosts Elemental Mastery and Lunar reaction damage, making it an excellent set for maximizing Ineffa's Lunar-Charged damage and team synergy. Main stats: ATK% · ATK% · CR/CD.
  • Silken Moon's Serenade (4 pcs) · Support: Provides Elemental Mastery and increases Elemental Energy, making it a useful alternative for supporting Ineffa's Lunar reaction damage and team performance. Main stats: ATK% · ATK% · CR/CD.
  • Gilded Dreams (4 pcs) · Support: Provides Elemental Mastery and an additional ATK or EM bonus based on team composition, boosting Ineffa's reaction damage and overall DPS. Main stats: ATK% · ATK% · CR/CD.

Main stats

  • Sands: ATK%
  • Goblet: ATK%
  • Circlet: CRIT Rate / CRIT DMG

Stat targets

  • ATK: 2,000+
  • CRIT Rate: 60% to 70%
  • CRIT DMG: 150%+
  • Elemental Mastery: situational

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 1+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 9+
  • Elemental Burst: Level 9+

Best teams for Ineffa

  • Moonfall: Ineffa (Support), Flins (Main DPS), Columbina (Sub DPS), Sucrose (Support). Flins drives Lunar Charged damage while Columbina supports Lunar reactions. Ineffa adds Electro damage and shielding, and Sucrose groups enemies.
  • Storm Surge: Ineffa (Support), Neuvillette (Main DPS), Furina (Sub DPS), Xilonen (Support). Neuvillette drives Hydro damage while Furina adds buffs and off field Hydro. Ineffa contributes Electro and shielding, while Xilonen heals and shreds resistance.
  • Electric Bubbles: Ineffa, Clorinde, Furina, Xilonen. Clorinde drives Electro damage, Furina enables Hydro reactions and buffs damage, Ineffa provides shielding and Electro, and Xilonen heals and shreds resistance.
  • Short Circuit: Ineffa, Ifa, Aino, Ororon. Ifa drives reactions with Aino’s Hydro and Ororon’s Electro. Ineffa adds shielding, Electro damage and Lunar Charged support.

Pros

  • Strong Lunar DMG.
  • Strong shielding and EM buffs.
  • Flexible teams.
  • Excellent off-field damage.

Cons

  • Requires Hydro teammates.
  • Lunar-Charged teams work best.
  • DMG potential depends on team synergy.

Build variations

Lunar-Charged Damage (Off-field DPS)

Focus on Ineffa’s personal off-field Lunar damage and shielding.

  • Weapons: Fractured Halo, Staff of the Scarlet Sands, Deathmatch
  • Artifacts: Aubade of Morningstar and Moon (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Favor ATK and CRIT; meet ER needs for your rotation.
  • Teams: Hydro application for Lunar-Charged.
  • When it applies: Reaction does not function without Hydro.

Lunar Team Buffer (Support)

Use Silken Moon when Ineffa should carry the teamwide Lunar buff.

  • Weapons: Fractured Halo, Deathmatch, Prospector's Shovel
  • Artifacts: Silken Moon's Serenade (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Favor enough ATK for shield strength; balance ER and CRIT.
  • Teams: Another character deals significant Lunar reaction damage.
  • When it applies: Avoid duplicating Silken Moon buffs.

Ineffa talents

Normal Attack: Cyclonic Duster

Normal Attack
Performs up to 4 consecutive spear strikes.

Charged Attack
Consumes a certain amount of Stamina to perform a spinning attack.

Plunging Attack
Plunges from mid-air to strike the ground below, damaging opponents along the path and dealing AoE DMG upon impact.

Elemental Skill: Cleaning Mode: Carrier Frequency

Cooldown: 16s

Ineffa engages her Enhanced Cleaning Module, dealing a single instance of AoE Electro DMG to nearby opponents, activating her Optical Flow Shield Barrier, and summoning the Multipurpose Smart Assistance Unit, Birgitta.
The Optical Flow Shield Barrier absorbs DMG based on Ineffa's ATK, and it will absorb Electro DMG with 250% effectiveness.

Elemental Burst: Supreme Instruction: Cyclonic Exterminator

Cooldown: 15s · Energy cost: 60

Through Multipurpose Smart Assistance Unit, Birgitta, Ineffa can use Rocket Punches to quickly clean up opponents! Shoots Birgitta into the fray, dealing AoE Electro DMG and allowing Birgitta to remain on the field.

If Birgitta is already on the field, having been summoned by Ineffa, this Skill will re-summon Birgitta at the target's location and reset her duration.

Passive talent: Overclocking Circuit

If there are thunderclouds created by Lunar-Charged reactions nearby when Birgitta unleashes Discharge attacks, she will initiate an additional attack, dealing AoE Electro DMG equal to 65% of Ineffa's ATK. This DMG is considered Lunar-Charged DMG.

Passive talent: Panoramic Permutation Protocol

When using the Elemental Burst Supreme Instruction: Cyclonic Exterminator, all your party members will gain the Parameter Permutation effect: Increases Ineffa and your own active party member's Elemental Mastery by 6% of Ineffa's ATK for 20s.

Passive talent: Moonsign Benediction: Assemblage Hub

When a party member triggers an Electro-Charged reaction, it will be converted into the Lunar-Charged reaction, with every 100 ATK that Ineffa has increasing Lunar-Charged's Base DMG by 0.7%, up to a maximum of 14%.

Additionally, when Ineffa is in the party, the party's Moonsign will increase by 1 level.

Passive talent: Flavor Synthesis Unit

When Ineffa uses food, there is a 30% chance of gaining a seasoning ingredient.

Additionally, in Nod-Krai, it seems you'll be able to change Birgitta's appearance through certain means...

Ineffa constellations

C1: Rectifying Processor

When Ineffa activates her Optical Flow Shield Barrier, all nearby party members will gain the Carrier Flow Composite effect for 20s: Increases Lunar-Charged DMG by 2.5% for every 100 ATK that Ineffa has, up to a maximum of 50%.

C2: Support Cleaning Module

After the Elemental Burst Supreme Instruction: Cyclonic Exterminator hits opponents, it will inflict the Punishment Edict status on one of those hit opponents: After a brief delay, or when this opponent is defeated, nearby opponents will take AoE Electro DMG equal to 300% of Ineffa's ATK. This DMG is considered Lunar-Charged DMG.
Additionally, when the Elemental Burst Supreme Instruction: Cyclonic Exterminator is unleashed, nearby party members will gain Optical Flow Shield Barriers.

C3: Enhanced Emotion Emulator

Increases the Level of Cleaning Mode: Carrier Frequency by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C4: The Edictless Path

When your own party members trigger Lunar-Charged reactions, recover 5 Elemental Energy. This effect can occur once every 4s.

C5: Mirror's Dream Transcension

Increases the Level of Supreme Instruction: Cyclonic Exterminator by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C6: A Dawning Morn for You

When Ineffa is affected by the Carrier Flow Composite effect, she will deal AoE Electro DMG equal to 135% of her ATK to opponents in the current active character's vicinity after nearby thunderclouds release bursts of lightning at opponents. This DMG is considered Lunar-Charged DMG. The aforementioned effect can trigger once every 3.5s.

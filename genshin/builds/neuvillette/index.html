Role: Main DPS

Neuvillette is an HP-scaling Hydro carry whose Charged Attacks deliver sustained area damage. Sourcewater Droplets speed up his beams and restore HP. At C0, a shield or careful positioning helps prevent interruptions; C1 gives him more freedom with team building.

Best weapons for Neuvillette

  1. Tome of the Eternal Flow
  2. Surf's Up
  3. Jadefall's Splendor
  4. Lost Prayer to the Sacred Winds
  5. Sacrificial Jade
  6. Prototype Amber
  7. Ash-Graven Drinking Horn
  8. The Widsith

Best artifacts

  • Marechaussee Hunter (4 pcs) · Best
  • Heart of Depth (4 pcs) · Alternative
  • Tenacity of the Millelith + Vourukasha's Glow (2 + 2 pcs) · HP Mix

Main stats

  • Sands: HP%
  • Goblet: Hydro DMG Bonus / HP%
  • Circlet: CRIT DMG / CRIT Rate / HP%

Stat targets

  • HP: 35,000 - 45,000+
  • CRIT Rate: 45% - 64% (before set)
  • CRIT DMG: 180% - 250%+
  • Energy Recharge: 100% - 135%
  • CRIT Rate cap: 64% with 4pc MH

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 10
  • Elemental Skill: Level 6 - 8
  • Elemental Burst: Level 8 - 9

Best teams

  • Judgment Day: Neuvillette, Furina, Kaedehara Kazuha, Xilonen
  • Moonlight Verdict: Neuvillette, Columbina, Ineffa, Kaedehara Kazuha
  • Frozen in Court: Neuvillette, Furina, Escoffier, Citlali
  • Bloom and Order: Neuvillette, Nahida, Raiden Shogun, Zhongli

Pros

  • Powerful Hydro beams
  • Strong area damage
  • Self healing
  • Flexible team options

Cons

  • C0 interruptions
  • Charged Attack focus
  • Hydro immune foes
  • Needs Droplet rotation

Build variations

  • C0 Shield Comfort (On-field Hydro DPS): Use when enemy attacks would regularly interrupt the Charged Attack beam.
  • Lunar-Charged Driver (On-field Hydro DPS): Use to combine his sustained Hydro beams with strong off-field Lunar-Charged damage.
  • Hyperbloom Hydro Driver (On-field Hydro DPS): Use when you want consistent Hyperbloom damage alongside his personal Hydro beams.
  • F2P Prototype Amber (On-field Hydro DPS): Choose this for accessible HP scaling, Burst refund and team healing without premium weapons.

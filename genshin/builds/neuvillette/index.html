Neuvillette Build Guide: Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

Neuvillette build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Tome of the Eternal Flow, Surf's Up), artifacts (Marechaussee Hunter), main stats.

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Role: Main DPS

Neuvillette is an HP-scaling Hydro carry whose Charged Attacks deliver sustained area damage. Sourcewater Droplets speed up his beams and restore HP. At C0, a shield or careful positioning helps prevent interruptions; C1 gives him more freedom with team building.

Best weapons for Neuvillette

  1. Tome of the Eternal Flow: CRIT DMG, HP and Charged Attack damage perfectly match his kit; refunds Energy when its HP change stacks are consumed.
  2. Surf's Up: High CD and HP; useful as a stat stick.
  3. Jadefall's Splendor: HP and some Energy support help his beam build, but its shield and Burst related passive is not fully utilized.
  4. Lost Prayer to the Sacred Winds: CRIT Rate and stacking Elemental DMG help sustained field time, but the stacks reset when he swaps.
  5. Sacrificial Jade: CR and an off-field HP buff boost his beams. Swap out before its HP buff expires to maintain uptime.
  6. Prototype Amber: HP, Burst Energy refund and party healing make it his most practical freely craftable weapon. Especially good when Energy or Furina Fanfare is needed.
  7. Ash-Graven Drinking Horn: HP and periodic area damage can edge out Prototype Amber if Energy needs are already low. Low ER requirement; no Burst Energy refund. R5 preferred.
  8. The Widsith: CRIT DMG is useful, but only its Elemental DMG song boosts his damage reliably; ATK and EM rolls are often wasted. Inconsistent passive.

Best artifacts for Neuvillette

  • Marechaussee Hunter (4 pcs) · Best: His own HP changes activate all three CR stacks. Main stats: HP% · Hydro DMG/HP% · CRIT DMG/CRIT Rate.
  • Heart of Depth (4 pcs) · Alternative: Boosts Hydro damage and Charged Attacks after using Skill, but falls behind Marechaussee Hunter. Main stats: HP% · Hydro DMG/HP% · CRIT.
  • Tenacity of the Millelith + Vourukasha's Glow (2 + 2 pcs) · HP Mix: Two HP bonuses make a workable temporary set while farming Marechaussee Hunter. Main stats: HP% · Hydro DMG/HP% · CRIT.

Main stats

  • Sands: HP%
  • Goblet: Hydro DMG Bonus / HP%
  • Circlet: CRIT DMG / CRIT Rate / HP%

Stat targets

  • HP: 35,000 - 45,000+
  • CRIT Rate: 45% - 64% (before set)
  • CRIT DMG: 180% - 250%+
  • Energy Recharge: 100% - 135%
  • CRIT Rate cap: 64% with 4pc MH

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 10
  • Elemental Skill: Level 6 - 8
  • Elemental Burst: Level 8 - 9

Best teams for Neuvillette

  • Judgment Day: Neuvillette (Main DPS), Furina (Hydro Buff), Kaedehara Kazuha (Anemo Buff), Xilonen (Healer / Shred). Furina buffs the beam and activates Hydro Resonance. Kazuha buffs Hydro and shreds RES; Xilonen adds shred and healing. C0 needs careful dodging.
  • Moonlight Verdict: Neuvillette (Main DPS), Columbina (Hydro / Lunar), Ineffa (Electro / Shield), Kaedehara Kazuha (Anemo Buff). Columbina and Ineffa enable Lunar-Charged while Neuvillette drives with Hydro beams. Kazuha groups enemies and shreds Hydro RES.
  • Frozen in Court: Neuvillette (Main DPS), Furina (Hydro Buff), Escoffier (Cryo Healer), Citlali (Cryo Shield). Furina buffs Neuvillette, Escoffier heals and shreds Cryo/Hydro RES, while Citlali shields and helps Freeze susceptible enemies.
  • Bloom and Order: Neuvillette (Hydro Driver), Nahida (Dendro), Raiden Shogun (Hyperbloom Trigger), Zhongli (Shield). Neuvillette creates cores with Nahida; full EM Raiden triggers Hyperbloom off-field, and Zhongli shields against interruptions.

Build variations

C0 Shield Comfort (On-field Hydro DPS)

Use when enemy attacks would regularly interrupt the Charged Attack beam.

  • Weapons: Tome of the Eternal Flow, Sacrificial Jade, Prototype Amber
  • Artifacts: Marechaussee Hunter (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Keep usual HP, Hydro and CRIT stats; ER must still cover Burst rotations.
  • Teams: A reliable shield such as Zhongli or Citlali plus two reaction types when feasible.
  • When it applies: At C1, interruption resistance makes shielding less necessary.
  • Notes: Furina double Hydro can still outperform a third reaction stack at C0.

Lunar-Charged Driver (On-field Hydro DPS)

Use to combine his sustained Hydro beams with strong off-field Lunar-Charged damage.

  • Weapons: Tome of the Eternal Flow, Sacrificial Jade, Prototype Amber
  • Artifacts: Marechaussee Hunter (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Maintain HP, CRIT and sufficient ER; do not convert Neuvillette into an EM reaction trigger.
  • Teams: Columbina and Ineffa enable Lunar-Charged; add Kazuha or a compatible support.
  • When it applies: Requires the relevant Lunar-Charged teammates.
  • Notes: Electro and Hydro reaction damage from teammates is separate from his Charged Attack scaling.

Hyperbloom Hydro Driver (On-field Hydro DPS)

Use when you want consistent Hyperbloom damage alongside his personal Hydro beams.

  • Weapons: Tome of the Eternal Flow, Prototype Amber, Sacrificial Jade
  • Artifacts: Marechaussee Hunter (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Keep normal HP and CRIT stats; stack EM on Raiden or Kuki, not on Neuvillette.
  • Teams: Dendro applicator plus full EM Electro trigger; shield is helpful at C0.
  • When it applies: Electro trigger needs Level 90 and substantial EM.
  • Notes: Neuvillette supplies Hydro for Bloom; the Electro character owns Hyperbloom damage.

F2P Prototype Amber (On-field Hydro DPS)

Choose this for accessible HP scaling, Burst refund and team healing without premium weapons.

  • Weapons: Prototype Amber, Ash-Graven Drinking Horn
  • Artifacts: Marechaussee Hunter (4 pcs); Heart of Depth (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Use HP sands, Hydro or HP goblet, and CRIT circlet. ER may be lower with Prototype Amber.
  • Teams: Any off-field reaction enablers; prioritize shield for C0 comfort.
  • When it applies: Ash-Graven is best when Energy needs are already low.
  • Notes: Prototype Amber supports Furina teams with extra party healing.

Neuvillette talents

Normal Attack: As Water Seeks Equilibrium

Normal Attack
With light flourishes, Neuvillette commands the tides to unleash a maximum of 3 attacks, dealing Hydro DMG.

Charged Attack Empowerment: Legal Evaluation
While charging up, Neuvillette will gather the power of water, forming it into a Seal of Arbitration. In this state, Neuvillette can move and change facing, and also absorb any Sourcewater Droplets in a certain AoE.
Every Droplet he absorbs will increase the formation speed of the Seal, and will heal Neuvillette.
When the charging is stopped, if the Symbol has yet to be formed, then a Charged Attack will be unleashed. If it has been formed, then a Charged Attack: Equitable Judgment will be unleashed.

Charged Attack
Consumes a fixed amount of Stamina to attack opponents with a rupturing blast of water, dealing AoE Hydro DMG.

Charged Attack: Equitable Judgment
Unleashes surging torrents, dealing continuous AoE Hydro DMG to all opponents in a straight-line area in front of him.
Equitable Judgment will not consume any Stamina and lasts 3s.
If Neuvillette's HP is above 50%, he will continuously lose HP while using this attack.

Plunging Attack
Gathering the might of Hydro, Neuvillette plunges towards the ground from mid-air, damaging all opponents in his path. Deals AoE Hydro DMG upon impact with the ground.

Elemental Skill: O Tears, I Shall Repay

Cooldown: 12s

Summons a Raging Waterfall that will deal AoE Hydro DMG to opponents in front of Neuvillette based on his Max HP. After hitting an opponent, this skill will generate 3 Sourcewater Droplets near that opponent.

Arkhe: Pneuma
At certain intervals, when the Raging Waterfall descends, a Spiritbreath Thorn will descend that will pierce opponents, dealing Pneuma-aligned Hydro DMG.

Elemental Burst: O Tides, I Have Returned

Cooldown: 18s · Energy cost: 70

Unleashes waves that will deal AoE Hydro DMG based on Neuvillette's Max HP. After a short interval, 2 waterfalls will descend and deal Hydro DMG in a somewhat smaller AoE, and will generate 6 Sourcewater Droplets within an area in front.

Passive talent: Heir to the Ancient Sea's Authority

When a party member triggers a Vaporize, Frozen, Electro-Charged, Lunar-Charged, Bloom, Lunar-Bloom, Hydro Swirl, Hydro Crystallize or a Lunar-Crystallize reaction on opponents, 1 stack of Past Draconic Glories will be granted to Neuvillette for 30s. Max 3 stacks. Past Draconic Glories causes Charged Attack: Equitable Judgment to deal 110%/125%/160% of its original DMG.
The stacks of Past Draconic Glories created by each kind of Elemental Reaction exist independently.

Passive talent: Discipline of the Supreme Arbitration

For each 1% of Neuvillette's current HP greater than 30% of Max HP, he will gain 0.6% Hydro DMG Bonus. A maximum bonus of 30% can be obtained this way.

Passive talent: Gather Like the Tide

Increases underwater Sprint SPD for your own party members by 15%.
Not stackable with Passive Talents that provide the exact same effects.

Neuvillette constellations

C1: Venerable Institution

When Neuvillette takes the field, he will obtain 1 stack of Past Draconic Glories from the Ascension Talent "Heir to the Ancient Sea's Authority." You must first unlock the Ascension Talent "Heir to the Ancient Sea's Authority."
Additionally, his interruption resistance will be increased while using the Charged Attack Empowerment: Legal Evaluation and the Charged Attack: Equitable Judgment.

C2: Juridical Exhortation

The Ascension Talent "Heir to the Ancient Sea's Authority" will be enhanced: Each stack of Past Draconic Glories will increase the CRIT DMG of Charged Attack: Equitable Judgment by 14%. The maximum increase that can be achieved this way is 42%.
You must first unlock the Ascension Talent "Heir to the Ancient Sea's Authority."

C3: Ancient Postulation

Increases the Level of Normal Attack: As Water Seeks Equilibrium by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C4: Crown of Commiseration

When Neuvillette is on the field and is healed, 1 Sourcewater Droplet will be generated. This effect can occur once every 4s.

C5: Axiomatic Judgment

Increases the Level of O Tides, I Have Returned by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C6: Wrathful Recompense

When using Charged Attack: Equitable Judgment, Neuvillette can absorb nearby Sourcewater Droplets in an AoE. Each absorbed Droplet will increase the duration of Charged Attack: Equitable Judgment by 1s.
Additionally, when Equitable Judgment hits opponents, it will fire off 2 additional currents every 2s, each of which will deal 10% of Neuvillette's Max HP as Hydro DMG. DMG dealt this way will count as DMG dealt by Equitable Judgment.

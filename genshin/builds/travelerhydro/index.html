Role: Support / Sub DPS

Hydro Traveler offers ranged Hydro hits through a stationary Hold Skill and a traveling Burst, but has limited application and little innate team utility. Use Favonius Sword or support artifacts in reaction teams, or build Skill DMG for a niche on-field playstyle.

Main stats

  • Sands: Energy Recharge

Stat targets

  • ER: Team-dependent; often very high for each Burst
  • CRIT Rate: 45% - 65% with Favonius Sword
  • Support: Prioritize enough ER for planned Burst frequency
  • Skill DPS: Prioritize CRIT and ATK over Burst uptime

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Not Priority
  • Elemental Skill: Lvl 8 - 10
  • Elemental Burst: Lvl 6 - 8 (optional)

  • Rainstorm Hyperbloom: Traveler (Hydro), Raiden Shogun, Nahida, Sangonomiya Kokomi
  • They're doing their best guys: Traveler (Hydro), Kaedehara Kazuha, Xiangling, Bennett

Pros

  • Free Hydro access
  • Ranged Hydro Skill
  • Pneuma-aligned hit
  • Flexible support sets

Cons

  • Weak Hydro application
  • Stationary Hold Skill
  • Unreliable traveling Burst
  • High Burst energy needs
  • Limited personal damage

Build variations

  • On-field Hold Skill DPS (On-field DPS): Use Hold Skill for repeated Dewdrops and a stronger ending Torrent Surge. HP drain activates Marechaussee CRIT Rate.
  • Vaporize Skill DPS (On-field DPS): Set up Pyro before firing Traveler’s ending Torrent Surge to Vaporize its hit.
  • Nilou Bloom Driver (On-field Hydro Driver): Aim Hold Skill at Dendro-afflicted targets to trigger additional Bountiful Blooms.
  • Hyperbloom Utility (Support): Supplement Hydro application while the Electro unit triggers Hyperblooms.
  • Free-to-Play Support (Support): A low-cost way to use Hydro Traveler as a reaction enabler and artifact buffer.

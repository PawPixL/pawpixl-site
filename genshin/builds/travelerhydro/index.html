Traveler (Hydro) Build Guide: Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

Traveler (Hydro) build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Freedom-Sworn, Key of Khaj-Nisut), artifacts (Instructor), main stats, talent priority.

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Role: Support / Sub DPS

Hydro Traveler offers ranged Hydro hits through a stationary Hold Skill and a traveling Burst, but has limited application and little innate team utility. Use Favonius Sword or support artifacts in reaction teams, or build Skill DMG for a niche on-field playstyle.

Best weapons for Traveler (Hydro)

  1. Freedom-Sworn: Provides team ATK and Normal/Charged/Plunging Attack buffs after Traveler triggers two reactions. Requires reliable reaction triggers; not a personal-DPS pick.
  2. Key of Khaj-Nisut: HP increases its Elemental Mastery team-share potential after three Skill hits; the Hold Skill can build stacks. For reaction support; hold Skill enough to stack before swapping.
  3. Favonius Sword: CRIT hits generate neutral particles.
  4. Sacrificial Sword: ER plus a chance to reset Skill adds another Hydro hit and improves support-set reaction uptime. Additional Skill costs field time. High refinements preferred.
  5. Fleuve Cendre Ferryman: Fishing sword grants Skill CRIT Rate and temporary Energy Recharge after using Skill; reliable free Burst support.
  6. Festering Desire: Energy Recharge, Elemental Skill DMG Bonus and Skill CRIT Rate make it useful for a Skill-oriented build. Only if you own the limited event weapon. Event-limited.
  7. Amenoma Kageuchi: Craftable ATK% sword refunds Energy after Burst based on Skill-use seeds, reducing the need for ER. Best with multiple Skill uses across a rotation.
  8. Skyrider Sword: Accessible Energy Recharge stat stick for early-game support; its Burst-triggered movement and ATK buff matter little. Budget option until a stronger ER sword is available.

Best artifacts for Traveler (Hydro)

  • Instructor (4 pcs) · Reaction support: Trigger a reaction while on field to grant the team 120 EM for 8 seconds. Best when a reaction carry benefits from EM. Main stats: ER · Any / Hydro DMG · CRIT Rate (Favonius).
  • Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs) · ATK buffer: After Burst, grants 20% team ATK for 12 seconds. Main stats: ER · Hydro DMG / Any · CRIT Rate.
  • Deepwood Memories (4 pcs) · Dendro teams: Situational Dendro RES shred in Bloom or Hyperbloom if no better teammate can hold the set; uptime is limited. Main stats: ER · Hydro DMG / Any · CRIT Rate.

Main stats

  • Sands: Energy Recharge
  • Goblet: Hydro DMG Bonus / Any
  • Circlet: CRIT Rate (Favonius) / CRIT DMG

Stat targets

  • ER: Team-dependent; often very high for each Burst
  • CRIT Rate: 45% - 65% with Favonius Sword
  • Support: Prioritize enough ER for planned Burst frequency
  • Skill DPS: Prioritize CRIT and ATK over Burst uptime

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Not Priority
  • Elemental Skill: Lvl 8 - 10
  • Elemental Burst: Lvl 6 - 8 (optional)

Best teams for Traveler (Hydro)

  • Fontaine Vaporize: Traveler (Hydro) (Hydro Support), Furina (Sub DPS / Buffer), Xiangling (Off-field DPS), Bennett (Healer / Buffer). Furina supplements Traveler’s weak Hydro application for Xiangling Vaporize. Bennett heals and batteries Xiangling; Traveler adds Fanfare via HP changes.
  • Bountiful Dewdrops: Traveler (Hydro) (Hydro Driver), Nilou (Bloom Enabler), Nahida (Dendro Support), Baizhu (Healer / Shielder). Traveler aims Hydro at Dendro-marked enemies to make Nilou Bountiful Cores; Nahida supplies Dendro and Baizhu heals Bloom self-damage.
  • Rainstorm Hyperbloom: Traveler (Hydro) (Hydro Support), Raiden Shogun (Hyperbloom Trigger), Nahida (Dendro Support), Sangonomiya Kokomi (Hydro Healer / Driver). Kokomi covers Traveler’s Hydro downtime; Nahida creates Cores and full-EM Raiden triggers Hyperbloom. Traveler offers extra Hydro hits.
  • They're doing their best guys: Traveler (Hydro) (On-field Skill DPS), Kaedehara Kazuha (Anemo Support), Xiangling (Pyro Enabler), Bennett (Healer / Buffer). Xiangling maintains Pyro for Traveler’s final Skill hit to Vaporize; Bennett buffs ATK and Kazuha groups foes. Timing is essential.

Build variations

On-field Hold Skill DPS (On-field DPS)

Use Hold Skill for repeated Dewdrops and a stronger ending Torrent Surge. HP drain activates Marechaussee CRIT Rate.

  • Weapons: Festering Desire, Skyward Blade, Fleuve Cendre Ferryman
  • Artifacts: Marechaussee Hunter (4 pcs); Heart of Depth (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ATK% sands, Hydro goblet, CRIT circlet; prioritize CRIT > ATK%. Ignore Burst ER if focusing on Skill.
  • Teams: Shielder or interruption resistance; Furina and Bennett can amplify damage.
  • When it applies: Stationary Skill; interruption severely hurts damage.
  • Notes: The Skill cannot trigger Xingqiu, Yelan or Beidou Burst follow-ups.

Vaporize Skill DPS (On-field DPS)

Set up Pyro before firing Traveler’s ending Torrent Surge to Vaporize its hit.

  • Weapons: Festering Desire, Freedom-Sworn, Fleuve Cendre Ferryman
  • Artifacts: Marechaussee Hunter (4 pcs); Heart of Depth (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ATK% or EM sands, Hydro goblet, CRIT circlet; damage and reaction stats before ER.
  • Teams: Reliable Pyro aura from Xiangling; Bennett; optional Anemo buffer.
  • When it applies: Careful Pyro aura and hit timing required.
  • Notes: This is a niche, lower-ceiling build; HP stacking is not optimal for Skill damage.

Nilou Bloom Driver (On-field Hydro Driver)

Aim Hold Skill at Dendro-afflicted targets to trigger additional Bountiful Blooms.

  • Weapons: Key of Khaj-Nisut, Sacrificial Sword, Fleuve Cendre Ferryman
  • Artifacts: Instructor (4 pcs); Deepwood Memories (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Level 90; invest in EM for Blooms Traveler owns. Maintain enough ER only if using Burst each rotation.
  • Teams: Nilou and Dendro units only; bring strong healing such as Baizhu.
  • When it applies: Only Hydro and Dendro party members with Nilou.
  • Notes: Bloom ownership varies with application and rotation; do not assume all cores belong to Traveler.

Hyperbloom Utility (Support)

Supplement Hydro application while the Electro unit triggers Hyperblooms.

  • Weapons: Favonius Sword, Sacrificial Sword, Fleuve Cendre Ferryman
  • Artifacts: Instructor (4 pcs); Deepwood Memories (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Build ER to desired Burst frequency, then Favonius CRIT Rate; Traveler does not need full EM.
  • Teams: Full-EM Kuki Shinobu or Raiden, Dendro application, and another Hydro source if needed.
  • When it applies: Often needs an extra Hydro unit for consistent cores.
  • Notes: Put Deepwood on a reliable Dendro unit when possible.

Free-to-Play Support (Support)

A low-cost way to use Hydro Traveler as a reaction enabler and artifact buffer.

  • Weapons: Fleuve Cendre Ferryman, Amenoma Kageuchi, Skyrider Sword
  • Artifacts: Instructor (4 pcs); Noblesse Oblige (4 pcs)
  • Stats: ER sands; CRIT Rate if using Favonius later. Damage stats are secondary to reliable support effects.
  • Teams: Reaction team for Instructor, or ATK-scaling carry for Noblesse.
  • When it applies: Noblesse is weaker if Burst cannot be cast regularly.
  • Notes: Fleuve Cendre Ferryman boosts Skill CRIT Rate and grants ER after Skill, not HP.

Traveler (Hydro) talents

Normal Attack: Foreign Stream

Normal Attack
Performs up to 5 rapid strikes.

Charged Attack
Consumes a certain amount of Stamina to unleash 2 rapid sword strikes.

Plunging Attack
Plunges from mid-air to strike the ground below, damaging opponents along the path and dealing AoE DMG upon impact.

Elemental Skill: Aquacrest Saber

Cooldown: 10s

Unleashes a torrent that can cleanse the world.

Press
Sends a Torrent Surge forward that will deal Hydro DMG to opponents it comes into contact with.

Hold
Enter Aiming Mode and constantly fire off Dewdrops in the direction in which you are aiming, dealing Hydro DMG to opponents they hit.
When the skill ends, it will send a Torrent Surge forward that will deal Hydro DMG to opponents it comes into contact with.
Suffusion: When using the Hold configuration of this skill, if the Traveler's HP is higher than 50%, the DMG dealt by Dewdrops will increase based on the Traveler's Max HP, and the Traveler will lose a fixed amount of HP every second.

Arkhe: Pneuma
At certain intervals, after using Torrent Surge, this skill will unleash a Spiritbreath Thorn that pierces opponents, dealing Pneuma-aligned Hydro DMG.

Elemental Burst: Rising Waters

Cooldown: 20s · Energy cost: 80

Unleashes a slow-moving floating bubble that deals continuous Hydro DMG to nearby opponents.

Passive talent: Spotless Waters

After the Dewdrop fired by the Hold Mode of the Aquacrest Saber hits an opponent, a Sourcewater Droplet will be generated near to the Traveler. If the Traveler picks it up, they will restore 7% HP.
1 Droplet can be created this way every second, and each use of Aquacrest Saber can create 4 Droplets at most.

Passive talent: Clear Waters

If HP has been consumed via Suffusion while using the Hold Mode Aquacrest Saber, the Torrent Surge at the skill's end will deal Bonus DMG equal to 45% of the total HP the Traveler has consumed in this skill use via Suffusion.
The maximum DMG Bonus that can be gained this way is 5,000.

Traveler (Hydro) constellations

C1: Swelling Lake

Picking up a Sourcewater Droplet will restore 2 Energy to the Traveler.
You must first unlock the Ascension Talent "Spotless Waters."

C2: Trickling Purity

The Movement SPD of Rising Waters' bubble will be decreased by 30%, and its duration increased by 3s.

C3: Turbulent Ripples

Increases the Level of Aquacrest Saber by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C4: Pouring Descent

When using Aquacrest Saber, an Aquacrest Aegis that can absorb 10% of the Traveler's Max HP in DMG will be created and will absorb Hydro DMG with 250% effectiveness. It will persist until the Traveler finishes using the skill.
Once every 2s, after a Dewdrop hits an opponent, if the Traveler is being protected by Aquacrest Aegis, the DMG Absorption of the Aegis will be restored to 10% of the Traveler's Max HP. If the Traveler is not presently being protected by an Aegis, one will be redeployed.

C5: Churning Whirlpool

Increases the Level of Rising Waters by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C6: Tides of Justice

When the Traveler picks up a Sourcewater Droplet, they will restore HP to a nearby party member with the lowest remaining HP percentage based on 6% of said member's Max HP.

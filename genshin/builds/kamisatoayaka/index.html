Kamisato Ayaka Build Guide | Weapons, Artifacts & Teams

Kamisato Ayaka build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Mistsplitter Reforged, Absolution), artifacts (Blizzard Strayer), main stats (ATK% / Cryo DMG…

Role: Main DPS

Ayaka is an on-field Cryo DPS whose powerful 80-Energy Burst delivers most of her damage. Freeze teams help keep enemies inside its path, while her Cryo-infused attacks add damage between Bursts. She needs strong Energy support and careful positioning.

Best weapons for Kamisato Ayaka

  1. Mistsplitter Reforged
  2. Absolution
  3. Haran Geppaku Futsu
  4. Light of Foliar Incision
  5. Primordial Jade Cutter
  6. Finale of the Deep
  7. Wolf-Fang
  8. Amenoma Kageuchi

Best artifacts

  • Blizzard Strayer (4 pcs)
  • Blizzard Strayer + Noblesse Oblige (2 + 2 pcs)
  • Blizzard Strayer + Gladiator's Finale (2 + 2 pcs)

Main stats

  • Sands: ATK%
  • Goblet: Cryo DMG
  • Circlet: Crit DMG / ATK%

Stat targets

  • CR: 30% - 45%
  • CD: 200% - 250%
  • ATK: 1.8k - 2.3k
  • ER: 130% - 150%

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 8-9+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 6-8
  • Elemental Burst: Level 8-9+

Best teams

  • Permafrost Waltz: Kamisato Ayaka, Shenhe, Sangonomiya Kokomi, Kaedehara Kazuha
  • Fanfare Frost: Kamisato Ayaka, Furina, Charlotte, Kaedehara Kazuha
  • Winter Court: Kamisato Ayaka, Mona, Diona, Sucrose
  • Snowbound Symphony: Kamisato Ayaka, Shenhe, Escoffier, Furina

Pros

  • Powerful Burst damage
  • Strong Freeze control
  • Cryo-infused attacks
  • Excellent AoE potential

Cons

  • High Burst cost
  • Needs careful positioning
  • Struggles vs mobile foes
  • Freeze fails on some bosses

Build variations

  • Boss / Unfreezable (On-field DPS): Against bosses and enemies that cannot be Frozen. Position carefully so her Burst connects fully.
  • Craftable / F2P (On-field DPS): When avoiding limited 5-star weapons. Finale improves damage; Amenoma is easier for Energy-hungry rotations.
  • Furina Freeze (On-field DPS): When leveraging Furina’s team damage buffs and Hydro application in an Ayaka Freeze team.

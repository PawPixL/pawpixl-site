Kamisato Ayaka Build Guide: Weapons, Artifacts and Teams

Kamisato Ayaka build guide for Genshin Impact: best weapons (Mistsplitter Reforged, Absolution), artifacts (Blizzard Strayer), main stats, talent priority.

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Role: Main DPS

Ayaka is an on-field Cryo DPS whose powerful 80-Energy Burst delivers most of her damage. Freeze teams help keep enemies inside its path, while her Cryo-infused attacks add damage between Bursts. She needs strong Energy support and careful positioning.

Best weapons for Kamisato Ayaka

  1. Mistsplitter Reforged: Huge CRIT DMG and Elemental DMG bonuses.
  2. Absolution: High ATK and CRIT DMG stat stick.
  3. Haran Geppaku Futsu: CRIT Rate and Elemental DMG boost. Avoid excess CRIT Rate in Freeze.
  4. Light of Foliar Incision: Strong CRIT DMG; passive is less useful.
  5. Primordial Jade Cutter: Great stats, but may overcap CRIT Rate. Adjust artifacts for Freeze CRIT buffs.
  6. Finale of the Deep: High ATK after clearing Bond of Life. Needs sufficient healing to clear Bond of Life.
  7. Wolf-Fang: Boosts Skill and Burst damage. Avoid CRIT Rate overcap. R5 recommended.
  8. Amenoma Kageuchi: Refunds Energy for reliable Bursts. R5 improves Energy refund.

Best artifacts for Kamisato Ayaka

  • Blizzard Strayer (4 pcs): Top Freeze CRIT and Cryo damage. Main stats: ATK% · Cryo DMG · CRIT DMG.
  • Blizzard Strayer + Noblesse Oblige (2 + 2 pcs): Fallback Cryo and Burst damage. Main stats: ATK% · Cryo DMG · CRIT.
  • Blizzard Strayer + Gladiator's Finale (2 + 2 pcs): Fallback Cryo and ATK bonuses. Main stats: ATK% · Cryo DMG · CRIT.

Main stats

  • Sands: ATK%
  • Goblet: Cryo DMG
  • Circlet: Crit DMG / ATK%

Stat targets

  • CR: 30% - 45%
  • CD: 200% - 250%
  • ATK: 1.8k - 2.3k
  • ER: 130% - 150%

Talent priority

  • Normal Attack: Level 8-9+
  • Elemental Skill: Level 6-8
  • Elemental Burst: Level 8-9+

Best teams for Kamisato Ayaka

  • Permafrost Waltz: Kamisato Ayaka, Shenhe, Sangonomiya Kokomi, Kaedehara Kazuha. Shenhe buffs Cryo hits, Kokomi keeps enemies Frozen and heals, while Kazuha groups foes and shreds Cryo RES.
  • Fanfare Frost: Kamisato Ayaka, Furina, Charlotte, Kaedehara Kazuha. Furina supplies Hydro and a large damage buff; Charlotte heals for Fanfare and helps battery Ayaka. Kazuha groups and shreds RES.
  • Winter Court: Kamisato Ayaka, Mona, Diona, Sucrose. Mona adds Hydro and Omen damage amplification, Diona shields, heals, and generates Cryo particles, while Sucrose groups and shreds RES.
  • Snowbound Symphony: Kamisato Ayaka, Shenhe, Escoffier, Furina. Shenhe boosts Cryo damage, Escoffier heals and reduces Cryo/Hydro RES, and Furina provides off-field Hydro and damage buffs.

Pros

  • Powerful Burst damage
  • Strong Freeze control
  • Cryo-infused attacks
  • Excellent AoE potential

Cons

  • High Burst cost
  • Needs careful positioning
  • Struggles vs mobile foes
  • Freeze fails on some bosses

Build variations

Boss / Unfreezable (On-field DPS)

Against bosses and enemies that cannot be Frozen. Position carefully so her Burst connects fully.

  • Weapons: Mistsplitter Reforged, Absolution, Finale of the Deep
  • Artifacts: Blizzard Strayer (4 pcs); Blizzard Strayer + Gladiator's Finale (2 + 2 pcs)
  • Stats: Aim for more sheet CRIT Rate than in full Freeze; Blizzard Strayer grants only 20% against Cryo-affected, unfrozen enemies.
  • Teams: Reliable Cryo application, a battery and damage buffers; Hydro is not required.
  • When it applies: Lower Blizzard Strayer CRIT bonus without Freeze.
  • Notes: Two-piece mixed sets can be easier to balance with CRIT Rate swords.

Craftable / F2P (On-field DPS)

When avoiding limited 5-star weapons. Finale improves damage; Amenoma is easier for Energy-hungry rotations.

  • Weapons: Finale of the Deep, Amenoma Kageuchi, Kagotsurube Isshin
  • Artifacts: Blizzard Strayer (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Use enough ER to Burst every rotation. Amenoma reduces ER needs, particularly with refinements.
  • Teams: Finale of the Deep prefers a strong healer to clear Bond of Life.
  • When it applies: Finale passive needs Bond of Life cleared; Amenoma appreciates high refinements.
  • Notes: Finale is crafted in Fontaine; Amenoma is crafted in Inazuma.

Furina Freeze (On-field DPS)

When leveraging Furina’s team damage buffs and Hydro application in an Ayaka Freeze team.

  • Weapons: Mistsplitter Reforged, Finale of the Deep, Amenoma Kageuchi
  • Artifacts: Blizzard Strayer (4 pcs)
  • Stats: Balance ER, CRIT DMG and ATK; an ATK% Goblet can be competitive with strong damage bonuses.
  • Teams: Furina and a reliable healer, plus Cryo Energy support.
  • When it applies: Healing must sustain Fanfare and rotations.
  • Notes: A Cryo damage Goblet remains the general default.

Kamisato Ayaka talents

Normal Attack: Kamisato Art: Kabuki

Normal Attack
Performs up to 5 rapid strikes.

Charged Attack
Consumes a certain amount of Stamina to unleash a flurry of sword ki.

Plunging Attack
Plunges from mid-air to strike the ground below, damaging enemies along the path and dealing AoE DMG upon impact.

Elemental Skill: Kamisato Art: Hyouka

Cooldown: 10s

Summons blooming ice to launch nearby opponents, dealing AoE Cryo DMG.

Alternate Sprint: Kamisato Art: Senho

Alternate Sprint
Ayaka consumes Stamina and cloaks herself in a frozen fog that moves with her.

In Senho form, she moves swiftly upon water.
When she reappears, the following effects occur:
·Ayaka unleashes frigid energy to apply Cryo on nearby opponents.
·Coldness condenses around Ayaka's blade, infusing her attacks with Cryo for a brief period.

Elemental Burst: Kamisato Art: Soumetsu

Cooldown: 20s · Energy cost: 80

Summons forth a snowstorm with flawless poise, unleashing a Frostflake Seki no To that moves forward continuously.

Frostflake Seki no To
·A storm of whirling icy winds that slashes repeatedly at every enemy it touches, dealing Cryo DMG.
·The snowstorm explodes after its duration ends, dealing AoE Cryo DMG.

Passive talent: Amatsumi Kunitsumi Sanctification

After using Kamisato Art: Hyouka, Kamisato Ayaka's Normal and Charged Attacks deal 30% increased DMG for 6s.

Passive talent: Kanten Senmyou Blessing

When the Cryo application at the end of Kamisato Art: Senho hits an opponent, Kamisato Ayaka gains the following effects:
·Restores 10 Stamina
·Gains 18% Cryo DMG Bonus for 10s.

Passive talent: Fruits of Shinsa

When Ayaka crafts Weapon Ascension Materials, she has a 10% chance to receive double the product.

Kamisato Ayaka constellations

C1: Snowswept Sakura

When Kamisato Ayaka's Normal or Charged Attacks deal Cryo DMG to opponents, it has a 50% chance of decreasing the CD of Kamisato Art: Hyouka by 0.3s. This effect can occur once every 0.1s.

C2: Blizzard Blade Seki no To

When casting Kamisato Art: Soumetsu, unleashes 2 smaller additional Frostflake Seki no To, each dealing 20% of the original storm's DMG.

C3: Frostbloom Kamifubuki

Increases the Level of Kamisato Art: Soumetsu by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C4: Ebb and Flow

Opponents damaged by Kamisato Art: Soumetsu's Frostflake Seki no To will have their DEF decreased by 30% for 6s.

C5: Blossom Cloud Irutsuki

Increases the Level of Kamisato Art: Hyouka by 3.
Maximum upgrade level is 15.

C6: Dance of Suigetsu

Kamisato Ayaka gains Usurahi Butou every 10s, increasing her Charged Attack DMG by 298%. This buff will be cleared 0.5s after Ayaka's Charged ATK hits an opponent, after which the timer for this ability will restart.

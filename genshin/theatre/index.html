Experimental Field Generator (Act 3, Geo)

What it does:

  • High Geo RES: 70% resistance to Geo damage.
  • Gravity Field: Increases jump height and enables Plunging Attacks.
  • Shockwaves: Sends out ground pulses that deal AoE damage.

How to beat it:

  • Bring Pyro or Hydro DPS: Avoid relying on Geo damage.
  • Use Pneuma Attacks: Three hits disable the Gravity Field.
  • Jump the Shockwaves: Dodge ground pulses and use Plunging Attacks for damage.

Suggested characters: Neuvillette, Lyney, Arlecchino, Furina, Xilonen, Xiangling, Xingqiu, Dehya.

Veteran Arithmetic Enhancer Mek (Act 6, Geo)

What it does:

  • Geo Ward: Gains a Geo shield that raises all resistances by 200 percentage points.
  • Gravity Field: Increases jump height and unleashes damaging Geo shockwaves.
  • Ousia Core: Vulnerable to Pneuma attacks once its Geo shield is destroyed.

How to beat it:

  • Break the Ward: Use Geo, Claymore or Plunging Attacks to destroy its shield.
  • Disable the Core: Strike with Pneuma after breaking the shield to stun the Mek.
  • Jump and Punish: Leap over shockwaves, then unleash damage while its defenses are down.

Suggested characters: Navia, Arataki Itto, Zhongli, Xilonen, Neuvillette, Diluc, Furina, Lyney.

Radiant Moongecko (Act 8, Geo)

What it does:

  • Moonstone Anchor: Summons a construct protected by layered Geo shields while preparing a powerful attack.
  • Radiant Cocoon: Drains party HP, suppresses damage and healing, and increases the boss's Geo Resistance.
  • Geo Resistance: Gains high Geo RES during Cocoon, but becomes vulnerable when the effect is cleared.

How to beat it:

  • Shatter the Anchor: Use Geo, Lunar-Crystallize or blunt attacks to break its shields quickly.
  • Heal to Full: Fully heal an affected character to clear Cocoon and reduce the boss's Geo Resistance.
  • Burst During Stagger: Unload your strongest attacks after breaking the Anchor or clearing Cocoon.

Suggested characters: Zibai, Linnea, Columbina, Noelle, Mavuika, Xilonen, Furina, Aino.

Gluttonous Yumkasaur Mountain King (Act 10, Dendro)

What it does:

  • Flamegranates: Summons explosive fruits that it collects and swallows.
  • Explosive Barrage: Launches dangerous projectiles if its fruit mechanic goes uninterrupted.
  • Dendro Attacks: Uses spinning strikes, body slams and Dendro projectiles.

How to beat it:

  • Bring Pyro: Ignite Flamegranates before the boss swallows them.
  • Trigger a Stun: Exploding fruits damage the boss and create an opening.
  • Burst While Down: Unleash your strongest attacks during its stagger.

Suggested characters: Lyney, Yoimiya, Arlecchino, Mavuika, Neuvillette, Tartaglia, Hu Tao, Klee.

Shadowy Husk x3 (Arcana Challenge I, Pyro)

What it does:

  • Standard Bearer (Pyro): Grants nearby Husks Pyro shields when striking a shielded character.
  • Line Breaker (Hydro): Heals nearby Husks when its attacks hit a shielded character.
  • Defender (Cryo): Gains ATK and a frontal guard when striking a shielded character.

How to beat it:

  • Avoid Shields: Use healing and dodging to prevent their special buffs.
  • Focus the Healer: Defeat the Hydro Line Breaker early to prevent recovery.
  • Use AoE and Hydro: Hit multiple Husks, break Pyro shields and flank the Defender.

Suggested characters: Neuvillette, Tartaglia, Mavuika, Arlecchino, Hu Tao, Sangonomiya Kokomi, Vodyanitsa, Bennett.

Consecrated Beasts ×4 (Arcana Challenge II, Electro)

What it does:

  • Phagocytic Form: Beasts gain increased resistance and unleash powerful elemental attacks.
  • Energy Blocks: Special attacks leave elemental blocks that can be destroyed to expose their weakness.
  • Relentless Assault: Aggressive charges, sweeping attacks and elemental bursts punish poor positioning.

How to beat it:

  • Break Energy Blocks: Use matching Pyro or Hydro attacks when possible, or repeated elemental hits.
  • Stun and Punish: After breaking a block, attack its beast to deal major damage and paralyze it.
  • Stay Protected: Bring shielding or healing, dodge heavy attacks and maintain consistent damage.

Suggested characters: Zhongli, Neuvillette, Navia, Noelle, Navia, Mavuika, Bennett, Xilonen.

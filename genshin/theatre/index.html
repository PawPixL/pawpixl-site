Jadeplume Terrorshroom (Act 3, Dendro)

What it does:

  • Electro fills its Activation/Fury gauge
  • Pyro reduces Activation — avoid it even if using a Special Guest
  • At full Activation, it unleashes an empowered attack sequence
  • Afterwards, it becomes paralyzed with reduced RES, creating your big DPS window

How to beat it:

  • Spam Electro → Fill Fury → Survive its empowered attack → BURST during paralysis.
  • Don't waste your strongest roster here. The entire gimmick actually helps you kill it. Feed it Electro until it goes berserk, dodge the attack, then unload while it's exhausted.

Suggested characters: Flins, Varesa, Fischl, Yae Miko, Furina, Yelan, Columbina, Fischl.

Lord of the Hidden Depths: Whisperer of Nightmares (Act 6, Electro)

What it does:

  • Shield Phase: Summons 4 Fishers; each defeated removes 25% of its shield.
  • Lunar Reactions: Effective for breaking the shield.
  • Shield Break: Deals 20% Max HP damage, reduces RES by 70%, and stuns for 10s.
  • Shield Failure: Triggers a powerful arena-wide Electro attack.

How to beat it:

  • Use Lunar reactions or defeat all 4 Fishers to break the shield.
  • Save Bursts for the stunned phase.
  • Unleash your strongest rotation during the 70% RES reduction.
  • Dodge teleport strikes and tail sweeps.

Suggested characters: Neuvillette, Columbina, Yelan, Zibai, Ineffa, Flins, Ororon, Illuga.

Churldric, Ladler of Broth (Act 8, Geo)

What it does:

  • Summons a Cooking Pot and begins his ritual
  • The pot counts as a Geo object and is particularly weak to Blunt attacks
  • You have roughly 16 seconds before the cooking resolves
  • Destroying the pot damages Churldric, stuns him and reduces ALL RES by 50% during the stun

How to beat it:

  • Pot appears → IGNORE CHURLDRIC → Destroy pot → Churldric gets stunned → BURST HIM.
  • Hydro or Electro can extinguish the pot's flame and slow the cooking mechanic, but destroying the pot is what you really want because it gives you the huge vulnerability window.

Suggested characters: Navia, Noelle, Arataki Itto, Zibai, Raiden Shogun, Xingqiu, Furina, Aino.

Radiant Moonfly (Act 10, Dendro)

What it does:

  • Uses Radiant Cocoon, damaging your party and debuffing affected characters
  • Cocoon gives −100% CRIT Rate, −50% DMG Bonus and −40% Incoming Healing
  • Healing a character back to 100% HP removes the Cocoon
  • Breaking free grants Disentangled: +15% CRIT Rate & +50% DMG Bonus for 15s

How to beat it:

  • Radiant Cocoon → HEAL TO 100% ASAP → Gain Disentangled → BURST.
  • Move away when you see the Dendro Razor
  • Heal to full to break the Cocoon
  • Lunar-Bloom or Hyperbloom work well; bring sustain

Suggested characters: Sangonomiya Kokomi, Kuki Shinobu, qiqi, Sigewinne, Varesa, Yae Miko, Iansan, Mualani.

Consecrated Fanged Beast (Arcana Challenge I, Dendro)

What it does:

  • Empowered state increases resistance.
  • Aggressive elemental attacks deal heavy damage.
  • Special attacks spawn elemental energy blocks.
  • Destroy blocks to stun beasts and deal bonus damage.

How to beat it:

  • Destroy energy blocks immediately.
  • Match the block's element for an instant break.
  • Burst during the paralysis window.
  • Dodge combos and prioritize survival.

Suggested characters: Vesna, Odette, Vodyanitsa, Ineffa, Raiden Shogun, Flins, Navia, Chiori.

Biting-Cold Wayob Manifestation (Arcana Challenge II, Cryo)

What it does:

  • Cryo beams and X-shaped hailstone attacks.
  • Arena drains Energy and prevents Energy gain.
  • Ward grants immunity to damage.
  • Failed shield break triggers a powerful Cryo blast.

How to beat it:

  • Break the ward before the arena expires.
  • Dodge X-shaped hailstone patterns.
  • Use Bursts before Energy Drain.
  • Break the ward to stun for 5 seconds.

Suggested characters: Navia, Sangonomiya Kokomi, Furina, Columbina, Linnea, Neuvillette, Varesa, Kuki Shinobu.

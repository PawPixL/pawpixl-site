Aquila Favonia

A 5-star sword. Base ATK 674 at Lv. 90, with 41.4% Physical DMG Bonus.

Passive: Falcon's Defiance

ATK is increased by 20%. Triggers on taking DMG: the soul of the Falcon of the West awakens, holding the banner of resistance aloft, regenerating HP equal to 100% of ATK and dealing 200% of ATK as DMG to surrounding opponents. This effect can only occur once every 15s.

At each refinement

  • Refinement 1: 20% / 100% / 200%
  • Refinement 2: 25% / 115% / 230%
  • Refinement 3: 30% / 130% / 260%
  • Refinement 4: 35% / 145% / 290%
  • Refinement 5: 40% / 160% / 320%

The soul of the Knights of Favonius. Millennia later, it still calls on the winds of swift justice to vanquish all evil — just like the last heroine who wielded it.

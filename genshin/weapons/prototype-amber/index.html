Prototype Amber

A 4-star catalyst. Base ATK 510 at Lv. 90, with 41.4% HP%.

Passive: Gilding

Using an Elemental Burst regenerates 4 Energy every 2s for 6s. All party members will regenerate 4% HP every 2s for this duration.

At each refinement

  • Refinement 2: 4.5 / 4.5%
  • Refinement 3: 5 / 5%
  • Refinement 4: 5.5 / 5.5%
  • Refinement 5: 6 / 6%

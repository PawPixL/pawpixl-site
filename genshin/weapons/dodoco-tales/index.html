Dodoco Tales

A 4-star catalyst. Base ATK 454 at Lv. 90, with 55.1% ATK%.

Passive: Dodoventure!

Normal Attack hits on opponents increase Charged Attack DMG by 16% for 6s. Charged Attack hits on opponents increase ATK by 8% for 6s.

At each refinement

  • Refinement 1: 16% / 8%
  • Refinement 2: 20% / 10%
  • Refinement 3: 24% / 12%
  • Refinement 4: 28% / 14%
  • Refinement 5: 32% / 16%

How to get it

Event: Midsummer Island Adventure event

Description

A children's book filled with childish short stories at which one cannot help but laugh.

Even those readers who have long reached adulthood cannot help but be absorbed by the innocent, naive little adventures portrayed within.

Added in version 1.6.

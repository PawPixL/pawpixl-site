Mountain-Bracing Bolt

A 4-star polearm. Base ATK 565 at Lv. 90, with 30.6% Energy Recharge.

Passive: Hope Beyond the Peaks

Decreases Climbing Stamina Consumption by 15% and increases Elemental Skill DMG by 12%. Also, after other nearby party members use Elemental Skills, the equipping character's Elemental Skill DMG will also increase by 12% for 8s.

At each refinement

  • Refinement 1: 12% / 12%
  • Refinement 2: 15% / 15%
  • Refinement 3: 18% / 18%
  • Refinement 4: 21% / 21%
  • Refinement 5: 24% / 24%

How to get it

Gacha: Event wish

Description

This polearm was once jointly forged by the craftsmen in the mines as a sacrifice to the spirits of the mountains.

Added in version 5.1.

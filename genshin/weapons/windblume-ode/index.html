Windblume Ode

A 4-star bow. Base ATK 510 at Lv. 90, with 165 Elemental Mastery.

Passive: Windblume Wish

After using an Elemental Skill, receive a boon from the ancient wish of the Windblume, increasing ATK by 16% for 6s.

At each refinement

  • Refinement 1: 16%
  • Refinement 2: 20%
  • Refinement 3: 24%
  • Refinement 4: 28%
  • Refinement 5: 32%

How to get it

Event: Invitation to Windblume (v1.4)

Description

A bow adorned with nameless flowers that bears the earnest hopes of an equally nameless person.

Added in version 1.4.

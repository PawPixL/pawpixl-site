Raven Bow

A 3-star bow. Base ATK 448 at Lv. 90, with 94 Elemental Mastery.

Passive: Bane of Flame and Water

Increases DMG against opponents affected by Hydro or Pyro by 12%.

At each refinement

  • Refinement 1: 12%
  • Refinement 2: 15%
  • Refinement 3: 18%
  • Refinement 4: 21%
  • Refinement 5: 24%

How to get it

Free: Wishes & treasure chests

Description

Ravens are known to be the ferrymen of the dead. This bow's limb is decorated with raven feathers, which forebode the imminent death of its target.

Lithic Blade

A 4-star claymore. Base ATK 510 at Lv. 90, with 41.4% ATK%.

Passive: Lithic Axiom: Unity

For every character in the party who hails from Liyue, the character who equips this weapon gains a 7% ATK increase and a 3% CRIT Rate increase. This effect stacks up to 4 times.

At each refinement

  • Refinement 1: 7% / 3%
  • Refinement 2: 8% / 4%
  • Refinement 3: 9% / 5%
  • Refinement 4: 10% / 6%
  • Refinement 5: 11% / 7%

Gacha

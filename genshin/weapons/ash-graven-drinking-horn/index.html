Ash-Graven Drinking Horn

A 4-star catalyst. Base ATK 510 at Lv. 90, with 41.4% HP%.

Passive: Tupac's Grip

When an attack hits an opponent, deal AoE DMG equal to 40% of Max HP at the target's location. This effect can be triggered once every 15s.

At each refinement

  • Refinement 1: 40%
  • Refinement 2: 50%
  • Refinement 3: 60%
  • Refinement 4: 70%
  • Refinement 5: 80%

How to get it

Event: Traces of Artistry (v5.0)

Description

A ritual vessel passed down amongst the Meztli. They say that this was once a treasure much-valued, yet never used, by the hero "Giant" Tupac.

Added in version 5.0.

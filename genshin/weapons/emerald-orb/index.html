Emerald Orb

A 3-star catalyst. Base ATK 448 at Lv. 90, with 94 Elemental Mastery.

Passive: Rapids

Upon causing a Vaporize, Electro-Charged, Frozen, Bloom, Lunar-Charged, Lunar-Bloom, or a Hydro-infused Swirl reaction, ATK is increased by 20% for 12s.

At each refinement

  • Refinement 1: 20%
  • Refinement 2: 25%
  • Refinement 3: 30%
  • Refinement 4: 35%
  • Refinement 5: 40%

How to get it

Free: Wishes & treasure chests

Description

A catalyst carved out of the hard jade from Jueyun Karst north of Liyue. Small, light, and durable, it is known more colloquially as the "jade ball."

Added in version 1.0.

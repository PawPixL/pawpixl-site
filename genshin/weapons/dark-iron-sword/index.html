Dark Iron Sword

A 3-star sword. Base ATK 401 at Lv. 90, with 141 Elemental Mastery.

Passive: Overloaded

Upon causing an Overloaded, Superconduct, Stellar-Conduct, Electro-Charged, Quicken, Aggravate, Hyperbloom, Lunar-Charged, or Electro-infused Swirl reaction, ATK is increased by 20% for 12s.

At each refinement

  • Refinement 1: 20%
  • Refinement 2: 25%
  • Refinement 3: 30%
  • Refinement 4: 35%
  • Refinement 5: 40%

How to get it

Free: Talk to Chen the Sharp

Description

A perfectly ordinary iron sword, just slightly darker than most.

Mailed Flower

A 4-star claymore. Base ATK 565 at Lv. 90, with 110 Elemental Mastery.

Passive: Whispers of Wind and Flower

Within 8s after the character's Elemental Skill hits an opponent or the character triggers an Elemental Reaction, their ATK and Elemental Mastery will be increased by 12% and 48 respectively.

At each refinement

  • Refinement 1: 12% / 48
  • Refinement 2: 15% / 60
  • Refinement 3: 18% / 72
  • Refinement 4: 21% / 84
  • Refinement 5: 24% / 96

How to get it

Event: Windblume's Breath event

Description

A graceful and elegant greatsword that is decorated with flowers and ribbons.

Added in version 3.5.

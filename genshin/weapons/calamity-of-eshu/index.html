Calamity of Eshu

A 4-star sword. Base ATK 565 at Lv. 90, with 27.6% ATK%.

Passive: Diffusing Boundary

While characters are protected by a Shield, DMG dealt by Normal and Charged Attacks is increased by 20%, and Normal and Charged Attack CRIT Rate is increased by 8%.

At each refinement

  • Refinement 1: 20% / 8%
  • Refinement 2: 25% / 10%
  • Refinement 3: 30% / 12%
  • Refinement 4: 35% / 14%
  • Refinement 5: 40% / 16%

How to get it

Event: Iktomi Spiritseeking Scrolls (v5.2)

Description

A ceremonial blade forged by Sanhaj, the great high priest of the Masters of the Night-Wind. In tales of bygone days, Sanhaj could use this curved blade to perform rituals, blurring the boundary between the Night Kingdom and the present world.

Added in version 5.2.

Echoes of the Heart

A 4-star catalyst. Base ATK 565 at Lv. 90, with 27.6% ATK%.

Passive: Echo of a Vow

Triggering an Elemental Reaction increases the equipping character's Elemental Mastery by 60 for 12s, while triggering a Stellar Glimmer reaction increases their Stellar Glimmer reaction DMG dealt by 16% for 12s. The aforementioned effects can trigger even when the character is not on the field.

At each refinement

  • Refinement 1: 60 / 16%
  • Refinement 2: 75 / 20%
  • Refinement 3: 90 / 24%
  • Refinement 4: 105 / 28%
  • Refinement 5: 120 / 32%

How to get it

Craft

Description

A Catalyst said to crystallize voice. Within it lies a sealed oath of loyalty, as well as a heart long since forgotten by mankind.

Added in version 7.0.

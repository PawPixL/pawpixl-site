Azurelight

A 5-star sword. Base ATK 674 at Lv. 90, with 22.1% CRIT Rate.

Passive: Whitehill's Bestowal

Within 12s after an Elemental Skill is used, ATK is increased by 24%. During this time, when the equipping character has 0 Energy, ATK will be further increased by 24%, and CRIT DMG will be increased by 40%.

At each refinement

  • Refinement 1: 24% / 24% / 40%
  • Refinement 2: 30% / 30% / 50%
  • Refinement 3: 36% / 36% / 60%
  • Refinement 4: 42% / 42% / 70%
  • Refinement 5: 48% / 48% / 80%

How to get it

Gacha

Signature weapon

Signature weapon of Skirk.

Description

This blade was once pure and pristine as a white dawn star, but it has since been tinted a deep, dark blue.

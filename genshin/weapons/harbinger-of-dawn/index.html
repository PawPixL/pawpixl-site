Harbinger of Dawn

A 3-star sword. Base ATK 401 at Lv. 90, with 46.9% CRIT DMG.

Passive: Vigorous

When HP is above 90%, increases CRIT Rate by 14%.

At each refinement

  • Refinement 1: 14%
  • Refinement 2: 17.5%
  • Refinement 3: 21%
  • Refinement 4: 24.5%
  • Refinement 5: 28%

How to get it

Free: Wishes & treasure chests

Description

A sword that once shone like the sun. The wielder of this sword will be blessed with a "feel-good" buff. The reflective material on the blade has long worn off.

Added in version 1.0.

Mistsplitter Reforged

A 5-star sword. Base ATK 674 at Lv. 90, with 44.1% CRIT DMG.

Passive: Mistsplitter's Edge

Gain a 12% Elemental DMG Bonus for all elements and receive the might of the Mistsplitter's Emblem. At stack levels 1/2/3, the Mistsplitter's Emblem provides a 8/16/28% Elemental DMG Bonus for the character's Elemental Type. The character will obtain 1 stack of the Mistsplitter's Emblem in each of the following scenarios: Normal Attack deals Elemental DMG (stack lasts 5s), casting Elemental Burst (stack lasts 10s); Energy is less than 100% (stack disappears when Energy is full). Each stack's duration is calculated independently.

At each refinement

  • Refinement 1: 12% / 8/16/28%
  • Refinement 2: 15% / 10/20/35%
  • Refinement 3: 18% / 12/24/42%
  • Refinement 4: 21% / 14/28/49%
  • Refinement 5: 24% / 16/32/56%

Starsealed Edict

A 5-star catalyst. Base ATK 542 at Lv. 90, with 88.2% CRIT DMG.

Passive: Dawnstar's Flowing Trail

Increases CRIT DMG by 24%. The equipping character gains the Crystal Compounding effect when they use their Elemental Skill, increasing Elemental Mastery by 48 for 20s. During this time, the equipping character gains a Law of Combustion stack when a nearby party member triggers an Elemental Reaction. Each stack increases Elemental Mastery by an additional 24. Max 3 stacks. These stacks last until Crystal Compounding ends or the equipping character uses their Elemental Skill again. Radiance: Stellar-Conduct: Each Law of Combustion stack also increases the equipping character's Stellar-Conduct CRIT DMG by 12%. These effects can be triggered even when the equipping character is off-field.

At each refinement

  • Refinement 1: 24% / 48 / 24 / 12%
  • Refinement 2: 30% / 60 / 30 / 15%
  • Refinement 3: 36% / 72 / 36 / 18%
  • Refinement 4: 42% / 84 / 42 / 21%
  • Refinement 5: 48% / 96 / 48 / 24%

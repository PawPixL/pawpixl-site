Blade of Atonement

A 4-star claymore. Base ATK 565 at Lv. 90, with 27.6% ATK%.

Passive: Repentance and Redemption

Triggering an Elemental Reaction increases the equipping character's Elemental Mastery by 64 for 12s, while triggering a Stellar Glimmer reaction increases their ATK by 16% for 12s. The aforementioned effects can trigger even when the character is not on the field.

At each refinement

  • Refinement 1: 64 / 16%
  • Refinement 2: 80 / 20%
  • Refinement 3: 96 / 24%
  • Refinement 4: 112 / 28%
  • Refinement 5: 128 / 32%

How to get it

Craft

Description

A greatsword passed down by the Luchkin. It is said that only the bravest warrior is qualified to wield it.

Added in version 7.0.

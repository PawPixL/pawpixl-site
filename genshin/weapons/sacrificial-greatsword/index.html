Sacrificial Greatsword

A 4-star claymore. Base ATK 565 at Lv. 90, with 30.6% Energy Recharge.

Passive: Composed

After damaging an opponent with an Elemental Skill, the skill has a 40% chance to end its own CD. Can only occur once every 30.

At each refinement

  • Refinement 1: 40% / 30
  • Refinement 2: 50% / 26
  • Refinement 3: 60% / 22s
  • Refinement 4: 70% / 19
  • Refinement 5: 80% / 16s

How to get it

Gacha

Description

A ceremonial greatsword that has become petrified over time. The trinkets on it are still visible. It grants the wielder the power to withstand the winds of time.

Added in version 1.0.

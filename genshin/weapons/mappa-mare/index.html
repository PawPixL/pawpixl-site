Mappa Mare

A 4-star catalyst. Base ATK 565 at Lv. 90, with 110 Elemental Mastery.

Passive: Infusion Scroll

Triggering an Elemental reaction grants a 8% Elemental DMG Bonus for 10s. Max 2 stacks.

At each refinement

  • Refinement 2: 10%
  • Refinement 3: 12%
  • Refinement 4: 14%
  • Refinement 5: 16%

Flower-Wreathed Feathers

A 4-star bow. Base ATK 510 at Lv. 90, with 41.4% ATK%.

Passive: Inflorescence Unattainable

Decreases Gliding Stamina consumption by 15%. When using Aimed Shots, the DMG dealt by Charged Attacks increases by 6% every 0.5s. This effect can stack up to 6 times and will be removed 10s after leaving Aiming Mode.

At each refinement

  • Refinement 1: 6%
  • Refinement 2: 7.5%
  • Refinement 3: 9%
  • Refinement 4: 10.5%
  • Refinement 5: 12%

How to get it

Gacha: Event wish

Description

A longbow that burns like a flame. The feathers that adorn it are said to have come from the ancient tyrant Ngoubou.

Added in version 5.2.

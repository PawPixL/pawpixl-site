Ballad of the Fjords

A 4-star polearm. Base ATK 510 at Lv. 90, with 27.6% CRIT Rate.

Passive: Tales of the Tundra

When there are at least 3 different Elemental Types in your party, Elemental Mastery will be increased by 120.

At each refinement

  • Refinement 1: 120.
  • Refinement 2: 150.
  • Refinement 3: 180.
  • Refinement 4: 210.
  • Refinement 5: 240.

How to get it

Battle Pass: Gnostic Hymn weapon chest

Description

A polearm used by those seeking to catch fish in the tundra. It lets none escape.

Added in version 4.0.

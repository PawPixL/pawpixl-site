Torrents of Spring

Preview: this weapon is not released yet, so details may change.

A 5-star claymore. Base ATK 741 at Lv. 90.

Passive: Wavebreaking Thunder

Increases the equipper's ATK by 16%. When the equipper's Electro attacks hit opponents, they will gain "Royal Decree" for 15s: Stellar-Conduct reaction DMG dealt by the active character on the field is increased by 20%. While the effect lasts, when the equipper's Electro Stellar-Conduct attacks hit opponents, the order is considered Complete, enhancing the "Royal Decree" effect: the equipper's ATK is further increased by 16%, and Stellar-Conduct reaction DMG dealt by the active character on the field is additionally increased by 20%. The above effects also take effect while off-field.

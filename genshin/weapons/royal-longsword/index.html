Royal Longsword

A 4-star sword. Base ATK 510 at Lv. 90, with 41.4% ATK%.

Passive: Focus

Upon damaging an opponent, increases CRIT Rate by 8%. Max 5 stacks. A CRIT Hit removes all stacks.

At each refinement

  • Refinement 1: 8%
  • Refinement 2: 10%
  • Refinement 3: 12%
  • Refinement 4: 14%
  • Refinement 5: 16%

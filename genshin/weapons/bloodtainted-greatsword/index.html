Bloodtainted Greatsword

A 3-star claymore. Base ATK 354 at Lv. 90, with 187 Elemental Mastery.

Passive: Bane of Fire and Thunder

Increases DMG against opponents affected by Pyro or Electro by 12%.

At each refinement

  • Refinement 1: 12%
  • Refinement 2: 15%
  • Refinement 3: 18%
  • Refinement 4: 21%
  • Refinement 5: 24%

How to get it

Free: Wishes & treasure chests

Description

A steel sword that is said to have been coated with dragon blood, rendering it invulnerable to damage. This effect is not extended to its wielder, however.

Added in version 1.0.

Sacrificer's Staff

A 4-star polearm. Base ATK 620 at Lv. 90, with 9.2% CRIT Rate.

Passive: Untainted Desire

For 6s after an Elemental Skill hits an opponent, ATK is increased by 8% and Energy Recharge is increased by 6%. Max 3 stacks. This effect can be triggered even when the equipping character is off-field.

At each refinement

  • Refinement 1: 8% / 6%
  • Refinement 2: 10% / 7.5%
  • Refinement 3: 12% / 9%
  • Refinement 4: 14% / 10.5%
  • Refinement 5: 16% / 12%

How to get it

Gacha: Event wish (v6.4)

Description

A silver-white polearm, pure and completely without blemish. Legend has it that it was used by the Archpriestesses of Hyperborea in ages past to guide their flock of Frostmoon Scions.

Added in version 6.1.

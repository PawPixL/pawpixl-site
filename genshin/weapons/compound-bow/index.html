Compound Bow

A 4-star bow. Base ATK 454 at Lv. 90, with 69% Physical DMG Bonus.

Passive: Infusion Arrow

Normal Attack and Charged Attack hits increase ATK by 4% and Normal ATK SPD by 1.2% for 6s. Max 4 stacks. Can only occur once every 0.3s.

At each refinement

  • Refinement 2: 5% / 1.5%
  • Refinement 3: 6% / 1.8%
  • Refinement 4: 7% / 2.1%
  • Refinement 5: 8% / 2.4%

Skyrider Greatsword

A 3-star claymore. Base ATK 401 at Lv. 90, with 43.9% Physical DMG Bonus.

Passive: Courage

On hit, Normal or Charged Attacks increase ATK by 6% for 6s. Max 4 stacks. Can occur once every 0.5s.

At each refinement

  • Refinement 2: 7%
  • Refinement 3: 8%
  • Refinement 4: 9%
  • Refinement 5: 10%

How to get it

Free: Stuck inside a stone in Guyun Stone Forest

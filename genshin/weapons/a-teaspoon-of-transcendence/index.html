A Teaspoon of Transcendence

A 5-star claymore. Base ATK 674 at Lv. 90, with 44.1% CRIT DMG.

Passive: White Fairy's Queening

ATK is increased by 28%. Additionally, each time the equipping character hits an opponent with their Charged Attack, they attain "Transcendence" for a short time: their Stellar-Conduct and Stellar Swirl reaction DMG is increased by 16% for 5s. This effect can stack once every 0.2s, max 3 stacks.

At each refinement

  • Refinement 1: 28% / 16%
  • Refinement 2: 35% / 20%
  • Refinement 3: 42% / 24%
  • Refinement 4: 49% / 28%
  • Refinement 5: 56% / 32%

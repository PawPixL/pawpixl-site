Staff of Homa

A 5-star polearm. Base ATK 608 at Lv. 90, with 66.2% CRIT DMG.

Passive: Reckless Cinnabar

HP increased by 20%. Additionally, provides an ATK Bonus based on 0.8% of the wielder's Max HP. When the wielder's HP is less than 50%, this ATK Bonus is increased by an additional 1% of Max HP.

At each refinement

  • Refinement 1: 20% / 0.8% / 1%
  • Refinement 2: 25% / 1% / 1.2%
  • Refinement 3: 30% / 1.2% / 1.4%
  • Refinement 4: 35% / 1.4% / 1.6%
  • Refinement 5: 40% / 1.6% / 1.8%

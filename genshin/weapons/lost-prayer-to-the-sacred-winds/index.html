Lost Prayer to the Sacred Winds

A 5-star catalyst. Base ATK 608 at Lv. 90, with 33.1% CRIT Rate.

Passive: Boundless Blessing

Increases Movement SPD by 10%. When in battle, gain a 8% Elemental DMG Bonus every 4s. Max 4 stacks. Lasts until the character falls or leaves combat.

At each refinement

  • Refinement 1: 8%
  • Refinement 2: 10%
  • Refinement 3: 12%
  • Refinement 4: 14%
  • Refinement 5: 16%

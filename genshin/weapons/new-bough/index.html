New Bough

A 4-star sword. Base ATK 510 at Lv. 90, with 55.1% CRIT DMG.

Passive: Wildgrowth

When the equipping character hits the opponent with an attack within 12s after using the Elemental Skill, they gain the "Verdant" effect, which increases their ATK by 4% and their Elemental Mastery by 20. This effect lasts 6s and can trigger once every second. Max 3 stacks. The aforementioned effects can still trigger even when the equipping character is not on the field. Radiance: Stellar Glimmer: The effect of "Verdant" is changed to: Increases ATK by 6% as well as Stellar Glimmer reaction DMG dealt by the equipping character by 8%.

At each refinement

  • Refinement 1: 4% / 20 / 6% / 8%
  • Refinement 2: 5% / 25 / 7.5% / 10%
  • Refinement 3: 6% / 30 / 9% / 12%
  • Refinement 4: 7% / 35 / 10.5% / 14%
  • Refinement 5: 8% / 40 / 12% / 16%

How to get it

Gacha: Event wish (v7.1)

Description

A sword made from sacred wood, said to have once turned the tide of battle.

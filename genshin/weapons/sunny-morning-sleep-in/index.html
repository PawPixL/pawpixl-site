Sunny Morning Sleep-In

A 5-star catalyst. Base ATK 542 at Lv. 90, with 265 Elemental Mastery.

Passive: Bathhouses, Hawks, and Narukami

Elemental Mastery increases by 120 for 6s after triggering Swirl or Stellar Swirl. Elemental Mastery increases by 96 for 9s after the wielder's Elemental Skill hits an opponent. Elemental Mastery increases by 32 for 30s after the wielder's Elemental Burst hits an opponent.

At each refinement

  • Refinement 1: 120 / 96 / 32
  • Refinement 2: 150 / 120 / 40
  • Refinement 3: 180 / 144 / 48
  • Refinement 4: 210 / 168 / 56
  • Refinement 5: 240 / 192 / 64

How to get it

Gacha

Description

A bell lantern forged from purple jade. They say it brings sweet dreams when placed beside one's pillow.

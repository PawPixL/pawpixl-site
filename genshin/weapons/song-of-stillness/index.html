Song of Stillness

A 4-star bow. Base ATK 510 at Lv. 90, with 41.4% ATK%.

Passive: Benthic Pulse

After the wielder is healed, they will deal 16% more DMG for 8s. This can be triggered even when the character is not on the field.

At each refinement

  • Refinement 1: 16%
  • Refinement 2: 20%
  • Refinement 3: 24%
  • Refinement 4: 28%
  • Refinement 5: 32%

How to get it

Craft: Forge (Fontaine)

Description

A strange longbow that resembles a sea creature. Its string makes no sound at all.

Added in version 4.0.

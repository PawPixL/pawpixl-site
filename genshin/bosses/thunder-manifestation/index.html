Thunder Manifestation

World boss · Elemental Lifeforms

Also known as: Stormy Resentment

An unusual Electro life form that weaves thunder and lightning as a song unto the resentment that drives its every move.

Even though this creature may seem similar to the Oceanids, it lacks their intelligence.

They say that this is an elemental being driven by pure regret.

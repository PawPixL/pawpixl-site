Lord of the Hidden Depths: Whisperer of Nightmares

World boss · The Abyss

An Abyssal creature that creeps through rifts in the hidden depths, quietly watching this world.

Its eerie, faceless form is said to be nothing more than a projection, while its true body exists only as a formless mass of pure Abyssal energy.

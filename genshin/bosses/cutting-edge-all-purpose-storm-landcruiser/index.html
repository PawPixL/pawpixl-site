Cutting Edge All-Purpose Storm Landcruiser

Elite enemy · Fatui

Also known as: Cutting Edge All-Purpose Storm

An autonomous machine that applies the principles of naval combat to land battles.

Landcruisers were engineered by the Armory Palace's finest engineers to serve as "armed ships that cruise on land." They are now deployed en masse at the bleeding edge of the Fatui's conquests.

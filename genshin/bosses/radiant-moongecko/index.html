Radiant Moongecko

World boss · Mystical Beasts

An ancient monster drawn to moonlight, this gecko dwells deep within layers of rock.

Though the memory of true moonlight has faded, instinct remains, compelling them, on nights of the full moon, to feed on rock and soil suffused with lunar radiance to preserve their bloodline.

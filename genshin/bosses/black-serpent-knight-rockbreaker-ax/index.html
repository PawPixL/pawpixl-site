Black Serpent Knight: Rockbreaker Ax

Elite enemy · The Abyss

Also known as: Adalgsio, Adelbert, Erhard, Roderigo

Once upon a time, these were knights of the realm and carriers of significant status.

The long years and a curse seems to have robbed them of their reason and memory. Now, all that remains within that armor is the will to "fight for something, someone, and some matter."

Depending on how much wear and tear they have undergone, they are by turns known as "Shadowy Husks" or "Black Serpent Knights."

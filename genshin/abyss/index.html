Spiral Abyss Floor 12 Guide: Bosses and Teams (7.2 · Nov 2026 · Nov 16 – Dec 15)

Spiral Abyss Floor 12 guide for 7.2, Nov 2026, Nov 16 – Dec 15, 2026: bosses, mechanics and recommended teams. Featuring Zibai, Columbina, Linnea, Illuga.

Last updated: November 12, 2026 · 7.2 · Nov 2026 · Nov 16 – Dec 15, 2026

Rules and bonuses

  • First Half: Increases Lunar-Crystallize damage by 75%, favoring Geo and crystallize-focused teams.
  • Second Half: Grants all party members +200 Elemental Mastery (EM), boosting reaction-based damage

Chambers and enemies

Battle-Hardened Prism Slime ×2 · Wavebinder: Stormfang · Frostnight Herra · Guardian Blade of Drifting Snow · Radiant Moongecko · Perpetual Mechanical Array

Battle-Hardened Prism Slime ×2 (Chamber 1, First Half)

What it does:

  • Two high-HP targets. Group damage where possible; the first-half Lunar-Crystallize bonus rewards Hydro + Geo teams.

Wavebinder: Stormfang (Chamber 1, Second Half)

What it does:

  • Drinking builds Courage; Electro DMG drains it.
  • Stellar Glimmer Electro reactions drain more Courage.
  • Apply Radiance: Stellar Glimmer while it drinks to slow Courage buildup.
  • Full Courage grants a shield and increased RES.
  • Depleting Courage removes the shield and deals massive backlash DMG.

Frostnight Herra (Chamber 2, First Half)

What it does:

  • At half HP: channeled attacks and an element-absorbing gauge
  • Fill the gauge to stop the channel
  • Then becomes immune to the element that filled it most

Guardian Blade of Drifting Snow (Chamber 2, Second Half)

What it does:

  • Cryo shield appears around 80% HP (up to twice)
  • Shield not broken in 20s = it heals 10%
  • Breaking the shield lowers its RES for 10s

Radiant Moongecko (Chamber 3, First Half)

What it does:

  • Geo shield bars (tail lights up with Geo)
  • Breaking shield bars also hurts its HP
  • Radiant Cocoon: more damage taken, no crits, less healing

Perpetual Mechanical Array (Chamber 3, Second Half)

What it does:

  • Shield goes up; four mini-sentinels appear
  • Kill the sentinels to break the shield
  • Laser beams and slams

Recommended teams

First Half

  • Zibai, Columbina, Linnea, Illuga
  • Mitya, Yae Miko, Odette, Valeriy

Second Half

  • Faruzan, Vesna, Diona, Traveler (Cryo)
  • Clorinde, Fischl, Nahida, Baizhu

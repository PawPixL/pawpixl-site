Crystalline Cyst Dust

3-star Character and Weapon Enhancement Material

A huge lump of spore powder collected from a structurally complete spore cyst. Put it in an appropriate environment, and the area might be filled with a thriving aggregation of Floating Fungi later...

Where to get it

  • Dropped by Lv. 60+ Fungi
  • Dropped uncommonly by Lv. 60+ Fungi (activated)
  • Dropped uncommonly by Lv. 60+ Fungi (scorched)
  • Stardust Exchange: Craft after exchanging

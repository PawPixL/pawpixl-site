Calla Lily

1-star Local Specialty (Mondstadt)

A flower that grows near water sources. When cooked, the petals have a chunky texture, yet are sweet and a little bitter.

Where to get it

  • Found near lakes
  • Recommendation: Found in Springvale
  • Grown in the Serenitea Pot
  • Go to collect

Characters who use it

  • Diona
  • Kaeya

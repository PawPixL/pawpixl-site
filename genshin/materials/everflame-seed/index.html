Everflame Seed

4-star Character Level-Up Material

A seed that fuels the unending flames of the Pyro Regisvine. It emits Pyro energy as if it will burn all that has ever sprouted from the ground.

Where to get it

  • Dropped by Lv. 30+ Pyro Regisvines
  • Pyro Regisvine

Characters who use it

  • Diluc
  • Klee
  • Xiangling
